32. as-Sajdah: The Adoration

001 alif-lām-meem Alif. Lam. Mim
002 tanzeelu alkitābi lā rayba feehi min rabbi alʿālameena The revelation of the Scripture whereof there is no doubt is from the Lord of the Worlds.
003 am yaqooloona iftarāhu bal huwa alḥaqqu min rabbika litunḏira qawman mā atāhum min naḏeerin min qablika laʿallahum yahtadoona Or say they: He hath invented it ? Nay, but it is the Truth from thy Lord, that thou mayst warn a folk to whom no warner came before thee, that haply they may walk aright.
004 allāhu allāḏee ḫalaqa alssamāwāti waal-arḍa wamā baynahumā fee sittati ayyāmin ṯumma istawā ʿalā alʿarši mā lakum min doonihi min waliyyin walā šafeeʿin afalā tataḏakkaroona Allah it is Who created the heavens and the earth, and that which is between them, in six Days. Then He mounted the Throne. Ye have not, beside Him, a protecting friend or mediator. Will ye not then remember ?
005 yudabbiru al-amra mina alssamā-i ilā al-arḍi ṯumma yaʿruju ilayhi fee yawmin kāna miqdāruhu alfa sanatin mimmā taʿuddoona He directeth the ordinance from the heaven unto the earth; then it ascendeth unto Him in a Day, whereof the measure is a thousand years of that ye reckon.
006 [romanization missing] Such is the Knower of the Invisible and the Visible, the Mighty, the Merciful,
007 allāḏee aḥsana kulla šay-in ḫalaqahu wabadaa ḫalqa al-insāni min ṭeenin Who made all things good which He created, and He began the creation of man from clay;
008 thumma jaʿala naslahu min sulālatin min mā-in maheenin Then He made his seed from a draught of despised fluid;
009 thumma sawwāhu wanafaḫa feehi min rooḥihi wajaʿala lakumu alssamʿa waal-absara waal-af-idata qaleelan mā taškuroona Then He fashioned him and breathed into him of His Spirit; and appointed for you hearing and sight and hearts. Small thanks give ye!
010 waqāloo a-iṯa ḍalālnā fee al-arḍi a-innā lafee ḫalqin jadeedin bal hum biliqā-i rabbihim kāfiroona And they say: When we are lost in the earth, how can we then be re-created ? Nay but they are disbelievers in the meeting with their Lord.
011 qul yatawaffākum malaku almawti allāḏee wukkila bikum ṯumma ilā rabbikum turjaʿoona Say: The angel of death, who hath charge concerning you, will gather you, and afterward unto your Lord ye will be returned.
012 walaw tarā iḏi almujrimoona nākisoo ruoosihim ʿinda rabbihim rabbanā abṣarnā wasamiʿnā faarjiʿnā naʿmal saliḥan innā mooqinoona Couldst thou but see when the guilty hang their heads before their Lord, (and say): Our Lord! We have now seen and heard, so send us back; we will do right, now we are sure.
013 walaw ši-nā laātaynā kulla nafsin hudāhā walākin ḥaqqa alqawlu minnee laamlaanna jahannama mina aljinnati waalnnāsi ajmaʿeena And if We had so willed, We could have given every soul its guidance, but the word from Me concerning evildoers took effect: that I will fill hell with the jinn and mankind together.
014 faḏooqoo bimā naseetum liqāa yawmikum haṯa innā naseenākum waḏooqoo ʿaṯaba alḫuldi bimā kuntum taʿmaloona So taste (the evil of your deeds). Forasmuch as ye forgot the meeting of this your day, lo! We forget you. Taste the doom of immortality because of what ye used to do.
015 innamā yu/minu bi-āyātinā allāḏeena iṯa ḏukkiroo bihā ḫarroo sujjadan wasabbaḥoo biḥamdi rabbihim wahum lā yastakbiroona Only those believe in Our revelations who, when they are reminded of them, fall down prostrate and hymn the praise of their Lord, and they are not scornful,
016 tatajāfā junoobuhum ʿani almadajiʿi yadʿoona rabbahum ḫawfan waṭamaʿan wamimmā razaqnāhum yunfiqoona Who forsake their beds to cry unto their Lord in fear and hope, and spend of that We have bestowed on them.
017 falā taʿlamu nafsun mā oḫfiya lahum min qurrati aʿyunin jazāan bimā kānoo yaʿmaloona No soul knoweth what is kept hid for them of joy, as a reward for what they used to do.
018 afaman kāna mu/minan kaman kāna fāsiqan lā yastawoona Is he who is a believer like unto him who is an evil-liver ? They are not alike.
019 ammā allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati falahum jannātu alma/wā nuzulan bimā kānoo yaʿmaloona But as for those who believe and do good works, for them are the Gardens of Retreat - a welcome (in reward) for what they used to do.
020 waammā allāḏeena fasaqoo fama/wāhumu alnnāru kullamā arādoo an yaḫrujoo minhā oʿeedoo feehā waqeela lahum ḏooqoo ʿaṯaba alnnāri allāḏee kuntum bihi tukaṯṯiboona And as for those who do evil, their retreat is the Fire. Whenever they desire to issue forth from thence, they are brought back thither. Unto them it is said: Taste the torment of the Fire which ye used to deny.
021 walanuḏeeqannahum mina alʿaṯabi al-adnā doona alʿaṯabi al-akbari laʿallahum yarjiʿoona And verily We make them taste the lower punishment before the greater, that haply they may return.
022 waman aḏlamu mimman ḏukkira bi-āyāti rabbihi ṯumma aʿraḍa ʿanhā innā mina almujrimeena muntaqimoona And who doth greater wrong than he who is reminded of the revelations of his Lord, then turneth from them. Lo! We shall requite the guilty.
023 walaqad ātaynā moosā alkitāba falā takun fee miryatin min liqā-ihi wajaʿalnāhu hudan libanee isrā-eela We verily gave Moses the Scripture; so be not ye in doubt of his receiving it; and We appointed it a guidance for the Children of Israel.
024 wajaʿalnā minhum a-immatan yahdoona bi-amrinā lammā ṣabaroo wakānoo bi-āyātinā yooqinoona And when they became steadfast and believed firmly in Our revelations, We appointed from among them leaders who guided by Our command.
025 inna rabbaka huwa yafṣilu baynahum yawma alqiyāmati feemā kānoo feehi yaḫtalifoona Lo! thy Lord will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection concerning that wherein they used to differ.
026 awa lam yahdi lahum kam ahlaknā min qablihim mina alqurooni yamšoona fee masākinihim inna fee ṯalika laāyātin afalā yasmaʿoona Is it not a guidance for them (to observe) how many generations We destroyed before them, amid whose dwelling places they do walk ? Lo! therein verily are portents! Will they not then heed ?
027 awa lam yaraw annā nasooqu almāa ilā al-arḍi aljuruzi fanuḫriju bihi zarʿan ta-kulu minhu anʿāmuhum waanfusuhum afalā yubṣiroona Have they not seen how We lead the water to the barren land and therewith bring forth crops whereof their cattle eat, and they themselves ? Will they not then see ?
028 wayaqooloona matā haṯa alfatḥu in kuntum sadiqeena And they say: When cometh this victory (of yours) if ye are truthful ?
029 qul yawma alfatḥi lā yanfaʿu allāḏeena kafaroo eemānuhum walā hum yunḏaroona Say (unto them): On the day of the victory the faith of those who disbelieve (and who then will believe) will not avail them, neither will they be reprieved.
30 
faaʿriḍ ʿanhum waintaḏir innahum muntaḏiroona
So withdraw from them (O Muhammad), and await (the event). Lo! they (also) are awaiting (it).
