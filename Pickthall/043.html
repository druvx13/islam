43. az-Zukhruf: The Embellishment

001 [romanization missing] Ha. Mim.
002 waalkitābi almubeeni By the Scripture which maketh plain,
003 innā jaʿalnāhu qur-ānan ʿarabiyyan laʿallakum taʿqiloona Lo! We have appointed it a Lecture, in Arabic that haply ye may understand.
004 wa-innahu fee ommi alkitābi ladaynā laʿaliyyun ḥakeemun And Lo! in the Source of Decrees, which We possess, it is indeed sublime, decisive.
005 afanaḍribu ʿankumu alṯṯikra ṣafḥan an kuntum qawman musrifeena Shall We utterly ignore you because ye are a wanton folk ?
006 wakam arsalnā min nabiyyin fee al-awwaleena How many a prophet did We send among the men of old!
007 wamā ya/teehim min nabiyyin illā kānoo bihi yastahzi-oona And never came there unto them a prophet but they used to mock him.
008 faahlaknā ašadda minhum baṭšan wamada maṯalu al-awwaleena Then We destroyed men mightier than these in prowess; and the example of the men of old hath gone (before them).
009 wala-in saaltahum man ḫalaqa alssamāwāti waal-arḍa layaqoolunna ḫalaqahunna alʿazeezu alʿaleemu And if thou (Muhammad) ask them: Who created the heavens and the earth, they will surely answer: The Mighty, the Knower created them;
010 allāḏee jaʿala lakumu al-arḍa mahdan wajaʿala lakum feehā subulan laʿallakum tahtadoona Who made the earth a resting-place for you, and placed roads for you therein, that haply ye may find your way;
011 waallaḏee nazzala mina alssamā-i māan biqadarin faanšarnā bihi baldatan maytan kaṯalika tuḫrajoona And Who sendeth down water from the sky in (due) measure, and We revive a dead land therewith. Even so will ye be brought forth;
012 waallaḏee ḫalaqa al-azwāja kullahā wajaʿala lakum mina alfulki waal-anʿāmi mā tarkaboona He Who created all the pairs, and appointed for you ships and cattle whereupon ye ride.
013 litastawoo ʿalā ḏuhoorihi ṯumma taḏkuroo niʿmata rabbikum iṯa istawaytum ʿalayhi wataqooloo subhana allāḏee saḫḫara lanā haṯa wamā kunnā lahu muqrineena That ye may mount upon their backs, and may remember your Lord's favour when ye mount thereon, and may say: Glorified be He Who hath subdued these unto us, and we were not capable (of subduing them);
014 wa-innā ilā rabbinā lamunqaliboona And lo! unto our Lord we surely are returning.
015 wajaʿaloo lahu min ʿibādihi juz-an inna al-insāna lakafoorun mubeenun And they allot to Him a portion of His bondmen! Lo! man is verily a mere ingrate.
016 ami ittaḫaḏa mimmā yaḫluqu banātin waaṣfākum bialbaneena Or chooseth He daughters of all that He hath created, and honoureth He you with sons ?
017 wa-iṯa buššira aḥaduhum bimā ḍaraba lilrraḥmāni maṯalan ḏalla wajhuhu muswaddan wahuwa kaḏeemun And if one of them hath tidings of that which he likeneth to the Beneficent One, his countenance becometh black and he is full of inward rage.
018 awaman yunaššao fee alḥilyati wahuwa fee alḫisami ġayru mubeenin (Liken they then to Allah) that which is bred up in outward show, and in dispute cannot make itself plain ?
019 wajaʿaloo almalā-ikata allāḏeena hum ʿibādu alrraḥmāni ināṯan ašahidoo ḫalqahum satuktabu šahādatuhum wayus-aloona And they make the angels, who are the slaves of the Beneficent, females. Did they witness their creation ? Their testimony will be recorded and they will be questioned.
020 waqāloo law šāa alrraḥmānu mā ʿabadnāhum mā lahum biṯalika min ʿilmin in hum illā yaḫruṣoona And they say: If the Beneficent One had (so) willed, we should not have worshipped them. They have no knowledge whatsoever of that. They do but guess.
021 am ātaynāhum kitāban min qablihi fahum bihi mustamsikoona Or have We given them any scripture before (this Qur'an) so that they are holding fast thereto ?
022 bal qāloo innā wajadnā ābāanā ʿalā ommatin wa-innā ʿalā āṯārihim muhtadoona Nay, for they say only: Lo! we found our fathers following a religion, and we are guided by their footprints.
023 wakaṯalika mā arsalnā min qablika fee qaryatin min naḏeerin illā qāla mutrafoohā innā wajadnā ābāanā ʿalā ommatin wa-innā ʿalā āṯārihim muqtadoona And even so We sent not a warner before thee (Muhammad) into any township but its luxurious ones said: Lo! we found our fathers following a religion, and we are following their footprints.
024 qāla awa law ji/tukum bi-ahdā mimmā wajadtum ʿalayhi ābāakum qāloo innā bimā orsiltum bihi kāfiroona (And the warner) said: What! Even though I bring you better guidance than that ye found your fathers following ? They answered: Lo! in what ye bring we are disbelievers.
025 faintaqamnā minhum faonḏur kayfa kāna ʿāqibatu almukaṯṯibeena So We requited them. Then see the nature of the consequence for the rejecters!
026 wa-iḏ qāla ibrāheemu li-abeehi waqawmihi innanee barāon mimmā taʿbudoona And when Abraham said unto his father and his folk: Lo! I am innocent of what ye worship
027 illā allāḏee faṭaranee fa-innahu sayahdeeni Save Him Who did create me, for He will surely guide me.
028 wajaʿalahā kalimatan bāqiyatan fee ʿaqibihi laʿallahum yarjiʿoona And he made it a word enduring among his seed, that haply they might return.
029 bal mattaʿtu hāolā-i waābāahum ḥattā jāahumu alḥaqqu warasoolun mubeenun Nay, but I let these and their fathers enjoy life (only) till there should come unto them the Truth and a messenger making plain.
030 walammā jāahumu alḥaqqu qāloo haṯa siḥrun wa-innā bihi kāfiroona And now that the Truth hath come unto them they say: This is mere magic, and lo! we are disbelievers therein.
031 waqāloo lawlā nuzzila haṯa alqur-ānu ʿalā rajulin mina alqaryatayni ʿaḏeemun And they say: If only this Qur'an had been revealed to some great man of the two towns ?
032 ahum yaqsimoona raḥmata rabbika naḥnu qasamnā baynahum maʿeešatahum fee alḥayāti alddunyā warafaʿnā baʿḍahum fawqa baʿḍin darajātin liyattaḫiḏa baʿḍuhum baʿḍan suḫriyyan waraḥmatu rabbika ḫayrun mimmā yajmaʿoona Is it they who apportion thy Lord's mercy ? We have apportioned among them their livelihood in the life of the world, and raised some of them above others in rank that some of them may take labour from others; and the mercy of thy Lord is better than (the wealth) that they amass.
033 walawlā an yakoona alnnāsu ommatan wahidatan lajaʿalnā liman yakfuru bialrraḥmāni libuyootihim suqufan min fiḍatin wamaʿārija ʿalayhā yaḏharoona And were it not that mankind would have become one community, We might well have appointed, for those who disbelieve in the Beneficent, roofs of silver for their houses and stairs (of silver) whereby to mount,
034 walibuyootihim abwāban wasururan ʿalayhā yattaki-oona And for their houses doors (of silver) and couches of silver whereon to recline,
035 wazuḫrufan wa-in kullu ṯalika lammā matāʿu alḥayāti alddunya waal-āḫiratu ʿinda rabbika lilmuttaqeena And ornaments of gold. Yet all that would have been but a provision of the life of the world. And the Hereafter with your Lord would have been for those who keep from evil.
036 waman yaʿšu ʿan ḏikri alrraḥmāni nuqayyiḍ lahu šaytanan fahuwa lahu qareenun And he whose sight is dim to the remembrance of the Beneficent, We assign unto him a devil who becometh his comrade;
037 wa-innahum layaṣuddoonahum ʿani alssabeeli wayaḥsaboona annahum muhtadoona And lo! they surely turn them from the way of Allah, and yet they deem that they are rightly guided;
038 [romanization missing] Till, when he cometh unto Us, he saith (unto his comrade): Ah, would that between me and thee there were the distance of the two horizons - an evil comrade!
039 walan yanfaʿakumu alyawma iḏ ḏalamtum annakum fee alʿaṯabi muštarikoona And it profiteth you not this day, because ye did wrong, that ye will be sharers in the doom.
040 afaanta tusmiʿu alṣumma aw tahdee alʿumya waman kāna fee ḍalālin mubeenin Canst thou (Muhammad) make the deaf to hear, or canst thou guide the blind or him who is in error manifest ?
041 fa-immā naḏhabanna bika fa-innā minhum muntaqimoona And if We take thee away, We surely shall take vengeance on them,
042 aw nuriyannaka allāḏee waʿadnāhum fa-innā ʿalayhim muqtadiroona Or (if) We show thee that wherewith We threaten them; for lo! We have complete command of them.
043 faistamsik biallaḏee ooḥiya ilayka innaka ʿalā ṣiratin mustaqeemin So hold thou fast to that which is inspired in thee. Lo! thou art on a right path.
044 wa-innahu laḏikrun laka waliqawmika wasawfa tus-aloona And lo! it is in truth a Reminder for thee and for thy folk; and ye will be questioned.
045 wais-al man arsalnā min qablika min rusulinā ajaʿalnā min dooni alrraḥmāni ālihatan yuʿbadoona And ask those of Our messengers whom We sent before thee: Did We ever appoint gods to be worshipped beside the Beneficent ?
046 walaqad arsalnā moosā bi-āyātinā ilā firʿawna wamala-ihi faqāla innee rasoolu rabbi alʿālameena And verily We sent Moses with Our revelations unto Pharaoh and his chiefs, and he said: I am a messenger of the Lord of the Worlds.
047 falammā jāahum bi-āyātinā iṯa hum minhā yadhakoona But when he brought them Our tokens, behold! they laughed at them.
048 wamā nureehim min āyatin illā hiya akbaru min oḫtihā waaḫaḏnāhum bialʿaṯabi laʿallahum yarjiʿoona And every token that We showed them was greater than its sister (token), and We grasped them with the torment, that haply they might turn again.
049 waqāloo yā ayyuhā alsahiru odʿu lanā rabbaka bimā ʿahida ʿindaka innanā lamuhtadoona And they said: O wizard! Entreat thy Lord for us by the pact that He hath made with thee. Lo! we verily will walk aright.
050 falammā kašafnā ʿanhumu alʿaṯaba iṯa hum yankuṯoona But when We eased them of the torment, behold! they broke their word.
051 wanādā firʿawnu fee qawmihi qāla yā qawmi alaysa lee mulku miṣra wahaṯihi al-anhāru tajree min taḥtee afalā tubṣiroona And Pharaoh caused a proclamation to be made among his people saying: O my people! Is not mine the sovereignty of Egypt and these rivers flowing under me ? Can ye not then discern ?
052 am anā ḫayrun min haṯa allāḏee huwa maheenun walā yakādu yubeenu I am surely better than this fellow, who is despicable and can hardly make (his meaning) plain!
053 falawlā olqiya ʿalayhi aswiratun min ḏahabin aw jāa maʿahu almalā-ikatu muqtarineena Why, then, have armlets of gold not been set upon him, or angels sent along with him ?
054 faistaḫaffa qawmahu faataʿoohu innahum kānoo qawman fāsiqeena Thus he persuaded his people to make light (of Moses), and they obeyed him. Lo! they were a wanton folk.
055 falammā āsafoonā intaqamnā minhum faaġraqnāhum ajmaʿeena So, when they angered Us, We punished them and drowned them every one.
056 fajaʿalnāhum salafan wamaṯalan lil-āḫireena And We made them a thing past, and an example for those after (them).
057 walammā ḍuriba ibnu maryama maṯalan iṯa qawmuka minhu yaṣiddoona And when the son of Mary is quoted as an example, behold! the folk laugh out,
058 waqāloo aālihatunā ḫayrun am huwa mā ḍaraboohu laka illā jadalan bal hum qawmun ḫaṣimoona And say: Are our gods better, or is he ? They raise not the objection save for argument. Nay! but they are a contentious folk.
059 in huwa illā ʿabdun anʿamnā ʿalayhi wajaʿalnāhu maṯalan libanee isrā-eela He is nothing but a slave on whom We bestowed favour, and We made him a pattern for the Children of Israel.
060 walaw našāo lajaʿalnā minkum malā-ikatan fee al-arḍi yaḫlufoona And had We willed We could have set among you angels to be viceroys in the earth.
061 wa-innahu laʿilmun lilssāʿati falā tamtarunna bihā waittabiʿooni haṯa ṣiratun mustaqeemun And lo! verily there is knowledge of the Hour. So doubt ye not concerning it, but follow Me. This is the right path.
062 walā yaṣuddannakumu alššaytanu innahu lakum ʿaduwwun mubeenun And let not Satan turn you aside. Lo! he is an open enemy for you.
063 walammā jāa ʿeesā bialbayyināti qāla qad ji/tukum bialḥikmati wali-obayyina lakum baʿḍa allāḏee taḫtalifoona feehi faittaqoo allāha waaṭeeʿooni When Jesus came with clear proofs (of Allah's Sovereignty), he said: I have come unto you with wisdom, and to make plain some of that concerning which ye differ. So keep your duty to Allah, and obey me.
064 inna allāha huwa rabbee warabbukum faoʿbudoohu haṯa ṣiratun mustaqeemun Lo! Allah, He is my Lord and your Lord. So worship Him. This is a right path.
065 faiḫtalafa al-aḥzābu min baynihim fawaylun lillaḏeena ḏalamoo min ʿaṯabi yawmin aleemin But the factions among them differed. Then woe unto those who do wrong from the doom of a painful day.
066 hal yanḏuroona illā alssāʿata an ta/tiyahum baġtatan wahum lā yašʿuroona Await they aught save the Hour, that it shall come upon them suddenly, when they know not ?
067 al-aḫillāo yawma-iḏin baʿḍuhum libaʿḍin ʿaduwwun illā almuttaqeena Friends on that day will be foes one to another, save those who kept their duty (to Allah).
068 [romanization missing] O My slaves! For you there is no fear this day, nor is it ye who grieve;
069 [romanization missing] (Ye) who believed Our revelations and were self-surrendered,
070 [romanization missing] Enter the Garden, ye and your wives, to be made glad.
071 [romanization missing] Therein are brought round for them trays of gold and goblets, and therein is all that souls desire and eyes find sweet. And ye are immortal therein.
072 [romanization missing] This is the Garden which ye are made to inherit because of what ye used to do.
073 [romanization missing] Therein for you is fruit in plenty whence to eat.
074 [romanization missing] Lo! the guilty are immortal in hell's torment.
075 [romanization missing] It is not relaxed for them, and they despair therein.
076 [romanization missing] We wronged them not, but they it was who did the wrong.
077 [romanization missing] And they cry: O master! Let thy Lord make an end of us. He saith: Lo! here ye must remain.
078 [romanization missing] We verily brought the Truth unto you, but ye were, most of you, averse to the Truth.
079 [romanization missing] Or do they determine any thing (against the Prophet) ? Lo! We (also) are determining.
080 [romanization missing] Or deem they that We cannot hear their secret thoughts and private confidences ? Nay, but Our envoys, present with them, do record.
081 [romanization missing] Say (O Muhammad): If the Beneficent One hath a son, then, I shall be first among the worshippers. (But there is no son).
082 [romanization missing] Glorified be the Lord of the heavens and the earth, the Lord of the Throne, from that which they ascribe (unto Him)!
083 [romanization missing] So let them flounder (in their talk) and play until they meet the Day which they are promised.
084 [romanization missing] And He it is Who in the heaven is God, and in the earth God. He is the Wise, the Knower.
085 [romanization missing] And blessed be He unto Whom belongeth the Sovereignty of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them, and with Whom is knowledge of the Hour, and unto Whom ye will be returned.
086 [romanization missing] And those unto whom they cry instead of Him possess no power of intercession, saving him who beareth witness unto the Truth knowingly.
