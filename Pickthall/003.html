3. Al-Imran: The Family Of Imran

001 alif-lām-meem Alim. Lam. Mim.
002 allāhu lā ilāha illā huwa alḥayyu alqayyoomu Allah! There is no God save Him, the Alive, the Eternal.
003 nazzala ʿalayka alkitāba bialḥaqqi muṣaddiqan limā bayna yadayhi waanzala alttawrāta waal-injeela He hath revealed unto thee (Muhammad) the Scripture with truth, confirming that which was (revealed) before it, even as He revealed the Torah and the Gospel.
004 min qablu hudan lilnnāsi waanzala alfurqāna inna allāḏeena kafaroo bi-āyāti allāhi lahum ʿaṯabun šadeedun waallāhu ʿazeezun ḏoo intiqāmin Aforetime, for a guidance to mankind; and hath revealed the Criterion (of right and wrong). Lo! those who disbelieve the revelations of Allah, theirs will be a heavy doom. Allah is Mighty, Able to Requite (the wrong).
005 inna allāha lā yaḫfā ʿalayhi šay-on fee al-arḍi walā fee alssamā/-i Lo! nothing in the earth or in the heavens is hidden from Allah.
006 huwa allāḏee yuṣawwirukum fee al-arhami kayfa yašāo lā ilāha illā huwa alʿazeezu alḥakeemu He it is Who fashioneth you in the wombs as pleaseth Him. There is no God save Him, the Almighty, the Wise.
007 huwa allāḏee anzala ʿalayka alkitāba minhu āyātun muḥkamātun hunna ommu alkitābi waoḫaru mutašābihātun faammā allāḏeena fee quloobihim zayġun fayattabiʿoona mā tašābaha minhu ibtiġāa alfitnati waibtiġāa ta/weelihi wamā yaʿlamu ta/weelahu illā allāhu waalrrāsiḫoona fee alʿilmi yaqooloona āmannā bihi kullun min ʿindi rabbinā wamā yaṯṯakkaru illā oloo al-albābi He it is Who hath revealed unto thee (Muhammad) the Scripture wherein are clear revelations - they are the substance of the Book - and others (which are) allegorical. But those in whose hearts is doubt pursue, forsooth, that which is allegorical seeking (to cause) dissension by seeking to explain it. None knoweth its explanation save Allah. And those who are of sound instruction say: We believe therein; the whole is from our Lord; but only men of understanding really heed.
008 rabbanā lā tuziġ quloobanā baʿda iḏ hadaytanā wahab lanā min ladunka raḥmatan innaka anta alwahhābu Our Lord! Cause not our hearts to stray after Thou hast guided us, and bestow upon us mercy from Thy Presence. Lo! Thou, only Thou, art the Bestower.
009 rabbanā innaka jāmiʿu alnnāsi liyawmin lā rayba feehi inna allāha lā yuḫlifu almeeʿāda Our Lord! Lo! it is Thou Who gatherest mankind together to a Day of which there is no doubt. Lo! Allah faileth not to keep the tryst.
010 inna allāḏeena kafaroo lan tuġniya ʿanhum amwāluhum walā awlāduhum mina allāhi šay-an waolā-ika hum waqoodu alnnāri (On that Day) neither the riches nor the progeny of those who disbelieve will aught avail them with Allah. They will be fuel for Fire.
011 kada/bi āli firʿawna waallaḏeena min qablihim kaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā faaḫaḏahumu allāhu biḏunoobihim waallāhu šadeedu alʿiqābi Like Pharaoh's folk and those who were before them, they disbelieved Our revelations and so Allah seized them for their sins. And Allah is severe in punishment.
012 qul lillaḏeena kafaroo satuġlaboona watuḥšaroona ilā jahannama wabi/sa almihādu Say (O Muhammad) unto those who disbelieve: Ye shall be overcome and gathered unto Hell, an evil resting-place.
013 qad kāna lakum āyatun fee fi-atayni iltaqatā fi-atun tuqātilu fee sabeeli allāhi waoḫrā kāfiratun yarawnahum miṯlayhim ra/ya alʿayni waallāhu yu-ayyidu binaṣrihi man yašāo inna fee ṯalika laʿibratan li-olee al-absari There was a token for you in two hosts which met: one army fighting in the way of Allah, and another disbelieving, whom they saw as twice their number, clearly, with their very eyes. Thus Allah strengtheneth with His succour whom He will. Lo! herein verily is a lesson for those who have eyes.
014 zuyyina lilnnāsi ḥubbu alššahawāti mina alnnisā-i waalbaneena waalqanateeri almuqanṭarati mina alṯṯahabi waalfiḍati waalḫayli almusawwamati waal-anʿāmi waalḥarṯi ṯalika matāʿu alḥayāti alddunyā waallāhu ʿindahu ḥusnu almaābi Beautified for mankind is love of the joys (that come) from women and offspring; and stored-up heaps of gold and silver, and horses branded (with their mark), and cattle and land. That is comfort of the life of the world. Allah! With Him is a more excellent abode.
015 qul aonabbi-okum biḫayrin min ṯalikum lillaḏeena ittaqaw ʿinda rabbihim jannātun tajree min taḥtihā al-anhāru ḫālideena feehā waazwājun muṭahharatun wariḍwānun mina allāhi waallāhu baṣeerun bialʿibādi Say: Shall I inform you of something better than that ? For those who keep from evil, with their Lord, are Gardens underneath which rivers flow wherein they will abide, and pure companions, and contentment from Allah. Allah is Seer of His bondmen,
016 allāḏeena yaqooloona rabbanā innanā āmannā faiġfir lanā ḏunoobanā waqinā ʿaṯaba alnnāri Those who say: Our Lord! Lo! we believe. So forgive us our sins and guard us from the punishment of Fire;
017 alssabireena waalssadiqeena waalqāniteena waalmunfiqeena waalmustaġfireena bial-ashari The steadfast, and the truthful, and the obedient, those who spend (and hoard not), those who pray for pardon in the watches of the night.
018 shahida allāhu annahu lā ilāha illā huwa waalmalā-ikatu waoloo alʿilmi qā-iman bialqisṭi lā ilāha illā huwa alʿazeezu alḥakeemu Allah (Himself) is Witness that there is no God save Him. And the angels and the men of learning (too are witness). Maintaining His creation in justice, there is no God save Him the Almighty, the Wise.
019 inna alddeena ʿinda allāhi al-islāmu wamā iḫtalafa allāḏeena ootoo alkitāba illā min baʿdi mā jāahumu alʿilmu baġyan baynahum waman yakfur bi-āyāti allāhi fa-inna allāha sareeʿu alḥisābi Lo! religion with Allah (is) the Surrender (to His Will and Guidance). Those who (formerly) received the Scripture differed only after knowledge came unto them, through transgression among themselves. Whoso disbelieveth the revelations of Allah (will find that) lo! Allah is swift at reckoning.
020 fa-in hajjooka faqul aslamtu wajhiya lillāhi wamani ittabaʿani waqul lillaḏeena ootoo alkitāba waal-ommiyyeena aaslamtum fa-in aslamoo faqadi ihtadaw wa-in tawallaw fa-innamā ʿalayka albalāġu waallāhu baṣeerun bialʿibādi And if they argue with thee, (O Muhammad), say: I have surrendered my purpose to Allah and (so have) those who follow me. And say unto those who have received the Scripture and those who read not: Have ye (too) surrendered ? If they surrender, then truly they are rightly guided, and if they turn away, then it is thy duty only to convey the message (unto them). Allah is Seer of (His) bondmen.
021 inna allāḏeena yakfuroona bi-āyāti allāhi wayaqtuloona alnnabiyyeena biġayri ḥaqqin wayaqtuloona allāḏeena ya/muroona bialqisṭi mina alnnāsi fabašširhum biʿaṯabin aleemin Lo! those who disbelieve the revelations of Allah, and slay the prophets wrongfully, and slay those of mankind who enjoin equity: promise them a painful doom.
022 olā-ika allāḏeena ḥabiṭat aʿmāluhum fee alddunyā waal-āḫirati wamā lahum min nasireena Those are they whose works have failed in the world and the Hereafter; and they have no helpers.
023 alam tara ilā allāḏeena ootoo naṣeeban mina alkitābi yudʿawna ilā kitābi allāhi liyaḥkuma baynahum ṯumma yatawallā fareequn minhum wahum muʿriḍoona Hast thou not seen how those who have received a portion of the Scripture invoke the Scripture of Allah (in their disputes) that it may judge between them; then a faction of them turn away, being opposed (to it) ?
024 [romanization missing] That is because they say: The Fire will not touch us save for a certain number of days. That which they used to invent hath deceived them regarding their religion.
025 fakayfa iṯa jamaʿnāhum liyawmin lā rayba feehi wawuffiyat kullu nafsin mā kasabat wahum lā yuḏlamoona How (will it be with them) when We have brought them all together to a Day of which there is no doubt, when every soul will be paid in full what it hath earned, and they will not be wronged.
026 quli allāhumma mālika almulki tu/tee almulka man tašāo watanziʿu almulka mimman tašāo watuʿizzu man tašāo watuḏillu man tašāo biyadika alḫayru innaka ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerun Say: O Allah! Owner of Sovereignty! Thou givest sovereignty unto whom Thou wilt, and Thou withdrawest sovereignty from whom Thou wilt. Thou exaltest whom Thou wilt, and Thou abasest whom Thou wilt. In Thy hand is the good. Lo! Thou art Able to do all things.
027 tooliju allāyla fee alnnahāri watooliju alnnahāra fee allāyli watuḫriju alḥayya mina almayyiti watuḫriju almayyita mina alḥayyi watarzuqu man tašāo biġayri ḥisābin Thou causest the night to pass into the day, and Thou causest the day to pass into the night. And Thou bringest forth the living from the dead, and Thou bringest forth the dead from the living. And Thou givest sustenance to whom Thou choosest, without stint.
028 lā yattaḫiḏi almu/minoona alkāfireena awliyāa min dooni almu/mineena waman yafʿal ṯalika falaysa mina allāhi fee šay-in illā an tattaqoo minhum tuqātan wayuḥaṯṯirukumu allāhu nafsahu wa-ilā allāhi almaṣeeru Let not the believers take disbelievers for their friends in preference to believers. Whoso doeth that hath no connection with Allah unless (it be) that ye but guard yourselves against them, taking (as it were) security. Allah biddeth you beware (only) of Himself. Unto Allah is the journeying.
029 qul in tuḫfoo mā fee ṣudoorikum aw tubdoohu yaʿlamhu allāhu wayaʿlamu mā fee alssamāwāti wamā fee al-arḍi waallāhu ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerun Say, (O Muhammad): Whether ye hide that which is in your breasts or reveal it, Allah knoweth it. He knoweth that which is in the heavens and that which is in the earth, and Allah is Able to do all things.
030 yawma tajidu kullu nafsin mā ʿamilat min ḫayrin muhdaran wamā ʿamilat min soo-in tawaddu law anna baynahā wabaynahu amadan baʿeedan wayuḥaṯṯirukumu allāhu nafsahu waallāhu raoofun bialʿibādi On the Day when every soul will find itself confronted with all that it hath done of good and all that it hath done of evil (every soul) will long that there might be a mighty space of distance between it and that (evil). Allah biddeth you beware of Him. And Allah is Full of Pity for (His) bondmen.
031 qul in kuntum tuḥibboona allāha faittabiʿoonee yuḥbibkumu allāhu wayaġfir lakum ḏunoobakum waallāhu ġafoorun raḥeemun Say, (O Muhammad, to mankind): If ye love Allah, follow me; Allah will love you and forgive you your sins. Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.
032 qul aṭeeʿoo allāha waalrrasoola fa-in tawallaw fa-inna allāha lā yuḥibbu alkāfireena Say: Obey Allah and the messenger. But if they turn away, lo! Allah loveth not the disbelievers (in His guidance).
033 inna allāha istafā ādama wanooḥan waāla ibrāheema waāla ʿimrāna ʿalā alʿālameena Lo! Allah preferred Adam and Noah and the Family of Abraham and the Family of 'Imran above (all His) creatures.
034 [romanization missing] They were descendants one of another. Allah is Hearer, Knower.
035 iḏ qālati imraatu ʿimrāna rabbi innee naḏartu laka mā fee baṭnee muḥarraran fataqabbal minnee innaka anta alssameeʿu alʿaleemu (Remember) when the wife of 'Imran said: My Lord! I have vowed unto Thee that which is in my belly as a consecrated (offering). Accept it from me. Lo! Thou, only Thou, art the Hearer, the Knower!
036 falammā waḍaʿat-hā qālat rabbi innee waḍaʿtuhā onṯā waallāhu aʿlamu bimā waḍaʿat walaysa alṯṯakaru kaalonṯā wa-innee sammaytuhā maryama wa-innee oʿeeḏuhā bika waḏurriyyatahā mina alššaytani alrrajeemi And when she was delivered she said: My Lord! Lo! I am delivered of a female - Allah knew best of what she was delivered - the male is not as the female; and lo! I have named her Mary, and lo! I crave Thy protection for her and for her offspring from Satan the outcast.
037 fataqabbalahā rabbuhā biqaboolin ḥasanin waanbatahā nabātan ḥasanan wakaffalahā zakariyyā kullamā daḫala ʿalayhā zakariyyā almiḥrāba wajada ʿindahā rizqan qāla yā maryamu annā laki haṯa qālat huwa min ʿindi allāhi inna allāha yarzuqu man yašāo biġayri ḥisābin And her Lord accepted her with full acceptance and vouchsafed to her a goodly growth; and made Zachariah her guardian. Whenever Zachariah went into the sanctuary where she was, he found that she had food. He said: O Mary! Whence cometh unto thee this (food) ? She answered: It is from Allah. Allah giveth without stint to whom He will.
038 hunālika daʿā zakariyyā rabbahu qāla rabbi hab lee min ladunka ḏurriyyatan ṭayyibatan innaka sameeʿu aldduʿā/-i Then Zachariah prayed unto his Lord and said: My Lord! Bestow upon me of Thy bounty goodly offspring. Lo! Thou art the Hearer of Prayer.
039 fanādat-hu almalā-ikatu wahuwa qā-imun yuṣallee fee almiḥrābi anna allāha yubašširuka biyaḥyā muṣaddiqan bikalimatin mina allāhi wasayyidan waḥaṣooran wanabiyyan mina alssaliḥeena And the angels called to him as he stood praying in the sanctuary: Allah giveth thee glad tidings of (a son whose name is) John, (who cometh) to confirm a word from Allah lordly, chaste, a prophet of the righteous.
040 qāla rabbi annā yakoonu lee ġulāmun waqad balaġaniya alkibaru waimraatee ʿāqirun qāla kaṯalika allāhu yafʿalu mā yašā/o He said: My Lord! How can I have a son when age hath overtaken me already and my wife is barren ? (The angel) answered: So (it will be). Allah doeth what He will.
041 qāla rabbi ijʿal lee āyatan qāla āyatuka allā tukallima alnnāsa ṯalāṯata ayyāmin illā ramzan waoḏkur rabbaka kaṯeeran wasabbiḥ bialʿašiyyi waal-ibkāri He said: My Lord! Appoint a token for me. (The angel) said: The token unto thee (shall be) that thou shalt not speak unto mankind three days except by signs. Remember thy Lord much, and praise (Him) in the early hours of night and morning.
042 wa-iḏ qālati almalā-ikatu yā maryamu inna allāha istafāki waṭahharaki waistafāki ʿalā nisā-i alʿālameena And when the angels said: O Mary! Lo! Allah hath chosen thee and made thee pure, and hath preferred thee above (all) the women of creation.
043 yā maryamu oqnutee lirabbiki waosjudee wairkaʿee maʿa alrrākiʿeena O Mary! Be obedient to thy Lord, prostrate thyself and bow with those who bow (in worship).
044 [romanization missing] This is of the tidings of things hidden. We reveal it unto thee (Muhammad). Thou wast not present with them when they threw their pens (to know) which of them should be the guardian of Mary, nor wast thou present with them when they quarrelled (thereupon).
045 iḏ qālati almalā-ikatu yā maryamu inna allāha yubašširuki bikalimatin minhu ismuhu almaseeḥu ʿeesā ibnu maryama wajeehan fee alddunyā waal-āḫirati wamina almuqarrabeena (And remember) when the angels said: O Mary! Lo! Allah giveth thee glad tidings of a word from him, whose name is the Messiah, Jesus, son of Mary, illustrious in the world and the Hereafter, and one of those brought near (unto Allah).
046 wayukallimu alnnāsa fee almahdi wakahlan wamina alssaliḥeena He will speak unto mankind in his cradle and in his manhood, and he is of the righteous.
047 qālat rabbi annā yakoonu lee waladun walam yamsasnee bašarun qāla kaṯaliki allāhu yaḫluqu mā yašāo iṯa qada amran fa-innamā yaqoolu lahu kun fayakoonu She said: My Lord! How can I have a child when no mortal hath touched me ? He said: So (it will be). Allah createth what He will. If He decreeth a thing, He saith unto it only: Be! and it is.
048 wayuʿallimuhu alkitāba waalḥikmata waalttawrāta waal-injeela And He will teach him the Scripture and wisdom, and the Torah and the Gospel,
049 warasoolan ilā banee isrā-eela annee qad ji/tukum bi-āyatin min rabbikum annee aḫluqu lakum mina altteeni kahay-ati alttayri faanfuḫu feehi fayakoonu ṭayran bi-iḏni allāhi waobri-o al-akmaha waal-abraṣa waoḥyee almawtā bi-iḏni allāhi waonabbi-okum bimā ta/kuloona wamā taddaḫiroona fee buyootikum inna fee ṯalika laāyatan lakum in kuntum mu/mineena And will make him a messenger unto the Children of Israel, (saying): Lo! I come unto you with a sign from your Lord. Lo! I fashion for you out of clay the likeness of a bird, and I breathe into it and it is a bird, by Allah's leave. I heal him who was born blind, and the leper, and I raise the dead, by Allah's leave. And I announce unto you what ye eat and what ye store up in your houses. Lo! herein verily is a portent for you, if ye are to be believers.
050 wamuṣaddiqan limā bayna yadayya mina alttawrāti wali-oḥilla lakum baʿḍa allāḏee ḥurrima ʿalaykum waji/tukum bi-āyatin min rabbikum faittaqoo allāha waaṭeeʿooni And (I come) confirming that which was before me of the Torah, and to make lawful some of that which was forbidden unto you. I come unto you with a sign from your Lord, so keep your duty to Allah and obey me.
051 inna allāha rabbee warabbukum faoʿbudoohu haṯa ṣiratun mustaqeemun Lo! Allah is my Lord and your Lord, so worship Him. That is a straight path.
052 falammā aḥassa ʿeesā minhumu alkufra qāla man ansaree ilā allāhi qāla alḥawāriyyoona naḥnu ansaru allāhi āmannā biallāhi waišhad bi-annā muslimoona But when Jesus became conscious of their disbelief, he cried: Who will be my helpers in the cause of Allah ? The disciples said: We will be Allah's helpers. We believe in Allah, and bear thou witness that we have surrendered (unto Him).
053 rabbanā āmannā bimā anzalta waittabaʿnā alrrasoola faoktubnā maʿa alššāhideena Our Lord! We believe in that which Thou hast revealed and we follow him whom Thou hast sent. Enrol us among those who witness (to the truth).
054 wamakaroo wamakara allāhu waallāhu ḫayru almākireena And they (the disbelievers) schemed, and Allah schemed (against them): and Allah is the best of schemers.
055 iḏ qāla allāhu yā ʿeesā innee mutawaffeeka warāfiʿuka ilayya wamuṭahhiruka mina allāḏeena kafaroo wajāʿilu allāḏeena ittabaʿooka fawqa allāḏeena kafaroo ilā yawmi alqiyāmati ṯumma ilayya marjiʿukum faaḥkumu baynakum feemā kuntum feehi taḫtalifoona (And remember) when Allah said: O Jesus! Lo! I am gathering thee and causing thee to ascend unto Me, and am cleansing thee of those who disbelieve and am setting those who follow thee above those who disbelieve until the Day of Resurrection. Then unto Me ye will (all) return, and I shall judge between you as to that wherein ye used to differ.
056 faammā allāḏeena kafaroo faoʿaṯṯibuhum ʿaṯaban šadeedan fee alddunyā waal-āḫirati wamā lahum min nasireena As for those who disbelieve I shall chastise them with a heavy chastisement in the world and the Hereafter; and they will have no helpers.
057 waammā allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati fayuwaffeehim ojoorahum waallāhu lā yuḥibbu alṯṯālimeena And as for those who believe and do good works, He will pay them their wages in full. Allah loveth not wrong-doers.
058 [romanization missing] This (which) We recite unto thee is a revelation and a wise reminder.
059 inna maṯala ʿeesā ʿinda allāhi kamaṯali ādama ḫalaqahu min turābin ṯumma qāla lahu kun fayakoonu Lo! the likeness of Jesus with Allah is as the likeness of Adam. He created him of dust, then He said unto him: Be! and he is.
060 alḥaqqu min rabbika falā takun mina almumtareena (This is) the truth from thy Lord (O Muhammad), so be not thou of those who waver.
061 faman hajjaka feehi min baʿdi mā jāaka mina alʿilmi faqul taʿālaw nadʿu abnāanā waabnāakum wanisāanā wanisāakum waanfusanā waanfusakum ṯumma nabtahil fanajʿal laʿnata allāhi ʿalā alkaṯibeena And whoso disputeth with thee concerning him, after the knowledge which hath come unto thee, say (unto him): Come! We will summon our sons and your sons, and our women and your women, and ourselves and yourselves, then we will pray humbly (to our Lord) and (solemnly) invoke the curse of Allah upon those who lie.
062 inna haṯa lahuwa alqaṣaṣu alḥaqqu wamā min ilāhin illā allāhu wa-inna allāha lahuwa alʿazeezu alḥakeemu Lo! This verily is the true narrative. There is no God save Allah, and lo! Allah, He verily is, is the Mighty, the Wise.
063 fa-in tawallaw fa-inna allāha ʿaleemun bialmufsideena And if they turn away, then lo! Allah is Aware of (who are) the corrupters.
064 qul yā ahla alkitābi taʿālaw ilā kalimatin sawā-in baynanā wabaynakum allā naʿbuda illā allāha walā nušrika bihi šay-an walā yattaḫiḏa baʿḍunā baʿḍan arbāban min dooni allāhi fa-in tawallaw faqooloo išhadoo bi-annā muslimoona Say: O People of the Scripture! Come to an agreement between us and you: that we shall worship none but Allah, and that we shall ascribe no partner unto Him, and that none of us shall take others for lords beside Allah. And if they turn away, then say: Bear witness that we are they who have surrendered (unto Him).
065 yā ahla alkitābi lima tuhajjoona fee ibrāheema wamā onzilati alttawrātu waal-injeelu illā min baʿdihi afalā taʿqiloona O People of the Scripture! Why will ye argue about Abraham, when the Torah and the Gospel were not revealed till after him ? Have ye then no sense ?
066 hā antum hāolā-i hajajtum feemā lakum bihi ʿilmun falima tuhajjoona feemā laysa lakum bihi ʿilmun waallāhu yaʿlamu waantum lā taʿlamoona Lo! ye are those who argue about that whereof ye have some knowledge: Why then argue ye concerning that whereof ye have no knowledge ? Allah knoweth. Ye know not.
067 mā kāna ibrāheemu yahoodiyyan walā naṣrāniyyan walākin kāna ḥaneefan musliman wamā kāna mina almušrikeena Abraham was not a Jew, nor yet a Christian; but he was an upright man who had surrendered (to Allah), and he was not of the idolaters.
068 inna awlā alnnāsi bi-ibrāheema lallaḏeena ittabaʿoohu wahaṯa alnnabiyyu waallaḏeena āmanoo waallāhu waliyyu almu/mineena Lo! those of mankind who have the best claim to Abraham are those who followed him, and this Prophet and those who believe (with him); and Allah is the Protecting Guardian of the believers.
069 waddat ta-ifatun min ahli alkitābi law yuḍilloonakum wamā yuḍilloona illā anfusahum wamā yašʿuroona A party of the People of the Scripture long to make you go astray; and they make none to go astray except themselves, but they perceive not.
070 yā ahla alkitābi lima takfuroona bi-āyāti allāhi waantum tašhadoona O People of the Scripture! Why disbelieve ye in the revelations of Allah, when ye (yourselves) bear witness (to their truth) ?
071 yā ahla alkitābi lima talbisoona alḥaqqa bialbatili wataktumoona alḥaqqa waantum taʿlamoona O People of the Scripture! Why confound ye truth with falsehood and knowingly conceal the truth ?

078 wa-inna minhum lafareeqan yalwoona alsinatahum bialkitābi litaḥsaboohu mina alkitābi wamā huwa mina alkitābi wayaqooloona huwa min ʿindi allāhi wamā huwa min ʿindi allāhi wayaqooloona ʿalā allāhi alkaḏiba wahum yaʿlamoona And lo! there is a party of them who distort the Scripture with their tongues, that ye may think that what they say is from the Scripture, when it is not from the Scripture. And they say: It is from Allah, when it is not from Allah; and they speak a lie concerning Allah knowingly.
079 mā kāna libašarin an yu/tiyahu allāhu alkitāba waalḥukma waalnnubuwwata ṯumma yaqoola lilnnāsi koonoo ʿibādan lee min dooni allāhi walākin koonoo rabbāniyyeena bimā kuntum tuʿallimoona alkitāba wabimā kuntum tadrusoona It is not (possible) for any human being unto whom Allah had given the Scripture and wisdom and the prophethood that he should afterwards have said unto mankind: Be slaves of me instead of Allah; but (what he said was): Be ye faithful servants of the Lord by virtue of your constant teaching of the Scripture and of your constant study thereof.
080 walā ya/murakum an tattaḫiḏoo almalā-ikata waalnnabiyyeena arbāban aya/murukum bialkufri baʿda iḏ antum muslimoona And he commanded you not that ye should take the angels and the prophets for lords. Would he command you to disbelieve after ye had surrendered (to Allah) ?

And afterward there will come unto you a messenger, confirming that which ye possess. Ye shall believe in him and ye shall help him. He said: Do ye agree, and will ye take up My burden (which I lay upon you) in this (matter) ? They answered: We agree. He said: Then bear ye witness. I will be a witness with you.
082 faman tawallā baʿda ṯalika faolā-ika humu alfāsiqoona Then whosoever after this shall turn away: they will be miscreants.
083 afaġayra deeni allāhi yabġoona walahu aslama man fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi ṭawʿan wakarhan wa-ilayhi yurjaʿoona Seek they other than the religion of Allah, when unto Him submitteth whosoever is in the heavens and the earth, willingly or unwillingly, and unto Him they will be returned.
084 qul āmannā biallāhi wamā onzila ʿalaynā wamā onzila ʿalā ibrāheema wa-ismāʿeela wa-ishaqa wayaʿqooba waal-asbati wamā ootiya moosā waʿeesā waalnnabiyyoona min rabbihim lā nufarriqu bayna aḥadin minhum wanaḥnu lahu muslimoona Say (O Muhammad): We believe in Allah and that which is revealed unto us and that which was revealed unto Abraham and Ishmael and Isaac and Jacob and the tribes, and that which was vouchsafed unto Moses and Jesus and the prophets from their Lord. We make no distinction between any of them, and unto Him we have surrendered.
085 waman yabtaġi ġayra al-islāmi deenan falan yuqbala minhu wahuwa fee al-āḫirati mina alḫāsireena And whoso seeketh as religion other than the Surrender (to Allah) it will not be accepted from him, and he will be a loser in the Hereafter.
086 kayfa yahdee allāhu qawman kafaroo baʿda eemānihim wašahidoo anna alrrasoola ḥaqqun wajāahumu albayyinātu waallāhu lā yahdee alqawma alṯṯālimeena How shall Allah guide a people who disbelieved after their belief and (after) they bore witness that the messenger is true and after clear proofs (of Allah's Sovereignty) had come unto them. And Allah guideth not wrongdoing folk.
087 olā-ika jazāohum anna ʿalayhim laʿnata allāhi waalmalā-ikati waalnnāsi ajmaʿeena As for such, their guerdon is that on them rests the curse of Allah and of angels and of men combined.
088 khālideena feehā lā yuḫaffafu ʿanhumu alʿaṯabu walā hum yunḏaroona They will abide therein. Their doom will not be lightened, neither will they be reprieved;
089 illā allāḏeena tāboo min baʿdi ṯalika waaṣlaḥoo fa-inna allāha ġafoorun raḥeemun Save those who afterward repent and do right. Lo! Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.
090 inna allāḏeena kafaroo baʿda eemānihim ṯumma izdādoo kufran lan tuqbala tawbatuhum waolā-ika humu alddalloona Lo! those who disbelieve after their (profession of) belief, and afterward grow violent in disbelief: their repentance will not be accepted. And such are those who are astray.
091 inna allāḏeena kafaroo wamātoo wahum kuffārun falan yuqbala min aḥadihim milo al-arḍi ḏahaban walawi iftadā bihi olā-ika lahum ʿaṯabun aleemun wamā lahum min nasireena Lo! those who disbelieve, and die in disbelief, the (whole) earth full of gold would not be accepted from such an one if it were offered as a ransom (for his soul). Theirs will be a painful doom and they will have no helpers.
092 lan tanāloo albirra ḥattā tunfiqoo mimmā tuḥibboona wamā tunfiqoo min šay-in fa-inna allāha bihi ʿaleemun Ye will not attain unto piety until ye spend of that which ye love. And whatsoever ye spend, Allah is Aware thereof.
093 kullu alttaʿāmi kāna ḥillan libanee isrā-eela illā mā ḥarrama isrā-eelu ʿalā nafsihi min qabli an tunazzala alttawrātu qul fa/too bialttawrāti faotloohā in kuntum sadiqeena All food was lawful unto the Children of Israel, save that which Israel forbade himself, (in days) before the Torah was revealed. Say: Produce the Torah and read it (unto us) if ye are truthful.
094 famani iftarā ʿalā allāhi alkaḏiba min baʿdi ṯalika faolā-ika humu alḏālimoona And whoever shall invent a falsehood after that concerning Allah, such will be wrong-doers.
095 qul ṣadaqa allāhu faittabiʿoo millata ibrāheema ḥaneefan wamā kāna mina almušrikeena Say: Allah speaketh truth. So follow the religion of Abraham, the upright. He was not of the idolaters.
096 inna awwala baytin wuḍiʿa lilnnāsi lallaḏee bibakkata mubārakan wahudan lilʿālameena Lo! the first Sanctuary appointed for mankind was that at Mecca, a blessed place, a guidance to the peoples;
097 feehi āyātun bayyinātun maqāmu ibrāheema waman daḫalahu kāna āminan walillāhi ʿalā alnnāsi ḥijju albayti mani istataʿa ilayhi sabeelan waman kafara fa-inna allāha ġaniyyun ʿani alʿālameena Wherein are plain memorials (of Allah's guidance); the place where Abraham stood up to pray; and whosoever entereth it is safe. And pilgrimage to the House is a duty unto Allah for mankind, for him who can find a way thither. As for him who disbelieveth, (let him know that) lo! Allah is Independent of (all) creatures.
098 qul yā ahla alkitābi lima takfuroona bi-āyāti allāhi waallāhu šaheedun ʿalā mā taʿmaloona Say: O People of the Scripture! Why disbelieve ye in the revelations of Allah, when Allah (Himself) is Witness of what ye do ?
099 qul yā ahla alkitābi lima taṣuddoona ʿan sabeeli allāhi man āmana tabġoonahā ʿiwajan waantum šuhadāo wamā allāhu biġāfilin ʿammā taʿmaloona Say: O People of the Scripture! Why drive ye back believers from the way of Allah, seeking to make it crooked, when ye are witnesses (to Allah's guidance) ? Allah is not unaware of what ye do.
100 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo in tuṭeeʿoo fareeqan mina allāḏeena ootoo alkitāba yaruddookum baʿda eemānikum kāfireena O ye who believe! If ye obey a party of those who have received the Scripture they will make you disbelievers after your belief.
101 wakayfa takfuroona waantum tutlā ʿalaykum āyātu allāhi wafeekum rasooluhu waman yaʿtaṣim biallāhi faqad hudiya ilā ṣiratin mustaqeemin How can ye disbelieve, when it is ye unto whom Allah's revelations are recited, and His messenger is in your midst ? He who holdeth fast to Allah, he indeed is guided unto a right path.
102 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo ittaqoo allāha ḥaqqa tuqātihi walā tamootunna illā waantum muslimoona O ye who believe! Observe your duty to Allah with right observance, and die not save as those who have surrendered (unto Him)
103 waiʿtaṣimoo biḥabli allāhi jameeʿan walā tafarraqoo waoḏkuroo niʿmata allāhi ʿalaykum iḏ kuntum aʿdāan faallafa bayna quloobikum faaṣbaḥtum biniʿmatihi iḫwānan wakuntum ʿalā šafā ḥufratin mina alnnāri faanqaḏakum minhā kaṯalika yubayyinu allāhu lakum āyātihi laʿallakum tahtadoona And hold fast, all of you together, to the cable of Allah, and do not separate. Thus Allah maketh clear His revelations unto you, that haply ye may be guided,
104 waltakun minkum ommatun yadʿoona ilā alḫayri waya/muroona bialmaʿroofi wayanhawna ʿani almunkari waolā-ika humu almufliḥoona And there may spring from you a nation who invite to goodness, and enjoin right conduct and forbid indecency. Such are they who are successful.

Then taste the punishment for that ye disbelieved.
107 waammā allāḏeena ibyaḍat wujoohuhum fafee raḥmati allāhi hum feehā ḫālidoona And as for those whose faces have been whitened, in the mercy of Allah they dwell for ever.
108 tilka āyātu allāhi natloohā ʿalayka bialḥaqqi wamā allāhu yureedu ḏulman lilʿālameena These are revelations of Allah. We recite them unto thee in truth. Allah willeth no injustice to (His) creatures.
109 walillāhi mā fee alssamāwāti wamā fee al-arḍi wa-ilā allāhi turjaʿu al-omooru Unto Allah belongeth whatsoever is in the heavens and whatsoever is in the earth; and unto Allah all things are returned.
110 kuntum ḫayra ommatin oḫrijat lilnnāsi ta/muroona bialmaʿroofi watanhawna ʿani almunkari watu/minoona biallāhi walaw āmana ahlu alkitābi lakāna ḫayran lahum minhumu almu/minoona waakṯaruhumu alfāsiqoona Ye are the best community that hath been raised up for mankind. 111 lan yaḍurrookum illā aḏan wa-in yuqātilookum yuwallookumu al-adbāra ṯumma lā yunṣaroona They will not harm you save a trifling hurt, and if they fight against you they will turn and flee. And afterward they will not be helped.

118 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo lā tattaḫiḏoo bitanatan min doonikum lā ya/loonakum ḫabālan waddoo mā ʿanittum qad badati albaġdao min afwāhihim wamā tuḫfee ṣudooruhum akbaru qad bayyannā lakumu al-āyāti in kuntum taʿqiloona O ye who believe! 127 liyaqṭaʿa ṭarafan mina allāḏeena kafaroo aw yakbitahum fayanqaliboo ḫā-ibeena That He may cut off a part of those who disbelieve, or overwhelm them so that they retire, frustrated.

130 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo lā ta/kuloo alrribā aḍʿāfan mudaʿafatan waittaqoo allāha laʿallakum tufliḥoona O ye who believe! Devour not usury, doubling and quadrupling (the sum lent). Observe your duty to Allah, that ye may be successful.

134 allāḏeena yunfiqoona fee alssarrā-i waalḍarrā-i waalkāḏimeena alġayḏa waalʿāfeena ʿani alnnāsi waallāhu yuḥibbu almuḥsineena Those who spend (of that which Allah hath given them) in ease and in adversity, those who control their wrath and are forgiving toward mankind; Allah loveth the good;

142 am ḥasibtum an tadḫuloo aljannata walammā yaʿlami allāhu allāḏeena jāhadoo minkum wayaʿlama alssabireena Or deemed ye that ye would enter paradise while yet Allah knoweth not those of you who really strive, nor knoweth those (of you) who are steadfast ?
143 walaqad kuntum tamannawna almawta min qabli an talqawhu faqad raaytumoohu waantum tanḏuroona And verily ye used to wish for death before ye met it (in the field). Now ye have seen it with your eyes!
144 wamā muḥammadun illā rasoolun qad ḫalat min qablihi alrrusulu afa-in māta aw qutila inqalabtum ʿalā aʿqābikum waman yanqalib ʿalā ʿaqibayhi falan yaḍurra allāha šay-an wasayajzee allāhu alššākireena Muhammad is but a messenger, messengers (the like of whom) have passed away before him. Will it be that, when he dieth or is slain, ye will turn back on your heels ? He who turneth back on his heels doth no hurt to Allah, and Allah will reward the thankful.
145 wamā kāna linafsin an tamoota illā bi-iḏni allāhi kitāban mu-ajjalan waman yurid ṯawāba alddunyā nu/tihi minhā waman yurid ṯawāba al-āḫirati nu/tihi minhā wasanajzee alššākireena No soul can ever die except by Allah's leave and at a term appointed. Whoso desireth the reward of the world, We bestow on him thereof; and whoso desireth the reward of the Hereafter, We bestow on him thereof. We shall reward the thankful.
146 wakaayyin min nabiyyin qātala maʿahu ribbiyyoona kaṯeerun famā wahanoo limā asabahum fee sabeeli allāhi wamā ḍaʿufoo wamā istakānoo waallāhu yuḥibbu alssabireena And with how many a prophet have there been a number of devoted men who fought (beside him). They quailed not for aught that befell them in the way of Allah, nor did they weaken, nor were they brought low. Allah loveth the steadfast.
147 wamā kāna qawlahum illā an qāloo rabbanā iġfir lanā ḏunoobanā wa-isrāfanā fee amrinā waṯabbit aqdāmanā waonṣurnā ʿalā alqawmi alkāfireena Their cry was only that they said: Our Lord! forgive us for our sins and wasted efforts, make our foothold sure, and give us victory over the disbelieving folk.
148 faātāhumu allāhu ṯawāba alddunyā waḥusna ṯawābi al-āḫirati waallāhu yuḥibbu almuḥsineena So Allah gave them the reward of the world and the good reward of the Hereafter. Allah loveth those whose deeds are good.
149 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo in tuṭeeʿoo allāḏeena kafaroo yaruddookum ʿalā aʿqābikum fatanqaliboo ḫāsireena O ye who believe! if ye obey those who disbelieve, they will make you turn back on your heels, and ye turn back as losers.
150 bali allāhu mawlākum wahuwa ḫayru alnnasireena But Allah is your Protector, and He is the Best of Helpers.
151 sanulqee fee quloobi allāḏeena kafaroo alrruʿba bimā ašrakoo biallāhi mā lam yunazzil bihi sultanan wama/wāhumu alnnāru wabi/sa maṯwā alṯṯālimeena We shall cast terror into the hearts of those who disbelieve because they ascribe unto Allah partners, for which no warrant hath been revealed. Their habitation is the Fire, and hapless the abode of the wrong-doers.
152 walaqad ṣadaqakumu allāhu waʿdahu iḏ taḥussoonahum bi-iḏnihi ḥattā iṯa fašiltum watanāzaʿtum fee al-amri waʿaṣaytum min baʿdi mā arākum mā tuḥibboona minkum man yureedu alddunyā waminkum man yureedu al-āḫirata ṯumma ṣarafakum ʿanhum liyabtaliyakum walaqad ʿafā ʿankum waallāhu ḏoo faḍlin ʿalā almu/mineena Allah verily made good His promise unto you when ye routed them by His leave, until (the moment) when your courage failed you, and ye disagreed about the order and ye disobeyed, after He had shown you that for which ye long. Some of you desired the world, and some of you desired the Hereafter. Therefore He made you flee from them, that He might try you. Yet now He hath forgiven you. Allah is a Lord of Kindness to believers.
153 iḏ tuṣʿidoona walā talwoona ʿalā aḥadin waalrrasoolu yadʿookum fee oḫrākum faaṯābakum ġamman biġammin likay lā taḥzanoo ʿalā mā fātakum walā mā asabakum waallāhu ḫabeerun bimā taʿmaloona When ye climbed (the hill) and paid no heed to anyone, while the messenger, in your rear, was calling you (to fight). Therefor He rewarded you grief for (his) grief, that (He might teach) you not to sorrow either for that which ye missed or for that which befell you. Allah is Informed of what ye do.
154 thumma anzala ʿalaykum min baʿdi alġammi amanatan nuʿāsan yaġšā ta-ifatan minkum wata-ifatun qad ahammat-hum anfusuhum yaḏunnoona biallāhi ġayra alḥaqqi ḏanna aljāhiliyyati yaqooloona hal lanā mina al-amri min šay-in qul inna al-amra kullahu lillāhi yuḫfoona fee anfusihim mā lā yubdoona laka yaqooloona law kāna lanā mina al-amri šay-on mā qutilnā hāhunā qul law kuntum fee buyootikum labaraza allāḏeena kutiba ʿalayhimu alqatlu ilā madajiʿihim waliyabtaliya allāhu mā fee ṣudoorikum waliyumahhiṣa mā fee quloobikum waallāhu ʿaleemun biṯati alṣudoori Then, after grief, He sent down security for you. As slumber did it overcome a party of you, while (the other) party, who were anxious on their own account, thought wrongly of Allah, the thought of ignorance. They said: Have we any part in the cause ? Say (O Muhammad): The cause belongeth wholly to Allah. They hide within themselves (a thought) which they reveal not unto thee, saying: Had we had any part in the cause we should not have been slain here. Say: Even though ye had been in your houses, those appointed to be slain would have gone forth to the places where they were to lie. (All this hath been) in order that Allah might try what is in your breasts and prove what is in your hearts. Allah is Aware of what is hidden in the breasts (of men).
155 inna allāḏeena tawallaw minkum yawma iltaqā aljamʿāni innamā istazallahumu alššaytanu bibaʿḍi mā kasaboo walaqad ʿafā allāhu ʿanhum inna allāha ġafoorun ḥaleemun Lo! those of you who turned back on the day when the two hosts met, Satan alone it was who caused them to backslide, because of some of that which they have earned. Now Allah hath forgiven them. Lo! Allah is Forgiving, Clement.
156 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo lā takoonoo kaallaḏeena kafaroo waqāloo li-iḫwānihim iṯa ḍaraboo fee al-arḍi aw kānoo ġuzzan law kānoo ʿindanā mā mātoo wamā qutiloo liyajʿala allāhu ṯalika ḥasratan fee quloobihim waallāhu yuḥyee wayumeetu waallāhu bimā taʿmaloona baṣeerun O ye who believe! Be not as those who disbelieved and said of their brethren who went abroad in the land or were fighting in the field: If they had been (here) with us they would not have died or been killed: that Allah may make it anguish in their hearts. Allah giveth life and causeth death; and Allah is Seer of what ye do.
157 wala-in qutiltum fee sabeeli allāhi aw muttum lamaġfiratun mina allāhi waraḥmatun ḫayrun mimmā yajmaʿoona And what though ye be slain in Allah's way or die therein ? Surely pardon from Allah and mercy are better than all that they amass.
158 wala-in muttum aw qutiltum la-ilā allāhi tuḥšaroona What though ye be slain or die, when unto Allah ye are gathered ?
159 fabimā raḥmatin mina allāhi linta lahum walaw kunta faṯṯan ġaleeḏa alqalbi lainfaḍoo min ḥawlika faoʿfu ʿanhum waistaġfir lahum wašāwirhum fee al-amri fa-iṯa ʿazamta fatawakkal ʿalā allāhi inna allāha yuḥibbu almutawakkileena It was by the mercy of Allah that thou wast lenient with them (O Muhammad), for if thou hadst been stern and fierce of heart they would have dispersed from round about thee. So pardon them and ask forgiveness for them and consult with them upon the conduct of affairs. And when thou art resolved, then put thy trust in Allah. Lo! Allah loveth those who put their trust (in Him).
160 in yanṣurkumu allāhu falā ġāliba lakum wa-in yaḫḏulkum faman ṯa allāḏee yanṣurukum min baʿdihi waʿalā allāhi falyatawakkali almu/minoona If Allah is your helper none can overcome you, and if He withdraw His help from you, who is there who can help you after Him ? In Allah let believers put their trust.
161 wamā kāna linabiyyin an yaġulla waman yaġlul ya/ti bimā ġalla yawma alqiyāmati ṯumma tuwaffā kullu nafsin mā kasabat wahum lā yuḏlamoona It is not for any prophet to embezzle. Whoso embezzleth will bring what he embezzled with him on the Day of Resurrection. Then every soul will be paid in full what it hath earned; and they will not be wronged.
162 afamani ittabaʿa riḍwāna allāhi kaman bāa bisaḫaṭin mina allāhi wama/wāhu jahannamu wabi/sa almaṣeeru Is one who followeth the pleasure of Allah as one who hath earned condemnation from Allah, whose habitation is the Fire, a hapless journey's end ?
163 hum darajātun ʿinda allāhi waallāhu baṣeerun bimā yaʿmaloona There are degrees (of grace and reprobation) with Allah, and Allah is Seer of what they do.
164 laqad manna allāhu ʿalā almu/mineena iḏ baʿaṯa feehim rasoolan min anfusihim yatloo ʿalayhim āyātihi wayuzakkeehim wayuʿallimuhumu alkitāba waalḥikmata wa-in kānoo min qablu lafee ḍalālin mubeenin Allah verily hath shown grace to the believers by sending unto them a messenger of their own who reciteth unto them His revelations, and causeth them to grow, and teacheth them the Scripture and wisdom; although before (he came to them) they were in flagrant error.
165 awa lammā asabatkum muṣeebatun qad aṣabtum miṯlayhā qultum annā haṯa qul huwa min ʿindi anfusikum inna allāha ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerun And was it so, when a disaster smote you, though ye had smitten (them with a disaster) twice (as great), that ye said: How is this ? Say (unto them, O Muhammad): It is from yourselves. Lo! Allah is Able to do all things.
166 wamā asabakum yawma iltaqā aljamʿāni fabi-iḏni allāhi waliyaʿlama almu/mineena That which befell you, on the day when the two armies met, was by permission of Allah; that He might know the true believers;
167 waliyaʿlama allāḏeena nāfaqoo waqeela lahum taʿālaw qātiloo fee sabeeli allāhi awi idfaʿoo qāloo law naʿlamu qitālan laittabaʿnākum hum lilkufri yawma-iḏin aqrabu minhum lil-eemāni yaqooloona bi-afwāhihim mā laysa fee quloobihim waallāhu aʿlamu bimā yaktumoona And that He might know the hypocrites, unto whom it was said: Come, fight in the way of Allah, or defend yourselves. They answered: If we knew aught of fighting we would follow you. On that day they were nearer disbelief than faith. They utter with their mouths a thing which is not in their hearts. Allah is Best Aware of what they hide.
168 allāḏeena qāloo li-iḫwānihim waqaʿadoo law ataʿoonā mā qutiloo qul faidraoo ʿan anfusikumu almawta in kuntum sadiqeena Those who, while they sat at home, said of their brethren (who were fighting for the cause of Allah): If they had been guided by us they would not have been slain. Say (unto them, O Muhammad): Then avert death from yourselves if ye are truthful.
169 walā taḥsabanna allāḏeena qutiloo fee sabeeli allāhi amwātan bal aḥyāon ʿinda rabbihim yurzaqoona Think not of those, who are slain in the way of Allah, as dead. Nay, they are living. With their Lord they have provision.
170 fariḥeena bimā ātāhumu allāhu min faḍlihi wayastabširoona biallaḏeena lam yalḥaqoo bihim min ḫalfihim allā ḫawfun ʿalayhim walā hum yaḥzanoona Jubilant (are they) because of that which Allah hath bestowed upon them of His bounty, rejoicing for the sake of those who have not joined them but are left behind: That there shall no fear come upon them neither shall they grieve.
171 yastabširoona biniʿmatin mina allāhi wafaḍlin waanna allāha lā yuḍeeʿu ajra almu/mineena They rejoice because of favour from Allah and kindness, and that Allah wasteth not the wage of the believers.
172 allāḏeena istajāboo lillāhi waalrrasooli min baʿdi mā asabahumu alqarḥu lillaḏeena aḥsanoo minhum waittaqaw ajrun ʿaḏeemun As for those who heard the call of Allah and His messenger after the harm befell them (in the fight); for such of them as do right and ward off (evil), there is great reward.
173 allāḏeena qāla lahumu alnnāsu inna alnnāsa qad jamaʿoo lakum faiḫšawhum fazādahum eemānan waqāloo ḥasbunā allāhu waniʿma alwakeelu Those unto whom men said: Lo! the people have gathered against you, therefor fear them. (The threat of danger) but increased the faith of them and they cried: Allah is Sufficient for us! Most Excellent is He in Whom we trust!
174 fainqalaboo biniʿmatin mina allāhi wafaḍlin lam yamsas-hum soo-on waittabaʿoo riḍwāna allāhi waallāhu ḏoo faḍlin ʿaḏeemin So they returned with grace and favour from Allah, and no harm touched them. They followed the good pleasure of Allah, and Allah is of Infinite Bounty.
175 innamā ṯalikumu alššaytanu yuḫawwifu awliyāahu falā taḫāfoohum waḫāfooni in kuntum mu/mineena It is only the devil who would make (men) fear his partisans. Fear them not; fear Me, if ye are true believers.
176 walā yaḥzunka allāḏeena yusāriʿoona fee alkufri innahum lan yaḍurroo allāha šay-an yureedu allāhu allā yajʿala lahum ḥaṯṯan fee al-āḫirati walahum ʿaṯabun ʿaḏeemun Let not their conduct grieve thee, who run easily to disbelief, for lo! they injure Allah not at all. It is Allah's Will to assign them no portion in the Hereafter, and theirs will be an awful doom.
177 inna allāḏeena ištarawoo alkufra bial-eemāni lan yaḍurroo allāha šay-an walahum ʿaṯabun aleemun Those who purchase disbelief at the price of faith harm Allah not at all, but theirs will be a painful doom.
178 walā yaḥsabanna allāḏeena kafaroo annamā numlee lahum ḫayrun li-anfusihim innamā numlee lahum liyazdādoo iṯman walahum ʿaṯabun muheenun And let not those who disbelieve imagine that the rein We give them bodeth good unto their souls. We only give them rein that they may grow in sinfulness. And theirs will be a shameful doom.
179 mā kāna allāhu liyaḏara almu/mineena ʿalā mā antum ʿalayhi ḥattā yameeza alḫabeeṯa mina alttayyibi wamā kāna allāhu liyuṭliʿakum ʿalā alġaybi walākinna allāha yajtabee min rusulihi man yašāo faāminoo biallāhi warusulihi wa-in tu/minoo watattaqoo falakum ajrun ʿaḏeemun It is not (the purpose) of Allah to leave you in your present state till He shall separate the wicked from the good. And it is not (the purpose of) Allah to let you know the Unseen. But Allah chooseth of His messengers whom He will, (to receive knowledge thereof). So believe in Allah and His messengers. If ye believe and ward off (evil), yours will be a vast reward.
180 walā yaḥsabanna allāḏeena yabḫaloona bimā ātāhummu allāhu min faḍlihi huwa ḫayran lahum bal huwa šarrun lahum sayuṭawwaqoona mā baḫiloo bihi yawma alqiyāmati walillāhi meerāṯu alssamāwāti waal-arḍi waallāhu bimā taʿmaloona ḫabeerun And let not those who hoard up that which Allah hath bestowed upon them of His bounty think that it is better for them. Nay, it is worse for them. That which they hoard will be their collar on the Day of Resurrection. Allah's is the heritage of the heavens and the earth, and Allah is Informed of what ye do.
181 laqad samiʿa allāhu qawla allāḏeena qāloo inna allāha faqeerun wanaḥnu aġniyāon sanaktubu mā qāloo waqatlahumu al-anbiyāa biġayri ḥaqqin wanaqoolu ḏooqoo ʿaṯaba alḥareeqi Verily Allah heard the saying of those who said, (when asked for contributions to the war): "Allah, forsooth, is poor, and we are rich!" We shall record their saying with their slaying of the prophets wrongfully and We shall say: Taste ye the punishment of burning!
182 [romanization missing] This is on account of that which your own hands have sent before (you to the judgment). Allah is no oppressor of (His) bondmen.
183 allāḏeena qāloo inna allāha ʿahida ilaynā allā nu/mina lirasoolin ḥattā ya/tiyanā biqurbānin ta/kuluhu alnnāru qul qad jāakum rusulun min qablee bialbayyināti wabiallaḏee qultum falima qataltumoohum in kutum sadiqeena (The same are) those who say: Lo! Allah hath charged us that we believe not in any messenger until he bring us an offering which fire (from heaven) shall devour. Say (unto them, O Muhammad): Messengers came unto you before me with miracles, and with that (very miracle) which ye describe. Why then did ye slay them ? (Answer that) if ye are truthful!
184 fa-in kaṯṯabooka faqad kuṯṯiba rusulun min qablika jāoo bialbayyināti waalzzuburi waalkitābi almuneeri And if they deny thee, even so did they deny messengers who were before thee, who came with miracles and with the Psalms and with the Scripture giving light.
185 kullu nafsin ṯa-iqatu almawti wa-innamā tuwaffawna ojoorakum yawma alqiyāmati faman zuḥziḥa ʿani alnnāri waodḫila aljannata faqad fāza wamā alḥayātu alddunyā illā matāʿu alġuroori Every soul will taste of death. And ye will be paid on the Day of Resurrection only that which ye have fairly earned. Whoso is removed from the Fire and is made to enter paradise, he indeed is triumphant. The life of this world is but comfort of illusion.
186 latublawunna fee amwālikum waanfusikum walatasmaʿunna mina allāḏeena ootoo alkitāba min qablikum wamina allāḏeena ašrakoo aḏan kaṯeeran wa-in taṣbiroo watattaqoo fa-inna ṯalika min ʿazmi al-omoori Assuredly ye will be tried in your property and in your persons, and ye will hear much wrong from those who were given the Scripture before you, and from the idolaters. But if ye persevere and ward off (evil), then that is of the steadfast heart of things.
187 wa-iḏ aḫaḏa allāhu meeṯāqa allāḏeena ootoo alkitāba latubayyinunnahu lilnnāsi walā taktumoonahu fanabaḏoohu warāa ḏuhoorihim waištaraw bihi ṯamanan qaleelan fabi/sa mā yaštaroona And (remember) when Allah laid a charge on those who had received the Scripture (He said): Ye are to expound it to mankind and not to hide it. But they flung it behind their backs and bought thereby a little gain. Verily evil is that which they have gained thereby.
188 lā taḥsabanna allāḏeena yafraḥoona bimā ataw wayuḥibboona an yuḥmadoo bimā lam yafʿaloo falā taḥsabannahum bimafāzatin mina alʿaṯabi walahum ʿaṯabun aleemun Think not that those who exult in what they have given, and love to be praised for what they have not done - Think not, they are in safety from the doom. A painful doom is theirs.
189 walillāhi mulku alssamāwāti waal-arḍi waallāhu ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerun Unto Allah belongeth the Sovereignty of the heavens and the earth. Allah is Able to do all things.
190 inna fee ḫalqi alssamāwāti waal-arḍi waiḫtilāfi allāyli waalnnahāri laāyātin li-olee al-albābi Lo! In the creation of the heavens and the earth and (in) the difference of night and day are tokens (of His Sovereignty) for men of understanding,
191 allāḏeena yaḏkuroona allāha qiyāman waquʿoodan waʿalā junoobihim wayatafakkaroona fee ḫalqi alssamāwāti waal-arḍi rabbanā mā ḫalaqta haṯa batilan subhanaka faqinā ʿaṯaba alnnāri Such as remember Allah, standing, sitting, and reclining, and consider the creation of the heavens and the earth, (and say): Our Lord! Thou createdst not this in vain. Glory be to Thee! Preserve us from the doom of Fire.
192 rabbanā innaka man tudḫili alnnāra faqad aḫzaytahu wamā lilṯṯālimeena min ansarin Our Lord! Whom Thou causest to enter the Fire: him indeed Thou hast confounded. For evil-doers there will be no helpers.
193 rabbanā innanā samiʿnā munādiyan yunādee lil-eemāni an āminoo birabbikum faāmannā rabbanā faiġfir lanā ḏunoobanā wakaffir ʿannā sayyi-ātinā watawaffanā maʿa al-abrāri Our Lord! Lo! we have heard a crier calling unto Faith: "Believe ye in your Lord!" So we believed. Our Lord! Therefor forgive us our sins, and remit from us our evil deeds, and make us die the death of the righteous.
194 rabbanā waātinā mā waʿadtanā ʿalā rusulika walā tuḫzinā yawma alqiyāmati innaka lā tuḫlifu almeeʿāda Our Lord! And give us that which Thou hast promised to us by Thy messengers. Confound us not upon the Day of Resurrection. Lo! Thou breakest not the tryst.
195 faistajāba lahum rabbuhum annee lā oḍeeʿu ʿamala ʿāmilin minkum min ḏakarin aw onṯā baʿḍukum min baʿḍin faallaḏeena hājaroo waoḫrijoo min diyārihim waooḏoo fee sabeelee waqātaloo waqutiloo laokaffiranna ʿanhum sayyi-ātihim walaodḫilannahum jannātin tajree min taḥtihā al-anhāru ṯawāban min ʿindi allāhi waallāhu ʿindahu ḥusnu alṯṯawābi And their Lord hath heard them (and He saith): Lo! I suffer not the work of any worker, male or female, to be lost. Ye proceed one from another. So those who fled and were driven forth from their homes and suffered damage for My cause, and fought and were slain, verily I shall remit their evil deeds from them and verily I shall bring them into Gardens underneath which rivers flow - A reward from Allah. And with Allah is the fairest of rewards.
196 lā yaġurrannaka taqallubu allāḏeena kafaroo fee albilādi Let not the vicissitude (of the success) of those who disbelieve, in the land, deceive thee (O Muhammad).
197 matāʿun qaleelun ṯumma ma/wāhum jahannamu wabi/sa almihādu It is but a brief comfort. And afterward their habitation will be hell, an ill abode.
198 lākini allāḏeena ittaqaw rabbahum lahum jannātun tajree min taḥtihā al-anhāru ḫālideena feehā nuzulan min ʿindi allāhi wamā ʿinda allāhi ḫayrun lil-abrāri But those who keep their duty to their Lord, for them are Gardens underneath which rivers flow, wherein they will be safe for ever. A gift of welcome from their Lord. That which Allah hath in store is better for the righteous.
199 wa-inna min ahli alkitābi laman yu/minu biallāhi wamā onzila ilaykum wamā onzila ilayhim ḫāšiʿeena lillāhi lā yaštaroona bi-āyāti allāhi ṯamanan qaleelan olā-ika lahum ajruhum ʿinda rabbihim inna allāha sareeʿu alḥisābi And lo! of the People of the Scripture there are some who believe in Allah and that which is revealed unto you and that which was revealed unto them, humbling themselves before Allah. They purchase not a trifling gain at the price of the revelations of Allah. Verily their reward is with their Lord. Lo! Allah is swift to take account.
