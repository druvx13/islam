61. as-Saff: The Ranks

1 
sabbaḥa lillāhi mā fee alssamāwāti wamā fee al-arḍi wahuwa alʿazeezu alḥakeemu
All that is in the heavens and all that is in the earth glorifieth Allah, and He is the Mighty, the Wise.

2 
yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo lima taqooloona mā lā tafʿaloona
O ye who believe! Why say ye that which ye do not ?

3 
kabura maqtan ʿinda allāhi an taqooloo mā lā tafʿaloona
It is most hateful in the sight of Allah that ye say that which ye do not.

4 
inna allāha yuḥibbu allāḏeena yuqātiloona fee sabeelihi ṣaffan kaannahum bunyānun marṣooṣun
Lo! Allah loveth them who battle for His cause in ranks, as if they were a solid structure.

5 
wa-iḏ qāla moosā liqawmihi yā qawmi lima tu/ḏoonanee waqad taʿlamoona annee rasoolu allāhi ilaykum falammā zāġoo azāġa allāhu quloobahum waallāhu lā yahdee alqawma alfāsiqeena
And (remember) when Moses said unto his people: O my people! Why persecute ye me, when ye well know that I am Allah's messenger unto you ? So when they went astray Allah sent their hearts astray. And Allah guideth not the evil-living folk.

6 
wa-iḏ qāla ʿeesā ibnu maryama yā banee isrā-eela innee rasoolu allāhi ilaykum muṣaddiqan limā bayna yadayya mina alttawrāti wamubašširan birasoolin ya/tee min baʿdee ismuhu aḥmadu falammā jāahum bialbayyināti qāloo haṯa siḥrun mubeenun
And when Jesus son of Mary said: O Children of Israel! Lo! I am the messenger of Allah unto you, confirming that which was (revealed) before me in the Torah, and bringing good tidings of a messenger who cometh after me, whose name is the Praised One. Yet when he hath come unto them with clear proofs, they say: This is mere magic.

7 
waman aḏlamu mimmani iftarā ʿalā allāhi alkaḏiba wahuwa yudʿā ilā al-islāmi waallāhu lā yahdee alqawma alṯṯālimeena
And who doeth greater wrong than he who inventeth a lie against Allah when he is summoned unto Al-Islam ? And Allah guideth not wrongdoing folk.

8 
yureedoona liyuṭfi-oo noora allāhi bi-afwāhihim waallāhu mutimmu noorihi walaw kariha alkāfiroona
Fain would they put out the light of Allah with their mouths, but Allah will perfect His light however much the disbelievers are averse.

9 
huwa allāḏee arsala rasoolahu bialhudā wadeeni alḥaqqi liyuḏhirahu ʿalā alddeeni kullihi walaw kariha almušrikoona
He it is Who hath sent His messenger with the guidance and the religion of truth, that He may make it conqueror of all religion however much idolaters may be averse.

10 
yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo hal adullukum ʿalā tijāratin tunjeekum min ʿaṯabin aleemin
O ye who believe! Shall I show you a commerce that will save you from a painful doom ?

11 
tu/minoona biallāhi warasoolihi watujāhidoona fee sabeeli allāhi bi-amwālikum waanfusikum ṯalikum ḫayrun lakum in kuntum taʿlamoona
Ye should believe in Allah and His messenger, and should strive for the cause of Allah with your wealth and your lives. That is better for you, if ye did but know.

12 
yaġfir lakum ḏunoobakum wayudḫilkum jannātin tajree min taḥtihā al-anhāru wamasākina ṭayyibatan fee jannāti ʿadnin ṯalika alfawzu alʿaḏeemu
He will forgive you your sins and bring you into Gardens underneath which rivers flow, and pleasant dwellings in Gardens of Eden. That is the supreme triumph.

13 
waoḫrā tuḥibboonahā naṣrun mina allāhi wafatḥun qarreebun wabašširi almu/mineena
And (He will give you) another (blessing) which ye love: help from Allah and present victory. Give good tidings (O Muhammad) to believers.

14 
yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo koonoo ansara allāhi kamā qāla ʿeesā ibnu maryama lilḥawāriyyeena man ansaree ilā allāhi qāla alḥawāriyyoona naḥnu ansaru allāhi faāmanat ta-ifatun min banee isrā-eela wakafarat ta-ifatun faayyadnā allāḏeena āmanoo ʿalā ʿaduwwihim faaṣbaḥoo ḏāhireena
O ye who believe! Be Allah's helpers, even as Jesus son of Mary said unto the disciples: Who are my helpers for Allah ? They said: We are Allah's helpers. And a party of the Children of Israel believed, while a party disbelieved. Then We strengthened those who believed against their foe, and they became the uppermost.
