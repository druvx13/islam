6. al-An`am: The Cattle

001 alḥamdu lillāhi allāḏee ḫalaqa alssamāwāti waal-arḍa wajaʿala alṯṯulumāti waalnnoora ṯumma allāḏeena kafaroo birabbihim yaʿdiloona Praise be to Allah, Who hath created the heavens and the earth, and hath appointed darkness and light. Yet those who disbelieve ascribe rivals unto their Lord.
002 huwa allāḏee ḫalaqakum min ṭeenin ṯumma qada ajalan waajalun musamman ʿindahu ṯumma antum tamtaroona He it is Who hath created you from clay, and hath decreed a term for you. A term is fixed with Him. Yet still ye doubt!
003 wahuwa allāhu fee alssamāwāti wafee al-arḍi yaʿlamu sirrakum wajahrakum wayaʿlamu mā taksiboona He is Allah in the heavens and in the earth. He knoweth both your secret and your utterance, and He knoweth what ye earn.
004 wamā ta/teehim min āyatin min āyāti rabbihim illā kānoo ʿanhā muʿriḍeena Never came there unto them a revelation of the revelations of Allah but they did turn away from it.
005 faqad kaṯṯaboo bialḥaqqi lammā jāahum fasawfa ya/teehim anbāo mā kānoo bihi yastahzi-oona And they denied the truth when it came unto them. But there will come unto them the tidings of that which they used to deride.
006 alam yaraw kam ahlaknā min qablihim min qarnin makkannāhum fee al-arḍi mā lam numakkin lakum waarsalnā alssamāa ʿalayhim midrāran wajaʿalnā al-anhāra tajree min taḥtihim faahlaknāhum biḏunoobihim waanša/nā min baʿdihim qarnan āḫareena See they not how many a generation We destroyed before them, whom We had established in the earth more firmly than We have established you, and We shed on them abundant showers from the sky, and made the rivers flow beneath them. Yet we destroyed them for their sins, and created after them another generation.
007 walaw nazzalnā ʿalayka kitāban fee qirtasin falamasoohu bi-aydeehim laqāla allāḏeena kafaroo in haṯa illā siḥrun mubeenun Had we sent down unto thee (Muhammad) (actual) writing upon parchment, so that they could feel it with their hands, those who disbelieve would have said: This is naught else than mere magic.
008 waqāloo lawlā onzila ʿalayhi malakun walaw anzalnā malakan laquḍiya al-amru ṯumma lā yunḏaroona They say: Why hath not an angel been sent down unto him ? If We sent down an angel, then the matter would be judged; no further time would be allowed them (for reflection).
009 walaw jaʿalnāhu malakan lajaʿalnāhu rajulan walalabasnā ʿalayhim mā yalbisoona Had we appointed him (Our messenger) an angel, We assuredly had made him (as) a man (that he might speak to men); and (thus) obscured for them (the truth) they (now) obscure.
010 walaqadi istuhzi-a birusulin min qablika fahaqa biallaḏeena saḫiroo minhum mā kānoo bihi yastahzi-oona Messengers (of Allah) have been derided before thee, but that whereat they scoffed surrounded such of them as did deride.
011 qul seeroo fee al-arḍi ṯumma onḏuroo kayfa kāna ʿāqibatu almukaṯṯibeena Say (unto the disbelievers): Travel in the land, and see the nature of the consequence for the rejecters!
012 qul liman mā fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi qul lillāhi kataba ʿalā nafsihi alrraḥmata layajmaʿannakum ilā yawmi alqiyāmati lā rayba feehi allāḏeena ḫasiroo anfusahum fahum lā yu/minoona Say: Unto whom belongeth whatsoever is in the heavens and the earth ? Say: Unto Allah. He hath prescribed for Himself mercy, that He may bring you all together to the Day of Resurrection whereof there is no doubt. Those who ruin their souls will not believe.
013 walahu mā sakana fee allāyli waalnnahāri wahuwa alssameeʿu alʿaleemu Unto Him belongeth whatsoever resteth in the night and the day. He is the Hearer, the Knower.
014 qul aġayra allāhi attaḫiḏu waliyyan fatiri alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wahuwa yuṭʿimu walā yuṭʿamu qul innee omirtu an akoona awwala man aslama walā takoonanna mina almušrikeena Say: Shall I choose for a protecting friend other than Allah, the Originator of the heavens and the earth, Who feedeth and is never fed ? Say: I am ordered to be the first to surrender (unto Him). And be not thou (O Muhammad) of the idolaters.
015 qul innee aḫāfu in ʿaṣaytu rabbee ʿaṯaba yawmin ʿaḏeemin Say: I fear, if I rebel against my Lord, the retribution of an Awful Day.
016 man yuṣraf ʿanhu yawma-iḏin faqad raḥimahu waṯalika alfawzu almubeenu He from whom (such retribution) is averted on that day, (Allah) hath in truth had mercy on him. That will be the signal triumph.
017 wa-in yamsaska allāhu biḍurrin falā kāšifa lahu illā huwa wa-in yamsaska biḫayrin fahuwa ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerun If Allah touch thee with affliction, there is none that can relieve therefrom save Him, and if He touch thee with good fortune (there is none that can impair it); for He is Able to do all things.
018 wahuwa alqāhiru fawqa ʿibādihi wahuwa alḥakeemu alḫabeeru He is the Omnipotent over His slaves, and He is the Wise, the Knower.
019 qul ayyu šay-in akbaru šahādatan quli allāhu šaheedun baynee wabaynakum waooḥiya ilayya haṯa alqur-ānu li-onḏirakum bihi waman balaġa a-innakum latašhadoona anna maʿa allāhi ālihatan oḫrā qul lā ašhadu qul innamā huwa ilāhun wahidun wa-innanee baree-on mimmā tušrikoona Say (O Muhammad): What thing is of most weight in testimony ? Say: Allah is Witness between me and you. And this Qur'an hath been inspired in me, that I may warn therewith you and whomsoever it may reach. Do ye in sooth bear witness that there are gods beside Allah ? Say: I bear no such witness. Say: He is only One God. Lo! I am innocent of that which ye associate (with Him).
020 allāḏeena ātaynāhumu alkitāba yaʿrifoonahu kamā yaʿrifoona abnāahum allāḏeena ḫasiroo anfusahum fahum lā yu/minoona Those unto whom We gave the Scripture recognise (this revelation) as they recognise their sons. Those who ruin their own souls will not believe.
021 waman aḏlamu mimmani iftarā ʿalā allāhi kaḏiban aw kaṯṯaba bi-āyātihi innahu lā yufliḥu alṯṯālimoona Who doth greater wrong than he who inventeth a lie against Allah or denieth His revelations ? Lo! the wrongdoers will not be successful.
022 wayawma naḥšuruhum jameeʿan ṯumma naqoolu lillaḏeena ašrakoo ayna šurakāokumu allāḏeena kuntum tazʿumoona And on the day We gather them together We shall say unto those who ascribed partners (unto Allah): Where are (now) those partners of your make-believe ?
023 thumma lam takun fitnatuhum illā an qāloo waallāhi rabbinā mā kunnā mušrikeena Then will they have no contention save that they will say: By Allah, our Lord, we never were idolaters.
024 onḏur kayfa kaḏaboo ʿalā anfusihim waḍalla ʿanhum mā kānoo yaftaroona See how they lie against themselves, and (how) the thing which they devised hath failed them!
025 waminhum man yastamiʿu ilayka wajaʿalnā ʿalā quloobihim akinnatan an yafqahoohu wafee aṯanihim waqran wa-in yaraw kulla āyatin lā yu/minoo bihā ḥattā iṯa jāooka yujādiloonaka yaqoolu allāḏeena kafaroo in haṯa illā asateeru al-awwaleena Of them are some who listen unto thee, but We have placed upon their hearts veils, lest they should understand, and in their ears a deafness. If they saw every token they would not believe therein; to the point that, when they come unto thee to argue with thee, the disbelievers say: This is naught else than fables of the men of old.
026 wahum yanhawna ʿanhu wayan-awna ʿanhu wa-in yuhlikoona illā anfusahum wamā yašʿuroona And they forbid (men) from it and avoid it, and they ruin none save themselves, though they perceive not.
027 walaw tarā iḏ wuqifoo ʿalā alnnāri faqāloo yā laytanā nuraddu walā nukaṯṯiba bi-āyāti rabbinā wanakoona mina almu/mineena If thou couldst see when they are set before the Fire and say: Oh, would that we might return! Then would we not deny the revelations of our Lord but we would be of the believers!
028 bal badā lahum mā kānoo yuḫfoona min qablu walaw ruddoo laʿādoo limā nuhoo ʿanhu wa-innahum lakaṯiboona Nay, but that hath become clear unto them which before they used to hide. And if they were sent back they would return unto that which they are forbidden. Lo! they are liars.
029 waqāloo in hiya illā ḥayātunā alddunyā wamā naḥnu bimabʿooṯeena And they say: There is naught save our life of the world, and we shall not be raised (again).
030 walaw tarā iḏ wuqifoo ʿalā rabbihim qāla alaysa haṯa bialḥaqqi qāloo balā warabbinā qāla faḏooqoo alʿaṯaba bimā kuntum takfuroona If thou couldst see when they are set before their Lord! He will say: Is not this real ? They will say: Yea, verily, by our Lord! He will say: Taste now the retribution for that ye used to disbelieve.
031 qad ḫasira allāḏeena kaṯṯaboo biliqā-i allāhi ḥattā iṯa jāat-humu alssāʿatu baġtatan qāloo yā ḥasratanā ʿalā mā farraṭnā feehā wahum yaḥmiloona awzārahum ʿalā ḏuhoorihim alā sāa mā yaziroona They indeed are losers who deny their meeting with Allah until, when the Hour cometh on them suddenly, they cry: Alas for us, that we neglected it! They bear upon their backs their burdens. Ah, evil is that which they bear!
032 wamā alḥayātu alddunyā illā laʿibun walahwun walalddāru al-āḫirati ḫayrun lillaḏeena yattaqoona afalā taʿqiloona Naught is the life of the world save a pastime and a spot. Better far is the abode of the Hereafter for those who keep their duty (to Allah). Have ye then no sense ?
033 qad naʿlamu innahu layaḥzunuka allāḏee yaqooloona fa-innahum lā yukaṯṯiboonaka walākinna alṯṯālimeena bi-āyāti allāhi yajḥadoona We know well how their talk grieveth thee, though in truth they deny not thee (Muhammad) but evil-doers flout the revelations of Allah.
034 walaqad kuṯṯibat rusulun min qablika faṣabaroo ʿalā mā kuṯṯiboo waooḏoo ḥattā atāhum naṣrunā walā mubaddila likalimāti allāhi walaqad jāaka min naba-i almursaleena Messengers indeed have been denied before thee, and they were patient under the denial and the persecution till Our succour reached them. There is none to alter the decisions of Allah. Already there hath reached thee (somewhat) of the tidings of the messengers (We sent before).
035 wa-in kāna kabura ʿalayka iʿraduhum fa-ini istaṭaʿta an tabtaġiya nafaqan fee al-arḍi aw sullaman fee alssamā-i fata/tiyahum bi-āyatin walaw šāa allāhu lajamaʿahum ʿalā alhudā falā takoonanna mina aljāhileena And if their aversion is grievous unto thee, then, if thou canst, seek a way down into the earth or a ladder unto the sky that thou mayst bring unto them a portent (to convince them all)! - If Allah willed, He could have brought them all together to the guidance - So be not thou among the foolish ones.
036 innamā yastajeebu allāḏeena yasmaʿoona waalmawtā yabʿaṯuhumu allāhu ṯumma ilayhi yurjaʿoona Only those can accept who hear. As for the dead, Allah will raise them up; then unto Him they will be returned.
037 waqāloo lawlā nuzzila ʿalayhi āyatun min rabbihi qul inna allāha qādirun ʿalā an yunazzila āyatan walākinna akṯarahum lā yaʿlamoona They say: Why hath no portent been sent down upon him from his Lord ? Say: Lo! Allah is Able to send down a portent. But most of them know not.
038 wamā min dābbatin fee al-arḍi walā ta-irin yaṭeeru bijanahayhi illā omamun amṯālukum mā farraṭnā fee alkitābi min šay-in ṯumma ilā rabbihim yuḥšaroona There is not an animal in the earth, nor a flying creature flying on two wings, but they are peoples like unto you. We have neglected nothing in the Book (of Our decrees). Then unto their Lord they will be gathered.
039 waallaḏeena kaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā ṣummun wabukmun fee alṯṯulumāti man yaša-i allāhu yuḍlilhu waman yaša/ yajʿalhu ʿalā ṣiratin mustaqeemin Those who deny Our revelations are deaf and dumb in darkness. Whom Allah will sendeth astray, and whom He will He placeth on a straight path.
040 qul araaytakum in atākum ʿaṯabu allāhi aw atatkumu alssāʿatu aġayra allāhi tadʿoona in kuntum sadiqeena Say: Can ye see yourselves, if the punishment of Allah come upon you or the Hour come upon you, (calling upon other than Allah) ? Do ye then call (for help) to any other than Allah ? (Answer that) if ye are truthful.
041 bal iyyāhu tadʿoona fayakšifu mā tadʿoona ilayhi in šāa watansawna mā tušrikoona Nay, but unto Him ye call, and He removeth that because of which ye call unto Him, if He will, and ye forget whatever partners ye ascribed unto Him.
042 walaqad arsalnā ilā omamin min qablika faaḫaḏnāhum bialba/sā-i waalḍarrā-i laʿallahum yataḍarraʿoona We have sent already unto peoples that were before thee, and We visited them with tribulation and adversity, in order that they might grow humble.
043 falawlā iḏ jāahum ba/sunā taḍarraʿoo walākin qasat quloobuhum wazayyana lahumu alššaytanu mā kānoo yaʿmaloona If only, when Our disaster came on them, they had been humble! But their hearts were hardened and the devil made all that they used to do seem fair unto them!
044 falammā nasoo mā ḏukkiroo bihi fataḥnā ʿalayhim abwāba kulli šay-in ḥattā iṯa fariḥoo bimā ootoo aḫaḏnāhum baġtatan fa-iṯa hum mublisoona Then, when they forgot that whereof they had been reminded, We opened unto them the gates of all things till, even as they were rejoicing in that which they were given, We seized them unawares, and lo! they were dumbfounded.
045 faquṭiʿa dābiru alqawmi allāḏeena ḏalamoo waalḥamdu lillāhi rabbi alʿālameena So of the people who did wrong the last remnant was cut off. Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds!
046 qul araaytum in aḫaḏa allāhu samʿakum waabsarakum waḫatama ʿalā quloobikum man ilāhun ġayru allāhi ya/teekum bihi onḏur kayfa nuṣarrifu al-āyāti ṯumma hum yaṣdifoona Say: Have ye imagined, if Allah should take away your hearing and your sight and seal your hearts, Who is the God Who could restore it to you save Allah ? See how We display the revelations unto them! Yet still they turn away.
047 qul araaytakum in atākum ʿaṯabu allāhi baġtatan aw jahratan hal yuhlaku illā alqawmu alṯṯālimoona Say: Can ye see yourselves, if the punishment of Allah come upon you unawares or openly ? Would any perish save wrongdoing folk ?
048 wamā nursilu almursaleena illā mubaššireena wamunḏireena faman āmana waaṣlaḥa falā ḫawfun ʿalayhim walā hum yaḥzanoona We send not the messengers save as bearers of good news and warners. Whoso believeth and doeth right, there shall no fear come upon them neither shall they grieve.
049 waallaḏeena kaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā yamassuhumu alʿaṯabu bimā kānoo yafsuqoona But as for those who deny Our revelations, torment will afflict them for that they used to disobey.
050 qul lā aqoolu lakum ʿindee ḫazā-inu allāhi walā aʿlamu alġayba walā aqoolu lakum innee malakun in attabiʿu illā mā yooha ilayya qul hal yastawee al-aʿmā waalbaṣeeru afalā tatafakkaroona Say (O Muhammad, to the disbelievers): I say not unto you (that) I possess the treasures of Allah, nor that I have knowledge of the Unseen; and I say not unto you: Lo! I am an angel. I follow only that which is inspired in me. Say: Are the blind man and the seer equal ? Will ye not then take thought ?
051 waanḏir bihi allāḏeena yaḫāfoona an yuḥšaroo ilā rabbihim laysa lahum min doonihi waliyyun walā šafeeʿun laʿallahum yattaqoona Warn hereby those who fear (because they know) that they will be gathered unto their Lord, for whom there is no protecting ally nor intercessor beside Him, that they may ward off (evil).
052 walā taṭrudi allāḏeena yadʿoona rabbahum bialġadāti waalʿašiyyi yureedoona wajhahu mā ʿalayka min ḥisābihim min šay-in wamā min ḥisābika ʿalayhim min šay-in fataṭrudahum fatakoona mina alṯṯālimeena Repel not those who call upon their Lord at morn and evening, seeking His Countenance. Thou art not accountable for them in aught, nor are they accountable for thee in aught, that thou shouldst repel them and be of the wrong-doers.
053 wakaṯalika fatannā baʿḍahum bibaʿḍin liyaqooloo ahāolā-i manna allāhu ʿalayhim min bayninā alaysa allāhu bi-aʿlama bialššākireena And even so do We try some of them by others, that they say: Are these they whom Allah favoureth among us ? Is not Allah best Aware of the thanksgivers ?
054 wa-iṯa jāaka allāḏeena yu/minoona bi-āyātinā faqul salāmun ʿalaykum kataba rabbukum ʿalā nafsihi alrraḥmata annahu man ʿamila minkum soo-an bijahālatin ṯumma tāba min baʿdihi waaṣlaḥa faannahu ġafoorun raḥeemun And when those who believe in Our revelations come unto thee, say: Peace be unto you! Your Lord hath prescribed for Himself mercy, that whoso of you doeth evil through ignorance and repenteth afterward thereof and doeth right, (for him) lo! He is Forgiving, Merciful.
055 wakaṯalika nufaṣilu al-āyāti walitastabeena sabeelu almujrimeena Thus do We expound the revelations that the way of the unrighteous may be manifest.
056 qul innee nuheetu an aʿbuda allāḏeena tadʿoona min dooni allāhi qul lā attabiʿu ahwāakum qad ḍalaltu iḏan wamā anā mina almuhtadeena Say: I am forbidden to worship those on whom ye call instead of Allah. Say: I will not follow your desires, for then should I go astray and I should not be of the rightly guided.
057 qul innee ʿalā bayyinatin min rabbee wakaṯṯabtum bihi mā ʿindee mā tastaʿjiloona bihi ini alḥukmu illā lillāhi yaquṣu alḥaqqa wahuwa ḫayru alfasileena Say: I am (relying) on clear proof from my Lord, while ye deny Him. I have not that for which ye are impatient. The decision is for Allah only. He telleth the truth and He is the Best of Deciders.
058 qul law anna ʿindee mā tastaʿjiloona bihi laquḍiya al-amru baynee wabaynakum waallāhu aʿlamu bialṯṯālimeena Say: If I had that for which ye are impatient, then would the case (ere this) have been decided between me and you. Allah is Best Aware of the wrong-doers.
059 waʿindahu mafātiḥu alġaybi lā yaʿlamuhā illā huwa wayaʿlamu mā fee albarri waalbaḥri wamā tasquṭu min waraqatin illā yaʿlamuhā walā ḥabbatin fee ḏulumāti al-arḍi walā raṭbin walā yābisin illā fee kitābin mubeenin And with Him are the keys of the Invisible. None but He knoweth them. And He knoweth what is in the land and the sea. Not a leaf falleth but He knoweth it, not a grain amid the darkness of the earth, naught of wet or dry but (it is noted) in a clear record.
060 wahuwa allāḏee yatawaffākum biallayli wayaʿlamu mā jaraḥtum bialnnahāri ṯumma yabʿaṯukum feehi liyuqda ajalun musamman ṯumma ilayhi marjiʿukum ṯumma yunabbi-okum bimā kuntum taʿmaloona He it is Who gathereth you at night and knoweth that which ye commit by day. Then He raiseth you again to life therein, that the term appointed (for you) may be accomplished. And afterward unto Him is your return. Then He will proclaim unto you what ye used to do.
061 wahuwa alqāhiru fawqa ʿibādihi wayursilu ʿalaykum ḥafaḏatan ḥattā iṯa jāa aḥadakumu almawtu tawaffat-hu rusulunā wahum lā yufarriṭoona He is the Omnipotent over His slaves. He sendeth guardians over you until, when death cometh unto one of you, Our messengers receive him, and they neglect not.
062 thumma ruddoo ilā allāhi mawlāhumu alḥaqqi alā lahu alḥukmu wahuwa asraʿu alhasibeena Then are they restored unto Allah, their Lord, the Just. Surely His is the judgment. And He is the most swift of reckoners.
063 qul man yunajjeekum min ḏulumāti albarri waalbaḥri tadʿoonahu taḍarruʿan waḫufyatan la-in anjānā min haṯihi lanakoonanna mina alššākireena Say: Who delivereth you from the darkness of the land and the sea ? Ye call upon Him humbly and in secret, (saying): If we are delivered from this (fear) we truly will be of the thankful.
064 quli allāhu yunajjeekum minhā wamin kulli karbin ṯumma antum tušrikoona Say: Allah delivereth you from this and from all affliction. Yet ye attribute partners unto Him.
065 qul huwa alqādiru ʿalā an yabʿaṯa ʿalaykum ʿaṯaban min fawqikum aw min taḥti arjulikum aw yalbisakum šiyaʿan wayuḏeeqa baʿḍakum ba/sa baʿḍin onḏur kayfa nuṣarrifu al-āyāti laʿallahum yafqahoona Say: He is able to send punishment upon you from above you or from beneath your feet, or to bewilder you with dissension and make you taste the tyranny one of another. See how We display the revelations so that they may understand.
066 wakaṯṯaba bihi qawmuka wahuwa alḥaqqu qul lastu ʿalaykum biwakeelin Thy people (O Muhammad) have denied it, though it is the Truth. Say: I am not put in charge of you.
067 likulli naba-in mustaqarrun wasawfa taʿlamoona For every announcement there is a term, and ye will come to know.
068 wa-iṯa raayta allāḏeena yaḫooḍoona fee āyātinā faaʿriḍ ʿanhum ḥattā yaḫooḍoo fee ḥadeeṯin ġayrihi wa-immā yunsiyannaka alššaytanu falā taqʿud baʿda alṯṯikrā maʿa alqawmi alṯṯālimeena And when thou seest those who meddle with Our revelations, withdraw from them until they meddle with another topic. And if the devil cause thee to forget, sit not, after the remembrance, with the congregation of wrong-doers.
069 wamā ʿalā allāḏeena yattaqoona min ḥisābihim min šay-in walākin ḏikrā laʿallahum yattaqoona Those who ward off (evil) are not accountable for them in aught, but the Reminder (must be given them) that haply they (too) may ward off (evil).
070 waḏari allāḏeena ittaḫaḏoo deenahum laʿiban walahwan waġarrat-humu alḥayātu alddunyā waḏakkir bihi an tubsala nafsun bimā kasabat laysa lahā min dooni allāhi waliyyun walā šafeeʿun wa-in taʿdil kulla ʿadlin lā yu/ḫaḏ minhā olā-ika allāḏeena obsiloo bimā kasaboo lahum šarābun min ḥameemin waʿaṯabun aleemun bimā kānoo yakfuroona And forsake those who take their religion for a pastime and a jest, and whom the life of the world beguileth. Remind (mankind) hereby lest a soul be destroyed by what it earneth. It hath beside Allah no protecting ally nor intercessor, and though it offer every compensation it will not be accepted from it. Those are they who perish by their own deserts. For them is drink of boiling water and a painful doom, because they disbelieved.

136 wajaʿaloo lillāhi mimmā ḏaraa mina alḥarṯi waal-anʿāmi naṣeeban faqāloo haṯa lillāhi bizaʿmihim wahaṯa lišurakā-inā famā kāna lišurakā-ihim falā yaṣilu ilā allāhi wamā kāna lillāhi fahuwa yaṣilu ilā šurakā-ihim sāa mā yaḥkumoona They assign unto Allah, of the crops and cattle which He created, a portion, and they say: "This is Allah's" - in their make-believe - "and this is for (His) partners in regard to us." Thus that which (they assign) unto His partners in them reacheth not Allah and that which (they assign) unto Allah goeth to their (so-called) partners. Evil is their ordinance.
137 wakaṯalika zayyana likaṯeerin mina almušrikeena qatla awlādihim šurakāohum liyurdoohum waliyalbisoo ʿalayhim deenahum walaw šāa allāhu mā faʿaloohu faḏarhum wamā yaftaroona Thus have their (so-called) partners (of Allah) made the killing of their children to seem fair unto many of the idolaters, that they may ruin them and make their faith obscure for them. Had Allah willed (it otherwise), they had not done so. So leave them alone with their devices.
138 waqāloo haṯihi anʿāmun waḥarṯun ḥijrun lā yaṭʿamuhā illā man našāo bizaʿmihim waanʿāmun ḥurrimat ḏuhooruhā waanʿāmun lā yaḏkuroona isma allāhi ʿalayhā iftirāan ʿalayhi sayajzeehim bimā kānoo yaftaroona And they say: Such cattle and crops are forbidden. No-one is to eat of them save whom we will - in their make-believe - cattle whose backs are forbidden, cattle over which they mention not the name of Allah. (All that is) a lie against Him. He will repay them for that which they invent.
139 waqāloo mā fee buṭooni haṯihi al-anʿāmi ḫāliṣatun liḏukoorinā wamuḥarramun ʿalā azwājinā wa-in yakun maytatan fahum feehi šurakāo sayajzeehim waṣfahum innahu ḥakeemun ʿaleemun And they say: That which is in the bellies of such cattle is reserved for our males and is forbidden to our wives; but if it be born dead, then they (all) may be partakers thereof. He will reward them for their attribution (of such ordinances unto Him). Lo, He is Wise, Aware.
140 qad ḫasira allāḏeena qataloo awlādahum safahan biġayri ʿilmin waḥarramoo mā razaqahumu allāhu iftirāan ʿalā allāhi qad ḍalloo wamā kānoo muhtadeena They are losers who besottedly have slain their children without knowledge, and have forbidden that which Allah bestowed upon them, inventing a lie against Allah. They indeed have gone astray and are not guided.
141 wahuwa allāḏee anšaa jannātin maʿroošātin waġayra maʿroošātin waalnnaḫla waalzzarʿa muḫtalifan okuluhu waalzzaytoona waalrrummāna mutašābihan waġayra mutašābihin kuloo min ṯamarihi iṯa aṯmara waātoo ḥaqqahu yawma ḥasadihi walā tusrifoo innahu lā yuḥibbu almusrifeena He it is Who produceth gardens trellised and untrellised, and the date-palm, and crops of divers flavour, and the olive and the pomegranate, like and unlike. Eat ye of the fruit thereof when it fruiteth, and pay the due thereof upon the harvest day, and be not prodigal. Lo! Allah loveth not the prodigals.
142 wamina al-anʿāmi ḥamoolatan wafaršan kuloo mimmā razaqakumu allāhu walā tattabiʿoo ḫuṭuwāti alššaytani innahu lakum ʿaduwwun mubeenun And of the cattle (He produceth) some for burdens, some for food. Eat of that which Allah hath bestowed upon you, and follow not the footsteps of the devil, for lo! he is an open foe to you.
143 thamāniyata azwājin mina alḍa/ni iṯnayni wamina almaʿzi iṯnayni qul ālṯṯakarayni ḥarrama ami alonṯayayni ammā ištamalat ʿalayhi arhamu alonṯayayni nabbi-oonee biʿilmin in kuntum sadiqeena Eight pairs: Of the sheep twain, and of the goats twain. Say: Hath He forbidden the two males or the two females, or that which the wombs of the two females contain ? Expound to me (the case) with knowledge, if ye are truthful.
144 wamina al-ibili iṯnayni wamina albaqari iṯnayni qul ālṯṯakarayni ḥarrama ami alonṯayayni ammā ištamalat ʿalayhi arhamu alonṯayayni am kuntum šuhadāa iḏ wassakumu allāhu bihaṯa faman aḏlamu mimmani iftarā ʿalā allāhi kaḏiban liyuḍilla alnnāsa biġayri ʿilmin inna allāha lā yahdee alqawma alṯṯālimeena And of the camels twain and of the oxen twain. Say: Hath He forbidden the two males or the two females, or that which the wombs of the two females contain; or were ye by to witness when Allah commanded you (all) this ? Then who doth greater wrong than he who deviseth a lie concerning Allah, that he may lead mankind astray without knowledge. Lo! Allah guideth not wrongdoing folk.
145 qul lā ajidu feemā ooḥiya ilayya muḥarraman ʿalā taʿimin yaṭʿamuhu illā an yakoona maytatan aw daman masfooḥan aw laḥma ḫinzeerin fa-innahu rijsun aw fisqan ohilla liġayri allāhi bihi famani idturra ġayra bāġin walā ʿādin fa-inna rabbaka ġafoorun raḥeemun Say: I find not in that which is revealed unto me aught prohibited to an eater that he eat thereof, except it be carrion, or blood poured forth, or swineflesh - for that verily is foul - or the abomination which was immolated to the name of other than Allah. But whoso is compelled (thereto), neither craving nor transgressing, (for him) lo! thy Lord is Forgiving, Merciful.
146 waʿalā allāḏeena hādoo ḥarramnā kulla ḏee ḏufurin wamina albaqari waalġanami ḥarramnā ʿalayhim šuḥoomahumā illā mā ḥamalat ḏuhooruhumā awi alḥawāyā aw mā iḫtalaṭa biʿaḏmin ṯalika jazaynāhum bibaġyihim wa-innā lasadiqoona Unto those who are Jews We forbade every animal with claws. And of the oxen and the sheep forbade We unto them the fat thereof save that upon the backs or the entrails, or that which is mixed with the bone. That we awarded them for their rebellion. And lo! we verily are truthful.
147 fa-in kaṯṯabooka faqul rabbukum ḏoo raḥmatin wāsiʿatin walā yuraddu ba/suhu ʿani alqawmi almujrimeena So if they give the lie to thee (Muhammad), say: Your Lord is a Lord of All- Embracing Mercy, and His wrath will never be withdrawn from guilty folk.
148 sayaqoolu allāḏeena ašrakoo law šāa allāhu mā ašraknā walā ābāonā walā ḥarramnā min šay-in kaṯalika kaṯṯaba allāḏeena min qablihim ḥattā ṯaqoo ba/sanā qul hal ʿindakum min ʿilmin fatuḫrijoohu lanā in tattabiʿoona illā alṯṯanna wa-in antum illā taḫruṣoona They who are idolaters will say: Had Allah willed, we had not ascribed (unto Him) partners neither had our fathers, nor had we forbidden aught. Thus did those who were before them give the lie (to Allah's messengers) till they tasted of the fear of Us. Say: Have ye any knowledge that ye can adduce for Us ? Lo! ye follow naught but an opinion, Lo! ye do but guess.
149 qul falillāhi alḥujjatu albāliġatu falaw šāa lahadākum ajmaʿeena Say - For Allah's is the final argument - Had He willed He could indeed have guided all of you.
150 qul halumma šuhadāakumu allāḏeena yašhadoona anna allāha ḥarrama haṯa fa-in šahidoo falā tašhad maʿahum walā tattabiʿ ahwāa allāḏeena kaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā waallaḏeena lā yu/minoona bial-āḫirati wahum birabbihim yaʿdiloona Say: Come, bring your witnesses who can bear witness that Allah forbade (all) this. And if they bear witness, do not thou bear witness with them. Follow thou not the whims of those who deny Our revelations, those who believe not in the Hereafter and deem (others) equal with their Lord.
151 qul taʿālaw atlu mā ḥarrama rabbukum ʿalaykum allā tušrikoo bihi šay-an wabialwālidayni iḥsānan walā taqtuloo awlādakum min imlāqin naḥnu narzuqukum wa-iyyāhum walā taqraboo alfawahiša mā ḏahara minhā wamā baṭana walā taqtuloo alnnafsa allātee ḥarrama allāhu illā bialḥaqqi ṯalikum wassakum bihi laʿallakum taʿqiloona Say: Come, I will recite unto you that which your Lord hath made a sacred duty for you: That ye ascribe no thing as partner unto Him and that ye do good to parents, and that ye slay not your children because of penury - We provide for you and for them - and that ye draw not nigh to lewd things whether open or concealed. And that ye slay not the life which Allah hath made sacred, save in the course of justice. This He hath command you, in order that ye may discern.
152 walā taqraboo māla alyateemi illā biallatee hiya aḥsanu ḥattā yabluġa ašuddahu waawfoo alkayla waalmeezāna bialqisṭi lā nukallifu nafsan illā wusʿahā wa-iṯa qultum faiʿdiloo walaw kāna ṯa qurbā wabiʿahdi allāhi awfoo ṯalikum wassakum bihi laʿallakum taḏakkaroona And approach not the wealth of the orphan save with that which is better, till he reach maturity. Give full measure and full weight, in justice. We task not any soul beyond its scope. And if ye give your word, do justice thereunto, even though it be (against) a kinsman; and fulfil the covenant of Allah. This He commandeth you that haply ye may remember.
153 waanna haṯa ṣiratee mustaqeeman faittabiʿoohu walā tattabiʿoo alssubula fatafarraqa bikum ʿan sabeelihi ṯalikum wassakum bihi laʿallakum tattaqoona And (He commandeth you, saying): This is My straight path, so follow it. Follow not other ways, lest ye be parted from His way. This hath He ordained for you, that ye may ward off (evil).
154 thumma ātaynā moosā alkitāba tamāman ʿalā allāḏee aḥsana watafṣeelan likulli šay-in wahudan waraḥmatan laʿallahum biliqā-i rabbihim yu/minoona Again, We gave the Scripture unto Moses, complete for him who would do good, an explanation of all things, a guidance and a mercy, that they might believe in the meeting with their Lord.
155 wahaṯa kitābun anzalnāhu mubārakun faittabiʿoohu waittaqoo laʿallakum turḥamoona And this is a blessed Scripture which We have revealed. So follow it and ward off (evil), that ye may find mercy.
156 an taqooloo innamā onzila alkitābu ʿalā ta-ifatayni min qablinā wa-in kunnā ʿan dirāsatihim laġāfileena Lest ye should say: The Scripture was revealed only to two sects before us, and we in sooth were unaware of what they read;
157 aw taqooloo law annā onzila ʿalaynā alkitābu lakunnā ahdā minhum faqad jāakum bayyinatun min rabbikum wahudan waraḥmatun faman aḏlamu mimman kaṯṯaba bi-āyāti allāhi waṣadafa ʿanhā sanajzee allāḏeena yaṣdifoona ʿan āyātinā soo-a alʿaṯabi bimā kānoo yaṣdifoona Or lest ye should say: If the Scripture had been revealed unto us, we surely had been better guided than are they. Now hath there come unto you a clear proof from your Lord, a guidance and mercy; and who doeth greater wrong than he who denieth the revelations of Allah, and turneth away from them ? We award unto those who turn away from Our revelations an evil doom because of their aversion.
158 hal yanḏuroona illā an ta/tiyahumu almalā-ikatu aw ya/tiya rabbuka aw ya/tiya baʿḍu āyāti rabbika yawma ya/tee baʿḍu āyāti rabbika lā yanfaʿu nafsan eemānuhā lam takun āmanat min qablu aw kasabat fee eemānihā ḫayran quli intaḏiroo innā muntaḏiroona Wait they, indeed, for nothing less than that the angels should come unto them, or thy Lord should come, or there should come one of the portents from thy Lord ? In the day when one of the portents from thy Lord cometh, its belief availeth naught a soul which theretofore believed not, nor in its belief earned good (by works). Say: Wait ye! Lo! We (too) are waiting.
159 inna allāḏeena farraqoo deenahum wakānoo šiyaʿan lasta minhum fee šay-in innamā amruhum ilā allāhi ṯumma yunabbi-ohum bimā kānoo yafʿaloona Lo! As for those who sunder their religion and become schismatics, no concern at all hast thou with them. Their case will go to Allah, Who then will tell them what they used to do.
160 man jāa bialḥasanati falahu ʿašru amṯālihā waman jāa bialssayyi-āti falā yujzā illā miṯlahā wahum lā yuḏlamoona Whoso bringeth a good deed will receive tenfold the like thereof, while whoso bringeth an ill-deed will be awarded but the like thereof; and they will not be wronged.
161 qul innanee hadānee rabbee ilā ṣiratin mustaqeemin deenan qiyaman millata ibrāheema ḥaneefan wamā kāna mina almušrikeena Say: Lo! As for me, my Lord hath guided me unto a straight path, a right religion, the community of Abraham, the upright, who was no idolater.
162 qul inna ṣalātee wanusukee wamaḥyāya wamamātee lillāhi rabbi alʿālameena Say: Lo! my worship and my sacrifice and my living and my dying are for Allah, Lord of the Worlds.
163 lā šareeka lahu wabiṯalika omirtu waanā awwalu almuslimeena He hath no partner. This am I commanded, and I am first of those who surrender (unto Him).
164 qul aġayra allāhi abġee rabban wahuwa rabbu kulli šay-in walā taksibu kullu nafsin illā ʿalayhā walā taziru wāziratun wizra oḫrā ṯumma ilā rabbikum marjiʿukum fayunabbi-okum bimā kuntum feehi taḫtalifoona Say: Shall I seek another than Allah for Lord, when He is Lord of all things ? Each soul earneth only on its own account, nor doth any laden bear another's load. Then unto your Lord is your return and He will tell you that wherein ye differed.
165 wahuwa allāḏee jaʿalakum ḫalā-ifa al-arḍi warafaʿa baʿḍakum fawqa baʿḍin darajātin liyabluwakum fee mā ātākum inna rabbaka sareeʿu alʿiqābi wa-innahu laġafoorun raḥeemun [Could not extract English for verse 165]
