77. al-Mursalat: The Emissaries

001 waalmursalāti ʿurfān By the emissary winds, (sent) one after another
002 faalʿasifāti ʿaṣfān By the raging hurricanes,
003 waalnnāširāti našrān By those which cause earth's vegetation to revive;
004 faalfāriqāti farqān By those who winnow with a winnowing,
005 faalmulqiyāti ḏikrān By those who bring down the Reminder,
006 ʿuḏran aw nuḏran To excuse or to warn,
007 innamā tooʿadoona lawāqiʿun Surely that which ye are promised will befall.
008 fa-iṯa alnnujoomu ṭumisat So when the stars are put out,
009 wa-iṯa alssamāo furijat And when the sky is riven asunder,
010 wa-iṯa aljibālu nusifat And when the mountains are blown away,
011 wa-iṯa alrrusulu oqqitat And when the messengers are brought unto their time appointed -
012 li-ayyi yawmin ojjilat For what day is the time appointed ?
013 liyawmi alfaṣli For the Day of Decision.
014 wamā adrāka mā yawmu alfaṣli And what will convey unto thee what the Day of Decision is! -
015 waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena Woe unto the repudiators on that day!
016 alam nuhliki al-awwaleena Destroyed We not the former folk,
017 thumma nutbiʿuhumu al-āḫireena Then caused the latter folk to follow after ?
018 kaṯalika nafʿalu bialmujrimeena Thus deal We ever with the guilty.
019 waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena Woe unto the repudiators on that day!
020 alam naḫluqkum min mā-in maheenin Did We not create you from a base fluid
021 fajaʿalnāhu fee qarārin makeenin Which We laid up in a safe abode
022 ilā qadarin maʿloomin For a known term ?
023 faqadarnā faniʿma alqādiroona Thus We arranged. How excellent is Our arranging!
024 waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena Woe unto the repudiators on that day!
025 alam najʿali al-arḍa kifātān Have We not made the earth a receptacle
026 aḥyāan waamwātān Both for the living and the dead,
027 wajaʿalnā feehā rawāsiya šāmiḫātin waasqaynākum māan furātān And placed therein high mountains and given you to drink sweet water therein ?
028 waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena Woe unto the repudiators on that day!
029 inṭaliqoo ilā mā kuntum bihi tukaṯṯiboona (It will be said unto them:) Depart unto that (doom) which ye used to deny;
030 inṭaliqoo ilā ḏillin ḏee ṯalāṯi šuʿabin Depart unto the shadow falling threefold,
031 lā ḏaleelin walā yuġnee mina allāhabi (Which yet is) no relief nor shelter from the flame.
032 innahā tarmee bišararin kaalqaṣri Lo! it throweth up sparks like the castles,
033 kaannahu jimālatun ṣufrun (Or) as it might be camels of bright yellow hue.
034 waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena Woe unto the repudiators on that day!
035 haṯa yawmu lā yanṭiqoona This is a day wherein they speak not,
036 walā yu/ḏanu lahum fayaʿtaḏiroona Nor are they suffered to put forth excuses.
037 waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena Woe unto the repudiators on that day!
038 haṯa yawmu alfaṣli jamaʿnākum waal-awwaleena This is the Day of Decision, We have brought you and the men of old together.
039 fa-in kāna lakum kaydun fakeedooni If now ye have any wit, outwit Me.
040 waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena Woe unto the repudiators on that day!
041 inna almuttaqeena fee ḏilālin waʿuyoonin Lo! those who kept their duty are amid shade and fountains,
042 wafawākiha mimmā yaštahoona And fruits such as they desire.
043 kuloo waišraboo hanee-an bimā kuntum taʿmaloona (Unto them it is said:) Eat, drink and welcome, O ye blessed, in return for what ye did.
044 innā kaṯalika najzee almuḥsineena Thus do We reward the good.
045 waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena Woe unto the repudiators on that day!
046 kuloo watamattaʿoo qaleelan innakum mujrimoona Eat and take your ease (on earth) a little. Lo! ye are guilty.
047 waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena Woe unto the repudiators on that day!
048 wa-iṯa qeela lahumu irkaʿoo lā yarkaʿoona When it is said unto them: Bow down, they bow not down!
049 waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena Woe unto the repudiators on that day!
