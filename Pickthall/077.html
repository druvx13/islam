77. al-Mursalat: The Emissaries

1 
waalmursalāti ʿurfān
By the emissary winds, (sent) one after another

2 
faalʿasifāti ʿaṣfān
By the raging hurricanes,

3 
waalnnāširāti našrān
By those which cause earth's vegetation to revive;

4 
faalfāriqāti farqān
By those who winnow with a winnowing,

5 
faalmulqiyāti ḏikrān
By those who bring down the Reminder,

6 
ʿuḏran aw nuḏran
To excuse or to warn,

7 
innamā tooʿadoona lawāqiʿun
Surely that which ye are promised will befall.

8 
fa-iṯa alnnujoomu ṭumisat
So when the stars are put out,

9 
wa-iṯa alssamāo furijat
And when the sky is riven asunder,

10 
wa-iṯa aljibālu nusifat
And when the mountains are blown away,

11 
wa-iṯa alrrusulu oqqitat
And when the messengers are brought unto their time appointed -

12 
li-ayyi yawmin ojjilat
For what day is the time appointed ?

13 
liyawmi alfaṣli
For the Day of Decision.

14 
wamā adrāka mā yawmu alfaṣli
And what will convey unto thee what the Day of Decision is! -

15 
waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena
Woe unto the repudiators on that day!

16 
alam nuhliki al-awwaleena
Destroyed We not the former folk,

17 
thumma nutbiʿuhumu al-āḫireena
Then caused the latter folk to follow after ?

18 
kaṯalika nafʿalu bialmujrimeena
Thus deal We ever with the guilty.

19 
waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena
Woe unto the repudiators on that day!

20 
alam naḫluqkum min mā-in maheenin
Did We not create you from a base fluid

21 
fajaʿalnāhu fee qarārin makeenin
Which We laid up in a safe abode

22 
ilā qadarin maʿloomin
For a known term ?

23 
faqadarnā faniʿma alqādiroona
Thus We arranged. How excellent is Our arranging!

24 
waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena
Woe unto the repudiators on that day!

25 
alam najʿali al-arḍa kifātān
Have We not made the earth a receptacle

26 
aḥyāan waamwātān
Both for the living and the dead,

27 
wajaʿalnā feehā rawāsiya šāmiḫātin waasqaynākum māan furātān
And placed therein high mountains and given you to drink sweet water therein ?

28 
waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena
Woe unto the repudiators on that day!

29 
inṭaliqoo ilā mā kuntum bihi tukaṯṯiboona
(It will be said unto them:) Depart unto that (doom) which ye used to deny;

30 
inṭaliqoo ilā ḏillin ḏee ṯalāṯi šuʿabin
Depart unto the shadow falling threefold,

31 
lā ḏaleelin walā yuġnee mina allāhabi
(Which yet is) no relief nor shelter from the flame.

32 
innahā tarmee bišararin kaalqaṣri
Lo! it throweth up sparks like the castles,

33 
kaannahu jimālatun ṣufrun
(Or) as it might be camels of bright yellow hue.

34 
waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena
Woe unto the repudiators on that day!

35 
haṯa yawmu lā yanṭiqoona
This is a day wherein they speak not,

36 
walā yu/ḏanu lahum fayaʿtaḏiroona
Nor are they suffered to put forth excuses.

37 
waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena
Woe unto the repudiators on that day!

38 
haṯa yawmu alfaṣli jamaʿnākum waal-awwaleena
This is the Day of Decision, We have brought you and the men of old together.

39 
fa-in kāna lakum kaydun fakeedooni
If now ye have any wit, outwit Me.

40 
waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena
Woe unto the repudiators on that day!

41 
inna almuttaqeena fee ḏilālin waʿuyoonin
Lo! those who kept their duty are amid shade and fountains,

42 
wafawākiha mimmā yaštahoona
And fruits such as they desire.

43 
kuloo waišraboo hanee-an bimā kuntum taʿmaloona
(Unto them it is said:) Eat, drink and welcome, O ye blessed, in return for what ye did.

44 
innā kaṯalika najzee almuḥsineena
Thus do We reward the good.

45 
waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena
Woe unto the repudiators on that day!

46 
kuloo watamattaʿoo qaleelan innakum mujrimoona
Eat and take your ease (on earth) a little. Lo! ye are guilty.

47 
waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena
Woe unto the repudiators on that day!

48 
wa-iṯa qeela lahumu irkaʿoo lā yarkaʿoona
When it is said unto them: Bow down, they bow not down!

49 
waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena
Woe unto the repudiators on that day!

50 
fabi-ayyi ḥadeeṯin baʿdahu yu/minoona
In what statement, after this, will they believe ?
