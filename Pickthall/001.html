1. al-Fatihah: The Opening

001 bismi allāhi alrraḥmāni alrraḥeemi In the name of Allah, the Beneficent, the Merciful.
002 alḥamdu lillāhi rabbi alʿālameena Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds,
003 alrraḥmāni alrraḥeemi The Beneficent, the Merciful.
004 māliki yawmi alddeeni Master of the Day of Judgment,
005 iyyāka naʿbudu wa-iyyāka nastaʿeenu Thee (alone) we worship; Thee (alone) we ask for help.
006 ihdinā alṣirata almustaqeema Show us the straight path,
7 
ṣirata allāḏeena anʿamta ʿalayhim ġayri almaġḍoobi ʿalayhim walā alddalleena
The path of those whom Thou hast favoured; Not the (path) of those who earn Thine anger nor of those who go astray.
