66. at-Tahrim: The Prohibition

001 yā ayyuhā alnnabiyyu lima tuḥarrimu mā aḥalla allāhu laka tabtaġee mardata azwājika waallāhu ġafoorun raḥeemun O Prophet! Why bannest thou that which Allah hath made lawful for thee, seeking to please thy wives ? And Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.
002 qad faraḍa allāhu lakum taḥillata aymānikum waallāhu mawlākum wahuwa alʿaleemu alḥakeemu Allah hath made lawful for you (Muslims) absolution from your oaths (of such a kind), and Allah is your Protector. He is the Knower, the Wise.
003 wa-iḏ asarra alnnabiyyu ilā baʿḍi azwājihi ḥadeeṯan falammā nabbaat bihi waaḏharahu allāhu ʿalayhi ʿarrafa baʿḍahu waaʿraḍa ʿan baʿḍin falammā nabbaahā bihi qālat man anbaaka haṯa qāla nabbaaniya alʿaleemu alḫabeeru When the Prophet confided a fact unto one of his wives and when she afterward divulged it and Allah apprised him thereof, he made known (to her) part thereof and passed over part. And when he told it her she said: Who hath told thee ? He said: The Knower, the Aware hath told me.
004 in tatoobā ilā allāhi faqad ṣaġat quloobukumā wa-in taḏāharā ʿalayhi fa-inna allāha huwa mawlāhu wajibreelu wasaliḥu almu/mineena waalmalā-ikatu baʿda ṯalika ḏaheerun If ye twain turn unto Allah repentant, (ye have cause to do so) for your hearts desired (the ban); and if ye aid one another against him (Muhammad) then lo! Allah, even He, is his Protecting Friend, and Gabriel and the righteous among the believers; and furthermore the angels are his helpers.
005 ʿasā rabbuhu in ṭallaqakunna an yubdilahu azwājan ḫayran minkunna muslimātin mu/minātin qānitātin tā-ibātin ʿābidātin sā-ihatin ṯayyibātin waabkārān It may happen that his Lord, if he divorce you, will give him in your stead wives better than you, submissive (to Allah), believing, pious, penitent, devout, inclined to fasting, widows and maids.
006 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo qoo anfusakum waahleekum nāran waqooduhā alnnāsu waalḥijāratu ʿalayhā malā-ikatun ġilāḏun šidādun lā yaʿṣoona allāha mā amarahum wayafʿaloona mā yu/maroona O ye who believe! Ward off from yourselves and your families a Fire whereof the fuel is men and stones, over which are set angels strong, severe, who resist not Allah in that which He commandeth them, but do that which they are commanded.
007 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena kafaroo lā taʿtaḏiroo alyawma innamā tujzawna mā kuntum taʿmaloona (Then it will be said): O ye who disbelieve! Make no excuses for yourselves this day. Ye are only being paid for what ye used to do.
008 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo tooboo ilā allāhi tawbatan naṣooḥan ʿasā rabbukum an yukaffira ʿankum sayyi-ātikum wayudḫilakum jannātin tajree min taḥtihā al-anhāru yawma lā yuḫzee allāhu alnnabiyya waallaḏeena āmanoo maʿahu nooruhum yasʿā bayna aydeehim wabi-aymānihim yaqooloona rabbanā atmim lanā nooranā waiġfir lanā innaka ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerun O ye who believe! Turn unto Allah in sincere repentance! It may be that your Lord will remit from you your evil deeds and bring you into Gardens underneath which rivers flow, on the day when Allah will not abase the Prophet and those who believe with him. Their light will run before them and on their right hands; they will say: Our Lord! Perfect our light for us, and forgive us! Lo! Thou art Able to do all things.
009 yā ayyuhā alnnabiyyu jāhidi alkuffāra waalmunāfiqeena waoġluḏ ʿalayhim wama/wāhum jahannamu wabi/sa almaṣeeru O Prophet! Strive against the disbelievers and the hypocrites, and be stern with them. Hell will be their home, a hapless journey's end.
010 [romanization missing] Allah citeth an example for those who disbelieve: the wife of Noah and the wife of Lot, who were under two of Our righteous slaves yet betrayed them so that they (the husbands) availed them naught against Allah and it was said (unto them): Enter the Fire along with those who enter.
011 waḍaraba allāhu maṯalan lillaḏeena āmanoo imraata firʿawna iḏ qālat rabbi ibni lee ʿindaka baytan fee aljannati wanajjinee min firʿawna waʿamalihi wanajjinee mina alqawmi alṯṯālimeena And Allah citeth an example for those who believe: the wife of Pharaoh when she said: My Lord! Build for me a home with thee in the Garden, and deliver me from Pharaoh and his work, and deliver me from evil-doing folk;
12 wamaryama ibnata ʿimrāna allātee ahsanat farjahā fanafaḫnā feehi min rooḥinā waṣaddaqat bikalimāti rabbihā wakutubihi wakānat mina alqāniteena [Could not extract English for verse 12]
