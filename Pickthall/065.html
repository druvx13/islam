65. at-Talaq: The Divorce

001 yā ayyuhā alnnabiyyu iṯa ṭallaqtumu alnnisāa faṭalliqoohunna liʿiddatihinna waahsoo alʿiddata waittaqoo allāha rabbakum lā tuḫrijoohunna min buyootihinna walā yaḫrujna illā an ya/teena bifahišatin mubayyinatin watilka ḥudoodu allāhi waman yataʿadda ḥudooda allāhi faqad ḏalama nafsahu lā tadree laʿalla allāha yuḥdiṯu baʿda ṯalika amrān O Prophet! When ye (men) put away women, put them away for their (legal) period and reckon the period, and keep your duty to Allah, your Lord. Expel them not from their houses nor let them go forth unless they commit open immorality. Such are the limits (imposed by) Allah; and whoso transgresseth Allah's limits, he verily wrongeth his soul. Thou knowest not: it may be that Allah will afterward bring some new thing to pass.
002 fa-iṯa balaġna ajalahunna faamsikoohunna bimaʿroofin aw fāriqoohunna bimaʿroofin waašhidoo ḏaway ʿadlin minkum waaqeemoo alššahādata lillāhi ṯalikum yooʿaḏu bihi man kāna yu/minu biallāhi waalyawmi al-āḫiri waman yattaqi allāha yajʿal lahu maḫrajān Then, when they have reached their term, take them back in kindness or part from them in kindness, and call to witness two just men among you, and keep your testimony upright for Allah. Whoso believeth in Allah and the Last Day is exhorted to act thus. And whosoever keepeth his duty to Allah, Allah will appoint a way out for him,
003 wayarzuqhu min ḥayṯu lā yaḥtasibu waman yatawakkal ʿalā allāhi fahuwa ḥasbuhu inna allāha bāliġu amrihi qad jaʿala allāhu likulli šay-in qadrān And will provide for him from (a quarter) whence he hath no expectation. And whosoever putteth his trust in Allah, He will suffice him. Lo! Allah bringeth His command to pass. Allah hath set a measure for all things.
004 waallā-ee ya-isna mina almaḥeeḍi min nisā-ikum ini irtabtum faʿiddatuhunna ṯalāṯatu ašhurin waallā-ee lam yaḥiḍna waolātu al-aḥmāli ajaluhunna an yaḍaʿna ḥamlahunna waman yattaqi allāha yajʿal lahu min amrihi yusrān And for such of your women as despair of menstruation, if ye doubt, their period (of waiting) shall be three months, along with those who have it not. And for those with child, their period shall be till they bring forth their burden. And whosoever keepeth his duty to Allah, He maketh his course easy for him.
005 [romanization missing] That is the commandment of Allah which He revealeth unto you. And whoso keepeth his duty to Allah, He will remit from him his evil deeds and magnify reward for him.
006 askinoohunna min ḥayṯu sakantum min wujdikum walā tudarroohunna lituḍayyiqoo ʿalayhinna wa-in kunna olāti ḥamlin faanfiqoo ʿalayhinna ḥattā yaḍaʿna ḥamlahunna fa-in arḍaʿna lakum faātoohunna ojoorahunna wa/tamiroo baynakum bimaʿroofin wa-in taʿāsartum fasaturḍiʿu lahu oḫrā Lodge them where ye dwell, according to your wealth, and harass them not so as to straiten life for them. And if they are with child, then spend for them till they bring forth their burden. Then, if they give suck for you, give them their due payment and consult together in kindness; but if ye make difficulties for one another, then let some other woman give suck for him (the father of the child).
007 liyunfiq ḏoo saʿatin min saʿatihi waman qudira ʿalayhi rizquhu falyunfiq mimmā ātāhu allāhu lā yukallifu allāhu nafsan illā mā ātāhā sayajʿalu allāhu baʿda ʿusrin yusrān Let him who hath abundance spend of his abundance, and he whose provision is measured, let him spend of that which Allah hath given him. Allah asketh naught of any soul save that which He hath given it. Allah will vouchsafe, after hardship, ease.
008 wakaayyin min qaryatin ʿatat ʿan amri rabbihā warusulihi fahasabnāhā ḥisāban šadeedan waʿaṯṯabnāhā ʿaṯaban nukrān And how many a community revolted against the ordinance of its Lord and His messengers, and We called it to a stern account and punished it with dire punishment,
009 faṯaqat wabāla amrihā wakāna ʿāqibatu amrihā ḫusrān So that it tasted the ill-effects of its conduct, and the consequence of its conduct was loss.
010 aʿadda allāhu lahum ʿaṯaban šadeedan faittaqoo allāha yā olee al-albābi allāḏeena āmanoo qad anzala allāhu ilaykum ḏikrān Allah hath prepared for them stern punishment; so keep your duty to Allah, O men of understanding! O ye who believe! Now Allah hath sent down unto you a reminder,
011 rasoolan yatloo ʿalaykum āyāti allāhi mubayyinātin liyuḫrija allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati mina alṯṯulumāti ilā alnnoori waman yu/min biallāhi wayaʿmal saliḥan yudḫilhu jannātin tajree min taḥtihā al-anhāru ḫālideena feehā abadan qad aḥsana allāhu lahu rizqān A messenger reciting unto you the revelations of Allah made plain, that He may bring forth those who believe and do good works from darkness unto light. And whosoever believeth in Allah and doeth right, He will bring him into Gardens underneath which rivers flow, therein to abide for ever. Allah hath made good provision for him.
12 
allāhu allāḏee ḫalaqa sabʿa samāwātin wamina al-arḍi miṯlahunna yatanazzalu al-amru baynahunna litaʿlamoo anna allāha ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerun waanna allāha qad ahata bikulli šay-in ʿilmān
Allah it is who hath created seven heavens, and of the earth the like thereof. The commandment cometh down among them slowly, that ye may know that Allah is Able to do all things, and that Allah surroundeth all things in knowledge.
