14. Ibrahim: Abraham

001 alif-lām-rā kitābun anzalnāhu ilayka lituḫrija alnnāsa mina alṯṯulumāti ilā alnnoori bi-iḏni rabbihim ilā ṣirati alʿazeezi alḥameedi Alif. Lam. Ra. (This is) a Scripture which We have revealed unto thee (Muhammad) that thereby thou mayst bring forth mankind from darkness unto light, by the permission of their Lord, unto the path of the Mighty, the Owner of Praise,
002 allāhi allāḏee lahu mā fee alssamāwāti wamā fee al-arḍi wawaylun lilkāfireena min ʿaṯabin šadeedin Allah, unto Whom belongeth whatsoever is in the heavens and whatsoever is in the earth. and woe unto the disbelievers from an awful doom;
003 allāḏeena yastaḥibboona alḥayāta alddunyā ʿalā al-āḫirati wayaṣuddoona ʿan sabeeli allāhi wayabġoonahā ʿiwajan olā-ika fee ḍalālin baʿeedin Those who love the life of the world more than the Hereafter, and debar (men) from the way of Allah and would have it crooked: such are far astray.
004 wamā arsalnā min rasoolin illā bilisāni qawmihi liyubayyina lahum fayuḍillu allāhu man yašāo wayahdee man yašāo wahuwa alʿazeezu alḥakeemu And We never sent a messenger save with the language of his folk, that he might make (the message) clear for them. Then Allah sendeth whom He will astray, and guideth whom He will. He is the Mighty, the Wise.
005 walaqad arsalnā moosā bi-āyātinā an aḫrij qawmaka mina alṯṯulumāti ilā alnnoori waḏakkirhum bi-ayyāmi allāhi inna fee ṯalika laāyātin likulli ṣabbārin šakoorin We verily sent Moses with Our revelations, saying: Bring thy people forth from darkness unto light. And remind them of the days of Allah. Lo! therein are revelations for each steadfast, thankful (heart).
006 wa-iḏ qāla moosā liqawmihi oḏkuroo niʿmata allāhi ʿalaykum iḏ anjākum min āli firʿawna yasoomoonakum soo-a alʿaṯabi wayuḏabbiḥoona abnāakum wayastaḥyoona nisāakum wafee ṯalikum balāon min rabbikum ʿaḏeemun And (remind them) how Moses said unto his people: Remember Allah's favour unto you when He delivered you from Pharaoh's folk who were afflicting you with dreadful torment, and were slaying your sons and sparing your women; that was a tremendous trial from your Lord.
007 wa-iḏ taaṯṯana rabbukum la-in šakartum laazeedannakum wala-in kafartum inna ʿaṯabee lašadeedun And when your Lord proclaimed: If ye give thanks, I will give you more; but if ye are thankless, lo! My punishment is dire.
008 waqāla moosā in takfuroo antum waman fee al-arḍi jameeʿan fa-inna allāha laġaniyyun ḥameedun And Moses said: Though ye and all who are in the earth prove thankless, lo! Allah verily is Absolute, Owner of Praise.
009 alam ya/tikum nabao allāḏeena min qablikum qawmi nooḥin waʿādin waṯamooda waallaḏeena min baʿdihim lā yaʿlamuhum illā allāhu jāat-hum rusuluhum bialbayyināti faraddoo aydiyahum fee afwāhihim waqāloo innā kafarnā bimā orsiltum bihi wa-innā lafee šakkin mimmā tadʿoonanā ilayhi mureebun Hath not the history of those before you reached you: the folk of Noah, and (the tribes of) A'ad and Thamud, and those after them ? None save Allah knoweth them. Their messengers came unto them with clear proofs, but they thrust their hands into their mouths, and said: Lo! we disbelieve in that wherewith ye have been sent, and lo! we are in grave doubt concerning that to which ye call us.
010 qālat rusuluhum afee allāhi šakkun fatiri alssamāwāti waal-arḍi yadʿookum liyaġfira lakum min ḏunoobikum wayu-aḫḫirakum ilā ajalin musamman qāloo in antum illā bašarun miṯlunā tureedoona an taṣuddoonā ʿammā kāna yaʿbudu ābāonā fa/toonā bisultanin mubeenin Their messengers said: Can there be doubt concerning Allah, the Creator of the heavens and the earth ? He calleth you that He may forgive you your sins and reprieve you unto an appointed term. They said: Ye are but mortals like us, who would fain turn us away from what our fathers used to worship. Then bring some clear warrant.
011 qālat lahum rusuluhum in naḥnu illā bašarun miṯlukum walākinna allāha yamunnu ʿalā man yašāo min ʿibādihi wamā kāna lanā an na/tiyakum bisultanin illā bi-iḏni allāhi waʿalā allāhi falyatawakkali almu/minoona Their messengers said unto them: We are but mortals like you, but Allah giveth grace unto whom He will of His slaves. It is not ours to bring you a warrant unless by the permission of Allah. In Allah let believers put their trust!
012 wamā lanā allā natawakkala ʿalā allāhi waqad hadānā subulanā walanaṣbiranna ʿalā mā aṯaytumoonā waʿalā allāhi falyatawakkali almutawakkiloona How should we not put our trust in Allah when He hath shown us our ways ? We surely will endure the hurt ye do us. In Allah let the trusting put their trust.
013 waqāla allāḏeena kafaroo lirusulihim lanuḫrijannakum min arḍinā aw lataʿoodunna fee millatinā faawha ilayhim rabbuhum lanuhlikanna alṯṯālimeena And those who disbelieved said unto their messengers: Verily we will drive you out from our land, unless ye return to our religion. Then their Lord inspired them, (saying): Verily we shall destroy the wrong-doers,
014 walanuskinannakumu al-arḍa min baʿdihim ṯalika liman ḫāfa maqāmee waḫāfa waʿeedi And verily We shall make you to dwell in the land after them. This is for him who feareth My Majesty and feareth My threats.
015 waistaftaḥoo waḫāba kullu jabbārin ʿaneedin And they sought help (from their Lord) and every froward potentate was bought to naught;
016 min warā-ihi jahannamu wayusqā min mā-in ṣadeedin Hell is before him, and he is made to drink a festering water,
017 yatajarraʿuhu walā yakādu yuseeġuhu waya/teehi almawtu min kulli makānin wamā huwa bimayyitin wamin warā-ihi ʿaṯabun ġaleeḏun Which he sippeth but can hardly swallow, and death cometh unto him from every side while yet he cannot die, and before him is a harsh doom.
018 maṯalu allāḏeena kafaroo birabbihim aʿmāluhum karamādin ištaddat bihi alrreeḥu fee yawmin ʿasifin lā yaqdiroona mimmā kasaboo ʿalā šay-in ṯalika huwa alḍalālu albaʿeedu A similitude of those who disbelieve in their Lord: Their works are as ashes which the wind bloweth hard upon a stormy day. They have no control of aught that they have earned. That is the extreme failure.
019 alam tara anna allāha ḫalaqa alssamāwāti waal-arḍa bialḥaqqi in yaša/ yuḏhibkum waya/ti biḫalqin jadeedin Hast thou not seen that Allah hath created the heavens and the earth with truth ? If He will, He can remove you and bring (in) some new creation;
020 wamā ṯalika ʿalā allāhi biʿazeezin And that is no great matter for Allah.
021 wabarazoo lillāhi jameeʿan faqāla alḍuʿafāo lillaḏeena istakbaroo innā kunnā lakum tabaʿan fahal antum muġnoona ʿannā min ʿaṯabi allāhi min šay-in qāloo law hadānā allāhu lahadaynākum sawāon ʿalaynā ajaziʿnā am ṣabarnā mā lanā min maḥeeṣin They all come forth unto their Lord. Then those who were despised say unto those who were scornful: We were unto you a following, can ye then avert from us aught of Allah's doom ? They say: Had Allah guided us, we should have guided you. Whether we rage or patiently endure is (now) all one for us; we have no place of refuge.
022 waqāla alššaytanu lammā quḍiya al-amru inna allāha waʿadakum waʿda alḥaqqi wawaʿadtukum faaḫlaftukum wamā kāna liya ʿalaykum min sultanin illā an daʿawtukum faistajabtum lee falā taloomoonee waloomoo anfusakum mā anā bimuṣriḫikum wamā antum bimuṣriḫiyya innee kafartu bimā ašraktumooni min qablu inna alṯṯālimeena lahum ʿaṯabun aleemun And Satan saith, when the matter hath been decided: Lo! Allah promised you a promise of truth; and I promised you, then failed you. And I had no power over you save that I called unto you and ye obeyed me. So blame not, but blame yourselves. I cannot help you, nor can ye help me, Lo! I disbelieved in that which ye before ascribed to me. Lo! for wrong-doers is a painful doom.
023 waodḫila allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati jannātin tajree min taḥtihā al-anhāru ḫālideena feehā bi-iḏni rabbihim taḥiyyatuhum feehā salāmun And those who believed and did good works are made to enter Gardens underneath which rivers flow, therein abiding by permission of their Lord, their greeting therein: Peace!
024 alam tara kayfa ḍaraba allāhu maṯalan kalimatan ṭayyibatan kašajaratin ṭayyibatin aṣluhā ṯābitun wafarʿuhā fee alssamā/-i Seest thou not how Allah coineth a similitude: A goodly saying, as a goodly tree, its root set firm, its branches reaching into heaven,
025 tu/tee okulahā kulla ḥeenin bi-iḏni rabbihā wayaḍribu allāhu al-amṯāla lilnnāsi laʿallahum yataḏakkaroona Giving its fruit at every season by permission of its Lord ? Allah coineth the similitudes for mankind in order that they may reflect.
026 wamaṯalu kalimatin ḫabeeṯatin kašajaratin ḫabeeṯatin ijtuṯṯat min fawqi al-arḍi mā lahā min qarārin And the similitude of a bad saying is as a bad tree, uprooted from upon the earth, possessing no stability.
027 yuṯabbitu allāhu allāḏeena āmanoo bialqawli alṯṯābiti fee alḥayāti alddunyā wafee al-āḫirati wayuḍillu allāhu alṯṯālimeena wayafʿalu allāhu mā yašā/o Allah confirmeth those who believe by a firm saying in the life of the world and in the Hereafter, and Allah sendeth wrong-doers astray. And Allah doeth what He will.
028 alam tara ilā allāḏeena baddaloo niʿmata allāhi kufran waaḥalloo qawmahum dāra albawāri Hast thou not seen those who gave the grace of Allah in exchange for thanklessness and led their people down to the Abode of Loss,
029 jahannama yaṣlawnahā wabi/sa alqarāru (Even to) hell ? They are exposed thereto. A hapless end!
030 wajaʿaloo lillāhi andādan liyuḍilloo ʿan sabeelihi qul tamattaʿoo fa-inna maṣeerakum ilā alnnāri And they set up rivals to Allah that they may mislead (men) from His way. Say: Enjoy life (while ye may) for lo! your journey's end will be the Fire.
031 qul liʿibādiya allāḏeena āmanoo yuqeemoo alṣalāta wayunfiqoo mimmā razaqnāhum sirran waʿalāniyatan min qabli an ya/tiya yawmun lā bayʿun feehi walā ḫilālun Tell My bondmen who believe to establish worship and spend of that which We have given them, secretly and publicly, before a day cometh wherein there will be neither traffick nor befriending.
032 allāhu allāḏee ḫalaqa alssamāwāti waal-arḍa waanzala mina alssamā-i māan faaḫraja bihi mina alṯṯamarāti rizqan lakum wasaḫḫara lakumu alfulka litajriya fee albaḥri bi-amrihi wasaḫḫara lakumu al-anhāra Allah is He Who created the heavens and the earth, and causeth water to descend from the sky, thereby producing fruits as food for you, and maketh the ships to be of service unto you, that they may run upon the sea at His command, and hath made of service unto you the rivers;
033 wasaḫḫara lakumu alššamsa waalqamara dā-ibayni wasaḫḫara lakumu allāyla waalnnahāra And maketh the sun and the moon, constant in their courses, to be of service unto you, and hath made of service unto you the night and the day.
034 waātākum min kulli mā saaltumoohu wa-in taʿuddoo niʿmata allāhi lā tuhsoohā inna al-insāna laḏaloomun kaffārun And He giveth you of all ye ask of Him, and if ye would count the bounty of Allah ye cannot reckon it. Lo! man is verily a wrong-doer, an ingrate.
035 wa-iḏ qāla ibrāheemu rabbi ijʿal haṯa albalada āminan waojnubnee wabaniyya an naʿbuda al-aṣnāma And when Abraham said: My Lord! Make safe this territory, and preserve me and my sons from serving idols.
036 rabbi innahunna aḍlalna kaṯeeran mina alnnāsi faman tabiʿanee fa-innahu minnee waman ʿasanee fa-innaka ġafoorun raḥeemun My Lord! Lo! they have led many of mankind astray. But whoso followeth me, he verily is of me. And whoso disobeyeth me - Still Thou art Forgiving, Merciful.
037 rabbanā innee askantu min ḏurriyyatee biwādin ġayri ḏee zarʿin ʿinda baytika almuḥarrami rabbanā liyuqeemoo alṣalāta faijʿal af-idatan mina alnnāsi tahwee ilayhim waorzuqhum mina alṯṯamarāti laʿallahum yaškuroona Our Lord! Lo! I have settled some of my posterity in an uncultivable valley near unto Thy holy House, our Lord! that they may establish proper worship; so incline some hearts of men that they may yearn toward them, and provide Thou them with fruits in order that they may be thankful.
038 rabbanā innaka taʿlamu mā nuḫfee wamā nuʿlinu wamā yaḫfā ʿalā allāhi min šay-in fee al-arḍi walā fee alssamā/-i Our Lord! Lo! Thou knowest that which we hide and that which we proclaim. Nothing in the earth or in the heaven is hidden from Allah.
039 alḥamdu lillāhi allāḏee wahaba lee ʿalā alkibari ismāʿeela wa-ishaqa inna rabbee lasameeʿu aldduʿā/-i Praise be to Allah Who hath given me, in my old age, Ishmael and Isaac! Lo! my Lord is indeed the Hearer of Prayer.
040 rabbi ijʿalnee muqeema alṣalāti wamin ḏurriyyatee rabbanā wtaqabbal duʿā/-i My Lord! Make me to establish proper worship, and some of my posterity (also); our Lord! and accept my prayer.
041 rabbanā iġfir lee waliwālidayya walilmu/mineena yawma yaqoomu alḥisābu Our Lord! Forgive me and my parents and believers on the day when the account is cast.
042 walā taḥsabanna allāha ġāfilan ʿammā yaʿmalu alṯṯālimoona innamā yu-aḫḫiruhum liyawmin tašḫaṣu feehi al-absaru Deem not that Allah is unaware of what the wicked do. He but giveth them a respite till a day when eyes will stare (in terror),
043 muhṭiʿeena muqniʿee ruoosihim lā yartaddu ilayhim ṭarfuhum waaf-idatuhum hawā/on As they come hurrying on in fear, their heads upraised, their gaze returning not to them, and their hearts as air.
044 waanḏiri alnnāsa yawma ya/teehimu alʿaṯabu fayaqoolu allāḏeena ḏalamoo rabbanā aḫḫirnā ilā ajalin qareebin nujib daʿwataka wanattabiʿi alrrusula awa lam takoonoo aqsamtum min qablu mā lakum min zawālin And warn mankind of a day when the doom will come upon them, and those who did wrong will say: Our Lord! Reprieve us for a little while. We will obey Thy call and will follow the messengers. (It will be answered): Did ye not swear before that there would be no end for you ?
045 wasakantum fee masākini allāḏeena ḏalamoo anfusahum watabayyana lakum kayfa faʿalnā bihim waḍarabnā lakumu al-amṯāla And (have ye not) dwelt in the dwellings of those who wronged themselves (of old) and (hath it not) become plain to you how We dealt with them and made examples for you ?
046 waqad makaroo makrahum waʿinda allāhi makruhum wa-in kāna makruhum litazoola minhu aljibālu Verily they have plotted their plot, and their plot is with Allah, though their plot were one whereby the mountains should be moved.
047 falā taḥsabanna allāha muḫlifa waʿdihi rusulahu inna allāha ʿazeezun ḏoo intiqāmin So think not that Allah will fail to keep His promise to His messengers. Lo! Allah is Mighty, Able to Requite (the wrong).
048 yawma tubaddalu al-arḍu ġayra al-arḍi waalssamāwātu wabarazoo lillāhi alwahidi alqahhāri On the day when the earth will be changed to other than the earth, and the heavens (also will be changed) and they will come forth unto Allah, the One, the Almighty,
049 watarā almujrimeena yawma-iḏin muqarraneena fee al-aṣfādi Thou wilt see the guilty on that day linked together in chains,
050 sarābeeluhum min qaṭrānin wataġšā wujoohahumu alnnāru Their raiment of pitch, and the Fire covering their faces,
051 liyajziya allāhu kulla nafsin mā kasabat inna allāha sareeʿu alḥisābi That Allah may repay each soul what it hath earned. Lo! Allah is swift at reckoning.
52 haṯa balāġun lilnnāsi waliyunḏaroo bihi waliyaʿlamoo annamā huwa ilāhun wahidun waliyaṯṯakkara oloo al-albābi [Could not extract English for verse 52]
