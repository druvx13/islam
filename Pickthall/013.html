13. ar-Ra`d: The Thunder

001 alif-lām-meem-rā tilka āyātu alkitābi waallaḏee onzila ilayka min rabbika alḥaqqu walākinna akṯara alnnāsi lā yu/minoona Alif. Lam. Mim. Ra. These are verses of the Scripture. That which is revealed unto thee from thy Lord is the Truth, but most of mankind believe not.
002 allāhu allāḏee rafaʿa alssamāwāti biġayri ʿamadin tarawnahā ṯumma istawā ʿalā alʿarši wasaḫḫara alššamsa waalqamara kullun yajree li-ajalin musamman yudabbiru al-amra yufaṣilu al-āyāti laʿallakum biliqā-i rabbikum tooqinoona Allah it is Who raised up the heavens without visible supports, then mounted the Throne, and compelled the sun and the moon to be of service, each runneth unto an appointed term; He ordereth the course; He detaileth the revelations, that haply ye may be certain of the meeting with your Lord.
003 wahuwa allāḏee madda al-arḍa wajaʿala feehā rawāsiya waanhāran wamin kulli alṯṯamarāti jaʿala feehā zawjayni iṯnayni yuġšee allāyla alnnahāra inna fee ṯalika laāyātin liqawmin yatafakkaroona And He it is Who spread out the earth and placed therein firm hills and flowing streams, and of all fruits He placed therein two spouses (male and female). He covereth the night with the day. Lo! herein verily are portents for people who take thought.
004 wafee al-arḍi qiṭaʿun mutajāwirātun wajannātun min aʿnābin wazarʿun wanaḫeelun ṣinwānun waġayru ṣinwānin yusqā bimā-in wahidin wanufaḍilu baʿḍahā ʿalā baʿḍin fee alokuli inna fee ṯalika laāyātin liqawmin yaʿqiloona And in the Earth are neighbouring tracts, vineyards and ploughed lands, and date-palms, like and unlike, which are watered with one water. And we have made some of them to excel others in fruit. Lo! herein verily are portents for people who have sense.
005 wa-in taʿjab faʿajabun qawluhum a-iṯa kunnā turāban a-innā lafee ḫalqin jadeedin olā-ika allāḏeena kafaroo birabbihim waolā-ika al-aġlālu fee aʿnāqihim waolā-ika aṣ-habu alnnāri hum feehā ḫālidoona And if thou wonderest, then wondrous is their saying: When we are dust, are we then forsooth (to be raised) in a new creation ? Such are they who disbelieve in their Lord; such have carcans on their necks; such are rightful owners of the Fire, they will abide therein.
006 wayastaʿjiloonaka bialssayyi-ati qabla alḥasanati waqad ḫalat min qablihimu almaṯulātu wa-inna rabbaka laḏoo maġfiratin lilnnāsi ʿalā ḏulmihim wa-inna rabbaka lašadeedu alʿiqābi And they bid thee hasten on the evil rather than the good, when exemplary punishments have indeed occurred before them. But lo! thy Lord is rich in pardon for mankind despite their wrong, and lo! thy Lord is strong in punishment.
007 wayaqoolu allāḏeena kafaroo lawlā onzila ʿalayhi āyatun min rabbihi innamā anta munḏirun walikulli qawmin hādin Those who disbelieve say: If only some portent were sent down upon him from his Lord! Thou art a warner only, and for every folk a guide.
008 allāhu yaʿlamu mā taḥmilu kullu onṯā wamā taġeeḍu al-arhamu wamā tazdādu wakullu šay-in ʿindahu bimiqdārin Allah knoweth that which every female beareth and that which the wombs absorb and that which they grow. And everything with Him is measured.
009 ʿālimu alġaybi waalššahādati alkabeeru almutaʿāli He is the Knower of the Invisible and the Visible, the Great, the High Exalted.
010 sawāon minkum man asarra alqawla waman jahara bihi waman huwa mustaḫfin biallayli wasāribun bialnnahāri Alike of you is he who hideth the saying and he who noiseth it abroad, he who lurketh in the night and he who goeth freely in the daytime.
011 lahu muʿaqqibātun min bayni yadayhi wamin ḫalfihi yaḥfaḏoonahu min amri allāhi inna allāha lā yuġayyiru mā biqawmin ḥattā yuġayyiroo mā bi-anfusihim wa-iṯa arāda allāhu biqawmin soo-an falā maradda lahu wamā lahum min doonihi min wālin For him are angels ranged before him and behind him, who guard him by Allah's command. Lo! Allah changeth not the condition of a folk until they (first) change that which is in their hearts; and if Allah willeth misfortune for a folk there is none that can repel it, nor have they a defender beside Him.
012 huwa allāḏee yureekumu albarqa ḫawfan waṭamaʿan wayunši-o alssahaba alṯṯiqāla He it is Who showeth you the lightning, a fear and a hope, and raiseth the heavy clouds.
013 wayusabbiḥu alrraʿdu biḥamdihi waalmalā-ikatu min ḫeefatihi wayursilu alṣawāʿiqa fayuṣeebu bihā man yašāo wahum yujādiloona fee allāhi wahuwa šadeedu almihali The thunder hymneth His praise and (so do) the angels for awe of Him. He launcheth the thunderbolts and smiteth with them whom He will while they dispute (in doubt) concerning Allah, and He is mighty in wrath.
014 lahu daʿwatu alḥaqqi waallaḏeena yadʿoona min doonihi lā yastajeeboona lahum bišay-in illā kabāsiṭi kaffayhi ilā almā-i liyabluġa fāhu wamā huwa bibāliġihi wamā duʿāo alkāfireena illā fee ḍalālin Unto Him is the real prayer. Those unto whom they pray beside Allah respond to them not at all, save as (is the response to) one who stretcheth forth his hands toward water (asking) that it may come unto his mouth, and it will never reach it. The prayer of disbelievers goeth (far) astray.
015 walillāhi yasjudu man fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi ṭawʿan wakarhan waḏilāluhum bialġuduwwi waal-asali And unto Allah falleth prostrate whosoever is in the heavens and the earth, willingly or unwillingly, as do their shadows in the morning and the evening hours.
016 qul man rabbu alssamāwāti waal-arḍi quli allāhu qul afaittaḫaḏtum min doonihi awliyāa lā yamlikoona li-anfusihim nafʿan walā ḍarran qul hal yastawee al-aʿmā waalbaṣeeru am hal tastawee alṯṯulumātu waalnnooru am jaʿaloo lillāhi šurakāa ḫalaqoo kaḫalqihi fatašābaha alḫalqu ʿalayhim quli allāhu ḫāliqu kulli šay-in wahuwa alwahidu alqahhāru Say (O Muhammad): Who is Lord of the heavens and the earth ? Say: Allah. Say: Take ye then (others) beside Him for protectors, which, even for themselves, have neither benefit nor hurt ? Say: Is the blind man equal to the seer, or is darkness equal to light ? Or assign they unto Allah partners who created the like of His creation so that the creation (which they made and His creation) seemed alike to them ? Say: Allah is the Creator of all things, and He is the One, the Almighty.
017 anzala mina alssamā-i māan fasālat awdiyatun biqadarihā faiḥtamala alssaylu zabadan rābiyan wamimmā yooqidoona ʿalayhi fee alnnāri ibtiġāa ḥilyatin aw matāʿin zabadun miṯluhu kaṯalika yaḍribu allāhu alḥaqqa waalbatila faammā alzzabadu fayaḏhabu jufāan waammā mā yanfaʿu alnnāsa fayamkuṯu fee al-arḍi kaṯalika yaḍribu allāhu al-amṯāla He sendeth down water from the sky, so that valleys flow according to their measure, and the flood beareth (on its surface) swelling foam - from that which they smelt in the fire in order to make ornaments and tools riseth a foam like unto it - thus Allah coineth (the similitude of) the true and the false. Then, as for the foam, it passeth away as scum upon the banks, while, as for that which is of use to mankind, it remaineth in the earth. Thus Allah coineth the similitudes.
018 lillaḏeena istajāboo lirabbihimu alḥusnā waallaḏeena lam yastajeeboo lahu law anna lahum mā fee al-arḍi jameeʿan wamiṯlahu maʿahu laiftadaw bihi olā-ika lahum soo-o alḥisābi wama/wāhum jahannamu wabi/sa almihādu For those who answered Allah's call is bliss; and for those who answered not His call, if they had all that is in the earth, and therewith the like thereof, they would proffer it as ransom. Such will have a woeful reckoning, and their habitation will be hell, a dire abode.
019 afaman yaʿlamu annamā onzila ilayka min rabbika alḥaqqu kaman huwa aʿmā innamā yataḏakkaru oloo al-albābi Is he who knoweth that what is revealed unto thee from thy Lord is the truth like him who is blind ? But only men of understanding heed;
020 allāḏeena yoofoona biʿahdi allāhi walā yanquḍoona almeeṯāqa Such as keep the pact of Allah, and break not the covenant;
021 waallaḏeena yaṣiloona mā amara allāhu bihi an yooṣala wayaḫšawna rabbahum wayaḫāfoona soo-a alḥisābi Such as unite that which Allah hath commandeth should be joined, and fear their Lord, and dread a woeful reckoning;
022 waallaḏeena ṣabaroo ibtiġāa wajhi rabbihim waaqāmoo alṣalāta waanfaqoo mimmā razaqnāhum sirran waʿalāniyatan wayadraoona bialḥasanati alssayyi-ata olā-ika lahum ʿuqbā alddāri Such as persevere in seeking their Lord's Countenance and are regular in prayer and spend of that which We bestow upon them secretly and openly, and overcome evil with good. Theirs will be the sequel of the (heavenly) Home,
023 jannātu ʿadnin yadḫuloonahā waman ṣalaḥa min ābā-ihim waazwājihim waḏurriyyātihim waalmalā-ikatu yadḫuloona ʿalayhim min kulli bābin Gardens of Eden which they enter, along with all who do right of their fathers and their helpmeets and their seed. The angels enter unto them from every gate,
024 salāmun ʿalaykum bimā ṣabartum faniʿma ʿuqbā alddāri (Saying): Peace be unto you because ye persevered. Ah, passing sweet will be the sequel of the (heavenly) Home.
025 waallaḏeena yanquḍoona ʿahda allāhi min baʿdi meeṯāqihi wayaqṭaʿoona mā amara allāhu bihi an yooṣala wayufsidoona fee al-arḍi olā-ika lahumu allāʿnatu walahum soo-o alddāri And those who break the covenant of Allah after ratifying it, and sever that which Allah hath commanded should be joined, and make mischief in the earth: theirs is the curse and theirs the ill abode.
026 allāhu yabsuṭu alrrizqa liman yašāo wayaqdiru wafariḥoo bialḥayāti alddunyā wamā alḥayātu alddunyā fee al-āḫirati illā matāʿun Allah enlargeth livelihood for whom He will, and straiteneth (it for whom He will); and they rejoice in the life of the world, whereas the life of the world is but brief comfort as compared with the Hereafter.
027 wayaqoolu allāḏeena kafaroo lawlā onzila ʿalayhi āyatun min rabbihi qul inna allāha yuḍillu man yašāo wayahdee ilayhi man anāba Those who disbelieve say: If only a portent were sent down upon him from his Lord! Say: Lo! Allah sendeth whom He will astray, and guideth unto Himself all who turn (unto Him),
028 allāḏeena āmanoo wataṭma-innu quloobuhum biḏikri allāhi alā biḏikri allāhi taṭma-innu alquloobu Who have believed and whose hearts have rest in the remembrance of Allah. Verily in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest!
029 allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati ṭoobā lahum waḥusnu maābin Those who believe and do right: Joy is for them, and bliss (their) journey's end.
030 kaṯalika arsalnāka fee ommatin qad ḫalat min qablihā omamun litatluwa ʿalayhimu allāḏee awḥaynā ilayka wahum yakfuroona bialrraḥmāni qul huwa rabbee lā ilāha illā huwa ʿalayhi tawakkaltu wa-ilayhi matābi Thus We send thee (O Muhammad) unto a nation, before whom other nations have passed away, that thou mayst recite unto them that which We have inspired in thee, while they are disbelievers in the Beneficent. Say: He is my Lord; there is no God save Him. In Him do I put my trust and unto Him is my recourse.
031 walaw anna qur-ānan suyyirat bihi aljibālu aw quttiʿat bihi al-arḍu aw kullima bihi almawtā bal lillāhi al-amru jameeʿan afalam yay-asi allāḏeena āmanoo an law yašāo allāhu lahadā alnnāsa jameeʿan walā yazālu allāḏeena kafaroo tuṣeebuhum bimā ṣanaʿoo qāriʿatun aw taḥullu qareeban min dārihim ḥattā ya/tiya waʿdu allāhi inna allāha lā yuḫlifu almeeʿāda Had it been possible for a Lecture to cause the mountains to move, or the earth to be torn asunder, or the dead to speak, (this Qur'an would have done so). Nay, but Allah's is the whole command. Do not those who believe know that, had Allah willed, He could have guided all mankind ? As for those who disbelieve, disaster ceaseth not to strike them because of what they do, or it dwelleth near their home until the threat of Allah come to pass. Lo! Allah faileth not to keep the tryst.
032 walaqadi istuhzi-a birusulin min qablika faamlaytu lillaḏeena kafaroo ṯumma aḫaḏtuhum fakayfa kāna ʿiqābi And verily messengers (of Allah) were mocked before thee, but long I bore with those who disbelieved. At length I seized them, and how (awful) was My punishment!
033 afaman huwa qā-imun ʿalā kulli nafsin bimā kasabat wajaʿaloo lillāhi šurakāa qul sammoohum am tunabbi-oonahu bimā lā yaʿlamu fee al-arḍi am biḏāhirin mina alqawli bal zuyyina lillaḏeena kafaroo makruhum waṣuddoo ʿani alssabeeli waman yuḍlili allāhu famā lahu min hādin Is He Who is aware of the deserts of every soul (as he who is aware of nothing) ? Yet they ascribe unto Allah partners. Say: Name them. Is it that ye would inform Him of something which He knoweth not in the earth ? Or is it but a way of speaking ? Nay but their contrivance is made seeming fair for those who disbelieve and they are kept from the right road. He whom Allah sendeth astray, for him there is no guide.
034 lahum ʿaṯabun fee alḥayāti alddunyā walaʿaṯabu al-āḫirati ašaqqu wamā lahum mina allāhi min wāqin For them is torment in the life of the world, and verily the doom of the Hereafter is more painful, and they have no defender from Allah.
035 maṯalu aljannati allātee wuʿida almuttaqoona tajree min taḥtihā al-anhāru okuluhā dā-imun waḏilluhā tilka ʿuqbā allāḏeena ittaqaw waʿuqbā alkāfireena alnnāru A similitude of the Garden which is promised unto those who keep their duty (to Allah): Underneath it rivers flow; its food is everlasting, and its shade; this is the reward of those who keep their duty, while the reward of disbelievers is the Fire.
036 waallaḏeena ātaynāhumu alkitāba yafraḥoona bimā onzila ilayka wamina al-aḥzābi man yunkiru baʿḍahu qul innamā omirtu an aʿbuda allāha walā ošrika bihi ilayhi adʿoo wa-ilayhi maābi Those unto whom We gave the Scripture rejoice in that which is revealed unto thee. And of the clans there are who deny some of it. Say: I am commanded only that I serve Allah and ascribe unto Him no partner. Unto Him I cry, and unto Him is my return.
037 wakaṯalika anzalnāhu ḥukman ʿarabiyyan wala-ini ittabaʿta ahwāahum baʿda mā jāaka mina alʿilmi mā laka mina allāhi min waliyyin walā wāqin Thus have We revealed it, a decisive utterance in Arabic; and if thou shouldst follow their desires after that which hath come unto thee of knowledge, then truly wouldst thou have from Allah no protecting friend nor defender.
038 walaqad arsalnā rusulan min qablika wajaʿalnā lahum azwājan waḏurriyyatan wamā kāna lirasoolin an ya/tiya bi-āyatin illā bi-iḏni allāhi likulli ajalin kitābun And verily We sent messengers (to mankind) before thee, and We appointed for them wives and offspring, and it was not (given) to any messenger that he should bring a portent save by Allah's leave. For everything there is a time prescribed.
039 yamḥoo allāhu mā yašāo wayuṯbitu waʿindahu ommu alkitābi Allah effaceth what He will, and establisheth (what He will), and with Him is the source of ordinance.
040 wa-in mā nuriyannaka baʿḍa allāḏee naʿiduhum aw natawaffayannaka fa-innamā ʿalayka albalāġu waʿalaynā alḥisābu Whether We let thee see something of that which We have promised them, or make thee die (before its happening), thine is but conveyance (of the message). Ours the reckoning.
041 awa lam yaraw annā na/tee al-arḍa nanquṣuhā min aṭrāfihā waallāhu yaḥkumu lā muʿaqqiba liḥukmihi wahuwa sareeʿu alḥisābi See they not how we aim to the land, reducing it of its outlying parts ? (When) Allah doometh there is none that can postpone His doom, and He is swift at reckoning.
042 waqad makara allāḏeena min qablihim falillāhi almakru jameeʿan yaʿlamu mā taksibu kullu nafsin wasayaʿlamu alkuffāru liman ʿuqbā alddāri Those who were before them plotted; but all plotting is Allah's. He knoweth that which each soul earneth. The disbelievers will come to know for whom will be the sequel of the (heavenly) Home.
