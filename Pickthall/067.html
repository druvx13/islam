67. al-Mulk: The Kingdom

001 tabāraka allāḏee biyadihi almulku wahuwa ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerun Blessed is He in Whose hand is the Sovereignty, and, He is Able to do all things.
002 allāḏee ḫalaqa almawta waalḥayāta liyabluwakum ayyukum aḥsanu ʿamalan wahuwa alʿazeezu alġafooru Who hath created life and death that He may try you which of you is best in conduct; and He is the Mighty, the Forgiving,
003 allāḏee ḫalaqa sabʿa samāwātin ṭibāqan mā tarā fee ḫalqi alrraḥmāni min tafāwutin fairjiʿi albaṣara hal tarā min fuṭoorin Who hath created seven heavens in harmony. Thou (Muhammad) canst see no fault in the Beneficent One's creation; then look again: Canst thou see any rifts ?
004 thumma irjiʿi albaṣara karratayni yanqalib ilayka albaṣaru ḫāsi-an wahuwa ḥaseerun Then look again and yet again, thy sight will return unto thee weakened and made dim.
005 walaqad zayyannā alssamāa alddunyā bimasabeeḥa wajaʿalnāhā rujooman lilššayateeni waaʿtadnā lahum ʿaṯaba alssaʿeeri And verily We have beautified the world's heaven with lamps, and We have made them missiles for the devils, and for them We have prepared the doom of flame.
006 walillaḏeena kafaroo birabbihim ʿaṯabu jahannama wabi/sa almaṣeeru And for those who disbelieve in their Lord there is the doom of hell, a hapless journey's end!
007 iṯa olqoo feehā samiʿoo lahā šaheeqan wahiya tafooru When they are flung therein they hear its roaring as it boileth up,
008 takādu tamayyazu mina alġayḏi kullamā olqiya feehā fawjun saalahum ḫazanatuhā alam ya/tikum naḏeerun As it would burst with rage. Whenever a (fresh) host is flung therein the wardens thereof ask them: Came there unto you no warner ?
009 qāloo balā qad jāanā naḏeerun fakaṯṯabnā waqulnā mā nazzala allāhu min šay-in in antum illā fee ḍalālin kabeerin They say: Yea, verily, a warner came unto us; but we denied and said: Allah hath naught revealed; ye are in naught but a great error.
010 waqāloo law kunnā nasmaʿu aw naʿqilu mā kunnā fee aṣ-habi alssaʿeeri And they say: Had we been wont to listen or have sense, we had not been among the dwellers in the flames.
011 faiʿtarafoo biḏanbihim fasuḥqan li-aṣ-habi alssaʿeeri So they acknowledge their sins; but far removed (from mercy) are the dwellers in the flames.
012 inna allāḏeena yaḫšawna rabbahum bialġaybi lahum maġfiratun waajrun kabeerun Lo! those who fear their Lord in secret, theirs will be forgiveness and a great reward.
013 waasirroo qawlakum awi ijharoo bihi innahu ʿaleemun biṯati alṣudoori And keep your opinion secret or proclaim it, lo! He is Knower of all that is in the breasts (of men).
014 alā yaʿlamu man ḫalaqa wahuwa allāṭeefu alḫabeeru Should He not know what He created ? And He is the Subtile, the Aware.
015 huwa allāḏee jaʿala lakumu al-arḍa ḏaloolan faimšoo fee manākibihā wakuloo min rizqihi wa-ilayhi alnnušooru He it is Who hath made the earth subservient unto you, so Walk in the paths thereof and eat of His providence. And unto Him will be the resurrection (of the dead).
016 aamintum man fee alssamā-i an yaḫsifa bikumu al-arḍa fa-iṯa hiya tamooru Have ye taken security from Him Who is in the heaven that He will not cause the earth to swallow you when lo! it is convulsed ?
017 am amintum man fee alssamā-i an yursila ʿalaykum hasiban fasataʿlamoona kayfa naḏeeri Or have ye taken security from Him Who is in the heaven that He will not let loose on you a hurricane ? But ye shall know the manner of My warning.
018 walaqad kaṯṯaba allāḏeena min qablihim fakayfa kāna nakeeri And verily those before them denied, then (see) the manner of My wrath (with them)!
019 awa lam yaraw ilā alttayri fawqahum saffātin wayaqbiḍna mā yumsikuhunna illā alrraḥmānu innahu bikulli šay-in baṣeerun Have they not seen the birds above them spreading out their wings and closing them ? Naught upholdeth them save the Beneficent. Lo! He is Seer of all things.
020 amman haṯa allāḏee huwa jundun lakum yanṣurukum min dooni alrraḥmāni ini alkāfiroona illā fee ġuroorin Or who is he that will be an army unto you to help you instead of the Beneficent ? The disbelievers are in naught but illusion.
021 amman haṯa allāḏee yarzuqukum in amsaka rizqahu bal lajjoo fee ʿutuwwin wanufoorin Or who is he that will provide for you if He should withhold His providence ? Nay, but they are set in pride and frowardness.
022 afaman yamšee mukibban ʿalā wajhihi ahdā amman yamšee sawiyyan ʿalā ṣiratin mustaqeemin Is he who goeth groping on his face more rightly guided, or he who walketh upright on a straight road ?
023 qul huwa allāḏee anšaakum wajaʿala lakumu alssamʿa waal-absara waal-af-idata qaleelan mā taškuroona Say (unto them, O Muhammad): He it is who gave you being, and hath assigned unto you ears and eyes and hearts. Small thanks give ye!
024 qul huwa allāḏee ḏaraakum fee al-arḍi wa-ilayhi tuḥšaroona Say: He it is Who multiplieth you in the earth, and unto Whom ye will be gathered.
025 wayaqooloona matā haṯa alwaʿdu in kuntum sadiqeena And they say: When (will) this promise (be fulfilled), if ye are truthful ?
026 qul innamā alʿilmu ʿinda allāhi wa-innamā anā naḏeerun mubeenun Say: The knowledge is with Allah only, and I am but a plain warner;
027 falammā raawhu zulfatan see-at wujoohu allāḏeena kafaroo waqeela haṯa allāḏee kuntum bihi taddaʿoona But when they see it nigh, the faces of those who disbelieve will be awry, and it will be said (unto them): This is that for which ye used to call.
028 qul araaytum in ahlakaniya allāhu waman maʿiya aw raḥimanā faman yujeeru alkāfireena min ʿaṯabin aleemin Say (O Muhammad): Have ye thought: Whether Allah causeth me (Muhammad) and those with me to perish or hath mercy on us, still, who will protect the disbelievers from a painful doom ?
029 qul huwa alrraḥmānu āmannā bihi waʿalayhi tawakkalnā fasataʿlamoona man huwa fee ḍalālin mubeenin Say: He is the Beneficent. In Him we believe and in Him we put our trust. And ye will soon know who it is that is in error manifest.
30 
qul araaytum in aṣbaḥa māokum ġawran faman ya/teekum bimā-in maʿeenin
Say: Have ye thought: If (all) your water were to disappear into the earth, who then could bring you gushing water ?
