54. al-Qamar: The Moon

1 
iqtarabati alssāʿatu wainšaqqa alqamaru
The hour drew nigh and the moon was rent in twain.

2 
wa-in yaraw āyatan yuʿriḍoo wayaqooloo siḥrun mustamirrun
And if they behold a portent they turn away and say: Prolonged illusion.

3 
wakaṯṯaboo waittabaʿoo ahwāahum wakullu amrin mustaqirrun
They denied (the Truth) and followed their own lusts. Yet everything will come to a decision

4 
walaqad jāahum mina al-anbā-i mā feehi muzdajarun
And surely there hath come unto them news whereof the purport should deter,

5 
hikmatun balighatun fama tughnee alnnuthuru
Effective wisdom; but warnings avail not.

6 
fatawalla ʿanhum yawma yadʿu alddāʿi ilā šay-in nukurin
So withdraw from them (O Muhammad) on the day when the Summoner summoneth unto a painful thing.

7 
khuššaʿan absaruhum yaḫrujoona mina al-ajdāṯi kaannahum jarādun muntaširun
With downcast eyes, they come forth from the graves as they were locusts spread abroad,

8 
muhṭiʿeena ilā alddāʿi yaqoolu alkāfiroona haṯa yawmun ʿasirun
Hastening toward the summoner; the disbelievers say: This is a hard day.

9 
kaṯṯabat qablahum qawmu nooḥin fakaṯṯaboo ʿabdanā waqāloo majnoonun waizdujira
The folk of Noah denied before them, yea, they denied Our slave and said: A madman; and he was repulsed.

10 
fadaʿā rabbahu annee maġloobun faintaṣir
So he cried unto his Lord, saying: I am vanquished, so give help.

11 
fafataḥnā abwāba alssamā-i bimā-in munhamirin
Then opened We the gates of heaven with pouring water

12 
wafajjarnā al-arḍa ʿuyoonan failtaqā almāo ʿalā amrin qad qudira
And caused the earth to gush forth springs, so that the waters met for a predestined purpose.

13 
waḥamalnāhu ʿalā ṯati alwahin wadusurin
And We carried him upon a thing of planks and nails,

14 
tajree bi-aʿyuninā jazāan liman kāna kufira
That ran (upon the waters) in Our sight, as a reward for him who was rejected.

15 
walaqad taraknāhā āyatan fahal min muddakirin
And verily We left it as a token; but is there any that remembereth ?

16 
fakayfa kāna ʿaṯabee wanuḏuri
Then see how (dreadful) was My punishment after My warnings!

17 
walaqad yassarnā alqur-āna lilṯṯikri fahal min muddakirin
And in truth We have made the Qur'an easy to remember; but is there any that remembereth ?

18 
kaṯṯabat ʿādun fakayfa kāna ʿaṯabee wanuḏuri
(The tribe of) A'ad rejected warnings. Then how (dreadful) was My punishment after My warnings.

19 
innā arsalnā ʿalayhim reeḥan ṣarṣaran fee yawmi naḥsin mustamirrin
Lo! We let loose on them a raging wind on a day of constant calamity,

20 
tanziʿu alnnāsa kaannahum aʿjāzu naḫlin munqaʿirin
Sweeping men away as though they were uprooted trunks of palm-trees.

21 
fakayfa kāna ʿaṯabee wanuḏuri
Then see how (dreadful) was My punishment after My warnings!

22 
walaqad yassarnā alqur-āna lilṯṯikri fahal min muddakirin
And in truth We have made the Qur'an easy to remember; but is there any that remembereth ?

23 
kaṯṯabat ṯamoodu bialnnuḏuri
(The tribe of) Thamud rejected warnings

24 
faqāloo abašaran minnā wahidan nattabiʿuhu innā iḏan lafee ḍalālin wasuʿurin
For they said; Is it a mortal man, alone among us, that we are to follow ? Then indeed we should fall into error and madness.

25 
aolqiya alṯṯikru ʿalayhi min bayninā bal huwa kaṯṯabun aširun
Hath the remembrance been given unto him alone among us ? Nay, but he is a rash liar.

26 
sayaʿlamoona ġadan mani alkaṯṯabu al-aširu
(Unto their warner it was said): To-morrow they will know who is the rash liar.

27 
innā mursiloo alnnāqati fitnatan lahum fairtaqibhum waistabir
Lo! We are sending the she-camel as a test for them; so watch them and have patience;

28 
wanabbi/hum anna almāa qismatun baynahum kullu širbin muḥtaḍarun
And inform them that the water is to be shared between (her and) them. Every drinking will be witnessed.

29 
fanādaw sahibahum fataʿata faʿaqara
But they call their comrade and he took and hamstrung (her).

30 
fakayfa kāna ʿaṯabee wanuḏuri
Then see how (dreadful) was My punishment after My warnings!

31 
innā arsalnā ʿalayhim ṣayḥatan wahidatan fakānoo kahašeemi almuḥtaḏiri
Lo! We sent upon them one Shout, and they became as the dry twigs (rejected by) the builder of a cattle-fold.

32 
walaqad yassarnā alqur-āna lilṯṯikri fahal min muddakirin
And in truth We have made the Qur'an easy to remember; but is there any that remembereth ?

33 
kaṯṯabat qawmu looṭin bialnnuḏuri
The folk of Lot rejected warnings.

34 
innā arsalnā ʿalayhim hasiban illā āla looṭin najjaynāhum bisaḥarin
Lo! We sent a storm of stones upon them (all) save the family of Lot, whom We rescued in the last watch of the night,

35 
niʿmatan min ʿindinā kaṯalika najzee man šakara
As grace from Us. Thus We reward him who giveth thanks.

36 
walaqad anḏarahum baṭšatanā fatamāraw bialnnuḏuri
And he indeed had warned them of Our blow, but they did doubt the warnings.

37 
walaqad rāwadoohu ʿan ḍayfihi faṭamasnā aʿyunahum faḏooqoo ʿaṯabee wanuḏuri
They even asked of him his guests for an ill purpose. Then We blinded their eyes (and said): Taste now My punishment after My warnings!

38 
walaqad ṣabbaḥahum bukratan ʿaṯabun mustaqirrun
And in truth the punishment decreed befell them early in the morning.

39 
faḏooqoo ʿaṯabee wanuḏuri
Now taste My punishment after My warnings!

40 
walaqad yassarnā alqur-āna lilṯṯikri fahal min muddakirin
And in truth We have made the Qur'an easy to remember; but is there any that remembereth ?

41 
walaqad jāa āla firʿawna alnnuḏuru
And warnings came in truth unto the house of Pharaoh

42 
kaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā kullihā faaḫaḏnāhum aḫḏa ʿazeezin muqtadirin
Who denied Our revelations, every one. Therefore We grasped them with the grasp of the Mighty, the Powerful.

43 
akuffārukum ḫayrun min olā-ikum am lakum barāatun fee alzzuburi
Are your disbelievers better than those, or have ye some immunity in the scriptures ?

44 
am yaqooloona naḥnu jameeʿun muntaṣirun
Or say they: We are a host victorious ?

45 
sayuhzamu aljamʿu wayuwalloona alddubura
The hosts will all be routed and will turn and flee.

46 
bali alssāʿatu mawʿiduhum waalssāʿatu adhā waamarru
Nay, but the Hour (of doom) is their appointed tryst, and the Hour will be more wretched and more bitter (than their earthly failure).

47 
inna almujrimeena fee ḍalālin wasuʿurin
Lo! the guilty are in error and madness.

48 
yawma yusḥaboona fee alnnāri ʿalā wujoohihim ḏooqoo massa saqara
On the day when they are dragged into the Fire upon their faces (it is said unto them): Feel the touch of hell.

49 
innā kulla šay-in ḫalaqnāhu biqadarin
Lo! We have created every thing by measure.

50 
wamā amrunā illā wahidatun kalamḥin bialbaṣari
And Our commandment is but one (commandment), as the twinkling of an eye.

51 
walaqad ahlaknā ašyāʿakum fahal min muddakirin
And verily We have destroyed your fellows; but is there any that remembereth ?

52 
wakullu šay-in faʿaloohu fee alzzuburi
And every thing they did is in the scriptures,

53 
wakullu ṣaġeerin wakabeerin mustaṭarun
And every small and great thing is recorded.

54 
inna almuttaqeena fee jannātin wanaharin
Lo! the righteous will dwell among gardens and rivers,

55 
fee maqʿadi ṣidqin ʿinda maleekin muqtadirin
Firmly established in the favour of a Mighty King.
