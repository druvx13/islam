54. al-Qamar: The Moon

001 iqtarabati alssāʿatu wainšaqqa alqamaru The hour drew nigh and the moon was rent in twain.
002 wa-in yaraw āyatan yuʿriḍoo wayaqooloo siḥrun mustamirrun And if they behold a portent they turn away and say: Prolonged illusion.
003 wakaṯṯaboo waittabaʿoo ahwāahum wakullu amrin mustaqirrun They denied (the Truth) and followed their own lusts. Yet everything will come to a decision
004 walaqad jāahum mina al-anbā-i mā feehi muzdajarun And surely there hath come unto them news whereof the purport should deter,
005 [romanization missing] Effective wisdom; but warnings avail not.
006 fatawalla ʿanhum yawma yadʿu alddāʿi ilā šay-in nukurin So withdraw from them (O Muhammad) on the day when the Summoner summoneth unto a painful thing.
007 khuššaʿan absaruhum yaḫrujoona mina al-ajdāṯi kaannahum jarādun muntaširun With downcast eyes, they come forth from the graves as they were locusts spread abroad,
008 muhṭiʿeena ilā alddāʿi yaqoolu alkāfiroona haṯa yawmun ʿasirun Hastening toward the summoner; the disbelievers say: This is a hard day.
009 kaṯṯabat qablahum qawmu nooḥin fakaṯṯaboo ʿabdanā waqāloo majnoonun waizdujira The folk of Noah denied before them, yea, they denied Our slave and said: A madman; and he was repulsed.
010 fadaʿā rabbahu annee maġloobun faintaṣir So he cried unto his Lord, saying: I am vanquished, so give help.
011 fafataḥnā abwāba alssamā-i bimā-in munhamirin Then opened We the gates of heaven with pouring water
012 wafajjarnā al-arḍa ʿuyoonan failtaqā almāo ʿalā amrin qad qudira And caused the earth to gush forth springs, so that the waters met for a predestined purpose.
013 waḥamalnāhu ʿalā ṯati alwahin wadusurin And We carried him upon a thing of planks and nails,
014 tajree bi-aʿyuninā jazāan liman kāna kufira That ran (upon the waters) in Our sight, as a reward for him who was rejected.
015 walaqad taraknāhā āyatan fahal min muddakirin And verily We left it as a token; but is there any that remembereth ?
016 fakayfa kāna ʿaṯabee wanuḏuri Then see how (dreadful) was My punishment after My warnings!
017 walaqad yassarnā alqur-āna lilṯṯikri fahal min muddakirin And in truth We have made the Qur'an easy to remember; but is there any that remembereth ?
018 kaṯṯabat ʿādun fakayfa kāna ʿaṯabee wanuḏuri (The tribe of) A'ad rejected warnings. Then how (dreadful) was My punishment after My warnings.
019 innā arsalnā ʿalayhim reeḥan ṣarṣaran fee yawmi naḥsin mustamirrin Lo! We let loose on them a raging wind on a day of constant calamity,
020 tanziʿu alnnāsa kaannahum aʿjāzu naḫlin munqaʿirin Sweeping men away as though they were uprooted trunks of palm-trees.
021 fakayfa kāna ʿaṯabee wanuḏuri Then see how (dreadful) was My punishment after My warnings!
022 walaqad yassarnā alqur-āna lilṯṯikri fahal min muddakirin And in truth We have made the Qur'an easy to remember; but is there any that remembereth ?
023 kaṯṯabat ṯamoodu bialnnuḏuri (The tribe of) Thamud rejected warnings
024 faqāloo abašaran minnā wahidan nattabiʿuhu innā iḏan lafee ḍalālin wasuʿurin For they said; Is it a mortal man, alone among us, that we are to follow ? Then indeed we should fall into error and madness.
025 aolqiya alṯṯikru ʿalayhi min bayninā bal huwa kaṯṯabun aširun Hath the remembrance been given unto him alone among us ? Nay, but he is a rash liar.
026 sayaʿlamoona ġadan mani alkaṯṯabu al-aširu (Unto their warner it was said): To-morrow they will know who is the rash liar.
027 innā mursiloo alnnāqati fitnatan lahum fairtaqibhum waistabir Lo! We are sending the she-camel as a test for them; so watch them and have patience;
028 wanabbi/hum anna almāa qismatun baynahum kullu širbin muḥtaḍarun And inform them that the water is to be shared between (her and) them. Every drinking will be witnessed.
029 fanādaw sahibahum fataʿata faʿaqara But they call their comrade and he took and hamstrung (her).
030 fakayfa kāna ʿaṯabee wanuḏuri Then see how (dreadful) was My punishment after My warnings!
031 innā arsalnā ʿalayhim ṣayḥatan wahidatan fakānoo kahašeemi almuḥtaḏiri Lo! We sent upon them one Shout, and they became as the dry twigs (rejected by) the builder of a cattle-fold.
032 walaqad yassarnā alqur-āna lilṯṯikri fahal min muddakirin And in truth We have made the Qur'an easy to remember; but is there any that remembereth ?
033 kaṯṯabat qawmu looṭin bialnnuḏuri The folk of Lot rejected warnings.
034 innā arsalnā ʿalayhim hasiban illā āla looṭin najjaynāhum bisaḥarin Lo! We sent a storm of stones upon them (all) save the family of Lot, whom We rescued in the last watch of the night,
035 niʿmatan min ʿindinā kaṯalika najzee man šakara As grace from Us. Thus We reward him who giveth thanks.
036 walaqad anḏarahum baṭšatanā fatamāraw bialnnuḏuri And he indeed had warned them of Our blow, but they did doubt the warnings.
037 walaqad rāwadoohu ʿan ḍayfihi faṭamasnā aʿyunahum faḏooqoo ʿaṯabee wanuḏuri They even asked of him his guests for an ill purpose. Then We blinded their eyes (and said): Taste now My punishment after My warnings!
038 walaqad ṣabbaḥahum bukratan ʿaṯabun mustaqirrun And in truth the punishment decreed befell them early in the morning.
039 faḏooqoo ʿaṯabee wanuḏuri Now taste My punishment after My warnings!
040 walaqad yassarnā alqur-āna lilṯṯikri fahal min muddakirin And in truth We have made the Qur'an easy to remember; but is there any that remembereth ?
041 walaqad jāa āla firʿawna alnnuḏuru And warnings came in truth unto the house of Pharaoh
042 kaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā kullihā faaḫaḏnāhum aḫḏa ʿazeezin muqtadirin Who denied Our revelations, every one. Therefore We grasped them with the grasp of the Mighty, the Powerful.
043 akuffārukum ḫayrun min olā-ikum am lakum barāatun fee alzzuburi Are your disbelievers better than those, or have ye some immunity in the scriptures ?
044 am yaqooloona naḥnu jameeʿun muntaṣirun Or say they: We are a host victorious ?
045 sayuhzamu aljamʿu wayuwalloona alddubura The hosts will all be routed and will turn and flee.
046 bali alssāʿatu mawʿiduhum waalssāʿatu adhā waamarru Nay, but the Hour (of doom) is their appointed tryst, and the Hour will be more wretched and more bitter (than their earthly failure).
047 inna almujrimeena fee ḍalālin wasuʿurin Lo! the guilty are in error and madness.
048 yawma yusḥaboona fee alnnāri ʿalā wujoohihim ḏooqoo massa saqara On the day when they are dragged into the Fire upon their faces (it is said unto them): Feel the touch of hell.
049 innā kulla šay-in ḫalaqnāhu biqadarin Lo! We have created every thing by measure.
050 wamā amrunā illā wahidatun kalamḥin bialbaṣari And Our commandment is but one (commandment), as the twinkling of an eye.
051 walaqad ahlaknā ašyāʿakum fahal min muddakirin And verily We have destroyed your fellows; but is there any that remembereth ?
052 wakullu šay-in faʿaloohu fee alzzuburi And every thing they did is in the scriptures,
053 wakullu ṣaġeerin wakabeerin mustaṭarun And every small and great thing is recorded.
054 inna almuttaqeena fee jannātin wanaharin Lo! the righteous will dwell among gardens and rivers,
55 
fee maqʿadi ṣidqin ʿinda maleekin muqtadirin
Firmly established in the favour of a Mighty King.
