45. al-Jathiyah: The Kneeling

001 [romanization missing] Ha. Mim.
002 tanzeelu alkitābi mina allāhi alʿazeezi alḥakeemi The revelation of the Scripture is from Allah, the Mighty, the Wise.
003 inna fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi laāyātin lilmu/mineena Lo! in the heavens and the earth are portents for believers.
004 wafee ḫalqikum wamā yabuṯṯu min dābbatin āyātun liqawmin yooqinoona And in your creation, and all the beasts that He scattereth in the earth, are portents for a folk whose faith is sure.
005 waiḫtilāfi allāyli waalnnahāri wamā anzala allāhu mina alssamā-i min rizqin faaḥyā bihi al-arḍa baʿda mawtihā wataṣreefi alrriyahi āyātun liqawmin yaʿqiloona And the difference of night and day and the provision that Allah sendeth down from the sky and thereby quickeneth the earth after her death, and the ordering of the winds, are portents for a people who have sense.
006 tilka āyātu allāhi natloohā ʿalayka bialḥaqqi fabi-ayyi ḥadeeṯin baʿda allāhi waāyātihi yu/minoona These are the portents of Allah which We recite unto thee (Muhammad) with truth. Then in what fact, after Allah and His portents, will they believe ?
007 waylun likulli affākin aṯeemin Woe unto each sinful liar,
008 yasmaʿu āyāti allāhi tutlā ʿalayhi ṯumma yuṣirru mustakbiran kaan lam yasmaʿhā fabašširhu biʿaṯabin aleemin Who heareth the revelations of Allah recited unto him, and then continueth in pride as though he heard them not. Give him tidings of a painful doom.
009 wa-iṯa ʿalima min āyātinā šay-an ittaḫaḏahā huzuwan olā-ika lahum ʿaṯabun muheenun And when he knoweth aught of Our revelations he maketh it a jest. For such there is a shameful doom.
010 min warā-ihim jahannamu walā yuġnee ʿanhum mā kasaboo šay-an walā mā ittaḫaḏoo min dooni allāhi awliyāa walahum ʿaṯabun ʿaḏeemun Beyond them there is hell, and that which they have earned will naught avail them, nor those whom they have chosen for protecting friends beside Allah. Theirs will be an awful doom.
011 haṯa hudan waallaḏeena kafaroo bi-āyāti rabbihim lahum ʿaṯabun min rijzin aleemin This is guidance. And those who disbelieve the revelations of their Lord, for them there is a painful doom of wrath.
012 allāhu allāḏee saḫḫara lakumu albaḥra litajriya alfulku feehi bi-amrihi walitabtaġoo min faḍlihi walaʿallakum taškuroona Allah it is Who hath made the sea of service unto you that the ships may run thereon by His command, and that ye may seek of His bounty, and that haply ye may be thankful;
013 wasaḫḫara lakum mā fee alssamāwāti wamā fee al-arḍi jameeʿan minhu inna fee ṯalika laāyātin liqawmin yatafakkaroona And hath made of service unto you whatsoever is in the heavens and whatsoever is in the earth; it is all from Him. Lo! herein verily are portents for a people who reflect.
014 qul lillaḏeena āmanoo yaġfiroo lillaḏeena lā yarjoona ayyāma allāhi liyajziya qawman bimā kānoo yaksiboona Tell those who believe to forgive those who hope not for the days of Allah; in order that He may requite folk what they used to earn.
015 man ʿamila saliḥan falinafsihi waman asāa faʿalayhā ṯumma ilā rabbikum turjaʿoona Whoso doeth right, it is for his soul, and whoso doeth wrong, it is against it. And afterward unto your Lord ye will be brought back.
016 walaqad ātaynā banee isrā-eela alkitāba waalḥukma waalnnubuwwata warazaqnāhum mina alttayyibāti wafaḍalnāhum ʿalā alʿālameena And verily we gave the Children of Israel the Scripture and the Command and the Prophethood, and provided them with good things and favoured them above (all) peoples;
017 waātaynāhum bayyinātin mina al-amri famā iḫtalafoo illā min baʿdi mā jāahumu alʿilmu baġyan baynahum inna rabbaka yaqḍee baynahum yawma alqiyāmati feemā kānoo feehi yaḫtalifoona And gave them plain commandments. And they differed not until after the knowledge came unto them, through rivalry among themselves. Lo! thy Lord will judge between them on the Day of Resurrection concerning that wherein they used to differ.
018 thumma jaʿalnāka ʿalā šareeʿatin mina al-amri faittabiʿhā walā tattabiʿ ahwāa allāḏeena lā yaʿlamoona And now have We set thee (O Muhammad) on a clear road of (Our) commandment; so follow it, and follow not the whims of those who know not.
019 innahum lan yuġnoo ʿanka mina allāhi šay-an wa-inna alṯṯālimeena baʿḍuhum awliyāo baʿḍin waallāhu waliyyu almuttaqeena Lo! they can avail thee naught against Allah. And lo! as for the wrong-doers, some of them are friends of others; and Allah is the Friend of those who ward off (evil).
020 haṯa basa-iru lilnnāsi wahudan waraḥmatun liqawmin yooqinoona This is clear indication for mankind, and a guidance and a mercy for a folk whose faith is sure.
021 am ḥasiba allāḏeena ijtaraḥoo alssayyi-āti an najʿalahum kaallaḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati sawāan maḥyāhum wamamātuhum sāa mā yaḥkumoona Or do those who commit ill-deeds suppose that We shall make them as those who believe and do good works, the same in life and death ? Bad is their judgment!
022 waḫalaqa allāhu alssamāwāti waal-arḍa bialḥaqqi walitujzā kullu nafsin bimā kasabat wahum lā yuḏlamoona And Allah hath created the heavens and the earth with truth, and that every soul may be repaid what it hath earned. And they will not be wronged.
023 afaraayta mani ittaḫaḏa ilāhahu hawāhu waaḍallahu allāhu ʿalā ʿilmin waḫatama ʿalā samʿihi waqalbihi wajaʿala ʿalā baṣarihi ġišāwatan faman yahdeehi min baʿdi allāhi afalā taḏakkaroona Hast thou seen him who maketh his desire his god, and Allah sendeth him astray purposely, and sealeth up his hearing and his heart, and setteth on his sight a covering ? Then who will lead him after Allah (hath condemned him) ? Will ye not then heed ?
024 waqāloo mā hiya illā ḥayātunā alddunyā namootu wanaḥyā wamā yuhlikunā illā alddahru wamā lahum biṯalika min ʿilmin in hum illā yaḏunnoona And they say: There is naught but our life of the world; we die and we live, and naught destroyeth us save time; when they have no knowledge whatsoever of (all) that; they do but guess.
025 wa-iṯa tutlā ʿalayhim āyātunā bayyinātin mā kāna ḥujjatahum illā an qāloo i/too bi-ābā-inā in kuntum sadiqeena And when Our clear revelations are recited unto them their only argument is that they say: Bring (back) our fathers. then, if ye are truthful.
026 quli allāhu yuḥyeekum ṯumma yumeetukum ṯumma yajmaʿukum ilā yawmi alqiyāmati lā rayba feehi walākinna akṯara alnnāsi lā yaʿlamoona Say (unto them, O Muhammad): Allah giveth life to you, then causeth you to die, then gathereth you unto the Day of Resurrection whereof there is no doubt. But most of mankind know not.
027 walillāhi mulku alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wayawma taqoomu alssāʿatu yawma-iḏin yaḫsaru almubṭiloona And unto Allah belongeth the Sovereignty of the heavens and the earth; and on the day when the Hour riseth, on that day those who follow falsehood will be lost.
028 watarā kulla ommatin jāṯiyatan kullu ommatin tudʿā ilā kitābihā alyawma tujzawna mā kuntum taʿmaloona And thou wilt see each nation crouching, each nation summoned to its record. (And it will be said unto them): This day ye are requited what ye used to do.
029 haṯa kitābunā yanṭiqu ʿalaykum bialḥaqqi innā kunnā nastansiḫu mā kuntum taʿmaloona This Our Book pronounceth against you with truth. Lo! We have caused (all) that ye did to be recorded.
030 faammā allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati fayudḫiluhum rabbuhum fee raḥmatihi ṯalika huwa alfawzu almubeenu Then, as for those who believed and did good works, their Lord will bring them in unto His mercy. That is the evident triumph.
031 waammā allāḏeena kafaroo afalam takun āyātee tutlā ʿalaykum faistakbartum wakuntum qawman mujrimeena And as for those who disbelieved (it will be said unto them): Were not Our revelations recited unto you ? But ye were scornful and became a guilty folk.
032 wa-iṯa qeela inna waʿda allāhi ḥaqqun waalssāʿatu lā rayba feehā qultum mā nadree mā alssāʿatu in naḏunnu illā ḏannan wamā naḥnu bimustayqineena And when it was said: Lo! Allah's promise is the truth, and there is no doubt of the Hour's coming, ye said: We know not what the Hour is. We deem it naught but a conjecture, and we are by no means convinced.
033 wabadā lahum sayyi-ātu mā ʿamiloo wahaqa bihim mā kānoo bihi yastahzi-oona And the evils of what they did will appear unto them, and that which they used to deride will befall them.
034 waqeela alyawma nansākum kamā naseetum liqāa yawmikum haṯa wama/wākumu alnnāru wamā lakum min nasireena And it will be said: This day We forget you, even as ye forgot the meeting of this your day; and your habitation is the Fire, and there is none to help you.
035 [romanization missing] This, forasmuch as ye made the revelations of Allah a jest, and the life of the world beguiled you. Therefor this day they come not forth from thence, nor can they make amends.
036 falillāhi alḥamdu rabbi alssamāwāti warabbi al-arḍi rabbi alʿālameena Then praise be to Allah, Lord of the heavens and Lord of the earth, the Lord of the Worlds.
37 
walahu alkibriyāo fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wahuwa alʿazeezu alḥakeemu
And unto Him (alone) belongeth Majesty in the heavens and the earth, and He is the Mighty, the Wise.
