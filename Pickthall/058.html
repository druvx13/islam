58. al-Mujadilah: She Who Pleaded

001 qad samiʿa allāhu qawla allātee tujādiluka fee zawjihā wataštakee ilā allāhi waallāhu yasmaʿu tahawurakumā inna allāha sameeʿun baṣeerun Allah hath heard the saying of her that disputeth with thee (Muhammad) concerning her husband, and complaineth unto Allah. And Allah heareth your colloquy. Lo! Allah is Hearer, Knower.
002 allāḏeena yuḏāhiroona minkum min nisā-ihim mā hunna ommahātihim in ommahātuhum illā allā-ee waladnahum wa-innahum layaqooloona munkaran mina alqawli wazooran wa-inna allāha laʿafuwwun ġafoorun Such of you as put away your wives (by saying they are as their mothers) - They are not their mothers; none are their mothers except those who gave them birth - they indeed utter an ill word and a lie. And lo! Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.
003 waallaḏeena yuḏāhiroona min nisā-ihim ṯumma yaʿoodoona limā qāloo fataḥreeru raqabatin min qabli an yatamāssā ṯalikum tooʿaḏoona bihi waallāhu bimā taʿmaloona ḫabeerun Those who put away their wives (by saying they are as their mothers) and afterward would go back on that which they have said, (the penalty) in that case (is) the freeing of a slave before they touch one another. Unto this ye are exhorted; and Allah is Informed of what ye do.
004 faman lam yajid faṣiyāmu šahrayni mutatābiʿayni min qabli an yatamāssā faman lam yastaṭiʿ fa-iṭʿāmu sitteena miskeenan ṯalika litu/minoo biallāhi warasoolihi watilka ḥudoodu allāhi walilkāfireena ʿaṯabun aleemun And he who findeth not (the wherewithal), let him fast for two successive months before they touch one another; and for him who is unable to do so (the penance is) the feeding of sixty needy ones. This, that ye may put trust in Allah and His messenger. Such are the limits (imposed by Allah); and for disbelievers is a painful doom.
005 inna allāḏeena yuhaddoona allāha warasoolahu kubitoo kamā kubita allāḏeena min qablihim waqad anzalnā āyātin bayyinātin walilkāfireena ʿaṯabun muheenun Lo! those who oppose Allah and His messenger will be abased even as those before them were abased; and We have sent down clear tokens, and for disbelievers is a shameful doom
006 yawma yabʿaṯuhumu allāhu jameeʿan fayunabbi-ohum bimā ʿamiloo ahsahu allāhu wanasoohu waallāhu ʿalā kulli šay-in šaheedun On the day when Allah will raise them all together and inform them of what they did. Allah hath kept account of it while they forgot it. And Allah is Witness over all things.
007 alam tara anna allāha yaʿlamu mā fee alssamāwāti wamā fee al-arḍi mā yakoonu min najwā ṯalāṯatin illā huwa rābiʿuhum walā ḫamsatin illā huwa sādisuhum walā adnā min ṯalika walā akṯara illā huwa maʿahum ayna mā kānoo ṯumma yunabbi-ohum bimā ʿamiloo yawma alqiyāmati inna allāha bikulli šay-in ʿaleemun Hast thou not seen that Allah knoweth all that is in the heavens and all that is in the earth ? There is no secret conference of three but He is their fourth, nor of five but He is their sixth, nor of less than that or more but He is with them wheresoever they may be; and afterward, on the Day of Resurrection, He will inform them of what they did. Lo! Allah is Knower of all things.
008 alam tara ilā allāḏeena nuhoo ʿani alnnajwā ṯumma yaʿoodoona limā nuhoo ʿanhu wayatanājawna bial-iṯmi waalʿudwāni wamaʿṣiyati alrrasooli wa-iṯa jāooka ḥayyawka bimā lam yuḥayyika bihi allāhu wayaqooloona fee anfusihim lawlā yuʿaṯṯibunā allāhu bimā naqoolu ḥasbuhum jahannamu yaṣlawnahā fabi/sa almaṣeeru Hast thou not observed those who were forbidden conspiracy and afterward returned to that which they had been forbidden, and (now) conspire together for crime and wrongdoing and disobedience toward the messenger ? And when they come unto thee they greet thee with a greeting wherewith Allah greeteth thee not, and say within themselves: Why should Allah punish us for what we say ? Hell will suffice them; they will feel the heat thereof - a hapless journey's end!
009 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo iṯa tanājaytum falā tatanājaw bial-iṯmi waalʿudwāni wamaʿṣiyati alrrasooli watanājaw bialbirri waalttaqwā waittaqoo allāha allāḏee ilayhi tuḥšaroona O ye who believe! When ye conspire together, conspire not together for crime and wrongdoing and disobedience toward the messenger, but conspire together for righteousness and piety, and keep your duty toward Allah, unto whom ye will be gathered.
010 innamā alnnajwā mina alššaytani liyaḥzuna allāḏeena āmanoo walaysa bidarrihim šay-an illā bi-iḏni allāhi waʿalā allāhi falyatawakkali almu/minoona Lo! Conspiracy is only of the devil, that he may vex those who believe; but he can harm them not at all unless by Allah's leave. In Allah let believers put their trust.
011 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo iṯa qeela lakum tafassaḥoo fee almajālisi faifsaḥoo yafsaḥi allāhu lakum wa-iṯa qeela onšuzoo faonšuzoo yarfaʿi allāhu allāḏeena āmanoo minkum waallaḏeena ootoo alʿilma darajātin waallāhu bimā taʿmaloona ḫabeerun O ye who believe! when it is said unto you, Make room! in assemblies, then make room; Allah will make way for you (hereafter). And when it is said, Come up higher! go up higher; Allah will exalt those who believe among you, and those who have knowledge, to high ranks. Allah is Informed of what ye do.
012 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo iṯa nājaytumu alrrasoola faqaddimoo bayna yaday najwākum ṣadaqatan ṯalika ḫayrun lakum waaṭharu fa-in lam tajidoo fa-inna allāha ġafoorun raḥeemun O ye who believe! When ye hold conference with the messenger, offer an alms before your conference. That is better and purer for you. But if ye cannot find (the wherewithal) then lo! Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.
013 aašfaqtum an tuqaddimoo bayna yaday najwākum ṣadaqātin fa-iḏ lam tafʿaloo watāba allāhu ʿalaykum faaqeemoo alṣalāta waātoo alzzakāta waaṭeeʿoo allāha warasoolahu waallāhu ḫabeerun bimā taʿmaloona Fear ye to offer alms before your conference ? Then, when ye do it not and Allah hath forgiven you, establish worship and pay the poor-due and obey Allah and His messenger. And Allah is Aware of what ye do.
014 alam tara ilā allāḏeena tawallaw qawman ġaḍiba allāhu ʿalayhim mā hum minkum walā minhum wayaḥlifoona ʿalā alkaḏibi wahum yaʿlamoona Hast thou not seen those who take for friends a folk with whom Allah is wroth ? They are neither of you nor of them, and they swear a false oath knowingly.
015 aʿadda allāhu lahum ʿaṯaban šadeedan innahum sāa mā kānoo yaʿmaloona Allah hath prepared for them a dreadful doom. Evil indeed is that which they are wont to do.
016 ittaḫaḏoo aymānahum junnatan faṣaddoo ʿan sabeeli allāhi falahum ʿaṯabun muheenun They make a shelter of their oaths and turn (men) from the way of Allah; so theirs will be a shameful doom.
017 lan tuġniya ʿanhum amwāluhum walā awlāduhum mina allāhi šay-an olā-ika aṣ-habu alnnāri hum feehā ḫālidoona Their wealth and their children will avail them naught against Allah. Such are rightful owners of the Fire; they will abide therein.
018 yawma yabʿaṯuhumu allāhu jameeʿan fayaḥlifoona lahu kamā yaḥlifoona lakum wayaḥsaboona annahum ʿalā šay-in alā innahum humu alkaṯiboona On the day when Allah will raise them all together, then will they swear unto Him as they (now) swear unto you, and they will fancy that they have some standing. Lo! is it not they who are the liars ?
019 istaḥwaḏa ʿalayhimu alššaytanu faansāhum ḏikra allāhi olā-ika ḥizbu alššaytani alā inna ḥizba alššaytani humu alḫāsiroona The devil hath engrossed them and so hath caused them to forget remembrance of Allah. They are the devil's party. Lo! is it not the devil's party who will be the losers ?
020 inna allāḏeena yuhaddoona allāha warasoolahu olā-ika fee al-aḏalleena Lo! those who oppose Allah and His messenger, they will be among the lowest.
021 kataba allāhu laaġlibanna anā warusulee inna allāha qawiyyun ʿazeezun Allah hath decreed: Lo! I verily shall conquer, I and My messengers. Lo! Allah is Strong, Almighty.
22 lā tajidu qawman yu/minoona biallāhi waalyawmi al-āḫiri yuwāddoona man hadda allāha warasoolahu walaw kānoo ābāahum aw abnāahum aw iḫwānahum aw ʿašeeratahum olā-ika kataba fee quloobihimu al-eemāna waayyadahum birooḥin minhu wayudḫiluhum jannātin tajree min taḥtihā al-anhāru ḫālideena feehā raḍiya allāhu ʿanhum waraḍoo ʿanhu olā-ika ḥizbu allāhi alā inna ḥizba allāhi humu almufliḥoona [Could not extract English for verse 22]
