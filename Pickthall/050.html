50. Qaf: Qaf

001 qāf waalqur-āni almajeedi Qaf. By the Glorious Qur'an,
002 bal ʿajiboo an jāahum munḏirun minhum faqāla alkāfiroona haṯa šay-on ʿajeebun Nay, but they marvel that a warner of their own hath come unto them; and the disbelievers say: This is a strange thing:
003 a-iṯa mitnā wakunnā turāban ṯalika rajʿun baʿeedun When we are dead and have become dust (shall we be brought back again) ? That would be a far return!
004 qad ʿalimnā mā tanquṣu al-arḍu minhum waʿindanā kitābun ḥafeeḏun We know that which the earth taketh of them, and with Us is a recording Book.
005 bal kaṯṯaboo bialḥaqqi lammā jāahum fahum fee amrin mareejin Nay, but they have denied the truth when it came unto them, therefor they are now in troubled case.
006 afalam yanḏuroo ilā alssamā-i fawqahum kayfa banaynāhā wazayyannāhā wamā lahā min furoojin Have they not then observed the sky above them, how We have constructed it and beautified it, and how there are no rifts therein ?
007 waal-arḍa madadnāhā waalqaynā feehā rawāsiya waanbatnā feehā min kulli zawjin baheejin And the earth have We spread out, and have flung firm hills therein, and have caused of every lovely kind to grow thereon,
008 tabṣiratan waḏikrā likulli ʿabdin muneebin A vision and a reminder for every penitent slave.
009 wanazzalnā mina alssamā-i māan mubārakan faanbatnā bihi jannātin waḥabba alḥaṣeedi And We send down from the sky blessed water whereby We give growth unto gardens and the grain of crops,
010 waalnnaḫla bāsiqātin lahā ṭalʿun naḍeedun And lofty date-palms with ranged clusters,
011 rizqan lilʿibādi waaḥyaynā bihi baldatan maytan kaṯalika alḫurooju Provision (made) for men; and therewith We quicken a dead land. Even so will be the resurrection of the dead.
012 kaṯṯabat qablahum qawmu nooḥin waaṣ-habu alrrassi waṯamoodu The folk of Noah denied (the truth) before them, and (so did) the dwellers at Ar-Rass and (the tribe of) Thamud,
013 waʿādun wafirʿawnu wa-iḫwānu looṭin And (the tribe of) A'ad, and Pharaoh, and the brethren of Lot,
014 waaṣ-habu al-aykati waqawmu tubbaʿin kullun kaṯṯaba alrrusula faḥaqqa waʿeedi And the dwellers in the wood, and the folk of Tubb'a: every one denied their messengers, therefor My threat took effect.
015 afaʿayeenā bialḫalqi al-awwali bal hum fee labsin min ḫalqin jadeedin Were We then worn out by the first creation ? Yet they are in doubt about a new creation.
016 walaqad ḫalaqnā al-insāna wanaʿlamu mā tuwaswisu bihi nafsuhu wanaḥnu aqrabu ilayhi min ḥabli alwareedi We verily created man and We know what his soul whispereth to him, and We are nearer to him than his jugular vein.
017 iḏ yatalaqqā almutalaqqiyāni ʿani alyameeni waʿani alššimāli qaʿeedun When the two Receivers receive (him), seated on the right hand and on the left,
018 mā yalfiḏu min qawlin illā ladayhi raqeebun ʿateedun He uttereth no word but there is with him an observer ready.
019 wajāat sakratu almawti bialḥaqqi ṯalika mā kunta minhu taḥeedu And the agony of death cometh in truth. (And it is said unto him): This is that which thou wast wont to shun.
020 wanufiḫa fee alṣoori ṯalika yawmu alwaʿeedi And the trumpet is blown. This is the threatened Day.
021 wajāat kullu nafsin maʿahā sā-iqun wašaheedun And every soul cometh, along with it a driver and a witness.
022 laqad kunta fee ġaflatin min haṯa fakašafnā ʿanka ġitaaka fabaṣaruka alyawma ḥadeedun (And unto the evil-doer it is said): Thou wast in heedlessness of this. Now We have removed from thee thy covering, and piercing is thy sight this day.
023 waqāla qareenuhu haṯa mā ladayya ʿateedun And (unto the evil-doer) his comrade saith: This is that which I have ready (as testimony).
024 alqiyā fee jahannama kulla kaffārin ʿaneedin (And it is said): Do ye twain hurl to hell each rebel ingrate,
025 mannāʿin lilḫayri muʿtadin mureebin Hinderer of good, transgressor, doubter,
026 allāḏee jaʿala maʿa allāhi ilāhan āḫara faalqiyāhu fee alʿaṯabi alššadeedi Who setteth up another god along with Allah. Do ye twain hurl him to the dreadful doom.
027 qāla qareenuhu rabbanā mā aṭġaytuhu walākin kāna fee ḍalālin baʿeedin His comrade saith: Our Lord! I did not cause him to rebel, but he was (himself) far gone in error.
028 qāla lā taḫtaṣimoo ladayya waqad qaddamtu ilaykum bialwaʿeedi He saith: Contend not in My presence, when I had already proffered unto you the warning.
029 mā yubaddalu alqawlu ladayya wamā anā biḏallāmin lilʿabeedi The sentence that cometh from Me cannot be changed, and I am in no wise a tyrant unto the slaves.
030 yawma naqoolu lijahannama hali imtala/ti wataqoolu hal min mazeedin On the day when We say unto hell: Art thou filled ? and it saith: Can there be more to come ?
031 waozlifati aljannatu lilmuttaqeena ġayra baʿeedin And the Garden is brought nigh for those who kept from evil, no longer distant.
032 haṯa mā tooʿadoona likulli awwābin ḥafeeḏin (And it is said): This is that which ye were promised. (It is) for every penitent and heedful one,
033 man ḫašiya alrraḥmāna bialġaybi wajāa biqalbin muneebin Who feareth the Beneficent in secret and cometh with a contrite heart.
034 odḫuloohā bisalāmin ṯalika yawmu alḫuloodi Enter it in peace. This is the day of immortality.
035 lahum mā yašāoona feehā waladaynā mazeedun There they have all that they desire, and there is more with Us.
036 wakam ahlaknā qablahum min qarnin hum ašaddu minhum baṭšan fanaqqaboo fee albilādi hal min maḥeeṣin And how many a generation We destroyed before them, who were mightier than these in prowess so that they overran the lands! Had they any place of refuge (when the judgment came) ?
037 inna fee ṯalika laḏikrā liman kāna lahu qalbun aw alqā alssamʿa wahuwa šaheedun Lo! therein verily is a reminder for him who hath a heart, or giveth ear with full intelligence.
038 walaqad ḫalaqnā alssamāwāti waal-arḍa wamā baynahumā fee sittati ayyāmin wamā massanā min luġoobin And verily We created the heavens and the earth, and all that is between them, in six Days, and naught of weariness touched Us.
039 faiṣbir ʿalā mā yaqooloona wasabbiḥ biḥamdi rabbika qabla ṭulooʿi alššamsi waqabla alġuroobi Therefor (O Muhammad) bear with what they say, and hymn the praise of thy Lord before the rising and before the setting of the sun;
040 wamina allāyli fasabbiḥhu waadbāra alssujoodi And in the night-time hymn His praise, and after the (prescribed) prostrations.
041 waistamiʿ yawma yunādi almunādi min makānin qareebin And listen on the day when the crier crieth from a near place,
042 yawma yasmaʿoona alṣayḥata bialḥaqqi ṯalika yawmu alḫurooji The day when they will hear the (Awful) Cry in truth. That is the day of coming forth (from the graves).
043 innā naḥnu nuḥyee wanumeetu wa-ilaynā almaṣeeru Lo! We it is Who quicken and give death, and unto Us is the journeying.
044 yawma tašaqqaqu al-arḍu ʿanhum sirāʿan ṯalika ḥašrun ʿalaynā yaseerun On the day when the earth splitteth asunder from them, hastening forth (they come). That is a gathering easy for Us (to make).
45 naḥnu aʿlamu bimā yaqooloona wamā anta ʿalayhim bijabbārin faḏakkir bialqur-āni man yaḫāfu waʿeedi [Could not extract English for verse 45]
