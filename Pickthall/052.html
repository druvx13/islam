52. at-Tur: The Mountain

1 
waalttoori
By the Mount,

2 
wakitābin masṭoorin
And a Scripture inscribed

3 
fee raqqin manšoorin
On fine parchment unrolled,

4 
waalbayti almaʿmoori
And the House frequented,

5 
waalssaqfi almarfooʿi
And the roof exalted,

6 
waalbaḥri almasjoori
And the sea kept filled,

7 
inna ʿaṯaba rabbika lawāqiʿun
Lo! the doom of thy Lord will surely come to pass;

8 
mā lahu min dāfiʿin
There is none that can ward it off.

9 
yawma tamooru alssamāo mawrān
On the day when the heaven will heave with (awful) heaving,

10 
wataseeru aljibālu sayrān
And the mountains move away with (awful) movement,

11 
fawaylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena
Then woe that day unto the deniers

12 
allāḏeena hum fee ḫawḍin yalʿaboona
Who play in talk of grave matters;

13 
yawma yudaʿʿoona ilā nāri jahannama daʿʿān
The day when they are thrust with a (disdainful) thrust, into the fire of hell

14 
haṯihi alnnāru allātee kuntum bihā tukaṯṯiboona
(And it is said unto them): This is the Fire which ye were wont to deny.

15 
afasiḥrun haṯa am antum lā tubṣiroona
Is this magic, or do ye not see ?

16 
iṣlawhā faiṣbiroo aw lā taṣbiroo sawāon ʿalaykum innamā tujzawna mā kuntum taʿmaloona
Endure the heat thereof, and whether ye are patient of it or impatient of it is all one for you. Ye are only being paid for what ye used to do.

17 
inna almuttaqeena fee jannātin wanaʿeemin
Lo! those who kept their duty dwell in gardens and delight,

18 
fākiheena bimā ātāhum rabbuhum wawaqāhum rabbuhum ʿaṯaba aljaḥeemi
Happy because of what their Lord hath given them, and (because) their Lord hath warded off from them the torment of hell-fire.

19 
kuloo waišraboo hanee-an bimā kuntum taʿmaloona
(And it is said unto them): Eat and drink in health (as a reward) for what ye used to do,

20 
muttaki-eena ʿalā sururin maṣfoofatin wazawwajnāhum biḥoorin ʿeenin
Reclining on ranged couches. And we wed them unto fair ones with wide, lovely eyes.

21 
waallaḏeena āmanoo waittabaʿat-hum ḏurriyyatuhum bi-eemānin alḥaqnā bihim ḏurriyyatahum wamā alatnāhum min ʿamalihim min šay-in kullu imri-in bimā kasaba raheenun
And they who believe and whose seed follow them in faith, We cause their seed to join them (there), and We deprive them of nought of their (life's) work. Every man is a pledge for that which he hath earned.

22 
waamdadnāhum bifākihatin walaḥmin mimmā yaštahoona
And We provide them with fruit and meat such as they desire.

23 
yatanāzaʿoona feehā ka/san lā laġwun feehā walā ta/ṯeemun
There they pass from hand to hand a cup wherein is neither vanity nor cause of sin.

24 
wayaṭoofu ʿalayhim ġilmānun lahum kaannahum lu/luon maknoonun
And there go round, waiting on them menservants of their own, as they were hidden pearls.

25 
waaqbala baʿḍuhum ʿalā baʿḍin yatasāaloona
And some of them draw near unto others, questioning,

26 
qāloo innā kunnā qablu fee ahlinā mušfiqeena
Saying: Lo! of old, when we were with our families, we were ever anxious;

27 
famanna allāhu ʿalaynā wawaqānā ʿaṯaba alssamoomi
But Allah hath been gracious unto us and hath preserved us from the torment of the breath of Fire.

28 
innā kunnā min qablu nadʿoohu innahu huwa albarru alrraḥeemu
Lo! we used to pray unto Him of old. Lo! He is the Benign, the Merciful.

29 
faḏakkir famā anta biniʿmati rabbika bikāhin walā majnoonin
Therefor warn (men, O Muhammad). By the grace of Allah thou art neither soothsayer nor madman.

30 
am yaqooloona šāʿirun natarabbaṣu bihi rayba almanooni
Or say they: (he is) a poet, (one) for whom we may expect the accident of time ?

31 
qul tarabbaṣoo fa-innee maʿakum mina almutarabbiṣeena
Say (unto them): Except (your fill)! Lo! I am with you among the expectant.

32 
am ta/muruhum aḥlāmuhum bihaṯa am hum qawmun taġoona
Do their minds command them to do this, or are they an outrageous folk ?

33 
am yaqooloona taqawwalahu bal lā yu/minoona
Or say they: He hath invented it ? Nay, but they will not believe!

34 
falya/too biḥadeeṯin miṯlihi in kānoo sadiqeena
Then let them produce speech the like thereof, if they are truthful.

35 
am ḫuliqoo min ġayri šay-in am humu alḫāliqoona
Or were they created out of naught ? Or are they the creators ?

36 
am ḫalaqoo alssamāwāti waal-arḍa bal lā yooqinoona
Or did they create the heavens and the earth ? Nay, but they are sure of nothing!

37 
am ʿindahum ḫazā-inu rabbika am humu almuṣayṭiroona
Or do they own the treasures of thy Lord ? Or have they been given charge (thereof) ?

38 
am lahum sullamun yastamiʿoona feehi falya/ti mustamiʿuhum bisultanin mubeenin
Or have they any stairway (unto heaven) by means of which they overhear (decrees). Then let their listener produce some warrant manifest!

39 
am lahu albanātu walakumu albanoona
Or hath He daughters whereas ye have sons ?

40 
am tas-aluhum ajran fahum min maġramin muṯqaloona
Or askest thou (Muhammad) a fee from them so that they are plunged in debt ?

41 
am ʿindahumu alġaybu fahum yaktuboona
Or possess they the Unseen so that they can write (it) down ?

42 
am yureedoona kaydan faallaḏeena kafaroo humu almakeedoona
Or seek they to ensnare (the messenger) ? But those who disbelieve, they are the ensnared!

43 
am lahum ilāhun ġayru allāhi subhana allāhi ʿammā yušrikoona
Or have they any god beside Allah ? Glorified be Allah from all that they ascribe as partner (unto Him)!

44 
wa-in yaraw kisfan mina alssamā-i sāqiṭan yaqooloo sahabun markoomun
And if they were to see a fragment of the heaven falling, they would say: A heap of clouds.

45 
faḏarhum ḥattā yulāqoo yawmahumu allāḏee feehi yuṣʿaqoona
Then let them be (O Muhammad), till they meet their day, in which they will be thunder-stricken,

46 
yawma lā yuġnee ʿanhum kayduhum šay-an walā hum yunṣaroona
A day in which their guile will naught avail them, nor will they be helped.

47 
wa-inna lillaḏeena ḏalamoo ʿaṯaban doona ṯalika walākinna akṯarahum lā yaʿlamoona
And verily, for those who do wrong, there is a punishment beyond that. But most of them know not.

48 
waiṣbir liḥukmi rabbika fa-innaka bi-aʿyuninā wasabbiḥ biḥamdi rabbika ḥeena taqoomu
So wait patiently (O Muhammad) for thy Lord's decree, for surely thou art in Our sight; and hymn the praise of thy Lord when thou uprisest,

49 
wamina allāyli fasabbiḥhu wa-idbāra alnnujoomi
And in the night-time also hymn His praise, and at the setting of the stars.
