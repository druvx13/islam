52. at-Tur: The Mountain

001 waalttoori By the Mount,
002 wakitābin masṭoorin And a Scripture inscribed
003 fee raqqin manšoorin On fine parchment unrolled,
004 waalbayti almaʿmoori And the House frequented,
005 waalssaqfi almarfooʿi And the roof exalted,
006 waalbaḥri almasjoori And the sea kept filled,
007 inna ʿaṯaba rabbika lawāqiʿun Lo! the doom of thy Lord will surely come to pass;
008 mā lahu min dāfiʿin There is none that can ward it off.
009 yawma tamooru alssamāo mawrān On the day when the heaven will heave with (awful) heaving,
010 wataseeru aljibālu sayrān And the mountains move away with (awful) movement,
011 fawaylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena Then woe that day unto the deniers
012 allāḏeena hum fee ḫawḍin yalʿaboona Who play in talk of grave matters;
013 yawma yudaʿʿoona ilā nāri jahannama daʿʿān The day when they are thrust with a (disdainful) thrust, into the fire of hell
014 haṯihi alnnāru allātee kuntum bihā tukaṯṯiboona (And it is said unto them): This is the Fire which ye were wont to deny.
015 afasiḥrun haṯa am antum lā tubṣiroona Is this magic, or do ye not see ?
016 iṣlawhā faiṣbiroo aw lā taṣbiroo sawāon ʿalaykum innamā tujzawna mā kuntum taʿmaloona Endure the heat thereof, and whether ye are patient of it or impatient of it is all one for you. Ye are only being paid for what ye used to do.
017 inna almuttaqeena fee jannātin wanaʿeemin Lo! those who kept their duty dwell in gardens and delight,
018 fākiheena bimā ātāhum rabbuhum wawaqāhum rabbuhum ʿaṯaba aljaḥeemi Happy because of what their Lord hath given them, and (because) their Lord hath warded off from them the torment of hell-fire.
019 kuloo waišraboo hanee-an bimā kuntum taʿmaloona (And it is said unto them): Eat and drink in health (as a reward) for what ye used to do,
020 muttaki-eena ʿalā sururin maṣfoofatin wazawwajnāhum biḥoorin ʿeenin Reclining on ranged couches. And we wed them unto fair ones with wide, lovely eyes.
021 waallaḏeena āmanoo waittabaʿat-hum ḏurriyyatuhum bi-eemānin alḥaqnā bihim ḏurriyyatahum wamā alatnāhum min ʿamalihim min šay-in kullu imri-in bimā kasaba raheenun And they who believe and whose seed follow them in faith, We cause their seed to join them (there), and We deprive them of nought of their (life's) work. Every man is a pledge for that which he hath earned.
022 waamdadnāhum bifākihatin walaḥmin mimmā yaštahoona And We provide them with fruit and meat such as they desire.
023 yatanāzaʿoona feehā ka/san lā laġwun feehā walā ta/ṯeemun There they pass from hand to hand a cup wherein is neither vanity nor cause of sin.
024 wayaṭoofu ʿalayhim ġilmānun lahum kaannahum lu/luon maknoonun And there go round, waiting on them menservants of their own, as they were hidden pearls.
025 waaqbala baʿḍuhum ʿalā baʿḍin yatasāaloona And some of them draw near unto others, questioning,
026 qāloo innā kunnā qablu fee ahlinā mušfiqeena Saying: Lo! of old, when we were with our families, we were ever anxious;
027 famanna allāhu ʿalaynā wawaqānā ʿaṯaba alssamoomi But Allah hath been gracious unto us and hath preserved us from the torment of the breath of Fire.
028 innā kunnā min qablu nadʿoohu innahu huwa albarru alrraḥeemu Lo! we used to pray unto Him of old. Lo! He is the Benign, the Merciful.
029 faḏakkir famā anta biniʿmati rabbika bikāhin walā majnoonin Therefor warn (men, O Muhammad). By the grace of Allah thou art neither soothsayer nor madman.
030 am yaqooloona šāʿirun natarabbaṣu bihi rayba almanooni Or say they: (he is) a poet, (one) for whom we may expect the accident of time ?
031 qul tarabbaṣoo fa-innee maʿakum mina almutarabbiṣeena Say (unto them): Except (your fill)! Lo! I am with you among the expectant.
032 am ta/muruhum aḥlāmuhum bihaṯa am hum qawmun taġoona Do their minds command them to do this, or are they an outrageous folk ?
033 am yaqooloona taqawwalahu bal lā yu/minoona Or say they: He hath invented it ? Nay, but they will not believe!
034 falya/too biḥadeeṯin miṯlihi in kānoo sadiqeena Then let them produce speech the like thereof, if they are truthful.
035 am ḫuliqoo min ġayri šay-in am humu alḫāliqoona Or were they created out of naught ? Or are they the creators ?
036 am ḫalaqoo alssamāwāti waal-arḍa bal lā yooqinoona Or did they create the heavens and the earth ? Nay, but they are sure of nothing!
037 am ʿindahum ḫazā-inu rabbika am humu almuṣayṭiroona Or do they own the treasures of thy Lord ? Or have they been given charge (thereof) ?
038 am lahum sullamun yastamiʿoona feehi falya/ti mustamiʿuhum bisultanin mubeenin Or have they any stairway (unto heaven) by means of which they overhear (decrees). Then let their listener produce some warrant manifest!
039 am lahu albanātu walakumu albanoona Or hath He daughters whereas ye have sons ?
040 am tas-aluhum ajran fahum min maġramin muṯqaloona Or askest thou (Muhammad) a fee from them so that they are plunged in debt ?
041 am ʿindahumu alġaybu fahum yaktuboona Or possess they the Unseen so that they can write (it) down ?
042 am yureedoona kaydan faallaḏeena kafaroo humu almakeedoona Or seek they to ensnare (the messenger) ? But those who disbelieve, they are the ensnared!
043 am lahum ilāhun ġayru allāhi subhana allāhi ʿammā yušrikoona Or have they any god beside Allah ? Glorified be Allah from all that they ascribe as partner (unto Him)!
044 wa-in yaraw kisfan mina alssamā-i sāqiṭan yaqooloo sahabun markoomun And if they were to see a fragment of the heaven falling, they would say: A heap of clouds.
045 faḏarhum ḥattā yulāqoo yawmahumu allāḏee feehi yuṣʿaqoona Then let them be (O Muhammad), till they meet their day, in which they will be thunder-stricken,
046 yawma lā yuġnee ʿanhum kayduhum šay-an walā hum yunṣaroona A day in which their guile will naught avail them, nor will they be helped.
047 wa-inna lillaḏeena ḏalamoo ʿaṯaban doona ṯalika walākinna akṯarahum lā yaʿlamoona And verily, for those who do wrong, there is a punishment beyond that. But most of them know not.
048 waiṣbir liḥukmi rabbika fa-innaka bi-aʿyuninā wasabbiḥ biḥamdi rabbika ḥeena taqoomu So wait patiently (O Muhammad) for thy Lord's decree, for surely thou art in Our sight; and hymn the praise of thy Lord when thou uprisest,
49 wamina allāyli fasabbiḥhu wa-idbāra alnnujoomi [Could not extract English for verse 49]
