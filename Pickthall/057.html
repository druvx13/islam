57. al-Hadid: The Iron

001 sabbaḥa lillāhi mā fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wahuwa alʿazeezu alḥakeemu All that is in the heavens and the earth glorifieth Allah; and He is the Mighty, the Wise.
002 lahu mulku alssamāwāti waal-arḍi yuḥyee wayumeetu wahuwa ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerun His is the Sovereignty of the heavens and the earth; He quickeneth and He giveth death; and He is Able to do all things.
003 huwa al-awwalu waal-āḫiru waalṯṯāhiru waalbatinu wahuwa bikulli šay-in ʿaleemun He is the First and the Last, and the Outward and the Inward; and He is Knower of all things.
004 huwa allāḏee ḫalaqa alssamāwāti waal-arḍa fee sittati ayyāmin ṯumma istawā ʿalā alʿarši yaʿlamu mā yaliju fee al-arḍi wamā yaḫruju minhā wamā yanzilu mina alssamā-i wamā yaʿruju feehā wahuwa maʿakum ayna mā kuntum waallāhu bimā taʿmaloona baṣeerun He it is Who created the heavens and the earth in six Days; then He mounted the Throne. He knoweth all that entereth the earth and all that emergeth therefrom and all that cometh down from the sky and all that ascendeth therein; and He is with you wheresoever ye may be. And Allah is Seer of what ye do.
005 lahu mulku alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wa-ilā allāhi turjaʿu al-omooru His is the Sovereignty of the heavens and the earth, and unto Allah (all) things are brought back.
006 yooliju allāyla fee alnnahāri wayooliju alnnahāra fee allāyi wahuwa ʿaleemun biṯati alṣudoori He causeth the night to pass into the day, and He causeth the day to pass into the night, and He is knower of all that is in the breasts.
007 [romanization missing] Believe in Allah and His messenger, and spend of that whereof He hath made you trustees; and such of you as believe and spend (aright), theirs will be a great reward.
008 wamā lakum lā tu/minoona biallāhi waalrrasoolu yadʿookum litu/minoo birabbikum waqad aḫaḏa meeṯāqakum in kuntum mu/mineena What aileth you that ye believe not in Allah, when the messenger calleth you to believe in your Lord, and He hath already made a covenant with you, if ye are believers ?
009 huwa allāḏee yunazzilu ʿalā ʿabdihi āyātin bayyinātin liyuḫrijakum mina alṯṯulumāti ilā alnnoori wa-inna allāha bikum laraoofun raḥeemun He it is Who sendeth down clear revelations unto His slave, that He may bring you forth from darkness unto light; and lo! for you, Allah is Full of Pity, Merciful.
010 wamā lakum allā tunfiqoo fee sabeeli allāhi walillāhi meerāṯu alssamāwāti waal-arḍi lā yastawee minkum man anfaqa min qabli alfatḥi waqātala olā-ika aʿḏamu darajatan mina allāḏeena anfaqoo min baʿdu waqātaloo wakullan waʿada allāhu alḥusnā waallāhu bimā taʿmaloona ḫabeerun And what aileth you that ye spend not in the way of Allah when unto Allah belongeth the inheritance of the heavens and the earth ? Those who spent and fought before the victory are not upon a level (with the rest of you). Such are greater in rank than those who spent and fought afterwards. Unto each hath Allah promised good. And Allah is Informed of what ye do.
011 man ṯa allāḏee yuqriḍu allāha qarḍan ḥasanan fayudaʿifahu lahu walahu ajrun kareemun Who is he that will lend unto Allah a goodly loan, that He may double it for him and his may be a rich reward ?
012 yawma tarā almu/mineena waalmu/mināti yasʿā nooruhum bayna aydeehim wabi-aymānihim bušrākumu alyawma jannātun tajree min taḥtihā al-anhāru ḫālideena feehā ṯalika huwa alfawzu alʿaḏeemu On the day when thou (Muhammad) wilt see the believers, men and women, their light shining forth before them and on their right hands, (and wilt hear it said unto them): Glad news for you this day: Gardens underneath which rivers flow, wherein ye are immortal. That is the supreme triumph.
013 yawma yaqoolu almunāfiqoona waalmunāfiqātu lillaḏeena āmanoo onḏuroonā naqtabis min noorikum qeela irjiʿoo warāakum failtamisoo nooran faḍuriba baynahum bisoorin lahu bābun batinuhu feehi alrraḥmatu waḏāhiruhu min qibalihi alʿaṯabu On the day when the hypocritical men and the hypocritical women will say unto those who believe: Look on us that we may borrow from your light! it will be said: Go back and seek for light! Then there will separate them a wall wherein is a gate, the inner side whereof containeth mercy, while the outer side thereof is toward the doom.
014 yunādoonahum alam nakun maʿakum qāloo balā walākinnakum fatantum anfusakum watarabbaṣtum wairtabtum waġarratkumu al-amāniyyu ḥattā jāa amru allāhi waġarrakum biallāhi alġarooru They will cry unto them (saying): Were we not with you ? They will say: Yea, verily; but ye tempted one another, and hesitated, and doubted, and vain desires beguiled you till the ordinance of Allah came to pass; and the deceiver deceived you concerning Allah;
015 faalyawma lā yu/ḫaḏu minkum fidyatun walā mina allāḏeena kafaroo ma/wākumu alnnāru hiya mawlākum wabi/sa almaṣeeru So this day no ransom can be taken from you nor from those who disbelieved. Your home is the Fire; that is your patron, and a hapless journey's end.
016 alam ya/ni lillaḏeena āmanoo an taḫšaʿa quloobuhum liḏikri allāhi wamā nazala mina alḥaqqi walā yakoonoo kaallaḏeena ootoo alkitāba min qablu fatala ʿalayhimu al-amadu faqasat quloobuhum wakaṯeerun minhum fāsiqoona Is not the time ripe for the hearts of those who believe to submit to Allah's reminder and to the truth which is revealed, that they become not as those who received the scripture of old but the term was prolonged for them and so their hearts were hardened, and many of them are evil-livers.
017 iʿlamoo anna allāha yuḥyee al-arḍa baʿda mawtihā qad bayyannā lakumu al-āyāti laʿallakum taʿqiloona Know that Allah quickeneth the earth after its death. We have made clear Our revelations for you, that haply ye may understand.
018 inna almuṣaddiqeena waalmuṣaddiqāti waaqraḍoo allāha qarḍan ḥasanan yudaʿafu lahum walahum ajrun kareemun Lo! those who give alms, both men and women, and lend unto Allah a goodly loan, it will be doubled for them, and theirs will be a rich reward.
019 waallaḏeena āmanoo biallāhi warusulihi olā-ika humu alṣiddeeqoona waalššuhadāo ʿinda rabbihim lahum ajruhum wanooruhum waallaḏeena kafaroo wakaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā olā-ika aṣ-habu aljaḥeemi And those who believe in Allah and His messengers, they are the loyal, and the martyrs are with their Lord; they have their reward and their light; while as for those who disbelieve and deny Our revelations, they are owners of hell-fire.
020 iʿlamoo annamā alḥayātu alddunyā laʿibun walahwun wazeenatun watafāḫurun baynakum watakāṯurun fee al-amwāli waal-awlādi kamaṯali ġayṯin aʿjaba alkuffāra nabātuhu ṯumma yaheeju fatarāhu muṣfarran ṯumma yakoonu ḥutaman wafee al-āḫirati ʿaṯabun šadeedun wamaġfiratun mina allāhi wariḍwānun wamā alḥayātu alddunyā illā matāʿu alġuroori Know that the life of the world is only play, and idle talk, and pageantry, and boasting among you, and rivalry in respect of wealth and children; as the likeness of vegetation after rain, whereof the growth is pleasing to the husbandman, but afterward it drieth up and thou seest it turning yellow, then it becometh straw. And in the Hereafter there is grievous punishment, and (also) forgiveness from Allah and His good pleasure, whereas the life of the world is but matter of illusion.
021 sābiqoo ilā maġfiratin min rabbikum wajannatin ʿarḍuhā kaʿarḍi alssamā-i waal-arḍi oʿiddat lillaḏeena āmanoo biallāhi warusulihi ṯalika faḍlu allāhi yu/teehi man yašāo waallāhu ḏoo alfaḍli alʿaḏeemi Race one with another for forgiveness from your Lord and a Garden whereof the breadth is as the breadth of the heavens and the earth, which is in store for those who believe in Allah and His messengers. Such is the bounty of Allah, which He bestoweth upon whom He will, and Allah is of Infinite Bounty.
022 mā asaba min muṣeebatin fee al-arḍi walā fee anfusikum illā fee kitābin min qabli an nabraahā inna ṯalika ʿalā allāhi yaseerun Naught of disaster befalleth in the earth or in yourselves but it is in a Book before we bring it into being - Lo! that is easy for Allah -
023 likaylā ta/saw ʿalā mā fātakum walā tafraḥoo bimā ātākum waallāhu lā yuḥibbu kulla muḫtālin faḫoorin That ye grieve not for the sake of that which hath escaped you, nor yet exult because of that which hath been given. Allah loveth not all prideful boasters,
024 allāḏeena yabḫaloona waya/muroona alnnāsa bialbuḫli waman yatawalla fa-inna allāha huwa alġaniyyu alḥameedu Who hoard and who enjoin upon the people avarice. And whosoever turneth away, still Allah is the Absolute, the Owner of Praise.
025 laqad arsalnā rusulanā bialbayyināti waanzalnā maʿahumu alkitāba waalmeezāna liyaqooma alnnāsu bialqisṭi waanzalnā alḥadeeda feehi ba/sun šadeedun wamanāfiʿu lilnnāsi waliyaʿlama allāhu man yanṣuruhu warusulahu bialġaybi inna allāha qawiyyun ʿazeezun We verily sent Our messengers with clear proofs, and revealed with them the Scripture and the Balance, that mankind may observe right measure; and He revealed iron, wherein is mighty power and (many) uses for mankind, and that Allah may know him who helpeth Him and His messengers, though unseen. Lo! Allah is Strong, Almighty.
026 walaqad arsalnā nooḥan wa-ibrāheema wajaʿalnā fee ḏurriyyatihimā alnnubuwwata waalkitāba faminhum muhtadin wakaṯeerun minhum fāsiqoona And We verily sent Noah and Abraham and placed the prophethood and the scripture among their seed, and among them there is he who goeth right, but many of them are evil-livers.
027 thumma qaffaynā ʿalā āṯārihim birusulinā waqaffaynā biʿeesā ibni maryama waātaynāhu al-injeela wajaʿalnā fee quloobi allāḏeena ittabaʿoohu ra/fatan waraḥmatan warahbāniyyatan ibtadaʿoohā mā katabnāhā ʿalayhim illā ibtiġāa riḍwāni allāhi famā raʿawhā ḥaqqa riʿāyatihā faātaynā allāḏeena āmanoo minhum ajrahum wakaṯeerun minhum fāsiqoona Then We caused Our messengers to follow in their footsteps; and We caused Jesus, son of Mary, to follow, and gave him the Gospel, and placed compassion and mercy in the hearts of those who followed him. But monasticism they invented - We ordained it not for them - only seeking Allah's pleasure, and they observed it not with right observance. So We give those of them who believe their reward, but many of them are evil-livers.
028 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo ittaqoo allāha waāminoo birasoolihi yu/tikum kiflayni min raḥmatihi wayajʿal lakum nooran tamšoona bihi wayaġfir lakum waallāhu ġafoorun raḥeemun O ye who believe! Be mindful of your duty to Allah and put faith in His messenger. He will give you twofold of His mercy and will appoint for you a light wherein ye shall walk, and will forgive you. Allah is Forgiving, Merciful;
29 li-allā yaʿlama ahlu alkitābi allā yaqdiroona ʿalā šay-in min faḍli allāhi waanna alfaḍla biyadi allāhi yu/teehi man yašāo waallāhu ḏoo alfaḍli alʿaḏeemi [Could not extract English for verse 29]
