69. al-Haqqah: The Inevitable

1 
alhaqqatu
The Reality!

2 
mā alhaqqatu
What is the Reality ?

3 
wamā adrāka mā alhaqqatu
Ah, what will convey unto thee what the reality is!

4 
kaṯṯabat ṯamoodu waʿādun bialqāriʿati
(The tribes of) Thamud and A'ad disbelieved in the judgment to come.

5 
faammā ṯamoodu faohlikoo bialttaġiyati
As for Thamud, they were destroyed by the lightning.

6 
waammā ʿādun faohlikoo bireeḥin ṣarṣarin ʿātiyatin
And as for A'ad, they were destroyed by a fierce roaring wind,

7 
saḫḫarahā ʿalayhim sabʿa layālin waṯamāniyata ayyāmin ḥusooman fatarā alqawma feehā ṣarʿā kaannahum aʿjāzu naḫlin ḫāwiyatun
Which He imposed on them for seven long nights and eight long days so that thou mightest have seen men lying overthrown, as they were hollow trunks of palm- trees.

8 
fahal tarā lahum min bāqiyatin
Canst thou (O Muhammad) see any remnant of them ?

9 
wajāa firʿawnu waman qablahu waalmu/tafikātu bialḫati-ati
And Pharaoh and those before him, and the communities that were destroyed, brought error,

10 
faʿaṣaw rasoola rabbihim faaḫaḏahum aḫḏatan rābiyatan
And they disobeyed the messenger of their Lord, therefor did He grip them with a tightening grip.

11 
innā lammā ṭaġā almāo ḥamalnākum fee aljāriyati
Lo! when the waters rose, We carried you upon the ship

12 
linajʿalahā lakum taḏkiratan wataʿiyahā oḏunun wāʿiyatun
That We might make it a memorial for you, and that remembering ears (that heard the story) might remember.

13 
fa-iṯa nufiḫa fee alṣoori nafḫatun wahidatun
And when the trumpet shall sound one blast

14 
waḥumilati al-arḍu waaljibālu fadukkatā dakkatan wahidatan
And the earth with the mountains shall be lifted up and crushed with one crash,

15 
fayawma-iḏin waqaʿati alwāqiʿatu
Then, on that day will the Event befall.

16 
wainšaqqati alssamāo fahiya yawma-iḏin wāhiyatun
And the heaven will split asunder, for that day it will be frail.

17 
waalmalaku ʿalā arjā-ihā wayaḥmilu ʿarša rabbika fawqahum yawma-iḏin ṯamāniyatun
And the angels will be on the sides thereof, and eight will uphold the Throne of thy Lord that day, above them.

18 
yawma-iḏin tuʿraḍoona lā taḫfā minkum ḫāfiyatun
On that day ye will be exposed; not a secret of you will be hidden.

19 
faammā man ootiya kitābahu biyameenihi fayaqoolu hāomu iqraoo kitābiyah
Then, as for him who is given his record in his right hand, he will say: Take, read my book!

20 
innee ḏanantu annee mulāqin ḥisābiyah
Surely I knew that I should have to meet my reckoning.

21 
fahuwa fee ʿeešatin radiyatin
Then he will be in blissful state

22 
fee jannatin ʿāliyatin
In a high garden

23 
quṭoofuhā dāniyatun
Whereof the clusters are in easy reach.

24 
kuloo waišraboo hanee-an bimā aslaftum fee al-ayyāmi alḫāliyati
(And it will be said unto those therein): Eat and drink at ease for that which ye sent on before you in past days.

25 
waammā man ootiya kitābahu bišimālihi fayaqoolu yā laytanee lam oota kitābiyah
But as for him who is given his record in his left hand, he will say: Oh, would that I had not been given my book

26 
walam adri mā ḥisābiyah
And knew not what my reckoning!

27 
yā laytahā kānati alqadiyatu
Oh, would that it had been death!

28 
mā aġnā ʿannee māliyah
My wealth hath not availed me,

29 
halaka ʿannee sultaniyah
My power hath gone from me.

30 
khuḏoohu faġulloohu
(It will be said): Take him and fetter him

31 
thumma aljaḥeema ṣalloohu
And then expose him to hell-fire

32 
thumma fee silsilatin ḏarʿuhā sabʿoona ḏirāʿan faoslukoohu
And then insert him in a chain whereof the length is seventy cubits.

33 
innahu kāna lā yu/minu biallāhi alʿaḏeemi
Lo! He used not to believe in Allah the Tremendous,

34 
walā yaḥuḍu ʿalā ṭaʿāmi almiskeeni
And urged not on the feeding of the wretched.

35 
falaysa lahu alyawma hāhunā ḥameemun
Therefor hath he no lover here this day,

36 
walā ṭaʿāmun illā min ġisleenin
Nor any food save filth

37 
lā ya/kuluhu illā alḫati-oona
Which none but sinners eat.

38 
falā oqsimu bimā tubṣiroona
But nay! I swear by all that ye see

39 
wamā lā tubṣiroona
And all that ye see not

40 
innahu laqawlu rasoolin kareemin
That it is indeed the speech of an illustrious messenger.

41 
wamā huwa biqawli šāʿirin qaleelan mā tu/minoona
It is not poet's speech - little is it that ye believe!

42 
walā biqawli kāhinin qaleelan mā taḏakkaroona
Nor diviner's speech - little is it that ye remember!

43 
tanzeelun min rabbi alʿālameena
It is a revelation from the Lord of the Worlds.

44 
walaw taqawwala ʿalaynā baʿḍa al-aqāweeli
And if he had invented false sayings concerning Us,

45 
laaḫaḏnā minhu bialyameeni
We assuredly had taken him by the right hand

46 
thumma laqaṭaʿnā minhu alwateena
And then severed his life-artery,

47 
famā minkum min aḥadin ʿanhu hajizeena
And not one of you could have held Us off from him.

48 
wa-innahu lataḏkiratun lilmuttaqeena
And lo! it is a warrant unto those who ward off (evil).

49 
wa-innā lanaʿlamu anna minkum mukaṯṯibeena
And lo! We know that some among you will deny (it).

50 
wa-innahu laḥasratun ʿalā alkāfireena
And lo! it is indeed an anguish for the disbelievers.

51 
wa-innahu laḥaqqu alyaqeeni
And lo! it is absolute truth.

52 
fasabbiḥ biismi rabbika alʿaḏeemi
So glorify the name of thy Tremendous Lord.
