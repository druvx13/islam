69. al-Haqqah: The Inevitable

001 alhaqqatu The Reality!
002 mā alhaqqatu What is the Reality ?
003 wamā adrāka mā alhaqqatu Ah, what will convey unto thee what the reality is!
004 kaṯṯabat ṯamoodu waʿādun bialqāriʿati (The tribes of) Thamud and A'ad disbelieved in the judgment to come.
005 faammā ṯamoodu faohlikoo bialttaġiyati As for Thamud, they were destroyed by the lightning.
006 waammā ʿādun faohlikoo bireeḥin ṣarṣarin ʿātiyatin And as for A'ad, they were destroyed by a fierce roaring wind,
007 saḫḫarahā ʿalayhim sabʿa layālin waṯamāniyata ayyāmin ḥusooman fatarā alqawma feehā ṣarʿā kaannahum aʿjāzu naḫlin ḫāwiyatun Which He imposed on them for seven long nights and eight long days so that thou mightest have seen men lying overthrown, as they were hollow trunks of palm- trees.
008 fahal tarā lahum min bāqiyatin Canst thou (O Muhammad) see any remnant of them ?
009 wajāa firʿawnu waman qablahu waalmu/tafikātu bialḫati-ati And Pharaoh and those before him, and the communities that were destroyed, brought error,
010 faʿaṣaw rasoola rabbihim faaḫaḏahum aḫḏatan rābiyatan And they disobeyed the messenger of their Lord, therefor did He grip them with a tightening grip.
011 innā lammā ṭaġā almāo ḥamalnākum fee aljāriyati Lo! when the waters rose, We carried you upon the ship
012 linajʿalahā lakum taḏkiratan wataʿiyahā oḏunun wāʿiyatun That We might make it a memorial for you, and that remembering ears (that heard the story) might remember.
013 fa-iṯa nufiḫa fee alṣoori nafḫatun wahidatun And when the trumpet shall sound one blast
014 waḥumilati al-arḍu waaljibālu fadukkatā dakkatan wahidatan And the earth with the mountains shall be lifted up and crushed with one crash,
015 fayawma-iḏin waqaʿati alwāqiʿatu Then, on that day will the Event befall.
016 wainšaqqati alssamāo fahiya yawma-iḏin wāhiyatun And the heaven will split asunder, for that day it will be frail.
017 waalmalaku ʿalā arjā-ihā wayaḥmilu ʿarša rabbika fawqahum yawma-iḏin ṯamāniyatun And the angels will be on the sides thereof, and eight will uphold the Throne of thy Lord that day, above them.
018 yawma-iḏin tuʿraḍoona lā taḫfā minkum ḫāfiyatun On that day ye will be exposed; not a secret of you will be hidden.
019 faammā man ootiya kitābahu biyameenihi fayaqoolu hāomu iqraoo kitābiyah Then, as for him who is given his record in his right hand, he will say: Take, read my book!
020 innee ḏanantu annee mulāqin ḥisābiyah Surely I knew that I should have to meet my reckoning.
021 fahuwa fee ʿeešatin radiyatin Then he will be in blissful state
022 fee jannatin ʿāliyatin In a high garden
023 quṭoofuhā dāniyatun Whereof the clusters are in easy reach.
024 kuloo waišraboo hanee-an bimā aslaftum fee al-ayyāmi alḫāliyati (And it will be said unto those therein): Eat and drink at ease for that which ye sent on before you in past days.
025 waammā man ootiya kitābahu bišimālihi fayaqoolu yā laytanee lam oota kitābiyah But as for him who is given his record in his left hand, he will say: Oh, would that I had not been given my book
026 walam adri mā ḥisābiyah And knew not what my reckoning!
027 yā laytahā kānati alqadiyatu Oh, would that it had been death!
028 mā aġnā ʿannee māliyah My wealth hath not availed me,
029 halaka ʿannee sultaniyah My power hath gone from me.
030 khuḏoohu faġulloohu (It will be said): Take him and fetter him
031 thumma aljaḥeema ṣalloohu And then expose him to hell-fire
032 thumma fee silsilatin ḏarʿuhā sabʿoona ḏirāʿan faoslukoohu And then insert him in a chain whereof the length is seventy cubits.
033 innahu kāna lā yu/minu biallāhi alʿaḏeemi Lo! He used not to believe in Allah the Tremendous,
034 walā yaḥuḍu ʿalā ṭaʿāmi almiskeeni And urged not on the feeding of the wretched.
035 falaysa lahu alyawma hāhunā ḥameemun Therefor hath he no lover here this day,
036 walā ṭaʿāmun illā min ġisleenin Nor any food save filth
037 lā ya/kuluhu illā alḫati-oona Which none but sinners eat.
038 falā oqsimu bimā tubṣiroona But nay! I swear by all that ye see
039 wamā lā tubṣiroona And all that ye see not
040 innahu laqawlu rasoolin kareemin That it is indeed the speech of an illustrious messenger.
041 wamā huwa biqawli šāʿirin qaleelan mā tu/minoona It is not poet's speech - little is it that ye believe!
042 walā biqawli kāhinin qaleelan mā taḏakkaroona Nor diviner's speech - little is it that ye remember!
043 tanzeelun min rabbi alʿālameena It is a revelation from the Lord of the Worlds.
044 walaw taqawwala ʿalaynā baʿḍa al-aqāweeli And if he had invented false sayings concerning Us,
045 laaḫaḏnā minhu bialyameeni We assuredly had taken him by the right hand
046 thumma laqaṭaʿnā minhu alwateena And then severed his life-artery,
047 famā minkum min aḥadin ʿanhu hajizeena And not one of you could have held Us off from him.
048 wa-innahu lataḏkiratun lilmuttaqeena And lo! it is a warrant unto those who ward off (evil).
049 wa-innā lanaʿlamu anna minkum mukaṯṯibeena And lo! We know that some among you will deny (it).
050 wa-innahu laḥasratun ʿalā alkāfireena And lo! it is indeed an anguish for the disbelievers.
051 wa-innahu laḥaqqu alyaqeeni And lo! it is absolute truth.
52 
fasabbiḥ biismi rabbika alʿaḏeemi
So glorify the name of thy Tremendous Lord.
