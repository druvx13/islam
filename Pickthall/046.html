46. al-Ahqaf: The Sandhills

001 [romanization missing] Ha. Mim.
002 tanzeelu alkitābi mina allāhi alʿazeezi alḥakeemi The revelation of the Scripture is from Allah the Mighty, the Wise.
003 mā ḫalaqnā alssamāwāti waal-arḍa wamā baynahumā illā bialḥaqqi waajalin musamman waallaḏeena kafaroo ʿammā onḏiroo muʿriḍoona We created not the heavens and the earth and all that is between them save with truth, and for a term appointed. But those who disbelieve turn away from that whereof they are warned.
004 qul araaytum mā tadʿoona min dooni allāhi aroonee maṯa ḫalaqoo mina al-arḍi am lahum širkun fee alssamāwāti eetoonee bikitābin min qabli haṯa aw aṯāratin min ʿilmin in kuntum sadiqeena Say (unto them, O Muhammad): Have ye thought on all that ye invoke beside Allah ? Show me what they have created of the earth. Or have they any portion in the heavens ? Bring me a scripture before this (Scripture), or some vestige of knowledge (in support of what ye say), if ye are truthful.
005 waman aḍallu mimman yadʿoo min dooni allāhi man lā yastajeebu lahu ilā yawmi alqiyāmati wahum ʿan duʿā-ihim ġāfiloona And who is further astray than those who, instead of Allah, pray unto such as hear not their prayer until the Day of Resurrection, and are unconscious of their prayer,
006 wa-iṯa ḥušira alnnāsu kānoo lahum aʿdāan wakānoo biʿibādatihim kāfireena And when mankind are gathered (to the Judgment) will become enemies for them, and will become deniers of having been worshipped.
007 wa-iṯa tutlā ʿalayhim āyātunā bayyinātin qāla allāḏeena kafaroo lilḥaqqi lammā jāahum haṯa siḥrun mubeenun And when Our clear revelations are recited unto them, those who disbelieve say of the Truth when it reacheth them: This is mere magic.
008 am yaqooloona iftarāhu qul ini iftaraytuhu falā tamlikoona lee mina allāhi šay-an huwa aʿlamu bimā tufeeḍoona feehi kafā bihi šaheedan baynee wabaynakum wahuwa alġafooru alrraḥeemu Or say they: He hath invented it ? Say (O Muhammad): If I have invented it, still ye have no power to support me against Allah. He is Best Aware of what ye say among yourselves concerning it. He sufficeth for a witness between me and you. And He is the Forgiving, the Merciful.
009 qul mā kuntu bidʿan mina alrrusuli wamā adree mā yufʿalu bee walā bikum in attabiʿu illā mā yooha ilayya wamā anā illā naḏeerun mubeenun Say: I am no new thing among the messengers (of Allah), nor know I what will be done with me or with you. I do but follow that which is inspired in me, and I am but a plain warner.
010 qul araaytum in kāna min ʿindi allāhi wakafartum bihi wašahida šāhidun min banee isrā-eela ʿalā miṯlihi faāmana waistakbartum inna allāha lā yahdee alqawma alṯṯālimeena Bethink you: If it is from Allah and ye disbelieve therein, and a witness of the Children of Israel hath already testified to the like thereof and hath believed, and ye are too proud (what plight is yours) ? Lo! Allah guideth not wrong-doing folk.
011 waqāla allāḏeena kafaroo lillaḏeena āmanoo law kāna ḫayran mā sabaqoonā ilayhi wa-iḏ lam yahtadoo bihi fasayaqooloona haṯa ifkun qadeemun And those who disbelieve say of those who believe: If it had been (any) good, they would not have been before us in attaining it. And since they will not be guided by it, they say: This is an ancient lie;
012 wamin qablihi kitābu moosā imāman waraḥmatan wahaṯa kitābun muṣaddiqun lisānan ʿarabiyyan liyunḏira allāḏeena ḏalamoo wabušrā lilmuḥsineena When before it there was the Scripture of Moses, an example and a mercy; and this is a confirming Scripture in the Arabic language, that it may warn those who do wrong and bring good tidings for the righteous.
013 inna allāḏeena qāloo rabbunā allāhu ṯumma istaqāmoo falā ḫawfun ʿalayhim walā hum yaḥzanoona Lo! those who say: Our Lord is Allah, and thereafter walk aright, there shall no fear come upon them neither shall they grieve.
014 olā-ika aṣ-habu aljannati ḫālideena feehā jazāan bimā kānoo yaʿmaloona Such are rightful owners of the Garden, immortal therein, as a reward for what they used to do.
015 wawaṣaynā al-insāna biwālidayhi iḥsānan ḥamalat-hu ommuhu kurhan wawaḍaʿat-hu kurhan waḥamluhu wafisaluhu ṯalāṯoona šahran ḥattā iṯa balaġa ašuddahu wabalaġa arbaʿeena sanatan qāla rabbi awziʿnee an aškura niʿmataka allātee anʿamta ʿalayya waʿalā wālidayya waan aʿmala saliḥan tardahu waaṣliḥ lee fee ḏurriyyatee innee tubtu ilayka wa-innee mina almuslimeena And We have commended unto man kindness toward parents. His mother beareth him with reluctance, and bringeth him forth with reluctance, and the bearing of him and the weaning of him is thirty months, till, when he attaineth full strength and reacheth forty years, he saith: My Lord! Arouse me that I may give thanks for the favour wherewith Thou hast favoured me and my parents, and that I may do right acceptable unto Thee. And be gracious unto me in the matter of my seed. Lo! I have turned unto Thee repentant, and lo! I am of those who surrender (unto Thee).
016 olā-ika allāḏeena nataqabbalu ʿanhum aḥsana mā ʿamiloo wanatajāwazu ʿan sayyi-ātihim fee aṣ-habi aljannati waʿda alṣidqi allāḏee kānoo yooʿadoona Those are they from whom We accept the best of what they do, and overlook their evil deeds. (They are) among the owners of the Garden. This is the true promise which they were promised (in the world).
017 waallaḏee qāla liwālidayhi offin lakumā ataʿidāninee an oḫraja waqad ḫalati alquroonu min qablee wahumā yastaġeeṯāni allāha waylaka āmin inna waʿda allāhi ḥaqqun fayaqoolu mā haṯa illā asateeru al-awwaleena And whoso saith unto his parents: Fie upon you both! Do ye threaten me that I shall be brought forth (again) when generations before me have passed away ? And they twain cry unto Allah for help (and say): Woe unto thee! Believe! Lo! the promise of Allah is true. But he saith: This is naught save fables of the men of old:
018 olā-ika allāḏeena ḥaqqa ʿalayhimu alqawlu fee umamin qad ḫalat min qablihim mina aljinni waal-insi innahum kānoo ḫāsireena Such are those on whom the Word concerning nations of the jinn and mankind which have passed away before them hath effect. Lo! they are the losers.
019 walikullin darajātun mimmā ʿamiloo waliyuwaffiyahum aʿmālahum wahum lā yuḏlamoona And for all there will be ranks from what they do, that He may pay them for their deeds; and they will not be wronged.
020 wayawma yuʿraḍu allāḏeena kafaroo ʿalā alnnāri aḏhabtum ṭayyibātikum fee ḥayātikumu alddunyā waistamtaʿtum bihā faalyawma tujzawna ʿaṯaba alhooni bimā kuntum tastakbiroona fee al-arḍi biġayri alḥaqqi wabimā kuntum tafsuqoona And on the day when those who disbelieve are exposed to the Fire (it will be said): Ye squandered your good things in the life of the world and sought comfort therein. Now this day ye are rewarded with the doom of ignominy because ye were disdainful in the land without a right, and because ye used to transgress.
021 waoḏkur aḫā ʿādin iḏ anḏara qawmahu bial-aḥqāfi waqad ḫalati alnnuḏuru min bayni yadayhi wamin ḫalfihi allā taʿbudoo illā allāha innee aḫāfu ʿalaykum ʿaṯaba yawmin ʿaḏeemin And make mention (O Muhammad) of the brother of A'ad when he warned his folk among the wind-curved sandhills - and verily warners came and went before and after him - saying: Serve none but Allah. Lo! I fear for you the doom of a tremendous Day.
022 qāloo aji/tanā lita/fikanā ʿan ālihatinā fa/tinā bimā taʿidunā in kunta mina alssadiqeena They said: Hast come to turn us away from our gods ? Then bring upon us that wherewith thou threatenest us, if thou art of the truthful.
023 qāla innamā alʿilmu ʿinda allāhi waoballiġukum mā orsiltu bihi walākinnee arākum qawman tajhaloona He said: The knowledge is with Allah only. I convey unto you that wherewith I have been sent, but I see you are a folk that know not.
024 falammā raawhu ʿāriḍan mustaqbila awdiyatihim qāloo haṯa ʿāriḍun mumṭirunā bal huwa mā istaʿjaltum bihi reeḥun feehā ʿaṯabun aleemun Then, when they beheld it as a dense cloud coming toward their valleys, they said: Here is a cloud bringing us rain. Nay, but it is that which ye did seek to hasten, a wind wherein is painful torment,
025 tudammiru kulla šay-in bi-amri rabbihā faaṣbaḥoo lā yurā illā masākinuhum kaṯalika najzee alqawma almujrimeena Destroying all things by commandment of its Lord. And morning found them so that naught could be seen save their dwellings. Thus do We reward the guilty folk.
026 walaqad makkannāhum feemā in makkannākum feehi wajaʿalnā lahum samʿan waabsaran waaf-idatan famā aġnā ʿanhum samʿuhum walā absaruhum walā af-idatuhum min šay-in iḏ kānoo yajḥadoona bi-āyāti allāhi wahaqa bihim mā kānoo bihi yastahzi-oona And verily We had empowered them with that wherewith We have not empowered you, and had assigned them ears and eyes and hearts; but their ears and eyes and hearts availed them naught since they denied the revelations of Allah; and what they used to mock befell them.
027 walaqad ahlaknā mā ḥawlakum mina alqurā waṣarrafnā al-āyāti laʿallahum yarjiʿoona And verily We have destroyed townships round about you, and displayed (for them) Our revelation, that haply they might return.
028 falawlā naṣarahumu allāḏeena ittaḫaḏoo min dooni allāhi qurbānan ālihatan bal ḍalloo ʿanhum waṯalika ifkuhum wamā kānoo yaftaroona Then why did those whom they had chosen for gods as a way of approach (unto Allah) not help them ? Nay, but they did fail them utterly. And (all) that was their lie, and what they used to invent.
029 wa-iḏ ṣarafnā ilayka nafaran mina aljinni yastamiʿoona alqur-āna falammā ḥaḍaroohu qāloo anṣitoo falammā quḍiya wallaw ilā qawmihim munḏireena And when We inclined toward thee (Muhammad) certain of the jinn, who wished to hear the Qur'an and, when they were in its presence, said: Give ear! and, when it was finished, turned back to their people, warning.
030 qāloo yā qawmanā innā samiʿnā kitāban onzila min baʿdi moosā muṣaddiqan limā bayna yadayhi yahdee ilā alḥaqqi wa-ilā ṭareeqin mustaqeemin They said: O our people! Lo! we have heard a scripture which hath been revealed after Moses, confirming that which was before it, guiding unto the truth and a right road.
031 yā qawmanā ajeeboo dāʿiya allāhi waāminoo bihi yaġfir lakum min ḏunoobikum wayujirkum min ʿaṯabin aleemin O our people! respond to Allah's summoner and believe in Him. He will forgive you some of your sins and guard you from a painful doom.
032 waman lā yujib dāʿiya allāhi falaysa bimuʿjizin fee al-arḍi walaysa lahu min doonihi awliyāa olā-ika fee ḍalālin mubeenin And whoso respondeth not to Allah's summoner he can nowise escape in the earth, and he hath no protecting friends instead of Him. Such are in error manifest.
033 awa lam yaraw anna allāha allāḏee ḫalaqa alssamāwāti waal-arḍa walam yaʿya biḫalqihinna biqādirin ʿalā an yuḥyiya almawtā balā innahu ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerun Have they not seen that Allah, Who created the heavens and the earth and was not wearied by their creation, is Able to give life to the dead ? Aye, He verily is Able to do all things.
034 wayawma yuʿraḍu allāḏeena kafaroo ʿalā alnnāri alaysa haṯa bialḥaqqi qāloo balā warabbinā qāla faḏooqoo alʿaṯaba bimā kuntum takfuroona And on the day when those who disbelieve are exposed to the Fire (they will be asked): Is not this real ? They will say: Yea, by our Lord. He will say: Then taste the doom for that ye disbelieved.
35 faiṣbir kamā ṣabara oloo alʿazmi mina alrrusuli walā tastaʿjil lahum kaannahum yawma yarawna mā yooʿadoona lam yalbaṯoo illā sāʿatan min nahārin balāġun fahal yuhlaku illā alqawmu alfāsiqoona [Could not extract English for verse 35]
