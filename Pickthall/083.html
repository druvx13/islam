83. at-Tatfif: The Defrauders

1 
waylun lilmuṭaffifeena
Woe unto the defrauders:

2 
allāḏeena iṯa iktāloo ʿalā alnnāsi yastawfoona
Those who when they take the measure from mankind demand it full,

3 
wa-iṯa kāloohum aw wazanoohum yuḫsiroona
But if they measure unto them or weight for them, they cause them loss.

4 
alā yaḏunnu olā-ika annahum mabʿooṯoona
Do such (men) not consider that they will be raised again

5 
liyawmin ʿaḏeemin
Unto an Awful Day,

6 
yawma yaqoomu alnnāsu lirabbi alʿālameena
The day when (all) mankind stand before the Lord of the Worlds ?

7 
kallā inna kitāba alfujjāri lafee sijjeenin
Nay, but the record of the vile is in Sijjin -

8 
wamā adrāka mā sijjeenun
Ah! what will convey unto thee what Sijjin is! -

9 
kitābun marqoomun
A written record.

10 
waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena
Woe unto the repudiators on that day!

11 
allāḏeena yukaṯṯiboona biyawmi alddeeni
Those who deny the Day of Judgment

12 
wamā yukaṯṯibu bihi illā kullu muʿtadin aṯeemin
Which none denieth save each criminal transgressor,

13 
iṯa tutlā ʿalayhi āyātunā qāla asateeru al-awwaleena
Who, when thou readest unto him Our revelations, saith: (Mere) fables of the men of old.

14 
kallā bal rāna ʿalā quloobihim mā kānoo yaksiboona
Nay, but that which they have earned is rust upon their hearts.

15 
kallā innahum ʿan rabbihim yawma-iḏin lamaḥjooboona
Nay, but surely on that day they will be covered from (the mercy of) their Lord.

16 
thumma innahum lasaloo aljaḥeemi
Then lo! they verily will burn in hell,

17 
thumma yuqālu haṯa allāḏee kuntum bihi tukaṯṯiboona
And it will be said (unto them): This is that which ye used to deny.

18 
kallā inna kitāba al-abrāri lafee ʿilliyyeena
Nay, but the record of the righteous is in 'Illiyin -

19 
wamā adrāka mā ʿilliyyoona
Ah, what will convey unto thee what 'Illiyin is! -

20 
kitābun marqoomun
A written record,

21 
yašhaduhu almuqarraboona
Attested by those who are brought near (unto their Lord).

22 
inna al-abrāra lafee naʿeemin
Lo! the righteous verily are in delight,

23 
ʿalā al-arā-iki yanḏuroona
On couches, gazing,

24 
taʿrifu fee wujoohihim naḍrata alnnaʿeemi
Thou wilt know in their faces the radiance of delight.

25 
yusqawna min raḥeeqin maḫtoomin
They are given to drink of a pure wine, sealed,

26 
khitāmuhu miskun wafee ṯalika falyatanāfasi almutanāfisoona
Whose seal is musk - for this let (all) those strive who strive for bliss -

27 
wamizājuhu min tasneemin
And mixed with water of Tasnim,

28 
ʿaynan yašrabu bihā almuqarraboona
A spring whence those brought near (to Allah) drink.

29 
inna allāḏeena ajramoo kānoo mina allāḏeena āmanoo yadhakoona
Lo! the guilty used to laugh at those who believed,

30 
wa-iṯa marroo bihim yataġāmazoona
And wink one to another when they passed them;

31 
wa-iṯa inqalaboo ilā ahlihimu inqalaboo fakiheena
And when they returned to their own folk, they returned jesting;

32 
wa-iṯa raawhum qāloo inna hāolā-i ladalloona
And when they saw them they said: Lo! these have gone astray.

33 
wamā orsiloo ʿalayhim hafiḏeena
Yet they were not sent as guardians over them.

34 
faalyawma allāḏeena āmanoo mina alkuffāri yadhakoona
This day it is those who believe who have the laugh of disbelievers,

35 
ʿalā al-arā-iki yanḏuroona
On high couches, gazing.

36 
hal ṯuwwiba alkuffāru mā kānoo yafʿaloona
Are not the disbelievers paid for what they used to do ?
