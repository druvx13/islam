83. at-Tatfif: The Defrauders

001 waylun lilmuṭaffifeena Woe unto the defrauders:
002 allāḏeena iṯa iktāloo ʿalā alnnāsi yastawfoona Those who when they take the measure from mankind demand it full,
003 wa-iṯa kāloohum aw wazanoohum yuḫsiroona But if they measure unto them or weight for them, they cause them loss.
004 alā yaḏunnu olā-ika annahum mabʿooṯoona Do such (men) not consider that they will be raised again
005 liyawmin ʿaḏeemin Unto an Awful Day,
006 yawma yaqoomu alnnāsu lirabbi alʿālameena The day when (all) mankind stand before the Lord of the Worlds ?
007 kallā inna kitāba alfujjāri lafee sijjeenin Nay, but the record of the vile is in Sijjin -
008 wamā adrāka mā sijjeenun Ah! what will convey unto thee what Sijjin is! -
009 kitābun marqoomun A written record.
010 waylun yawma-iḏin lilmukaṯṯibeena Woe unto the repudiators on that day!
011 allāḏeena yukaṯṯiboona biyawmi alddeeni Those who deny the Day of Judgment
012 wamā yukaṯṯibu bihi illā kullu muʿtadin aṯeemin Which none denieth save each criminal transgressor,
013 iṯa tutlā ʿalayhi āyātunā qāla asateeru al-awwaleena Who, when thou readest unto him Our revelations, saith: (Mere) fables of the men of old.
014 kallā bal rāna ʿalā quloobihim mā kānoo yaksiboona Nay, but that which they have earned is rust upon their hearts.
015 kallā innahum ʿan rabbihim yawma-iḏin lamaḥjooboona Nay, but surely on that day they will be covered from (the mercy of) their Lord.
016 thumma innahum lasaloo aljaḥeemi Then lo! they verily will burn in hell,
017 thumma yuqālu haṯa allāḏee kuntum bihi tukaṯṯiboona And it will be said (unto them): This is that which ye used to deny.
018 kallā inna kitāba al-abrāri lafee ʿilliyyeena Nay, but the record of the righteous is in 'Illiyin -
019 wamā adrāka mā ʿilliyyoona Ah, what will convey unto thee what 'Illiyin is! -
020 kitābun marqoomun A written record,
021 yašhaduhu almuqarraboona Attested by those who are brought near (unto their Lord).
022 inna al-abrāra lafee naʿeemin Lo! the righteous verily are in delight,
023 ʿalā al-arā-iki yanḏuroona On couches, gazing,
024 taʿrifu fee wujoohihim naḍrata alnnaʿeemi Thou wilt know in their faces the radiance of delight.
025 yusqawna min raḥeeqin maḫtoomin They are given to drink of a pure wine, sealed,
026 khitāmuhu miskun wafee ṯalika falyatanāfasi almutanāfisoona Whose seal is musk - for this let (all) those strive who strive for bliss -
027 wamizājuhu min tasneemin And mixed with water of Tasnim,
028 ʿaynan yašrabu bihā almuqarraboona A spring whence those brought near (to Allah) drink.
029 inna allāḏeena ajramoo kānoo mina allāḏeena āmanoo yadhakoona Lo! the guilty used to laugh at those who believed,
030 wa-iṯa marroo bihim yataġāmazoona And wink one to another when they passed them;
031 wa-iṯa inqalaboo ilā ahlihimu inqalaboo fakiheena And when they returned to their own folk, they returned jesting;
032 wa-iṯa raawhum qāloo inna hāolā-i ladalloona And when they saw them they said: Lo! these have gone astray.
033 wamā orsiloo ʿalayhim hafiḏeena Yet they were not sent as guardians over them.
034 faalyawma allāḏeena āmanoo mina alkuffāri yadhakoona This day it is those who believe who have the laugh of disbelievers,
035 ʿalā al-arā-iki yanḏuroona On high couches, gazing.
36 hal ṯuwwiba alkuffāru mā kānoo yafʿaloona [Could not extract English for verse 36]
