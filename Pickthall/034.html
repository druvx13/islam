34. Saba': Sheba

001 alḥamdu lillāhi allāḏee lahu mā fee alssamāwāti wamā fee al-arḍi walahu alḥamdu fee al-āḫirati wahuwa alḥakeemu alḫabeeru Praise be to Allah, unto Whom belongeth whatsoever is in the heavens and whatsoever is in the earth. His is the praise in the Hereafter, and He is the Wise, the Aware.
002 yaʿlamu mā yaliju fee al-arḍi wamā yaḫruju minhā wamā yanzilu mina alssamā-i wamā yaʿruju feehā wahuwa alrraḥeemu alġafooru He knoweth that which goeth into the earth and that which cometh forth from it, and that descendeth from the heaven and that which ascendeth into it. He is the Merciful, the Forgiving.
003 waqāla allāḏeena kafaroo lā ta/teenā alssāʿatu qul balā warabbee lata/tiyannakum ʿālimi alġaybi lā yaʿzubu ʿanhu miṯqālu ḏarratin fee alssamāwāti walā fee al-arḍi walā aṣġaru min ṯalika walā akbaru illā fee kitābin mubeenin Those who disbelieve say: The Hour will never come unto us. Say: Nay, by my Lord, but it is coming unto you surely. (He is) the Knower of the Unseen. Not an atom's weight, or less than that or greater, escapeth Him in the heavens or in the earth, but it is in a clear Record,
004 liyajziya allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati olā-ika lahum maġfiratun warizqun kareemun That He may reward those who believe and do good works. For them is pardon and a rich provision.
005 waallaḏeena saʿaw fee āyātinā muʿājizeena olā-ika lahum ʿaṯabun min rijzin aleemin But those who strive against Our revelations, challenging (Us), theirs will be a painful doom of wrath.
006 wayarā allāḏeena ootoo alʿilma allāḏee onzila ilayka min rabbika huwa alḥaqqa wayahdee ilā ṣirati alʿazeezi alḥameedi Those who have been given knowledge see that what is revealed unto thee from thy Lord is the truth and leadeth unto the path of the Mighty, the Owner of Praise.
007 waqāla allāḏeena kafaroo hal nadullukum ʿalā rajulin yunabbi-okum iṯa muzziqtum kulla mumazzaqin innakum lafee ḫalqin jadeedin Those who disbelieve say: Shall we show you a man who will tell you (that) when ye have become dispersed in dust with most complete dispersal still, even then, ye will be created anew ?
008 aftarā ʿalā allāhi kaḏiban am bihi jinnatun bali allāḏeena lā yu/minoona bial-āḫirati fee alʿaṯabi waalḍalāli albaʿeedi Hath he invented a lie concerning Allah, or is there in him a madness ? Nay, but those who disbelieve in the Hereafter are in torment and far error.
009 afalam yaraw ilā mā bayna aydeehim wamā ḫalfahum mina alssamā-i waal-arḍi in naša/ naḫsif bihimu al-arḍa aw nusqiṭ ʿalayhim kisafan mina alssamā-i inna fee ṯalika laāyatan likulli ʿabdin muneebin Have they not observed what is before them and what is behind them of the sky and the earth ? If We will, We can make the earth swallow them, or cause obliteration from the sky to fall on them. Lo! herein surely is a portent for every slave who turneth (to Allah) repentant.
010 walaqad ātaynā dāwooda minnā faḍlan yā jibālu awwibee maʿahu waalttayra waalannā lahu alḥadeeda And assuredly We gave David grace from Us, (saying): O ye hills and birds, echo his psalms of praise! And We made the iron supple unto him,
011 ani iʿmal sābiġātin waqaddir fee alssardi waiʿmaloo saliḥan innee bimā taʿmaloona baṣeerun Saying: Make thou long coats of mail and measure the links (thereof). And do ye right. Lo! I am Seer of what ye do.
012 walisulaymāna alrreeḥa ġuduwwuhā šahrun warawahuhā šahrun waasalnā lahu ʿayna alqiṭri wamina aljinni man yaʿmalu bayna yadayhi bi-iḏni rabbihi waman yaziġ minhum ʿan amrinā nuḏiqhu min ʿaṯabi alssaʿeeri And unto Solomon (We gave) the wind, whereof the morning course was a month's journey and the evening course a month's journey, and We caused the fount of copper to gush forth for him, and (We gave him) certain of the jinn who worked before him by permission of his Lord. And such of them as deviated from Our command, them We caused to taste the punishment of flaming Fire.
013 yaʿmaloona lahu mā yašāo min mahareeba watamāṯeela wajifānin kaaljawābi waqudoorin rāsiyātin iʿmaloo āla dāwooda šukran waqaleelun min ʿibādiya alššakooru They made for him what he willed: synagogues and statues, basins like wells and boilers built into the ground. Give thanks, O House of David! Few of My bondmen are thankful.
014 falammā qaḍaynā ʿalayhi almawta mā dallahum ʿalā mawtihi illā dābbatu al-arḍi ta/kulu minsaatahu falammā ḫarra tabayyanati aljinnu an law kānoo yaʿlamoona alġayba mā labiṯoo fee alʿaṯabi almuheeni And when We decreed death for him, nothing showed his death to them save a creeping creature of the earth which gnawed away his staff. And when he fell the jinn saw clearly how, if they had known the Unseen, they would not have continued in despised toil.
015 laqad kāna lisaba-in fee maskanihim āyatun jannatāni ʿan yameenin wašimālin kuloo min rizqi rabbikum waoškuroo lahu baldatun ṭayyibatun warabbun ġafoorun There was indeed a sign for Sheba in their dwelling-place: Two gardens on the right hand and the left (as who should say): Eat of the provision of your Lord and render thanks to Him. A fair land and an indulgent Lord!
016 faaʿraḍoo faarsalnā ʿalayhim sayla alʿarimi wabaddalnāhum bijannatayhim jannatayni ḏawātay okulin ḫamṭin waaṯlin wašay-in min sidrin qaleelin But they were froward, so We sent on them the flood of 'Iram, and in exchange for their two gardens gave them two gardens bearing bitter fruit, the tamarisk and here and there a lote-tree.
017 [romanization missing] This We awarded them because of their ingratitude. Punish We ever any save the ingrates ?
018 wajaʿalnā baynahum wabayna alqurā allātee bāraknā feehā quran ḏāhiratan waqaddarnā feehā alssayra seeroo feehā layāliya waayyāman āmineena And We set, between them and the towns which We had blessed, towns easy to be seen, and We made the stage between them easy, (saying): Travel in them safely both by night and day.
019 faqāloo rabbanā bāʿid bayna asfārinā waḏalamoo anfusahum fajaʿalnāhum ahadeeṯa wamazzaqnāhum kulla mumazzaqin inna fee ṯalika laāyātin likulli ṣabbārin šakoorin But they said: Our Lord! Make the stage between our journeys longer. And they wronged themselves, therefore We made them bywords (in the land) and scattered them abroad, a total scattering. Lo! herein verily are portents for each steadfast, grateful (heart).
020 walaqad ṣaddaqa ʿalayhim ibleesu ḏannahu faittabaʿoohu illā fareeqan mina almu/mineena And Satan indeed found his calculation true concerning them, for they follow him, all save a group of true believers.
021 wamā kāna lahu ʿalayhim min sultanin illā linaʿlama man yu/minu bial-āḫirati mimman huwa minhā fee šakkin warabbuka ʿalā kulli šay-in ḥafeeḏun And he had no warrant whatsoever against them, save that We would know him who believeth in the Hereafter from him who is in doubt thereof; and thy Lord (O Muhammad) taketh note of all things.
022 quli odʿoo allāḏeena zaʿamtum min dooni allāhi lā yamlikoona miṯqāla ḏarratin fee alssamāwāti walā fee al-arḍi wamā lahum feehimā min širkin wamā lahu minhum min ḏaheerin Say (O Muhammad): Call upon those whom ye set up beside Allah! They possess not an atom's weight either in the heavens or in the earth, nor have they any share in either, nor hath He an auxiliary among them.
023 walā tanfaʿu alššafāʿatu ʿindahu illā liman aḏina lahu ḥattā iṯa fuzziʿa ʿan quloobihim qāloo maṯa qāla rabbukum qāloo alḥaqqa wahuwa alʿaliyyu alkabeeru No intercession availeth with Him save for him whom He permitteth. Yet, when fear is banished from their hearts, they say: What was it that your Lord said ? They say: The Truth. And He is the Sublime, the Great.
024 qul man yarzuqukum mina alssamāwāti waal-arḍi quli allāhu wa-innā aw iyyākum laʿalā hudan aw fee ḍalālin mubeenin Say: Who giveth you provision from the sky and the earth ? Say: Allah, Lo! we or you assuredly are rightly guided or in error manifest.
025 qul lā tus-aloona ʿammā ajramnā walā nus-alu ʿammā taʿmaloona Say: Ye will not be asked of what we committed, nor shall we be asked of what ye do.
026 qul yajmaʿu baynanā rabbunā ṯumma yaftaḥu baynanā bialḥaqqi wahuwa alfattahu alʿaleemu Say: Our Lord will bring us all together, then He will judge between us with truth. He is the All-knowing Judge.
027 qul arooniya allāḏeena alḥaqtum bihi šurakāa kallā bal huwa allāhu alʿazeezu alḥakeemu Say: Show me those whom ye have joined unto Him as partners. Nay (ye dare not)! For He is Allah, the Mighty, the Wise.
028 wamā arsalnāka illā kāffatan lilnnāsi bašeeran wanaḏeeran walākinna akṯara alnnāsi lā yaʿlamoona And We have not sent thee (O Muhammad) save as a bringer of good tidings and a warner unto all mankind; but most of mankind know not.
029 wayaqooloona matā haṯa alwaʿdu in kuntum sadiqeena And they say: When is this promise (to be fulfilled) if ye are truthful ?
030 qul lakum meeʿādu yawmin lā tasta/ḫiroona ʿanhu sāʿatan walā tastaqdimoona Say (O Muhammad): Yours is the promise of a Day which ye cannot postpone nor hasten by an hour.
031 waqāla allāḏeena kafaroo lan nu/mina bihaṯa alqur-āni walā biallaḏee bayna yadayhi walaw tarā iḏi alṯṯālimoona mawqoofoona ʿinda rabbihim yarjiʿu baʿḍuhum ilā baʿḍin alqawla yaqoolu allāḏeena istuḍʿifoo lillaḏeena istakbaroo lawlā antum lakunnā mu/mineena And those who disbelieve say: We believe not in this Qur'an nor in that which was before it; but oh, if thou couldst see, when the wrong-doers are brought up before their Lord, how they cast the blame one to another; how those who were despised (in the earth) say unto those who were proud: But for you, we should have been believers.
032 qāla allāḏeena istakbaroo lillaḏeena istuḍʿifoo anaḥnu ṣadadnākum ʿani alhudā baʿda iḏ jāakum bal kuntum mujrimeena Those who were proud say unto those who were despised: Did we drive you away from the guidance after it had come unto you ? Nay, but ye were guilty.
033 waqāla allāḏeena istuḍʿifoo lillaḏeena istakbaroo bal makru allāyli waalnnahāri iḏ ta/muroonanā an nakfura biallāhi wanajʿala lahu andādan waasarroo alnnadāmata lammā raawoo alʿaṯaba wajaʿalnā al-aġlāla fee aʿnāqi allāḏeena kafaroo hal yujzawna illā mā kānoo yaʿmaloona Those who were despised say unto those who were proud: Nay but (it was your) scheming night and day, when ye commanded us to disbelieve in Allah and set up rivals unto Him. And they are filled with remorse when they behold the doom; and We place carcans on the necks of those who disbelieved. Are they requited aught save what they used to do ?
034 wamā arsalnā fee qaryatin min naḏeerin illā qāla mutrafoohā innā bimā orsiltum bihi kāfiroona And We sent not unto any township a warner, but its pampered ones declared: Lo! we are disbelievers in that wherewith ye have been sent.
035 waqāloo naḥnu akṯaru amwālan waawlādan wamā naḥnu bimuʿaṯṯabeena And they say: We are more (than you) in wealth and children. We are not the punished!
036 qul inna rabbee yabsuṭu alrrizqa liman yašāo wayaqdiru walākinna akṯara alnnāsi lā yaʿlamoona Say (O Muhammad): Lo! my Lord enlargeth the provision for whom He will and narroweth it (for whom He will). But most of mankind know not.
037 wamā amwālukum walā awlādukum biallatee tuqarribukum ʿindanā zulfā illā man āmana waʿamila saliḥan faolā-ika lahum jazāo alḍiʿfi bimā ʿamiloo wahum fee alġurufāti āminoona And it is not your wealth nor your children that will bring you near unto Us, but he who believeth and doeth good (he draweth near). As for such, theirs will be twofold reward for what they did and they will dwell secure in lofty halls.
038 waallaḏeena yasʿawna fee āyātinā muʿājizeena olā-ika fee alʿaṯabi muhdaroona And as for those who strive against Our revelations, challenging, they will be brought to the doom.
039 qul inna rabbee yabsuṭu alrrizqa liman yašāo min ʿibādihi wayaqdiru lahu wamā anfaqtum min šay-in fahuwa yuḫlifuhu wahuwa ḫayru alrrāziqeena Say: Lo! my Lord enlargeth the provision for whom He will of His bondmen, and narroweth (it) for him. And whatsoever ye spend (for good) He replaceth it. And He is the Best of Providers.
040 wayawma yaḥšuruhum jameeʿan ṯumma yaqoolu lilmalā-ikati ahāolā-i iyyākum kānoo yaʿbudoona And on the day when He will gather them all together, He will say unto the angels: Did these worship you ?
041 qāloo subhanaka anta waliyyunā min doonihim bal kānoo yaʿbudoona aljinna akṯaruhum bihim mu/minoona They will say: Be Thou Glorified. Thou (alone) art our Guardian, not them! Nay, but they worshipped the jinn; most of them were believers in them.
042 faalyawma lā yamliku baʿḍukum libaʿḍin nafʿan walā ḍarran wanaqoolu lillaḏeena ḏalamoo ḏooqoo ʿaṯaba alnnāri allātee kuntum bihā tukaṯṯiboona That day ye will possess no use nor hurt one for another. And We shall say unto those who did wrong: Taste the doom of the Fire which ye used to deny.
043 wa-iṯa tutlā ʿalayhim āyātunā bayyinātin qāloo mā haṯa illā rajulun yureedu an yaṣuddakum ʿammā kāna yaʿbudu ābāokum waqāloo mā haṯa illā ifkun muftaran waqāla allāḏeena kafaroo lilḥaqqi lammā jāahum in haṯa illā siḥrun mubeenun And if Our revelations are recited unto them in plain terms, they say: This is naught else than a man who would turn you away from what your fathers used to worship; and they say: This is naught else than an invented lie. Those who disbelieve say of the truth when it reacheth them: This is naught else than mere magic.
044 wamā ātaynāhum min kutubin yadrusoonahā wamā arsalnā ilayhim qablaka min naḏeerin And We have given them no scriptures which they study, nor sent We unto them, before thee, any warner.
045 wakaṯṯaba allāḏeena min qablihim wamā balaġoo miʿšāra mā ātaynāhum fakaṯṯaboo rusulee fakayfa kāna nakeeri Those before them denied, and these have not attained a tithe of that which We bestowed on them (of old); yet they denied My messengers. How intense then was My abhorrence (of them)!
046 qul innamā aʿiḏukum biwahidatin an taqoomoo lillāhi maṯnā wafurādā ṯumma tatafakkaroo mā bisahibikum min jinnatin in huwa illā naḏeerun lakum bayna yaday ʿaṯabin šadeedin Say (unto them, O Muhammad): I exhort you unto one thing only: that ye awake, for Allah's sake, by twos and singly, and then reflect: There is no madness in your comrade. He is naught else than a warner unto you in face of a terrific doom.
047 qul mā saaltukum min ajrin fahuwa lakum in ajriya illā ʿalā allāhi wahuwa ʿalā kulli šay-in šaheedun Say: Whatever reward I might have asked of you is yours. My reward is the affair of Allah only. He is Witness over all things.
048 qul inna rabbee yaqḏifu bialḥaqqi ʿallāmu alġuyoobi Say: Lo! my Lord hurleth the truth. (He is) the Knower of Things Hidden.
049 qul jāa alḥaqqu wamā yubdi-o albatilu wamā yuʿeedu Say: The Truth hath come, and falsehood showeth not its face and will not return.
050 qul in ḍalaltu fa-innamā aḍillu ʿalā nafsee wa-ini ihtadaytu fabimā yooḥee ilayya rabbee innahu sameeʿun qareebun Say: If I err, I err only to my own loss, and if I am rightly guided it is because of that which my Lord hath revealed unto me. Lo! He is Hearer, Nigh.
051 walaw tarā iḏ faziʿoo falā fawta waoḫiḏoo min makānin qareebin Couldst thou but see when they are terrified with no escape, and are seized from near at hand,
052 waqāloo āmannā bihi waannā lahumu alttanāwušu min makānin baʿeedin And say: We (now) believe therein. But how can they reach (faith) from afar off,
053 waqad kafaroo bihi min qablu wayaqḏifoona bialġaybi min makānin baʿeedin When they disbelieved in it of yore. They aim at the unseen from afar off.
