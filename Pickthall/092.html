92. al-Layl: The Night

001 waallayli iṯa yaġšā By the night enshrouding
002 waalnnahāri iṯa tajallā And the day resplendent
003 wamā ḫalaqa alṯṯakara waal-onṯā And Him Who hath created male and female,
004 inna saʿyakum lašattā Lo! your effort is dispersed (toward divers ends).
005 faammā man aʿta waittaqā As for him who giveth and is dutiful (toward Allah)
006 waṣaddaqa bialḥusnā And believeth in goodness;
007 fasanuyassiruhu lilyusrā Surely We will ease his way unto the state of ease.
008 waammā man baḫila waistaġnā But as for him who hoardeth and deemeth himself independent,
009 wakaṯṯaba bialḥusnā And disbelieveth in goodness;
010 fasanuyassiruhu lilʿusrā Surely We will ease his way unto adversity.
011 wamā yuġnee ʿanhu māluhu iṯa taraddā His riches will not save him when he perisheth.
012 inna ʿalaynā lalhudā Lo! Ours it is (to give) the guidance
013 wa-inna lanā lal-āḫirata waal-oolā And lo! unto Us belong the latter portion and the former.
014 faanḏartukum nāran talaṯṯā Therefor have I warned you of the flaming Fire
015 lā yaṣlāhā illā al-ašqā Which only the most wretched must endure,
016 allāḏee kaṯṯaba watawallā He who denieth and turneth away.
017 wasayujannabuhā al-atqā Far removed from it will be the righteous
018 allāḏee yu/tee mālahu yatazakkā Who giveth his wealth that he may grow (in goodness).
019 wamā li-aḥadin ʿindahu min niʿmatin tujzā And none hath with him any favour for reward,
020 illā ibtiġāa wajhi rabbihi al-aʿlā Except as seeking (to fulfil) the purpose of his Lord Most High.
21 walasawfa yarda [Could not extract English for verse 21]
