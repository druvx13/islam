89. al-Fajr: The Dawn

1 
waalfajri
By the Dawn

2 
walayālin ʿašrin
And ten nights,

3 
waalššafʿi waalwatri
And the Even and the Odd,

4 
waallayli iṯa yasri
And the night when it departeth,

5 
hal fee ṯalika qasamun liḏee ḥijrin
There surely is an oath for thinking man.

6 
alam tara kayfa faʿala rabbuka biʿādin
Dost thou not consider how thy Lord dealt with (the tribe of) A'ad,

7 
irama ṯati alʿimādi
With many-columned Iram,

8 
allātee lam yuḫlaq miṯluhā fee albilādi
The like of which was not created in the lands;

9 
waṯamooda allāḏeena jāboo alṣaḫra bialwādi
And with (the tribe of) Thamud, who clove the rocks in the valley;

10 
wafirʿawna ḏee al-awtādi
And with Pharaoh, firm of might,

11 
allāḏeena ṭaġaw fee albilādi
Who (all) were rebellious (to Allah) in these lands,

12 
faakṯaroo feehā alfasāda
And multiplied iniquity therein ?

13 
faṣabba ʿalayhim rabbuka sawṭa ʿaṯabin
Therefore thy Lord poured on them the disaster of His punishment.

14 
inna rabbaka labialmirsadi
Lo! thy Lord is ever watchful.

15 
faammā al-insānu iṯa mā ibtalāhu rabbuhu faakramahu wanaʿʿamahu fayaqoolu rabbee akramani
As for man, whenever his Lord trieth him by honouring him, and is gracious unto him, he saith: My Lord honoureth me.

16 
waammā iṯa mā ibtalāhu faqadara ʿalayhi rizqahu fayaqoolu rabbee ahānani
But whenever He trieth him by straitening his means of life, he saith: My Lord despiseth me.

17 
kallā bal lā tukrimoona alyateema
Nay, but ye (for your part) honour not the orphan

18 
walā tahaddoona ʿalā ṭaʿāmi almiskeeni
And urge not on the feeding of the poor.

19 
wata/kuloona altturāṯa aklan lammān
And ye devour heritages with devouring greed.

20 
watuḥibboona almāla ḥubban jammān
And love wealth with abounding love.

21 
kallā iṯa dukkati al-arḍu dakkan dakkān
Nay, but when the earth is ground to atoms, grinding, grinding,

22 
wajāa rabbuka waalmalaku ṣaffan ṣaffān
And thy Lord shall come with angels, rank on rank,

23 
wajee-a yawma-iḏin bijahannama yawma-iḏin yataḏakkaru al-insānu waannā lahu alṯṯikrā
And hell is brought near that day; on that day man will remember, but how will the remembrance (then avail him) ?

24 
yaqoolu yā laytanee qaddamtu liḥayātee
He will say: Ah, would that I had sent before me (some provision) for my life!

25 
fayawma-iḏin lā yuʿaṯṯibu ʿaṯabahu aḥadun
None punisheth as He will punish on that day!

26 
walā yooṯiqu waṯāqahu aḥadun
None bindeth as He then will bind.

27 
yā ayyatuhā alnnafsu almuṭma-innatu
But ah! thou soul at peace!

28 
irjiʿee ilā rabbiki radiyatan marḍiyyatan
Return unto thy Lord, content in His good pleasure!

29 
faodḫulee fee ʿibādee
Enter thou among My bondmen!

30 
waodḫulee jannatee
Enter thou My Garden!
