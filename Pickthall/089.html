89. al-Fajr: The Dawn

001 waalfajri By the Dawn
002 walayālin ʿašrin And ten nights,
003 waalššafʿi waalwatri And the Even and the Odd,
004 waallayli iṯa yasri And the night when it departeth,
005 hal fee ṯalika qasamun liḏee ḥijrin There surely is an oath for thinking man.
006 alam tara kayfa faʿala rabbuka biʿādin Dost thou not consider how thy Lord dealt with (the tribe of) A'ad,
007 irama ṯati alʿimādi With many-columned Iram,
008 allātee lam yuḫlaq miṯluhā fee albilādi The like of which was not created in the lands;
009 waṯamooda allāḏeena jāboo alṣaḫra bialwādi And with (the tribe of) Thamud, who clove the rocks in the valley;
010 wafirʿawna ḏee al-awtādi And with Pharaoh, firm of might,
011 allāḏeena ṭaġaw fee albilādi Who (all) were rebellious (to Allah) in these lands,
012 faakṯaroo feehā alfasāda And multiplied iniquity therein ?
013 faṣabba ʿalayhim rabbuka sawṭa ʿaṯabin Therefore thy Lord poured on them the disaster of His punishment.
014 inna rabbaka labialmirsadi Lo! thy Lord is ever watchful.
015 faammā al-insānu iṯa mā ibtalāhu rabbuhu faakramahu wanaʿʿamahu fayaqoolu rabbee akramani As for man, whenever his Lord trieth him by honouring him, and is gracious unto him, he saith: My Lord honoureth me.
016 waammā iṯa mā ibtalāhu faqadara ʿalayhi rizqahu fayaqoolu rabbee ahānani But whenever He trieth him by straitening his means of life, he saith: My Lord despiseth me.
017 kallā bal lā tukrimoona alyateema Nay, but ye (for your part) honour not the orphan
018 walā tahaddoona ʿalā ṭaʿāmi almiskeeni And urge not on the feeding of the poor.
019 wata/kuloona altturāṯa aklan lammān And ye devour heritages with devouring greed.
020 watuḥibboona almāla ḥubban jammān And love wealth with abounding love.
021 kallā iṯa dukkati al-arḍu dakkan dakkān Nay, but when the earth is ground to atoms, grinding, grinding,
022 wajāa rabbuka waalmalaku ṣaffan ṣaffān And thy Lord shall come with angels, rank on rank,
023 wajee-a yawma-iḏin bijahannama yawma-iḏin yataḏakkaru al-insānu waannā lahu alṯṯikrā And hell is brought near that day; on that day man will remember, but how will the remembrance (then avail him) ?
024 yaqoolu yā laytanee qaddamtu liḥayātee He will say: Ah, would that I had sent before me (some provision) for my life!
025 fayawma-iḏin lā yuʿaṯṯibu ʿaṯabahu aḥadun None punisheth as He will punish on that day!
026 walā yooṯiqu waṯāqahu aḥadun None bindeth as He then will bind.
027 yā ayyatuhā alnnafsu almuṭma-innatu But ah! thou soul at peace!
028 irjiʿee ilā rabbiki radiyatan marḍiyyatan Return unto thy Lord, content in His good pleasure!
029 faodḫulee fee ʿibādee Enter thou among My bondmen!
30 
waodḫulee jannatee
Enter thou My Garden!
