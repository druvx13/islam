38. Sad: Sad

001 [romanization missing] Sad. By the renowned Qur'an,
002 bali allāḏeena kafaroo fee ʿizzatin wašiqāqin Nay, but those who disbelieve are in false pride and schism.
003 kam ahlaknā min qablihim min qarnin fanādaw walāta ḥeena manasin How many a generation We destroyed before them, and they cried out when it was no longer the time for escape!
004 waʿajiboo an jāahum munḏirun minhum waqāla alkāfiroona haṯa sahirun kaṯṯabun And they marvel that a warner from among themselves hath come unto them, and the disbelievers say: This is a wizard, a charlatan.
005 ajaʿala al-ālihata ilāhan wahidan inna haṯa lašay-on ʿujābun Maketh he the gods One God ? Lo! that is an astounding thing.
006 wainṭalaqa almalao minhum ani imšoo waiṣbiroo ʿalā ālihatikum inna haṯa lašay-on yurādu The chiefs among them go about, exhorting: Go and be staunch to your gods! Lo! this is a thing designed.
007 mā samiʿnā bihaṯa fee almillati al-āḫirati in haṯa illā iḫtilāqun We have not heard of this in later religion. This is naught but an invention.
008 aonzila ʿalayhi alṯṯikru min bayninā bal hum fee šakkin min ḏikree bal lammā yaḏooqoo ʿaṯabi Hath the reminder been unto him (alone) among us ? Nay, but they are in doubt concerning My reminder; nay but they have not yet tasted My doom.
009 am ʿindahum ḫazā-inu raḥmati rabbika alʿazeezi alwahhābi Or are theirs the treasures of the mercy of thy Lord, the Mighty, the Bestower ?
010 am lahum mulku alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wamā baynahumā falyartaqoo fee al-asbābi Or is the kingdom of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them theirs ? Then let them ascend by ropes!
011 jundun mā hunālika mahzoomun mina al-aḥzābi A defeated host are (all) the factions that are there.
012 kaṯṯabat qablahum qawmu nooḥin waʿādun wafirʿawnu ḏoo al-awtādi The folk of Noah before them denied (their messenger) and (so did the tribe of) A'ad, and Pharaoh firmly planted,
013 waṯamoodu waqawmu looṭin waaṣ-habu al-aykati olā-ika al-aḥzābu And (the tribe of) Thamud, and the folk of Lot, and the dwellers in the wood: these were the factions.
014 in kullun illā kaṯṯaba alrrusula faḥaqqa ʿiqābi Not one of them but did deny the messengers, therefor My doom was justified,
015 wamā yanḏuru hāolā-i illā ṣayḥatan wahidatan mā lahā min fawāqin These wait for but one Shout, there will be no second thereto.
016 waqāloo rabbanā ʿajjil lanā qittanā qabla yawmi alḥisābi They say: Our Lord! Hasten on for us our fate before the Day of Reckoning.
017 iṣbir ʿalā mā yaqooloona waoḏkur ʿabdanā dāwooda ṯa al-aydi innahu awwābun Bear with what they say, and remember Our bondman David, lord of might, Lo! he was ever turning in repentance (toward Allah).
018 innā saḫḫarnā aljibāla maʿahu yusabbiḥna bialʿašiyyi waal-išrāqi Lo! We subdued the hills to hymn the praises (of their Lord) with him at nightfall and sunrise,
019 waalttayra maḥšooratan kullun lahu awwābun And the birds assembled; all were turning unto Him.
020 wašadadnā mulkahu waātaynāhu alḥikmata wafaṣla alḫitabi We made his kingdom strong and gave him wisdom and decisive speech.
021 wahal atāka nabao alḫaṣmi iḏ tasawwaroo almiḥrāba And hath the story of the litigants come unto thee ? How they climbed the wall into the royal chamber;
022 iṯ daḫaloo ʿalā dāwooda fafaziʿa minhum qāloo lā taḫaf ḫaṣmāni baġā baʿḍunā ʿalā baʿḍin faoḥkum baynanā bialḥaqqi walā tušṭiṭ waihdinā ilā sawā-i alṣirati How they burst in upon David, and he was afraid of them. They said: Be not afraid! (We are) two litigants, one of whom hath wronged the other, therefor judge aright between us; be not unjust; and show us the fair way.
023 inna haṯa aḫee lahu tisʿun watisʿoona naʿjatan waliya naʿjatun wahidatun faqāla akfilneehā waʿazzanee fee alḫitabi Lo! this my brother hath ninety and nine ewes while I had one ewe; and he said: Entrust it to me, and he conquered me in speech.
024 qāla laqad ḏalamaka bisu-āli naʿjatika ilā niʿājihi wa-inna kaṯeeran mina alḫulata-i layabġee baʿḍuhum ʿalā baʿḍin illā allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati waqaleelun mā hum waḏanna dāwoodu annamā fatannāhu faistaġfara rabbahu waḫarra rākiʿan waanāba (David) said: He hath wronged thee in demanding thine ewe in addition to his ewes, and lo! many partners oppress one another, save such as believe and do good works, and they are few. And David guessed that We had tried him, and he sought forgiveness of his Lord, and he bowed himself and fell down prostrate and repented.
025 faġafarnā lahu ṯalika wa-inna lahu ʿindanā lazulfā waḥusna maābin So We forgave him that; and lo! he had access to Our presence and a happy journey's end.
026 yā dāwoodu innā jaʿalnāka ḫaleefatan fee al-arḍi faoḥkum bayna alnnāsi bialḥaqqi walā tattabiʿi alhawā fayuḍillaka ʿan sabeeli allāhi inna allāḏeena yaḍilloona ʿan sabeeli allāhi lahum ʿaṯabun šadeedun bimā nasoo yawma alḥisābi (And it was said unto him): O David! Lo! We have set thee as a viceroy in the earth; therefor judge aright between mankind, and follow not desire that it beguile thee from the way of Allah. Lo! those who wander from the way of Allah have an awful doom, forasmuch as they forgot the Day of Reckoning.
027 wamā ḫalaqnā alssamāa waal-arḍa wamā baynahumā batilan ṯalika ḏannu allāḏeena kafaroo fawaylun lillaḏeena kafaroo mina alnnāri And We created not the heaven and the earth and all that is between them in vain. That is the opinion of those who disbelieve. And woe unto those who disbelieve, from the Fire!
028 am najʿalu allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati kaalmufsideena fee al-arḍi am najʿalu almuttaqeena kaalfujjāri Shall We treat those who believe and do good works as those who spread corruption in the earth; or shall We treat the pious as the wicked ?
029 kitābun anzalnāhu ilayka mubārakun liyaddabbaroo āyātihi waliyataḏakkara oloo al-albābi (This is) a Scripture that We have revealed unto thee, full of blessing, that they may ponder its revelations, and that men of understanding may reflect.
030 wawahabnā lidāwooda sulaymāna niʿma alʿabdu innahu awwābun And We bestowed on David, Solomon. How excellent a slave! Lo! he was ever turning in repentance (toward Allah).
031 iḏ ʿuriḍa ʿalayhi bialʿašiyyi alssafinātu aljiyādu When there were shown to him at eventide lightfooted coursers
032 faqāla innee aḥbabtu ḥubba alḫayri ʿan ḏikri rabbee ḥattā tawārat bialḥijābi And he said: Lo! I have preferred the good things (of the world) to the remembrance of my Lord; till they were taken out of sight behind the curtain.
033 ruddoohā ʿalayya faṭafiqa masḥan bialssooqi waal-aʿnāqi (Then he said): Bring them back to me, and fell to slashing (with his sword their) legs and necks.
034 walaqad fatannā sulaymāna waalqaynā ʿalā kursiyyihi jasadan ṯumma anāba And verily We tried Solomon, and set upon his throne a (mere) body. Then did he repent.
035 qāla rabbi iġfir lee wahab lee mulkan lā yanbaġee li-aḥadin min baʿdee innaka anta alwahhābu He said: My Lord! Forgive me and bestow on me sovereignty such as shall not belong to any after me. Lo! Thou art the Bestower.
036 fasaḫḫarnā lahu alrreeḥa tajree bi-amrihi ruḫāan ḥayṯu asaba So We made the wind subservient unto him, setting fair by his command whithersoever he intended.
037 waalššayateena kulla bannā-in waġawwasin And the unruly, every builder and diver (made We subservient),
038 waāḫareena muqarraneena fee al-aṣfādi And others linked together in chains,
039 haṯa ʿataonā faomnun aw amsik biġayri ḥisābin (Saying): This is Our gift, so bestow thou, or withhold, without reckoning.
040 wa-inna lahu ʿindanā lazulfā waḥusna maābin And lo! he hath favour with Us, and a happy journey's end.
041 waoḏkur ʿabdanā ayyooba iḏ nādā rabbahu annee massaniya alššaytanu binuṣbin waʿaṯabin And make mention (O Muhammad) of Our bondman Job, when he cried unto his Lord (saying): Lo! the devil doth afflict me with distress and torment.
042 orkuḍ birijlika haṯa muġtasalun bāridun wašarābun (And it was said unto him): Strike the ground with thy foot. This (spring) is a cool bath and a refreshing drink.
043 wawahabnā lahu ahlahu wamiṯlahum maʿahum raḥmatan minna waḏikrā li-olee al-albābi And We bestowed on him (again) his household and therewith the like thereof, a mercy from Us, and a memorial for men of understanding.
044 waḫuḏ biyadika ḍiġṯan faiḍrib bihi walā taḥnaṯ innā wajadnāhu sabiran niʿma alʿabdu innahu awwābun And (it was said unto him): Take in thine hand a branch and smite therewith, and break not thine oath. Lo! We found him steadfast, how excellent a slave! Lo! he was ever turning in repentance (to his Lord).
045 waoḏkur ʿibādanā ibrāheema wa-ishaqa wayaʿqooba olee al-aydee waal-absari And make mention of Our bondmen, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, men of parts and vision.
046 innā aḫlaṣnāhum biḫāliṣatin ḏikrā alddāri Lo! We purified them with a pure thought, remembrance of the Home (of the Hereafter).
047 wa-innahum ʿindanā lamina almustafayna al-aḫyāri Lo! in Our sight they are verily of the elect, the excellent.
048 waoḏkur ismāʿeela wa-ilyasaʿa waṯa alkifli wakullun mina al-aḫyāri And make mention of Ishmael and Elisha and Dhu'l-Kifl. All are of the chosen.
049 haṯa ḏikrun wa-inna lilmuttaqeena laḥusna maābin This is a reminder. And lo! for those who ward off (evil) is a happy journey's end,
050 jannāti ʿadnin mufattaḥatan lahumu al-abwābu Gardens of Eden, whereof the gates are opened for them,
051 muttaki-eena feehā yadʿoona feehā bifākihatin kaṯeeratin wašarābin Wherein, reclining, they call for plenteous fruit and cool drink (that is) therein.
052 waʿindahum qasirātu alttarfi atrābun And with them are those of modest gaze, companions.
053 haṯa mā tooʿadoona liyawmi alḥisābi This it is that ye are promised for the Day of Reckoning.
054 inna haṯa larizqunā mā lahu min nafādin Lo! this in truth is Our provision, which will never waste away.
055 haṯa wa-inna lilttaġeena lašarra maābin This (is for the righteous). And lo! for the transgressors there with be an evil journey's end,
056 jahannama yaṣlawnahā fabi/sa almihādu Hell, where they will burn, an evil resting-place.
057 haṯa falyaḏooqoohu ḥameemun waġassāqun Here is a boiling and an ice-cold draught, so let them taste it,
058 waāḫaru min šaklihi azwājun And other (torment) of the kind in pairs (the two extremes)!
059 haṯa fawjun muqtaḥimun maʿakum lā marḥaban bihim innahum saloo alnnāri Here is an army rushing blindly with you. (Those who are already in the Fire say): No word of welcome for them. Lo! they will roast at the Fire.
060 qāloo bal antum lā marḥaban bikum antum qaddamtumoohu lanā fabi/sa alqarāru They say: Nay, but you (misleaders), for you there is no word of welcome. Ye prepared this for us (by your misleading). Now hapless is the plight.
061 qāloo rabbanā man qaddama lanā haṯa fazidhu ʿaṯaban ḍiʿfan fee alnnāri They say: Our Lord! Whoever did prepare this for us, oh, give him double portion of the Fire!
062 waqāloo mā lanā lā narā rijālan kunnā naʿudduhum mina al-ašrāri And they say: What aileth us that we behold not men whom we were wont to count among the wicked ?
063 attaḫaḏnāhum siḫriyyan am zāġat ʿanhumu al-absaru Did we take them (wrongly) for a laughing-stock, or have our eyes missed them ?
064 inna ṯalika laḥaqqun taḫasumu ahli alnnāri Lo! that is very truth: the wrangling of the dwellers in the Fire.
065 qul innamā anā munḏirun wamā min ilāhin illā allāhu alwahidu alqahhāru Say (unto them, O Muhammad): I am only a warner, and there is no God save Allah, the One, the Absolute,
066 rabbu alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wamā baynahumā alʿazeezu alġaffāru Lord of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them, the Mighty, the Pardoning.
067 qul huwa nabaon ʿaḏeemun Say: It is tremendous tidings
068 antum ʿanhu muʿriḍoona Whence ye turn away!
069 mā kāna liya min ʿilmin bialmala-i al-aʿlā iḏ yaḫtaṣimoona I had no knowledge of the Highest Chiefs when they disputed;
070 in yooha ilayya illā annamā anā naḏeerun mubeenun It is revealed unto me only that I may be a plain warner.
071 iḏ qāla rabbuka lilmalā-ikati innee ḫāliqun bašaran min ṭeenin When thy Lord said unto the angels: Lo! I am about to create a mortal out of mire,
072 fa-iṯa sawwaytuhu wanafaḫtu feehi min rooḥee faqaʿoo lahu sājideena And when I have fashioned him and breathed into him of My Spirit, then fall down before him prostrate,
073 fasajada almalā-ikatu kulluhum ajmaʿoona The angels fell down prostrate, every one,
074 illā ibleesa istakbara wakāna mina alkāfireena Saving Iblis; he was scornful and became one of the disbelievers.
075 qāla yā ibleesu mā manaʿaka an tasjuda limā ḫalaqtu biyadayya astakbarta am kunta mina alʿāleena He said: O Iblis! What hindereth thee from falling prostrate before that which I have created with both My hands ? Art thou too proud or art thou of the high exalted ?
076 qāla anā ḫayrun minhu ḫalaqtanee min nārin waḫalaqtahu min ṭeenin He said: I am better than him. Thou createdst me of fire, whilst him Thou didst create of clay.
077 qāla faoḫruj minhā fa-innaka rajeemun He said: Go forth from hence, for lo! thou art outcast,
078 wa-inna ʿalayka laʿnatee ilā yawmi alddeeni And lo! My curse is on thee till the Day of Judgment.
079 qāla rabbi faanḏirnee ilā yawmi yubʿaṯoona He said: My Lord! Reprieve me till the day when they are raised.
080 qāla fa-innaka mina almunḏareena He said: Lo! thou art of those reprieved
081 ilā yawmi alwaqti almaʿloomi Until the day of the time appointed.
082 qāla fabiʿizzatika laoġwiyannahum ajmaʿeena He said: Then, by Thy might, I surely will beguile them every one,
083 illā ʿibādaka minhumu almuḫlaṣeena Save Thy single-minded slaves among them.
084 qāla faalḥaqqu waalḥaqqa aqoolu He said: The Truth is, and the Truth I speak,
085 laamlaanna jahannama minka wamimman tabiʿaka minhum ajmaʿeena That I shall fill hell with thee and with such of them as follow thee, together.
086 qul mā as-alukum ʿalayhi min ajrin wamā anā mina almutakallifeena Say (O Muhammad, unto mankind): I ask of you no fee for this, and I am no simulating.
087 in huwa illā ḏikrun lilʿālameena Lo! it is naught else than a reminder for all peoples
88 walataʿlamunna nabaahu baʿda ḥeenin [Could not extract English for verse 88]
