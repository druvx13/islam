80. `Abasa: He Frowned

1 
ʿabasa watawallā
He frowned and turned away

2 
an jāahu al-aʿmā
Because the blind man came unto him.

3 
wamā yudreeka laʿallahu yazzakkā
What could inform thee but that he might grow (in grace)

4 
aw yaṯṯakkaru fatanfaʿahu alṯṯikrā
Or take heed and so the reminder might avail him ?

5 
ammā mani istaġnā
As for him who thinketh himself independent,

6 
faanta lahu taṣaddā
Unto him thou payest regard.

7 
wamā ʿalayka allā yazzakkā
Yet it is not thy concern if he grow not (in grace).

8 
waammā man jāaka yasʿā
But as for him who cometh unto thee with earnest purpose

9 
wahuwa yaḫšā
And hath fear,

10 
faanta ʿanhu talahhā
From him thou art distracted.

11 
kallā innahā taḏkiratun
Nay, but verily it is an Admonishment,

12 
faman šāa ḏakarahu
So let whosoever will pay heed to it,

13 
fee ṣuḥufin mukarramatin
On honoured leaves

14 
marfooʿatin muṭahharatin
Exalted, purified,

15 
bi-aydee safaratin
(Set down) by scribes

16 
kirāmin bararatin
Noble and righteous.

17 
qutila al-insānu mā akfarahu
Man is (self-)destroyed: how ungrateful!

18 
min ayyi šay-in ḫalaqahu
From what thing doth He create him ?

19 
min nuṭfatin ḫalaqahu faqaddarahu
From a drop of seed. He createth him and proportioneth him,

20 
thumma alssabeela yassarahu
Then maketh the way easy for him,

21 
thumma amātahu faaqbarahu
Then causeth him to die, and burieth him;

22 
thumma iṯa šāa anšarahu
Then, when He will, He bringeth him again to life.

23 
kallā lammā yaqḍi mā amarahu
Nay, but (man) hath not done what He commanded him.

24 
falyanḏuri al-insānu ilā ṭaʿāmihi
Let man consider his food:

25 
annā ṣababnā almāa ṣabbān
How We pour water in showers

26 
thumma šaqaqnā al-arḍa šaqqān
Then split the earth in clefts

27 
faanbatnā feehā ḥabbān
And cause the grain to grow therein

28 
waʿinaban waqaḍbān
And grapes and green fodder

29 
wazaytoonan wanaḫlān
And olive-trees and palm-trees

30 
waḥadā-iqa ġulbān
And garden-closes of thick foliage

31 
wafākihatan waabbān
And fruits and grasses:

32 
matāʿan lakum wali-anʿāmikum
Provision for you and your cattle.

33 
fa-iṯa jāati alssaḫḫatu
But when the Shout cometh

34 
yawma yafirru almaro min aḫeehi
On the day when a man fleeth from his brother

35 
waommihi waabeehi
And his mother and his father

36 
wasahibatihi wabaneehi
And his wife and his children,

37 
likulli imri-in minhum yawma-iḏin ša/nun yuġneehi
Every man that day will have concern enough to make him heedless (of others).

38 
wujoohun yawma-iḏin musfiratun
On that day faces will be bright as dawn,

39 Laughing, rejoicing at good news;

40 
wawujoohun yawma-iḏin ʿalayhā ġabaratun
And other faces, on that day, with dust upon them,

41 
tarhaquhā qataratun
Veiled in darkness,

42 
olā-ika humu alkafaratu alfajaratu
Those are the disbelievers, the wicked.
