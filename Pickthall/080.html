80. `Abasa: He Frowned

001 ʿabasa watawallā He frowned and turned away
002 an jāahu al-aʿmā Because the blind man came unto him.
003 wamā yudreeka laʿallahu yazzakkā What could inform thee but that he might grow (in grace)
004 aw yaṯṯakkaru fatanfaʿahu alṯṯikrā Or take heed and so the reminder might avail him ?
005 ammā mani istaġnā As for him who thinketh himself independent,
006 faanta lahu taṣaddā Unto him thou payest regard.
007 wamā ʿalayka allā yazzakkā Yet it is not thy concern if he grow not (in grace).
008 waammā man jāaka yasʿā But as for him who cometh unto thee with earnest purpose
009 wahuwa yaḫšā And hath fear,
010 faanta ʿanhu talahhā From him thou art distracted.
011 kallā innahā taḏkiratun Nay, but verily it is an Admonishment,
012 faman šāa ḏakarahu So let whosoever will pay heed to it,
013 fee ṣuḥufin mukarramatin On honoured leaves
014 marfooʿatin muṭahharatin Exalted, purified,
015 bi-aydee safaratin (Set down) by scribes
016 kirāmin bararatin Noble and righteous.
017 qutila al-insānu mā akfarahu Man is (self-)destroyed: how ungrateful!
018 min ayyi šay-in ḫalaqahu From what thing doth He create him ?
019 min nuṭfatin ḫalaqahu faqaddarahu From a drop of seed. He createth him and proportioneth him,
020 thumma alssabeela yassarahu Then maketh the way easy for him,
021 thumma amātahu faaqbarahu Then causeth him to die, and burieth him;
022 thumma iṯa šāa anšarahu Then, when He will, He bringeth him again to life.
023 kallā lammā yaqḍi mā amarahu Nay, but (man) hath not done what He commanded him.
024 falyanḏuri al-insānu ilā ṭaʿāmihi Let man consider his food:
025 annā ṣababnā almāa ṣabbān How We pour water in showers
026 thumma šaqaqnā al-arḍa šaqqān Then split the earth in clefts
027 faanbatnā feehā ḥabbān And cause the grain to grow therein
028 waʿinaban waqaḍbān And grapes and green fodder
029 wazaytoonan wanaḫlān And olive-trees and palm-trees
030 waḥadā-iqa ġulbān And garden-closes of thick foliage
031 wafākihatan waabbān And fruits and grasses:
032 matāʿan lakum wali-anʿāmikum Provision for you and your cattle.
033 fa-iṯa jāati alssaḫḫatu But when the Shout cometh
034 yawma yafirru almaro min aḫeehi On the day when a man fleeth from his brother
035 waommihi waabeehi And his mother and his father
036 wasahibatihi wabaneehi And his wife and his children,
037 likulli imri-in minhum yawma-iḏin ša/nun yuġneehi Every man that day will have concern enough to make him heedless (of others).
038 wujoohun yawma-iḏin musfiratun On that day faces will be bright as dawn,
039 [romanization missing] Laughing, rejoicing at good news;
040 wawujoohun yawma-iḏin ʿalayhā ġabaratun And other faces, on that day, with dust upon them,
041 tarhaquhā qataratun Veiled in darkness,
42 olā-ika humu alkafaratu alfajaratu [Could not extract English for verse 42]
