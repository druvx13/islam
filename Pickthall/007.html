7. al-A`raf: The Elevated Places

001 alif-lām-meem-sad Alif. Lam. Mim. Sad.
002 kitābun onzila ilayka falā yakun fee ṣadrika ḥarajun minhu litunḏira bihi waḏikrā lilmu/mineena (It is) a Scripture that is revealed unto thee (Muhammad) - so let there be no heaviness in thy heart therefrom - that thou mayst warn thereby, and (it is) a Reminder unto believers.
003 ittabiʿoo mā onzila ilaykum min rabbikum walā tattabiʿoo min doonihi awliyāa qaleelan mā taḏakkaroona (Saying): Follow that which is sent down unto you from your Lord, and follow no protecting friends beside Him. Little do ye recollect!
004 wakam min qaryatin ahlaknāhā fajāahā ba/sunā bayātan aw hum qā-iloona How many a township have We destroyed! As a raid by night, or while they slept at noon, Our terror came unto them.
005 famā kāna daʿwāhum iḏ jāahum ba/sunā illā an qāloo innā kunnā ḏālimeena No plea had they, when Our terror came unto them, save that they said: Lo! We were wrong-doers.
006 falanas-alanna allāḏeena orsila ilayhim walanas-alanna almursaleena Then verily We shall question those unto whom (Our message) hath been sent, and verily We shall question the messengers.
007 falanaquṣanna ʿalayhim biʿilmin wamā kunnā ġā-ibeena Then verily We shall narrate unto them (the event) with knowledge, for We were not absent (when it came to pass).
008 waalwaznu yawma-iḏini alḥaqqu faman ṯaqulat mawāzeenuhu faolā-ika humu almufliḥoona The weighing on that day is the true (weighing). As for those whose scale is heavy, they are the successful.
009 waman ḫaffat mawāzeenuhu faolā-ika allāḏeena ḫasiroo anfusahum bimā kānoo bi-āyātinā yaḏlimoona And as for those whose scale is light: those are they who lose their souls because they used to wrong Our revelations.
010 walaqad makkannākum fee al-arḍi wajaʿalnā lakum feehā maʿāyiša qaleelan mā taškuroona And We have given you (mankind) power in the earth, and appointed for you therein livelihoods. Little give ye thanks!
011 walaqad ḫalaqnākum ṯumma ṣawwarnākum ṯumma qulnā lilmalā-ikati osjudoo li-ādama fasajadoo illā ibleesa lam yakun mina alssājideena And We created you, then fashioned you, then told the angels: Fall ye prostrate before Adam! And they fell prostrate, all save Iblis, who was not of those who make prostration.
012 qāla mā manaʿaka allā tasjuda iḏ amartuka qāla anā ḫayrun minhu ḫalaqtanee min nārin waḫalaqtahu min ṭeenin He said: What hindered thee that thou didst not fall prostrate when I bade thee ? (Iblis) said: I am better than him. Thou createdst me of fire while him Thou didst create of mud.
013 qāla faihbiṭ minhā famā yakoonu laka an tatakabbara feehā faoḫruj innaka mina alssaġireena He said: Then go down hence! It is not for thee to show pride here, so go forth! Lo! thou art of those degraded.
014 qāla anḏirnee ilā yawmi yubʿaṯoona He said: Reprieve me till the day when they are raised (from the dead).
015 qāla innaka mina almunḏareena He said: Lo! thou art of those reprieved.
016 qāla fabimā aġwaytanee laaqʿudanna lahum ṣirataka almustaqeema He said: Now, because Thou hast sent me astray, verily I shall lurk in ambush for them on Thy Right Path.
017 thumma laātiyannahum min bayni aydeehim wamin ḫalfihim waʿan aymānihim waʿan šamā-ilihim walā tajidu akṯarahum šākireena Then I shall come upon them from before them and from behind them and from their right hands and from their left hands, and Thou wilt not find most of them beholden (unto Thee).
018 qāla oḫruj minhā maḏooman madḥooran laman tabiʿaka minhum laamlaanna jahannama minkum ajmaʿeena He said: Go forth from hence, degraded, banished. As for such of them as follow thee, surely I will fill hell with all of you.
019 wayā ādamu oskun anta wazawjuka aljannata fakulā min ḥayṯu ši/tumā walā taqrabā haṯihi alššajarata fatakoonā mina alṯṯālimeena And (unto man): O Adam! Dwell thou and thy wife in the Garden and eat from whence ye will, but come not nigh this tree lest ye become wrong-doers.
020 fawaswasa lahumā alššaytanu liyubdiya lahumā mā wooriya ʿanhumā min saw-ātihimā waqāla mā nahākumā rabbukumā ʿan haṯihi alššajarati illā an takoonā malakayni aw takoonā mina alḫālideena Then Satan whispered to them that he might manifest unto them that which was hidden from them of their shame, and he said: Your Lord forbade you from this tree only lest ye should become angels or become of the immortals.
021 waqāsamahumā innee lakumā lamina alnnasiḥeena And he swore unto them (saying): Lo! I am a sincere adviser unto you.
022 fadallāhumā biġuroorin falammā ṯaqā alššajarata badat lahumā saw-ātuhumā waṭafiqā yaḫṣifāni ʿalayhimā min waraqi aljannati wanādāhumā rabbuhumā alam anhakumā ʿan tilkumā alššajarati waaqul lakumā inna alššaytana lakumā ʿaduwwun mubeenun Thus did he lead them on with guile. And when they tasted of the tree their shame was manifest to them and they began to hide (by heaping) on themselves some of the leaves of the Garden. And their Lord called them, (saying): Did I not forbid you from that tree and tell you: Lo! Satan is an open enemy to you ?
023 qālā rabbanā ḏalamnā anfusanā wa-in lam taġfir lanā watarḥamna lanakoonanna mina alḫāsireena They said: Our Lord! We have wronged ourselves. If thou forgive us not and have not mercy on us, surely we are of the lost!
024 qāla ihbiṭoo baʿḍukum libaʿḍin ʿaduwwun walakum fee al-arḍi mustaqarrun wamatāʿun ilā ḥeenin He said: Go down (from hence), one of you a foe unto the other. There will be for you on earth a habitation and provision for a while.
025 qāla feehā taḥyawna wafeehā tamootoona waminhā tuḫrajoona He said: There shall ye live, and there shall ye die, and thence shall ye be brought forth.
026 yā banee ādama qad anzalnā ʿalaykum libāsan yuwāree saw-ātikum wareešan walibāsu alttaqwā ṯalika ḫayrun ṯalika min āyāti allāhi laʿallahum yaṯṯakkaroona O Children of Adam! We have revealed unto you raiment to conceal your shame, and splendid vesture, but the raiment of restraint from evil, that is best. This is of the revelations of Allah, that they may remember.
027 yā banee ādama lā yaftinannakumu alššaytanu kamā aḫraja abawaykum mina aljannati yanziʿu ʿanhumā libāsahumā liyuriyahumā saw-ātihimā innahu yarākum huwa waqabeeluhu min ḥayṯu lā tarawnahum innā jaʿalnā alššayateena awliyāa lillaḏeena lā yu/minoona O Children of Adam! Let not Satan seduce you as he caused your (first) parents to go forth from the Garden and tore off from them their robe (of innocence) that he might manifest their shame to them. Lo! he seeth you, he and his tribe, from whence ye see him not. Lo! We have made the devils protecting friends for those who believe not.
028 wa-iṯa faʿaloo fahišatan qāloo wajadnā ʿalayhā ābāanā waallāhu amaranā bihā qul inna allāha lā ya/muru bialfaḥšā-i ataqooloona ʿalā allāhi mā lā taʿlamoona And when they do some lewdness they say: We found our fathers doing it and Allah hath enjoined it on us. Say: Allah, verily, enjoineth not lewdness. Tell ye concerning Allah that which ye know not ?
029 qul amara rabbee bialqisṭi waaqeemoo wujoohakum ʿinda kulli masjidin waodʿoohu muḫliṣeena lahu alddeena kamā badaakum taʿoodoona Say: My Lord enjoineth justice. And set your faces upright (toward Him) at every place of worship and call upon Him, making religion pure for Him (only). As He brought you into being, so return ye (unto Him).
030 fareeqan hadā wafareeqan ḥaqqa ʿalayhimu alḍalālatu innahumu ittaḫaḏoo alššayateena awliyāa min dooni allāhi wayaḥsaboona annahum muhtadoona A party hath He led aright, while error hath just hold over (another) party, for lo! they choose the devils for protecting supporters instead of Allah and deem that they are rightly guided.
031 yā banee ādama ḫuḏoo zeenatakum ʿinda kulli masjidin wakuloo waišraboo walā tusrifoo innahu lā yuḥibbu almusrifeena O Children of Adam! Look to your adornment at every place of worship, and eat and drink, but be not prodigal. Lo! He loveth not the prodigals.
032 qul man ḥarrama zeenata allāhi allātee aḫraja liʿibādihi waalttayyibāti mina alrrizqi qul hiya lillaḏeena āmanoo fee alḥayāti alddunyā ḫāliṣatan yawma alqiyāmati kaṯalika nufaṣilu al-āyāti liqawmin yaʿlamoona Say: Who hath forbidden the adornment of Allah which He hath brought forth for His bondmen, and the good things of His providing ? Say: Such, on the Day of Resurrection, will be only for those who believed during the life of the world. Thus do we detail Our revelations for people who have knowledge.
033 qul innamā ḥarrama rabbiya alfawahiša mā ḏahara minhā wamā baṭana waal-iṯma waalbaġya biġayri alḥaqqi waan tušrikoo biallāhi mā lam yunazzil bihi sultanan waan taqooloo ʿalā allāhi mā lā taʿlamoona Say: My Lord forbiddeth only indecencies, such of them as are apparent and such as are within, and sin and wrongful oppression, and that ye associate with Allah that for which no warrant hath been revealed, and that ye tell concerning Allah that which ye know not.
034 walikulli ommatin ajalun fa-iṯa jāa ajaluhum lā yasta/ḫiroona sāʿatan walā yastaqdimoona And every nation hath its term, and when its term cometh, they cannot put it off an hour nor yet advance (it).
035 yā banee ādama immā ya/tiyannakum rusulun minkum yaquṣoona ʿalaykum āyātee famani ittaqā waaṣlaḥa falā ḫawfun ʿalayhim walā hum yaḥzanoona O Children of Adam! When messengers of your own come unto you who narrate unto you My revelations, then whosoever refraineth from evil and amendeth - there shall no fear come upon them neither shall they grieve.
036 waallaḏeena kaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā waistakbaroo ʿanhā olā-ika aṣ-habu alnnāri hum feehā ḫālidoona But they who deny Our revelations and scorn them - each are rightful owners of the Fire; they will abide therein.
037 faman aḏlamu mimmani iftarā ʿalā allāhi kaḏiban aw kaṯṯaba bi-āyātihi olā-ika yanāluhum naṣeebuhum mina alkitābi ḥattā iṯa jāat-hum rusulunā yatawaffawnahum qāloo ayna mā kuntum tadʿoona min dooni allāhi qāloo ḍalloo ʿannā wašahidoo ʿalā anfusihim annahum kānoo kāfireena Who doeth greater wrong than he who inventeth a lie concerning Allah or denieth Our tokens. (For such) their appointed portion of the Book (of destiny) reacheth them till, when Our messengers come to gather them, they say: Where (now) is that to which ye cried beside Allah ? They say: They have departed from us. And they testify against themselves that they were disbelievers.
038 qāla odḫuloo fee omamin qad ḫalat min qablikum mina aljinni waal-insi fee alnnāri kullamā daḫalat ommatun laʿanat oḫtahā ḥattā iṯa iddārakoo feehā jameeʿan qālat oḫrāhum li-oolāhum rabbanā hāolā-i aḍalloonā faātihim ʿaṯaban ḍiʿfan mina alnnāri qāla likullin ḍiʿfun walākin lā taʿlamoona He saith: Enter into the Fire among nations of the jinn and humankind who passed away before you. Every time a nation entereth, it curseth its sister (nation) till, when they have all been made to follow one another thither, the last of them saith unto the first of them: Our Lord! These led us astray, so give them double torment of the Fire. He saith: For each one there is double (torment), but ye know not.
039 waqālat oolāhum li-oḫrāhum famā kāna lakum ʿalaynā min faḍlin faḏooqoo alʿaṯaba bimā kuntum taksiboona And the first of them saith unto the last of them: Ye were no whit better than us, so taste the doom for what ye used to earn.
040 inna allāḏeena kaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā waistakbaroo ʿanhā lā tufattaḥu lahum abwābu alssamā-i walā yadḫuloona aljannata ḥattā yalija aljamalu fee sammi alḫiyati wakaṯalika najzee almujrimeena Lo! they who deny Our revelations and scorn them, for them the gates of heaven will nor be opened not will they enter the Garden until the camel goeth through the needle's eye. Thus do We requite the guilty.
041 lahum min jahannama mihādun wamin fawqihim ġawāšin wakaṯalika najzee alṯṯālimeena Theirs will be a bed of hell, and over them coverings (of hell). Thus do We requite wrong-doers.
042 waallaḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati lā nukallifu nafsan illā wusʿahā olā-ika aṣ-habu aljannati hum feehā ḫālidoona But (as for) those who believe and do good works - We tax not any soul beyond its scope - Such are rightful owners of the Garden. They abide therein.
043 wanazaʿnā mā fee ṣudoorihim min ġillin tajree min taḥtihimu al-anhāru waqāloo alḥamdu lillāhi allāḏee hadānā lihaṯa wamā kunnā linahtadiya lawlā an hadānā allāhu laqad jāat rusulu rabbinā bialḥaqqi wanoodoo an tilkumu aljannatu ooriṯtumoohā bimā kuntum taʿmaloona And We remove whatever rancour may be in their hearts. Rivers flow beneath them. And they say: The praise to Allah, Who hath guided us to this. We could not truly have been led aright if Allah had not guided us. Verily the messengers of our Lord did bring the Truth. And it is cried unto them: This is the Garden. Ye inherit it for what ye used to do.
044 wanādā aṣ-habu aljannati aṣ-haba alnnāri an qad wajadnā mā waʿadanā rabbunā ḥaqqan fahal wajadtum mā waʿada rabbukum ḥaqqan qāloo naʿam faaṯṯana mu-aṯṯinun baynahum an laʿnatu allāhi ʿalā alṯṯālimeena And the dwellers of the Garden cry unto the dwellers of the Fire: We have found that which our Lord promised us (to be) the Truth. Have ye (too) found that which your Lord promised the Truth ? They say: Yea, verily. And a crier in between them crieth: The curse of Allah is on evil-doers,
045 allāḏeena yaṣuddoona ʿan sabeeli allāhi wayabġoonahā ʿiwajan wahum bial-āḫirati kāfiroona Who debar (men) from the path of Allah and would have it crooked, and who are disbelievers in the Last Day.
046 wabaynahumā ḥijābun waʿalā al-aʿrāfi rijālun yaʿrifoona kullan biseemāhum wanādaw aṣ-haba aljannati an salāmun ʿalaykum lam yadḫuloohā wahum yaṭmaʿoona Between them is a veil. And on the Heights are men who know them all by their marks. And they call unto the dwellers of the Garden: Peace be unto you! They enter it not although they hope (to enter).
047 wa-iṯa ṣurifat absaruhum tilqāa aṣ-habi alnnāri qāloo rabbanā lā tajʿalnā maʿa alqawmi alṯṯālimeena And when their eyes are turned toward the dwellers of the Fire, they say: Our Lord! Place us not with the wrong-doing folk.
048 wanādā aṣ-habu al-aʿrāfi rijālan yaʿrifoonahum biseemāhum qāloo mā aġnā ʿankum jamʿukum wamā kuntum tastakbiroona And the dwellers on the Heights call unto men whom they know by their marks, (saying): What did your multitude and that in which ye took your pride avail you ?
049 ahāolā-i allāḏeena aqsamtum lā yanāluhumu allāhu biraḥmatin odḫuloo aljannata lā ḫawfun ʿalaykum walā antum taḥzanoona Are these they of whom ye swore that Allah would not show them mercy ? (Unto them it hath been said): Enter the Garden. No fear shall come upon you nor is it ye who will grieve.
050 wanādā aṣ-habu alnāri aṣ-haba aljannati an afeeḍoo ʿalaynā mina almā-i aw mimmā razaqakumu allāhu qāloo inna allāha ḥarramahumā ʿalā alkāfireena And the dwellers of the Fire cry out unto the dwellers of the Garden: Pour on us some water or some wherewith Allah hath provided you. They say: Lo! Allah hath forbidden both to disbelievers (in His guidance),
051 allāḏeena ittaḫaḏoo deenahum lahwan walaʿiban waġarrat-humu alḥayātu alddunyā faalyawma nansāhum kamā nasoo liqāa yawmihim haṯa wamā kānoo bi-āyātinā yajḥadoona Who took their religion for a sport and pastime, and whom the life of the world beguiled. So this day We have forgotten them even as they forgot the meeting of this their Day and as they used to deny Our tokens.
052 walaqad ji/nāhum bikitābin faṣalnāhu ʿalā ʿilmin hudan waraḥmatan liqawmin yu/minoona Verily We have brought them a Scripture which We expounded with knowledge, a guidance and a mercy for a people who believe.
053 hal yanḏuroona illā ta/weelahu yawma ya/tee ta/weeluhu yaqoolu allāḏeena nasoohu min qablu qad jāat rusulu rabbinā bialḥaqqi fahal lanā min šufaʿāa fayašfaʿoo lanā aw nuraddu fanaʿmala ġayra allāḏee kunnā naʿmalu qad ḫasiroo anfusahum waḍalla ʿanhum mā kānoo yaftaroona Await they aught save the fulfilment thereof ? On the day when the fulfilment thereof cometh, those who were before forgetful thereof will say: The messengers of our Lord did bring the Truth! Have we any intercessors, that they may intercede for us ? Or can we be returned (to life on earth), that we may act otherwise than we used to act ? They have lost their souls, and that which they devised hath failed them.
054 inna rabbakumu allāhu allāḏee ḫalaqa alssamāwāti waal-arḍa fee sittati ayyāmin ṯumma istawā ʿalā alʿarši yuġšee allāyla alnnahāra yaṭlubuhu ḥaṯeeṯan waalššamsa waalqamara waalnnujooma musaḫḫarātin bi-amrihi alā lahu alḫalqu waal-amru tabāraka allāhu rabbu alʿālameena Lo! your Lord is Allah Who created the heavens and the earth in six Days, then mounted He the Throne. He covereth the night with the day, which is in haste to follow it, and hath made the sun and the moon and the stars subservient by His command. His verily is all creation and commandment. Blessed be Allah, the Lord of the Worlds!
055 odʿoo rabbakum taḍarruʿan waḫufyatan innahu lā yuḥibbu almuʿtadeena (O mankind!) Call upon your Lord humbly and in secret. Lo! He loveth not aggressors.
056 walā tufsidoo fee al-arḍi baʿda iṣlahihā waodʿoohu ḫawfan waṭamaʿan inna raḥmata allāhi qareebun mina almuḥsineena Work not confusion in the earth after the fair ordering (thereof). and call on Him in fear and hope. Lo! the mercy of Allah is nigh unto the good.
057 wahuwa allāḏee yursilu alrriyaha bušran bayna yaday raḥmatihi ḥattā iṯa aqallat sahaban ṯiqālan suqnāhu libaladin mayyitin faanzalnā bihi almāa faaḫrajnā bihi min kulli alṯṯamarāti kaṯalika nuḫriju almawtā laʿallakum taḏakkaroona And He it is Who sendeth the winds as tidings heralding His mercy, till, when they bear a cloud heavy (with rain), We lead it to a dead land, and then cause water to descend thereon and thereby bring forth fruits of every kind. Thus bring We forth the dead. Haply ye may remember.
058 waalbaladu alttayyibu yaḫruju nabātuhu bi-iḏni rabbihi waallaḏee ḫabuṯa lā yaḫruju illā nakidan kaṯalika nuṣarrifu al-āyāti liqawmin yaškuroona As for the good land, its vegetation cometh forth by permission of its Lord; while as for that which is bad, only the useless cometh forth (from it). Thus do We recount the tokens for people who give thanks.
059 laqad arsalnā nooḥan ilā qawmihi faqāla yā qawmi oʿbudoo allāha mā lakum min ilāhin ġayruhu innee aḫāfu ʿalaykum ʿaṯaba yawmin ʿaḏeemin We sent Noah (of old) unto his people, and he said: O my people! Serve Allah. Ye have no other God save Him. Lo! I fear for you the retribution of an Awful Day.
060 qāla almalao min qawmihi innā lanarāka fee ḍalālin mubeenin The chieftains of his people said: Lo! we see thee surely in plain error.
061 qāla yā qawmi laysa bee ḍalālatun walākinnee rasoolun min rabbi alʿālameena He said: O my people! There is no error in me, but I am a messenger from the Lord of the Worlds.
062 oballiġukum risālāti rabbee waanṣaḥu lakum waaʿlamu mina allāhi mā lā taʿlamoona I convey unto you the messages of my Lord and give good counsel unto you, and know from Allah that which ye know not.
063 awa ʿajibtum an jāakum ḏikrun min rabbikum ʿalā rajulin minkum liyunḏirakum walitattaqoo walaʿallakum turḥamoona Marvel ye that there should come unto you a Reminder from your Lord by means of a man among you, that he may warn you, and that ye may keep from evil, and that haply ye may find mercy.
064 fakaṯṯaboohu faanjaynāhu waallaḏeena maʿahu fee alfulki waaġraqnā allāḏeena kaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā innahum kānoo qawman ʿameena But they denied him, so We saved him and those with him in the ship, and We drowned those who denied Our tokens. Lo! they were blind folk.
065 wa-ilā ʿādin aḫāhum hoodan qāla yā qawmi oʿbudoo allāha mā lakum min ilāhin ġayruhu afalā tattaqoona And unto (the tribe of) A'ad (We sent) their brother, Hud. He said: O my people! Serve Allah. Ye have no other God save Him. Will ye not ward off (evil) ?
066 qāla almalao allāḏeena kafaroo min qawmihi innā lanarāka fee safāhatin wa-innā lanaḏunnuka mina alkaṯibeena The chieftains of his people, who were disbelieving, said: Lo! we surely see thee in foolishness, and lo! we deem thee of the liars.
067 qāla yā qawmi laysa bee safāhatun walākinnee rasoolun min rabbi alʿālameena He said: O my people! There is no foolishness in me, but I am a messenger from the Lord of the Worlds.
068 oballiġukum risālāti rabbee waanā lakum nasiḥun ameenun I convey unto you the messages of my Lord and am for you a true adviser.
069 awa ʿajibtum an jāakum ḏikrun min rabbikum ʿalā rajulin minkum liyunḏirakum waoḏkuroo iḏ jaʿalakum ḫulafāa min baʿdi qawmi nooḥin wazādakum fee alḫalqi basṭatan faoḏkuroo ālāa allāhi laʿallakum tufliḥoona Marvel ye that there should come unto you a Reminder from your Lord by means of a man among you, that he may warn you ? Remember how He made you viceroys after Noah's folk, and gave you growth of stature. Remember (all) the bounties of your Lord, that haply ye may be successful.
070 qāloo aji/tanā linaʿbuda allāha waḥdahu wanaḏara mā kāna yaʿbudu ābāonā fa/tinā bimā taʿidunā in kunta mina alssadiqeena They said: Hast come unto us that we should serve Allah alone, and forsake what our fathers worshipped ? Then bring upon us that wherewith thou threatenest us if thou art of the truthful!
071 qāla qad waqaʿa ʿalaykum min rabbikum rijsun waġaḍabun atujādiloonanee fee asmā-in sammaytumoohā antum waābāokum mā nazzala allāhu bihā min sultanin faintaḏiroo innee maʿakum mina almuntaḏireena He said: Terror and wrath from your Lord have already fallen on you. Would ye wrangle with me over names which ye have named, ye and your fathers, for which no warrant from Allah hath been revealed ? Then await (the consequence), lo! I (also) am of those awaiting (it).

151 qāla rabbi iġfir lee wali-aḫee waadḫilnā fee raḥmatika waanta arḥamu alrrahimeena He said: My Lord! Have mercy on me and on my brother; bring us into Thy mercy, Thou the Most Merciful of all who show mercy.

If Thou hadst willed Thou hadst destroyed them long before, and me with them. Wilt thou destroy us for that which the ignorant among us did ? It is but Thy trial (of us). Thou sendest whom Thou wilt astray and guidest whom Thou wilt: Thou art our Protecting Friend, therefore forgive us and have mercy on us, Thou, the Best of all who show forgiveness.

He said: I smite with My punishment whom I will, and My mercy embraceth all things, therefore I shall ordain it for those who ward off (evil) and pay the poor-due, and those who believe Our revelations;
157 allāḏeena yattabiʿoona alrrasoola alnnabiyya al-ommiyya allāḏee yajidoonahu maktooban ʿindahum fee alttawrāti waal-injeeli ya/muruhum bialmaʿroofi wayanhāhum ʿani almunkari wayuḥillu lahumu alttayyibāti wayuḥarrimu ʿalayhimu alḫabā-iṯa wayaḍaʿu ʿanhum iṣrahum waal-aġlāla allātee kānat ʿalayhim faallaḏeena āmanoo bihi waʿazzaroohu wanaṣaroohu waittabaʿoo alnnoora allāḏee onzila maʿahu olā-ika humu almufliḥoona Those who follow the messenger, the Prophet who can neither read nor write, whom they will find described in the Torah and the Gospel (which are) with them. He will enjoin on them that which is right and forbid them that which is wrong. He will make lawful for them all good things and prohibit for them only the foul; and he will relieve them of their burden and the fetters that they used to wear. Then those who believe in him, and honour him, and help him, and follow the light which is sent down with him: they are the successful.
158 qul yā ayyuhā alnnāsu innee rasoolu allāhi ilaykum jameeʿan allāḏee lahu mulku alssamāwāti waal-arḍi lā ilāha illā huwa yuḥyee wayumeetu faāminoo biallāhi warasoolihi alnnabiyyi al-ommiyyi allāḏee yu/minu biallāhi wakalimātihi waittabiʿoohu laʿallakum tahtadoona Say (O Muhammad): O mankind! Lo! I am the messenger of Allah to you all - (the messenger of) Him unto Whom belongeth the Sovereignty of the heavens and the earth. There is no God save Him. He quickeneth and He giveth death. So believe in Allah and His messenger, the Prophet who can neither read nor write, who believeth in Allah and in His Words, and follow him that haply ye may be led aright.
159 wamin qawmi moosā ommatun yahdoona bialḥaqqi wabihi yaʿdiloona And of Moses' folk there is a community who lead with truth and establish justice therewith.
160 waqattaʿnāhumu iṯnatay ʿašrata asbatan omaman waawḥaynā ilā moosā iḏi istasqāhu qawmuhu ani iḍrib biʿasaka alḥajara fainbajasat minhu iṯnatā ʿašrata ʿaynan qad ʿalima kullu onāsin mašrabahum waḏallalnā ʿalayhimu alġamāma waanzalnā ʿalayhimu almanna waalssalwā kuloo min ṭayyibāti mā razaqnākum wamā ḏalamoonā walākin kānoo anfusahum yaḏlimoona We divided them into twelve tribes, nations; and We inspired Moses, when his people asked him for water, saying: Smite with thy staff the rock! And there gushed forth therefrom twelve springs, so that each tribe knew their drinking-place. And we caused the white cloud to overshadow them and sent down for them the manna and the quails (saying): Eat of the good things wherewith we have provided you. They wronged Us not, but they were wont to wrong themselves.
161 wa-iḏ qeela lahumu oskunoo haṯihi alqaryata wakuloo minhā ḥayṯu ši/tum waqooloo ḥittatun waodḫuloo albāba sujjadan naġfir lakum ḫaṭee-ātikum sanazeedu almuḥsineena And when it was said unto them: Dwell in this township and eat therefrom whence ye will, and say "Repentance," and enter the gate prostrate; We shall forgive you your sins; We shall increase (reward) for the right-doers.
162 fabaddala allāḏeena ḏalamoo minhum qawlan ġayra allāḏee qeela lahum faarsalnā ʿalayhim rijzan mina alssamā-i bimā kānoo yaḏlimoona But those of them who did wrong changed the word which had been told them for another saying, and We sent down upon them wrath from heaven for their wrongdoing.
163 wais-alhum ʿani alqaryati allātee kānat hadirata albaḥri iḏ yaʿdoona fee alssabti iḏ ta/teehim ḥeetānuhum yawma sabtihim šurraʿan wayawma lā yasbitoona lā ta/teehim kaṯalika nabloohum bimā kānoo yafsuqoona Ask them (O Muhammad) of the township that was by the sea, how they did break the Sabbath, how their big fish came unto them visibly upon their Sabbath day and on a day when they did not keep Sabbath came they not unto them. Thus did We try them for that they were evil-livers.
164 wa-iḏ qālat ommatun minhum lima taʿiḏoona qawman allāhu muhlikuhum aw muʿaṯṯibuhum ʿaṯaban šadeedan qāloo maʿḏiratan ilā rabbikum walaʿallahum yattaqoona And when a community among them said: Why preach ye to a folk whom Allah is about to destroy or punish with an awful doom, they said: In order to be free from guilt before your Lord, and that haply they may ward off (evil).
165 falammā nasoo mā ḏukkiroo bihi anjaynā allāḏeena yanhawna ʿani alssoo-i waaḫaḏnā allāḏeena ḏalamoo biʿaṯabin ba-eesin bimā kānoo yafsuqoona And when they forgot that whereof they had been reminded, We rescued those who forbade wrong, and visited those who did wrong with dreadful punishment because they were evil-livers.
166 falammā ʿataw ʿan mā nuhoo ʿanhu qulnā lahum koonoo qiradatan ḫāsi-eena So when they took pride in that which they had been forbidden, We said unto them: Be ye apes despised and loathed!
167 wa-iḏ taaṯṯana rabbuka layabʿaṯanna ʿalayhim ilā yawmi alqiyāmati man yasoomuhum soo-a alʿaṯabi inna rabbaka lasareeʿu alʿiqābi wa-innahu laġafoorun raḥeemun And (remember) when thy Lord proclaimed that He would raise against them till the Day of Resurrection those who would lay on them a cruel torment. Lo! verily thy Lord is swift in prosecution and lo! verily He is Forgiving, Merciful.
168 waqattaʿnāhum fee al-arḍi omaman minhumu alssaliḥoona waminhum doona ṯalika wabalawnāhum bialḥasanāti waalssayyi-āti laʿallahum yarjiʿoona And We have sundered them in the earth as (separate) nations. Some of them are righteous, and some far from that. And We have tried them with good things and evil things that haply they might return.
169 faḫalafa min baʿdihim ḫalfun wariṯoo alkitāba ya/ḫuḏoona ʿaraḍa haṯa al-adnā wayaqooloona sayuġfaru lanā wa-in ya/tihim ʿaraḍun miṯluhu ya/ḫuḏoohu alam yu/ḫaḏ ʿalayhim meeṯāqu alkitābi an lā yaqooloo ʿalā allāhi illā alḥaqqa wadarasoo mā feehi waalddāru al-āḫiratu ḫayrun lillaḏeena yattaqoona afalā taʿqiloona And a generation hath succeeded them who inherited the scriptures. They grasp the goods of this low life (as the price of evil-doing) and say: It will be forgiven us. And if there came to them (again) the offer of the like, they would accept it (and would sin again). Hath not the covenant of the Scripture been taken on their behalf that they should not speak aught concerning Allah save the truth ? And they have studied that which is therein. And the abode of the Hereafter is better, for those who ward off (evil). Have ye then no sense ?
170 waallaḏeena yumassikoona bialkitābi waaqāmoo alṣalāta innā lā nuḍeeʿu ajra almuṣliḥeena And as for those who make (men) keep the Scripture, and establish worship - lo! We squander not the wages of reformers.
171 wa-iḏ nataqnā aljabala fawqahum kaannahu ḏullatun waḏannoo annahu wāqiʿun bihim ḫuḏoo mā ātaynākum biquwwatin waoḏkuroo mā feehi laʿallakum tattaqoona And when We shook the Mount above them as it were a covering, and they supposed that it was going to fall upon them (and We said): Hold fast that which We have given you, and remember that which is therein, that ye may ward off (evil).
172 wa-iḏ aḫaḏa rabbuka min banee ādama min ḏuhoorihim ḏurriyyatahum waašhadahum ʿalā anfusihim alastu birabbikum qāloo balā šahidnā an taqooloo yawma alqiyāmati innā kunnā ʿan haṯa ġāfileena And (remember) when thy Lord brought forth from the Children of Adam, from their reins, their seed, and made them testify of themselves, (saying): Am I not your Lord ? They said: Yea, verily. We testify. (That was) lest ye should say at the Day of Resurrection: Lo! of this we were unaware;
173 aw taqooloo innamā ašraka ābāonā min qablu wakunnā ḏurriyyatan min baʿdihim afatuhlikunā bimā faʿala almubṭiloona Or lest ye should say: (It is) only (that) our fathers ascribed partners to Allah of old and we were (their) seed after them. Wilt Thou destroy us on account of that which those who follow falsehood did ?

177 sāa maṯalan alqawmu allāḏeena kaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā waanfusahum kānoo yaḏlimoona Evil as an example are the folk who denied Our revelations, and were wont to wrong themselves.

179 walaqad ḏara/nā lijahannama kaṯeeran mina aljinni waal-insi lahum quloobun lā yafqahoona bihā walahum aʿyunun lā yubṣiroona bihā walahum aṯanun lā yasmaʿoona bihā olā-ika kaal-anʿāmi bal hum aḍallu olā-ika humu alġāfiloona Already have We urged unto hell many of the jinn and humankind, having hearts wherewith they understand not, and having eyes wherewith they see not, and having ears wherewith they hear not.
