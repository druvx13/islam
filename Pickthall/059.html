59. al-Hashr: The Exile

001 sabbaḥa lillāhi mā fee alssamāwāti wamā fee al-arḍi wahuwa alʿazeezu alḥakeemu All that is in the heavens and all that is in the earth glorifieth Allah, and He is the Mighty, the Wise.
002 huwa allāḏee aḫraja allāḏeena kafaroo min ahli alkitābi min diyārihim li-awwali alḥašri mā ḏanantum an yaḫrujoo waḏannoo annahum māniʿatuhum ḥuṣoonuhum mina allāhi faatāhumu allāhu min ḥayṯu lam yaḥtasiboo waqaḏafa fee quloobihimu alrruʿba yuḫriboona buyootahum bi-aydeehim waaydee almu/mineena faiʿtabiroo yā olee al-absari He it is Who hath caused those of the People of the Scripture who disbelieved to go forth from their homes unto the first exile. Ye deemed not that they would go forth, while they deemed that their strongholds would protect them from Allah. But Allah reached them from a place whereof they recked not, and cast terror in their hearts so that they ruined their houses with their own hands and the hands of the believers. So learn a lesson, O ye who have eyes!
003 walawlā an kataba allāhu ʿalayhimu aljalāa laʿaṯṯabahum fee alddunyā walahum fee al-āḫirati ʿaṯabu alnnāri And if Allah had not decreed migration for them, He verily would have punished them in the world, and theirs in the Hereafter is the punishment of the Fire.
004 [romanization missing] That is because they were opposed to Allah and His messenger; and whoso is opposed to Allah, (for him) verily Allah is stern in reprisal.
005 mā qaṭaʿtum min leenatin aw taraktumoohā qā-imatan ʿalā oṣoolihā fabi-iḏni allāhi waliyuḫziya alfāsiqeena Whatsoever palm-trees ye cut down or left standing on their roots, it was by Allah's leave, in order that He might confound the evil-livers.
006 wamā afāa allāhu ʿalā rasoolihi minhum famā awjaftum ʿalayhi min ḫaylin walā rikābin walākinna allāha yusalliṭu rusulahu ʿalā man yašāo waallāhu ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerun And that which Allah gave as spoil unto His messenger from them, ye urged not any horse or riding-camel for the sake thereof, but Allah giveth His messenger lordship over whom He will. Allah is Able to do all things.
007 mā afāa allāhu ʿalā rasoolihi min ahli alqurā falillāhi walilrrasooli waliḏee alqurbā waalyatāmā waalmasākeeni waibni alssabeeli kay lā yakoona doolatan bayna al-aġniyā-i minkum wamā ātākumu alrrasoolu faḫuḏoohu wamā nahākum ʿanhu faintahoo waittaqoo allāha inna allāha šadeedu alʿiqābi That which Allah giveth as spoil unto His messenger from the people of the townships, it is for Allah and His messenger and for the near of kin and the orphans and the needy and the wayfarer, that it become not a commodity between the rich among you. And whatsoever the messenger giveth you, take it. And whatsoever he forbiddeth, abstain (from it). And keep your duty to Allah. Lo! Allah is stern in reprisal.
008 lilfuqarā-i almuhājireena allāḏeena oḫrijoo min diyārihim waamwālihim yabtaġoona faḍlan mina allāhi wariḍwānan wayanṣuroona allāha warasoolahu olā-ika humu alssadiqoona And (it is) for the poor fugitives who have been driven out from their homes and their belongings, who seek bounty from Allah and help Allah and His messenger. They are the loyal.
009 waallaḏeena tabawwaoo alddāra waal-eemāna min qablihim yuḥibboona man hājara ilayhim walā yajidoona fee ṣudoorihim hajatan mimmā ootoo wayu/ṯiroona ʿalā anfusihim walaw kāna bihim ḫasasatun waman yooqa šuhha nafsihi faolā-ika humu almufliḥoona Those who entered the city and the faith before them love those who flee unto them for refuge, and find in their breasts no need for that which hath been given them, but prefer (the fugitives) above themselves though poverty become their lot. And whoso is saved from his own avarice - such are they who are successful.
010 waallaḏeena jāoo min baʿdihim yaqooloona rabbanā iġfir lanā wali-iḫwāninā allāḏeena sabaqoonā bial-eemāni walā tajʿal fee quloobinā ġillan lillaḏeena āmanoo rabbanā innaka raoofun raḥeemun And those who came (into the faith) after them say: Our Lord! Forgive us and our brethren who were before us in the faith, and place not in our hearts any rancour toward those who believe. Our Lord! Thou art Full of Pity, Merciful.
011 alam tara ilā allāḏeena nāfaqoo yaqooloona li-iḫwānihimu allāḏeena kafaroo min ahli alkitābi la-in oḫrijtum lanaḫrujanna maʿakum walā nuṭeeʿu feekum aḥadan abadan wa-in qootiltum lananṣurannakum waallāhu yašhadu innahum lakaṯiboona Hast thou not observed those who are hypocrites, (how) they tell their brethren who disbelieve among the People of the Scripture: If ye are driven out, we surely will go out with you, and we will never obey anyone against you, and if ye are attacked we verily will help you. And Allah beareth witness that they verily are liars.
012 la-in oḫrijoo lā yaḫrujoona maʿahum wala-in qootiloo lā yanṣuroonahum wala-in naṣaroohum layuwallunna al-adbāra ṯumma lā yunṣaroona (For) indeed if they are driven out they go not out with them, and indeed if they are attacked they help them not, and indeed if they had helped them they would have turned and fled, and then they would not have been victorious.
013 laantum ašaddu rahbatan fee ṣudoorihim mina allāhi ṯalika bi-annahum qawmun lā yafqahoona Ye are more awful as a fear in their bosoms than Allah. That is because they are a folk who understand not.
014 lā yuqātiloonakum jameeʿan illā fee quran muḥaṣanatin aw min warā-i judurin ba/suhum baynahum šadeedun taḥsabuhum jameeʿan waquloobuhum šattā ṯalika bi-annahum qawmun lā yaʿqiloona They will not fight against you in a body save in fortified villages or from behind walls. Their adversity among themselves is very great. Ye think of them as a whole whereas their hearts are divers. That is because they are a folk who have no sense.
015 kamaṯali allāḏeena min qablihim qareeban ṯaqoo wabāla amrihim walahum ʿaṯabun aleemun On the likeness of those (who suffered) a short time before them, they taste the ill-effects of their own conduct, and theirs is painful punishment.
016 kamaṯali alššaytani iḏ qāla lil-insāni okfur falammā kafara qāla innee baree-on minka innee aḫāfu allāha rabba alʿālameena (And the hypocrites are) on the likeness of the devil when he telleth man to disbelieve, then, when he disbelieveth saith: Lo! I am quit of thee. Lo! I fear Allah, the Lord of the Worlds.
017 fakāna ʿāqibatahumā annahumā fee alnnāri ḫālidayni feehā waṯalika jazāo alṯṯālimeena And the consequence for both will be that they are in the Fire, therein abiding. Such is the reward of evil-doers.
018 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo ittaqoo allāha waltanḏur nafsun mā qaddamat liġadin waittaqoo allāha inna allāha ḫabeerun bimā taʿmaloona O ye who believe! Observe your duty to Allah. And let every soul look to that which it sendeth on before for the morrow. And observe your duty to Allah. Lo! Allah is Informed of what ye do.
019 walā takoonoo kaallaḏeena nasoo allāha faansāhum anfusahum olā-ika humu alfāsiqoona And be not ye as those who forgot Allah, therefor He caused them to forget their souls. Such are the evil-doers.
020 lā yastawee aṣ-habu alnnāri waaṣ-habu aljannati aṣ-habu aljannati humu alfā-izoona Not equal are the owners of the Fire and the owners of the Garden. The owners of the Garden, they are the victorious.
021 law anzalnā haṯa alqur-āna ʿalā jabalin laraaytahu ḫāšiʿan mutaṣaddiʿan min ḫašyati allāhi watilka al-amṯālu naḍribuhā lilnnāsi laʿallahum yatafakkaroona If We had caused this Qur'an to descend upon a mountain, thou (O Muhammad) verily hadst seen it humbled, rent asunder by the fear of Allah. Such similitudes coin We for mankind that haply they may reflect.
022 huwa allāhu allāḏee lā ilāha illā huwa ʿālimu alġaybi waalššahādati huwa alrraḥmānu alrraḥeemu He is Allah, than Whom there is no other God, the Knower of the Invisible and the Visible. He is the Beneficent, Merciful.
023 huwa allāhu allāḏee lā ilāha illā huwa almaliku alquddoosu alssalāmu almu/minu almuhayminu alʿazeezu aljabbāru almutakabbiru subhana allāhi ʿammā yušrikoona He is Allah, than Whom there is no other God, the Sovereign Lord, the Holy One, Peace, the Keeper of Faith, the Guardian, the Majestic, the Compeller, the Superb. Glorified be Allah from all that they ascribe as partner (unto Him).
24 huwa allāhu alḫāliqu albāri-o almuṣawwiru lahu al-asmāo alḥusnā yusabbiḥu lahu mā fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wahuwa alʿazeezu alḥakeemu [Could not extract English for verse 24]
