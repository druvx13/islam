28. al-Qasas: The Narrative

001 [romanization missing] Ta. Sin. Mim.
002 tilka āyātu alkitābi almubeeni These are revelations of the Scripture that maketh plain.
003 natloo ʿalayka min naba-i moosā wafirʿawna bialḥaqqi liqawmin yu/minoona We narrate unto thee (somewhat) of the story of Moses and Pharaoh with truth, for folk who believe.
004 inna firʿawna ʿalā fee al-arḍi wajaʿala ahlahā šiyaʿan yastaḍʿifu ta-ifatan minhum yuḏabbiḥu abnāahum wayastaḥyee nisāahum innahu kāna mina almufsideena Lo! Pharaoh exalted himself in the earth and made its people castes. A tribe among them he oppressed, killing their sons and sparing their women. Lo! he was of those who work corruption.
005 wanureedu an namunna ʿalā allāḏeena istuḍʿifoo fee al-arḍi wanajʿalahum a-immatan wanajʿalahumu alwāriṯeena And We desired to show favour unto those who were oppressed in the earth, and to make them examples and to make them the inheritors,
006 wanumakkina lahum fee al-arḍi wanuriya firʿawna wahāmāna wajunoodahumā minhum mā kānoo yahṯaroona And to establish them in the earth, and to show Pharaoh and Haman and their hosts that which they feared from them.
007 waawḥaynā ilā ommi moosā an arḍiʿeehi fa-iṯa ḫifti ʿalayhi faalqeehi fee alyammi walā taḫāfee walā taḥzanee innā rāddoohu ilayki wajāʿiloohu mina almursaleena And We inspired the mother of Moses, saying: Suckle him and, when thou fearest for him, then cast him into the river and fear not nor grieve. Lo! We shall bring him back unto thee and shall make him (one) of Our messengers.
008 failtaqaṭahu ālu firʿawna liyakoona lahum ʿaduwwan waḥazanan inna firʿawna wahāmāna wajunoodahumā kānoo ḫati-eena And the family of Pharaoh took him up, that he might become for them an enemy and a sorrow, Lo! Pharaoh and Haman and their hosts were ever sinning.
009 waqālati imraatu firʿawna qurratu ʿaynin lee walaka lā taqtuloohu ʿasā an yanfaʿanā aw nattaḫiḏahu waladan wahum lā yašʿuroona And the wife of Pharaoh said: (He will be) a consolation for me and for thee. Kill him not. Peradventure he may be of use to us, or we may choose him for a son. And they perceived not.
010 waaṣbaḥa fu-ādu ommi moosā fāriġan in kādat latubdee bihi lawlā an rabaṭnā ʿalā qalbihā litakoona mina almu/mineena And the heart of the mother of Moses became void, and she would have betrayed him if We had not fortified her heart, that she might be of the believers.
011 waqālat li-oḫtihi quṣeehi fabaṣurat bihi ʿan junubin wahum lā yašʿuroona And she said unto his sister: Trace him. So she observed him from afar, and they perceived not.
012 waḥarramnā ʿalayhi almaradiʿa min qablu faqālat hal adullukum ʿalā ahli baytin yakfuloonahu lakum wahum lahu nasiḥoona And We had before forbidden foster-mothers for him, so she said: Shall I show you a household who will rear him for you and take care of him ?
013 faradadnāhu ilā ommihi kay taqarra ʿaynuhā walā taḥzana walitaʿlama anna waʿda allāhi ḥaqqun walākinna akṯarahum lā yaʿlamoona So We restored him to his mother that she might be comforted and not grieve, and that she might know that the promise of Allah is true. But most of them know not.
014 walammā balaġa ašuddahu waistawā ātaynāhu ḥukman waʿilman wakaṯalika najzee almuḥsineena And when he reached his full strength and was ripe, We gave him wisdom and knowledge. Thus do We reward the good.
015 wadaḫala almadeenata ʿalā ḥeeni ġaflatin min ahlihā fawajada feehā rajulayni yaqtatilāni haṯa min šeeʿatihi wahaṯa min ʿaduwwihi faistaġāṯahu allāḏee min šeeʿatihi ʿalā allāḏee min ʿaduwwihi fawakazahu moosā faqada ʿalayhi qāla haṯa min ʿamali alššaytani innahu ʿaduwwun muḍillun mubeenun And he entered the city at a time of carelessness of its folk, and he found therein two men fighting, one of his own caste, and the other of his enemies; and he who was of his caste asked him for help against him who was of his enemies. So Moses struck him with his fist and killed him. He said: This is of the devil's doing. Lo! he is an enemy, a mere misleader.
016 qāla rabbi innee ḏalamtu nafsee faiġfir lee faġafara lahu innahu huwa alġafooru alrraḥeemu He said: My Lord! Lo! I have wronged my soul, so forgive me. Then He forgave him. Lo! He is the Forgiving, the Merciful.
017 qāla rabbi bimā anʿamta ʿalayya falan akoona ḏaheeran lilmujrimeena He said: My Lord! Forasmuch as Thou hast favoured me, I will nevermore be a supporter of the guilty.
018 faaṣbaḥa fee almadeenati ḫā-ifan yataraqqabu fa-iṯa allāḏee istanṣarahu bial-amsi yastaṣriḫuhu qāla lahu moosā innaka laġawiyyun mubeenun And morning found him in the city, fearing, vigilant, when behold! he who had appealed to him the day before cried out to him for help. Moses said unto him: Lo! thou art indeed a mere hothead.
019 falammā an arāda an yabṭiša biallaḏee huwa ʿaduwwun lahumā qāla yā moosā atureedu an taqtulanee kamā qatalta nafsan bial-amsi in tureedu illā an takoona jabbāran fee al-arḍi wamā tureedu an takoona mina almuṣliḥeena And when he would have fallen upon the man who was an enemy unto them both, he said: O Moses! Wouldst thou kill me as thou didst kill a person yesterday. Thou wouldst be nothing but a tyrant in the land, thou wouldst not be of the reformers.
020 wajāa rajulun min aqsa almadeenati yasʿā qāla yā moosā inna almalaa ya/tamiroona bika liyaqtulooka faoḫruj innee laka mina alnnasiḥeena And a man came from the uttermost part of the city, running. He said: O Moses! Lo! the chiefs take counsel against thee to slay thee; therefor escape. Lo! I am of those who give thee good advice.
021 faḫaraja minhā ḫā-ifan yataraqqabu qāla rabbi najjinee mina alqawmi alṯṯālimeena So he escaped from thence, fearing, vigilant. He said: My Lord! Deliver me from the wrongdoing folk.
022 walammā tawajjaha tilqāa madyana qāla ʿasā rabbee an yahdiyanee sawāa alssabeeli And when he turned his face toward Midian, he said: Peradventure my Lord will guide me in the right road.
023 walammā warada māa madyana wajada ʿalayhi ommatan mina alnnāsi yasqoona wawajada min doonihimu imraatayni taḏoodāni qāla mā ḫaṭbukumā qālatā lā nasqee ḥattā yuṣdira alrriʿāo waaboonā šayḫun kabeerun And when he came unto the water of Midian he found there a whole tribe of men, watering. And he found apart from them two women keeping back (their flocks). He said: What aileth you ? The two said: We cannot give (our flocks) to drink till the shepherds return from the water; and our father is a very old man.
024 fasaqā lahumā ṯumma tawallā ilā alṯṯilli faqāla rabbi innee limā anzalta ilayya min ḫayrin faqeerun So he watered (their flock) for them. Then he turned aside into the shade, and said: My Lord! I am needy of whatever good Thou sendest down for me.
025 fajāat-hu iḥdāhumā tamšee ʿalā istiḥyā-in qālat inna abee yadʿooka liyajziyaka ajra mā saqayta lanā falammā jāahu waqaṣa ʿalayhi alqaṣaṣa qāla lā taḫaf najawta mina alqawmi alṯṯālimeena Then there came unto him one of the two women, walking shyly. She said: Lo! my father biddeth thee, that he may reward thee with a payment for that thou didst water (the flock) for us. Then, when he came unto him and told him the (whole) story, he said: Fear not! Thou hast escaped from the wrongdoing folk.
026 qālat iḥdāhumā yā abati ista/jirhu inna ḫayra mani ista/jarta alqawiyyu al-ameenu One of the two women said: O my father! Hire him! For the best (man) that thou canst hire in the strong, the trustworthy.
027 qāla innee oreedu an onkiḥaka iḥdā ibnatayya hātayni ʿalā an ta/juranee ṯamāniya ḥijajin fa-in atmamta ʿašran famin ʿindika wamā oreedu an ašuqqa ʿalayka satajidunee in šāa allāhu mina alssaliḥeena He said: Lo! I fain would marry thee to one of these two daughters of mine on condition that thou hirest thyself to me for (the term of) eight pilgrimages. Then if thou completest ten it will be of thine own accord, for I would not make it hard for thee. Allah willing, thou wilt find me of the righteous.
028 qāla ṯalika baynee wabaynaka ayyamā al-ajalayni qaḍaytu falā ʿudwāna ʿalayya waallāhu ʿalā mā naqoolu wakeelun He said: That (is settled) between thee and me. Whichever of the two terms I fulfil, there will be no injustice to me, and Allah is Surety over what we say.
029 falammā qada moosā al-ajala wasāra bi-ahlihi ānasa min jānibi alttoori nāran qāla li-ahlihi omkuṯoo innee ānastu nāran laʿallee āteekum minhā biḫabarin aw jaḏwatin mina alnnāri laʿallakum tastaloona Then, when Moses had fulfilled the term, and was travelling with his housefolk, he saw in the distance a fire and said unto his housefolk: Bide ye (here). Lo! I see in the distance a fire; peradventure I shall bring you tidings thence, or a brand from the fire that ye may warm yourselves.
030 falammā atāhā noodiya min šati-i alwādi al-aymani fee albuqʿati almubārakati mina alššajarati an yā moosā innee anā allāhu rabbu alʿālameena And when he reached it, he was called from the right side of the valley in the blessed field, from the tree: O Moses! Lo! I, even I, am Allah, the Lord of the Worlds;
031 waan alqi ʿasaka falammā raāhā tahtazzu kaannahā jānnun wallā mudbiran walam yuʿaqqib yā moosā aqbil walā taḫaf innaka mina al-āmineena Throw down thy staff. And when he saw it writhing as it had been a demon, he turned to flee headlong, (and it was said unto him): O Moses! Draw nigh and fear not. Lo! thou art of those who are secure.
032 osluk yadaka fee jaybika taḫruj baydaa min ġayri soo-in waoḍmum ilayka janahaka mina alrrahbi faṯanika burhānāni min rabbika ilā firʿawna wamala-ihi innahum kānoo qawman fāsiqeena Thrust thy hand into the bosom of thy robe it will come forth white without hurt. And guard thy heart from fear. Then these shall be two proofs from your Lord unto Pharaoh and his chiefs. Lo! they are evil-living folk.
033 qāla rabbi innee qataltu minhum nafsan faaḫāfu an yaqtulooni He said: My Lord! Lo! I killed a man among them and I fear that they will kill me.
034 waaḫee hāroonu huwa afṣaḥu minnee lisānan faarsilhu maʿiya rid-an yuṣaddiqunee innee aḫāfu an yukaṯṯibooni My brother Aaron is more eloquent than me in speech. Therefor send him with me as a helper to confirm me. Lo! I fear that they will give the lie to me.
035 qāla sanašuddu ʿaḍudaka bi-aḫeeka wanajʿalu lakumā sultanan falā yaṣiloona ilaykumā bi-āyātinā antumā wamani ittabaʿakumā alġāliboona He said: We will strengthen thine arm with thy brother, and We will give unto you both power so that they cannot reach you for Our portents. Ye twain, and those who follow you, will be the winners.
036 falammā jāahum moosā bi-āyātinā bayyinātin qāloo mā haṯa illā siḥrun muftaran wamā samiʿnā bihaṯa fee ābā-inā al-awwaleena But when Moses came unto them with Our clear tokens, they said: This is naught but invented magic. We never heard of this among our fathers of old.
037 waqāla moosā rabbee aʿlamu biman jāa bialhudā min ʿindihi waman takoonu lahu ʿāqibatu alddāri innahu lā yufliḥu alṯṯālimoona And Moses said: My Lord is Best Aware of him who bringeth guidance from His presence, and whose will be the sequel of the Home (of bliss). Lo! wrong-doers will not be successful.
038 waqāla firʿawnu yā ayyuhā almalao mā ʿalimtu lakum min ilāhin ġayree faawqid lee yā hāmānu ʿalā altteeni faijʿal lee ṣarḥan laʿallee attaliʿu ilā ilāhi moosā wa-innee laaḏunnuhu mina alkaṯibeena And Pharaoh said: O chiefs! I know not that ye have a god other than me, so kindle for me (a fire), O Haman, to bake the mud; and set up for me a lofty tower in order that I may survey the God of Moses; and lo! I deem him of the liars.
039 waistakbara huwa wajunooduhu fee al-arḍi biġayri alḥaqqi waḏannoo annahum ilaynā lā yurjaʿoona And he and his hosts were haughty in the land without right, and deemed that they would never be brought back to Us.
040 faaḫaḏnāhu wajunoodahu fanabaḏnāhum fee alyammi faonḏur kayfa kāna ʿāqibatu alṯṯālimeena Therefor We seized him and his hosts, and abandoned them unto the sea. Behold the nature of the consequence for evil-doers!
041 wajaʿalnāhum a-immatan yadʿoona ilā alnnāri wayawma alqiyāmati lā yunṣaroona And We made them patterns that invite unto the Fire, and on the Day of Resurrection they will not be helped.
042 waatbaʿnāhum fee haṯihi alddunyā laʿnatan wayawma alqiyāmati hum mina almaqbooḥeena And We made a curse to follow them in this world, and on the Day of Resurrection they will be among the hateful.
043 walaqad ātaynā moosā alkitāba min baʿdi mā ahlaknā alquroona al-oolā basa-ira lilnnāsi wahudan waraḥmatan laʿallahum yataḏakkaroona And We verily gave the Scripture unto Moses after We had destroyed the generations of old: clear testimonies for mankind, and a guidance and a mercy, that haply they might reflect.
044 wamā kunta bijānibi alġarbiyyi iḏ qaḍaynā ilā moosā al-amra wamā kunta mina alššāhideena And thou (Muhammad) wast not on the western side (of the Mount) when We expounded unto Moses the commandment, and thou wast not among those present;
045 walākinnā anša-nā quroonan fatatawala ʿalayhimu alʿumuru wamā kunta ṯāwiyan fee ahli madyana tatloo ʿalayhim āyātinā walākinnā kunnā mursileena But We brought forth generations, and their lives dragged on for them. And thou wast not a dweller in Midian, reciting unto them Our revelations, but We kept sending (messengers to men).
046 wamā kunta bijānibi alttoori iḏ nādaynā walākin raḥmatan min rabbika litunḏira qawman mā atāhum min naḏeerin min qablika laʿallahum yataḏakkaroona And thou was not beside the Mount when We did call; but (the knowledge of it is) a mercy from thy Lord that thou mayst warn a folk unto whom no warner came before thee, that haply they may give heed.
047 walawlā an tuṣeebahum muṣeebatun bimā qaddamat aydeehim fayaqooloo rabbanā lawlā arsalta ilaynā rasoolan fanattabiʿa āyātika wanakoona mina almu/mineena Otherwise, if disaster should afflict them because of that which their own hands have sent before (them), they might say: Our Lord! Why sentest Thou no messenger unto us, that we might have followed Thy revelations and been of the believers ?
048 falammā jāahumu alḥaqqu min ʿindinā qāloo lawlā ootiya miṯla mā ootiya moosā awa lam yakfuroo bimā ootiya moosā min qablu qāloo siḥrāni taḏāharā waqāloo innā bikullin kāfiroona But when there came unto them the Truth from Our presence, they said: Why is he not given the like of what was given unto Moses ? Did they not disbelieve in that which was given unto Moses of old ? They say: Two magics that support each other; and they say: Lo! in both we are disbelievers.
049 qul fa/too bikitābin min ʿindi allāhi huwa ahdā minhumā attabiʿhu in kuntum sadiqeena Say (unto them, O Muhammad): Then bring a scripture from the presence of Allah that giveth clearer guidance than these two (that) I may follow it, if ye are truthful.
050 fa-in lam yastajeeboo laka faiʿlam annamā yattabiʿoona ahwāahum waman aḍallu mimmani ittabaʿa hawāhu biġayri hudan mina allāhi inna allāha lā yahdee alqawma alṯṯālimeena And if they answer thee not, then know that what they follow is their lusts. And who goeth farther astray than he who followeth his lust without guidance from Allah. Lo! Allah guideth not wrongdoing folk.
051 walaqad waṣalnā lahumu alqawla laʿallahum yataḏakkaroona And now verily We have caused the Word to reach them, that haply they may give heed.
052 allāḏeena ātaynāhumu alkitāba min qablihi hum bihi yu/minoona Those unto whom We gave the Scripture before it, they believe in it,
053 wa-iṯa yutlā ʿalayhim qāloo āmannā bihi innahu alḥaqqu min rabbinā innā kunnā min qablihi muslimeena And when it is recited unto them, they say: We believe in it. Lo! it is the Truth from our Lord. Lo! even before it we were of those who surrender (unto Him).
054 olā-ika yu/tawna ajrahum marratayni bimā ṣabaroo wayadraoona bialḥasanati alssayyi-ata wamimmā razaqnāhum yunfiqoona These will be given their reward twice over, because they are steadfast and repel evil with good, and spend of that wherewith We have provided them,
055 wa-iṯa samiʿoo allāġwa aʿraḍoo ʿanhu waqāloo lanā aʿmālunā walakum aʿmālukum salāmun ʿalaykum lā nabtaġee aljāhileena And when they hear vanity they withdraw from it and say: Unto us our works and unto you your works. Peace be unto you! We desire not the ignorant.
056 innaka lā tahdee man aḥbabta walākinna allāha yahdee man yašāo wahuwa aʿlamu bialmuhtadeena Lo! thou (O Muhammad) guidest not whom thou lovest, but Allah guideth whom He will. And He is Best Aware of those who walk aright.
057 waqāloo in nattabiʿi alhudā maʿaka nutaḫattaf min arḍinā awa lam numakkin lahum ḥaraman āminan yujbā ilayhi ṯamarātu kulli šay-in rizqan min ladunnā walākinna akṯarahum lā yaʿlamoona And they say: If we were to follow the Guidance with thee we should be torn out of our land. Have We not established for them a sure sanctuary, whereunto the produce of all things is brought (in trade), a provision from Our presence ? But most of them know not.
058 wakam ahlaknā min qaryatin baṭirat maʿeešatahā fatilka masākinuhum lam tuskan min baʿdihim illā qaleelan wakunnā naḥnu alwāriṯeena And how many a community have We destroyed that was thankless for its means of livelihood! And yonder are their dwellings, which have not been inhabited after them save a little. And We, even We, were the inheritors.
059 wamā kāna rabbuka muhlika alqurā ḥattā yabʿaṯa fee ommihā rasoolan yatloo ʿalayhim āyātinā wamā kunnā muhlikee alqurā illā waahluhā ḏālimoona And never did thy Lord destroy the townships, till He had raised up in their mother(-town) a messenger reciting unto them Our revelations. And never did We destroy the townships unless the folk thereof were evil-doers.
060 wamā ooteetum min šay-in famatāʿu alḥayāti alddunyā wazeenatuhā wamā ʿinda allāhi ḫayrun waabqā afalā taʿqiloona And whatsoever ye have been given is a comfort of the life of the world and an ornament thereof; and that which Allah hath is better and more lasting. Have ye then no sense ?
061 afaman waʿadnāhu waʿdan ḥasanan fahuwa lāqeehi kaman mattaʿnāhu matāʿa alḥayāti alddunyā ṯumma huwa yawma alqiyāmati mina almuhdareena Is he whom We have promised a fair promise which he will find (true) like him whom We suffer to enjoy awhile the comfort of the life of the world, then on the Day of Resurrection he will be of those arraigned ?
062 wayawma yunādeehim fayaqoolu ayna šurakā-iya allāḏeena kuntum tazʿumoona On the day when He will call unto them and say: Where are My partners whom ye imagined ?
063 qāla allāḏeena ḥaqqa ʿalayhimu alqawlu rabbanā hāolā-i allāḏeena aġwaynā aġwaynāhum kamā ġawaynā tabarra/nā ilayka mā kānoo iyyānā yaʿbudoona Those concerning whom the Word will have come true will say: Our Lord! These are they whom we led astray. We led them astray even as we ourselves were astray. We declare our innocence before Thee: us they never worshipped.
064 waqeela odʿoo šurakāakum fadaʿawhum falam yastajeeboo lahum waraawoo alʿaṯaba law annahum kānoo yahtadoona And it will be said: Cry unto your (so-called) partners (of Allah). And they will cry unto them, and they will give no answer unto them, and they will see the Doom. Ah, if they had but been guided!
065 wayawma yunādeehim fayaqoolu maṯa ajabtumu almursaleena And on the Day when He will call unto them and say: What answer gave ye to the messengers ?
066 faʿamiyat ʿalayhimu al-anbāo yawma-iḏin fahum lā yatasāaloona On that day (all) tidings will be dimmed for them, nor will they ask one of another,
067 faammā man tāba waāmana waʿamila saliḥan faʿasā an yakoona mina almufliḥeena But as for him who shall repent and believe and do right, he haply may be one of the successful.
068 warabbuka yaḫluqu mā yašāo wayaḫtāru mā kāna lahumu alḫiyaratu subhana allāhi wataʿālā ʿammā yušrikoona Thy Lord bringeth to pass what He willeth and chooseth. They have never any choice. Glorified be Allah and Exalted above all that they associate (with Him)!
069 warabbuka yaʿlamu mā tukinnu ṣudooruhum wamā yuʿlinoona And thy Lord knoweth what their breasts conceal, and what they publish.
070 wahuwa allāhu lā ilāha illā huwa lahu alḥamdu fee al-oolā waal-āḫirati walahu alḥukmu wa-ilayhi turjaʿoona And He is Allah; there is no God save Him. His is all praise in the former and the latter (state), and His is the command, and unto Him ye will be brought back.
071 qul araaytum in jaʿala allāhu ʿalaykumu allāyla sarmadan ilā yawmi alqiyāmati man ilāhun ġayru allāhi ya/teekum biḍiyā-in afalā tasmaʿoona Say: Have ye thought, if Allah made night everlasting for you till the Day of Resurrection, who is a god beside Allah who could bring you light ? Will ye not then hear ?
072 qul araaytum in jaʿala allāhu ʿalaykumu alnnahāra sarmadan ilā yawmi alqiyāmati man ilāhun ġayru allāhi ya/teekum bilaylin taskunoona feehi afalā tubṣiroona Say: Have ye thought, if Allah made day everlasting for you till the Day of Resurrection, who is a god beside Allah who could bring you night wherein ye rest ? Will ye not then see ?
073 wamin raḥmatihi jaʿala lakumu allāyla waalnnahāra litaskunoo feehi walitabtaġoo min faḍlihi walaʿallakum taškuroona Of His mercy hath He appointed for you night and day, that therein ye may rest, and that ye may seek His bounty, and that haply ye may be thankful.
074 wayawma yunādeehim fayaqoolu ayna šurakā-iya allāḏeena kuntum tazʿumoona And on the Day when He shall call unto them and say: Where are My partners whom ye pretended ?
075 wanazaʿnā min kulli ommatin šaheedan faqulnā hātoo burhānakum faʿalimoo anna alḥaqqa lillāhi waḍalla ʿanhum mā kānoo yaftaroona And We shall take out from every nation a witness and We shall say: Bring your proof. Then they will know that Allah hath the Truth, and all that they invented will have failed them.
076 inna qāroona kāna min qawmi moosā fabaġā ʿalayhim waātaynāhu mina alkunoozi mā inna mafātiḥahu latanoo-o bialʿuṣbati olee alquwwati iḏ qāla lahu qawmuhu lā tafraḥ inna allāha lā yuḥibbu alfariḥeena Now Korah was of Moses' folk, but he oppressed them; and We gave him so much treasure that the stores thereof would verily have been a burden for a troop of mighty men. When his own folk said unto him: Exult not; lo! Allah loveth not the exultant;
077 waibtaġi feemā ātāka allāhu alddāra al-āḫirata walā tansa naṣeebaka mina alddunyā waaḥsin kamā aḥsana allāhu ilayka walā tabġi alfasāda fee al-arḍi inna allāha lā yuḥibbu almufsideena But seek the abode of the Hereafter in that which Allah hath given thee and neglect not thy portion of the world, and be thou kind even as Allah hath been kind to thee, and seek not corruption in the earth; lo! Allah loveth not corrupters,
078 qāla innamā ooteetuhu ʿalā ʿilmin ʿindee awa lam yaʿlam anna allāha qad ahlaka min qablihi mina alqurooni man huwa ašaddu minhu quwwatan waakṯaru jamʿan walā yus-alu ʿan ḏunoobihimu almujrimoona He said: I have been given it only on account of knowledge I possess. Knew he not that Allah had destroyed already of the generations before him men who were mightier than him in strength and greater in respect of following ? The guilty are not questioned of their sins.
079 faḫaraja ʿalā qawmihi fee zeenatihi qāla allāḏeena yureedoona alḥayāta alddunyā yā layta lanā miṯla mā ootiya qāroonu innahu laḏoo ḥaṯṯin ʿaḏeemin Then went he forth before his people in his pomp. Those who were desirous of the life of the world said: Ah, would that we had the like of what hath been given unto Korah! Lo! he is lord of rare good fortune.
080 waqāla allāḏeena ootoo alʿilma waylakum ṯawābu allāhi ḫayrun liman āmana waʿamila saliḥan walā yulaqqāhā illā alssabiroona But those who had been given knowledge said: Woe unto you! The reward of Allah for him who believeth and doeth right is better, and only the steadfast will obtain it.
081 faḫasafnā bihi wabidārihi al-arḍa famā kāna lahu min fi-atin yanṣuroonahu min dooni allāhi wamā kāna mina almuntaṣireena So We caused the earth to swallow him and his dwelling-place. Then he had no host to help him against Allah, nor was he of those who can save themselves.
082 waaṣbaḥa allāḏeena tamannaw makānahu bial-amsi yaqooloona waykaanna allāha yabsuṭu alrrizqa liman yašāo min ʿibādihi wayaqdiru lawlā an manna allāhu ʿalaynā laḫasafa binā waykaannahu lā yufliḥu alkāfiroona And morning found those who had coveted his place but yesterday crying: Ah, welladay! Allah enlargeth the provision for whom He will of His slaves and straiteneth it (for whom He will). If Allah had not been gracious unto us He would have caused it to swallow us (also). Ah, welladay! the disbelievers never prosper.
083 tilka alddāru al-āḫiratu najʿaluhā lillaḏeena lā yureedoona ʿuluwwan fee al-arḍi walā fasādan waalʿāqibatu lilmuttaqeena As for that Abode of the Hereafter We assign it unto those who seek not oppression in the earth, nor yet corruption. The sequel is for those who ward off (evil).
084 man jāa bialḥasanati falahu ḫayrun minhā waman jāa bialssayyi-ati falā yujzā allāḏeena ʿamiloo alssayyi-āti illā mā kānoo yaʿmaloona Whoso bringeth a good deed, he will have better than the same; while as for him who bringeth an ill-deed, those who do ill-deeds will be requited only what they did.
085 inna allāḏee faraḍa ʿalayka alqur-āna larādduka ilā maʿādin qul rabbee aʿlamu man jāa bialhudā waman huwa fee ḍalālin mubeenin Lo! He Who hath given thee the Qur'an for a law will surely bring thee home again. Say: My Lord is Best Aware of him who bringeth guidance and him who is in error manifest.
086 wamā kunta tarjoo an yulqā ilayka alkitābu illā raḥmatan min rabbika falā takoonanna ḏaheeran lilkāfireena Thou hadst no hope that the Scripture would be inspired in thee; but it is a mercy from thy Lord, so never be a helper to the disbelievers.
087 walā yaṣuddunnaka ʿan āyāti allāhi baʿda iḏ onzilat ilayka waodʿu ilā rabbika walā takoonanna mina almušrikeena And let them not divert thee from the revelations of Allah after they have been sent down unto thee; but call (mankind) unto thy Lord, and be not of those who ascribe partners (unto Him).
