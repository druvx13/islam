73. al-Muzammil: The Mantled One

1 
yā ayyuhā almuzzammilu
O thou wrapped up in thy raiment!

2 
qumi allāyla illā qaleelān
Keep vigil the night long, save a little -

3 
niṣfahu awi onquṣ minhu qaleelān
A half thereof, or abate a little thereof

4 
aw zid ʿalayhi warattili alqur-āna tarteelān
Or add (a little) thereto - and chant the Qur'an in measure,

5 
innā sanulqee ʿalayka qawlan ṯaqeelān
For we shall charge thee with a word of weight.

6 
inna nāši-ata allāyli hiya ašaddu waṭ-an waaqwamu qeelān
Lo! the vigil of the night is (a time) when impression is more keen and speech more certain.

7 
inna laka fee alnnahāri sabḥan ṭaweelān
Lo! thou hast by day a chain of business.

8 
waoḏkuri isma rabbika watabattal ilayhi tabteelān
So remember the name of thy Lord and devote thyself with a complete devotion -

9 
rabbu almašriqi waalmaġribi lā ilāha illā huwa faittaḫiḏhu wakeelān
Lord of the East and the West; there is no God save Him; so choose thou Him alone for thy defender -

10 
waiṣbir ʿalā mā yaqooloona waohjurhum hajran jameelān
And bear with patience what they utter, and part from them with a fair leave- taking.

11 
waḏarnee waalmukaṯṯibeena olee alnnaʿmati wamahhilhum qaleelān
Leave Me to deal with the deniers, lords of ease and comfort (in this life); and do thou respite them awhile.

12 
inna ladaynā ankālan wajaḥeemān
Lo! with Us are heavy fetters and a raging fire,

13 
waṭaʿāman ṯa ġuṣatin waʿaṯaban aleemān
And food which choketh (the partaker), and a painful doom

14 
yawma tarjufu al-arḍu waaljibālu wakānati aljibālu kaṯeeban maheelān
On the day when the earth and the hills rock, and the hills become a heap of running sand.

15 
innā arsalnā ilaykum rasoolan šāhidan ʿalaykum kamā arsalnā ilā firʿawna rasoolān
Lo! We have sent unto you a messenger as witness against you, even as We sent unto Pharaoh a messenger.

16 
faʿasa firʿawnu alrrasoola faaḫaḏnāhu aḫḏan wabeelān
But Pharaoh rebelled against the messenger, whereupon We seized him with no gentle grip.

17 
fakayfa tattaqoona in kafartum yawman yajʿalu alwildāna šeebān
Then how, if ye disbelieve, will ye protect yourselves upon the day which will turn children grey,

18 
alssamāo munfaṭirun bihi kāna waʿduhu mafʿoolān
The very heaven being then rent asunder. His promise is to be fulfilled.

19 
inna haṯihi taḏkiratun faman šāa ittaḫaḏa ilā rabbihi sabeelān
Lo! This is a Reminder. Let him who will, then, choose a way unto his Lord.

20 
inna rabbaka yaʿlamu annaka taqoomu adnā min ṯuluṯayi allāyli waniṣfahu waṯuluṯahu wata-ifatun mina allāḏeena maʿaka waallāhu yuqaddiru allāyla waalnnahāra ʿalima an lan tuhsoohu fatāba ʿalaykum faiqraoo mā tayassara mina alqur-āni ʿalima an sayakoonu minkum marda waāḫaroona yaḍriboona fee al-arḍi yabtaġoona min faḍli allāhi waāḫaroona yuqātiloona fee sabeeli allāhi faiqraoo mā tayassara minhu waaqeemoo alṣalāta waātoo alzzakāta waaqriḍoo allāha qarḍan ḥasanan wamā tuqaddimoo li-anfusikum min ḫayrin tajidoohu ʿinda allāhi huwa ḫayran waaʿḏama ajran waistaġfiroo allāha inna allāha ġafoorun raḥeemun
Lo! thy Lord knoweth how thou keepest vigil sometimes nearly two-thirds of the night, or (sometimes) half or a third thereof, as do a party of those with thee. Allah measureth the night and the day. He knoweth that ye count it not, and turneth unto you in mercy. Recite, then, of the Qur'an that which is easy for you. He knoweth that there are sick folk among you, while others travel in the land in search of Allah's bounty, and others (still) are fighting for the cause of Allah. So recite of it that which is easy (for you), and establish worship and pay the poor-due, and (so) lend unto Allah a goodly loan. Whatsoever good ye send before you for your souls, ye will find it with Allah, better and greater in the recompense. And seek forgiveness of Allah. Lo! Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.
