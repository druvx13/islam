73. al-Muzammil: The Mantled One

001 yā ayyuhā almuzzammilu O thou wrapped up in thy raiment!
002 qumi allāyla illā qaleelān Keep vigil the night long, save a little -
003 niṣfahu awi onquṣ minhu qaleelān A half thereof, or abate a little thereof
004 aw zid ʿalayhi warattili alqur-āna tarteelān Or add (a little) thereto - and chant the Qur'an in measure,
005 innā sanulqee ʿalayka qawlan ṯaqeelān For we shall charge thee with a word of weight.
006 inna nāši-ata allāyli hiya ašaddu waṭ-an waaqwamu qeelān Lo! the vigil of the night is (a time) when impression is more keen and speech more certain.
007 inna laka fee alnnahāri sabḥan ṭaweelān Lo! thou hast by day a chain of business.
008 waoḏkuri isma rabbika watabattal ilayhi tabteelān So remember the name of thy Lord and devote thyself with a complete devotion -
009 rabbu almašriqi waalmaġribi lā ilāha illā huwa faittaḫiḏhu wakeelān Lord of the East and the West; there is no God save Him; so choose thou Him alone for thy defender -
010 waiṣbir ʿalā mā yaqooloona waohjurhum hajran jameelān And bear with patience what they utter, and part from them with a fair leave- taking.
011 waḏarnee waalmukaṯṯibeena olee alnnaʿmati wamahhilhum qaleelān Leave Me to deal with the deniers, lords of ease and comfort (in this life); and do thou respite them awhile.
012 inna ladaynā ankālan wajaḥeemān Lo! with Us are heavy fetters and a raging fire,
013 waṭaʿāman ṯa ġuṣatin waʿaṯaban aleemān And food which choketh (the partaker), and a painful doom
014 yawma tarjufu al-arḍu waaljibālu wakānati aljibālu kaṯeeban maheelān On the day when the earth and the hills rock, and the hills become a heap of running sand.
015 innā arsalnā ilaykum rasoolan šāhidan ʿalaykum kamā arsalnā ilā firʿawna rasoolān Lo! We have sent unto you a messenger as witness against you, even as We sent unto Pharaoh a messenger.
016 faʿasa firʿawnu alrrasoola faaḫaḏnāhu aḫḏan wabeelān But Pharaoh rebelled against the messenger, whereupon We seized him with no gentle grip.
017 fakayfa tattaqoona in kafartum yawman yajʿalu alwildāna šeebān Then how, if ye disbelieve, will ye protect yourselves upon the day which will turn children grey,
018 alssamāo munfaṭirun bihi kāna waʿduhu mafʿoolān The very heaven being then rent asunder. His promise is to be fulfilled.
019 inna haṯihi taḏkiratun faman šāa ittaḫaḏa ilā rabbihi sabeelān Lo! This is a Reminder. Let him who will, then, choose a way unto his Lord.
20 inna rabbaka yaʿlamu annaka taqoomu adnā min ṯuluṯayi allāyli waniṣfahu waṯuluṯahu wata-ifatun mina allāḏeena maʿaka waallāhu yuqaddiru allāyla waalnnahāra ʿalima an lan tuhsoohu fatāba ʿalaykum faiqraoo mā tayassara mina alqur-āni ʿalima an sayakoonu minkum marda waāḫaroona yaḍriboona fee al-arḍi yabtaġoona min faḍli allāhi waāḫaroona yuqātiloona fee sabeeli allāhi faiqraoo mā tayassara minhu waaqeemoo alṣalāta waātoo alzzakāta waaqriḍoo allāha qarḍan ḥasanan wamā tuqaddimoo li-anfusikum min ḫayrin tajidoohu ʿinda allāhi huwa ḫayran waaʿḏama ajran waistaġfiroo allāha inna allāha ġafoorun raḥeemun [Could not extract English for verse 20]
