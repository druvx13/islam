21. al-Anbiya': The Prophets

001 iqtaraba lilnnāsi ḥisābuhum wahum fee ġaflatin muʿriḍoona Their reckoning draweth nigh for mankind, while they turn away in heedlessness.
002 mā ya/teehim min ḏikrin min rabbihim muḥdaṯin illā istamaʿoohu wahum yalʿaboona Never cometh there unto them a new reminder from their Lord but they listen to it while they play,
003 lāhiyatan quloobuhum waasarroo alnnajwā allāḏeena ḏalamoo hal haṯa illā bašarun miṯlukum afata/toona alssiḥra waantum tubṣiroona With hearts preoccupied. And they confer in secret. The wrong-doers say: Is this other than a mortal like you ? Will ye then succumb to magic when ye see (it) ?
004 qāla rabbee yaʿlamu alqawla fee alssamā-i waal-arḍi wahuwa alssameeʿu alʿaleemu He saith: My Lord knoweth what is spoken in the heaven and the earth. He is the Hearer, the Knower.
005 bal qāloo aḍġāṯu aḥlāmin bali iftarāhu bal huwa šāʿirun falya/tinā bi-āyatin kamā orsila al-awwaloona Nay, say they, (these are but) muddled dreams; nay, he hath but invented it; nay, he is but a poet. Let him bring us a portent even as those of old (who were God's messengers) were sent (with portents).
006 mā āmanat qablahum min qaryatin ahlaknāhā afahum yu/minoona Not a township believed of those which We destroyed before them (though We sent them portents): would they then believe ?
007 wamā arsalnā qablaka illā rijālan nooḥee ilayhim fais-aloo ahla alṯṯikri in kuntum lā taʿlamoona And We sent not (as Our messengers) before thee other than men, whom We inspired. Ask the followers of the Reminder if ye know not ?
008 wamā jaʿalnāhum jasadan lā ya/kuloona alttaʿāma wamā kānoo ḫālideena We gave them not bodies that would not eat food, nor were they immortals.
009 thumma ṣadaqnāhumu alwaʿda faanjaynāhum waman našāo waahlaknā almusrifeena Then we fulfilled the promise unto them. So we delivered them and whom We would, and We destroyed the prodigals.
010 laqad anzalnā ilaykum kitāban feehi ḏikrukum afalā taʿqiloona Now We have revealed unto you a Scripture wherein is your Reminder. Have ye then no sense ?
011 wakam qaṣamnā min qaryatin kānat ḏālimatan waanša/nā baʿdahā qawman āḫareena How many a community that dealt unjustly have We shattered, and raised up after them another folk!
012 falammā aḥassoo ba/sanā iṯa hum minhā yarkuḍoona And, when they felt Our might, behold them fleeing from it!
013 lā tarkuḍoo wairjiʿoo ilā mā otriftum feehi wamasākinikum laʿallakum tus-aloona (But it was said unto them): Flee not, but return to that (existence) which emasculated you and to your dwellings, that ye may be questioned.
014 qāloo yā waylanā innā kunnā ḏālimeena They cried: Alas for us! we were wrong-doers.
015 famā zālat tilka daʿwāhum ḥattā jaʿalnāhum ḥaṣeedan ḫāmideena And this their crying ceased not till We made them as reaped corn, extinct.
016 wamā ḫalaqnā alssamāa waal-arḍa wamā baynahumā lāʿibeena We created not the heaven and the earth and all that is between them in play.
017 law aradnā an nattaḫiḏa lahwan laittaḫaḏnāhu min ladunnā in kunnā fāʿileena If We had wished to find a pastime, We could have found it in Our presence - if We ever did.
018 bal naqḏifu bialḥaqqi ʿalā albatili fayadmaġuhu fa-iṯa huwa zāhiqun walakumu alwaylu mimmā taṣifoona Nay, but We hurl the true against the false, and it doth break its head and lo! it vanisheth. And yours will be woe for that which ye ascribe (unto Him).
019 walahu man fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi waman ʿindahu lā yastakbiroona ʿan ʿibādatihi walā yastaḥsiroona Unto Him belongeth whosoever is in the heavens and the earth. And those who dwell in His presence are not too proud to worship Him, nor do they weary;
020 yusabbiḥoona allāyla waalnnahāra lā yafturoona They glorify (Him) night and day; they flag not.
021 ami ittaḫaḏoo ālihatan mina al-arḍi hum yunširoona Or have they chosen gods from the earth who raise the dead ?
022 law kāna feehimā ālihatun illā allāhu lafasadatā fasubhana allāhi rabbi alʿarši ʿammā yaṣifoona If there were therein gods beside Allah, then verily both (the heavens and the earth) had been disordered. Glorified be Allah, the Lord of the Throne, from all that they ascribe (unto Him).
023 lā yus-alu ʿammā yafʿalu wahum yus-aloona He will not be questioned as to that which He doeth, but they will be questioned.
024 ami ittaḫaḏoo min doonihi ālihatan qul hātoo burhānakum haṯa ḏikru man maʿiya waḏikru man qablee bal akṯaruhum lā yaʿlamoona alḥaqqa fahum muʿriḍoona Or have they chosen other gods beside Him ? say: Bring your proof (of their godhead). This is the Reminder of those with me and those before me, but most of them know not the Truth and so they are averse.
025 wamā arsalnā min qablika min rasoolin illā nooḥee ilayhi annahu lā ilāha illā anā faoʿbudooni And We sent no messenger before thee but We inspired him, (saying): There is no God save Me (Allah), so worship Me.
026 waqāloo ittaḫaḏa alrraḥmānu waladan subhanahu bal ʿibādun mukramoona And they say: The Beneficent hath taken unto Himself a son. Be He Glorified! Nay, but (those whom they call sons) are honoured slaves;
027 lā yasbiqoonahu bialqawli wahum bi-amrihi yaʿmaloona They speak not until He hath spoken, and they act by His command.
028 yaʿlamu mā bayna aydeehim wamā ḫalfahum walā yašfaʿoona illā limani irtada wahum min ḫašyatihi mušfiqoona He knoweth what is before them and what is behind them, and they cannot intercede except for him whom He accepteth, and they quake for awe of Him.
029 waman yaqul minhum innee ilāhun min doonihi faṯalika najzeehi jahannama kaṯalika najzee alṯṯālimeena And one of them who should say: Lo! I am a god beside Him, that one We should repay with hell. Thus We Repay wrong-doers.
030 awa lam yarā allāḏeena kafaroo anna alssamāwāti waal-arḍa kānatā ratqan fafataqnāhumā wajaʿalnā mina almā-i kulla šay-in ḥayyin afalā yu/minoona Have not those who disbelieve known that the heavens and the earth were of one piece, then We parted them, and we made every living thing of water ? Will they not then believe ?
031 wajaʿalnā fee al-arḍi rawāsiya an tameeda bihim wajaʿalnā feeha fijājan subulan laʿallahum yahtadoona And We have placed in the earth firm hills lest it quake with them, and We have placed therein ravines as roads that haply they may find their way.
032 wajaʿalnā alssamāa saqfan maḥfooḏan wahum ʿan āyātihā muʿriḍoona And we have made the sky a roof withheld (from them). Yet they turn away from its portents.
033 wahuwa allāḏee ḫalaqa allāyla waalnnahāra waalššamsa waalqamara kullun fee falakin yasbaḥoona And He it is Who created the night and the day, and the sun and the moon. They float, each in an orbit.
034 wamā jaʿalnā libašarin min qablika alḫulda afa-in mitta fahumu alḫālidoona We appointed immortality for no mortal before thee. What! if thou diest, can they be immortal!
035 kullu nafsin ṯa-iqatu almawti wanablookum bialššarri waalḫayri fitnatan wa-ilaynā turjaʿoona Every soul must taste of death, and We try you with evil and with good, for ordeal. And unto Us ye will be returned.
036 wa-iṯa raāka allāḏeena kafaroo in yattaḫiḏoonaka illā huzuwan ahaṯa allāḏee yaḏkuru ālihatakum wahum biḏikri alrraḥmāni hum kāfiroona And when those who disbelieve behold thee, they but choose thee out for mockery, (saying): Is this he who maketh mention of your gods ? And they would deny all mention of the Beneficent.
037 khuliqa al-insānu min ʿajalin saoreekum āyātee falā tastaʿjiloona Man is made of haste. I shall show you My portents, but ask Me not to hasten.
038 wayaqooloona matā haṯa alwaʿdu in kuntum sadiqeena And they say: When will this promise (be fulfilled), if ye are truthful ?
039 law yaʿlamu allāḏeena kafaroo ḥeena lā yakuffoona ʿan wujoohihimu alnnāra walā ʿan ḏuhoorihim walā hum yunṣaroona If those who disbelieved but knew the time when they will not be able to drive off the fire from their faces and from their backs, and they will not be helped!
040 bal ta/teehim baġtatan fatabhatuhum falā yastaṭeeʿoona raddahā walā hum yunḏaroona Nay, but it will come upon them unawares so that it will stupefy them, and they will be unable to repel it, neither will they be reprieved.
041 walaqadi istuhzi-a birusulin min qablika fahaqa biallaḏeena saḫiroo minhum mā kānoo bihi yastahzi-oona Messengers before thee, indeed, were mocked, but that whereat they mocked surrounded those who scoffed at them.
042 qul man yaklaokum biallayli waalnnahāri mina alrraḥmāni bal hum ʿan ḏikri rabbihim muʿriḍoona Say: Who guardeth you in the night or in the day from the Beneficent ? Nay, but they turn away from mention of their Lord!
043 am lahum ālihatun tamnaʿuhum min dooninā lā yastaṭeeʿoona naṣra anfusihim walā hum minnā yušaboona Or have they gods who can shield them from Us ? They cannot help themselves nor can they be defended from Us.
044 bal mattaʿnā hāolā-i waābāahum ḥattā tala ʿalayhimu alʿumuru afalā yarawna annā na/tee al-arḍa nanquṣuhā min aṭrāfihā afahumu alġāliboona Nay, but We gave these and their fathers ease until life grew long for them. See they not how we aim to the land, reducing it of its outlying parts ? Can they then be the victors ?
045 qul innamā onḏirukum bialwaḥyi walā yasmaʿu alṣummu aldduʿāa iṯa mā yunḏaroona Say (O Muhammad, unto mankind): I warn you only by the Inspiration. But the deaf hear not the call when they are warned.
046 wala-in massat-hum nafḥatun min ʿaṯabi rabbika layaqoolunna ya waylanā innā kunnā ḏālimeena And if a breath of thy Lord's punishment were to touch them, they assuredly would say: Alas for us! Lo! we were wrong-doers.
047 wanaḍaʿu almawāzeena alqisṭa liyawmi alqiyāmati falā tuḏlamu nafsun šay-an wa-in kāna miṯqāla ḥabbatin min ḫardalin ataynā bihā wakafā binā hasibeena And We set a just balance for the Day of Resurrection so that no soul is wronged in aught. Though it be of the weight of a grain of mustard seed, We bring it. And We suffice for reckoners.
048 walaqad ātaynā moosā wahāroona alfurqāna waḍiyāan waḏikran lilmuttaqeena And We verily gave Moses and Aaron the Criterion (of right and wrong) and a light and a Reminder for those who keep from evil,
049 allāḏeena yaḫšawna rabbahum bialġaybi wahum mina alssāʿati mušfiqoona Those who fear their Lord in secret and who dread the Hour (of doom).
050 wahaṯa ḏikrun mubārakun anzalnāhu afaantum lahu munkiroona This is a blessed Reminder that we have revealed: Will ye then reject it ?
051 walaqad ātaynā ibrāheema rušdahu min qablu wakunnā bihi ʿālimeena And We verily gave Abraham of old his proper course, and We were Aware of him,
052 iḏ qāla li-abeehi waqawmihi mā haṯihi alttamāṯeelu allātee antum lahā ʿākifoona When he said unto his father and his folk: What are these images unto which ye pay devotion ?
053 qāloo wajadnā ābāanā lahā ʿābideena They said: We found our fathers worshippers of them.
054 qāla laqad kuntum antum waābāokum fee ḍalālin mubeenin He said: Verily ye and your fathers were in plain error.
055 qāloo aji/tanā bialḥaqqi am anta mina allāʿibeena They said: Bringest thou unto us the truth, or art thou some jester ?
056 qāla bal rabbukum rabbu alssamāwāti waal-arḍi allāḏee faṭarahunna waanā ʿalā ṯalikum mina alššāhideena He said: Nay, but your Lord is the Lord of the heavens and the earth, Who created them; and I am of those who testify unto that.
057 wataallāhi laakeedanna aṣnāmakum baʿda an tuwalloo mudbireena And, by Allah, I shall circumvent your idols after ye have gone away and turned your backs.
058 fajaʿalahum juṯaṯan illā kabeeran lahum laʿallahum ilayhi yarjiʿoona Then he reduced them to fragments, all save the chief of them, that haply they might have recourse to it.
059 qāloo man faʿala haṯa bi-ālihatinā innahu lamina alṯṯālimeena They said: Who hath done this to our gods ? Surely it must be some evil- doer.
060 qāloo samiʿnā fatan yaḏkuruhum yuqālu lahu ibrāheemu They said: We heard a youth make mention of them, who is called Abraham.
061 qāloo fa/too bihi ʿalā aʿyuni alnnāsi laʿallahum yašhadoona They said: Then bring him (hither) before the people's eyes that they may testify.
062 qāloo aanta faʿalta haṯa bi-ālihatinā yā ibrāheemu They said: Is it thou who hast done this to our gods, O Abraham ?
063 qāla bal faʿalahu kabeeruhum haṯa fais-aloohum in kānoo yanṭiqoona He said: But this, their chief hath done it. So question them, if they can speak.
064 farajaʿoo ilā anfusihim faqāloo innakum antumu alṯṯālimoona Then gathered they apart and said: Lo! ye yourselves are the wrong-doers.
065 thumma nukisoo ʿalā ruoosihim laqad ʿalimta mā hāolā-i yanṭiqoona And they were utterly confounded, and they said: Well thou knowest that these speak not.
066 qāla afataʿbudoona min dooni allāhi mā lā yanfaʿukum šay-an walā yaḍurrukum He said: Worship ye then instead of Allah that which cannot profit you at all, nor harm you ?
067 offin lakum walimā taʿbudoona min dooni allāhi afalā taʿqiloona Fie on you and all that ye worship instead of Allah! Have ye then no sense ?
068 qāloo ḥarriqoohu waonṣuroo ālihatakum in kuntum fāʿileena They cried: Burn him and stand by your gods, if ye will be doing.
069 qulnā yā nāru koonee bardan wasalāman ʿalā ibrāheema We said: O fire, be coolness and peace for Abraham,
070 waarādoo bihi kaydan fajaʿalnāhumu al-aḫsareena And they wished to set a snare for him, but We made them the greater losers.
071 wanajjaynāhu walooṭan ilā al-arḍi allātee bāraknā feehā lilʿālameena And We rescued him and Lot (and brought them) to the land which We have blessed for (all) peoples.
072 wawahabnā lahu ishaqa wayaʿqooba nāfilatan wakullan jaʿalnā saliḥeena And We bestowed upon him Isaac, and Jacob as a grandson. Each of them We made righteous.
073 wajaʿalnāhum a-immatan yahdoona bi-amrinā waawḥaynā ilayhim fiʿla alḫayrāti wa-iqāma alṣalāti wa-eetāa alzzakāti wakānoo lanā ʿābideena And We made them chiefs who guide by Our command, and We inspired in them the doing of good deeds and the right establishment of worship and the giving of alms, and they were worshippers of Us (alone).
074 walooṭan ātaynāhu ḥukman waʿilman wanajjaynāhu mina alqaryati allātee kānat taʿmalu alḫabā-iṯa innahum kānoo qawma saw-in fāsiqeena And unto Lot we gave judgment and knowledge, and We delivered him from the community that did abominations. Lo! they were folk of evil, lewd.
075 waadḫalnāhu fee raḥmatinā innahu mina alssaliḥeena And We brought him in unto Our mercy. Lo! he was of the righteous.
076 wanooḥan iḏ nādā min qablu faistajabnā lahu fanajjaynāhu waahlahu mina alkarbi alʿaḏeemi And Noah, when he cried of old, We heard his prayer and saved him and his household from the great affliction.
077 wanaṣarnāhu mina alqawmi allāḏeena kaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā innahum kānoo qawma saw-in faaġraqnāhum ajmaʿeena And delivered him from the people who denied Our revelations. Lo! they were folk of evil, therefor did We drown them all.
078 wadāwooda wasulaymāna iḏ yaḥkumāni fee alḥarṯi iḏ nafašat feehi ġanamu alqawmi wakunnā liḥukmihim šāhideena And David and Solomon, when they gave judgment concerning the field, when people's sheep had strayed and browsed therein by night; and We were witnesses to their judgment.
079 fafahhamnāhā sulaymāna wakullan ātaynā ḥukman waʿilman wasaḫḫarnā maʿa dāwooda aljibāla yusabbiḥna waalttayra wakunnā fāʿileena And We made Solomon to understand (the case); and unto each of them We gave judgment and knowledge. And we subdued the hills and the birds to hymn (His) praise along with David. We were the doers (thereof).
080 waʿallamnāhu ṣanʿata laboosin lakum lituhsinakum min ba/sikum fahal antum šākiroona And We taught him the art of making garments (of mail) to protect you in your daring. Are ye then thankful ?
081 walisulaymāna alrreeḥa ʿasifatan tajree bi-amrihi ilā al-arḍi allātee bāraknā feehā wakunnā bikulli šay-in ʿālimeena And unto Solomon (We subdued) the wind in its raging. It set by his command toward the land which We had blessed. And of everything We are Aware.
082 wamina alššayateeni man yaġooṣoona lahu wayaʿmaloona ʿamalan doona ṯalika wakunnā lahum hafiḏeena And of the evil ones (subdued We unto him) some who dived (for pearls) for him and did other work, and We were warders unto them.
083 waayyooba iḏ nādā rabbahu annee massaniya alḍurru waanta arḥamu alrrahimeena And Job, when he cried unto his Lord, (saying): Lo! adversity afflicteth me, and Thou art Most Merciful of all who show mercy.
084 faistajabnā lahu fakašafnā mā bihi min ḍurrin waātaynāhu ahlahu wamiṯlahum maʿahum raḥmatan min ʿindinā waḏikrā lilʿābideena Then We heard his prayer and removed that adversity from which he suffered, and We gave him his household (that he had lost) and the like thereof along with them, a mercy from Our store, and a remembrance for the worshippers;
085 wa-ismāʿeela wa-idreesa waṯa alkifli kullun mina alssabireena And (mention) Ishmael, and Idris, and Dhu'l-Kifl. All were of the steadfast.
086 waadḫalnāhum fee raḥmatinā innahum mina alssaliḥeena And We brought them in unto Our mercy. Lo! they are among the righteous.
087 waṯa alnnooni iḏ ḏahaba muġadiban faḏanna an lan naqdira ʿalayhi fanādā fee alṯṯulumāti an lā ilāha illā anta subhanaka innee kuntu mina alṯṯālimeena And (mention) Dhu'n-Nun, when he went off in anger and deemed that We had no power over him, but he cried out in the darkness, saying: There is no God save Thee. Be Thou Glorified! Lo! I have been a wrong-doer.
088 faistajabnā lahu wanajjaynāhu mina alġammi wakaṯalika nunjee almu/mineena Then we heard his prayer and saved him from the anguish. Thus we save believers.
089 wazakariyyā iḏ nādā rabbahu rabbi lā taḏarnee fardan waanta ḫayru alwāriṯeena And Zachariah, when he cried unto his Lord: My Lord! Leave me not childless, though Thou art the Best of inheritors.
090 faistajabnā lahu wawahabnā lahu yaḥyā waaṣlaḥnā lahu zawjahu innahum kānoo yusāriʿoona fee alḫayrāti wayadʿoonanā raġaban warahaban wakānoo lanā ḫāšiʿeena Then We heard his prayer, and bestowed upon him John, and adjusted his wife (to bear a child) for him. Lo! they used to vie one with the other in good deeds, and they cried unto Us in longing and in fear, and were submissive unto Us.
091 waallatee ahsanat farjahā fanafaḫnā feehā min rooḥinā wajaʿalnāhā waibnahā āyatan lilʿālameena And she who was chaste, therefor We breathed into her (something) of Our Spirit and made her and her son a token for (all) peoples.
092 inna haṯihi ommatukum ommatan wahidatan waanā rabbukum faoʿbudooni Lo! this, your religion, is one religion, and I am your Lord, so worship Me.
093 wataqattaʿoo amrahum baynahum kullun ilaynā rājiʿoona And they have broken their religion (into fragments) among them, (yet) all are returning unto Us.
094 faman yaʿmal mina alssalihati wahuwa mu/minun falā kufrāna lisaʿyihi wa-innā lahu kātiboona Then whoso doeth some good works and is a believer, there will be no rejection of his effort. Lo! We record (it) for him.
095 waḥarāmun ʿalā qaryatin ahlaknāhā annahum lā yarjiʿoona And there is a ban upon any community which We have destroyed: that they shall not return.
096 [romanization missing] Until, when Gog and Magog are let loose, and they hasten out of every mound,
097 waiqtaraba alwaʿdu alḥaqqu fa-iṯa hiya šāḫiṣatun absaru allāḏeena kafaroo yā waylanā qad kunnā fee ġaflatin min haṯa bal kunnā ḏālimeena And the True Promise draweth nigh; then behold them, staring wide (in terror), the eyes of those who disbelieve! (They say): Alas for us! We (lived) in forgetfulness of this. Ah, but we were wrong-doers!
098 innakum wamā taʿbudoona min dooni allāhi ḥaṣabu jahannama antum lahā wāridoona Lo! ye (idolaters) and that which ye worship beside Allah are fuel of hell. Thereunto ye will come.
099 law kāna hāolā-i ālihatan mā waradoohā wakullun feehā ḫālidoona If these had been gods they would not have come thither, but all will abide therein.
100 lahum feehā zafeerun wahum feehā lā yasmaʿoona Therein wailing is their portion, and therein they hear not.
101 inna allāḏeena sabaqat lahum minnā alḥusnā olā-ika ʿanha mubʿadoona Lo! those unto whom kindness hath gone forth before from Us, they will be far removed from thence.
102 lā yasmaʿoona ḥaseesahā wahum fee mā ištahat anfusuhum ḫālidoona They will not hear the slightest sound thereof, while they abide in that which their souls desire.
103 lā yaḥzunuhumu alfazaʿu al-akbaru watatalaqqāhumu almalā-ikatu haṯa yawmukumu allāḏee kuntum tooʿadoona The Supreme Horror will not grieve them, and the angels will welcome them, (saying): This is your Day which ye were promised;
104 yawma naṭwee alssamāa kaṭayyi alssijlli lilkutubi kamā bada/nā awwala ḫalqin nuʿeeduhu waʿdan ʿalaynā innā kunnā fāʿileena The Day when We shall roll up the heavens as a recorder rolleth up a written scroll. As We began the first creation, We shall repeat it. (It is) a promise (binding) upon Us. Lo! We are to perform it.
105 walaqad katabnā fee alzzaboori min baʿdi alṯṯikri anna al-arḍa yariṯuhā ʿibādiya alssaliḥoona And verily we have written in the Scripture, after the Reminder: My righteous slaves will inherit the earth:
106 inna fee haṯa labalāġan liqawmin ʿābideena Lo! there is a plain statement for folk who are devout.
107 wamā arsalnāka illā raḥmatan lilʿālameena We sent thee not save as a mercy for the peoples.
108 qul innamā yooha ilayya annamā ilāhukum ilāhun wahidun fahal antum muslimoona Say: It is only inspired in me that your God is One God. Will ye then surrender (unto Him) ?
109 fa-in tawallaw faqul aṯantukum ʿalā sawā-in wa-in adree aqareebun am baʿeedun mā tooʿadoona But if they are averse, then say: I have warned you all alike, although I know not whether nigh or far is that which ye are promised.
110 innahu yaʿlamu aljahra mina alqawli wayaʿlamu mā taktumoona Lo! He knoweth that which is said openly, and that which ye conceal.
111 wa-in adree laʿallahu fitnatun lakum wamatāʿun ilā ḥeenin And I know not but that this may be a trial for you, and enjoyment for a while.
