51. adh-Dhariyat: The Scatterers

001 waalṯṯariyāti ḏarwān By those that winnow with a winnowing
002 faalhamilāti wiqrān And those that bear the burden (of the rain)
003 faaljāriyāti yusrān And those that glide with ease (upon the sea)
004 faalmuqassimāti amrān And those who distribute (blessings) by command,
005 innamā tooʿadoona lasadiqun Lo! that wherewith ye are threatened is indeed true,
006 wa-inna alddeena lawāqiʿun And lo! the judgment will indeed befall.
007 waalssamā-i ṯati alḥubuki By the heaven full of paths,
008 innakum lafee qawlin muḫtalifin Lo! ye, forsooth, are of various opinion (concerning the truth).
009 yu/faku ʿanhu man ofika He is made to turn away from it who is (himself) averse.
010 qutila alḫarrasoona Accursed be the conjecturers
011 allāḏeena hum fee ġamratin sāhoona Who are careless in an abyss!
012 yas-aloona ayyāna yawmu alddeeni They ask: When is the Day of Judgment ?
013 yawma hum ʿalā alnnāri yuftanoona (It is) the day when they will be tormented at the Fire,
014 [romanization missing] (And it will be said unto them): Taste your torment (which ye inflicted). This is what ye sought to hasten.
015 inna almuttaqeena fee jannātin waʿuyoonin Lo! those who keep from evil will dwell amid gardens and watersprings,
016 [romanization missing] Taking that which their Lord giveth them; for lo! aforetime they were doers of good;
017 kānoo qaleelan mina allāyli mā yahjaʿoona They used to sleep but little of the night,
018 wabial-ashari hum yastaġfiroona And ere the dawning of each day would seek forgiveness,
019 wafee amwālihim ḥaqqun lilssā-ili waalmaḥroomi And in their wealth the beggar and the outcast had due share.
020 wafee al-arḍi āyātun lilmooqineena And in the earth are portents for those whose faith is sure.
021 wafee anfusikum afalā tubṣiroona And (also) in yourselves. Can ye then not see ?
022 wafee alssamā-i rizqukum wamā tooʿadoona And in the heaven is your providence and that which ye are promised;
023 fawarabbi alssamā-i waal-arḍi innahu laḥaqqun miṯla mā annakum tanṭiqoona And by the Lord of the heavens and the earth, it is the truth, even as (it is true) that ye speak.
024 hal atāka ḥadeeṯu ḍayfi ibrāheema almukrameena Hath the story of Abraham's honoured guests reached thee (O Muhammad) ?
025 iḏ daḫaloo ʿalayhi faqāloo salāman qāla salāmun qawmun munkaroona When they came in unto him and said: Peace! he answered, Peace! (and thought): Folk unknown (to me).
026 farāġa ilā ahlihi fajāa biʿijlin sameenin Then he went apart unto his housefolk so that they brought a fatted calf;
027 faqarrabahu ilayhim qāla alā ta/kuloona And he set it before them, saying: Will ye not eat ?
028 faawjasa minhum ḫeefatan qāloo lā taḫaf wabaššaroohu biġulāmin ʿaleemin Then he conceived a fear of them. They said: Fear not! and gave him tidings of (the birth of) a wise son.
029 faaqbalati imraatuhu fee ṣarratin faṣakkat wajhahā waqālat ʿajoozun ʿaqeemun Then his wife came forward, making moan, and smote her face, and cried: A barren old woman!
030 qāloo kaṯaliki qāla rabbuki innahu huwa alḥakeemu alʿaleemu They said: Even so saith thy Lord. Lo! He is the Wise, the Knower.
031 qāla famā ḫaṭbukum ayyuhā almursaloona (Abraham) said: And (afterward) what is your errand, O ye sent (from Allah) ?
032 qāloo innā orsilnā ilā qawmin mujrimeena They said: Lo! we are sent unto a guilty folk,
033 linursila ʿalayhim ḥijāratan min ṭeenin That we may send upon them stones of clay,
034 musawwamatan ʿinda rabbika lilmusrifeena Marked by thy Lord for (the destruction of) the wanton.
035 faaḫrajnā man kāna feehā mina almu/mineena Then we brought forth such believers as were there.
036 famā wajadnā feehā ġayra baytin mina almuslimeena But We found there but one house of those surrendered (to Allah).
037 wataraknā feehā āyatan lillaḏeena yaḫāfoona alʿaṯaba al-aleema And We left behind therein a portent for those who fear a painful doom.
038 wafee moosā iḏ arsalnāhu ilā firʿawna bisultanin mubeenin And in Moses (too, there is a portent) when We sent him unto Pharaoh with clear warrant,
039 fatawallā biruknihi waqāla sahirun aw majnoonun But he withdrew (confiding) in his might, and said: A wizard or a madman.
040 faaḫaḏnāhu wajunoodahu fanabaḏnāhum fee alyammi wahuwa muleemun So We seized him and his hosts and flung them in the sea, for he was reprobate.
041 wafee ʿādin iḏ arsalnā ʿalayhimu alrreeḥa alʿaqeema And in (the tribe of) A'ad (there is a portent) when we sent the fatal wind against them.
042 mā taḏaru min šay-in atat ʿalayhi illā jaʿalat-hu kaalrrameemi It spared naught that it reached, but made it (all) as dust.
043 wafee ṯamooda iḏ qeela lahum tamattaʿoo ḥattā ḥeenin And in (the tribe of) Thamud (there is a portent) when it was told them: Take your ease awhile.
044 faʿataw ʿan amri rabbihim faaḫaḏat-humu alssaʿiqatu wahum yanḏuroona But they rebelled against their Lord's decree, and so the thunderbolt overtook them even while they gazed;
045 famā istataʿoo min qiyāmin wamā kānoo muntaṣireena And they were unable to rise up, nor could they help themselves.
046 waqawma nooḥin min qablu innahum kānoo qawman fāsiqeena And the folk of Noah aforetime. Lo! they were licentious folk.
047 waalssamāa banaynāhā bi-aydin wa-innā lamoosiʿoona We have built the heaven with might, and We it is Who make the vast extent (thereof).
048 waal-arḍa farašnāhā faniʿma almāhidoona And the earth have We laid out, how gracious is the Spreader (thereof)!
049 wamin kulli šay-in ḫalaqnā zawjayni laʿallakum taḏakkaroona And all things We have created by pairs, that haply ye may reflect.
050 fafirroo ilā allāhi innee lakum minhu naḏeerun mubeenun Therefor flee unto Allah; lo! I am a plain warner unto you from him.
051 walā tajʿaloo maʿa allāhi ilāhan āḫara innee lakum minhu naḏeerun mubeenun And set not any other god along with Allah; lo! I am a plain warner unto you from Him.
052 kaṯalika mā atā allāḏeena min qablihim min rasoolin illā qāloo sahirun aw majnoonun Even so there came no messenger unto those before them but they said: A wizard or a madman!
053 atawasaw bihi bal hum qawmun taġoona Have they handed down (the saying) as an heirloom one unto another ? Nay, but they are froward folk.
054 fatawalla ʿanhum famā anta bimaloomin So withdraw from them (O Muhammad), for thou art in no wise blameworthy,
055 waḏakkir fa-inna alṯṯikrā tanfaʿu almu/mineena And warn, for warning profiteth believers.
056 wamā ḫalaqtu aljinna waal-insa illā liyaʿbudooni I created the jinn and humankind only that they might worship Me.
057 mā oreedu minhum min rizqin wamā oreedu an yuṭʿimooni I seek no livelihood from them, nor do I ask that they should feed Me.
058 inna allāha huwa alrrazzāqu ḏoo alquwwati almateenu Lo! Allah! He it is that giveth livelihood, the Lord of unbreakable might.
059 fa-inna lillaḏeena ḏalamoo ḏanooban miṯla ḏanoobi aṣ-habihim falā yastaʿjiloona And lo! for those who (now) do wrong there is an evil day like unto the evil day (which came for) their likes (of old); so let them not ask Me to hasten on (that day).
60 
fawaylun lillaḏeena kafaroo min yawmihimu allāḏee yooʿadoona
And woe unto those who disbelieve, from (that) their day which they are promised.
