51. adh-Dhariyat: The Scatterers

1 
waalṯṯariyāti ḏarwān
By those that winnow with a winnowing

2 
faalhamilāti wiqrān
And those that bear the burden (of the rain)

3 
faaljāriyāti yusrān
And those that glide with ease (upon the sea)

4 
faalmuqassimāti amrān
And those who distribute (blessings) by command,

5 
innamā tooʿadoona lasadiqun
Lo! that wherewith ye are threatened is indeed true,

6 
wa-inna alddeena lawāqiʿun
And lo! the judgment will indeed befall.

7 
waalssamā-i ṯati alḥubuki
By the heaven full of paths,

8 
innakum lafee qawlin muḫtalifin
Lo! ye, forsooth, are of various opinion (concerning the truth).

9 
yu/faku ʿanhu man ofika
He is made to turn away from it who is (himself) averse.

10 
qutila alḫarrasoona
Accursed be the conjecturers

11 
allāḏeena hum fee ġamratin sāhoona
Who are careless in an abyss!

12 
yas-aloona ayyāna yawmu alddeeni
They ask: When is the Day of Judgment ?

13 
yawma hum ʿalā alnnāri yuftanoona
(It is) the day when they will be tormented at the Fire,

14 (And it will be said unto them): Taste your torment (which ye inflicted). This is what ye sought to hasten.

15 
inna almuttaqeena fee jannātin waʿuyoonin
Lo! those who keep from evil will dwell amid gardens and watersprings,

16 Taking that which their Lord giveth them; for lo! aforetime they were doers of good;

17 
kānoo qaleelan mina allāyli mā yahjaʿoona
They used to sleep but little of the night,

18 
wabial-ashari hum yastaġfiroona
And ere the dawning of each day would seek forgiveness,

19 
wafee amwālihim ḥaqqun lilssā-ili waalmaḥroomi
And in their wealth the beggar and the outcast had due share.

20 
wafee al-arḍi āyātun lilmooqineena
And in the earth are portents for those whose faith is sure.

21 
wafee anfusikum afalā tubṣiroona
And (also) in yourselves. Can ye then not see ?

22 
wafee alssamā-i rizqukum wamā tooʿadoona
And in the heaven is your providence and that which ye are promised;

23 
fawarabbi alssamā-i waal-arḍi innahu laḥaqqun miṯla mā annakum tanṭiqoona
And by the Lord of the heavens and the earth, it is the truth, even as (it is true) that ye speak.

24 
hal atāka ḥadeeṯu ḍayfi ibrāheema almukrameena
Hath the story of Abraham's honoured guests reached thee (O Muhammad) ?

25 
iḏ daḫaloo ʿalayhi faqāloo salāman qāla salāmun qawmun munkaroona
When they came in unto him and said: Peace! he answered, Peace! (and thought): Folk unknown (to me).

26 
farāġa ilā ahlihi fajāa biʿijlin sameenin
Then he went apart unto his housefolk so that they brought a fatted calf;

27 
faqarrabahu ilayhim qāla alā ta/kuloona
And he set it before them, saying: Will ye not eat ?

28 
faawjasa minhum ḫeefatan qāloo lā taḫaf wabaššaroohu biġulāmin ʿaleemin
Then he conceived a fear of them. They said: Fear not! and gave him tidings of (the birth of) a wise son.

29 
faaqbalati imraatuhu fee ṣarratin faṣakkat wajhahā waqālat ʿajoozun ʿaqeemun
Then his wife came forward, making moan, and smote her face, and cried: A barren old woman!

30 
qāloo kaṯaliki qāla rabbuki innahu huwa alḥakeemu alʿaleemu
They said: Even so saith thy Lord. Lo! He is the Wise, the Knower.

31 
qāla famā ḫaṭbukum ayyuhā almursaloona
(Abraham) said: And (afterward) what is your errand, O ye sent (from Allah) ?

32 
qāloo innā orsilnā ilā qawmin mujrimeena
They said: Lo! we are sent unto a guilty folk,

33 
linursila ʿalayhim ḥijāratan min ṭeenin
That we may send upon them stones of clay,

34 
musawwamatan ʿinda rabbika lilmusrifeena
Marked by thy Lord for (the destruction of) the wanton.

35 
faaḫrajnā man kāna feehā mina almu/mineena
Then we brought forth such believers as were there.

36 
famā wajadnā feehā ġayra baytin mina almuslimeena
But We found there but one house of those surrendered (to Allah).

37 
wataraknā feehā āyatan lillaḏeena yaḫāfoona alʿaṯaba al-aleema
And We left behind therein a portent for those who fear a painful doom.

38 
wafee moosā iḏ arsalnāhu ilā firʿawna bisultanin mubeenin
And in Moses (too, there is a portent) when We sent him unto Pharaoh with clear warrant,

39 
fatawallā biruknihi waqāla sahirun aw majnoonun
But he withdrew (confiding) in his might, and said: A wizard or a madman.

40 
faaḫaḏnāhu wajunoodahu fanabaḏnāhum fee alyammi wahuwa muleemun
So We seized him and his hosts and flung them in the sea, for he was reprobate.

41 
wafee ʿādin iḏ arsalnā ʿalayhimu alrreeḥa alʿaqeema
And in (the tribe of) A'ad (there is a portent) when we sent the fatal wind against them.

42 
mā taḏaru min šay-in atat ʿalayhi illā jaʿalat-hu kaalrrameemi
It spared naught that it reached, but made it (all) as dust.

43 
wafee ṯamooda iḏ qeela lahum tamattaʿoo ḥattā ḥeenin
And in (the tribe of) Thamud (there is a portent) when it was told them: Take your ease awhile.

44 
faʿataw ʿan amri rabbihim faaḫaḏat-humu alssaʿiqatu wahum yanḏuroona
But they rebelled against their Lord's decree, and so the thunderbolt overtook them even while they gazed;

45 
famā istataʿoo min qiyāmin wamā kānoo muntaṣireena
And they were unable to rise up, nor could they help themselves.

46 
waqawma nooḥin min qablu innahum kānoo qawman fāsiqeena
And the folk of Noah aforetime. Lo! they were licentious folk.

47 
waalssamāa banaynāhā bi-aydin wa-innā lamoosiʿoona
We have built the heaven with might, and We it is Who make the vast extent (thereof).

48 
waal-arḍa farašnāhā faniʿma almāhidoona
And the earth have We laid out, how gracious is the Spreader (thereof)!

49 
wamin kulli šay-in ḫalaqnā zawjayni laʿallakum taḏakkaroona
And all things We have created by pairs, that haply ye may reflect.

50 
fafirroo ilā allāhi innee lakum minhu naḏeerun mubeenun
Therefor flee unto Allah; lo! I am a plain warner unto you from him.

51 
walā tajʿaloo maʿa allāhi ilāhan āḫara innee lakum minhu naḏeerun mubeenun
And set not any other god along with Allah; lo! I am a plain warner unto you from Him.

52 
kaṯalika mā atā allāḏeena min qablihim min rasoolin illā qāloo sahirun aw majnoonun
Even so there came no messenger unto those before them but they said: A wizard or a madman!

53 
atawasaw bihi bal hum qawmun taġoona
Have they handed down (the saying) as an heirloom one unto another ? Nay, but they are froward folk.

54 
fatawalla ʿanhum famā anta bimaloomin
So withdraw from them (O Muhammad), for thou art in no wise blameworthy,

55 
waḏakkir fa-inna alṯṯikrā tanfaʿu almu/mineena
And warn, for warning profiteth believers.

56 
wamā ḫalaqtu aljinna waal-insa illā liyaʿbudooni
I created the jinn and humankind only that they might worship Me.

57 
mā oreedu minhum min rizqin wamā oreedu an yuṭʿimooni
I seek no livelihood from them, nor do I ask that they should feed Me.

58 
inna allāha huwa alrrazzāqu ḏoo alquwwati almateenu
Lo! Allah! He it is that giveth livelihood, the Lord of unbreakable might.

59 
fa-inna lillaḏeena ḏalamoo ḏanooban miṯla ḏanoobi aṣ-habihim falā yastaʿjiloona
And lo! for those who (now) do wrong there is an evil day like unto the evil day (which came for) their likes (of old); so let them not ask Me to hasten on (that day).

60 
fawaylun lillaḏeena kafaroo min yawmihimu allāḏee yooʿadoona
And woe unto those who disbelieve, from (that) their day which they are promised.
