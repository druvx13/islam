41. Ha Mim Sajdah: Revelations Well Expounded

001 [romanization missing] Ha. Mim.
002 tanzeelun mina alrraḥmāni alrraḥeemi A revelation from the Beneficent, the Merciful,
003 kitābun fuṣilat āyātuhu qur-ānan ʿarabiyyan liqawmin yaʿlamoona A Scripture whereof the verses are expounded, a Lecture in Arabic for people who have knowledge,
004 bašeeran wanaḏeeran faaʿraḍa akṯaruhum fahum lā yasmaʿoona Good tidings and a warning. But most of them turn away so that they hear not.
005 waqāloo quloobunā fee akinnatin mimmā tadʿoonā ilayhi wafee aṯaninā waqrun wamin bayninā wabaynika ḥijābun faiʿmal innanā ʿāmiloona And they say: Our hearts are protected from that unto which thou (O Muhammad) callest us, and in our ears there is a deafness, and between us and thee there is a veil. Act, then. Lo! we also shall be acting.
006 qul innamā anā bašarun miṯlukum yooha ilayya annamā ilāhukum ilāhun wahidun faistaqeemoo ilayhi waistaġfiroohu wawaylun lilmušrikeena Say (unto them O Muhammad): I am only a mortal like you. It is inspired in me that your God is One God, therefor take the straight path unto Him and seek forgiveness of Him. And woe unto the idolaters,
007 allāḏeena lā yu/toona alzzakāta wahum bial-āḫirati hum kāfiroona Who give not the poor-due, and who are disbelievers in the Hereafter.
008 inna allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati lahum ajrun ġayru mamnoonin Lo! as for those who believe and do good works, for them is a reward enduring.
009 qul a-innakum latakfuroona biallaḏee ḫalaqa al-arḍa fee yawmayni watajʿaloona lahu andādan ṯalika rabbu alʿālameena Say (O Muhammad, unto the idolaters): Disbelieve ye verily in Him Who created the earth in two Days, and ascribe ye unto Him rivals ? He (and none else) is the Lord of the Worlds.
010 wajaʿala feehā rawāsiya min fawqihā wabāraka feehā waqaddara feehā aqwātahā fee arbaʿati ayyāmin sawāan lilssā-ileena He placed therein firm hills rising above it, and blessed it and measured therein its sustenance in four Days, alike for (all) who ask;
011 thumma istawā ilā alssamā-i wahiya duḫānun faqāla lahā walil-arḍi i/tiyā ṭawʿan aw karhan qālatā ataynā ta-iʿeena Then turned He to the heaven when it was smoke, and said unto it and unto the earth: Come both of you, willingly or loth. They said: We come, obedient.
012 faqadahunna sabʿa samāwātin fee yawmayni waawha fee kulli samā-in amrahā wazayyannā alssamāa alddunyā bimasabeeḥa waḥifḏan ṯalika taqdeeru alʿazeezi alʿaleemi Then He ordained them seven heavens in two Days and inspired in each heaven its mandate; and We decked the nether heaven with lamps, and rendered it inviolable. That is the measuring of the Mighty, the Knower.
013 fa-in aʿraḍoo fuqul anḏartukum saʿiqatan miṯla saʿiqati ʿādin waṯamooda But if they turn away, then say: I warn you of a thunderbolt like the thunderbolt (which fell of old upon the tribes) of A'ad and Thamud;
014 iḏ jāat-humu alrrusulu min bayni aydeehim wamin ḫalfihim allā taʿbudoo illā allāha qāloo law šāa rabbunā laanzala malā-ikatan fa-innā bimā orsiltum bihi kāfiroona When their messengers came unto them from before them and behind them, saying: Worship none but Allah! they said: If our Lord had willed, He surely would have sent down angels (unto us), so lo! we are disbelievers in that wherewith ye have been sent.
015 faammā ʿādun faistakbaroo fee al-arḍi biġayri alḥaqqi waqāloo man ašaddu minnā quwwatan awa lam yaraw anna allāha allāḏee ḫalaqahum huwa ašaddu minhum quwwatan wakānoo bi-āyātinā yajḥadoona As for A'ad, they were arrogant in the land without right, and they said: Who is mightier than us in power ? Could they not see that Allah Who created them, He was mightier than them in power ? And they denied Our revelations.
016 faarsalnā ʿalayhim reeḥan ṣarṣaran fee ayyāmin naḥisātin linuḏeeqahum ʿaṯaba alḫizyi fee alḥayāti alddunyā walaʿaṯabu al-āḫirati aḫzā wahum lā yunṣaroona Therefor We let loose on them a raging wind in evil days, that We might make them taste the torment of disgrace in the life of the world. And verily the doom of the Hereafter will be more shameful, and they will not be helped.
017 waammā ṯamoodu fahadaynāhum faistaḥabboo alʿamā ʿalā alhudā faaḫaḏat-hum saʿiqatu alʿaṯabi alhooni bimā kānoo yaksiboona And as for Thamud, We gave them guidance, but they preferred blindness to the guidance, so the bolt of the doom of humiliation overtook them because of what they used to earn.
018 wanajjaynā allāḏeena āmanoo wakānoo yattaqoona And We delivered those who believed and used to keep their duty to Allah.
019 wayawma yuḥšaru aʿdāo allāhi ilā alnnāri fahum yoozaʿoona And (make mention of) the day when the enemies of Allah are gathered unto the Fire, they are driven on
020 [romanization missing] Till, when they reach it, their ears and their eyes and their skins testify against them as to what they used to do.
021 waqāloo lijuloodihim lima šahidtum ʿalaynā qāloo anṭaqanā allāhu allāḏee anṭaqa kulla šay-in wahuwa ḫalaqakum awwala marratin wa-ilayhi turjaʿoona And they say unto their skins: Why testify ye against us ? They say: Allah hath given us speech Who giveth speech to all things, and Who created you at the first, and unto Whom ye are returned.
022 wamā kuntum tastatiroona an yašhada ʿalaykum samʿukum walā absarukum walā juloodukum walākin ḏanantum anna allāha lā yaʿlamu kaṯeeran mimmā taʿmaloona Ye did not hide yourselves lest your ears and your eyes and your skins should testify against you, but ye deemed that Allah knew not much of what ye did.
023 waṯalikum ḏannukumu allāḏee ḏanantum birabbikum ardākum faaṣbaḥtum mina alḫāsireena That, your thought which ye did think about your Lord, hath ruined you; and ye find yourselves (this day) among the lost.
024 fa-in yaṣbiroo faalnnāru maṯwan lahum wa-in yastaʿtiboo famā hum mina almuʿtabeena And though they are resigned, yet the Fire is still their home; and if they ask for favour, yet they are not of those unto whom favour can be shown.
025 waqayyaḍnā lahum quranāa fazayyanoo lahum mā bayna aydeehim wamā ḫalfahum waḥaqqa ʿalayhimu alqawlu fee omamin qad ḫalat min qablihim mina aljinni waal-insi innahum kānoo ḫāsireena And We assigned them comrades (in the world), who made their present and their past fairseeming unto them. And the Word concerning nations of the jinn and humankind who passed away before them hath effect for them. Lo! they were ever losers.
026 waqāla allāḏeena kafaroo lā tasmaʿoo lihaṯa alqur-āni wailġaw feehi laʿallakum taġliboona Those who disbelieve say: Heed not this Qur'an, and drown the hearing of it; haply ye may conquer.
027 falanuḏeeqanna allāḏeena kafaroo ʿaṯaban šadeedan walanajziyannahum aswaa allāḏee kānoo yaʿmaloona But verily We shall cause those who disbelieve to taste an awful doom, and verily We shall requite them the worst of what they used to do.
028 [romanization missing] That is the reward of Allah's enemies: the Fire. Therein is their immortal home, payment forasmuch as they denied Our revelations.
029 waqāla allāḏeena kafaroo rabbanā arinā allāḏayni aḍallānā mina aljinni waal-insi najʿalhumā taḥta aqdāminā liyakoonā mina al-asfaleena And those who disbelieve will say: Our Lord! Show us those who beguiled us of the jinn and humankind. We will place them underneath our feet that they may be among the nethermost.
030 inna allāḏeena qāloo rabbunā allāhu ṯumma istaqāmoo tatanazzalu ʿalayhimu almalā-ikatu allā taḫāfoo walā taḥzanoo waabširoo bialjannati allātee kuntum tooʿadoona Lo! those who say: Our Lord is Allah, and afterward are upright, the angels descend upon them, saying: Fear not nor grieve, but hear good tidings of the paradise which ye are promised.
031 naḥnu awliyāokum fee alḥayāti alddunyā wafee al-āḫirati walakum feehā mā taštahee anfusukum walakum feehā mā taddaʿoona We are your protecting friends in the life of the world and in the Hereafter. There ye will have (all) that your souls desire, and there ye will have (all) for which ye pray.
032 nuzulan min ġafoorin raḥeemin A gift of welcome from One Forgiving, Merciful.
033 waman aḥsanu qawlan mimman daʿā ilā allāhi waʿamila saliḥan waqāla innanee mina almuslimeena And who is better in speech than him who prayeth unto his Lord and doeth right, and saith: Lo! I am of those who are muslims (surrender unto Him).
034 walā tastawee alḥasanatu walā alssayyi-atu idfaʿ biallatee hiya aḥsanu fa-iṯa allāḏee baynaka wabaynahu ʿadāwatun kaannahu waliyyun ḥameemun The good deed and the evil deed are not alike. Repel the evil deed with one which is better, then lo! he, between whom and thee there was enmity (will become) as though he was a bosom friend.
035 wamā yulaqqāhā illā allāḏeena ṣabaroo wamā yulaqqāhā illā ḏoo ḥaṯṯin ʿaḏeemin But none is granted it save those who are steadfast, and none is granted it save the owner of great happiness.
036 wa-immā yanzaġannaka mina alššaytani nazġun faistaʿiḏ biallāhi innahu huwa alssameeʿu alʿaleemu And if a whisper from the devil reach thee (O Muhammad) then seek refuge in Allah. Lo! He is the Hearer, the Knower.
037 wamin āyātihi allāylu waalnnahāru waalššamsu waalqamaru lā tasjudoo lilššamsi walā lilqamari waosjudoo lillāhi alaḏee ḫalaqahunna in kuntum iyyāhu taʿbudoona And of His portents are the night and the day and the sun and the moon. Adore not the sun not the moon; but adore Allah Who created them, if it is in truth Him Whom ye worship.
038 fa-ini istakbaroo faallaḏeena ʿinda rabbika yusabbiḥoona lahu biallayli waalnnahāri wahum lā yas-amoona But if they are too proud - still those who are with thy Lord glorify Him night and day, and tire not.
039 wamin āyātihi annaka tarā al-arḍa ḫāšiʿatan fa-iṯa anzalnā ʿalayhā almāa ihtazzat warabat inna allāḏee aḥyāhā lamuḥyee almawtā innahu ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerun And of His portents (is this): that thou seest the earth lowly, but when We send down water thereon it thrilleth and groweth. Lo! He Who quickeneth it is verily the Quickener of the Dead. Lo! He is Able to do all things.
040 inna allāḏeena yulḥidoona fee āyātinā lā yaḫfawna ʿalaynā afaman yulqā fee alnnāri ḫayrun amman ya/tee āminan yawma alqiyāmati iʿmaloo mā ši/tum innahu bimā taʿmaloona baṣeerun Lo! those who distort Our revelations are not hid from Us. Is he who is hurled into the Fire better, or he who cometh secure on the Day of Resurrection ? Do what ye will. Lo! He is Seer of what ye do.
041 inna allāḏeena kafaroo bialṯṯikri lammā jāahum wa-innahu lakitābun ʿazeezun Lo! those who disbelieve in the Reminder when it cometh unto them (are guilty), for lo! it is an unassailable Scripture.
042 lā ya/teehi albatilu min bayni yadayhi walā min ḫalfihi tanzeelun min ḥakeemin ḥameedin Falsehood cannot come at it from before it or from behind it. (It is) a revelation from the Wise, the Owner of Praise.
043 mā yuqālu laka illā mā qad qeela lilrrusuli min qablika inna rabbaka laḏoo maġfiratin waḏoo ʿiqābin aleemin Naught is said unto thee (Muhammad) save what was said unto the messengers before thee. Lo! thy Lord is owner of forgiveness, and owner (also) of dire punishment.
044 walaw jaʿalnāhu qur-ānan aʿjamiyyan laqāloo lawlā fuṣilat āyātuhu aaʿjamiyyun waʿarabiyyun qul huwa lillaḏeena āmanoo hudan wašifāon waallaḏeena lā yu/minoona fee aṯanihim waqrun wahuwa ʿalayhim ʿaman olā-ika yunādawna min makānin baʿeedin And if We had appointed it a Lecture in a foreign tongue they would assuredly have said: If only its verses were expounded (so that we might understand) ? What! A foreign tongue and an Arab ? - Say unto them (O Muhammad): For those who believe it is a guidance and a healing; and as for those who disbelieve, there is a deafness in their ears, and it is blindness for them. Such are called to from afar.
045 walaqad ātaynā moosā alkitāba faiḫtulifa feehi walawlā kalimatun sabaqat min rabbika laquḍiya baynahum wa-innahum lafee šakkin minhu mureebin And We verily gave Moses the Scripture, but there hath been dispute concerning it; and but for a Word that had already gone forth from thy Lord, it would ere now have been judged between them; but lo! they are in hopeless doubt concerning it.
046 man ʿamila saliḥan falinafsihi waman asāa faʿalayhā wamā rabbuka biḏallāmin lilʿabeedi Whoso doeth right it is for his soul, and whoso doeth wrong it is against it. And thy Lord is not at all a tyrant to His slaves.
047 ilayhi yuraddu ʿilmu alssāʿati wamā taḫruju min ṯamarātin min akmāmihā wamā taḥmilu min onṯā walā taḍaʿu illā biʿilmihi wayawma yunādeehim ayna šurakā-ee qāloo aṯannāka mā minnā min šaheedin Unto Him is referred (all) knowledge of the Hour. And no fruits burst forth from their sheaths, and no female carrieth or bringeth forth but with His knowledge. And on the day when He calleth unto them: Where are now My partners ? they will say: We confess unto Thee, not one of us is a witness (for them).
048 waḍalla ʿanhum mā kānoo yadʿoona min qablu waḏannoo mā lahum min maḥeeṣin And those to whom they used to cry of old have failed them, and they perceive they have no place of refuge.
049 lā yas-amu al-insānu min duʿā-i alḫayri wa-in massahu alššarru fayaoosun qanooṭun Man tireth not of praying for good, and if ill toucheth him, then he is disheartened, desperate.
050 wala-in aḏaqnāhu raḥmatan minnā min baʿdi ḍarrāa massat-hu layaqoolanna haṯa lee wamā aḏunnu alssāʿata qā-imatan wala-in rujiʿtu ilā rabbee inna lee ʿindahu lalḥusnā falanunabbi-anna allāḏeena kafaroo bimā ʿamiloo walanuḏeeqannahum min ʿaṯabin ġaleeḏin And verily, if We cause him to taste mercy after some hurt that hath touched him, he will say: This is my own; and I deem not that the Hour will ever rise, and if I am brought back to my Lord, I surely shall be better off with Him - But We verily shall tell those who disbelieve (all) that they did, and We verily shall make them taste hard punishment.
051 wa-iṯa anʿamnā ʿalā al-insāni aʿraḍa wanaā bijānibihi wa-iṯa massahu alššarru faḏoo duʿā-in ʿareeḍin When We show favour unto man, he withdraweth and turneth aside, but when ill toucheth him then he aboundeth in prayer.
052 qul araaytum in kāna min ʿindi allāhi ṯumma kafartum bihi man aḍallu mimman huwa fee šiqāqin baʿeedin Bethink you: If it is from Allah and ye reject it - Who is further astray than one who is at open feud (with Allah) ?
053 sanureehim āyātinā fee al-āfāqi wafee anfusihim ḥattā yatabayyana lahum annahu alḥaqqu awa lam yakfi birabbika annahu ʿalā kulli šay-in šaheedun We shall show them Our portents on the horizons and within themselves until it will be manifest unto them that it is the Truth. Doth not thy Lord suffice, since He is Witness over all things ?
54 alā innahum fee miryatin min liqā-i rabbihim alā innahu bikulli šay-in muḥeeṭun [Could not extract English for verse 54]
