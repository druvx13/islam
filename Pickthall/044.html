44. ad-Dukhan: The Evident Smoke

001 [romanization missing] Ha. Mim.
002 waalkitābi almubeeni By the Scripture that maketh plain
003 innā anzalnāhu fee laylatin mubārakatin innā kunnā munḏireena Lo! We revealed it on a blessed night - Lo! We are ever warning -
004 feehā yufraqu kullu amrin ḥakeemin Whereon every wise command is made clear
005 amran min ʿindinā innā kunnā mursileena As a command from Our presence - Lo! We are ever sending -
006 raḥmatan min rabbika innahu huwa alssameeʿu alʿaleemu A mercy from thy Lord. Lo! He, even He is the Hearer, the Knower,
007 rabbi alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wamā baynahumā in kuntum mooqineena Lord of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them, if ye would be sure.
008 lā ilāha illā huwa yuḥyee wayumeetu rabbukum warabbu ābā-ikumu al-awwaleena There is no God save Him. He quickeneth and giveth death; your Lord and Lord of your forefathers.
009 bal hum fee šakkin yalʿaboona Nay, but they play in doubt.
010 fairtaqib yawma ta/tee alssamāo biduḫānin mubeenin But watch thou (O Muhammad) for the day when the sky will produce visible smoke
011 yaġšā alnnāsa haṯa ʿaṯabun aleemun That will envelop the people. This will be a painful torment.
012 rabbanā ikšif ʿannā alʿaṯaba innā mu/minoona (Then they will say): Our Lord relieve us of the torment. Lo! we are believers.
013 annā lahumu alṯṯikrā waqad jāahum rasoolun mubeenun How can there be remembrance for them, when a messenger making plain (the Truth) had already come unto them,
014 thumma tawallaw ʿanhu waqāloo muʿallamun majnoonun And they had turned away from him and said: One taught (by others), a madman ?
015 innā kāšifoo alʿaṯabi qaleelan innakum ʿā-idoona Lo! We withdraw the torment a little. Lo! ye return (to disbelief).
016 yawma nabṭišu albaṭšata alkubrā innā muntaqimoona On the day when We shall seize them with the greater seizure, (then) in truth We shall punish.
017 walaqad fatannā qablahum qawma firʿawna wajāahum rasoolun kareemun And verily We tried before them Pharaoh's folk, when there came unto them a noble messenger,
018 an addoo ilayya ʿibāda allāhi innee lakum rasoolun ameenun Saying: Give up to me the slaves of Allah. Lo! I am a faithful messenger unto you.
019 waan lā taʿloo ʿalā allāhi innee āteekum bisultanin mubeenin And saying: Be not proud against Allah. Lo! I bring you a clear warrant.
020 wa-innee ʿuḏtu birabbee warabbikum an tarjumooni And lo! I have sought refuge in my Lord and your Lord lest ye stone me to death.
021 wa-in lam tu/minoo lee faiʿtazilooni And if ye put no faith in me, then let me go.
022 fadaʿā rabbahu anna hāolā-i qawmun mujrimoona And he cried unto his Lord, (saying): These are guilty folk.
023 faasri biʿibādee laylan innakum muttabaʿoona Then (his Lord commanded): Take away My slaves by night. Lo! ye will be followed,
024 waotruki albaḥra rahwan innahum jundun muġraqoona And leave the sea behind at rest, for lo! they are a drowned host.
025 kam tarakoo min jannātin waʿuyoonin How many were the gardens and the watersprings that they left behind,
026 wazurooʿin wamaqāmin kareemin And the cornlands and the goodly sites
027 wanaʿmatin kānoo feehā fākiheena And pleasant things wherein they took delight!
028 kaṯalika waawraṯnāhā qawman āḫareena Even so (it was), and We made it an inheritance for other folk;
029 famā bakat ʿalayhimu alssamāo waal-arḍu wamā kānoo munḏareena And the heaven and the earth wept not for them, nor were they reprieved.
030 [romanization missing] And We delivered the Children of Israel from the shameful doom;
031 [romanization missing] (We delivered them) from Pharaoh. Lo! he was a tyrant of the wanton ones.
032 [romanization missing] And We chose them, purposely, above (all) creatures.
033 [romanization missing] And We gave them portents wherein was a clear trial.
034 [romanization missing] Lo! these, forsooth, are saying:
035 [romanization missing] There is naught but our first death, and we shall not be raised again.
036 [romanization missing] Bring back our fathers, if ye speak the truth!
037 [romanization missing] Are they better, or the folk of Tubb'a and those before them ? We destroyed them, for surely they were guilty.
038 [romanization missing] And We created not the heavens and the earth, and all that is between them, in play.
039 [romanization missing] We created them not save with truth; but most of them know not.
040 [romanization missing] Assuredly the Day of Decision is the term for all of them,
041 [romanization missing] A day when friend can in naught avail friend, nor can they be helped,
042 [romanization missing] Save him on whom Allah hath mercy. Lo! He is the Mighty, the Merciful.
043 inna šajarata alzzaqqoomi Lo! the tree of Zaqqum,
044 [romanization missing] The food of the sinner!
045 kaalmuhli yaġlee fee albuṭooni Like molten brass, it seetheth in their bellies
046 kaġalyi alḥameemi As the seething of boiling water.
047 khuḏoohu faiʿtiloohu ilā sawā-i aljaḥeemi (And it will be said): Take him and drag him to the midst of hell,
048 thumma ṣubboo fawqa ra/sihi min ʿaṯabi alḥameemi Then pour upon his head the torment of boiling water.
049 [romanization missing] (Saying): Taste! Lo! thou wast forsooth the mighty, the noble!
050 inna haṯa mā kuntum bihi tamtaroona Lo! this is that whereof ye used to doubt.
051 inna almuttaqeena fee maqāmin ameenin Lo! those who kept their duty will be in a place secured.
052 fee jannātin waʿuyoonin Amid gardens and watersprings,
053 yalbasoona min sundusin wa-istabraqin mutaqābileena Attired in silk and silk embroidery, facing one another.
054 kaṯalika wazawwajnāhum biḥoorin ʿeenin Even so (it will be). And We shall wed them unto fair ones with wide, lovely eyes.
055 yadʿoona feehā bikulli fākihatin āmineena They call therein for every fruit in safety.
056 lā yaḏooqoona feehā almawta illā almawtata al-oolā wawaqāhum ʿaṯaba aljaḥeemi They taste not death therein, save the first death. And He hath saved them from the doom of hell,
057 faḍlan min rabbika ṯalika huwa alfawzu alʿaḏeemu A bounty from thy Lord. That is the supreme triumph.
058 fa-innamā yassarnāhu bilisānika laʿallahum yataḏakkaroona And We have made (this Scripture) easy in thy language only that they may heed.
59 
fairtaqib innahum murtaqiboona
Wait then (O Muhammad). Lo! they (too) are waiting.
