79. an-Nazi`at: Those Who Pull Out

001 waalnnāziʿāti ġarqān By those who drag forth to destruction,
002 waalnnāšitati naštan By the meteors rushing,
003 waalssābihati sabhan By the lone stars floating,
004 faalssābiqāti sabqān By the angels hastening,
005 faalmudabbirāti amrān And those who govern the event,
006 yawma tarjufu alrrājifatu On the day when the first trump resoundeth.
007 tatbaʿuhā alrrādifatu And the second followeth it,
008 quloobun yawma-iḏin wājifatun On that day hearts beat painfully
009 absaruhā ḫāšiʿatun While eyes are downcast
010 yaqooloona a-innā lamardoodoona fee alhafirati (Now) they are saying: Shall we really be restored to our first state
011 a-iṯa kunnā ʿiḏāman naḫiratan Even after we are crumbled bones ?
012 qāloo tilka iḏan karratun ḫāsiratun They say: Then that would be a vain proceeding.
013 fa-innamā hiya zajratun wahidatun Surely it will need but one shout,
014 fa-iṯa hum bialssāhirati And lo! they will be awakened.
015 hal atāka ḥadeeṯu moosā Hath there come unto thee the history of Moses ?
016 iḏ nādāhu rabbuhu bialwādi almuqaddasi ṭuwān How his Lord called him in the holy vale of Tuwa,
017 iḏhab ilā firʿawna innahu ṭaġā (Saying:) Go thou unto Pharaoh - Lo! he hath rebelled -
018 faqul hal laka ilā an tazakkā And say (unto him): Hast thou (will) to grow (in grace) ?
019 waahdiyaka ilā rabbika fataḫšā Then I will guide thee to thy Lord and thou shalt fear (Him).
020 faarāhu al-āyata alkubrā And he showed him the tremendous token.
021 fakaṯṯaba waʿasa But he denied and disobeyed,
022 thumma adbara yasʿā Then turned he away in haste,
023 faḥašara fanādā Then gathered he and summoned
024 faqāla anā rabbukumu al-aʿlā And proclaimed: "I (Pharaoh) am your Lord the Highest."
025 faaḫaḏahu allāhu nakāla al-āḫirati waal-oolā So Allah seized him (and made him) an example for the after (life) and for the former.
026 inna fee ṯalika laʿibratan liman yaḫšā Lo! herein is indeed a lesson for him who feareth.
027 aantum ašaddu ḫalqan ami alssamāo banāhā Are ye the harder to create, or is the heaven that He built ?
028 rafaʿa samkahā fasawwāhā He raised the height thereof and ordered it;
029 waaġṭaša laylahā waaḫraja ḍuhahā And He made dark the night thereof, and He brought forth the morn thereof.
030 waal-arḍa baʿda ṯalika dahahā And after that He spread the earth,
031 aḫraja minhā māahā wamarʿāhā And produced therefrom the water thereof and the pasture thereof,
032 waaljibāla arsāhā And He made fast the hills,
033 matāʿan lakum wali-anʿāmikum A provision for you and for your cattle.
034 fa-iṯa jāati alttammatu alkubrā But when the great disaster cometh,
035 yawma yataḏakkaru al-insānu mā saʿā The day when man will call to mind his (whole) endeavour,
036 waburrizati aljaḥeemu liman yarā And hell will stand forth visible to him who seeth,
037 faammā man ṭaġā Then, as for him who rebelled
038 waāṯara alḥayāta alddunyā And chose the life of the world,
039 fa-inna aljaḥeema hiya alma/wā Lo! hell will be his home.
040 waamā man ḫāfa maqāma rabbihi wanahā alnnafsa ʿani alhawā But as for him who feared to stand before his Lord and restrained his soul from lust,
041 fa-inna aljannata hiya alma/wā Lo! the Garden will be his home.
042 yas-aloonaka ʿani alssāʿati ayyāna mursāhā They ask thee of the Hour: when will it come to port ?
043 feema anta min ḏikrāhā Why (ask they) ? What hast thou to tell thereof ?
044 ilā rabbika muntahāhā Unto thy Lord belongeth (knowledge of) the term thereof.
045 innamā anta munḏiru man yaḫšāhā Thou art but a warner unto him who feareth it.
46 kaannahum yawma yarawnahā lam yalbaṯoo illā ʿašiyyatan aw ḍuhahā [Could not extract English for verse 46]
