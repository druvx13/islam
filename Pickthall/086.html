86. at-Tariq: The Night-Comer

001 waalssamā-i waalttariqi By the heaven and the Morning Star
002 wamā adrāka mā alttariqu - Ah, what will tell thee what the Morning Star is!
003 alnnajmu alṯṯāqibu - The piercing Star!
004 in kullu nafsin lammā ʿalayhā hafiḏun No human soul but hath a guardian over it.
005 falyanḏuri al-insānu mimma ḫuliqa So let man consider from what he is created.
006 khuliqa min mā-in dāfiqin He is created from a gushing fluid
007 yaḫruju min bayni alṣulbi waalttarā-ibi That issued from between the loins and ribs.
008 innahu ʿalā rajʿihi laqādirun Lo! He verily is Able to return him (unto life)
009 yawma tublā alssarā-iru On the day when hidden thoughts shall be searched out.
010 famā lahu min quwwatin walā nasirin Then will he have no might nor any helper.
011 waalssamā-i ṯati alrrajʿi By the heaven which giveth the returning rain,
012 waal-arḍi ṯati alṣadʿi And the earth which splitteth (with the growth of trees and plants)
013 innahu laqawlun faṣlun Lo! this (Qur'an) is a conclusive word,
014 wamā huwa bialhazli It is no pleasantry.
015 innahum yakeedoona kaydān Lo! they plot a plot (against thee, O Muhammad)
016 waakeedu kaydān And I plot a plot (against them).
17 
famahhili alkāfireena amhilhum ruwaydān
So give a respite to the disbelievers. Deal thou gently with them for a while.
