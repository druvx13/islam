23. al-Mu'minun: The Believers

001 qad aflaḥa almu/minoona Successful indeed are the believers
002 allāḏeena hum fee ṣalātihim ḫāšiʿoona Who are humble in their prayers,
003 waallaḏeena hum ʿani allāġwi muʿriḍoona And who shun vain conversation,
004 waallaḏeena hum lilzzakāti fāʿiloona And who are payers of the poor-due;
005 waallaḏeena hum lifuroojihim hafiḏoona And who guard their modesty -
006 illā ʿalā azwājihim aw mā malakat aymānuhum fa-innahum ġayru maloomeena Save from their wives or the (slaves) that their right hands possess, for then they are not blameworthy,
007 famani ibtaġā warāa ṯalika faolā-ika humu alʿādoona But whoso craveth beyond that, such are transgressors -
008 waallaḏeena hum li-amānātihim waʿahdihim rāʿoona And who are shepherds of their pledge and their covenant,
009 waallaḏeena hum ʿalā ṣalawātihim yuhafiḏoona And who pay heed to their prayers.
010 olā-ika humu alwāriṯoona These are the heirs
011 allāḏeena yariṯoona alfirdawsa hum feehā ḫālidoona Who will inherit paradise. There they will abide.
012 walaqad ḫalaqnā al-insāna min sulālatin min ṭeenin Verily We created man from a product of wet earth;
013 thumma jaʿalnāhu nuṭfatan fee qarārin makeenin Then placed him as a drop (of seed) in a safe lodging;
014 thumma ḫalaqnā alnnuṭfata ʿalaqatan faḫalaqnā alʿalaqata muḍġatan faḫalaqnā almuḍġata ʿiḏāman fakasawnā alʿiḏāma laḥman ṯumma anša/nāhu ḫalqan āḫara fatabāraka allāhu aḥsanu alḫāliqeena Then fashioned We the drop a clot, then fashioned We the clot a little lump, then fashioned We the little lump bones, then clothed the bones with flesh, and then produced it as another creation. So blessed be Allah, the Best of creators!
015 thumma innakum baʿda ṯalika lamayyitoona Then lo! after that ye surely die.
016 thumma innakum yawma alqiyāmati tubʿaṯoona Then lo! on the Day of Resurrection ye are raised (again).
017 walaqad ḫalaqnā fawqakum sabʿa ṭarā-iqa wamā kunnā ʿani alḫalqi ġāfileena And We have created above you seven paths, and We are never unmindful of creation.
018 waanzalnā mina alssamā-i māan biqadarin faaskannāhu fee al-arḍi wa-innā ʿalā ḏahābin bihi laqādiroona And we send down from the sky water in measure, and We give it lodging in the earth, and lo! We are Able to withdraw it.
019 faanša/nā lakum bihi jannātin min naḫeelin waaʿnābin lakum feehā fawākihu kaṯeeratun waminhā ta/kuloona Then We produce for you therewith gardens of date-palms and grapes, wherein is much fruit for you and whereof ye eat;
020 wašajaratan taḫruju min ṭoori saynāa tanbutu bialdduhni waṣibġin lilākileena And a tree that springeth forth from Mount Sinai that groweth oil and relish for the eaters.
021 wa-inna lakum fee al-anʿāmi laʿibratan nusqeekum mimmā fee buṭoonihā walakum feehā manāfiʿu kaṯeeratun waminhā ta/kuloona And lo! in the cattle there is verily a lesson for you. We give you to drink of that which is in their bellies, and many uses have ye in them, and of them do ye eat;
022 waʿalayhā waʿalā alfulki tuḥmaloona And on them and on the ship ye are carried.
023 walaqad arsalnā nooḥan ilā qawmihi faqāla yā qawmi oʿbudoo allāha mā lakum min ilāhin ġayruhu afalā tattaqoona And We verily sent Noah unto his folk, and he said: O my people! Serve Allah. Ye have no other God save Him. Will ye not ward off (evil) ?
024 faqāla almalao allāḏeena kafaroo min qawmihi mā haṯa illā bašarun miṯlukum yureedu an yatafaḍala ʿalaykum walaw šāa allāhu laanzala malā-ikatan mā samiʿnā bihaṯa fee ābā-inā al-awwaleena But the chieftains of his folk, who disbelieved, said: This is only a mortal like you who would make himself superior to you. Had Allah willed, He surely could have sent down angels. We heard not of this in the case of our fathers of old.
025 in huwa illā rajulun bihi jinnatun fatarabbaṣoo bihi ḥattā ḥeenin He is only a man in whom is a madness, so watch him for a while.
026 qāla rabbi onṣurnee bimā kaṯṯabooni He said: My Lord! Help me because they deny me.
027 faawḥaynā ilayhi ani iṣnaʿi alfulka bi-aʿyuninā wawaḥyinā fa-iṯa jāa amrunā wafāra alttannooru faosluk feehā min kullin zawjayni iṯnayni waahlaka illā man sabaqa ʿalayhi alqawlu minhum walā tuḫatibnee fee allāḏeena ḏalamoo innahum muġraqoona Then We inspired in him, saying: Make the ship under Our eyes and Our inspiration. Then, when Our command cometh and the oven gusheth water, introduce therein of every (kind) two spouses, and thy household save him thereof against whom the Word hath already gone forth. And plead not with Me on behalf of those who have done wrong. Lo! they will be drowned.
028 fa-iṯa istawayta anta waman maʿaka ʿalā alfulki faquli alḥamdu lillāhi allāḏee najjānā mina alqawmi alṯṯālimeena And when thou art on board the ship, thou and whoso is with thee, then say: Praise be to Allah Who hath saved us from the wrongdoing folk!
029 waqul rabbi anzilnee munzalan mubārakan waanta ḫayru almunzileena And say: My Lord! Cause me to land at a blessed landing-place, for Thou art Best of all who bring to land.
030 inna fee ṯalika laāyātin wa-in kunnā lamubtaleena Lo! herein verily are portents, for lo! We are ever putting (mankind) to the test.
031 thumma anša/nā min baʿdihim qarnan āḫareena Then, after them, We brought forth another generation;
032 faarsalnā feehim rasoolan minhum ani oʿbudoo allāha mā lakum min ilāhin ġayruhu afalā tattaqoona And we sent among them a messenger of their own, saying: Serve Allah, Ye have no other God save Him. Will ye not ward off (evil) ?
033 waqāla almalao min qawmihi allāḏeena kafaroo wakaṯṯaboo biliqā-i al-āḫirati waatrafnāhum fee alḥayāti alddunyā mā haṯa illā bašarun miṯlukum ya/kulu mimmā ta/kuloona minhu wayašrabu mimmā tašraboona And the chieftains of his folk, who disbelieved and denied the meeting of the Hereafter, and whom We had made soft in the life of the world, said: This is only a mortal like you, who eateth of that whereof ye eat and drinketh of that ye drink.
034 wala-in aṭaʿtum bašaran miṯlakum innakum iḏan laḫāsiroona If ye were to obey a mortal like yourselves, then, lo! ye surely would be losers.
035 ayaʿidukum annakum iṯa mittum wakuntum turāban waʿiḏāman annakum muḫrajoona Doth he promise you that you, when ye are dead and have become dust and bones, will (again) be brought forth ?
036 hayhāta hayhāta limā tooʿadoona Begone, begone, with that which ye are promised!
037 in hiya illā ḥayātunā alddunyā namootu wanaḥyā wamā naḥnu bimabʿooṯeena There is naught but our life of the world; we die and we live, and we shall not be raised (again).
038 in huwa illā rajulun iftarā ʿalā allāhi kaḏiban wamā naḥnu lahu bimu/mineena He is only a man who hath invented a lie about Allah. We are not going to put faith in him.
039 qāla rabbi onṣurnee bimā kaṯṯabooni He said: My Lord! Help me because they deny me.
040 qāla ʿammā qaleelin layuṣbiḥunna nādimeena He said: In a little while they surely will become repentant.
041 faaḫaḏat-humu alṣayḥatu bialḥaqqi fajaʿalnāhum ġuṯāan fabuʿdan lilqawmi alṯṯālimeena So the (Awful) Cry overtook them rightfully, and We made them like as wreckage (that a torrent hurleth). A far removal for wrongdoing folk!
042 thumma anša/nā min baʿdihim quroonan āḫareena Then after them We brought forth other generations.
043 mā tasbiqu min ommatin ajalahā wamā yasta/ḫiroona No nation can outstrip its term, nor yet postpone it.
044 thumma arsalnā rusulanā tatrā kulla mā jāa ommatan rasooluhā kaṯṯaboohu faatbaʿnā baʿḍahum baʿḍan wajaʿalnāhum ahadeeṯa fabuʿdan liqawmin lā yu/minoona Then We sent our messengers one after another. Whenever its messenger came unto a nation they denied him; so We caused them to follow one another (to disaster) and We made them bywords. A far removal for folk who believe not!
045 thumma arsalnā moosā waaḫāhu hāroona bi-āyātinā wasultanin mubeenin Then We sent Moses and his brother Aaron with Our tokens and a clear warrant
046 ilā firʿawna wamala-ihi faistakbaroo wakānoo qawman ʿāleena Unto Pharaoh and his chiefs, but they scorned (them) and they were despotic folk.
047 faqāloo anu/minu libašarayni miṯlinā waqawmuhumā lanā ʿābidoona And they said: Shall we put faith in two mortals like ourselves, and whose folk are servile unto us ?
048 fakaṯṯaboohumā fakānoo mina almuhlakeena So they denied them, and became of those who were destroyed.
049 walaqad ātaynā moosā alkitāba laʿallahum yahtadoona And We verily gave Moses the Scripture, that haply they might go aright.
050 wajaʿalnā ibna maryama waommahu āyatan waāwaynāhumā ilā rabwatin ṯati qarārin wamaʿeenin And We made the son of Mary and his mother a portent, and We gave them refuge on a height, a place of flocks and watersprings.
051 yā ayyuhā alrrusulu kuloo mina alttayyibāti waiʿmaloo saliḥan innee bimā taʿmaloona ʿaleemun O ye messengers! Eat of the good things, and do right. Lo! I am Aware of what ye do.
052 wa-inna haṯihi ommatukum ommatan wahidatan waanā rabbukum faittaqooni And lo! this your religion is one religion and I am your Lord, so keep your duty unto Me.
053 fataqattaʿoo amrahum baynahum zuburan kullu ḥizbin bimā ladayhim fariḥoona But they (mankind) have broken their religion among them into sects, each group rejoicing in its tenets.
054 faḏarhum fee ġamratihim ḥattā ḥeenin So leave them in their error till a time.
055 ayaḥsaboona annamā numidduhum bihi min mālin wabaneena Think they that in the wealth and sons wherewith We provide them
056 nusāriʿu lahum fee alḫayrāti bal lā yašʿuroona We hasten unto them with good things ? Nay, but they perceive not.
057 inna allāḏeena hum min ḫašyati rabbihim mušfiqoona Lo! those who go in awe for fear of their Lord.
058 waallaḏeena hum bi-āyāti rabbihim yu/minoona And those who believe in the revelations of their Lord,
059 waallaḏeena hum birabbihim lā yušrikoona And those who ascribe not partners unto their Lord,
060 waallaḏeena yu/toona mā ātaw waquloobuhum wajilatun annahum ilā rabbihim rājiʿoona And those who give that which they give with hearts afraid because they are about to return unto their Lord,
061 olā-ika yusāriʿoona fee alḫayrāti wahum lahā sābiqoona These race for the good things, and they shall win them in the race.
062 walā nukallifu nafsan illā wusʿahā waladaynā kitābun yanṭiqu bialḥaqqi wahum lā yuḏlamoona And we task not any soul beyond its scope, and with Us is a Record which speaketh the truth, and they will not be wronged.
063 bal quloobuhum fee ġamratin min haṯa walahum aʿmālun min dooni ṯalika hum lahā ʿāmiloona Nay, but their hearts are in ignorance of this (Qur'an), and they have other works, besides, which they are doing;
064 [romanization missing] Till when We grasp their luxurious ones with the punishment, behold! they supplicate.
065 lā taj-aroo alyawma innakum minnā lā tunṣaroona Supplicate not this day! Assuredly ye will not be helped by Us.
066 qad kānat āyātee tutlā ʿalaykum fakuntum ʿalā aʿqābikum tankiṣoona My revelations were recited unto you, but ye used to turn back on your heels,
067 mustakbireena bihi sāmiran tahjuroona In scorn thereof. Nightly did ye rave together.
068 afalam yaddabbaroo alqawla am jāahum mā lam ya/ti ābāahumu al-awwaleena Have they not pondered the Word, or hath that come unto them which came not unto their fathers of old ?
069 am lam yaʿrifoo rasoolahum fahum lahu munkiroona Or know they not their messenger, and so reject him ?
070 am yaqooloona bihi jinnatun bal jāahum bialḥaqqi waakṯaruhum lilḥaqqi kārihoona Or say they: There is a madness in him ? Nay, but he bringeth them the Truth; and most of them are haters of the Truth.
071 walawi ittabaʿa alḥaqqu ahwāahum lafasadati alssamāwātu waal-arḍu waman feehinna bal ataynāhum biḏikrihim fahum ʿan ḏikrihim muʿriḍoona And if the Truth had followed their desires, verily the heavens and the earth and whosoever is therein had been corrupted. Nay, We have brought them their Reminder, but from their Reminder they now turn away.
072 am tas-aluhum ḫarjan faḫarāju rabbika ḫayrun wahuwa ḫayru alrrāziqeena Or dost thou ask of them (O Muhammad) any tribute ? But the bounty of thy Lord is better, for He is Best of all who make provision.
073 wa-innaka latadʿoohum ilā ṣiratin mustaqeemin And lo! thou summonest them indeed unto a straight path.
074 wa-inna allāḏeena lā yu/minoona bial-āḫirati ʿani alṣirati lanākiboona And lo! those who believe not in the Hereafter are indeed astray from the path.
075 walaw raḥimnāhum wakašafnā mā bihim min ḍurrin lalajjoo fee ṭuġyānihim yaʿmahoona Though We had mercy on them and relieved them of the harm afflicting them, they still would wander blindly on in their contumacy.
076 walaqad aḫaḏnāhum bialʿaṯabi famā istakānoo lirabbihim wamā yataḍarraʿoona Already have We grasped them with punishment, but they humble not themselves unto their Lord, nor do they pray,
077 [romanization missing] Until, when We open for them the gate of extreme punishment, behold! they are aghast thereat.
078 wahuwa allāḏee anšaa lakumu alssamʿa waal-absara waal-af-idata qaleelan mā taškuroona He it is Who hath created for you ears and eyes and hearts. Small thanks give ye!
079 wahuwa allāḏee ḏaraakum fee al-arḍi wa-ilayhi tuḥšaroona And He it is Who hath sown you broadcast in the earth, and unto Him ye will be gathered.
080 wahuwa allāḏee yuḥyee wayumeetu walahu iḫtilāfu allāyli waalnnahāri afalā taʿqiloona And He it is Who giveth life and causeth death, and His is the difference of night and day. Have ye then no sense ?
081 bal qāloo miṯla mā qāla al-awwaloona Nay, but they say the like of that which said the men of old;
082 qāloo a-iṯa mitnā wakunnā turāban waʿiḏāman a-innā lamabʿooṯoona They say: When we are dead and have become (mere) dust and bones, shall we then, forsooth, be raised again ?
083 laqad wuʿidnā naḥnu waābāonā haṯa min qablu in haṯa illā asateeru al-awwaleena We were already promised this, we and our forefathers. Lo! this is naught but fables of the men of old.
084 qul limani al-arḍu waman feehā in kuntum taʿlamoona Say: Unto Whom (belongeth) the earth and whosoever is therein, if ye have knowledge ?
085 sayaqooloona lillāhi qul afalā taḏakkaroona They will say: Unto Allah. Say: Will ye not then remember ?
086 qul man rabbu alssamāwāti alssabʿi warabbu alʿarši alʿaḏeemi Say: Who is Lord of the seven heavens, and Lord of the Tremendous Throne ?
087 sayaqooloona lillāhi qul afalā tattaqoona They will say: Unto Allah (all that belongeth). Say: Will ye not then keep duty (unto Him) ?
088 qul man biyadihi malakootu kulli šay-in wahuwa yujeeru walā yujāru ʿalayhi in kuntum taʿlamoona Say: In Whose hand is the dominion over all things and He protecteth, while against Him there is no protection, if ye have knowledge ?
089 sayaqooloona lillāhi qul faannā tusḥaroona They will say: Unto Allah (all that belongeth). Say: How then are ye bewitched ?
090 bal ataynāhum bialḥaqqi wa-innahum lakaṯiboona Nay, but We have brought them the Truth, and lo! they are liars.
091 mā ittaḫaḏa allāhu min waladin wamā kāna maʿahu min ilāhin iḏan laḏahaba kullu ilāhin bimā ḫalaqa walaʿalā baʿḍuhum ʿalā baʿḍin subhana allāhi ʿammā yaṣifoona Allah hath not chosen any son, nor is there any god along with Him; else would each god have assuredly championed that which he created, and some of them would assuredly have overcome others. Glorified be Allah above all that they allege.
092 ʿālimi alġaybi waalššahādati fataʿālā ʿammā yušrikoona Knower of the Invisible and the Visible! and Exalted be He over all that they ascribe as partners (unto Him)!
093 qul rabbi immā turiyannee mā yooʿadoona Say: My Lord! If Thou shouldst show me that which they are promised.
094 rabbi falā tajʿalnee fee alqawmi alṯṯālimeena My Lord! then set me not among the wrongdoing folk.
095 wa-innā ʿalā an nuriyaka mā naʿiduhum laqādiroona And verily We are Able to show thee that which We have promised them.
096 idfaʿ biallatee hiya aḥsanu alssayyi-ata naḥnu aʿlamu bimā yaṣifoona Repel evil with that which is better. We are Best Aware of that which they allege.
097 waqul rabbi aʿooḏu bika min hamazāti alššayateeni And say: My Lord! I seek refuge in Thee from suggestions of the evil ones,
098 waaʿooḏu bika rabbi an yahdurooni And I seek refuge in Thee, my Lord, lest they be present with me,
099 [romanization missing] Until, when death cometh unto one of them, he saith: My Lord! Send me back,
100 laʿallee aʿmalu saliḥan feemā taraktu kallā innahā kalimatun huwa qā-iluhā wamin warā-ihim barzaḫun ilā yawmi yubʿaṯoona That I may do right in that which I have left behind! But nay! It is but a word that he speaketh; and behind them is a barrier until the day when they are raised.
101 fa-iṯa nufiḫa fee alṣoori falā ansāba baynahum yawma-iḏin walā yatasāaloona And when the trumpet is blown there will be no kinship among them that day, nor will they ask of one another.
102 faman ṯaqulat mawāzeenuhu faolā-ika humu almufliḥoona Then those whose scales are heavy, they are the successful.
103 waman ḫaffat mawāzeenuhu faolā-ika allāḏeena ḫasiroo anfusahum fee jahannama ḫālidoona And those whose scales are light are those who lose their souls, in hell abiding.
104 talfaḥu wujoohahumu alnnāru wahum feehā kāliḥoona The fire burneth their faces, and they are glum therein.
105 alam takun āyātee tutlā ʿalaykum fakuntum bihā tukaṯṯiboona (It will be said): Were not My revelations recited unto you, and then ye used to deny them ?
106 qāloo rabbanā ġalabat ʿalaynā šiqwatunā wakunnā qawman dalleena They will say: Our Lord! Our evil fortune conquered us, and we were erring folk.
107 rabbanā aḫrijnā minhā fa-in ʿudnā fa-innā ḏālimoona Our Lord! Oh, bring us forth from hence! If we return (to evil) then indeed we shall be wrong-doers.
108 qāla iḫsaoo feehā walā tukallimooni He saith: Begone therein, and speak not unto Me.
109 innahu kāna fareequn min ʿibādee yaqooloona rabbanā āmannā faiġfir lanā wairḥamnā waanta ḫayru alrrahimeena Lo! there was a party of My slaves who said: Our Lord! We believe, therefor forgive us and have mercy on us for Thou art Best of all who show mercy;
110 faittaḫaḏtumoohum siḫriyyan ḥattā ansawkum ḏikree wakuntum minhum tadhakoona But ye chose them for a laughing-stock until they caused you to forget remembrance of Me, while ye laughed at them.
111 innee jazaytuhumu alyawma bimā ṣabaroo annahum humu alfā-izoona Lo! I have rewarded them this day forasmuch as they were steadfast in that they, even they, are the triumphant.
112 qāla kam labiṯtum fee al-arḍi ʿadada sineena He will say: How long tarried ye in the earth, counting by years ?
113 qāloo labiṯnā yawman aw baʿḍa yawmin fais-ali alʿāddeena They will say: We tarried by a day or part of a day. Ask of those who keep count!
114 qāla in labiṯtum illā qaleelan law annakum kuntum taʿlamoona He will say: Ye tarried but a little if ye only knew.
115 afaḥasibtum annamā ḫalaqnākum ʿabaṯan waannakum ilaynā lā turjaʿoona Deemed ye then that We had created you for naught, and that ye would not be returned unto Us ?
116 fataʿālā allāhu almaliku alḥaqqu lā ilāha illā huwa rabbu alʿarši alkareemi Now Allah be Exalted, the True King! There is no God save Him, the Lord of the Throne of Grace.
117 waman yadʿu maʿa allāhi ilāhan āḫara lā burhāna lahu bihi fa-innamā ḥisābuhu ʿinda rabbihi innahu lā yufliḥu alkāfiroona He who crieth unto any other god along with Allah hath no proof thereof. His reckoning is only with his Lord. Lo! disbelievers will not be successful.
118 waqul rabbi iġfir wairḥam waanta ḫayru alrrahimeena [Could not extract English for verse 118]
