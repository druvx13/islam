72. al-Jinn: The Jinn

001 qul ooḥiya ilayya annahu istamaʿa nafarun mina aljinni faqāloo innā samiʿnā qur-ānan ʿajabān Say (O Muhammad): It is revealed unto me that a company of the Jinn gave ear, and they said: Lo! we have heard a marvellous Qur'an,
002 yahdee ilā alrrušdi faāmannā bihi walan nušrika birabbinā aḥadān Which guideth unto righteousness, so we believe in it and we ascribe no partner unto our Lord.
003 waannahu taʿālā jaddu rabbinā mā ittaḫaḏa sahibatan walā waladān And (we believe) that He - exalted be the glory of our Lord! - hath taken neither wife nor son,
004 waannahu kāna yaqoolu safeehunā ʿalā allāhi šaṭatan And that the foolish one among us used to speak concerning Allah an atrocious lie.
005 waannā ḏanannā an lan taqoola al-insu waaljinnu ʿalā allāhi kaḏibān And lo! we had supposed that humankind and jinn would not speak a lie concerning Allah -
006 waannahu kāna rijālun mina al-insi yaʿooḏoona birijālin mina aljinni fazādoohum rahaqān And indeed (O Muhammad) individuals of humankind used to invoke the protection of individuals of the jinn, so that they increased them in revolt against Allah);
007 waannahum ḏannoo kamā ḏanantum an lan yabʿaṯa allāhu aḥadān And indeed they supposed, even as ye suppose, that Allah would not raise anyone (from the dead) -
008 waannā lamasnā alssamāa fawajadnāhā muli-at ḥarasan šadeedan wašuhubān And (the Jinn who had listened to the Qur'an said): We had sought the heaven but had found it filled with strong warders and meteors.
009 waannā kunnā naqʿudu minhā maqāʿida lilssamʿi faman yastamiʿi al-āna yajid lahu šihāban raṣadān And we used to sit on places (high) therein to listen. But he who listeneth now findeth a flame in wait for him;
010 waannā lā nadree ašarrun oreeda biman fee al-arḍi am arāda bihim rabbuhum rašadān And we know not whether harm is boded unto all who are in the earth, or whether their Lord intendeth guidance for them.
011 waannā minnā alssaliḥoona waminnā doona ṯalika kunnā ṭarā-iqa qidadān And among us there are righteous folk and among us there are far from that. We are sects having different rules.
012 wannā ḏanannā an lan nuʿjiza allāha fee al-arḍi walan nuʿjizahu harabān And we know that we cannot escape from Allah in the earth, nor can we escape by flight.
013 waannā lammā samiʿnā alhudā āmannā bihi faman yu/min birabbihi falā yaḫāfu baḫsan walā rahaqān And when we heard the guidance, we believed therein, and whoso believeth in his Lord, he feareth neither loss nor oppression.
014 waannā minnā almuslimoona waminnā alqāsiṭoona faman aslama faolā-ika taḥarraw rašadān And there are among us some who have surrendered (to Allah) and there are among us some who are unjust. And whoso hath surrendered to Allah, such have taken the right path purposefully.
015 waamā alqāsiṭoona fakānoo lijahannama ḥaṭabān And as for those who are unjust, they are firewood for hell.
016 waallawi istaqāmoo ʿalā alttareeqati laasqaynāhum māan ġadaqān If they (the idolaters) tread the right path, We shall give them to drink of water in abundance
017 linaftinahum feehi waman yuʿriḍ ʿan ḏikri rabbihi yasluk-hu ʿaṯaban ṣaʿadān That We may test them thereby, and whoso turneth away from the remembrance of his Lord; He will thrust him into ever-growing torment.
018 waanna almasājida lillāhi falā tadʿoo maʿa allāhi aḥadān And the places of worship are only for Allah, so pray not unto anyone along with Allah.
019 waannahu lammā qāma ʿabdu allāhi yadʿoohu kādoo yakoonoona ʿalayhi libadān And when the slave of Allah stood up in prayer to Him, they crowded on him, almost stifling.
020 qul innamā adʿoo rabbee walā ošriku bihi aḥadān Say (unto them, O Muhammad): I pray unto Allah only, and ascribe unto Him no partner.
021 qul innee lā amliku lakum ḍarran walā rašadān Say: Lo! I control not hurt nor benefit for you.
022 qul innee lan yujeeranee mina allāhi aḥadun walan ajida min doonihi multaḥadān Say: Lo! none can protect me from Allah, nor can I find any refuge beside Him
023 illā balāġan mina allāhi warisālātihi waman yaʿṣi allāha warasoolahu fa-inna lahu nāra jahannama ḫālideena feehā abadān (Mine is) but conveyance (of the Truth) from Allah, and His messages; and whoso disobeyeth Allah and His messenger, lo! his is fire of hell, wherein such dwell for ever.
024 [romanization missing] Till (the day) when they shall behold that which they are promised (they may doubt); but then they will know (for certain) who is weaker in allies and less in multitude.
025 qul in adree aqareebun mā tooʿadoona am yajʿalu lahu rabbee amadān Say (O Muhammad, unto the disbelievers): I know not whether that which ye are promised is nigh, or if my Lord hath set a distant term for it.
026 ʿālimu alġaybi falā yuḏhiru ʿalā ġaybihi aḥadān (He is) the Knower of the Unseen, and He revealeth unto none His secret,
027 illā mani irtada min rasoolin fa-innahu yasluku min bayni yadayhi wamin ḫalfihi raṣadān Save unto every messenger whom He hath chosen, and then He maketh a guard to go before him and a guard behind him
28 liyaʿlama an qad ablaġoo risālāti rabbihim waahata bimā ladayhim waahsa kulla šay-in ʿadadān [Could not extract English for verse 28]
