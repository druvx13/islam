27. an-Naml: The Ant

001 [romanization missing] Ta. Sin. These are revelations of the Qur'an and a Scripture that maketh plain;
002 hudan wabušrā lilmu/mineena A guidance and good tidings for believers
003 allāḏeena yuqeemoona alṣalāta wayu/toona alzzakāta wahum bial-āḫirati hum yooqinoona Who establish worship and pay the poor-due and are sure of the Hereafter.
004 inna allāḏeena lā yu/minoona bial-āḫirati zayyannā lahum aʿmālahum fahum yaʿmahoona Lo! as for those who believe not in the Hereafter, We have made their works fairseeming unto them so that they are all astray.
005 olā-ika allāḏeena lahum soo-o alʿaṯabi wahum fee al-āḫirati humu al-aḫsaroona Those are they for whom is the worst of punishment, and in the Hereafter they will be the greatest losers.
006 wa-innaka latulaqqā alqur-āna min ladun ḥakeemin ʿaleemin Lo! as for thee (Muhammad), thou verily receivest the Qur'an from the presence of One Wise, Aware.
007 iḏ qāla moosā li-ahlihi innee ānastu nāran saāteekum minhā biḫabarin aw āteekum bišihābin qabasin laʿallakum tastaloona (Remember) when Moses said unto his household: Lo! I spy afar off a fire; I will bring you tidings thence, or bring to you a borrowed flame that ye may warm yourselves.
008 falammā jāahā noodiya an boorika man fee alnnāri waman ḥawlahā wasubhana allāhi rabbi alʿālameena But when he reached it, he was called, saying: Blessed is Whosoever is in the fire and Whosoever is round about it! And Glorified be Allah, the Lord of the Worlds!
009 yā moosā innahu anā allāhu alʿazeezu alḥakeemu O Moses! Lo! it is I, Allah, the Mighty, the Wise.
010 waalqi ʿasaka falammā raāhā tahtazzu kaannahā jānnun wallā mudbiran walam yuʿaqqib yā moosā lā taḫaf innee lā yaḫāfu ladayya almursaloona And throw down thy staff! But when he saw it writhing as it were a demon, he turned to flee headlong; (but it was said unto him): O Moses! Fear not! the emissaries fear not in My presence,
011 illā man ḏalama ṯumma baddala ḥusnan baʿda soo-in fa-innee ġafoorun raḥeemun Save him who hath done wrong and afterward hath changed evil for good. And lo! I am Forgiving, Merciful.
012 waadḫil yadaka fee jaybika taḫruj baydaa min ġayri soo-in fee tisʿi āyātin ilā firʿawna waqawmihi innahum kānoo qawman fāsiqeena And put thy hand into the bosom of thy robe, it will come forth white but unhurt. (This will be one) among nine tokens unto Pharaoh and his people Lo! they were ever evil-living folk.
013 falammā jāat-hum āyātunā mubṣiratan qāloo haṯa siḥrun mubeenun But when Our tokens came unto them, plain to see, they said: This is mere magic,
014 wajaḥadoo bihā waistayqanat-hā anfusuhum ḏulman waʿuluwwan faonḏur kayfa kāna ʿāqibatu almufsideena And they denied them, though their souls acknowledged them, for spite and arrogance. Then see the nature of the consequence for the wrong-doers!
015 walaqad ātaynā dāwooda wasulaymāna ʿilman waqālā alḥamdu lillāhi allāḏee faḍalanā ʿalā kaṯeerin min ʿibādihi almu/mineena And We verily gave knowledge unto David and Solomon, and they said: Praise be to Allah, Who hath preferred us above many of His believing slaves!
016 wawariṯa sulaymānu dāwooda waqāla yā ayyuhā alnnāsu ʿullimnā manṭiqa alttayri waooteenā min kulli šay-in inna haṯa lahuwa alfaḍlu almubeenu And Solomon was David's heir. And he said: O mankind! Lo! we have been taught the language of birds, and have been given (abundance) of all things. This surely is evident favour.
017 waḥušira lisulaymāna junooduhu mina aljinni waal-insi waalttayri fahum yoozaʿoona And there were gathered together unto Solomon his armies of the jinn and humankind, and of the birds, and they were set in battle order;
018 [romanization missing] Till, when they reached the Valley of the Ants, an ant exclaimed: O ants! Enter your dwellings lest Solomon and his armies crush you, unperceiving.
019 fatabassama dahikan min qawlihā waqāla rabbi awziʿnee an aškura niʿmataka allātee anʿamta ʿalayya waʿalā wālidayya waan aʿmala saliḥan tardahu waadḫilnee biraḥmatika fee ʿibādika alssaliḥeena And (Solomon) smiled, laughing at her speech, and said: My Lord, arouse me to be thankful for Thy favour wherewith Thou hast favoured me and my parents, and to do good that shall be pleasing unto Thee, and include me in (the number of) Thy righteous slaves.
020 watafaqqada alttayra faqāla mā liya lā arā alhudhuda am kāna mina alġā-ibeena And he sought among the birds and said: How is it that I see not the hoopoe, or is he among the absent ?
021 laoʿaṯṯibannahu ʿaṯaban šadeedan aw laaḏbaḥannahu aw laya/tiyannee bisultanin mubeenin I verily will punish him with hard punishment or I verily will slay him, or he verily shall bring me a plain excuse.
022 famakaṯa ġayra baʿeedin faqāla ahattu bimā lam tuḥiṭ bihi waji/tuka min saba-in binaba-in yaqeenin But he was not long in coming, and he said: I have found out (a thing) that thou apprehendest not, and I come unto thee from Sheba with sure tidings.
023 innee wajadtu imraatan tamlikuhum waootiyat min kulli šay-in walahā ʿaršun ʿaḏeemun Lo! I found a woman ruling over them, and she hath been given (abundance) of all things, and hers is a mighty throne.
024 wajadtuhā waqawmahā yasjudoona lilššamsi min dooni allāhi wazayyana lahumu alššaytanu aʿmālahum faṣaddahum ʿani alssabeeli fahum lā yahtadoona I found her and her people worshipping the sun instead of Allah; and Satan maketh their works fairseeming unto them, and debarreth them from the way (of Truth), so that they go not aright;
025 allā yasjudoo lillāhi allāḏee yuḫriju alḫabaa fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wayaʿlamu mā tuḫfoona wamā tuʿlinoona So that they worship not Allah, Who bringeth forth the hidden in the heavens and the earth, and knoweth what ye hide and what ye proclaim,
026 allāhu lā ilāha illā huwa rabbu alʿarši alʿaḏeemi Allah; there is no God save Him, the Lord of the Tremendous Throne.
027 qāla sananḏuru aṣadaqta am kunta mina alkaṯibeena (Solomon) said: We shall see whether thou speakest truth or whether thou art of the liars.
028 iḏhab bikitābee haṯa faalqih ilayhim ṯumma tawalla ʿanhum faonḏur maṯa yarjiʿoona Go with this my letter and throw it down unto them; then turn away and see what (answer) they return,
029 qālat yā ayyuhā almalao innee olqiya ilayya kitābun kareemun (The Queen of Sheba) said (when she received the letter): O chieftains! Lo! there hath been thrown unto me a noble letter.
030 innahu min sulaymāna wa-innahu bismi allāhi alrraḥmāni alrraḥeemi Lo! it is from Solomon, and lo! it is: In the name of Allah, the Beneficent, the Merciful;
031 allā taʿloo ʿalayya wa/toonee muslimeena Exalt not yourselves against me, but come unto me as those who surrender.
032 qālat yā ayyuhā almalao aftoonee fee amree mā kuntu qatiʿatan amran ḥattā tašhadoona She said: O chieftains! Pronounce for me in my case. I decide no case till ye are present with me.
033 qāloo naḥnu oloo quwwatin waoloo ba/sin šadeedin waal-amru ilayki faonḏuree maṯa ta/mureena They said: We are lords of might and lords of great prowess, but it is for thee to command; so consider what thou wilt command.
034 qālat inna almulooka iṯa daḫaloo qaryatan afsadoohā wajaʿaloo aʿizzata ahlihā aḏillatan wakaṯalika yafʿaloona She said: Lo! kings, when they enter a township, ruin it and make the honour of its people shame. Thus will they do.
035 wa-innee mursilatun ilayhim bihadiyyatin fanāḏiratun bima yarjiʿu almursaloona But lo! I am going to send a present unto them, and to see with what (answer) the messengers return.
036 falammā jāa sulaymāna qāla atumiddoonani bimālin famā ātāniya allāhu ḫayrun mimmā ātākum bal antum bihadiyyatikum tafraḥoona So when (the envoy) came unto Solomon, (the King) said: What! Would ye help me with wealth ? But that which Allah hath given me is better than that which He hath given you. Nay it is ye (and not I) who exult in your gift.
037 irjiʿ ilayhim falana/tiyannahum bijunoodin lā qibala lahum bihā walanuḫrijannahum minhā aḏillatan wahum saġiroona Return unto them. We verily shall come unto them with hosts that they cannot resist, and we shall drive them out from thence with shame, and they will be abased.
038 qāla yā ayyuhā almalao ayyukum ya/teenee biʿaršihā qabla an ya/toonee muslimeena He said: O chiefs! Which of you will bring me her throne before they come unto me, surrendering ?
039 qāla ʿifreetun mina aljinni anā āteeka bihi qabla an taqooma min maqāmika wa-inne ʿalayhi laqawiyyun ameenun A stalwart of the jinn said: I will bring it thee before thou canst rise from thy place. Lo! I verily am strong and trusty for such work.
040 qāla allāḏee ʿindahu ʿilmun mina alkitābi anā āteeka bihi qabla an yartadda ilayka ṭarfuka falammā raāhu mustaqirran ʿindahu qāla haṯa min faḍli rabbee liyabluwanee aaškuru am akfuru waman šakara fa-innamā yaškuru linafsihi waman kafara fa-inna rabbee ġaniyyun kareemun One with whom was knowledge of the Scripture said: I will bring it thee before thy gaze returneth unto thee. And when he saw it set in his presence, (Solomon) said: This is of the bounty of my Lord, that He may try me whether I give thanks or am ungrateful. Whosoever giveth thanks he only giveth thanks for (the good of) his own soul; and whosoever is ungrateful (is ungrateful only to his own soul's hurt). For lo! my Lord is Absolute in independence, Bountiful.
041 qāla nakkiroo lahā ʿaršahā nanḏur atahtadee am takoonu mina allāḏeena lā yahtadoona He said: Disguise her throne for her that we may see whether she will go aright or be of those not rightly guided.
042 falammā jāat qeela ahākaṯa ʿaršuki qālat kaannahu huwa waooteenā alʿilma min qablihā wakunnā muslimeena So, when she came, it was said (unto her): Is thy throne like this ? She said: (It is) as though it were the very one. And (Solomon said): We were given the knowledge before her and we had surrendered (to Allah).
043 waṣaddahā mā kānat taʿbudu min dooni allāhi innahā kānat min qawmin kāfireena And (all) that she was wont to worship instead of Allah hindered her, for she came of disbelieving folk.
044 qeela lahā odḫulee alṣarḥa falammā raat-hu ḥasibat-hu lujjatan wakašafat ʿan sāqayhā qāla innahu ṣarḥun mumarradun min qawāreera qālat rabbi innee ḏalamtu nafsee waaslamtu maʿa sulaymāna lillāhi rabbi alʿālameena It was said unto her: Enter the hall. And when she saw it she deemed it a pool and bared her legs. (Solomon) said: Lo! it is a hall, made smooth, of glass. She said: My Lord! Lo! I have wronged myself, and I surrender with Solomon unto Allah, the Lord of the Worlds.
045 walaqad arsalnā ilā ṯamooda aḫāhum saliḥan ani oʿbudoo allāha fa-iṯa hum fareeqāni yaḫtaṣimoona And We verily sent unto Thamud their brother Salih, saying: Worship Allah. And lo! they (then became two parties quarrelling.
046 qāla yā qawmi lima tastaʿjiloona bialssayyi-ati qabla alḥasanati lawlā tastaġfiroona allāha laʿallakum turḥamoona He said: O my people! Why will ye hasten on the evil rather than the good ? Why will ye not ask pardon of Allah, that ye may receive mercy.
047 qāloo ittayyarnā bika wabiman maʿaka qāla ta-irukum ʿinda allāhi bal antum qawmun tuftanoona They said: We augur evil of thee and those with thee. He said: Your evil augury is with Allah. Nay, but ye are folk that are being tested.
048 wakāna fee almadeenati tisʿatu rahṭin yufsidoona fee al-arḍi walā yuṣliḥoona And there were in the city nine persons who made mischief in the land and reformed not.
049 qāloo taqāsamoo biallāhi lanubayyitannahu waahlahu ṯumma lanaqoolanna liwaliyyihi mā šahidnā mahlika ahlihi wa-innā lasadiqoona They said: Swear one to another by Allah that we verily will attack him and his household by night, and afterward we will surely say unto his friend: We witnessed not the destruction of his household. And lo! we are truthtellers.
050 wamakaroo makran wamakarnā makran wahum lā yašʿuroona So they plotted a plot: and We plotted a plot, while they perceived not.
051 faonḏur kayfa kāna ʿāqibatu makrihim annā dammarnāhum waqawmahum ajmaʿeena Then see the nature of the consequence of their plotting, for lo! We destroyed them and their people, every one.
052 fatilka buyootuhum ḫāwiyatan bimā ḏalamoo inna fee ṯalika laāyatan liqawmin yaʿlamoona See, yonder are their dwellings empty and in ruins because they did wrong. Lo! herein is indeed a portent for a people who have knowledge.
053 waanjaynā allāḏeena āmanoo wakānoo yattaqoona And we saved those who believed and used to ward off (evil).
054 walooṭan iḏ qāla liqawmihi ata/toona alfahišata waantum tubṣiroona And Lot! when he said unto his folk: Will ye commit abomination knowingly ?
055 a-innakum lata/toona alrrijāla šahwatan min dooni alnnisā-i bal antum qawmun tajhaloona Must ye needs lust after men instead of women ? Nay, but ye are folk who act senselessly.
056 famā kāna jawāba qawmihi illā an qāloo aḫrijoo āla looṭin min qaryatikum innahum onāsun yataṭahharoona But the answer of his folk was naught save that they said: Expel the household of Lot from your township, for they (forsooth) are folk who would keep clean!
057 faanjaynāhu waahlahu illā imraatahu qaddarnāhā mina alġābireena Then We saved him and his household save his wife; We destined her to be of those who stayed behind.
058 waamṭarnā ʿalayhim maṭaran fasāa maṭaru almunḏareena And We rained a rain upon them. Dreadful is the rain of those who have been warned.
059 quli alḥamdu lillāhi wasalāmun ʿalā ʿibādihi allāḏeena istafā āllāhu ḫayrun ammā yušrikoona Say (O Muhammad): Praise be to Allah, and peace be on His slaves whom He hath chosen! Is Allah best, or (all) that ye ascribe as partners (unto Him) ?
060 amman ḫalaqa alssamāwāti waal-arḍa waanzala lakum mina alssamā-i māan faanbatnā bihi ḥadā-iqa ṯata bahjatin mā kāna lakum an tunbitoo šajarahā a-ilāhun maʿa allāhi bal hum qawmun yaʿdiloona Is not He (best) Who created the heavens and the earth, and sendeth down for you water from the sky wherewith We cause to spring forth joyous orchards, whose trees it never hath been yours to cause to grow. Is there any God beside Allah ? Nay, but they are folk who ascribe equals (unto Him)!
061 amman jaʿala al-arḍa qarāran wajaʿala ḫilālahā anhāran wajaʿala lahā rawāsiya wajaʿala bayna albaḥrayni hajizan a-ilāhun maʿa allāhi bal akṯaruhum lā yaʿlamoona Is not He (best) Who made the earth a fixed abode, and placed rivers in the folds thereof, and placed firm hills therein, and hath set a barrier between the two seas ? Is there any God beside Allah ? Nay, but most of them know not!
062 amman yujeebu almudtarra iṯa daʿāhu wayakšifu alssoo-a wayajʿalukum ḫulafāa al-arḍi a-ilāhun maʿa allāhi qaleelan mā taḏakkaroona Is not He (best) Who answereth the wronged one when he crieth unto Him and removeth the evil, and hath made you viceroys of the earth ? Is there any God beside Allah ? Little do they reflect!
063 amman yahdeekum fee ḏulumāti albarri waalbaḥri waman yursilu alrriyaha bušran bayna yaday raḥmatihi a-ilāhun maʿa allāhi taʿālā allāhu ʿammā yušrikoona Is not He (best) Who guideth you in the darkness of the land and the sea, He Who sendeth the winds as heralds of His mercy ? Is there any God beside Allah ? High Exalted be Allah from all that they ascribe as partner (unto Him)!
064 amman yabdao alḫalqa ṯumma yuʿeeduhu waman yarzuqukum mina alssamā-i waal-arḍi a-ilāhun maʿa allāhi qul hātoo burhānakum in kuntum sadiqeena Is not He (best) Who produceth creation, then reproduceth it, and Who provideth for you from the heaven and the earth ? Is there any God beside Allah ? Say: Bring your proof, if ye are truthful!
065 qul lā yaʿlamu man fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi alġayba illā allāhu wamā yašʿuroona ayyāna yubʿaṯoona Say (O Muhammad): None in the heavens and the earth knoweth the Unseen save Allah; and they know not when they will be raised (again).
066 bali iddāraka ʿilmuhum fee al-āḫirati bal hum fee šakkin minhā bal hum minhā ʿamoona Nay, but doth their knowledge reach to the Hereafter ? Nay, for they are in doubt concerning it. Nay, for they cannot see it.
067 waqāla allāḏeena kafaroo a-iṯa kunnā turāban waābāonā a-innā lamuḫrajoona Yet those who disbelieve say: When we have become dust like our fathers, shall we verily be brought forth (again) ?
068 laqad wuʿidnā haṯa naḥnu waābāonā min qablu in haṯa illā asateeru al-awwaleena We were promised this, forsooth, we and our fathers. (All) this is naught but fables of the men of old.
069 qul seeroo fee al-arḍi faonḏuroo kayfa kāna ʿāqibatu almujrimeena Say (unto them, O Muhammad): Travel in the land and see the nature of the sequel for the guilty!
070 walā taḥzan ʿalayhim walā takun fee ḍayqin mimmā yamkuroona And grieve thou not for them, nor be in distress because of what they plot (against thee).
071 wayaqooloona matā haṯa alwaʿdu in kuntum sadiqeena And they say: When (will) this promise (be fulfilled), if ye are truthful ?
072 qul ʿasā an yakoona radifa lakum baʿḍu allāḏee tastaʿjiloona Say: It may be that a part of that which ye would hasten on is close behind you.
073 wa-inna rabbaka laḏoo faḍlin ʿalā alnnāsi walākinna akṯarahum lā yaškuroona Lo! thy Lord is full of bounty for mankind, but most of them do not give thanks.
074 wa-inna rabbaka layaʿlamu mā tukinnu ṣudooruhum wamā yuʿlinoona Lo! thy Lord knoweth surely all that their bosoms hide, and all that they proclaim.
075 wamā min ġā-ibatin fee alssamā-i waal-arḍi illā fee kitābin mubeenin And there is nothing hidden in the heaven or the earth but it is in a clear Record.
076 inna haṯa alqur-āna yaquṣu ʿalā banee isrā-eela akṯara allāḏee hum feehi yaḫtalifoona Lo! this Qur'an narrateth unto the Children of Israel most of that concerning which they differ.
077 wa-innahu lahudan waraḥmatun lilmu/mineena And lo! it is a guidance and a mercy for believers.
078 inna rabbaka yaqḍee baynahum biḥukmihi wahuwa alʿazeezu alʿaleemu Lo! thy Lord will judge between them of His wisdom, and He is the Mighty, the Wise.
079 fatawakkal ʿalā allāhi innaka ʿalā alḥaqqi almubeeni Therefor (O Muhammad) put thy trust in Allah, for thou (standest) on the plain Truth.
080 innaka lā tusmiʿu almawtā walā tusmiʿu alṣumma aldduʿāa iṯa wallaw mudbireena Lo! thou canst not make the dead to hear, nor canst thou make the deaf to hear the call when they have turned to flee;
081 wamā anta bihādee alʿumyi ʿan ḍalālatihim in tusmiʿu illā man yu/minu bi-āyātinā fahum muslimoona Nor canst thou lead the blind out of their error. Thou canst make none to hear, save those who believe Our revelations and who have surrendered.
082 wa-iṯa waqaʿa alqawlu ʿalayhim aḫrajnā lahum dābbatan mina al-arḍi tukallimuhum anna alnnāsa kānoo bi-āyātinā lā yooqinoona And when the word is fulfilled concerning them, We shall bring forth a beast of the earth to speak unto them because mankind had not faith in Our revelations.
083 wayawma naḥšuru min kulli ommatin fawjan mimman yukaṯṯibu bi-āyātinā fahum yoozaʿoona And (remind them of) the Day when We shall gather out of every nation a host of those who denied Our revelations, and they will be set in array;
084 [romanization missing] Till, when they come (before their Lord), He will say: Did ye deny My revelations when ye could not compass them in knowledge, or what was it that ye did ?
085 wawaqaʿa alqawlu ʿalayhim bimā ḏalamoo fahum lā yanṭiqoona And the Word will be fulfilled concerning them because they have done wrong, and they will not speak.
086 alam yaraw annā jaʿalnā allāyla liyaskunoo feehi waalnnahāra mubṣiran inna fee ṯalika laāyātin liqawmin yu/minoona Have they not seen how We have appointed the night that they may rest therein, and the day sight-giving ? Lo! therein verily are portents for a people who believe.
087 wayawma yunfaḫu fee alṣoori fafaziʿa man fee alssamāwāti waman fee al-arḍi illā man šāa allāhu wakullun atawhu dāḫireena And (remind them of) the Day when the Trumpet will be blown, and all who are in the heavens and the earth will start in fear, save him whom Allah willeth. And all come unto Him, humbled.
088 watarā aljibāla taḥsabuhā jāmidatan wahiya tamurru marra alssahabi ṣunʿa allāhi allāḏee atqana kulla šay-in innahu ḫabeerun bimā tafʿaloona And thou seest the hills thou deemest solid flying with the flight of clouds: the doing of Allah Who perfecteth all things. Lo! He is Informed of what ye do.
089 man jāa bialḥasanati falahu ḫayrun minhā wahum min fazaʿin yawma-iḏin āminoona Whoso bringeth a good deed will have better than its worth; and such are safe from fear that Day.
090 waman jāa bialssayyi-ati fakubbat wujoohuhum fee alnnāri hal tujzawna illā mā kuntum taʿmaloona And whoso bringeth an ill-deed, such will be flung down on their faces in the Fire. Are ye rewarded aught save what ye did ?
091 innamā omirtu an aʿbuda rabba haṯihi albaldati allāḏee ḥarramahā walahu kullu šay-in waomirtu an akoona mina almuslimeena (Say): I (Muhammad) am commanded only to serve the Lord of this land which He hath hallowed, and unto Whom all things belong. And I am commanded to be of those who surrender (unto Him),
092 waan atluwa alqur-āna famani ihtadā fa-innamā yahtadee linafsihi waman ḍalla faqul innamā anā mina almunḏireena And to recite the Qur'an. And whoso goeth right, goeth right only for (the good of) his own soul; and as for him who goeth astray - (Unto him) say: Lo! I am only a warner.
93 
waquli alḥamdu lillāhi sayureekum āyātihi fataʿrifoonahā wamā rabbuka biġāfilin ʿammā taʿmaloona
And say: Praise be to Allah Who will show you His portents so that ye shall know them. And thy Lord is not unaware of what ye (mortals) do.
