62. al-Jumu`ah: The Day of Congregation

001 yusabbiḥu lillāhi mā fee alssamāwāti wamā fee al-arḍi almaliki alquddoosi alʿazeezi alḥakeemi All that is in the heavens and all that is in the earth glorifieth Allah, the Sovereign Lord, the Holy One, the Mighty, the Wise.
002 huwa allāḏee baʿaṯa fee al-ommiyyeena rasoolan minhum yatloo ʿalayhim āyātihi wayuzakkeehim wayuʿallimuhumu alkitāba waalḥikmata wa-in kānoo min qablu lafee ḍalālin mubeenin He it is Who hath sent among the unlettered ones a messenger of their own, to recite unto them His revelations and to make them grow, and to teach them the Scripture and wisdom, though heretofore they were indeed in error manifest,
003 waāḫareena minhum lammā yalḥaqoo bihim wahuwa alʿazeezu alḥakeemu Along with others of them who have not yet joined them. He is the Mighty, the Wise.
004 [romanization missing] That is the bounty of Allah; which He giveth unto whom He will. Allah is of Infinite Bounty.
005 maṯalu allāḏeena ḥummiloo alttawrāta ṯumma lam yaḥmiloohā kamaṯali alḥimāri yaḥmilu asfāran bi/sa maṯalu alqawmi allāḏeena kaṯṯaboo bi-āyāti allāhi waallāhu lā yahdee alqawma alṯṯālimeena The likeness of those who are entrusted with the Law of Moses, yet apply it not, is as the likeness of the ass carrying books. Wretched is the likeness of folk who deny the revelations of Allah. And Allah guideth not wrongdoing folk.
006 qul yā ayyuhā allāḏeena hādoo in zaʿamtum annakum awliyāo lillāhi min dooni alnnāsi fatamannawoo almawta in kuntum sadiqeena Say (O Muhammad): O ye who are Jews! If ye claim that ye are favoured of Allah apart from (all) mankind, then long for death if ye are truthful.
007 walā yatamannawnahu abadan bimā qaddamat aydeehim waallāhu ʿaleemun bialṯṯālimeena But they will never long for it because of all that their own hands have sent before, and Allah is Aware of evil-doers.
008 qul inna almawta allāḏee tafirroona minhu fa-innahu mulāqeekum ṯumma turaddoona ilā ʿālimi alġaybi waalššahādati fayunabbi-okum bimā kuntum taʿmaloona Say (unto them, O Muhammad): Lo! the death from which ye shrink will surely meet you, and afterward ye will be returned unto the Knower of the Invisible and the Visible, and He will tell you what ye used to do.
009 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo iṯa noodiya lilṣalāti min yawmi aljumuʿati faisʿaw ilā ḏikri allāhi waḏaroo albayʿa ṯalikum ḫayrun lakum in kuntum taʿlamoona O ye who believe! When the call is heard for the prayer of the day of congregation, haste unto remembrance of Allah and leave your trading. That is better for you if ye did but know.
010 fa-iṯa quḍiyati alṣalātu faintaširoo fee al-arḍi waibtaġoo min faḍli allāhi waoḏkuroo allāha kaṯeeran laʿallakum tufliḥoona And when the prayer is ended, then disperse in the land and seek of Allah's bounty, and remember Allah much, that ye may be successful.
11 wa-iṯa raaw tijāratan aw lahwan infaḍoo ilayhā watarakooka qā-iman qul mā ʿinda allāhi ḫayrun mina allāhwi wamina alttijārati waallāhu ḫayru alrrāziqeena [Could not extract English for verse 11]
