18. al-Kahf: The Cave

001 alḥamdu lillāhi allāḏee anzala ʿalā ʿabdihi alkitāba walam yajʿal lahu ʿiwajān Praise be to Allah Who hath revealed the Scripture unto His slave, and hath not placed therein any crookedness,
002 qayyiman liyunḏira ba/san šadeedan min ladunhu wayubaššira almu/mineena allāḏeena yaʿmaloona alssalihati anna lahum ajran ḥasanān (But hath made it) straight, to give warning of stern punishment from Him, and to bring unto the believers who do good works the news that theirs will be a fair reward,
003 mākiṯeena feehi abadān Wherein they will abide for ever;
004 wayunḏira allāḏeena qāloo ittaḫaḏa allāhu waladān And to warn those who say: Allah hath chosen a son,
005 mā lahum bihi min ʿilmin walā li-ābā-ihim kaburat kalimatan taḫruju min afwāhihim in yaqooloona illā kaḏibān (A thing) whereof they have no knowledge, nor (had) their fathers, Dreadful is the word that cometh out of their mouths. They speak naught but a lie.
006 falaʿallaka bāḫiʿun nafsaka ʿalā āṯārihim in lam yu/minoo bihaṯa alḥadeeṯi asafān Yet it may be, if they believe not in this statement, that thou (Muhammad) wilt torment thy soul with grief over their footsteps.
007 innā jaʿalnā mā ʿalā al-arḍi zeenatan lahā linabluwahum ayyuhum aḥsanu ʿamalān Lo! We have placed all that is on the earth as an ornament thereof that We may try them: which of them is best in conduct.
008 wa-innā lajāʿiloona mā ʿalayhā ṣaʿeedan juruzān And lo! We shall make all that is thereon a barren mound.
009 am ḥasibta anna aṣ-haba alkahfi waalrraqeemi kānoo min āyātinā ʿajabān Or deemest thou that the People of the Cave and the Inscription are a wonder among Our portents ?
010 iḏ awā alfityatu ilā alkahfi faqāloo rabbanā ātinā min ladunka raḥmatan wahayyi/ lanā min amrinā rašadān When the young men fled for refuge to the Cave and said: Our Lord! Give us mercy from Thy presence, and shape for us right conduct in our plight.
011 faḍarabnā ʿalā aṯanihim fee alkahfi sineena ʿadadān Then We sealed up their hearing in the Cave for a number of years.
012 thumma baʿaṯnāhum linaʿlama ayyu alḥizbayni ahsa limā labiṯoo amadān And afterward We raised them up that We might know which of the two parties would best calculate the time that they had tarried.
013 naḥnu naquṣu ʿalayka nabaahum bialḥaqqi innahum fityatun āmanoo birabbihim wazidnāhum hudān We narrate unto thee their story with truth. Lo! they were young men who believed in their Lord, and We increased them in guidance.
014 warabaṭnā ʿalā quloobihim iḏ qāmoo faqāloo rabbunā rabbu alssamāwāti waal-arḍi lan nadʿuwa min doonihi ilāhan laqad qulnā iḏan šaṭatan And We made firm their hearts when they stood forth and said: Our Lord is the Lord of the heavens and the earth. We cry unto no God beside Him, for then should we utter an enormity.
015 hāolā-i qawmunā ittaḫaḏoo min doonihi ālihatan lawlā ya/toona ʿalayhim bisultanin bayyinin faman aḏlamu mimmani iftarā ʿalā allāhi kaḏibān These, our people, have chosen (other) gods beside Him though they bring no clear warrant (vouchsafed) to them. And who doth greater wrong than he who inventeth a lie concerning Allah ?
016 wa-iḏi iʿtazaltumoohum wamā yaʿbudoona illā allāha fa/woo ilā alkahfi yanšur lakum rabbukum min raḥmatihi wayuhayyi/ lakum min amrikum mirfaqān And when ye withdraw from them and that which they worship except Allah, then seek refuge in the Cave; your Lord will spread for you of His mercy and will prepare for you a pillow in your plight.
017 watarā alššamsa iṯa ṭalaʿat tazāwaru ʿan kahfihim ṯata alyameeni wa-iṯa ġarabat taqriḍuhum ṯata alššimāli wahum fee fajwatin minhu ṯalika min āyāti allāhi man yahdi allāhu fahuwa almuhtadi waman yuḍlil falan tajida lahu waliyyan muršidān And thou mightest have seen the sun when it rose move away from their cave to the right, and when it set go past them on the left, and they were in the cleft thereof. That was (one) of the portents of Allah. He whom Allah guideth, he indeed is led aright, and he whom He sendeth astray, for him thou wilt not find a guiding friend.
018 wataḥsabuhum ayqāḏan wahum ruqoodun wanuqallibuhum ṯata alyameeni waṯata alššimāli wakalbuhum bāsiṭun ḏirāʿayhi bialwaṣeedi lawi ittalaʿta ʿalayhim lawallayta minhum firāran walamuli/ta minhum ruʿbān And thou wouldst have deemed them waking though they were asleep, and We caused them to turn over to the right and the left, and their dog stretching out his paws on the threshold. If thou hadst observed them closely thou hadst assuredly turned away from them in flight, and hadst been filled with awe of them.
019 wakaṯalika baʿaṯnāhum liyatasāaloo baynahum qāla qā-ilun minhum kam labiṯtum qāloo labiṯnā yawman aw baʿḍa yawmin qāloo rabbukum aʿlamu bimā labiṯtum faibʿaṯoo aḥadakum biwariqikum haṯihi ilā almadeenati falyanḏur ayyuhā azkā ṭaʿāman falya/tikum birizqin minhu walyatalattaf walā yušʿiranna bikum aḥadān And in like manner We awakened them that they might question one another. A speaker from among them said: How long have ye tarried ? They said: We have tarried a day or some part of a day, (Others) said: Your Lord best knoweth what ye have tarried. Now send one of you with this your silver coin unto the city, and let him see what food is purest there and bring you a supply thereof. Let him be courteous and let no man know of you.
020 innahum in yaḏharoo ʿalaykum yarjumookum aw yuʿeedookum fee millatihim walan tufliḥoo iḏan abadān For they, if they should come to know of you, will stone you or turn you back to their religion; then ye will never prosper.
021 wakaṯalika aʿṯarnā ʿalayhim liyaʿlamoo anna waʿda allāhi ḥaqqun waanna alsāʿata lā rayba feehā iḏ yatanāzaʿoona baynahum amrahum faqāloo ibnoo ʿalayhim bunyānan rabbuhum aʿlamu bihim qāla allāḏeena ġalaboo ʿalā amrihim lanattaḫiḏanna ʿalayhim masjidān And in like manner We disclosed them (to the people of the city) that they might know that the promise of Allah is true, and that, as for the Hour, there is no doubt concerning it. When (the people of the city) disputed of their case among themselves, they said: Build over them a building; their Lord knoweth best concerning them. Those who won their point said: We verily shall build a place of worship over them.
022 sayaqooloona ṯalāṯatun rābiʿuhum kalbuhum wayaqooloona ḫamsatun sādisuhum kalbuhum rajman bialġaybi wayaqooloona sabʿatun waṯāminuhum kalbuhum qul rabbee aʿlamu biʿiddatihim mā yaʿlamuhum illā qaleelun falā tumāri feehim illā mirāan ḏāhiran walā tastafti feehim minhum aḥadān (Some) will say: They were three, their dog the fourth, and (some) say: Five, their dog the sixth, guessing at random; and (some) say: Seven, and their dog the eighth. Say (O Muhammad): My Lord is Best Aware of their number. None knoweth them save a few. So contend not concerning them except with an outward contending, and ask not any of them to pronounce concerning them.
023 walā taqoolanna lišay-in innee fāʿilun ṯalika ġadān And say not of anything: Lo! I shall do that tomorrow,
024 illā an yašāa allāhu waoḏkur rabbaka iṯa naseeta waqul ʿasā an yahdiyani rabbee li-aqraba min haṯa rašadān Except if Allah will. And remember thy Lord when thou forgettest, and say: It may be that my Lord guideth me unto a nearer way of truth than this.
025 walabiṯoo fee kahfihim ṯalāṯa mi-atin sineena waizdādoo tisʿān And (it is said) they tarried in their Cave three hundred years and add nine.
026 quli allāhu aʿlamu bimā labiṯoo lahu ġaybu alssamāwāti waal-arḍi abṣir bihi waasmiʿ mā lahum min doonihi min waliyyin walā yušriku fee ḥukmihi aḥadān Say: Allah is Best Aware how long they tarried. His is the Invisible of the heavens and the earth. How clear of sight is He and keen of hearing! They have no protecting friend beside Him, and He maketh none to share in His government.
027 waotlu mā ooḥiya ilayka min kitābi rabbika lā mubaddila likalimātihi walan tajida min doonihi multaḥadān And recite that which hath been revealed unto thee of the Scripture of thy Lord. There is none who can change His words, and thou wilt find no refuge beside Him.
028 waiṣbir nafsaka maʿa allāḏeena yadʿoona rabbahum bialġadāti waalʿašiyyi yureedoona wajhahu walā taʿdu ʿaynāka ʿanhum tureedu zeenata alḥayāti alddunyā walā tuṭiʿ man aġfalnā qalbahu ʿan ḏikrinā waittabaʿa hawāhu wakāna amruhu furutan Restrain thyself along with those who cry unto their Lord at morn and evening, seeking His Countenance; and let not thine eyes overlook them, desiring the pomp of the life of the world; and obey not him whose heart We have made heedless of Our remembrance, who followeth his own lust and whose case hath been abandoned.
029 waquli alḥaqqu min rabbikum faman šāa falyu/min waman šāa falyakfur innā aʿtadnā lilṯṯālimeena nāran ahata bihim surādiquhā wa-in yastaġeeṯoo yuġāṯoo bimā-in kaalmuhli yašwee alwujooha bi/sa alššarābu wasāat murtafaqān Say: (It is) the truth from the Lord of you (all). Then whosoever will, let him believe, and whosoever will, let him disbelieve. Lo! We have prepared for disbelievers Fire. Its tent encloseth them. If they ask for showers, they will be showered with water like to molten lead which burneth the faces. Calamitous the drink and ill the resting-place!
030 inna allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati innā lā nuḍeeʿu ajra man aḥsana ʿamalān Lo! as for those who believe and do good works - Lo! We suffer not the reward of one whose work is goodly to be lost.
031 olā-ika lahum jannātu ʿadnin tajree min taḥtihimu al-anhāru yuḥallawna feehā min asāwira min ḏahabin wayalbasoona ṯiyāban ḫuḍran min sundusin wa-istabraqin muttaki-eena feehā ʿalā al-arā-iki niʿma alṯṯawābu waḥasunat murtafaqān As for such, theirs will be Gardens of Eden, wherein rivers flow beneath them; therein they will be given armlets of gold and will wear green robes of finest silk and gold embroidery, reclining upon throne therein. Blest the reward, and fair the resting-place!
032 waiḍrib lahum maṯalan rajulayni jaʿalnā li-aḥadihimā jannatayni min aʿnābin waḥafafnāhumā binaḫlin wajaʿalnā baynahumā zarʿān Coin for them a similitude: Two men, unto one of whom We had assigned two gardens of grapes, and We had surrounded both with date-palms and had put between them tillage.
033 kiltā aljannatayni ātat okulahā walam taḏlim minhu šay-an wafajjarnā ḫilālahumā naharān Each of the gardens gave its fruit and withheld naught thereof. And We caused a river to gush forth therein.
034 wakāna lahu ṯamarun faqāla lisahibihi wahuwa yuhawiruhu anā akṯaru minka mālan waaʿazzu nafarān And he had fruit. And he said unto his comrade, when he spake with him: I am more than thee in wealth, and stronger in respect of men.
035 wadaḫala jannatahu wahuwa ḏālimun linafsihi qāla mā aḏunnu an tabeeda haṯihi abadān And he went into his garden, while he (thus) wronged himself. He said: I think not that all this will ever perish.
036 wamā aḏunnu alssāʿata qā-imatan wala-in rudidtu ilā rabbee laajidanna ḫayran minhā munqalabān I think not that the Hour will ever come, and if indeed I am brought back unto my Lord I surely shall find better than this as a resort.
037 qāla lahu sahibuhu wahuwa yuhawiruhu akafarta biallaḏee ḫalaqaka min turābin ṯumma min nuṭfatin ṯumma sawwāka rajulān His comrade, when he (thus) spake with him, exclaimed: Disbelievest thou in Him Who created thee of dust, then of a drop (of seed), and then fashioned thee a man ?
038 lākinna huwa allāhu rabbee walā ošriku birabbee aḥadān But He is Allah, my Lord, and I ascribe unto my Lord no partner.
039 walawlā iḏ daḫalta jannataka qulta mā šāa allāhu lā quwwata illā biallāhi in tarani anā aqalla minka mālan wawaladān If only, when thou enteredst thy garden, thou hadst said: That which Allah willeth (will come to pass)! There is no strength save in Allah! Though thou seest me as less than thee in wealth and children,
040 faʿasā rabbee an yu/tiyani ḫayran min jannatika wayursila ʿalayhā ḥusbānan mina alssamā-i fatuṣbiḥa ṣaʿeedan zalaqān Yet it may be that my Lord will give me better than thy garden, and will send on it a bolt from heaven, and some morning it will be a smooth hillside,
041 aw yuṣbiḥa māohā ġawran falan tastaṭeeʿa lahu ṭalabān Or some morning the water thereof will be lost in the earth so that thou canst not make search for it.
042 waoḥeeṭa biṯamarihi faaṣbaḥa yuqallibu kaffayhi ʿalā mā anfaqa feehā wahiya ḫāwiyatun ʿalā ʿuroošihā wayaqoolu yā laytanee lam ošrik birabbee aḥadān And his fruit was beset (with destruction). Then began he to wring his hands for all that he had spent upon it, when (now) it was all ruined on its trellises, and to say: Would that I had ascribed no partner to my Lord!
043 walam takun lahu fi-atun yanṣuroonahu min dooni allāhi wamā kāna muntaṣirān And he had no troop of men to help him as against Allah, nor could he save himself.
044 hunālika alwalāyatu lillāhi alḥaqqi huwa ḫayrun ṯawāban waḫayrun ʿuqbān In this case is protection only from Allah, the True, He is Best for reward, and best for consequence.
045 waiḍrib lahum maṯala alḥayāti alddunyā kamā-in anzalnāhu mina alssamā-i faiḫtalaṭa bihi nabātu al-arḍi faaṣbaḥa hašeeman taḏroohu alrriyahu wakāna allāhu ʿalā kulli šay-in muqtadirān And coin for them the similitude of the life of the world as water which We send down from the sky, and the vegetation of the earth mingleth with it and then becometh dry twigs that the winds scatter. Allah is able to do all things.
046 almālu waalbanoona zeenatu alḥayāti alddunyā waalbāqiyātu alssalihatu ḫayrun ʿinda rabbika ṯawāban waḫayrun amalān Wealth and children are an ornament of the life of the world. But the good deeds which endure are better in thy Lord's sight for reward, and better in respect of hope.
047 wayawma nusayyiru aljibāla watarā al-arḍa bārizatan waḥašarnāhum falam nuġādir minhum aḥadān And (bethink you of) the Day when we remove the hills and ye see the earth emerging, and We gather them together so as to leave not one of them behind.
048 waʿuriḍoo ʿalā rabbika ṣaffan laqad ji/tumoonā kamā ḫalaqnākum awwala marratin bal zaʿamtum allān najʿala lakum mawʿidān And they are set before thy Lord in ranks (and it is said unto them): Now verily have ye come unto Us as We created you at the first. But ye thought that We had set no tryst for you.
049 wawuḍiʿa alkitābu fatarā almujrimeena mušfiqeena mimmā feehi wayaqooloona yā waylatanā mā lihaṯa alkitābi lā yuġādiru ṣaġeeratan walā kabeeratan illā ahsahā wawajadoo mā ʿamiloo hadiran walā yaḏlimu rabbuka aḥadān And the Book is placed, and thou seest the guilty fearful of that which is therein, and they say: What kind of a Book is this that leaveth not a small thing nor a great thing but hath counted it! And they find all that they did confronting them, and thy Lord wrongeth no-one.
050 wa-iḏ qulnā lilmalā-ikati osjudoo li-ādama fasajadoo illā ibleesa kāna mina aljinni fafasaqa ʿan amri rabbihi afatattaḫiḏoonahu waḏurriyyatahu awliyāa min doonee wahum lakum ʿaduwwun bi/sa lilṯṯālimeena badalān And (remember) when We said unto the angels: Fall prostrate before Adam, and they fell prostrate, all save Iblis. He was of the jinn, so he rebelled against his Lord's command. Will ye choose him and his seed for your protecting friends instead of Me, when they are an enemy unto you ? Calamitous is the exchange for evil-doers.
051 mā ašhadtuhum ḫalqa alssamāwāti waal-arḍi walā ḫalqa anfusihim wamā kuntu muttaḫiḏa almuḍilleena ʿaḍudān I made them not to witness the creation of the heavens and the earth, nor their own creation; nor choose I misleaders for (My) helpers.
052 wayawma yaqoolu nādoo šurakā-iya allāḏeena zaʿamtum fadaʿawhum falam yastajeeboo lahum wajaʿalnā baynahum mawbiqān And (be mindful of) the Day when He will say: Call those partners of Mine whom ye pretended. Then they will cry unto them, but they will not hear their prayer, and We shall set a gulf of doom between them.
053 waraā almujrimoona alnnāra faḏannoo annahum muwāqiʿoohā walam yajidoo ʿanhā maṣrifān And the guilty behold the Fire and know that they are about to fall therein, and they find no way of escape thence.
054 walaqad ṣarrafnā fee haṯa alqur-āni lilnnāsi min kulli maṯalin wakāna al-insānu akṯara šay-in jadalān And verily We have displayed for mankind in this Qur'an all manner of similitudes, but man is more than anything contentious.
055 wamā manaʿa alnnāsa an yu/minoo iḏ jāahumu alhudā wayastaġfiroo rabbahum illā an ta/tiyahum sunnatu al-awwaleena aw ya/tiyahumu alʿaṯabu qubulān And naught hindereth mankind from believing when the guidance cometh unto them, and from asking forgiveness of their Lord unless (it be that they wish) that the judgment of the men of old should come upon them or (that) they should be confronted with the Doom.
056 wamā nursilu almursaleena illā mubaššireena wamunḏireena wayujādilu allāḏeena kafaroo bialbatili liyudḥiḍoo bihi alḥaqqa waittaḫaḏoo āyātee wamā onḏiroo huzuwān We send not the messengers save as bearers of good news and warners. Those who disbelieve contend with falsehood in order to refute the Truth thereby. And they take Our revelations and that wherewith they are threatened as a jest.
057 waman aḏlamu mimman ḏukkira bi-āyāti rabbihi faaʿraḍa ʿanhā wanasiya mā qaddamat yadāhu innā jaʿalnā ʿalā quloobihim akinnatan an yafqahoohu wafee aṯanihim waqran wa-in tadʿuhum ilā alhudā falan yahtadoo iḏan abadān And who doth greater wrong than he who hath been reminded of the revelations of his Lord, yet turneth away from them and forgetteth what his hands send forward (to the Judgment) ? Lo! on their hearts We have placed coverings so that they understand not, and in their ears a deafness. And though thou call them to the guidance, in that case they can never be led aright.
058 warabbuka alġafooru ḏoo alrraḥmati law yu-āḫiḏuhum bimā kasaboo laʿajjala lahumu alʿaṯaba bal lahum mawʿidun lan yajidoo min doonihi maw-ilān Thy Lord is the Forgiver, Full of Mercy. If He took them to task (now) for what they earn, He would hasten on the doom for them; but theirs is an appointed term from which they will find no escape.
059 watilka alqurā ahlaknāhum lammā ḏalamoo wajaʿalnā limahlikihim mawʿidān And (all) those townships! We destroyed them when they did wrong, and We appointed a fixed time for their destruction.
060 wa-iḏ qāla moosā lifatāhu lā abraḥu ḥattā abluġa majmaʿa albaḥrayni aw amḍiya ḥuqubān And when Moses said unto his servant: I will not give up until I reach the point where the two rivers meet, though I march on for ages.
061 falammā balaġā majmaʿa baynihimā nasiyā ḥootahumā faittaḫaḏa sabeelahu fee albaḥri sarabān And when they reached the point where the two met, they forgot their fish, and it took its way into the waters, being free.
062 falammā jāwazā qāla lifatāhu ātinā ġadāanā laqad laqeenā min safarinā haṯa naṣabān And when they had gone further, he said unto his servant: Bring us our breakfast. Verily we have found fatigue in this our journey.
063 qāla araayta iḏ awaynā ilā alṣaḫrati fa-innee naseetu alḥoota wamā ansāneehu illā alššaytanu an aḏkurahu waittaḫaḏa sabeelahu fee albaḥri ʿajabān He said: Didst thou see, when we took refuge on the rock, and I forgot the fish - and none but Satan caused me to forget to mention it - it took its way into the waters by a marvel.
064 qāla ṯalika mā kunnā nabġi fairtaddā ʿalā āṯārihimā qaṣasan He said: This is that which we have been seeking. So they retraced their steps again.
065 fawajadā ʿabdan min ʿibādinā ātaynāhu raḥmatan min ʿindinā waʿallamnāhu min ladunnā ʿilmān Then found they one of Our slaves, unto whom We had given mercy from Us, and had taught him knowledge from Our presence.
066 qāla lahu moosā hal attabiʿuka ʿalā an tuʿallimani mimmā ʿullimta rušdān Moses said unto him: May I follow thee, to the end that thou mayst teach me right conduct of that which thou hast been taught ?
067 qāla innaka lan tastaṭeeʿa maʿiya ṣabrān He said: Lo! thou canst not bear with me.
068 wakayfa taṣbiru ʿalā mā lam tuḥiṭ bihi ḫubrān How canst thou bear with that whereof thou canst not compass any knowledge ?
069 qāla satajidunee in šāa allāhu sabiran walā aʿṣee laka amrān He said: Allah willing, thou shalt find me patient and I shall not in aught gainsay thee.
070 qāla fa-ini ittabaʿtanee falā tas-alnee ʿan šay-in ḥattā oḥdiṯa laka minhu ḏikrān He said: Well, if thou go with me, ask me not concerning aught till I myself make mention of it unto thee.
071 fainṭalaqā ḥattā iṯa rakibā fee alssafeenati ḫaraqahā qāla aḫaraqtahā lituġriqa ahlahā laqad ji/ta šay-an imrān So they twain set out till, when they were in the ship, he made a hole therein. (Moses) said: Hast thou made a hole therein to drown the folk thereof ? Thou verily hast done a dreadful thing.
072 qāla alam aqul innaka lan tastaṭeeʿa maʿiya ṣabrān He said: Did I not tell thee that thou couldst not bear with me ?
073 qāla lā tu-āḫiḏnee bimā naseetu walā turhiqnee min amree ʿusrān (Moses) said: Be not wroth with me that I forgot, and be not hard upon me for my fault.
074 fainṭalaqā ḥattā iṯa laqiyā ġulāman faqatalahu qāla aqatalta nafsan zakiyyatan biġayri nafsin laqad ji/ta šay-an nukrān So they twain journeyed on till, when they met a lad, he slew him. (Moses) said: What! Hast thou slain an innocent soul who hath slain no man ? Verily thou hast done a horrid thing.
075 qāla alam aqul laka innaka lan tastaṭeeʿa maʿiya ṣabrān He said: Did I not tell thee that thou couldst not bear with me ?
076 qāla in saaltuka ʿan šay-in baʿdahā falā tusahibnee qad balaġta min ladunnee ʿuḏrān (Moses) said: If I ask thee after this concerning aught, keep not company with me. Thou hast received an excuse from me.
077 fainṭalaqā ḥattā iṯa atayā ahla qaryatin istaṭʿamā ahlahā faabaw an yuḍayyifoohumā fawajadā feehā jidāran yureedu an yanqaḍa faaqāmahu qāla law ši/ta laittaḫaḏta ʿalayhi ajrān So they twain journeyed on till, when they came unto the folk of a certain township, they asked its folk for food, but they refused to make them guests. And they found therein a wall upon the point of falling into ruin, and he repaired it. (Moses) said: If thou hadst wished, thou couldst have taken payment for it.
078 qāla haṯa firāqu baynee wabaynika saonabbi-oka bita/weeli mā lam tastaṭiʿ ʿalayhi ṣabrān He said: This is the parting between thee and me! I will announce unto thee the interpretation of that thou couldst not bear with patience.
079 ammā alssafeenatu fakānat limasākeena yaʿmaloona fee albaḥri faaradtu an aʿeebahā wakāna warāahum malikun ya/ḫuḏu kulla safeenatin ġaṣbān As for the ship, it belonged to poor people working on the river, and I wished to mar it, for there was a king behind them who is taking every ship by force.
080 waammā alġulāmu fakāna abawāhu mu/minayni faḫašeenā an yurhiqahumā ṭuġyānan wakufrān And as for the lad, his parents were believers and we feared lest he should oppress them by rebellion and disbelief.
081 faaradnā an yubdilahumā rabbuhumā ḫayran minhu zakātan waaqraba ruḥmān And we intended that their Lord should change him for them for one better in purity and nearer to mercy.
082 waammā aljidāru fakāna liġulāmayni yateemayni fee almadeenati wakāna taḥtahu kanzun lahumā wakāna aboohumā saliḥan faarāda rabbuka an yabluġā ašuddahumā wayastaḫrijā kanzahumā raḥmatan min rabbika wamā faʿaltuhu ʿan amree ṯalika ta/weelu mā lam tasṭiʿ ʿalayhi ṣabrān And as for the wall, it belonged to two orphan boys in the city, and there was beneath it a treasure belonging to them, and their father had been righteous, and thy Lord intended that they should come to their full strength and should bring forth their treasure as a mercy from their Lord; and I did it not upon my own command. Such is the interpretation of that wherewith thou couldst not bear.
083 wayas-aloonaka ʿan ḏee alqarnayni qul saatloo ʿalaykum minhu ḏikrān They will ask thee of Dhu'l-Qarneyn. Say: I shall recite unto you a remembrance of him.
084 innā makkannā lahu fee al-arḍi waātaynāhu min kulli šay-in sababān Lo! We made him strong in the land and gave him unto every thing a road.
085 faatbaʿa sababān And he followed a road
086 [romanization missing] Till, when he reached the setting-place of the sun, he found it setting in a muddy spring, and found a people thereabout. We said: O Dhu'l-Qarneyn! Either punish or show them kindness.
087 qāla ammā man ḏalama fasawfa nuʿaṯṯibuhu ṯumma yuraddu ilā rabbihi fayuʿaṯṯibuhu ʿaṯaban nukrān He said: As for him who doeth wrong, we shall punish him, and then he will be brought back unto his Lord, Who will punish him with awful punishment!
088 waammā man āmana waʿamila saliḥan falahu jazāan alḥusnā wasanaqoolu lahu min amrinā yusrān But as for him who believeth and doeth right, good will be his reward, and We shall speak unto him a mild command.
089 thumma atbaʿa sababān Then he followed a road
090 [romanization missing] Till, when he reached the rising-place of the sun, he found it rising on a people for whom We had appointed no shelter therefrom.
091 kaṯalika waqad aḥaṭnā bimā ladayhi ḫubrān So (it was). And We knew all concerning him.
092 thumma atbaʿa sababān Then he followed a road
093 [romanization missing] Till, when he came between the two mountains, he found upon their hither side a folk that scarce could understand a saying.
094 qāloo yā ṯa alqarnayni inna ya/jooja wama/jooja mufsidoona fee al-arḍi fahal najʿalu laka ḫarjan ʿalā an tajʿala baynanā wabaynahum saddān They said: O Dhu'l-Qarneyn! Lo! Gog and Magog are spoiling the land. So may we pay thee tribute on condition that thou set a barrier between us and them ?
095 qāla mā makkannee feehi rabbee ḫayrun faaʿeenoonee biquwwatin ajʿal baynakum wabaynahum radmān He said: That wherein my Lord hath established me is better (than your tribute). Do but help me with strength (of men), I will set between you and them a bank.
096 [romanization missing] Give me pieces of iron - till, when he had levelled up (the gap) between the cliffs, he said: Blow! - till, when he had made it a fire, he said: Bring me molten copper to pour thereon.
097 famā istaʿoo an yaḏharoohu wamā istataʿoo lahu naqbān And (Gog and Magog) were not able to surmount, nor could they pierce (it).
098 qāla haṯa raḥmatun min rabbee fa-iṯa jāa waʿdu rabbee jaʿalahu dakkāa wakāna waʿdu rabbee ḥaqqān He said: This is a mercy from my Lord; but when the promise of my Lord cometh to pass, He will lay it low, for the promise of my Lord is true.
099 wataraknā baʿḍahum yawma-iḏin yamooju fee baʿḍin wanufiḫa fee alṣoori fajamaʿnāhum jamʿān And on that day we shall let some of them surge against others, and the Trumpet will be blown. Then We shall gather them together in one gathering.
100 waʿaraḍnā jahannama yawma-iḏin lilkāfireena ʿardan On that day we shall present hell to the disbelievers, plain to view,
101 allāḏeena kānat aʿyunuhum fee ġita-in ʿan ḏikree wakānoo lā yastaṭeeʿoona samʿān Those whose eyes were hoodwinked from My reminder, and who could not bear to hear.
102 afaḥasiba allāḏeena kafaroo an yattaḫiḏoo ʿibādee min doonee awliyāa innā aʿtadnā jahannama lilkāfireena nuzulān Do the disbelievers reckon that they can choose My bondmen as protecting friends beside Me ? Lo! We have prepared hell as a welcome for the disbelievers.
103 qul hal nunabbi-okum bial-aḫsareena aʿmālān Say: Shall We inform you who will be the greatest losers by their works ?
104 allāḏeena ḍalla saʿyuhum fee alḥayāti alddunyā wahum yaḥsaboona annahum yuḥsinoona ṣunʿān Those whose effort goeth astray in the life of the world, and yet they reckon that they do good work.
105 olā-ika allāḏeena kafaroo bi-āyāti rabbihim waliqā-ihi faḥabiṭat aʿmāluhum falā nuqeemu lahum yawma alqiyāmati waznān Those are they who disbelieve in the revelations of their Lord and in the meeting with Him. Therefor their works are vain, and on the Day of Resurrection We assign no weight to them.
106 [romanization missing] That is their reward: hell, because they disbelieved, and made a jest of Our revelations and Our messengers.
107 inna allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati kānat lahum jannātu alfirdawsi nuzulān Lo! those who believe and do good works, theirs are the Gardens of Paradise for welcome,
108 khālideena feehā lā yabġoona ʿanhā ḥiwalān Wherein they will abide, with no desire to be removed from thence.
109 qul law kāna albaḥru midādan likalimāti rabbee lanafida albaḥru qabla an tanfada kalimātu rabbee walaw ji/nā bimiṯlihi madadān Say: Though the sea became ink for the Words of my Lord, verily the sea would be used up before the words of my Lord were exhausted, even though We brought the like thereof to help.
110 
qul innamā anā bašarun miṯlukum yooha ilayya annamā ilāhukum ilāhun wahidun faman kāna yarjoo liqāa rabbihi falyaʿmal ʿamalan saliḥan walā yušrik biʿibādati rabbihi aḥadān
Say: I am only a mortal like you. My Lord inspireth in me that your God is only One God. And whoever hopeth for the meeting with his Lord, let him do righteous work, and make none sharer of the worship due unto his Lord.
