74. al-Mudathir: The Clothed One

001 yā ayyuhā almuddaṯṯiru O thou enveloped in thy cloak,
002 qum faanḏir Arise and warn!
003 warabbaka fakabbir Thy Lord magnify,
004 waṯiyābaka faṭahhir Thy raiment purify,
005 waalrrujza faohjur Pollution shun!
006 walā tamnun tastakṯiru And show not favour, seeking wordly gain!
007 walirabbika faiṣbir For the sake of thy Lord, be patient!
008 fa-iṯa nuqira fee alnnāqoori For when the trumpet shall sound,
009 faṯalika yawma-iḏin yawmun ʿaseerun Surely that day will be a day of anguish,
010 ʿalā alkāfireena ġayru yaseerin Not of ease, for disbelievers.
011 [romanization missing] Leave Me (to deal) with him whom I created lonely,
012 wajaʿaltu lahu mālan mamdoodān And then bestowed upon him ample means,
013 wabaneena šuhoodān And sons abiding in his presence
014 wamahhadtu lahu tamheedān And made (life) smooth for him.
015 thumma yaṭmaʿu an azeeda Yet he desireth that I should give more.
016 kallā innahu kāna li-āyātinā ʿaneedān Nay! For lo! he hath been stubborn to Our revelations.
017 saorhiquhu ṣaʿoodān On him I shall impose a fearful doom.
018 innahu fakkara waqaddara For lo! he did consider; then he planned -
019 faqutila kayfa qaddara (Self-)destroyed is he, how he planned!
020 thumma qutila kayfa qaddara Again (self-)destroyed is he, how he planned! -
021 thumma naḏara Then looked he,
022 thumma ʿabasa wabasara Then frowned he and showed displeasure.
023 thumma adbara waistakbara Then turned he away in pride
024 faqāla in haṯa illā siḥrun yu/ṯaru And said: This is naught else than magic from of old;
025 in haṯa illā qawlu albašari This is naught else than speech of mortal man.
026 saoṣleehi saqara Him shall I fling unto the burning.
027 wamā adrāka mā saqaru - Ah, what will convey unto thee what that burning is! -
028 lā tubqee walā taḏaru It leaveth naught; it spareth naught
029 lawwahatun lilbašari It shrivelleth the man.
030 ʿalayhā tisʿata ʿašara Above it are nineteen.
031 wamā jaʿalnā aṣ-haba alnnāri illā malā-ikatan wamā jaʿalnā ʿiddatahum illā fitnatan lillaḏeena kafaroo liyastayqina allāḏeena ootoo alkitāba wayazdāda allāḏeena āmanoo eemānan walā yartāba allāḏeena ootoo alkitāba waalmu/minoona waliyaqoola allāḏeena fee quloobihim maraḍun waalkāfiroona maṯa arāda allāhu bihaṯa maṯalan kaṯalika yuḍillu allāhu man yašāo wayahdee man yašāo wamā yaʿlamu junooda rabbika illā huwa wamā hiya illā ḏikrā lilbašari We have appointed only angels to be wardens of the Fire, and their number have We made to be a stumbling-block for those who disbelieve; that those to whom the Scripture hath been given may have certainty, and that believers may increase in faith; and that those to whom the Scripture hath been given and believers may not doubt; and that those in whose hearts there is disease, and disbelievers, may say: What meaneth Allah by this similitude ? Thus Allah sendeth astray whom He will, and whom He will He guideth. None knoweth the hosts of thy Lord save Him. This is naught else than a Reminder unto mortals.
032 kallā waalqamari Nay, by the Moon
033 waallayli iḏ adbara And the night when it withdraweth
034 waalṣubḥi iṯa asfara And the dawn when it shineth forth,
035 innahā la-iḥdā alkubari Lo! this is one of the greatest (portents)
036 naḏeeran lilbašari As a warning unto men,
037 liman šāa minkum an yataqaddama aw yataaḫḫara Unto him of you who will advance or hang back.
038 kullu nafsin bimā kasabat raheenatun Every soul is a pledge for its own deeds;
039 illā aṣ-haba alyameeni Save those who will stand on the right hand.
040 fee jannātin yatasāaloona In gardens they will ask one another
041 ʿani almujrimeena Concerning the guilty:
042 mā salakakum fee saqara What hath brought you to this burning ?
043 qāloo lam naku mina almuṣalleena They will answer: We were not of those who prayed
044 walam naku nuṭʿimu almiskeena Nor did we feed the wretched.
045 wakunnā naḫooḍu maʿa alḫā-iḍeena We used to wade (in vain dispute) with (all) waders,
046 wakunnā nukaṯṯibu biyawmi alddeeni And we used to deny the Day of Judgment,
047 [romanization missing] Till the Inevitable came unto us.
048 famā tanfaʿuhum šafāʿatu alššāfiʿeena The mediation of no mediators will avail them then.
049 famā lahum ʿani alttaḏkirati muʿriḍeena Why now turn they away from the Admonishment,
050 kaannahum ḥumurun mustanfiratun As they were frightened asses
051 farrat min qaswaratin Fleeing from a lion ?
052 bal yureedu kullu imri-in minhum an yu/tā ṣuḥufan munaššaratan Nay, but everyone of them desireth that he should be given open pages (from Allah).
053 kallā bal lā yaḫāfoona al-āḫirata Nay, verily. They fear not the Hereafter.
054 kallā innahu taḏkiratun Nay, verily. Lo! this is an Admonishment.
055 faman šāa ḏakarahu So whosoever will may heed.
56 wamā yaḏkuroona illā an yašāa allāhu huwa ahlu alttaqwā waahlu almaġfirati [Could not extract English for verse 56]
