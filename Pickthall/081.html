81. at-Takwir: The Cessation

1 
iṯa alššamsu kuwwirat
When the sun is overthrown,

2 
wa-iṯa alnnujoomu inkadarat
And when the stars fall,

3 
wa-iṯa aljibālu suyyirat
And when the hills are moved,

4 
wa-iṯa alʿišāru ʿuttilat
And when the camels big with young are abandoned,

5 
wa-iṯa alwuḥoošu ḥuširat
And when the wild beasts are herded together,

6 
wa-iṯa albiharu sujjirat
And when the seas rise,

7 
wa-iṯa alnnufoosu zuwwijat
And when souls are reunited,

8 
wa-iṯa almawoodatu su-ilat
And when the girl-child that was buried alive is asked

9 
bi-ayyi ḏanbin qutilat
For what sin she was slain,

10 
wa-iṯa alṣuḥufu nuširat
And when the pages are laid open,

11 
wa-iṯa alssamāo kušiṭat
And when the sky is torn away,

12 
wa-iṯa aljaḥeemu suʿʿirat
And when hell is lighted,

13 
wa-iṯa aljannatu ozlifat
And when the Garden is brought nigh,

14 
ʿalimat nafsun mā ahdarat
(Then) every soul will know what it hath made ready.

15 
falā oqsimu bialḫunnasi
Oh, but I call to witness the planets,

16 
aljawāri alkunnasi
The stars which rise and set,

17 
waallayli iṯa ʿasʿasa
And the close of night,

18 
waalṣubḥi iṯa tanaffasa
And the breath of morning

19 
innahu laqawlu rasoolin kareemin
That this is in truth the word of an honoured messenger,

20 Mighty, established in the presence of the Lord of the Throne,

21 
mutaʿin ṯamma ameenin
(One) to be obeyed, and trustworthy;

22 
wamā sahibukum bimajnoonin
And your comrade is not mad.

23 
walaqad raāhu bialofuqi almubeeni
Surely he beheld Him on the clear horizon.

24 
wamā huwa ʿalā alġaybi biḍaneenin
And he is not avid of the Unseen.

25 
wamā huwa biqawli šaytanin rajeemin
Nor is this the utterance of a devil worthy to be stoned.

26 
faayna taḏhaboona
Whither then go ye ?

27 
in huwa illā ḏikrun lilʿālameena
This is naught else than a reminder unto creation,

28 
liman šāa minkum an yastaqeema
Unto whomsoever of you willeth to walk straight.

29 
wamā tašāoona illā an yašāa allāhu rabbu alʿālameena
And ye will not, unless (it be) that Allah willeth, the Lord of Creation.
