81. at-Takwir: The Cessation

001 iṯa alššamsu kuwwirat When the sun is overthrown,
002 wa-iṯa alnnujoomu inkadarat And when the stars fall,
003 wa-iṯa aljibālu suyyirat And when the hills are moved,
004 wa-iṯa alʿišāru ʿuttilat And when the camels big with young are abandoned,
005 wa-iṯa alwuḥoošu ḥuširat And when the wild beasts are herded together,
006 wa-iṯa albiharu sujjirat And when the seas rise,
007 wa-iṯa alnnufoosu zuwwijat And when souls are reunited,
008 wa-iṯa almawoodatu su-ilat And when the girl-child that was buried alive is asked
009 bi-ayyi ḏanbin qutilat For what sin she was slain,
010 wa-iṯa alṣuḥufu nuširat And when the pages are laid open,
011 wa-iṯa alssamāo kušiṭat And when the sky is torn away,
012 wa-iṯa aljaḥeemu suʿʿirat And when hell is lighted,
013 wa-iṯa aljannatu ozlifat And when the Garden is brought nigh,
014 ʿalimat nafsun mā ahdarat (Then) every soul will know what it hath made ready.
015 falā oqsimu bialḫunnasi Oh, but I call to witness the planets,
016 aljawāri alkunnasi The stars which rise and set,
017 waallayli iṯa ʿasʿasa And the close of night,
018 waalṣubḥi iṯa tanaffasa And the breath of morning
019 innahu laqawlu rasoolin kareemin That this is in truth the word of an honoured messenger,
020 [romanization missing] Mighty, established in the presence of the Lord of the Throne,
021 mutaʿin ṯamma ameenin (One) to be obeyed, and trustworthy;
022 wamā sahibukum bimajnoonin And your comrade is not mad.
023 walaqad raāhu bialofuqi almubeeni Surely he beheld Him on the clear horizon.
024 wamā huwa ʿalā alġaybi biḍaneenin And he is not avid of the Unseen.
025 wamā huwa biqawli šaytanin rajeemin Nor is this the utterance of a devil worthy to be stoned.
026 faayna taḏhaboona Whither then go ye ?
027 in huwa illā ḏikrun lilʿālameena This is naught else than a reminder unto creation,
028 liman šāa minkum an yastaqeema Unto whomsoever of you willeth to walk straight.
29 
wamā tašāoona illā an yašāa allāhu rabbu alʿālameena
And ye will not, unless (it be) that Allah willeth, the Lord of Creation.
