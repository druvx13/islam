64. at-Taghabun: The Cheating

001 yusabbiḥu lillāhi mā fee alssamāwāti wamā fee al-arḍi lahu almulku walahu alḥamdu wahuwa ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerun All that is in the heavens and all that is in the earth glorifieth Allah; unto Him belongeth Sovereignty and unto Him belongeth praise, and He is Able to do all things.
002 huwa allāḏee ḫalaqakum faminkum kāfirun waminkum mu/minun waallāhu bimā taʿmaloona baṣeerun He it is Who created you, but one of you is a disbeliever and one of you is a believer, and Allah is Seer of what ye do.
003 khalaqa alssamāwāti waal-arḍa bialḥaqqi waṣawwarakum faaḥsana ṣuwarakum wa-ilayhi almaṣeeru He created the heavens and the earth with truth, and He shaped you and made good your shapes, and unto Him is the journeying.
004 yaʿlamu mā fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wayaʿlamu mā tusirroona wamā tuʿlinoona waallāhu ʿaleemun biṯati alṣudoori He knoweth all that is in the heavens and the earth, and He knoweth what ye conceal and what ye publish. And Allah is Aware of what is in the breasts (of men).
005 alam ya/tikum nabao allāḏeena kafaroo min qablu faṯaqoo wabāla amrihim walahum ʿaṯabun aleemun Hath not the story reached you of those who disbelieved of old and so did taste the ill-effects of their conduct, and theirs will be a painful doom.
006 [romanization missing] That was because their messengers (from Allah) kept coming unto them with clear proofs (of Allah's Sovereignty), but they said: Shall mere mortals guide us ? So they disbelieved and turned away, and Allah was independent (of them). Allah is Absolute, Owner of Praise.
007 zaʿama allāḏeena kafaroo an lan yubʿaṯoo qul balā warabbee latubʿaṯunna ṯumma latunabbaonna bimā ʿamiltum waṯalika ʿalā allāhi yaseerun Those who disbelieve assert that they will not be raised again. Say (unto them, O Muhammad): Yea, verily, by my Lord! ye will be raised again and then ye will be informed of what ye did; and that is easy for Allah.
008 faāminoo biallāhi warasoolihi waalnnoori allāḏee anzalnā waallāhu bimā taʿmaloona ḫabeerun So believe in Allah and His messenger and the light which We have revealed. And Allah is Informed of what ye do.
009 yawma yajmaʿukum liyawmi aljamʿi ṯalika yawmu alttaġābuni waman yu/min biallāhi wayaʿmal saliḥan yukaffir ʿanhu sayyi-ātihi wayudḫilhu jannātin tajree min taḥtihā al-anhāru ḫālideena feehā abadan ṯalika alfawzu alʿaḏeemu The day when He shall gather you unto the Day of Assembling, that will be a day of mutual disillusion. And whoso believeth in Allah and doeth right, He will remit from him his evil deeds and will bring him unto Gardens underneath which rivers flow, therein to abide for ever. That is the supreme triumph.
010 waallaḏeena kafaroo wakaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā olā-ika aṣ-habu alnnāri ḫālideena feehā wabi/sa almaṣeeru But those who disbelieve and deny Our revelations, such are owners of the Fire; they will abide therein - a hapless journey's end!
011 mā asaba min muṣeebatin illā bi-iḏni allāhi waman yu/min biallāhi yahdi qalbahu waallāhu bikulli šay-in ʿaleemun No calamity befalleth save by Allah's leave. And whosoever believeth in Allah, He guideth his heart. And Allah is Knower of all things.
012 waaṭeeʿoo allāha waaṭeeʿoo alrrasoola fa-in tawallaytum fa-innamā ʿalā rasoolinā albalāġu almubeenu Obey Allah and obey His messenger; but if ye turn away, then the duty of Our messenger is only to convey (the message) plainly.
013 allāhu lā ilāha illā huwa waʿalā allāhi falyatawakkali almu/minoona Allah! There is no God save Him. In Allah, therefore, let believers put their trust.
014 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo inna min azwājikum waawlādikum ʿaduwwan lakum faihṯaroohum wa-in taʿfoo wataṣfaḥoo wataġfiroo fa-inna allāha ġafoorun raḥeemun O ye who believe! Lo! among your wives and your children there are enemies for you, therefor beware of them. And if ye efface and overlook and forgive, then lo! Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.
015 innamā amwālukum waawlādukum fitnatun waallāhu ʿindahu ajrun ʿaḏeemun Your wealth and your children are only a temptation, whereas Allah! with Him is an immense reward.
016 faittaqoo allāha mā istaṭaʿtum waismaʿoo waaṭeeʿoo waanfiqoo ḫayran li-anfusikum waman yooqa šuhha nafsihi faolā-ika humu almufliḥoona So keep your duty to Allah as best ye can, and listen, and obey, and spend; that is better for your souls. And whoso is saved from his own greed, such are the successful.
017 in tuqriḍoo allāha qarḍan ḥasanan yudaʿifhu lakum wayaġfir lakum waallāhu šakoorun ḥaleemun If ye lend unto Allah a goodly loan, He will double it for you and will forgive you, for Allah is Responsive, Clement,

ʿālimu alġaybi waalššahādati alʿazeezu alḥakeemu
Knower of the Invisible and the Visible, the Mighty, the Wise.
