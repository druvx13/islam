47. Muhammad: Muhammad

001 allāḏeena kafaroo waṣaddoo ʿan sabeeli allāhi aḍalla aʿmālahum Those who disbelieve and turn (men) from the way of Allah, He rendereth their actions vain.
002 waallaḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati waāmanoo bimā nuzzila ʿalā muḥammadin wahuwa alḥaqqu min rabbihim kaffara ʿanhum sayyi-ātihim waaṣlaḥa bālahum And those who believe and do good works and believe in that which is revealed unto Muhammad - and it is the truth from their Lord - He riddeth them of their ill-deeds and improveth their state.
003 [romanization missing] That is because those who disbelieve follow falsehood and because those who believe follow the truth from their Lord. Thus Allah coineth their similitudes for mankind.
004 fa-iṯa laqeetumu allāḏeena kafaroo faḍarba alrriqābi ḥattā iṯa aṯḫantumoohum fašuddoo alwaṯāqa fa-immā mannan baʿdu wa-immā fidāan ḥattā taḍaʿa alḥarbu awzārahā ṯalika walaw yašāo allāhu laintaṣara minhum walākin liyabluwa baʿḍakum bibaʿḍin waallaḏeena qutiloo fee sabeeli allāhi falan yuḍilla aʿmālahum Now when ye meet in battle those who disbelieve, then it is smiting of the necks until, when ye have routed them, then making fast of bonds; and afterward either grace or ransom till the war lay down its burdens. That (is the ordinance). And if Allah willed He could have punished them (without you) but (thus it is ordained) that He may try some of you by means of others. And those who are slain in the way of Allah, He rendereth not their actions vain.
005 sayahdeehim wayuṣliḥu bālahum He will guide them and improve their state,
006 wayudḫiluhumu aljannata ʿarrafahā lahum And bring them in unto the Garden which He hath made known to them.
007 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo in tanṣuroo allāha yanṣurkum wayuṯabbit aqdāmakum O ye who believe! If ye help Allah, He will help you and will make your foothold firm.
008 waallaḏeena kafaroo fataʿsan lahum waaḍalla aʿmālahum And those who disbelieve, perdition is for them, and He will make their actions vain.
009 [romanization missing] That is because they are averse to that which Allah hath revealed, therefor maketh He their actions fruitless.
010 afalam yaseeroo fee al-arḍi fayanḏuroo kayfa kāna ʿāqibatu allāḏeena min qablihim dammara allāhu ʿalayhim walilkāfireena amṯāluhā Have they not travelled in the land to see the nature of the consequence for those who were before them ? Allah wiped them out. And for the disbelievers there will be the like thereof.
011 [romanization missing] That is because Allah is patron of those who believe, and because the disbelievers have no patron.
012 inna allāha yudḫilu allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati jannātin tajree min taḥtihā al-anhāru waallaḏeena kafaroo yatamattaʿoona waya/kuloona kamā ta/kulu al-anʿāmu waalnnāru maṯwan lahum Lo! Allah will cause those who believe and do good works to enter Gardens underneath which rivers flow; while those who disbelieve take their comfort in this life and eat even as the cattle eat, and the Fire is their habitation.
013 wakaayyin min qaryatin hiya ašaddu quwwatan min qaryatika allātee aḫrajatka ahlaknāhum falā nasira lahum And how many a township stronger than thy township (O Muhammad) which hath cast thee out, have We destroyed, and they had no helper!
014 afaman kāna ʿalā bayyinatin min rabbihi kaman zuyyina lahu soo-o ʿamalihi waittabaʿoo ahwāahum Is he who relieth on a clear proof from his Lord like those for whom the evil that they do is beautified while they follow their own lusts ?
015 maṯalu aljannati allātee wuʿida almuttaqoona feehā anhārun min mā-in ġayri āsinin waanhārun min labanin lam yataġayyar ṭaʿmuhu waanhārun min ḫamrin laṯṯatin lilššāribeena waanhārun min ʿasalin muṣaffan walahum feehā min kulli alṯṯamarāti wamaġfiratun min rabbihim kaman huwa ḫālidun fee alnnāri wasuqoo māan ḥameeman faqattaʿa amʿāahum A similitude of the Garden which those who keep their duty (to Allah) are promised: Therein are rivers of water unpolluted, and rivers of milk whereof the flavour changeth not, and rivers of wine delicious to the drinkers, and rivers of clear- run honey; therein for them is every kind of fruit, with pardon from their Lord. (Are those who enjoy all this) like those who are immortal in the Fire and are given boiling water to drink so that it teareth their bowels ?
016 waminhum man yastamiʿu ilayka ḥattā iṯa ḫarajoo min ʿindika qāloo lillaḏeena ootoo alʿilma maṯa qāla ānifan olā-ika allāḏeena ṭabaʿa allāhu ʿalā quloobihim waittabaʿoo ahwāahum Among them are some who give ear unto thee (Muhammad) till, when they go forth from thy presence they say unto those who have been given knowledge: What was that he said just now ? Those are they whose hearts Allah hath sealed, and they follow their own lusts.
017 waallaḏeena ihtadaw zādahum hudan waātāhum taqwāhum While as for those who walk aright, He addeth to their guidance, and giveth them their protection (against evil).
018 fahal yanḏuroona illā alssāʿata an ta/tiyahum baġtatan faqad jāa ašratuhā faannā lahum iṯa jāat-hum ḏikrāhum Await they aught save the Hour, that it should come upon them unawares ? And the beginnings thereof have already come. But how, when it hath come upon them, can they take their warning ?
019 faiʿlam annahu lā ilāha illā allāhu waistaġfir liḏanbika walilmu/mineena waalmu/mināti waallāhu yaʿlamu mutaqallabakum wamaṯwākum So know (O Muhammad) that there is no God save Allah, and ask forgiveness for thy sin and for believing men and believing women. Allah knoweth (both) your place of turmoil and your place of rest.
020 wayaqoolu allāḏeena āmanoo lawlā nuzzilat sooratun fa-iṯa onzilat sooratun muḥkamatun waḏukira feehā alqitālu raayta allāḏeena fee quloobihim maraḍun yanḏuroona ilayka naḏara almaġšiyyi ʿalayhi mina almawti faawlā lahum And those who believe say: If only a surah were revealed! But when a decisive surah is revealed and war is mentioned therein, thou seest those in whose hearts is a disease looking at thee with the look of men fainting unto death. Therefor woe unto them!
021 [romanization missing] Obedience and a civil word. Then, when the matter is determined, if they are loyal to Allah it will be well for them.
022 fahal ʿasaytum in tawallaytum an tufsidoo fee al-arḍi watuqattiʿoo arhamakum Would ye then, if ye were given the command, work corruption in the land and sever your ties of kinship ?
023 olā-ika allāḏeena laʿanahumu allāhu faaṣammahum waaʿmā absarahum Such are they whom Allah curseth so that He deafeneth them and maketh blind their eyes.
024 afalā yatadabbaroona alqur-āna am ʿalā quloobin aqfāluhā Will they then not meditate on the Qur'an, or are there locks on the hearts ?
025 inna allāḏeena irtaddoo ʿalā adbārihim min baʿdi mā tabayyana lahumu alhudā alššaytanu sawwala lahum waamlā lahum Lo! those who turn back after the guidance hath been manifested unto them, Satan hath seduced them, and He giveth them the rein.
026 [romanization missing] That is because they say unto those who hate what Allah hath revealed: We will obey you in some matters; and Allah knoweth their secret talk.
027 fakayfa iṯa tawaffat-humu almalā-ikatu yaḍriboona wujoohahum waadbārahum Then how (will it be with them) when the angels gather them, smiting their faces and their backs!
028 [romanization missing] That will be because they followed that which angereth Allah, and hated that which pleaseth Him. Therefor He hath made their actions vain.
029 am ḥasiba allāḏeena fee quloobihim maraḍun an lan yuḫrija allāhu aḍġānahum Or do those in whose hearts is a disease deem that Allah will not bring to light their (secret) hates ?
030 walaw našāo laaraynākahum falaʿaraftahum biseemāhum walataʿrifannahum fee laḥni alqawli waallāhu yaʿlamu aʿmālakum And if We would, We could show them unto thee (Muhammad) so that thou shouldst know them surely by their marks. And thou shalt know them by the burden of their talk. And Allah knoweth your deeds.
031 walanabluwannakum ḥattā naʿlama almujāhideena minkum waalssabireena wanabluwa aḫbārakum And verily We shall try you till We know those of you who strive hard (for the cause of Allah) and the steadfast, and till We test your record.
032 inna allāḏeena kafaroo waṣaddoo ʿan sabeeli allāhi wašāqqoo alrrasoola min baʿdi mā tabayyana lahumu alhudā lan yaḍuroo allāha šay-an wasayuḥbiṭu aʿmālahum Lo! those who disbelieve and turn from the way of Allah and oppose the messenger after the guidance hath been manifested unto them, they hurt Allah not a jot, and He will make their actions fruitless.
033 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo aṭeeʿoo allāha waaṭeeʿoo alrrasoola walā tubṭiloo aʿmālakum O ye who believe! Obey Allah and obey the messenger, and render not your actions vain.
034 inna allāḏeena kafaroo waṣaddoo ʿan sabeeli allāhi ṯumma mātoo wahum kuffārun falan yaġfira allāhu lahum Lo! those who disbelieve and turn from the way of Allah and then die disbelievers, Allah surely will not pardon them.
035 falā tahinoo watadʿoo ilā alssalmi waantumu al-aʿlawna waallāhu maʿakum walan yatirakum aʿmālakum So do not falter and cry out for peace when ye (will be) the uppermost, and Allah is with you, and He will not grudge (the reward of) your actions.
036 innamā alḥayātu alddunyā laʿibun walahwun wa-in tu/minoo watattaqoo yu/tikum ojoorakum walā yas-alkum amwālakum The life of the world is but a sport and a pastime. And if ye believe and ward off (evil). He will give you your wages, and will not ask of you your wordly wealth.
037 in yas-alkumoohā fayuḥfikum tabḫaloo wayuḫrij aḍġānakum If He should ask it of you and importune you, ye would hoard it, and He would bring to light your (secret) hates.
38 hā antum hāolā-i tudʿawna litunfiqoo fee sabeeli allāhi faminkum man yabḫalu waman yabḫal fa-innamā yabḫalu ʿan nafsihi waallāhu alġaniyyu waantumu alfuqarāo wa-in tatawallaw yastabdil qawman ġayrakum ṯumma lā yakoonoo amṯālakum [Could not extract English for verse 38]
