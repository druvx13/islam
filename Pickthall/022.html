22. al-Hajj: The Pilgrimage

001 yā ayyuhā alnnāsu ittaqoo rabbakum inna zalzalata alssāʿati šay-on ʿaḏeemun O mankind! Fear your Lord. Lo! the earthquake of the Hour (of Doom) is a tremendous thing.
002 yawma tarawnahā taḏhalu kullu murḍiʿatin ʿammā arḍaʿat wataḍaʿu kullu ṯati ḥamlin ḥamlahā watarā alnnāsa sukārā wamā hum bisukārā walākinna ʿaṯaba allāhi šadeedun On the day when ye behold it, every nursing mother will forget her nursling and every pregnant one will be delivered of her burden, and thou (Muhammad) wilt see mankind as drunken, yet they will not be drunken, but the Doom of Allah will be strong (upon them).
003 wamina alnnāsi man yujādilu fee allāhi biġayri ʿilmin wayattabiʿu kulla šaytanin mareedin Among mankind is he who disputeth concerning Allah without knowledge, and followeth each froward devil;
004 kutiba ʿalayhi annahu man tawallāhu faannahu yuḍilluhu wayahdeehi ilā ʿaṯabi alssaʿeeri For him it is decreed that whoso taketh him for friend, he verily will mislead him and will guide him to the punishment of the Flame.
005 yā ayyuhā alnnāsu in kuntum fee raybin mina albaʿṯi fa-innā ḫalaqnākum min turābin ṯumma min nuṭfatin ṯumma min ʿalaqatin ṯumma min muḍġatin muḫallaqatin waġayri muḫallaqatin linubayyina lakum wanuqirru fee al-arhami mā našāo ilā ajalin musamman ṯumma nuḫrijukum ṭiflan ṯumma litabluġoo ašuddakum waminkum man yutawaffā waminkum man yuraddu ilā arḏali alʿumuri likaylā yaʿlama min baʿdi ʿilmin šay-an watarā al-arḍa hāmidatan fa-iṯa anzalnā ʿalayhā almāa ihtazzat warabat waanbatat min kulli zawjin baheejin O mankind! if ye are in doubt concerning the Resurrection, then lo! We have created you from dust, then from a drop of seed, then from a clot, then from a little lump of flesh shapely and shapeless, that We may make (it) clear for you. And We cause what We will to remain in the wombs for an appointed time, and afterward We bring you forth as infants, then (give you growth) that ye attain your full strength. And among you there is he who dieth (young), and among you there is he who is brought back to the most abject time of life, so that, after knowledge, he knoweth naught. And thou (Muhammad) seest the earth barren, but when We send down water thereon, it doth thrill and swell and put forth every lovely kind (of growth).
006 [romanization missing] That is because Allah, He is the Truth and because He quickeneth the dead, and because He is Able to do all things;
007 waanna alssāʿata ātiyatun lā rayba feehā waanna allāha yabʿaṯu man fee alquboori And because the Hour will come, there is no doubt thereof; and because Allah will raise those who are in the graves.
008 wamina alnnāsi man yujādilu fee allāhi biġayri ʿilmin walā hudan walā kitābin muneerin And among mankind is he who disputeth concerning Allah without knowledge or guidance or a scripture giving light,
009 thāniya ʿiṭfihi liyuḍilla ʿan sabeeli allāhi lahu fee alddunyā ḫizyun wanuḏeequhu yawma alqiyāmati ʿaṯaba alḥareeqi Turning away in pride to beguile (men) from the way of Allah. For him in this world is ignominy, and on the Day of Resurrection We make him taste the doom of burning.
010 [romanization missing] (And unto him it will be said): This is for that which thy two hands have sent before, and because Allah is no oppressor of His slaves.
011 wamina alnnāsi man yaʿbudu allāha ʿalā ḥarfin fa-in asabahu ḫayrun iṭmaanna bihi wa-in asabat-hu fitnatun inqalaba ʿalā wajhihi ḫasira alddunyā waal-āḫirata ṯalika huwa alḫusrānu almubeenu And among mankind is he who worshippeth Allah upon a narrow marge so that if good befalleth him he is content therewith, but if a trial befalleth him, he falleth away utterly. He loseth both the world and the Hereafter. That is the sheer loss.
012 yadʿoo min dooni allāhi mā lā yaḍurruhu wamā lā yanfaʿuhu ṯalika huwa alḍalālu albaʿeedu He calleth, beside Allah, unto that which hurteth him not nor benefiteth him. That is the far error.
013 yadʿoo laman ḍarruhu aqrabu min nafʿihi labi/sa almawlā walabi/sa alʿašeeru He calleth unto him whose harm is nearer than his benefit; verily an evil patron and verily an evil friend!
014 inna allāha yudḫilu allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati jannātin tajree min taḥtihā al-anhāru inna allāha yafʿalu mā yureedu Lo! Allah causeth those who believe and do good works to enter Gardens underneath which rivers flow. Lo! Allah doth what He intendeth.
015 man kāna yaḏunnu an lan yanṣurahu allāhu fee alddunyā waal-āḫirati falyamdud bisababin ilā alssamā-i ṯumma liyaqṭaʿ falyanḏur hal yuḏhibanna kayduhu mā yaġeeḏu Whoso is wont to think (through envy) that Allah will not give him (Muhammad) victory in the world and the Hereafter (and is enraged at the thought of his victory), let him stretch a rope up to the roof (of his dwelling), and let him hang himself. Then let him see whether his strategy dispelleth that whereat he rageth!.
016 wakaṯalika anzalnāhu āyātin bayyinātin waanna allāha yahdee man yureedu Thus We reveal it as plain revelations, and verily Allah guideth whom He will.
017 inna allāḏeena āmanoo waallaḏeena hādoo waalssabi-eena waalnnasarā waalmajoosa waallaḏeena ašrakoo inna allāha yafṣilu baynahum yawma alqiyāmati inna allāha ʿalā kulli šay-in šaheedun Lo! those who believe (this revelation), and those who are Jews, and the Sabaeans and the Christians and the Magians and the idolaters - Lo! Allah will decide between them on the Day of Resurrection. Lo! Allah is Witness over all things.
018 alam tara anna allāha yasjudu lahu man fee alssamāwāti waman fee al-arḍi waalššamsu waalqamaru waalnnujoomu waaljibālu waalššajaru waalddawābbu wakaṯeerun mina alnnāsi wakaṯeerun ḥaqqa ʿalayhi alʿaṯabu waman yuhini allāhu famā lahu min mukrimin inna allāha yafʿalu mā yašā/o Hast thou not seen that unto Allah payeth adoration whosoever is in the heavens and whosoever is in the earth, and the sun, and the moon, and the stars, and the hills, and the trees, and the beasts, and many of mankind, while there are many unto whom the doom is justly due. He whom Allah scorneth, there is none to give him honour. Lo! Allah doeth what He will.
019 haṯani ḫaṣmāni iḫtaṣamoo fee rabbihim faallaḏeena kafaroo quttiʿat lahum ṯiyābun min nārin yuṣabbu min fawqi ruoosihimu alḥameemu These twain (the believers and the disbelievers) are two opponents who contend concerning their Lord. But as for those who disbelieve, garments of fire will be cut out for them; boiling fluid will be poured down on their heads,
020 yuṣharu bihi mā fee buṭoonihim waaljuloodu Whereby that which is in their bellies, and their skins too, will be melted;
021 walahum maqāmiʿu min ḥadeedin And for them are hooked rods of iron.
022 kullamā arādoo an yaḫrujoo minhā min ġammin oʿeedoo feehā waḏooqoo ʿaṯaba alḥareeqi Whenever, in their anguish, they would go forth from thence they are driven back therein and (it is said unto them): Taste the doom of burning.
023 inna allāha yudḫilu allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati jannātin tajree min taḥtihā al-anhāru yuḥallawna feehā min asāwira min ḏahabin walu/lu-an walibāsuhum feehā ḥareerun Lo! Allah will cause those who believe and do good works to enter Gardens underneath which rivers flow, wherein they will be allowed armlets of gold, and pearls, and their raiment therein will be silk.
024 wahudoo ilā alttayyibi mina alqawli wahudoo ilā ṣirati alḥameedi They are guided unto gentle speech; they are guided unto the path of the Glorious One.
025 inna allāḏeena kafaroo wayaṣuddoona ʿan sabeeli allāhi waalmasjidi alḥarāmi allāḏee jaʿalnāhu lilnnāsi sawāan alʿākifu feehi waalbādi waman yurid feehi bi-ilhadin biḏulmin nuḏiqhu min ʿaṯabin aleemin Lo! those who disbelieve and bar (men) from the way of Allah and from the Inviolable Place of Worship, which We have appointed for mankind together, the dweller therein and the nomad: whosoever seeketh wrongful partiality therein, him We shall cause to taste a painful doom.
026 wa-iḏ bawwa/nā li-ibrāheema makāna albayti an lā tušrik bee šay-an waṭahhir baytiya liltta-ifeena waalqā-imeena waalrrukkaʿi alssujoodi And (remember) when We prepared for Abraham the place of the (holy) House, saying: Ascribe thou no thing as partner unto Me, and purify My House for those who make the round (thereof) and those who stand and those who bow and make prostration.
027 waaṯṯin fee alnnāsi bialḥajji ya/tooka rijālan waʿalā kulli damirin ya/teena min kulli fajjin ʿameeqin And proclaim unto mankind the pilgrimage. They will come unto thee on foot and on every lean camel; they will come from every deep ravine,
028 liyašhadoo manāfiʿa lahum wayaḏkuroo isma allāhi fee ayyāmin maʿloomātin ʿalā mā razaqahum min baheemati al-anʿāmi fakuloo minhā waaṭʿimoo albā-isa alfaqeera That they may witness things that are of benefit to them, and mention the name of Allah on appointed days over the beast of cattle that He hath bestowed upon them. Then eat thereof and feed therewith the poor unfortunate.
029 thumma lyaqḍoo tafaṯahum walyoofoo nuḏoorahum walyattawwafoo bialbayti alʿateeqi Then let them make an end of their unkemptness and pay their vows and go around the ancient House.
030 [romanization missing] That (is the command). And whoso magnifieth the sacred things of Allah, it will be well for him in the sight of his Lord. The cattle are lawful unto you save that which hath been told you. So shun the filth of idols, and shun lying speech,
031 [romanization missing] Turning unto Allah (only), not ascribing partners unto Him; for whoso ascribeth partners unto Allah, it is as if he had fallen from the sky and the birds had snatched him or the wind had blown him to a far-off place.
032 [romanization missing] That (is the command). And whoso magnifieth the offerings consecrated to Allah, it surely is from devotion of the hearts,
033 lakum feehā manāfiʿu ilā ajalin musamman ṯumma maḥilluhā ilā albayti alʿateeqi Therein are benefits for you for an appointed term; and afterward they are brought for sacrifice unto the ancient House.
034 walikulli ommatin jaʿalnā mansakan liyaḏkuroo isma allāhi ʿalā mā razaqahum min baheemati al-anʿāmi fa-ilāhukum ilāhun wahidun falahu aslimoo wabašširi almuḫbiteena And for every nation have We appointed a ritual, that they may mention the name of Allah over the beast of cattle that He hath given them for food; and your God is One God, therefor surrender unto Him. And give good tidings (O Muhammad) to the humble,
035 allāḏeena iṯa ḏukira allāhu wajilat quloobuhum waalssabireena ʿalā mā asabahum waalmuqeemee alṣalāti wamimmā razaqnāhum yunfiqoona Whose hearts fear when Allah is mentioned, and the patient of whatever may befall them, and those who establish worship and who spend of that We have bestowed on them.
036 waalbudna jaʿalnāhā lakum min šaʿā-iri allāhi lakum feehā ḫayrun faoḏkuroo isma allāhi ʿalayhā ṣawāffa fa-iṯa wajabat junoobuhā fakuloo minhā waaṭʿimoo alqāniʿa waalmuʿtarra kaṯalika saḫḫarnāhā lakum laʿallakum taškuroona And the camels! We have appointed them among the ceremonies of Allah. Therein ye have much good. So mention the name of Allah over them when they are drawn up in lines. Then when their flanks fall (dead), eat thereof and feed the beggar and the suppliant. Thus have We made them subject unto you, that haply ye may give thanks.
037 lan yanāla allāha luḥoomuhā walā dimāohā walākin yanāluhu alttaqwā minkum kaṯalika saḫḫarahā lakum litukabbiroo allāha ʿalā mā hadākum wabašširi almuḥsineena Their flesh and their food reach not Allah, but the devotion from you reacheth Him. Thus have We made them subject unto you that ye may magnify Allah that He hath guided you. And give good tidings (O Muhammad) to the good.
038 inna allāha yudāfiʿu ʿani allāḏeena āmanoo inna allāha lā yuḥibbu kulla ḫawwānin kafoorin Lo! Allah defendeth those who are true. Lo! Allah loveth not each treacherous ingrate.
039 oḏina lillaḏeena yuqātaloona bi-annahum ḏulimoo wa-inna allāha ʿalā naṣrihim laqadeerun Sanction is given unto those who fight because they have been wronged; and Allah is indeed Able to give them victory;
040 allāḏeena oḫrijoo min diyārihim biġayri ḥaqqin illā an yaqooloo rabbunā allāhu walawlā dafʿu allāhi alnnāsa baʿḍahum bibaʿḍin lahuddimat ṣawāmiʿu wabiyaʿun waṣalawātun wamasājidu yuḏkaru feehā ismu allāhi kaṯeeran walayanṣuranna allāhu man yanṣuruhu inna allāha laqawiyyun ʿazeezun Those who have been driven from their homes unjustly only because they said: Our Lord is Allah - For had it not been for Allah's repelling some men by means of others, cloisters and churches and oratories and mosques, wherein the name of Allah is oft mentioned, would assuredly have been pulled down. Verily Allah helpeth one who helpeth Him. Lo! Allah is Strong, Almighty -
041 allāḏeena in makkannāhum fee al-arḍi aqāmoo alṣalāta waātawoo alzzakāta waamaroo bialmaʿroofi wanahaw ʿani almunkari walillāhi ʿāqibatu al-omoori Those who, if We give them power in the land, establish worship and pay the poor-due and enjoin kindness and forbid iniquity. And Allah's is the sequel of events.
042 wa-in yukaṯṯibooka faqad kaṯṯabat qablahum qawmu nooḥin waʿādun waṯamoodu If they deny thee (Muhammad), even so the folk of Noah, and (the tribes of) A'ad and Thamud, before thee, denied (Our messengers);
043 waqawmu ibrāheema waqawmu looṭin And the folk of Abraham and the folk of Lot;
044 waaṣ-habu madyana wakuṯṯiba moosā faamlaytu lilkāfireena ṯumma aḫaḏtuhum fakayfa kāna nakeeri (And) the dwellers in Midian. And Moses was denied; but I indulged the disbelievers a long while, then I seized them, and how (terrible) was My abhorrence!
045 fakaayyin min qaryatin ahlaknāhā wahiya ḏālimatun fahiya ḫāwiyatun ʿalā ʿuroošihā wabi/rin muʿattalatin waqaṣrin mašeedin How many a township have We destroyed while it was sinful, so that it lieth (to this day) in ruins, and (how many) a deserted well and lofty tower!
046 afalam yaseeroo fee al-arḍi fatakoona lahum quloobun yaʿqiloona bihā aw aṯanun yasmaʿoona bihā fa-innahā lā taʿmā al-absaru walākin taʿmā alquloobu allātee fee alṣudoori Have they not travelled in the land, and have they hearts wherewith to feel and ears wherewith to hear ? For indeed it is not the eyes that grow blind, but it is the hearts, which are within the bosoms, that grow blind.
047 wayastaʿjiloonaka bialʿaṯabi walan yuḫlifa allāhu waʿdahu wa-inna yawman ʿinda rabbika kaalfi sanatin mimmā taʿuddoona And they will bid thee hasten on the Doom, and Allah faileth not His promise, but lo! a Day with Allah is as a thousand years of what ye reckon.
048 wakaayyin min qaryatin amlaytu lahā wahiya ḏālimatun ṯumma aḫaḏtuhā wa-ilayya almaṣeeru And how many a township did I suffer long though it was sinful! Then I grasped it. Unto Me is the return.
049 qul yā ayyuhā alnnāsu innamā anā lakum naḏeerun mubeenun Say: O mankind! I am only a plain warner unto you.
050 faallaḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati lahum maġfiratun warizqun kareemun Those who believe and do good works, for them is pardon and a rich provision;
051 waallaḏeena saʿaw fee āyātinā muʿājizeena olā-ika aṣ-habu aljaḥeemi While those who strive to thwart Our revelations, such are rightful owners of the Fire.
052 wamā arsalnā min qablika min rasoolin walā nabiyyin illā iṯa tamannā alqā alššaytanu fee omniyyatihi fayansaḫu allāhu mā yulqee alššaytanu ṯumma yuḥkimu allāhu āyātihi waallāhu ʿaleemun ḥakeemun Never sent We a messenger or a prophet before thee but when He recited (the message) Satan proposed (opposition) in respect of that which he recited thereof. But Allah abolisheth that which Satan proposeth. Then Allah establisheth His revelations. Allah is Knower, Wise;
053 liyajʿala mā yulqee alššaytanu fitnatan lillaḏeena fee quloobihim maraḍun waalqāsiyati quloobuhum wa-inna alṯṯālimeena lafee šiqāqin baʿeedin That He may make that which the devil proposeth a temptation for those in whose hearts is a disease, and those whose hearts are hardened - Lo! the evil-doers are in open schism -
054 waliyaʿlama allāḏeena ootoo alʿilma annahu alḥaqqu min rabbika fayu/minoo bihi fatuḫbita lahu quloobuhum wa-inna allāha lahādi allāḏeena āmanoo ilā ṣiratin mustaqeemin And that those who have been given knowledge may know that it is the truth from thy Lord, so that they may believe therein and their hearts may submit humbly unto Him. Lo! Allah verily is guiding those who believe unto a right path.
055 walā yazālu allāḏeena kafaroo fee miryatin minhu ḥattā ta/tiyahumu alssāʿatu baġtatan aw ya/tiyahum ʿaṯabu yawmin ʿaqeemin And those who disbelieve will not cease to be in doubt thereof until the Hour come upon them unawares, or there come unto them the doom of a disastrous day.
056 almulku yawma-iḏin lillāhi yaḥkumu baynahum faallaḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati fee jannāti alnnaʿeemi The Sovereignty on that day will be Allah's, He will judge between them. Then those who believed and did good works will be in Gardens of Delight,
057 waallaḏeena kafaroo wakaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā faolā-ika lahum ʿaṯabun muheenun While those who disbelieved and denied Our revelations, for them will be a shameful doom.
058 waallaḏeena hājaroo fee sabeeli allāhi ṯumma qutiloo aw mātoo layarzuqannahumu allāhu rizqan ḥasanan wa-inna allāha lahuwa ḫayru alrrāziqeena Those who fled their homes for the cause of Allah and then were slain or died, Allah verily will provide for them a good provision. Lo! Allah, He verily is Best of all who make provision.
059 layudḫilannahum mudḫalan yarḍawnahu wa-inna allāha laʿaleemun ḥaleemun Assuredly He will cause them to enter by an entry that they will love. Lo! Allah verily is Knower, Indulgent.
060 [romanization missing] That (is so). And whoso hath retaliated with the like of that which he was made to suffer and then hath (again) been wronged, Allah will succour him. Lo! Allah verily is Mild, Forgiving.
061 [romanization missing] That is because Allah maketh the night to pass into the day and maketh the day to pass into the night, and because Allah is Hearer, Seer.
062 [romanization missing] That is because Allah, He is the True, and that whereon they call instead of Him, it is the false, and because Allah, He is the High, the Great.
063 alam tara anna allāha anzala mina alssamā-i māan fatuṣbiḥu al-arḍu muḫḍarratan inna allāha laṭeefun ḫabeerun Seest thou not how Allah sendeth down water from the sky and then the earth becometh green upon the morrow ? Lo! Allah is Subtile, Aware.
064 lahu mā fee alssamāwāti wamā fee al-arḍi wa-inna allāha lahuwa alġaniyyu alḥameedu Unto Him belongeth all that is in the heavens and all that is in the earth. Lo! Allah, He verily is the Absolute, the Owner of Praise.
065 alam tara anna allāha saḫḫara lakum mā fee al-arḍi waalfulka tajree fee albaḥri bi-amrihi wayumsiku alssamāa an taqaʿa ʿalā al-arḍi illā bi-iḏnihi inna allāha bialnnāsi laraoofun raḥeemun Hast thou not seen how Allah hath made all that is in the earth subservient unto you ? And the ship runneth upon the sea by His command, and He holdeth back the heaven from falling on the earth unless by His leave. Lo! Allah is, for mankind, Full of Pity, Merciful.
066 wahuwa allāḏee aḥyākum ṯumma yumeetukum ṯumma yuḥyeekum inna al-insāna lakafoorun And He it is Who gave you life, then He will cause you to die, and then will give you life (again). Lo! man is verily an ingrate.
067 likulli ommatin jaʿalnā mansakan hum nāsikoohu falā yunāziʿunnaka fee al-amri waodʿu ilā rabbika innaka laʿalā hudan mustaqeemin Unto each nation have We given sacred rites which they are to perform; so let them not dispute with thee of the matter, but summon thou unto thy Lord. Lo! thou indeed followest right guidance.
068 wa-in jādalooka faquli allāhu aʿlamu bimā taʿmaloona And if they wrangle with thee, say: Allah is Best Aware of what ye do.
069 allāhu yaḥkumu baynakum yawma alqiyāmati feemā kuntum feehi taḫtalifoona Allah will judge between you on the Day of Resurrection concerning that wherein ye used to differ.
070 alam taʿlam anna allāha yaʿlamu mā fee alssamā-i waal-arḍi inna ṯalika fee kitābin inna ṯalika ʿalā allāhi yaseerun Hast thou not known that Allah knoweth all that is in the heaven and the earth ? Lo! it is in a record. Lo! that is easy for Allah.
071 wayaʿbudoona min dooni allāhi mā lam yunazzil bihi sultanan wamā laysa lahum bihi ʿilmun wamā lilṯṯālimeena min naṣeerin And they worship instead of Allah that for which He hath sent down no warrant, and that whereof they have no knowledge. For evil-doers there is no helper.
072 wa-iṯa tutlā ʿalayhim āyātunā bayyinātin taʿrifu fee wujoohi allāḏeena kafaroo almunkara yakādoona yasṭoona biallaḏeena yatloona ʿalayhim āyātinā qul afaonabbi-okum bišarrin min ṯalikum alnnāru waʿadahā allāhu allāḏeena kafaroo wabi/sa almaṣeeru And when Our revelations are recited unto them, thou knowest the denial in the faces of those who disbelieve; they all but attack those who recite Our revelations unto them. Say: Shall I proclaim unto you worse than that ? The Fire! Allah hath promised it for those who disbelieve. A hapless journey's end!
073 yā ayyuhā alnnāsu ḍuriba maṯalun faistamiʿoo lahu inna allāḏeena tadʿoona min dooni allāhi lan yaḫluqoo ḏubāban walawi ijtamaʿoo lahu wa-in yaslubuhumu alṯṯubābu šay-an lā yastanqiḏoohu minhu ḍaʿufa alttalibu waalmaṭloobu O mankind! A similitude is coined, so pay ye heed to it: Lo! those on whom ye call beside Allah will never create a fly though they combine together for the purpose. And if the fly took something from them, they could not rescue it from it. So weak are (both) the seeker and the sought!
074 mā qadaroo allāha ḥaqqa qadrihi inna allāha laqawiyyun ʿazeezun They measure not Allah His rightful measure. Lo! Allah is Strong, Almighty.
075 allāhu yastafee mina almalā-ikati rusulan wamina alnnāsi inna allāha sameeʿun baṣeerun Allah chooseth from the angels messengers, and (also) from mankind. Lo! Allah is Hearer, Seer.
076 yaʿlamu mā bayna aydeehim wamā ḫalfahum wa-ilā allāhi turjaʿu al-omooru He knoweth all that is before them and all that is behind them, and unto Allah all things are returned.
077 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo irkaʿoo waosjudoo waoʿbudoo rabbakum waifʿaloo alḫayra laʿallakum tufliḥoona O ye who believe! Bow down and prostrate yourselves, and worship your Lord, and do good, that haply ye may prosper.
