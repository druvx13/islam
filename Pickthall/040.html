40. al-Mu'min: The Forgiving One

001 [romanization missing] Ha. Mim.
002 tanzeelu alkitābi mina allāhi alʿazeezi alʿaleemi The revelation of the Scripture is from Allah, the Mighty, the Knower,
003 ghāfiri alṯṯanbi waqābili alttawbi šadeedi alʿiqābi ḏee alttawli lā ilāha illā huwa ilayhi almaṣeeru The Forgiver of sin, the Accepter of repentance, the Stern in punishment, the Bountiful. There is no God save Him. Unto Him is the journeying.
004 mā yujādilu fee āyāti allāhi illā allāḏeena kafaroo falā yaġrurka taqallubuhum fee albilādi None argue concerning the revelations of Allah save those who disbelieve, so let not their turn of fortune in the land deceive thee (O Muhammad).
005 kaṯṯabat qablahum qawmu nooḥin waal-aḥzābu min baʿdihim wahammat kullu ommatin birasoolihim liya/ḫuḏoohu wajādaloo bialbatili liyudḥiḍoo bihi alḥaqqa faaḫaḏtuhum fakayfa kāna ʿiqābi The folk of Noah and the factions after them denied (their messengers) before these, and every nation purposed to seize their messenger and argued falsely, (thinking) thereby to refute the Truth. Then I seized them, and how (awful) was My punishment.
006 wakaṯalika ḥaqqat kalimatu rabbika ʿalā allāḏeena kafaroo annahum aṣ-habu alnnāri Thus was the word of thy Lord concerning those who disbelieve fulfilled: That they are owners of the Fire.
007 allāḏeena yaḥmiloona alʿarša waman ḥawlahu yusabbiḥoona biḥamdi rabbihim wayu/minoona bihi wayastaġfiroona lillaḏeena āmanoo rabbanā wasiʿta kulla šay-in raḥmatan waʿilman faiġfir lillaḏeena tāboo waittabaʿoo sabeelaka waqihim ʿaṯaba aljaḥeemi Those who bear the Throne, and all who are round about it, hymn the praises of their Lord and believe in Him and ask forgiveness for those who believe (saying): Our Lord! Thou comprehendest all things in mercy and knowledge, therefor forgive those who repent and follow Thy way. Ward off from them the punishment of hell.
008 rabbanā waadḫilhum jannāti ʿadnin allātee waʿadtahum waman ṣalaḥa min ābā-ihim waazwājihim waḏurriyyātihim innaka anta alʿazeezu alḥakeemu Our Lord! And make them enter the Gardens of Eden which thou hast promised them, with such of their fathers and their wives and their descendants as do right. Lo! Thou, only Thou, art the Mighty, the Wise.
009 waqihimu alssayyi-āti waman taqi alssayyi-āti yawma-iḏin faqad raḥimtahu waṯalika huwa alfawzu alʿaḏeemu And ward off from them ill-deeds; and he from whom Thou wardest off ill- deeds that day, him verily hast Thou taken into mercy. That is the supreme triumph.
010 inna allāḏeena kafaroo yunādawna lamaqtu allāhi akbaru min maqtikum anfusakum iḏ tudʿawna ilā al-eemāni fatakfuroona Lo! (on that day) those who disbelieve are informed by proclamation: Verily Allah's abhorrence is more terrible than your abhorrence one of another, when ye were called unto the faith but did refuse.
011 qāloo rabbanā amattanā iṯnatayni waaḥyaytanā iṯnatayni faiʿtarafnā biḏunoobinā fahal ilā ḫuroojin min sabeelin They say: Our Lord! Twice hast Thou made us die, and twice hast Thou made us live. Now we confess our sins. Is there any way to go out ?
012 [romanization missing] (It is said unto them): This is (your plight) because, when Allah only was invoked, ye disbelieved, but when some partner was ascribed to Him ye were believing. But the command belongeth only to Allah, the Sublime, the Majestic.
013 huwa allāḏee yureekum āyātihi wayunazzilu lakum mina alssamā-i rizqan wamā yataḏakkaru illā man yuneebu He it is Who showeth you His portents, and sendeth down for you provision from the sky. None payeth heed save him who turneth (unto Him) repentant.
014 faodʿoo allāha muḫliṣeena lahu alddeena walaw kariha alkāfiroona Therefor (O believers) pray unto Allah, making religion pure for Him (only), however much the disbelievers be averse -
015 rafeeʿu alddarajāti ḏoo alʿarši yulqee alrrooḥa min amrihi ʿalā man yašāo min ʿibādihi liyunḏira yawma alttalāqi The Exalter of Ranks, the Lord of the Throne. He causeth the Spirit of His command upon whom He will of His slaves, that He may warn of the Day of Meeting,
016 yawma hum bārizoona lā yaḫfā ʿalā allāhi minhum šay-on limani almulku alyawma lillāhi alwahidi alqahhāri The day when they come forth, nothing of them being hidden from Allah. Whose is the Sovereignty this day ? It is Allah's, the One, the Almighty.
017 alyawma tujzā kullu nafsin bimā kasabat lā ḏulma alyawma inna allāha sareeʿu alḥisābi This day is each soul requited that which it hath earned; no wrong (is done) this day. Lo! Allah is swift at reckoning.
018 waanḏirhum yawma al-āzifati iḏi alquloobu ladā alḥanājiri kāḏimeena mā lilṯṯālimeena min ḥameemin walā šafeeʿin yutaʿu Warn them (O Muhammad) of the Day of the approaching (doom), when the hearts will be choking the throats, (when) there will be no friend for the wrong-doers, nor any intercessor who will be heard.
019 yaʿlamu ḫā-inata al-aʿyuni wamā tuḫfee alṣudooru He knoweth the traitor of the eyes, and that which the bosoms hide.
020 waallāhu yaqḍee bialḥaqqi waallaḏeena yadʿoona min doonihi lā yaqḍoona bišay-in inna allāha huwa alssameeʿu albaṣeeru Allah judgeth with truth, while those to whom they cry instead of Him judge not at all. Lo! Allah, He is the Hearer, the Seer.
021 awa lam yaseeroo fee al-arḍi fayanḏuroo kayfa kāna ʿāqibatu allāḏeena kānoo min qablihim kānoo hum ašadda minhum quwwatan waāṯāran fee al-arḍi faaḫaḏahumu allāhu biḏunoobihim wamā kāna lahum mina allāhi min wāqin Have they not travelled in the land to see the nature of the consequence for those who disbelieved before them ? They were mightier than these in power and (in the) traces (which they left behind them) in the earth. Yet Allah seized them for their sins, and they had no protector from Allah.
022 [romanization missing] That was because their messengers kept bringing them clear proofs (of Allah's Sovereignty) but they disbelieved; so Allah seized them. Lo! He is Strong, severe in punishment.
023 walaqad arsalnā moosā bi-āyātinā wasultanin mubeenin And verily We sent Moses with Our revelations and a clear warrant
024 ilā firʿawna wahāmāna waqāroona faqāloo sahirun kaṯṯabun Unto Pharaoh and Haman and Korah, but they said: A lying sorcerer!
025 falammā jāahum bialḥaqqi min ʿindinā qāloo oqtuloo abnāa allāḏeena āmanoo maʿahu waistaḥyoo nisāahum wamā kaydu alkāfireena illā fee ḍalālin And when he brought them the Truth from Our presence, they said: Slay the sons of those who believe with him, and spare their women. But the plot of disbelievers is in naught but error.
026 waqāla firʿawnu ḏaroonee aqtul moosā walyadʿu rabbahu innee aḫāfu an yubaddila deenakum aw an yuḏhira fee al-arḍi alfasāda And Pharaoh said: Suffer me to kill Moses, and let him cry unto his Lord. Lo! I fear that he will alter your religion or that he will cause confusion in the land.
027 waqāla moosā innee ʿuḏtu birabbee warabbikum min kulli mutakabbirin lā yu/minu biyawmi alḥisābi Moses said: Lo! I seek refuge in my Lord and your Lord from every scorner who believeth not in a Day of Reckoning.
028 waqāla rajulun mu/minun min āli firʿawna yaktumu eemānahu ataqtuloona rajulan an yaqoola rabbiyya allāhu waqad jāakum bialbayyināti min rabbikum wa-in yaku kaṯiban faʿalayhi kaḏibuhu wa-in yaku sadiqan yuṣibkum baʿḍu allāḏee yaʿidukum inna allāha lā yahdee man huwa musrifun kaṯṯabun And a believing man of Pharaoh's family, who hid his faith, said: Would ye kill a man because he saith: My Lord is Allah, and hath brought you clear proofs from your Lord ? If he is lying, then his lie is upon him; and if he is truthful, then some of that wherewith he threateneth you will strike you. Lo! Allah guideth not one who is a prodigal, a liar.
029 yā qawmi lakumu almulku alyawma ḏāhireena fee al-arḍi faman yanṣurunā min ba/si allāhi in jāanā qāla firʿawnu mā oreekum illā mā arā wamā ahdeekum illā sabeela alrrašādi O my people! Yours is the kingdom to-day, ye being uppermost in the land. But who would save us from the wrath of Allah should it reach us ? Pharaoh said: I do but show you what I think, and I do but guide you to wise policy.
030 waqāla allāḏee āmana yā qawmi innee aḫāfu ʿalaykum miṯla yawmi al-aḥzābi And he who believed said: O my people! Lo! I fear for you a fate like that of the factions (of old);
031 miṯla da/bi qawmi nooḥin waʿādin waṯamooda waallaḏeena min baʿdihim wamā allāhu yureedu ḏulman lilʿibādi A plight like that of Noah's folk, and A'ad and Thamud, and those after them, and Allah willeth no injustice for (His) slaves.
032 wayā qawmi innee aḫāfu ʿalaykum yawma alttanādi And, O my people! Lo! I fear for you a Day of Summoning,
033 yawma tuwalloona mudbireena mā lakum mina allāhi min ʿasimin waman yuḍlili allāhu famā lahu min hādin A day when ye will turn to flee, having no preserver from Allah: and he whom Allah sendeth astray, for him there is no guide.
034 walaqad jāakum yoosufu min qablu bialbayyināti famā ziltum fee šakkin mimmā jāakum bihi ḥattā iṯa halaka qultum lan yabʿaṯa allāhu min baʿdihi rasoolan kaṯalika yuḍillu allāhu man huwa musrifun murtābun And verily Joseph brought you of old clear proofs, yet ye ceased not to be in doubt concerning what he brought you till, when he died, ye said: Allah will not send any messenger after him. Thus Allah deceiveth him who is a prodigal, a doubter.
035 allāḏeena yujādiloona fee āyāti allāhi biġayri sultanin atāhum kabura maqtan ʿinda allāhi waʿinda allāḏeena āmanoo kaṯalika yaṭbaʿu allāhu ʿalā kulli qalbi mutakabbirin jabbārin Those who wrangle concerning the revelations of Allah without any warrant that hath come unto them, it is greatly hateful in the sight of Allah and in the sight of those who believe. Thus doth Allah print on every arrogant, disdainful heart.
036 waqāla firʿawnu yā hāmānu ibni lee ṣarḥan laʿallee abluġu al-asbāba And Pharaoh said: O Haman! Build for me a tower that haply I may reach the roads,
037 asbāba alssamāwāti faattaliʿa ilā ilāhi moosā wa-innee laaḏunnuhu kaṯiban wakaṯalika zuyyina lifirʿawna soo-o ʿamalihi waṣudda ʿani alssabeeli wamā kaydu firʿawna illā fee tabābin The roads of the heavens, and may look upon the God of Moses, though verily I think him a liar. Thus was the evil that he did made fairseeming unto Pharaoh, and he was debarred from the (right) way. The plot of Pharaoh ended but in ruin.
038 waqāla allāḏee āmana yā qawmi ittabiʿooni ahdikum sabeela alrrašādi And he who believed said: O my people! Follow me. I will show you the way of right conduct.
039 yā qawmi innamā haṯihi alḥayātu alddunyā matāʿun wa-inna al-āḫirata hiya dāru alqarāri O my people! Lo! this life of the world is but a passing comfort, and lo! the Hereafter, that is the enduring home.
040 man ʿamila sayyi-atan falā yujzā illā miṯlahā waman ʿamila saliḥan min ḏakarin aw onṯā wahuwa mu/minun faolā-ika yadḫuloona aljannata yurzaqoona feehā biġayri ḥisābin Whoso doeth an ill-deed, he will be repaid the like thereof, while whoso doeth right, whether male or female, and is a believer, (all) such will enter the Garden, where they will be nourished without stint.
041 wayā qawmi mālee adʿookum ilā alnnajāti watadʿoonanee ilā alnnāri And, O my people! What aileth me that I call you unto deliverance when ye call me unto the Fire ?
042 tadʿoonanee li-akfura biallāhi waošrika bihi mā laysa lee bihi ʿilmun waanā adʿookum ilā alʿazeezi alġaffāri Ye call me to disbelieve in Allah and ascribe unto Him as partners that whereof I have no knowledge, while I call you unto the Mighty, the Forgiver.
043 lā jarama annamā tadʿoonanee ilayhi laysa lahu daʿwatun fee alddunyā walā fee al-āḫirati waanna maraddanā ilā allāhi waanna almusrifeena hum aṣ-habu alnnāri Assuredly that whereunto ye call me hath no claim in the world or in the Hereafter, and our return will be unto Allah, and the prodigals will be owners of the Fire.
044 fasataḏkuroona mā aqoolu lakum waofawwiḍu amree ilā allāhi inna allāha baṣeerun bialʿibādi And ye will remember what I say unto you. I confide my cause unto Allah. Lo! Allah is Seer of (His) slaves.
045 fawaqāhu allāhu sayyi-āti mā makaroo wahaqa bi-āli firʿawna soo-o alʿaṯabi So Allah warded off from him the evils which they plotted, while a dreadful doom encompassed Pharaoh's folk,
046 alnnāru yuʿraḍoona ʿalayhā ġuduwwan waʿašiyyan wayawma taqoomu alssāʿatu adḫiloo āla firʿawna ašadda alʿaṯabi The Fire; they are exposed to it morning and evening; and on the day when the Hour upriseth (it is said): Cause Pharaoh's folk to enter the most awful doom.
047 wa-iḏ yatahajjoona fee alnnāri fayaqoolu alḍuʿafāo lillaḏeena istakbaroo innā kunnā lakum tabaʿan fahal antum muġnoona ʿannā naṣeeban mina alnnāri And when they wrangle in the Fire, the weak say unto those who were proud: Lo! we were a following unto you; will ye therefor rid us of a portion of the Fire ?
048 qāla allāḏeena istakbaroo innā kullun feehā inna allāha qad ḥakama bayna alʿibādi Those who were proud say: Lo! we are all (together) herein. Lo! Allah hath judged between (His) slaves.
049 waqāla allāḏeena fee alnnāri liḫazanati jahannama odʿoo rabbakum yuḫaffif ʿannā yawman mina alʿaṯabi And those in the Fire say unto the guards of hell: Entreat your Lord that He relieve us of a day of the torment.
050 qāloo awa lam taku ta/teekum rusulukum bialbayyināti qāloo balā qāloo faodʿoo wamā duʿāo alkāfireena illā fee ḍalālin They say: Came not your messengers unto you with clear proofs ? They say: Yea, verily. They say: Then do ye pray, although the prayer of disbelievers is in vain.
051 innā lananṣuru rusulanā waallaḏeena āmanoo fee alḥayāti alddunyā wayawma yaqoomu al-ašhādu Lo! We verily do help Our messengers, and those who believe, in the life of the world and on the day when the witnesses arise,
052 yawma lā yanfaʿu alṯṯālimeena maʿḏiratuhum walahumu allāʿnatu walahum soo-o alddāri The day when their excuse availeth not the evil-doers, and theirs is the curse, and theirs the ill abode.
053 walaqad ātaynā moosā alhudā waawraṯnā banee isrā-eela alkitāba And We verily gave Moses the guidance, and We caused the Children of Israel to inherit the Scripture,
054 hudan waḏikrā li-olee al-albābi A guide and a reminder for men of understanding.
055 faiṣbir inna waʿda allāhi ḥaqqun waistaġfir liḏanbika wasabbiḥ biḥamdi rabbika bialʿašiyyi waal-ibkāri Then have patience (O Muhammad). Lo! the promise of Allah is true. And ask forgiveness of thy sin, and hymn the praise of thy Lord at fall of night and in the early hours.
056 inna allāḏeena yujādiloona fee āyāti allāhi biġayri sultanin atāhum in fee ṣudoorihim illā kibrun mā hum bibāliġeehi faistaʿiḏ biallāhi innahu huwa alssameeʿu albaṣeeru Lo! those who wrangle concerning the revelations of Allah without a warrant having come unto them, there is naught else in their breasts save pride which they will never attain. So take thou refuge in Allah. Lo! He, only He, is the Hearer, the Seer.
057 laḫalqu alssamāwāti waal-arḍi akbaru min ḫalqi alnnāsi walākinna akṯara alnnāsi lā yaʿlamoona Assuredly the creation of the heavens and the earth is greater than the creation of mankind; but most of mankind know not.
058 wamā yastawee al-aʿmā waalbaṣeeru waallaḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati walā almusee-o qaleelan mā tataḏakkaroona And the blind man and the seer are not equal, neither are those who believe and do good works (equal with) the evil-doer. Little do ye reflect!
059 inna alssāʿata laātiyatun lā rayba feehā walākinna akṯara alnnāsi lā yu/minoona Lo! the Hour is surely coming, there is no doubt thereof; yet most of mankind believe not.
060 waqāla rabbukumu odʿoonee astajib lakum inna allāḏeena yastakbiroona ʿan ʿibādatee sayadḫuloona jahannama dāḫireena And your Lord hath said: Pray unto Me and I will hear your prayer. Lo! those who scorn My service, they will enter hell, disgraced.
061 allāhu allāḏee jaʿala lakumu allāyla litaskunoo feehi waalnnahāra mubṣiran inna allāha laḏoo faḍlin ʿalā alnnāsi walākinna akṯara alnnāsi lā yaškuroona Allah it is Who hath appointed for you night that ye may rest therein, and day for seeing. Lo! Allah is a Lord of bounty for mankind, yet most of mankind give not thanks.
062 [romanization missing] Such is Allah, your Lord, the Creator of all things, There is no God save Him. How then are ye perverted ?
063 kaṯalika yu/faku allāḏeena kānoo bi-āyāti allāhi yajḥadoona Thus are they perverted who deny the revelations of Allah.
064 allāhu allāḏee jaʿala lakumu al-arḍa qarāran waalssamāa bināan waṣawwarakum faaḥsana ṣuwarakum warazaqakum mina alttayyibāti ṯalikumu allāhu rabbukum fatabāraka allāhu rabbu alʿālameena Allah it is Who appointed for you the earth for a dwelling-place and the sky for a canopy, and fashioned you and perfected your shapes, and hath provided you with good things. Such is Allah, your Lord. Then blessed be Allah, the Lord of the Worlds!
065 huwa alḥayyu lā ilāha illā huwa faodʿoohu muḫliṣeena lahu alddeena alḥamdu lillāhi rabbi alʿālameena He is the Living One. There is no God save Him. So pray unto Him, making religion pure for Him (only). Praise be to Allah, the Lord of the Worlds!
066 qul innee nuheetu an aʿbuda allāḏeena tadʿoona min dooni allāhi lammā jāaniya albayyinātu min rabbee waomirtu an oslima lirabbi alʿālameena Say (O Muhammad): I am forbidden to worship those unto whom ye cry beside Allah since there have come unto me clear proofs from my Lord, and I am commanded to surrender to the Lord of the Worlds.
067 huwa allāḏee ḫalaqakum min turābin ṯumma min nuṭfatin ṯumma min ʿalaqatin ṯumma yuḫrijukum ṭiflan ṯumma litabluġoo ašuddakum ṯumma litakoonoo šuyooḫan waminkum man yutawaffā min qablu walitabluġoo ajalan musamman walaʿallakum taʿqiloona He it is Who created you from dust, then from a drop (of seed) then from a clot, then bringeth you forth as a child, then (ordaineth) that ye attain full strength and afterward that ye become old men - though some among you die before - and that ye reach an appointed term, that haply ye may understand.
068 huwa allāḏee yuḥyee wayumeetu fa-iṯa qada amran fa-innamā yaqoolu lahu kun fayakoonu He it is Who quickeneth and giveth death. When He ordaineth a thing, He saith unto it only: Be! and it is.
069 alam tara ilā allāḏeena yujādiloona fee āyāti allāhi annā yuṣrafoona Hast thou not seen those who wrangle concerning the revelations of Allah, how they are turned away ? -
070 allāḏeena kaṯṯaboo bialkitābi wabimā arsalnā bihi rusulanā fasawfa yaʿlamoona Those who deny the Scripture and that wherewith We send Our messengers. But they will come to know,
071 iḏi al-aġlālu fee aʿnāqihim waalssalāsilu yusḥaboona When carcans are about their necks and chains. They are dragged
072 fee alḥameemi ṯumma fee alnnāri yusjaroona Through boiling waters; then they are thrust into the Fire.
073 thumma qeela lahum ayna mā kuntum tušrikoona Then it is said unto them: Where are (all) that ye used to make partners (in the Sovereignty)
074 min dooni allāhi qāloo ḍalloo ʿannā bal lam nakun nadʿoo min qablu šay-an kaṯalika yuḍillu allāhu alkāfireena Beside Allah ? They say: They have failed us; but we used not to pray to anything before. Thus doth Allah send astray the disbelievers (in His guidance).
075 [romanization missing] (And it is said unto them): This is because ye exulted in the earth without right, and because ye were petulant.
076 odḫuloo abwāba jahannama ḫālideena feehā fabi/sa maṯwā almutakabbireena Enter ye the gates of hell, to dwell therein. Evil is the habitation of the scornful.
077 faiṣbir inna waʿda allāhi ḥaqqun fa-immā nuriyannaka baʿḍa allāḏee naʿiduhum aw natawaffayannaka fa-ilaynā yurjaʿoona Then have patience (O Muhammad). Lo! the promise of Allah is true. And whether we let thee see a part of that which We promise them, or (whether) We cause thee to die, still unto Us they will be brought back.
078 walaqad arsalnā rusulan min qablika minhum man qaṣaṣnā ʿalayka waminhum man lam naqṣuṣ ʿalayka wamā kāna lirasoolin an ya/tiya bi-āyatin illā bi-iḏni allāhi fa-iṯa jāa amru allāhi quḍiya bialḥaqqi waḫasira hunālika almubṭiloona Verily We sent messengers before thee, among them those of whom We have told thee, and some of whom We have not told thee; and it was not given to any messenger that he should bring a portent save by Allah's leave, but when Allah's commandment cometh (the cause) is judged aright, and the followers of vanity will then be lost.
079 allāhu allāḏee jaʿala lakumu al-anʿāma litarkaboo minhā waminhā ta/kuloona Allah it is Who hath appointed for you cattle, that ye may ride on some of them, and eat of some -
080 walakum feehā manāfiʿu walitabluġoo ʿalayhā hajatan fee ṣudoorikum waʿalayhā waʿalā alfulki tuḥmaloona (Many) benefits ye have from them - and that ye may satisfy by their means a need that is in your breasts, and may be borne upon them as upon the ship.
081 wayureekum āyātihi faayya āyāti allāhi tunkiroona And He showeth you His tokens. Which, then, of the tokens of Allah do ye deny ?
082 afalam yaseeroo fee al-arḍi fayanḏuroo kayfa kāna ʿāqibatu allāḏeena min qablihim kānoo akṯara minhum waašadda quwwatan waāṯāran fee al-arḍi famā aġnā ʿanhum mā kānoo yaksiboona Have they not travelled in the land to see the nature of the consequence for those before them ? They were more numerous than these, and mightier in power and (in the) traces (which they left behind them) in the earth. But all that they used to earn availed them not.
083 falammā jāat-hum rusuluhum bialbayyināti fariḥoo bimā ʿindahum mina alʿilmi wahaqa bihim mā kānoo bihi yastahzi-oona And when their messengers brought them clear proofs (of Allah's Sovereignty) they exulted in the knowledge they (themselves) possessed.
