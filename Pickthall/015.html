15. al-Hijr: The Rock

001 alif-lām-rā tilka āyātu alkitābi waqur-ānin mubeenin Alif. Lam. Ra. These are verses of the Scripture and a plain Reading.
002 rubamā yawaddu allāḏeena kafaroo law kānoo muslimeena It may be that those who disbelieve wish ardently that they were Muslims.
003 [romanization missing] Let them eat and enjoy life, and let (false) hope beguile them. They will come to know!
004 wamā ahlaknā min qaryatin illā walahā kitābun maʿloomun And We destroyed no township but there was a known decree for it.
005 mā tasbiqu min ommatin ajalahā wamā yasta/ḫiroona No nation can outstrip its term nor can they lag behind.
006 waqāloo yā ayyuhā allāḏee nuzzila ʿalayhi alṯṯikru innaka lamajnoonun And they say: O thou unto whom the Reminder is revealed, lo! thou art indeed a madman!
007 law mā ta/teenā bialmalā-ikati in kunta mina alssadiqeena Why bringest thou not angels unto us, if thou art of the truthful ?
008 mā nunazzilu almalā-ikata illā bialḥaqqi wamā kānoo iḏan munḏareena We send not down the angels save with the Fact, and in that case (the disbelievers) would not be tolerated.
009 innā naḥnu nazzalnā alṯṯikra wa-innā lahu lahafiḏoona Lo! We, even We, reveal the Reminder, and lo! We verily are its Guardian.
010 walaqad arsalnā min qablika fee šiyaʿi al-awwaleena We verily sent (messengers) before thee among the factions of the men of old.
011 wamā ya/teehim min rasoolin illā kānoo bihi yastahzi-oona And never came there unto them a messenger but they did mock him.
012 kaṯalika naslukuhu fee quloobi almujrimeena Thus do We make it traverse the hearts of the guilty:
013 lā yu/minoona bihi waqad ḫalat sunnatu al-awwaleena They believe not therein, though the example of the men of old hath gone before.
014 walaw fataḥnā ʿalayhim bāban mina alssamā-i faḏalloo feehi yaʿrujoona And even if We opened unto them a gate of heaven and they kept mounting through it,
015 laqāloo innamā sukkirat absarunā bal naḥnu qawmun masḥooroona They would say: Our sight is wrong - nay, but we are folk bewitched.
016 walaqad jaʿalnā fee alssamā-i buroojan wazayyannāhā lilnnāḏireena And verily in the heaven we have set mansions of the stars, and We have beautified it for beholders.
017 waḥafiḏnāhā min kulli šaytanin rajeemin And We have guarded it from every outcast devil,
018 illā mani istaraqa alssamʿa faatbaʿahu šihābun mubeenun Save him who stealeth the hearing, and them doth a clear flame pursue.
019 waal-arḍa madadnāhā waalqaynā feehā rawāsiya waanbatnā feehā min kulli šay-in mawzoonin And the earth have We spread out, and placed therein firm hills, and caused each seemly thing to grow therein.
020 wajaʿalnā lakum feehā maʿāyiša waman lastum lahu birāziqeena And we have given unto you livelihoods therein, and unto those for whom ye provide not.
021 wa-in min šay-in illā ʿindanā ḫazā-inuhu wamā nunazziluhu illā biqadarin maʿloomin And there is not a thing but with Us are the stores thereof. And we send it not down save in appointed measure.
022 waarsalnā alrriyaha lawāqiḥa faanzalnā mina alssamā-i māan faasqaynākumoohu wamā antum lahu biḫāzineena And We send the winds fertilising, and cause water to descend from the sky, and give it you to drink. It is not ye who are the holders of the store thereof.
023 wa-innā lanaḥnu nuḥyee wanumeetu wanaḥnu alwāriṯoona Lo! and it is We, even We, Who quicken and give death, and We are the Inheritor.
024 walaqad ʿalimnā almustaqdimeena minkum walaqad ʿalimnā almusta/ḫireena And verily We know the eager among you and verily We know the laggards.
025 wa-inna rabbaka huwa yaḥšuruhum innahu ḥakeemun ʿaleemun Lo! thy Lord will gather them together. Lo! He is Wise, Aware.
026 walaqad ḫalaqnā al-insāna min ṣalsalin min ḥama-in masnoonin Verily We created man of potter's clay of black mud altered,
027 waaljānna ḫalaqnāhu min qablu min nāri alssamoomi And the jinn did We create aforetime of essential fire.
028 wa-iḏ qāla rabbuka lilmalā-ikati innee ḫāliqun bašaran min ṣalsalin min ḥama-in masnoonin And (remember) when thy Lord said unto the angels: Lo! I am creating a mortal out of potter's clay of black mud altered,
029 fa-iṯa sawwaytuhu wanafaḫtu feehi min rooḥee faqaʿoo lahu sājideena So, when I have made him and have breathed into him of My Spirit, do ye fall down, prostrating yourselves unto him.
030 fasajada almalā-ikatu kulluhum ajmaʿoona So the angels fell prostrate, all of them together
031 illā ibleesa abā an yakoona maʿa alssājideena Save Iblis. He refused to be among the prostrate.
032 qāla yā ibleesu mā laka allā takoona maʿa alssājideena He said: O Iblis! What aileth thee that thou art not among the prostrate ?
033 qāla lam akun li-asjuda libašarin ḫalaqtahu min ṣalsalin min ḥama-in masnoonin He said: I am not one to prostrate myself unto a mortal whom Thou hast created out of potter's clay of black mud altered!
034 qāla faoḫruj minhā fa-innaka rajeemun He said: Then go thou forth from hence, for lo! thou art outcast.
035 wa-inna ʿalayka allāʿnata ilā yawmi alddeeni And lo! the curse shall be upon thee till the Day of Judgment.
036 qāla rabbi faanḏirnee ilā yawmi yubʿaṯoona He said: My Lord! Reprieve me till the day when they are raised.
037 qāla fa-innaka mina almunḏareena He said: Then lo! thou art of those reprieved
038 ilā yawmi alwaqti almaʿloomi Till the Day of appointed time.
039 qāla rabbi bimā aġwaytanee laozayyinanna lahum fee al-arḍi walaoġwiyannahum ajmaʿeena He said: My Lord! Because Thou hast sent me astray, I verily shall adorn the path of error for them in the earth, and shall mislead them every one,
040 illā ʿibādaka minhumu almuḫlaṣeena Save such of them as are Thy perfectly devoted slaves.
041 qāla haṯa ṣiratun ʿalayya mustaqeemun He said: This is a right course incumbent upon Me:
042 inna ʿibādee laysa laka ʿalayhim sultanun illā mani ittabaʿaka mina alġāweena Lo! as for My slaves, thou hast no power over any of them save such of the froward as follow thee,
043 wa-inna jahannama lamawʿiduhum ajmaʿeena And lo! for all such, hell will be the promised place.
044 lahā sabʿatu abwābin likulli bābin minhum juz-on maqsoomun It hath seven gates, and each gate hath an appointed portion.
045 inna almuttaqeena fee jannātin waʿuyoonin Lo! those who ward off (evil) are among gardens and watersprings.
046 odḫuloohā bisalāmin āmineena (And it is said unto them): Enter them in peace, secure.
047 wanazaʿnā mā fee ṣudoorihim min ġillin iḫwānan ʿalā sururin mutaqābileena And We remove whatever rancour may be in their breasts. As brethren, face to face, (they rest) on couches raised.
048 lā yamassuhum feehā naṣabun wamā hum minhā bimuḫrajeena Toil cometh not unto them there, nor will they be expelled from thence.
049 nabbi/ ʿibādee annee anā alġafooru alrraḥeemu Announce, (O Muhammad) unto My slaves that verily I am the Forgiving, the Merciful,
050 waanna ʿaṯabee huwa alʿaṯabu al-aleemu And that My doom is the dolorous doom.
051 wanabbi/hum ʿan ḍayfi ibrāheema And tell them of Abraham's guests,
052 iḏ daḫaloo ʿalayhi faqāloo salāman qāla innā minkum wajiloona (How) when they came in unto him, and said: Peace. He said: Lo! we are afraid of you.
053 qāloo lā tawjal innā nubašširuka biġulāmin ʿaleemin They said: Be not afraid! Lo! we bring thee good tidings of a boy possessing wisdom.
054 qāla abaššartumoonee ʿalā an massaniya alkibaru fabima tubašširooni He said: Bring ye me good tidings (of a son) when old age hath overtaken me ? Of what then can ye bring good tidings ?
055 qāloo baššarnāka bialḥaqqi falā takun mina alqāniṭeena They said: We bring thee good tidings in truth. So be not thou of the despairing.
056 qāla waman yaqnaṭu min raḥmati rabbihi illā alddalloona He said: And who despaireth of the mercy of his Lord save those who are astray ?
057 qāla famā ḫaṭbukum ayyuhā almursaloona He said: And afterward what is your business, O ye messengers (of Allah) ?
058 qāloo innā orsilnā ilā qawmin mujrimeena They said: We have been sent unto a guilty folk,
059 illā āla looṭin innā lamunajjoohum ajmaʿeena (All) save the family of Lot. Them we shall deliver every one,
060 illā imraatahu qaddarnā innahā lamina alġābireena Except his wife, of whom We had decreed that she should be of those who stay behind.
061 falammā jāa āla looṭin almursaloona And when the messengers came unto the family of Lot,
062 qāla innakum qawmun munkaroona He said: Lo! ye are folk unknown (to me).
063 qāloo bal ji/nāka bimā kānoo feehi yamtaroona They said: Nay, but we bring thee that concerning which they keep disputing,
064 waataynāka bialḥaqqi wa-innā lasadiqoona And bring thee the Truth, and lo! we are truth-tellers.
065 faasri bi-ahlika biqiṭʿin mina allāyli waittabiʿ adbārahum walā yaltafit minkum aḥadun waimḍoo ḥayṯu tu/maroona So travel with thy household in a portion of the night, and follow thou their backs. Let none of you turn round, but go whither ye are commanded.
066 waqaḍaynā ilayhi ṯalika al-amra anna dābira hāolā-i maqṭooʿun muṣbiḥeena And We made plain the case to him, that the root of them (who did wrong) was to be cut at early morn.
067 wajāa ahlu almadeenati yastabširoona And the people of the city came, rejoicing at the news (of new arrivals).
068 qāla inna hāolā-i ḍayfee falā tafḍaḥooni He said: Lo! they are my guests. Affront me not!
069 waittaqoo allāha walā tuḫzooni And keep your duty to Allah, and shame me not!
070 qāloo awa lam nanhaka ʿani alʿālameena They said; Have we not forbidden you from (entertaining) anyone ?
071 qāla hāolā-i banātee in kuntum fāʿileena He said: Here are my daughters, if ye must be doing (so).
072 laʿamruka innahum lafee sakratihim yaʿmahoona By thy life (O Muhammad) they moved blindly in the frenzy of approaching death.
073 faaḫaḏat-humu alṣayḥatu mušriqeena Then the (Awful) Cry overtook them at the sunrise.
074 fajaʿalnā ʿāliyahā sāfilahā waamṭarnā ʿalayhim ḥijāratan min sijjeelin And We utterly confounded them, and We rained upon them stones of heated clay.
075 inna fee ṯalika laāyātin lilmutawassimeena Lo! therein verily are portents for those who read the signs.
076 wa-innahā labisabeelin muqeemin And lo! it is upon a road still uneffaced.
077 inna fee ṯalika laāyatan lilmu/mineena Lo! therein is indeed a portent for believers.
078 wa-in kāna aṣ-habu al-aykati laḏālimeena And the dwellers in the wood indeed were evil-doers.
079 faintaqamnā minhum wa-innahumā labi-imāmin mubeenin So we took vengeance on them; and lo! they both are on a high-road plain to see.
080 walaqad kaṯṯaba aṣ-habu alḥijri almursaleena And the dwellers in Al-Hijr denied (Our) messengers.
081 waātaynāhum āyātinā fakānoo ʿanhā muʿriḍeena And we gave them Our revelations, but they were averse to them.
082 wakānoo yanḥitoona mina aljibāli buyootan āmineena And they used to hew out dwellings from the hills, (wherein they dwelt) secure.
083 faaḫaḏat-humu alṣayḥatu muṣbiḥeena But the (Awful) Cry overtook them at the morning hour,
084 famā aġnā ʿanhum mā kānoo yaksiboona And that which they were wont to count as gain availed them not.
085 wamā ḫalaqnā alssamāwāti waal-arḍa wamā baynahumā illā bialḥaqqi wa-inna alssāʿata laātiyatun faiṣfaḥi alṣafḥa aljameela We created not the heavens and the earth and all that is between them save with truth, and lo! the Hour is surely coming. So forgive, (O Muhammad), with a gracious forgiveness.
086 inna rabbaka huwa alḫallāqu alʿaleemu Lo! Thy Lord! He is the All-Wise Creator.
087 walaqad ātaynāka sabʿan mina almaṯānee waalqur-āna alʿaḏeema We have given thee seven of the oft-repeated (verses) and the great Qur'an.
088 lā tamuddanna ʿaynayka ilā mā mattaʿnā bihi azwājan minhum walā taḥzan ʿalayhim waiḫfiḍ janahaka lilmu/mineena Strain not thine eyes toward that which We cause some wedded pairs among them to enjoin, and be not grieved on their account, and lower thy wing (in tenderness) for the believers.
089 waqul innee anā alnnaḏeeru almubeenu And say: Lo! I, even I, am a plain warner,
090 kamā anzalnā ʿalā almuqtasimeena Such as We send down for those who make division,
091 allāḏeena jaʿaloo alqur-āna ʿiḍeena Those who break the Qur'an into parts.
092 fawarabbika lanas-alannahum ajmaʿeena Them, by thy Lord, We shall question, every one,
093 ʿammā kānoo yaʿmaloona Of what they used to do.
094 faiṣdaʿ bimā tu/maru waaʿriḍ ʿani almušrikeena So proclaim that which thou art commanded, and withdraw from the idolaters.
095 innā kafaynāka almustahzi-eena Lo! We defend thee from the scoffers,
096 allāḏeena yajʿaloona maʿa allāhi ilāhan āḫara fasawfa yaʿlamoona Who set some other god along with Allah. But they will come to know.
097 walaqad naʿlamu annaka yaḍeequ ṣadruka bimā yaqooloona Well know We that thy bosom is oppressed by what they say,
098 fasabbiḥ biḥamdi rabbika wakun mina alssājideena But hymn the praise of thy Lord, and be of those who make prostration (unto Him).
99 
waoʿbud rabbaka ḥattā ya/tiyaka alyaqeenu
And serve thy Lord till the Inevitable cometh unto thee.
