15. al-Hijr: The Rock

1 
alif-lām-rā tilka āyātu alkitābi waqur-ānin mubeenin
Alif. Lam. Ra. These are verses of the Scripture and a plain Reading.

2 
rubamā yawaddu allāḏeena kafaroo law kānoo muslimeena
It may be that those who disbelieve wish ardently that they were Muslims.

3 Let them eat and enjoy life, and let (false) hope beguile them. They will come to know!

4 
wamā ahlaknā min qaryatin illā walahā kitābun maʿloomun
And We destroyed no township but there was a known decree for it.

5 
mā tasbiqu min ommatin ajalahā wamā yasta/ḫiroona
No nation can outstrip its term nor can they lag behind.

6 
waqāloo yā ayyuhā allāḏee nuzzila ʿalayhi alṯṯikru innaka lamajnoonun
And they say: O thou unto whom the Reminder is revealed, lo! thou art indeed a madman!

7 
law mā ta/teenā bialmalā-ikati in kunta mina alssadiqeena
Why bringest thou not angels unto us, if thou art of the truthful ?

8 
mā nunazzilu almalā-ikata illā bialḥaqqi wamā kānoo iḏan munḏareena
We send not down the angels save with the Fact, and in that case (the disbelievers) would not be tolerated.

9 
innā naḥnu nazzalnā alṯṯikra wa-innā lahu lahafiḏoona
Lo! We, even We, reveal the Reminder, and lo! We verily are its Guardian.

10 
walaqad arsalnā min qablika fee šiyaʿi al-awwaleena
We verily sent (messengers) before thee among the factions of the men of old.

11 
wamā ya/teehim min rasoolin illā kānoo bihi yastahzi-oona
And never came there unto them a messenger but they did mock him.

12 
kaṯalika naslukuhu fee quloobi almujrimeena
Thus do We make it traverse the hearts of the guilty:

13 
lā yu/minoona bihi waqad ḫalat sunnatu al-awwaleena
They believe not therein, though the example of the men of old hath gone before.

14 
walaw fataḥnā ʿalayhim bāban mina alssamā-i faḏalloo feehi yaʿrujoona
And even if We opened unto them a gate of heaven and they kept mounting through it,

15 
laqāloo innamā sukkirat absarunā bal naḥnu qawmun masḥooroona
They would say: Our sight is wrong - nay, but we are folk bewitched.

16 
walaqad jaʿalnā fee alssamā-i buroojan wazayyannāhā lilnnāḏireena
And verily in the heaven we have set mansions of the stars, and We have beautified it for beholders.

17 
waḥafiḏnāhā min kulli šaytanin rajeemin
And We have guarded it from every outcast devil,

18 
illā mani istaraqa alssamʿa faatbaʿahu šihābun mubeenun
Save him who stealeth the hearing, and them doth a clear flame pursue.

19 
waal-arḍa madadnāhā waalqaynā feehā rawāsiya waanbatnā feehā min kulli šay-in mawzoonin
And the earth have We spread out, and placed therein firm hills, and caused each seemly thing to grow therein.

20 
wajaʿalnā lakum feehā maʿāyiša waman lastum lahu birāziqeena
And we have given unto you livelihoods therein, and unto those for whom ye provide not.

21 
wa-in min šay-in illā ʿindanā ḫazā-inuhu wamā nunazziluhu illā biqadarin maʿloomin
And there is not a thing but with Us are the stores thereof. And we send it not down save in appointed measure.

22 
waarsalnā alrriyaha lawāqiḥa faanzalnā mina alssamā-i māan faasqaynākumoohu wamā antum lahu biḫāzineena
And We send the winds fertilising, and cause water to descend from the sky, and give it you to drink. It is not ye who are the holders of the store thereof.

23 
wa-innā lanaḥnu nuḥyee wanumeetu wanaḥnu alwāriṯoona
Lo! and it is We, even We, Who quicken and give death, and We are the Inheritor.

24 
walaqad ʿalimnā almustaqdimeena minkum walaqad ʿalimnā almusta/ḫireena
And verily We know the eager among you and verily We know the laggards.

25 
wa-inna rabbaka huwa yaḥšuruhum innahu ḥakeemun ʿaleemun
Lo! thy Lord will gather them together. Lo! He is Wise, Aware.

26 
walaqad ḫalaqnā al-insāna min ṣalsalin min ḥama-in masnoonin
Verily We created man of potter's clay of black mud altered,

27 
waaljānna ḫalaqnāhu min qablu min nāri alssamoomi
And the jinn did We create aforetime of essential fire.

28 
wa-iḏ qāla rabbuka lilmalā-ikati innee ḫāliqun bašaran min ṣalsalin min ḥama-in masnoonin
And (remember) when thy Lord said unto the angels: Lo! I am creating a mortal out of potter's clay of black mud altered,

29 
fa-iṯa sawwaytuhu wanafaḫtu feehi min rooḥee faqaʿoo lahu sājideena
So, when I have made him and have breathed into him of My Spirit, do ye fall down, prostrating yourselves unto him.

30 
fasajada almalā-ikatu kulluhum ajmaʿoona
So the angels fell prostrate, all of them together

31 
illā ibleesa abā an yakoona maʿa alssājideena
Save Iblis. He refused to be among the prostrate.

32 
qāla yā ibleesu mā laka allā takoona maʿa alssājideena
He said: O Iblis! What aileth thee that thou art not among the prostrate ?

33 
qāla lam akun li-asjuda libašarin ḫalaqtahu min ṣalsalin min ḥama-in masnoonin
He said: I am not one to prostrate myself unto a mortal whom Thou hast created out of potter's clay of black mud altered!

34 
qāla faoḫruj minhā fa-innaka rajeemun
He said: Then go thou forth from hence, for lo! thou art outcast.

35 
wa-inna ʿalayka allāʿnata ilā yawmi alddeeni
And lo! the curse shall be upon thee till the Day of Judgment.

36 
qāla rabbi faanḏirnee ilā yawmi yubʿaṯoona
He said: My Lord! Reprieve me till the day when they are raised.

37 
qāla fa-innaka mina almunḏareena
He said: Then lo! thou art of those reprieved

38 
ilā yawmi alwaqti almaʿloomi
Till the Day of appointed time.

39 
qāla rabbi bimā aġwaytanee laozayyinanna lahum fee al-arḍi walaoġwiyannahum ajmaʿeena
He said: My Lord! Because Thou hast sent me astray, I verily shall adorn the path of error for them in the earth, and shall mislead them every one,

40 
illā ʿibādaka minhumu almuḫlaṣeena
Save such of them as are Thy perfectly devoted slaves.

41 
qāla haṯa ṣiratun ʿalayya mustaqeemun
He said: This is a right course incumbent upon Me:

42 
inna ʿibādee laysa laka ʿalayhim sultanun illā mani ittabaʿaka mina alġāweena
Lo! as for My slaves, thou hast no power over any of them save such of the froward as follow thee,

43 
wa-inna jahannama lamawʿiduhum ajmaʿeena
And lo! for all such, hell will be the promised place.

44 
lahā sabʿatu abwābin likulli bābin minhum juz-on maqsoomun
It hath seven gates, and each gate hath an appointed portion.

45 
inna almuttaqeena fee jannātin waʿuyoonin
Lo! those who ward off (evil) are among gardens and watersprings.

46 
odḫuloohā bisalāmin āmineena
(And it is said unto them): Enter them in peace, secure.

47 
wanazaʿnā mā fee ṣudoorihim min ġillin iḫwānan ʿalā sururin mutaqābileena
And We remove whatever rancour may be in their breasts. As brethren, face to face, (they rest) on couches raised.

48 
lā yamassuhum feehā naṣabun wamā hum minhā bimuḫrajeena
Toil cometh not unto them there, nor will they be expelled from thence.

49 
nabbi/ ʿibādee annee anā alġafooru alrraḥeemu
Announce, (O Muhammad) unto My slaves that verily I am the Forgiving, the Merciful,

50 
waanna ʿaṯabee huwa alʿaṯabu al-aleemu
And that My doom is the dolorous doom.

51 
wanabbi/hum ʿan ḍayfi ibrāheema
And tell them of Abraham's guests,

52 
iḏ daḫaloo ʿalayhi faqāloo salāman qāla innā minkum wajiloona
(How) when they came in unto him, and said: Peace. He said: Lo! we are afraid of you.

53 
qāloo lā tawjal innā nubašširuka biġulāmin ʿaleemin
They said: Be not afraid! Lo! we bring thee good tidings of a boy possessing wisdom.

54 
qāla abaššartumoonee ʿalā an massaniya alkibaru fabima tubašširooni
He said: Bring ye me good tidings (of a son) when old age hath overtaken me ? Of what then can ye bring good tidings ?

55 
qāloo baššarnāka bialḥaqqi falā takun mina alqāniṭeena
They said: We bring thee good tidings in truth. So be not thou of the despairing.

56 
qāla waman yaqnaṭu min raḥmati rabbihi illā alddalloona
He said: And who despaireth of the mercy of his Lord save those who are astray ?

57 
qāla famā ḫaṭbukum ayyuhā almursaloona
He said: And afterward what is your business, O ye messengers (of Allah) ?

58 
qāloo innā orsilnā ilā qawmin mujrimeena
They said: We have been sent unto a guilty folk,

59 
illā āla looṭin innā lamunajjoohum ajmaʿeena
(All) save the family of Lot. Them we shall deliver every one,

60 
illā imraatahu qaddarnā innahā lamina alġābireena
Except his wife, of whom We had decreed that she should be of those who stay behind.

61 
falammā jāa āla looṭin almursaloona
And when the messengers came unto the family of Lot,

62 
qāla innakum qawmun munkaroona
He said: Lo! ye are folk unknown (to me).

63 
qāloo bal ji/nāka bimā kānoo feehi yamtaroona
They said: Nay, but we bring thee that concerning which they keep disputing,

64 
waataynāka bialḥaqqi wa-innā lasadiqoona
And bring thee the Truth, and lo! we are truth-tellers.

65 
faasri bi-ahlika biqiṭʿin mina allāyli waittabiʿ adbārahum walā yaltafit minkum aḥadun waimḍoo ḥayṯu tu/maroona
So travel with thy household in a portion of the night, and follow thou their backs. Let none of you turn round, but go whither ye are commanded.

66 
waqaḍaynā ilayhi ṯalika al-amra anna dābira hāolā-i maqṭooʿun muṣbiḥeena
And We made plain the case to him, that the root of them (who did wrong) was to be cut at early morn.

67 
wajāa ahlu almadeenati yastabširoona
And the people of the city came, rejoicing at the news (of new arrivals).

68 
qāla inna hāolā-i ḍayfee falā tafḍaḥooni
He said: Lo! they are my guests. Affront me not!

69 
waittaqoo allāha walā tuḫzooni
And keep your duty to Allah, and shame me not!

70 
qāloo awa lam nanhaka ʿani alʿālameena
They said; Have we not forbidden you from (entertaining) anyone ?

71 
qāla hāolā-i banātee in kuntum fāʿileena
He said: Here are my daughters, if ye must be doing (so).

72 
laʿamruka innahum lafee sakratihim yaʿmahoona
By thy life (O Muhammad) they moved blindly in the frenzy of approaching death.

73 
faaḫaḏat-humu alṣayḥatu mušriqeena
Then the (Awful) Cry overtook them at the sunrise.

74 
fajaʿalnā ʿāliyahā sāfilahā waamṭarnā ʿalayhim ḥijāratan min sijjeelin
And We utterly confounded them, and We rained upon them stones of heated clay.

75 
inna fee ṯalika laāyātin lilmutawassimeena
Lo! therein verily are portents for those who read the signs.

76 
wa-innahā labisabeelin muqeemin
And lo! it is upon a road still uneffaced.

77 
inna fee ṯalika laāyatan lilmu/mineena
Lo! therein is indeed a portent for believers.

78 
wa-in kāna aṣ-habu al-aykati laḏālimeena
And the dwellers in the wood indeed were evil-doers.

79 
faintaqamnā minhum wa-innahumā labi-imāmin mubeenin
So we took vengeance on them; and lo! they both are on a high-road plain to see.

80 
walaqad kaṯṯaba aṣ-habu alḥijri almursaleena
And the dwellers in Al-Hijr denied (Our) messengers.

81 
waātaynāhum āyātinā fakānoo ʿanhā muʿriḍeena
And we gave them Our revelations, but they were averse to them.

82 
wakānoo yanḥitoona mina aljibāli buyootan āmineena
And they used to hew out dwellings from the hills, (wherein they dwelt) secure.

83 
faaḫaḏat-humu alṣayḥatu muṣbiḥeena
But the (Awful) Cry overtook them at the morning hour,

84 
famā aġnā ʿanhum mā kānoo yaksiboona
And that which they were wont to count as gain availed them not.

85 
wamā ḫalaqnā alssamāwāti waal-arḍa wamā baynahumā illā bialḥaqqi wa-inna alssāʿata laātiyatun faiṣfaḥi alṣafḥa aljameela
We created not the heavens and the earth and all that is between them save with truth, and lo! the Hour is surely coming. So forgive, (O Muhammad), with a gracious forgiveness.

86 
inna rabbaka huwa alḫallāqu alʿaleemu
Lo! Thy Lord! He is the All-Wise Creator.

87 
walaqad ātaynāka sabʿan mina almaṯānee waalqur-āna alʿaḏeema
We have given thee seven of the oft-repeated (verses) and the great Qur'an.

88 
lā tamuddanna ʿaynayka ilā mā mattaʿnā bihi azwājan minhum walā taḥzan ʿalayhim waiḫfiḍ janahaka lilmu/mineena
Strain not thine eyes toward that which We cause some wedded pairs among them to enjoin, and be not grieved on their account, and lower thy wing (in tenderness) for the believers.

89 
waqul innee anā alnnaḏeeru almubeenu
And say: Lo! I, even I, am a plain warner,

90 
kamā anzalnā ʿalā almuqtasimeena
Such as We send down for those who make division,

91 
allāḏeena jaʿaloo alqur-āna ʿiḍeena
Those who break the Qur'an into parts.

92 
fawarabbika lanas-alannahum ajmaʿeena
Them, by thy Lord, We shall question, every one,

93 
ʿammā kānoo yaʿmaloona
Of what they used to do.

94 
faiṣdaʿ bimā tu/maru waaʿriḍ ʿani almušrikeena
So proclaim that which thou art commanded, and withdraw from the idolaters.

95 
innā kafaynāka almustahzi-eena
Lo! We defend thee from the scoffers,

96 
allāḏeena yajʿaloona maʿa allāhi ilāhan āḫara fasawfa yaʿlamoona
Who set some other god along with Allah. But they will come to know.

97 
walaqad naʿlamu annaka yaḍeequ ṣadruka bimā yaqooloona
Well know We that thy bosom is oppressed by what they say,

98 
fasabbiḥ biḥamdi rabbika wakun mina alssājideena
But hymn the praise of thy Lord, and be of those who make prostration (unto Him).

99 
waoʿbud rabbaka ḥattā ya/tiyaka alyaqeenu
And serve thy Lord till the Inevitable cometh unto thee.
