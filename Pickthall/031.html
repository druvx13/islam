31. Luqman: Lukman

001 alif-lām-meem Alif. Lam. Mim.
002 tilka āyātu alkitābi alḥakeemi These are revelations of the wise Scripture,
003 hudan waraḥmatan lilmuḥsineena A guidance and a mercy for the good,
004 allāḏeena yuqeemoona alṣalāta wayu/toona alzzakāta wahum bial-āḫirati hum yooqinoona Those who establish worship and pay the poor-due and have sure faith in the Hereafter.
005 olā-ika ʿalā hudan min rabbihim waolā-ika humu almufliḥoona Such have guidance from their Lord. Such are the successful.
006 wamina alnnāsi man yaštaree lahwa alḥadeeṯi liyuḍilla ʿan sabeeli allāhi biġayri ʿilmin wayattaḫiḏahā huzuwan olā-ika lahum ʿaṯabun muheenun And of mankind is he who payeth for mere pastime of discourse, that he may mislead from Allah's way without knowledge, and maketh it the butt of mockery. For such there is a shameful doom.
007 wa-iṯa tutlā ʿalayhi āyātunā wallā mustakbiran kaan lam yasmaʿhā kaanna fee oḏunayhi waqran fabašširhu biʿaṯabin aleemin And when Our revelations are recited unto him he turneth away in pride as if he heard them not, as if there were a deafness in his ears. So give him tidings of a painful doom.
008 inna allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati lahum jannātu alnnaʿeemi Lo! those who believe and do good works, for them are the gardens of delight,
009 khālideena feehā waʿda allāhi ḥaqqan wahuwa alʿazeezu alḥakeemu Wherein they will abide. It is a promise of Allah in truth. He is the Mighty, the Wise.
010 khalaqa alssamāwāti biġayri ʿamadin tarawnahā waalqā fee al-arḍi rawāsiya an tameeda bikum wabaṯṯa feehā min kulli dābbatin waanzalnā mina alssamā-i māan faanbatnā feehā min kulli zawjin kareemin He hath created the heavens without supports that ye can see, and hath cast into the earth firm hills, so that it quake not with you; and He hath dispersed therein all kinds of beasts. And We send down water from the sky and We cause (plants) of every goodly kind to grow therein.
011 haṯa ḫalqu allāhi faaroonee maṯa ḫalaqa allāḏeena min doonihi bali alṯṯālimoona fee ḍalālin mubeenin This is the Creation of Allah. Now show me that which those (ye worship) beside Him have created. Nay, but the wrong-doers are in error manifest!
012 walaqad ātaynā luqmāna alḥikmata ani oškur lillāhi waman yaškur fa-innamā yaškuru linafsihi waman kafara fa-inna allāha ġaniyyun ḥameedun And verily We gave Luqman wisdom, saying: Give thanks unto Allah; and whosoever giveth thanks, he giveth thanks for (the good of) his soul. And whosoever refuseth - Lo! Allah is Absolute, Owner of Praise.
013 wa-iḏ qāla luqmānu liibnihi wahuwa yaʿiḏuhu yā bunayya lā tušrik biallāhi inna alšširka laḏulmun ʿaḏeemun And (remember) when Luqman said unto his son, when he was exhorting him: O my dear son! Ascribe no partners unto Allah. Lo! to ascribe partners (unto Him) is a tremendous wrong -
014 wawaṣaynā al-insāna biwālidayhi ḥamalat-hu ommuhu wahnan ʿalā wahnin wafisaluhu fee ʿāmayni ani oškur lee waliwālidayka ilayya almaṣeeru And We have enjoined upon man concerning his partners - His mother beareth him in weakness upon weakness, and his weaning is in two years - Give thanks unto Me and unto thy parents. Unto Me is the journeying.
015 wa-in jāhadāka ʿalā an tušrika bee mā laysa laka bihi ʿilmun falā tuṭiʿhumā wasahibhumā fee alddunyā maʿroofan waittabiʿ sabeela man anāba ilayya ṯumma ilayya marjiʿukum faonabbi-okum bimā kuntum taʿmaloona But if they strive with thee to make thee ascribe unto Me as partner that of which thou hast no knowledge, then obey them not. Consort with them in the world kindly, and follow the path of him who repenteth unto Me. Then unto Me will be your return, and I shall tell you what ye used to do -
016 yā bunayya innahā in taku miṯqāla ḥabbatin min ḫardalin fatakun fee ṣaḫratin aw fee alssamāwāti aw fee al-arḍi ya/ti bihā allāhu inna allāha laṭeefun ḫabeerun O my dear son! Lo! though it be but the weight of a grain of mustard-seed, and though it be in a rock, or in the heavens, or in the earth, Allah will bring it forth. Lo! Allah is Subtile, Aware.
017 yā bunayya aqimi alṣalāta wa/mur bialmaʿroofi wainha ʿani almunkari waiṣbir ʿalā mā asabaka inna ṯalika min ʿazmi al-omoori O my dear son! Establish worship and enjoin kindness and forbid iniquity, and persevere whatever may befall thee. Lo! that is of the steadfast heart of things.
018 walā tuṣaʿʿir ḫaddaka lilnnāsi walā tamši fee al-arḍi maraḥan inna allāha lā yuḥibbu kulla muḫtālin faḫoorin Turn not thy cheek in scorn toward folk, nor walk with pertness in the land. Lo! Allah loveth not each braggart boaster.
019 waiqṣid fee mašyika waoġḍuḍ min ṣawtika inna ankara al-aṣwāti laṣawtu alḥameeri Be modest in thy bearing and subdue thy voice. Lo! the harshest of all voices is the voice of the ass.
020 alam taraw anna allāha saḫḫara lakum mā fee alssamāwāti wamā fee al-arḍi waasbaġa ʿalaykum niʿamahu ḏāhiratan wabatinatan wamina alnnāsi man yujādilu fee allāhi biġayri ʿilmin walā hudan walā kitābin muneerin See ye not how Allah hath made serviceable unto you whatsoever is in the skies and whatsoever is in the earth and hath loaded you with His favours both without and within ? Yet of mankind is he who disputeth concerning Allah, without knowledge or guidance or a scripture giving light.
021 wa-iṯa qeela lahumu ittabiʿoo mā anzala allāhu qāloo bal nattabiʿu mā wajadnā ʿalayhi ābāanā awa law kāna alššaytanu yadʿoohum ilā ʿaṯabi alssaʿeeri And if it be said unto them: Follow that which Allah hath revealed, they say: Nay, but we follow that wherein we found our fathers. What! Even though the devil were inviting them unto the doom of flame ?
022 waman yuslim wajhahu ilā allāhi wahuwa muḥsinun faqadi istamsaka bialʿurwati alwuṯqā wa-ilā allāhi ʿāqibatu al-omoori Whosoever surrendereth his purpose to Allah while doing good, he verily hath grasped the firm hand-hold. Unto Allah belongeth the sequel of all things.
023 waman kafara falā yaḥzunka kufruhu ilaynā marjiʿuhum fanunabbi-ohum bimā ʿamiloo inna allāha ʿaleemun biṯati alṣudoori And whosoever disbelieveth, let not his disbelief afflict thee (O Muhammad). Unto Us is their return, and We shall tell them what they did. Lo! Allah is Aware of what is in the breasts (of men).
024 numattiʿuhum qaleelan ṯumma nadtarruhum ilā ʿaṯabin ġaleeḏin We give them comfort for a little, and then We drive them to a heavy doom.
025 wala-in saaltahum man ḫalaqa alssamāwāti waal-arḍa layaqoolunna allāhu quli alḥamdu lillāhi bal akṯaruhum lā yaʿlamoona If thou shouldst ask them: Who created the heavens and the earth ? they would answer: Allah. Say: Praise be to Allah! But most of them know not.
026 lillāhi mā fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi inna allāha huwa alġaniyyu alḥameedu Unto Allah belongeth whatsoever is in the heavens and the earth. Lo! Allah, He is the Absolute, the Owner of Praise.
027 walaw annamā fee al-arḍi min šajaratin aqlāmun waalbaḥru yamudduhu min baʿdihi sabʿatu abḥurin mā nafidat kalimātu allāhi inna allāha ʿazeezun ḥakeemun And if all the trees in the earth were pens, and the sea, with seven more seas to help it, (were ink), the words of Allah could not be exhausted. Lo! Allah is Mighty, Wise.
028 mā ḫalqukum walā baʿṯukum illā kanafsin wahidatin inna allāha sameeʿun baṣeerun Your creation and your raising (from the dead) are only as (the creation and the raising of) a single soul. Lo! Allah is Hearer, Knower.
029 alam tara anna allāha yooliju allāyla fee alnnahāri wayooliju alnnahāra fee allāyli wasaḫḫara alššamsa waalqamara kullun yajree ilā ajalin musamman waanna allāha bimā taʿmaloona ḫabeerun Hast thou not seen how Allah causeth the night to pass into the day and causeth the day to pass into the night, and hath subdued the sun and the moon (to do their work), each running unto an appointed term; and that Allah is Informed of what ye do ?
030 [romanization missing] That (is so) because Allah, He is the True, and that which they invoke beside Him is the False, and because Allah, He is the Sublime, the Great.
031 alam tara anna alfulka tajree fee albaḥri biniʿmati allāhi liyuriyakum min āyātihi inna fee ṯalika laāyātin likulli ṣabbārin šakoorin Hast thou not seen how the ships glide on the sea by Allah's grace, that He may show you of His wonders ? Lo! therein indeed are portents for every steadfast, grateful (heart).
032 wa-iṯa ġašiyahum mawjun kaalṯṯulali daʿawoo allāha muḫliṣeena lahu alddeena falammā najjāhum ilā albarri faminhum muqtaṣidun wamā yajḥadu bi-āyātinā illā kullu ḫattārin kafoorin And if a wave enshroudeth them like awnings, they cry unto Allah, making their faith pure for Him only. But when He bringeth them safe to land, some of them compromise. None denieth Our signs save every traitor ingrate.
033 yā ayyuhā alnnāsu ittaqoo rabbakum waiḫšaw yawman lā yajzee wālidun ʿan waladihi walā mawloodun huwa jāzin ʿan wālidihi šay-an inna waʿda allāhi ḥaqqun falā taġurrannakumu alḥayātu alddunyā walā yaġurrannakum biallāhi alġarooru O mankind! Keep your duty to your Lord and fear a Day when the parent will not be able to avail the child in aught, nor the child to avail the parent. Lo! Allah's promise is the very truth. Let not the life of the world beguile you, nor let the deceiver beguile you, in regard to Allah.
34 inna allāha ʿindahu ʿilmu alssāʿati wayunazzilu alġayṯa wayaʿlamu mā fee al-arhami wamā tadree nafsun maṯa taksibu ġadan wamā tadree nafsun bi-ayyi arḍin tamootu inna allāha ʿaleemun ḫabeerun [Could not extract English for verse 34]
