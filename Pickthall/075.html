75. al-Qiyamah: The Resurrection

001 lā oqsimu biyawmi alqiyāmati Nay, I swear by the Day of Resurrection;
002 walā oqsimu bialnnafsi allāwwāmati Nay, I swear by the accusing soul (that this Scripture is true).
003 ayaḥsabu al-insānu allān najmaʿa ʿiḏāmahu Thinketh man that We shall not assemble his bones ?
004 balā qādireena ʿalā an nusawwiya banānahu Yea, verily. We are Able to restore his very fingers!
005 bal yureedu al-insānu liyafjura amāmahu But man would fain deny what is before him.
006 yas-alu ayyāna yawmu alqiyāmati He asketh: When will be this Day of Resurrection ?
007 fa-iṯa bariqa albaṣaru But when sight is confounded
008 waḫasafa alqamaru And the moon is eclipsed
009 wajumiʿa alššamsu waalqamaru And sun and moon are united,
010 yaqoolu al-insānu yawma-iḏin ayna almafarru On that day man will cry: Whither to flee!
011 kallā lā wazara Alas! No refuge!
012 ilā rabbika yawma-iḏin almustaqarru Unto thy Lord is the recourse that day.
013 yunabbao al-insānu yawma-iḏin bimā qaddama waaḫḫara On that day man is told the tale of that which he hath sent before and left behind.
014 bali al-insānu ʿalā nafsihi baṣeeratun Oh, but man is a telling witness against himself,
015 walaw alqā maʿaṯeerahu Although he tender his excuses.
016 lā tuḥarrik bihi lisānaka litaʿjala bihi Stir not thy tongue herewith to hasten it.
017 inna ʿalaynā jamʿahu waqur-ānahu Lo! upon Us (resteth) the putting together thereof and the reading thereof.
018 fa-iṯa qara/nāhu faittabiʿ qur-ānahu And when We read it, follow thou the reading;
019 thumma inna ʿalaynā bayānahu Then lo! upon Us (resteth) the explanation thereof.
020 kallā bal tuḥibboona alʿājilata Nay, but ye do love the fleeting Now
021 wataḏaroona al-āḫirata And neglect the Hereafter.
022 wujoohun yawma-iḏin nadiratun That day will faces be resplendent,
023 ilā rabbihā nāḏiratun Looking toward their Lord;
024 wawujoohun yawma-iḏin bāsiratun And that day will other faces be despondent,
025 taḏunnu an yufʿala bihā fāqiratun Thou wilt know that some great disaster is about to fall on them.
026 kallā iṯa balaġati alttarāqiya Nay, but when the life cometh up to the throat
027 waqeela man rāqin And men say: Where is the wizard (who can save him now) ?
028 waḏanna annahu alfirāqu And he knoweth that it is the parting;
029 wailtaffati alssāqu bialssāqi And agony is heaped on agony;
030 ilā rabbika yawma-iḏin almasāqu Unto thy Lord that day will be the driving.
031 falā ṣaddaqa walā ṣallā For he neither trusted, nor prayed.
032 walākin kaṯṯaba watawallā But he denied and flouted.
033 thumma ḏahaba ilā ahlihi yatamatta Then went he to his folk with glee.
034 awlā laka faawlā Nearer unto thee and nearer,
035 thumma awlā laka faawlā Again nearer unto thee and nearer (is the doom).
036 ayaḥsabu al-insānu an yutraka sudān Thinketh man that he is to be left aimless ?
037 alam yaku nuṭfatan min manayyin yumnā Was he not a drop of fluid which gushed forth ?
038 thumma kāna ʿalaqatan faḫalaqa fasawwā Then he became a clot; then (Allah) shaped and fashioned
039 fajaʿala minhu alzzawjayni alṯṯakara waal-onṯā And made of him a pair, the male and female.
40 alaysa ṯalika biqādirin ʿalā an yuḥyiya almawtā [Could not extract English for verse 40]
