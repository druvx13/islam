76. ad-Dahr: The Man

1 
hal atā ʿalā al-insāni ḥeenun mina alddahri lam yakun šay-an maḏkoorān
Hath there come upon man (ever) any period of time in which he was a thing unremembered ?

2 
innā ḫalaqnā al-insāna min nuṭfatin amšājin nabtaleehi fajaʿalnāhu sameeʿan baṣeerān
Lo! We create man from a drop of thickened fluid to test him; so We make him hearing, knowing.

3 
innā hadaynāhu alssabeela immā šākiran wa-immā kafoorān
Lo! We have shown him the way, whether he be grateful or disbelieving.

4 
innā aʿtadnā lilkāfireena salāsila waaġlālan wasaʿeerān
Lo! We have prepared for disbelievers manacles and carcans and a raging fire.

5 
inna al-abrāra yašraboona min ka/sin kāna mizājuhā kāfoorān
Lo! the righteous shall drink of a cup whereof the mixture is of Kafur,

6 
ʿaynan yašrabu bihā ʿibādu allāhi yufajjiroonahā tafjeerān
A spring wherefrom the slaves of Allah drink, making it gush forth abundantly,

7 
yoofoona bialnnaḏri wayaḫāfoona yawman kāna šarruhu mustaṭeerān
(Because) they perform the vow and fear a day whereof the evil is wide- spreading,

8 
wayuṭʿimona alttaʿāma ʿalā ḥubbihi miskeenan wayateeman waaseerān
And feed with food the needy wretch, the orphan and the prisoner, for love of Him,

9 
innamā nuṭʿimukum liwajhi allāhi lā nureedu minkum jazāan walā šukoorān
(Saying): We feed you, for the sake of Allah only. We wish for no reward nor thanks from you;

10 
innā naḫāfu min rabbinā yawman ʿaboosan qamṭareerān
Lo! we fear from our Lord a day of frowning and of fate.

11 
fawaqāhumu allāhu šarra ṯalika alyawmi walaqqāhum naḍratan wasuroorān
Therefor Allah hath warded off from them the evil of that day, and hath made them find brightness and joy;

12 
wajazāhum bimā ṣabaroo jannatan waḥareerān
And hath awarded them for all that they endured, a Garden and silk attire;

13 
muttaki-eena feehā ʿalā al-arā-iki lā yarawna feehā šamsan walā zamhareerān
Reclining therein upon couches, they will find there neither (heat of) a sun nor bitter cold.

14 
wadāniyatan ʿalayhim ḏilāluhā waḏullilat quṭoofuhā taḏleelān
The shade thereof is close upon them and the clustered fruits thereof bow down.

15 
wayutafu ʿalayhim bi-āniyatin min fiḍatin waakwābin kānat qawāreerā
Goblets of silver are brought round for them, and beakers (as) of glass

16 
qawāreera min fiḍatin qaddaroohā taqdeerān
(Bright as) glass but (made) of silver, which they (themselves) have measured to the measure (of their deeds).

17 
wayusqawna feehā ka/san kāna mizājuhā zanjabeelān
There are they watered with a cup whereof the mixture is of Zanjabil,

18 
ʿaynan feehā tusammā salsabeelān
(The water of) a spring therein, named Salsabil.

19 
wayaṭoofu ʿalayhim wildānun muḫalladoona iṯa raaytahum ḥasibtahum lu/lu-an manṯoorān
There wait on them immortal youths, whom, when thou seest, thou wouldst take for scattered pearls.

20 
wa-iṯa raayta ṯamma raayta naʿeeman wamulkan kabeerān
When thou seest, thou wilt see there bliss and high estate.

21 
ʿāliyahum ṯiyābu sundusin ḫuḍrun wa-istabraqun waḥulloo asāwira min fiḍatin wasaqāhum rabbuhum šarāban ṭahoorān
Their raiment will be fine green silk and gold embroidery. Bracelets of silver will they wear. Their Lord will slake their thirst with a pure drink.

22 
inna haṯa kāna lakum jazāan wakāna saʿyukum maškoorān
(And it will be said unto them): Lo! this is a reward for you. Your endeavour (upon earth) hath found acceptance.

23 
innā naḥnu nazzalnā ʿalayka alqur-āna tanzeelān
Lo! We, even We, have revealed unto thee the Qur'an, a revelation;

24 
faiṣbir liḥukmi rabbika walā tuṭiʿ minhum āṯiman aw kafoorān
So submit patiently to thy Lord's command, and obey not of them any guilty one or disbeliever.

25 
waoḏkuri isma rabbika bukratan waaṣeelān
Remember the name of thy Lord at morn and evening.

26 
wamina allāyli faosjud lahu wasabbiḥhu laylan ṭaweelān
And worship Him (a portion) of the night. And glorify Him through the livelong night.

27 
inna hāolā-i yuḥibboona alʿājilata wayaḏaroona warāahum yawman ṯaqeelān
Lo! these love fleeting life, and put behind them (the remembrance of) a grievous day.

28 
naḥnu ḫalaqnāhum wašadadnā asrahum wa-iṯa ši/nā baddalnā amṯālahum tabdeelān
We, even We, created them, and strengthened their frame. And when We will, We can replace them, bringing others like them in their stead.

29 
inna haṯihi taḏkiratun faman šāa ittaḫaḏa ilā rabbihi sabeelān
Lo! this is an Admonishment, that whosoever will may choose a way unto his Lord.

30 
wamā tašāoona illā an yašāa allāhu inna allāha kāna ʿaleeman ḥakeemān
Yet ye will not, unless Allah willeth. Lo! Allah is Knower, Wise.

31 
yudḫilu man yašāo fee raḥmatihi waalṯṯālimeena aʿadda lahum ʿaṯaban aleemān
He maketh whom He will to enter His mercy, and for evil-doers hath prepared a painful doom.
