76. ad-Dahr: The Man

001 hal atā ʿalā al-insāni ḥeenun mina alddahri lam yakun šay-an maḏkoorān Hath there come upon man (ever) any period of time in which he was a thing unremembered ?
002 innā ḫalaqnā al-insāna min nuṭfatin amšājin nabtaleehi fajaʿalnāhu sameeʿan baṣeerān Lo! We create man from a drop of thickened fluid to test him; so We make him hearing, knowing.
003 innā hadaynāhu alssabeela immā šākiran wa-immā kafoorān Lo! We have shown him the way, whether he be grateful or disbelieving.
004 innā aʿtadnā lilkāfireena salāsila waaġlālan wasaʿeerān Lo! We have prepared for disbelievers manacles and carcans and a raging fire.
005 inna al-abrāra yašraboona min ka/sin kāna mizājuhā kāfoorān Lo! the righteous shall drink of a cup whereof the mixture is of Kafur,
006 ʿaynan yašrabu bihā ʿibādu allāhi yufajjiroonahā tafjeerān A spring wherefrom the slaves of Allah drink, making it gush forth abundantly,
007 yoofoona bialnnaḏri wayaḫāfoona yawman kāna šarruhu mustaṭeerān (Because) they perform the vow and fear a day whereof the evil is wide- spreading,
008 wayuṭʿimona alttaʿāma ʿalā ḥubbihi miskeenan wayateeman waaseerān And feed with food the needy wretch, the orphan and the prisoner, for love of Him,
009 innamā nuṭʿimukum liwajhi allāhi lā nureedu minkum jazāan walā šukoorān (Saying): We feed you, for the sake of Allah only. We wish for no reward nor thanks from you;
010 innā naḫāfu min rabbinā yawman ʿaboosan qamṭareerān Lo! we fear from our Lord a day of frowning and of fate.
011 fawaqāhumu allāhu šarra ṯalika alyawmi walaqqāhum naḍratan wasuroorān Therefor Allah hath warded off from them the evil of that day, and hath made them find brightness and joy;
012 wajazāhum bimā ṣabaroo jannatan waḥareerān And hath awarded them for all that they endured, a Garden and silk attire;
013 muttaki-eena feehā ʿalā al-arā-iki lā yarawna feehā šamsan walā zamhareerān Reclining therein upon couches, they will find there neither (heat of) a sun nor bitter cold.
014 wadāniyatan ʿalayhim ḏilāluhā waḏullilat quṭoofuhā taḏleelān The shade thereof is close upon them and the clustered fruits thereof bow down.
015 wayutafu ʿalayhim bi-āniyatin min fiḍatin waakwābin kānat qawāreerā Goblets of silver are brought round for them, and beakers (as) of glass
016 qawāreera min fiḍatin qaddaroohā taqdeerān (Bright as) glass but (made) of silver, which they (themselves) have measured to the measure (of their deeds).
017 wayusqawna feehā ka/san kāna mizājuhā zanjabeelān There are they watered with a cup whereof the mixture is of Zanjabil,
018 ʿaynan feehā tusammā salsabeelān (The water of) a spring therein, named Salsabil.
019 wayaṭoofu ʿalayhim wildānun muḫalladoona iṯa raaytahum ḥasibtahum lu/lu-an manṯoorān There wait on them immortal youths, whom, when thou seest, thou wouldst take for scattered pearls.
020 wa-iṯa raayta ṯamma raayta naʿeeman wamulkan kabeerān When thou seest, thou wilt see there bliss and high estate.
021 ʿāliyahum ṯiyābu sundusin ḫuḍrun wa-istabraqun waḥulloo asāwira min fiḍatin wasaqāhum rabbuhum šarāban ṭahoorān Their raiment will be fine green silk and gold embroidery. Bracelets of silver will they wear. Their Lord will slake their thirst with a pure drink.
022 inna haṯa kāna lakum jazāan wakāna saʿyukum maškoorān (And it will be said unto them): Lo! this is a reward for you. Your endeavour (upon earth) hath found acceptance.
023 innā naḥnu nazzalnā ʿalayka alqur-āna tanzeelān Lo! We, even We, have revealed unto thee the Qur'an, a revelation;
024 faiṣbir liḥukmi rabbika walā tuṭiʿ minhum āṯiman aw kafoorān So submit patiently to thy Lord's command, and obey not of them any guilty one or disbeliever.
025 waoḏkuri isma rabbika bukratan waaṣeelān Remember the name of thy Lord at morn and evening.
026 wamina allāyli faosjud lahu wasabbiḥhu laylan ṭaweelān And worship Him (a portion) of the night. And glorify Him through the livelong night.
027 inna hāolā-i yuḥibboona alʿājilata wayaḏaroona warāahum yawman ṯaqeelān Lo! these love fleeting life, and put behind them (the remembrance of) a grievous day.
028 naḥnu ḫalaqnāhum wašadadnā asrahum wa-iṯa ši/nā baddalnā amṯālahum tabdeelān We, even We, created them, and strengthened their frame. And when We will, We can replace them, bringing others like them in their stead.
029 inna haṯihi taḏkiratun faman šāa ittaḫaḏa ilā rabbihi sabeelān Lo! this is an Admonishment, that whosoever will may choose a way unto his Lord.
030 wamā tašāoona illā an yašāa allāhu inna allāha kāna ʿaleeman ḥakeemān Yet ye will not, unless Allah willeth. Lo! Allah is Knower, Wise.
31 yudḫilu man yašāo fee raḥmatihi waalṯṯālimeena aʿadda lahum ʿaṯaban aleemān [Could not extract English for verse 31]
