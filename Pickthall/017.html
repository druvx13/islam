17. bani Isra'il: The Israelites

001 subhana allāḏee asrā biʿabdihi laylan mina almasjidi alḥarāmi ilā almasjidi al-aqsa allāḏee bāraknā ḥawlahu linuriyahu min āyātinā innahu huwa alssameeʿu albaṣeeru Glorified be He Who carried His servant by night from the Inviolable Place of Worship to the Far distant place of worship the neighbourhood whereof We have blessed, that We might show him of Our tokens! Lo! He, only He, is the Hearer, the Seer.
002 waātaynā moosā alkitāba wajaʿalnāhu hudan libanee isrā-eela allā tattaḫiḏoo min doonee wakeelān We gave unto Moses the Scripture, and We appointed it a guidance for the children of Israel, saying: Choose no guardian beside Me.
003 [romanization missing] (They were) the seed of those whom We carried (in the ship) along with Noah. Lo! he was a grateful slave.
004 waqaḍaynā ilā banee isrā-eela fee alkitābi latufsidunna fee al-arḍi marratayni walataʿlunna ʿuluwwan kabeerān And We decreed for the Children of Israel in the Scripture: Ye verily will work corruption in the earth twice, and ye will become great tyrants.
005 fa-iṯa jāa waʿdu oolāhumā baʿaṯnā ʿalaykum ʿibādan lanā olee ba/sin šadeedin fajāsoo ḫilāla alddiyāri wakāna waʿdan mafʿoolān So when the time for the first of the two came, We roused against you slaves of Ours of great might who ravaged (your) country, and it was a threat performed.
006 thumma radadnā lakumu alkarrata ʿalayhim waamdadnākum bi-amwālin wabaneena wajaʿalnākum akṯara nafeerān Then we gave you once again your turn against them, and We aided you with wealth and children and made you more in soldiery.
007 in aḥsantum aḥsantum li-anfusikum wa-in asa/tum falahā fa-iṯa jāa waʿdu al-āḫirati liyasoo-oo wujoohakum waliyadḫuloo almasjida kamā daḫaloohu awwala marratin waliyutabbiroo mā ʿalaw tatbeerān (Saying): If ye do good, ye do good for your own souls, and if ye do evil, it is for them (in like manner). So, when the time for the second (of the judgments) came (We roused against you others of Our slaves) to ravage you, and to enter the Temple even as they entered it the first time, and to lay waste all that they conquered with an utter wasting.
008 ʿasā rabbukum an yarḥamakum wa-in ʿudtum ʿudnā wajaʿalnā jahannama lilkāfireena ḥaṣeerān It may be that your Lord will have mercy on you, but if ye repeat (the crime) We shall repeat (the punishment), and We have appointed hell a dungeon for the disbelievers.
009 inna haṯa alqur-āna yahdee lillatee hiya aqwamu wayubašširu almu/mineena allāḏeena yaʿmaloona alssalihati anna lahum ajran kabeerān Lo! this Qur'an guideth unto that which is straightest, and giveth tidings unto the believers who do good works that theirs will be a great reward.
010 waanna allāḏeena lā yu/minoona bial-āḫirati aʿtadnā lahum ʿaṯaban aleemān And that those who believe not in the Hereafter, for them We have prepared a painful doom.
011 wayadʿu al-insānu bialššarri duʿāahu bialḫayri wakāna al-insānu ʿajoolān Man prayeth for evil as he prayeth for good; for man was ever hasty.
012 wajaʿalnā allāyla waalnnahāra āyatayni famaḥawnā āyata allāyli wajaʿalnā āyata alnnahāri mubṣiratan litabtaġoo faḍlan min rabbikum walitaʿlamoo ʿadada alssineena waalḥisāba wakulla šay-in faṣalnāhu tafṣeelān And We appoint the night and the day two portents. Then We make dark the portent of the night, and We make the portent of the day sight-giving, that ye may seek bounty from your Lord, and that ye may know the computation of the years, and the reckoning; and everything have We expounded with a clear expounding.
013 wakulla insānin alzamnāhu ta-irahu fee ʿunuqihi wanuḫriju lahu yawma alqiyāmati kitāban yalqāhu manšoorān And every man's augury have We fastened to his own neck, and We shall bring forth for him on the Day of Resurrection a book which he will find wide open.
014 iqra/ kitābaka kafā binafsika alyawma ʿalayka ḥaseebān (And it will be said unto him): Read thy Book. Thy soul sufficeth as reckoner against thee this day.
015 mani ihtadā fa-innamā yahtadee linafsihi waman ḍalla fa-innamā yaḍillu ʿalayhā walā taziru wāziratun wizra oḫrā wamā kunnā muʿaṯṯibeena ḥattā nabʿaṯa rasoolān Whosoever goeth right, it is only for (the good of) his own soul that he goeth right, and whosoever erreth, erreth only to its hurt. No laden soul can bear another's load, We never punish until we have sent a messenger.
016 wa-iṯa aradnā an nuhlika qaryatan amarnā mutrafeehā fafasaqoo feehā faḥaqqa ʿalayhā alqawlu fadammarnāhā tadmeerān And when We would destroy a township We send commandment to its folk who live at ease, and afterward they commit abomination therein, and so the Word (of doom) hath effect for it, and we annihilate it with complete annihilation.
017 wakam ahlaknā mina alqurooni min baʿdi nooḥin wakafā birabbika biḏunoobi ʿibādihi ḫabeeran baṣeerān How many generations have We destroyed since Noah! And Allah sufficeth as Knower and Beholder of the sins of His slaves.
018 man kāna yureedu alʿājilata ʿajjalnā lahu feehā mā našāo liman nureedu ṯumma jaʿalnā lahu jahannama yaṣlāhā maḏmooman madḥoorān Whoso desireth that (life) which hasteneth away, We hasten for him therein what We will for whom We please. And afterward We have appointed for him hell; he will endure the heat thereof, condemned, rejected.
019 waman arāda al-āḫirata wasaʿā lahā saʿyahā wahuwa mu/minun faolā-ika kāna saʿyuhum maškoorān And whoso desireth the Hereafter and striveth for it with the effort necessary, being a believer; for such, their effort findeth favour (with their Lord).
020 kullan numiddu hāolā-i wahāolā-i min ʿata-i rabbika wamā kāna ʿatao rabbika maḥḏoorān Each do We supply, both these and those, from the bounty of thy Lord. And the bounty of thy Lord can never be walled up.
021 onḏur kayfa faḍalnā baʿḍahum ʿalā baʿḍin walal-āḫiratu akbaru darajātin waakbaru tafḍeelān See how We prefer one of them above another, and verily the Hereafter will be greater in degrees and greater in preferment.
022 lā tajʿal maʿa allāhi ilāhan āḫara fataqʿuda maḏmooman maḫḏoolān Set not up with Allah any other god (O man) lest thou sit down reproved, forsaken.
023 waqada rabbuka allā taʿbudoo illā iyyāhu wabialwālidayni iḥsānan immā yabluġanna ʿindaka alkibara aḥaduhumā aw kilāhumā falā taqul lahumā offin walā tanharhumā waqul lahumā qawlan kareemān Thy Lord hath decreed, that ye worship none save Him, and (that ye show) kindness to parents. If one of them or both of them attain old age with thee, say not "Fie" unto them nor repulse them, but speak unto them a gracious word.
024 waiḫfiḍ lahumā janaha alṯṯulli mina alrraḥmati waqul rabbi irḥamhumā kamā rabbayānee ṣaġeerān And lower unto them the wing of submission through mercy, and say: My Lord! Have mercy on them both as they did care for me when I was little.
025 rabbukum aʿlamu bimā fee nufoosikum in takoonoo saliḥeena fa-innahu kāna lil-awwābeena ġafoorān Your Lord is Best Aware of what is in your minds. If ye are righteous, then lo! He was ever Forgiving unto those who turn (unto Him).
026 waāti ṯa alqurbā ḥaqqahu waalmiskeena waibna alssabeeli walā tubaṯṯir tabḏeerān Give the kinsman his due, and the needy, and the wayfarer, and squander not (thy wealth) in wantonness.
027 inna almubaṯṯireena kānoo iḫwāna alššayateeni wakāna alššaytanu lirabbihi kafoorān Lo! the squanderers were ever brothers of the devils, and the devil was ever an ingrate to his Lord.
028 wa-immā tuʿriḍanna ʿanhumu ibtiġāa raḥmatin min rabbika tarjoohā faqul lahum qawlan maysoorān But if thou turn away from them, seeking mercy from thy Lord, for which thou hopest, then speak unto them a reasonable word.
029 walā tajʿal yadaka maġloolatan ilā ʿunuqika walā tabsuṭhā kulla albasṭi fataqʿuda malooman maḥsoorān And let not thy hand be chained to thy neck nor open it with a complete opening, lest thou sit down rebuked, denuded.
030 inna rabbaka yabsuṭu alrrizqa liman yašāo wayaqdiru innahu kāna biʿibādihi ḫabeeran baṣeerān Lo! thy Lord enlargeth the provision for whom He will, and straiteneth (it for whom He will). Lo, He was ever Knower, Seer of His slaves.
031 walā taqtuloo awlādakum ḫašyata imlāqin naḥnu narzuquhum wa-iyyākum inna qatlahum kāna ḫiṭ-an kabeerān Slay not your children, fearing a fall to poverty, We shall provide for them and for you. Lo! the slaying of them is great sin.
032 walā taqraboo alzzinā innahu kāna fahišatan wasāa sabeelān And come not near unto adultery. Lo! it is an abomination and an evil way.
033 walā taqtuloo alnnafsa allātee ḥarrama allāhu illā bialḥaqqi waman qutila maḏlooman faqad jaʿalnā liwaliyyihi sultanan falā yusrif fee alqatli innahu kāna manṣoorān And slay not the life which Allah hath forbidden save with right. Whoso is slain wrongfully, We have given power unto his heir, but let him not commit excess in slaying. Lo! he will be helped.
034 walā taqraboo māla alyateemi illā biallatee hiya aḥsanu ḥattā yabluġa ašuddahu waawfoo bialʿahdi inna alʿahda kāna mas-oolān Come not near the wealth of the orphan save with that which is better till he come to strength; and keep the covenant. Lo! of the covenant it will be asked.
035 waawfoo alkayla iṯa kiltum wazinoo bialqistasi almustaqeemi ṯalika ḫayrun waaḥsanu ta/weelān Fill the measure when ye measure, and weigh with a right balance; that is meet, and better in the end.
036 walā taqfu mā laysa laka bihi ʿilmun inna alssamʿa waalbaṣara waalfu-āda kullu olā-ika kāna ʿanhu mas-oolān (O man), follow not that whereof thou hast no knowledge. Lo! the hearing and the sight and the heart - of each of these it will be asked.
037 walā tamši fee al-arḍi maraḥan innaka lan taḫriqa al-arḍa walan tabluġa aljibāla ṭoolān And walk not in the earth exultant. Lo! thou canst not rend the earth, nor canst thou stretch to the height of the hills.
038 kullu ṯalika kāna sayyi-ohu ʿinda rabbika makroohān The evil of all that is hateful in the sight of thy Lord.
039 [romanization missing] This is (part) of that wisdom wherewith thy Lord hath inspired thee (O Muhammad). And set not up with Allah any other god, lest thou be cast into hell, reproved, abandoned.
040 afaaṣfākum rabbukum bialbaneena waittaḫaḏa mina almalā-ikati ināṯan innakum lataqooloona qawlan ʿaḏeemān Hath your Lord then distinguished you (O men of Makka) by giving you sons, and hath chosen for Himself females from among the angels ? Lo! verily ye speak an awful word!
041 walaqad ṣarrafnā fee haṯa alqur-āni liyaṯṯakkaroo wamā yazeeduhum illā nufoorān We verily have displayed (Our warnings) in this Qur'an that they may take heed, but it increaseth them in naught save aversion.
042 qul law kāna maʿahu ālihatun kamā yaqooloona iḏan laibtaġaw ilā ḏee alʿarši sabeelān Say (O Muhammad, to the disbelievers): If there were other gods along with Him, as they say, then had they sought a way against the Lord of the Throne.
043 subhanahu wataʿālā ʿammā yaqooloona ʿuluwwan kabeerān Glorified is He, and High Exalted above what they say!
044 tusabbiḥu lahu alssamāwātu alssabʿu waal-arḍu waman feehinna wa-in min šay-in illā yusabbiḥu biḥamdihi walākin lā tafqahoona tasbeeḥahum innahu kāna ḥaleeman ġafoorān The seven heavens and the earth and all that is therein praise Him, and there is not a thing but hymneth His praise; but ye understand not their praise. Lo! He is ever Clement, Forgiving.
045 wa-iṯa qara/ta alqur-āna jaʿalnā baynaka wabayna allāḏeena lā yu/minoona bial-āḫirati ḥijāban mastoorān And when thou recitest the Qur'an we place between thee and those who believe not in the Hereafter a hidden barrier;
046 wajaʿalnā ʿalā quloobihim akinnatan an yafqahoohu wafee aṯanihim waqran wa-iṯa ḏakarta rabbaka fee alqur-āni waḥdahu wallaw ʿalā adbārihim nufoorān And We place upon their hearts veils lest they should understand it, and in their ears a deafness; and when thou makest mention of thy Lord alone in the Qur'an, they turn their backs in aversion.
047 naḥnu aʿlamu bimā yastamiʿoona bihi iḏ yastamiʿoona ilayka wa-iḏ hum najwā iḏ yaqoolu alṯṯālimoona in tattabiʿoona illā rajulan masḥoorān We are Best Aware of what they wish to hear when they give ear to thee and when they take secret counsel, when the evil-doers say: Ye follow but a man bewitched.
048 onḏur kayfa ḍaraboo laka al-amṯāla faḍalloo falā yastaṭeeʿoona sabeelān See what similitudes they coin for thee, and thus are all astray, and cannot find a road!
049 waqāloo a-iṯa kunnā ʿiḏāman warufātan a-innā lamabʿooṯoona ḫalqan jadeedān And they say: When we are bones and fragments, shall we forsooth, be raised up as a new creation ?
050 qul koonoo ḥijāratan aw ḥadeedān Say: Be ye stones or iron
051 aw ḫalqan mimmā yakburu fee ṣudoorikum fasayaqooloona man yuʿeedunā quli allāḏee faṭarakum awwala marratin fasayunġiḍoona ilayka ruoosahum wayaqooloona matā huwa qul ʿasā an yakoona qareebān Or some created thing that is yet greater in your thoughts! Then they will say: Who shall bring us back (to life). Say: He Who created you at the first. Then will they shake their heads at thee, and say: When will it be ? Say: It will perhaps be soon;
052 yawma yadʿookum fatastajeeboona biḥamdihi wataḏunnoona in labiṯtum illā qaleelān A day when He will call you and ye will answer with His praise, and ye will think that ye have tarried but a little while.
053 waqul liʿibādee yaqooloo allātee hiya aḥsanu inna alššaytana yanzaġu baynahum inna alššaytana kāna lil-insāni ʿaduwwan mubeenān Tell My bondmen to speak that which is kindlier. Lo! the devil soweth discord among them. Lo! the devil is for man an open foe.
054 rabbukum aʿlamu bikum in yaša/ yarḥamkum aw in yaša/ yuʿaṯṯibkum wamā arsalnāka ʿalayhim wakeelān Your Lord is Best Aware of you. If He will, He will have mercy on you, or if He will, He will punish you. We have not sent thee (O Muhammad) as a warden over them.
055 warabbuka aʿlamu biman fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi walaqad faḍalnā baʿḍa alnnabiyyeena ʿalā baʿḍin waātaynā dāwooda zaboorān And thy Lord is Best Aware of all who are in the heavens and the earth. And we preferred some of the prophets above others, and unto David We gave the Psalms.
056 quli odʿu allāḏeena zaʿamtum min doonihi falā yamlikoona kašfa alḍurri ʿankum walā taḥweelān Say: Cry unto those (saints and angels) whom ye assume (to be gods) beside Him, yet they have no power to rid you of misfortune nor to change.
057 olā-ika allāḏeena yadʿoona yabtaġoona ilā rabbihimu alwaseelata ayyuhum aqrabu wayarjoona raḥmatahu wayaḫāfoona ʿaṯabahu inna ʿaṯaba rabbika kāna mahṯoorān Those unto whom they cry seek the way of approach to their Lord, which of them shall be the nearest; they hope for His mercy and they fear His doom. Lo! the doom of thy Lord is to be shunned.
058 wa-in min qaryatin illā naḥnu muhlikoohā qabla yawmi alqiyāmati aw muʿaṯṯiboohā ʿaṯaban šadeedan kāna ṯalika fee alkitābi masṭoorān There is not a township but We shall destroy it ere the Day of Resurrection, or punish it with dire punishment. That is set forth in the Book (of Our decrees).
059 wamā manaʿanā an nursila bial-āyāti illā an kaṯṯaba bihā al-awwaloona waātaynā ṯamooda alnnāqata mubṣiratan faḏalamoo bihā wamā nursilu bial-āyāti illā taḫweefān Naught hindereth Us from sending portents save that the folk of old denied them. And We gave Thamud the she-camel - a clear portent save to warn.
060 wa-iḏ qulnā laka inna rabbaka ahata bialnnāsi wamā jaʿalnā alrru/yā allātee araynāka illā fitnatan lilnnāsi waalššajarata almalʿoonata fee alqur-āni wanuḫawwifuhum famā yazeeduhum illā ṭuġyānan kabeerān And (it was a warning) when we told thee: Lo! thy Lord encompasseth mankind, and We appointed the sight which We showed thee as an ordeal for mankind, and (likewise) the Accursed Tree in the Qur'an. We warn them, but it increaseth them in naught save gross impiety.
061 wa-iḏ qulnā lilmalā-ikati osjudoo li-ādama fasajadoo illā ibleesa qāla aasjudu liman ḫalaqta ṭeenān And when We said unto the angels: Fall down prostrate before Adam and they fell prostrate all save Iblis, he said: Shall I fall prostrate before that which Thou hast created of clay ?
062 qāla araaytaka haṯa allāḏee karramta ʿalayya la-in aḫḫartani ilā yawmi alqiyāmati laaḥtanikanna ḏurriyyatahu illā qaleelān He said: Seest Thou this (creature) whom Thou hast honoured above me, if Thou give me grace until the Day of Resurrection I verily will seize his seed, save but a few.
063 qāla iḏhab faman tabiʿaka minhum fa-inna jahannama jazāokum jazāan mawfoorān He said: Go, and whosoever of them followeth thee - lo! hell will be your payment, ample payment.
064 waistafziz mani istaṭaʿta minhum biṣawtika waajlib ʿalayhim biḫaylika warajlika wašārik-hum fee al-amwāli waal-awlādi waʿidhum wamā yaʿiduhumu alššaytanu illā ġuroorān And excite any of them whom thou canst with thy voice, and urge thy horse and foot against them, and be a partner in their wealth and children, and promise them. Satan promiseth them only to deceive.
065 inna ʿibādee laysa laka ʿalayhim sultanun wakafā birabbika wakeelān Lo! My (faithful) bondmen - over them thou hast no power, and thy Lord sufficeth as (their) guardian.
066 rabbukumu allāḏee yuzjee lakumu alfulka fee albaḥri litabtaġoo min faḍlihi innahu kāna bikum raḥeemān (O mankind), your Lord is He Who driveth for you the ship upon the sea that ye may seek of His bounty. Lo! He was ever Merciful toward you.
067 wa-iṯa massakumu alḍurru fee albaḥri ḍalla man tadʿoona illā iyyāhu falammā najjākum ilā albarri aʿraḍtum wakāna al-insānu kafoorān And when harm toucheth you upon the sea, all unto whom ye cry (for succour) fail save Him (alone), but when He bringeth you safe to land, ye turn away, for man was ever thankless.
068 afaamintum an yaḫsifa bikum jāniba albarri aw yursila ʿalaykum hasiban ṯumma lā tajidoo lakum wakeelān Feel ye then secure that He will not cause a slope of the land to engulf you, or send a sand-storm upon you, and then ye will find that ye have no protector ?
069 am amintum an yuʿeedakum feehi tāratan oḫrā fayursila ʿalaykum qasifan mina alrreeḥi fayuġriqakum bimā kafartum ṯumma lā tajidoo lakum ʿalaynā bihi tabeeʿān Or feel ye secure that He will not return you to that (plight) a second time, and send against you a hurricane of wind and drown you for your thanklessness, and then ye will not find therein that ye have any avenger against Us ?
070 walaqad karramnā banee ādama waḥamalnāhum fee albarri waalbaḥri warazaqnāhum mina alttayyibāti wafaḍalnāhum ʿalā kaṯeerin mimman ḫalaqnā tafḍeelān Verily we have honoured the Children of Adam. We carry them on the land and the sea, and have made provision of good things for them, and have preferred them above many of those whom We created with a marked preferment.
071 yawma nadʿoo kulla onāsin bi-imāmihim faman ootiya kitābahu biyameenihi faolā-ika yaqraoona kitābahum walā yuḏlamoona fateelān On the day when We shall summon all men with their record, whoso is given his book in his right hand - such will read their book and they will not be wronged a shred.
072 waman kāna fee haṯihi aʿmā fahuwa fee al-āḫirati aʿmā waaḍallu sabeelān Whoso is blind here will be blind in the Hereafter, and yet further from the road.
073 wa-in kādoo layaftinoonaka ʿani allāḏee awḥaynā ilayka litaftariya ʿalaynā ġayrahu wa-iḏan laittaḫaḏooka ḫaleelān And they indeed strove hard to beguile thee (Muhammad) away from that wherewith We have inspired thee, that thou shouldst invent other than it against Us; and then would they have accepted thee as a friend.
074 walawlā an ṯabbatnāka laqad kidta tarkanu ilayhim šay-an qaleelān And if We had not made thee wholly firm thou mightest almost have inclined unto them a little.
075 iḏan laaḏaqnāka ḍiʿfa alḥayāti waḍiʿfa almamāti ṯumma lā tajidu laka ʿalaynā naṣeerān Then had we made thee taste a double (punishment) of living and a double (punishment) of dying, then hadst thou found no helper against Us.
076 wa-in kādoo layastafizzoonaka mina al-arḍi liyuḫrijooka minhā wa-iḏan lā yalbaṯoona ḫilāfaka illā qaleelān And they indeed wished to scare thee from the land that they might drive thee forth from thence, and then they would have stayed (there) but a little after thee.
077 sunnata man qad arsalnā qablaka min rusulinā walā tajidu lisunnatinā taḥweelān (Such was Our) method in the case of those whom We sent before thee (to mankind), and thou wilt not find for Our method aught of power to change.
078 aqimi alṣalāta lidulooki alššamsi ilā ġasaqi allāyli waqur-āna alfajri inna qur-āna alfajri kāna mašhoodān Establish worship at the going down of the sun until the dark of night, and (the recital of) the Qur'an at dawn. Lo! (the recital of) the Qur'an at dawn is ever witnessed.
079 wamina allāyli fatahajjad bihi nāfilatan laka ʿasā an yabʿaṯaka rabbuka maqāman maḥmoodān And some part of the night awake for it, a largess for thee. It may be that thy Lord will raise thee to a praised estate.
080 waqul rabbi adḫilnee mudḫala ṣidqin waaḫrijnee muḫraja ṣidqin waijʿal lee min ladunka sultanan naṣeerān And say: My Lord! Cause me to come in with a firm incoming and to go out with a firm outgoing. And give me from Thy presence a sustaining Power.
081 waqul jāa alḥaqqu wazahaqa albatilu inna albatila kāna zahooqān And say: Truth hath come and falsehood hath vanished away. Lo! falsehood is ever bound to vanish.
082 wanunazzilu mina alqur-āni mā huwa šifāon waraḥmatun lilmu/mineena walā yazeedu alṯṯālimeena illā ḫasārān And We reveal of the Qur'an that which is a healing and a mercy for believers though it increase the evil-doers in naught save ruin.
083 wa-iṯa anʿamnā ʿalā al-insāni aʿraḍa wanaā bijānibihi wa-iṯa massahu alššarru kāna yaoosān And when We make life pleasant unto man, he turneth away and is averse; and when ill toucheth him he is in despair.
084 qul kullun yaʿmalu ʿalā šākilatihi farabbukum aʿlamu biman huwa ahdā sabeelān Say: Each one doth according to his rule of conduct, and thy Lord is Best Aware of him whose way is right.
085 wayas-aloonaka ʿani alrrooḥi quli alrrooḥu min amri rabbee wamā ooteetum mina alʿilmi illā qaleelān They are asking thee concerning the Spirit. Say: The Spirit is by command of my Lord, and of knowledge ye have been vouchsafed but little.
086 wala-in ši/nā lanaḏhabanna biallaḏee awḥaynā ilayka ṯumma lā tajidu laka bihi ʿalaynā wakeelān And if We willed We could withdraw that which We have revealed unto thee, then wouldst thou find no guardian for thee against Us in respect thereof.
087 illā raḥmatan min rabbika inna faḍlahu kāna ʿalayka kabeerān (It is naught) save mercy from thy Lord. Lo! His kindness unto thee was ever great.
088 qul la-ini ijtamaʿati al-insu waaljinnu ʿalā an ya/too bimiṯli haṯa alqur-āni lā ya/toona bimiṯlihi walaw kāna baʿḍuhum libaʿḍin ḏaheerān Say: Verily, though mankind and the jinn should assemble to produce the like of this Qur'an, they could not produce the like thereof though they were helpers one of another.
089 walaqad ṣarrafnā lilnnāsi fee haṯa alqur-āni min kulli maṯalin faabā akṯaru alnnāsi illā kufoorān And verily We have displayed for mankind in this Qur'an all kind of similitudes, but most of mankind refuse aught save disbelief.
090 waqāloo lan nu/mina laka ḥattā tafjura lanā mina al-arḍi yanbooʿān And they say: We will not put faith in thee till thou cause a spring to gush forth from the earth for us;
091 aw takoona laka jannatun min naḫeelin waʿinabin fatufajjira al-anhāra ḫilālahā tafjeerān Or thou have a garden of date-palms and grapes, and cause rivers to gush forth therein abundantly;
092 aw tusqiṭa alssamāa kamā zaʿamta ʿalaynā kisafan aw ta/tiya biallāhi waalmalā-ikati qabeelān Or thou cause the heaven to fall upon us piecemeal, as thou hast pretended, or bring Allah and the angels as a warrant;
093 aw yakoona laka baytun min zuḫrufin aw tarqā fee alssamā-i walan nu/mina liruqiyyika ḥattā tunazzila ʿalaynā kitāban naqraohu qul subhana rabbee hal kuntu illā bašaran rasoolān Or thou have a house of gold; or thou ascend up into heaven, and even then we will put no faith in thine ascension till thou bring down for us a book that we can read. Say (O Muhammad): My Lord be Glorified! Am I aught save a mortal messenger ?
094 wamā manaʿa alnnāsa an yu/minoo iḏ jāahumu alhudā illā an qāloo abaʿaṯa allāhu bašaran rasoolān And naught prevented mankind from believing when the guidance came unto them save that they said: Hath Allah sent a mortal as (His) messenger ?
095 qul law kāna fee al-arḍi malā-ikatun yamšoona muṭma-inneena lanazzalnā ʿalayhim mina alssamā-i malakan rasoolān Say: If there were in the earth angels walking secure, We had sent down for them from heaven an angel as messenger.
096 qul kafā biallāhi šaheedan baynee wabaynakum innahu kāna biʿibādihi ḫabeeran baṣeerān Say: Allah sufficeth for a witness between me and you. Lo! He is Knower, Seer of His slaves.
097 waman yahdi allāhu fahuwa almuhtadi waman yuḍlil falan tajida lahum awliyāa min doonihi wanaḥšuruhum yawma alqiyāmati ʿalā wujoohihim ʿumyan wabukman waṣumman ma/wāhum jahannamu kullamā ḫabat zidnāhum saʿeerān And he whom Allah guideth, he is led aright; while, as for him whom He sendeth astray, for them thou wilt find no protecting friends beside Him, and We shall assemble them on the Day of Resurrection on their faces, blind, dumb and deaf; their habitation will be hell; whenever it abateth, We increase the flame for them.
098 [romanization missing] That is their reward because they disbelieved Our revelations and said: When we are bones and fragments shall we, forsooth, be raised up as a new creation ?
099 awa lam yaraw anna allāha allāḏee ḫalaqa alssamāwāti waal-arḍa qādirun ʿalā an yaḫluqa miṯlahum wajaʿala lahum ajalan lā rayba feehi faabā alṯṯālimoona illā kufoorān Have they not seen that Allah Who created the heavens and the earth is Able to create the like of them, and hath appointed for them an end whereof there is no doubt ? But the wrong-doers refuse aught save disbelief.
100 qul law antum tamlikoona ḫazā-ina raḥmati rabbee iḏan laamsaktum ḫašyata al-infāqi wakāna al-insānu qatoorān Say (unto them): If ye possessed the treasures of the mercy of my Lord, ye would surely hold them back for fear of spending, for man was ever grudging.
101 walaqad ātaynā moosā tisʿa āyātin bayyinātin fais-al banee isrā-eela iḏ jāahum faqāla lahu firʿawnu innee laaḏunnuka yā moosā masḥoorān And verily We gave unto Moses nine tokens, clear proofs (of Allah's Sovereignty). Do but ask the Children of Israel how he came unto them, then Pharaoh said unto him: Lo! I deem thee one bewitched, O Moses.
102 qāla laqad ʿalimta mā anzala hāolā-i illā rabbu alssamāwāti waal-arḍi basa-ira wa-innee laaḏunnuka yā firʿawnu maṯboorān He said: In truth thou knowest that none sent down these (portents) save the Lord of the heavens and the earth as proofs, and lo! (for my part) I deem thee lost, O Pharaoh.
103 faarāda an yastafizzahum mina al-arḍi faaġraqnāhu waman maʿahu jameeʿān And he wished to scare them from the land, but We drowned him and those with him, all together.
104 waqulnā min baʿdihi libanee isrā-eela oskunoo al-arḍa fa-iṯa jāa waʿdu al-āḫirati ji/nā bikum lafeefān And We said unto the Children of Israel after him: Dwell in the land; but when the promise of the Hereafter cometh to pass We shall bring you as a crowd gathered out of various nations.
105 wabialḥaqqi anzalnāhu wabialḥaqqi nazala wamā arsalnāka illā mubašširan wanaḏeerān With truth have We sent it down, and with truth hath it descended. And We have sent thee as naught else save a bearer of good tidings and a warner.
106 waqur-ānan faraqnāhu litaqraahu ʿalā alnnāsi ʿalā mukṯin wanazzalnāhu tanzeelān And (it is) a Qur'an that We have divided, that thou mayst recite it unto mankind at intervals, and We have revealed it by (successive) revelation.
107 qul āminoo bihi aw lā tu/minoo inna allāḏeena ootoo alʿilma min qablihi iṯa yutlā ʿalayhim yaḫirroona lil-aḏqāni sujjadān Say: Believe therein or believe not, lo! those who were given knowledge before it, when it is read unto them, fall down prostrate on their faces, adoring,
108 wayaqooloona subhana rabbinā in kāna waʿdu rabbinā lamafʿoolān Saying: Glory to our Lord! Verily the promise of our Lord must be fulfilled.
109 wayaḫirroona lil-aḏqāni yabkoona wayazeeduhum ḫušooʿān They fall down on their faces, weeping, and it increaseth humility in them.
110 quli odʿoo allāha awi odʿoo alrraḥmāna ayyan mā tadʿoo falahu al-asmāo alḥusnā walā tajhar biṣalātika walā tuḫāfit bihā waibtaġi bayna ṯalika sabeelān Say (unto mankind): Cry unto Allah, or cry unto the Beneficent, unto whichsoever ye cry (it is the same). His are the most beautiful names. And thou (Muhammad), be not loud-voiced in thy worship nor yet silent therein, but follow a way between.
111 
waquli alḥamdu lillāhi allāḏee lam yattaḫiḏ waladan walam yakun lahu šareekun fee almulki walam yakun lahu waliyyun mina alṯṯulli wakabbirhu takbeerān
And say: Praise be to Allah, Who hath not taken unto Himself a son, and Who hath no partner in the Sovereignty, nor hath He any protecting friend through dependence. And magnify Him with all magnificence.
