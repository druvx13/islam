8. al-Anfal: The Spoils Of War

001 yas-aloonaka ʿani al-anfāli quli al-anfālu lillāhi waalrrasooli faittaqoo allāha waaṣliḥoo ṯata baynikum waaṭeeʿoo allāha warasoolahu in kuntum mu/mineena They ask thee (O Muhammad) of the spoils of war. Say: The spoils of war belong to Allah and the messenger, so keep your duty to Allah, and adjust the matter of your difference, and obey Allah and His messenger, if ye are (true) believers.
002 innamā almu/minoona allāḏeena iṯa ḏukira allāhu wajilat quloobuhum wa-iṯa tuliyat ʿalayhim āyātuhu zādat-hum eemānan waʿalā rabbihim yatawakkaloona They only are the (true) believers whose hearts feel fear when Allah is mentioned, and when His revelations are recited unto them they increase their faith, and who trust in their Lord;
003 allāḏeena yuqeemoona alṣalāta wamimmā razaqnāhum yunfiqoona Who establish worship and spend of that We have bestowed on them.
004 olā-ika humu almu/minoona ḥaqqan lahum darajātun ʿinda rabbihim wamaġfiratun warizqun kareemun Those are they who are in truth believers. For them are grades (of honour) with their Lord, and pardon, and a bountiful provision.
005 kamā aḫrajaka rabbuka min baytika bialḥaqqi wa-inna fareeqan mina almu/mineena lakārihoona Even as thy Lord caused thee (Muhammad) to go forth from thy home with the Truth, and lo! a party of the believers were averse (to it).
006 yujādiloonaka fee alḥaqqi baʿda mā tabayyana kaannamā yusāqoona ilā almawti wahum yanḏuroona Disputing with thee of the Truth after it had been made manifest, as if they were being driven to death visible.
007 wa-iḏ yaʿidukumu allāhu iḥdā altta-ifatayni annahā lakum watawaddoona anna ġayra ṯati alššawkati takoonu lakum wayureedu allāhu an yuḥiqqa alḥaqqa bikalimātihi wayaqṭaʿa dābira alkāfireena And when Allah promised you one of the two bands (of the enemy) that it should be yours, and ye longed that other than the armed one might be yours. And Allah willed that He should cause the Truth to triumph by His words, and cut the root of the disbelievers;
008 liyuḥiqqa alḥaqqa wayubṭila albatila walaw kariha almujrimoona That He might cause the Truth to triumph and bring vanity to naught, however much the guilty might oppose;
009 iḏ tastaġeeṯoona rabbakum faistajāba lakum annee mumiddukum bi-alfin mina almalā-ikati murdifeena When ye sought help of your Lord and He answered you (saying): I will help you with a thousand of the angels, rank on rank.
010 wamā jaʿalahu allāhu illā bušrā walitaṭma-inna bihi quloobukum wamā alnnaṣru illā min ʿindi allāhi inna allāha ʿazeezun ḥakeemun Allah appointed it only as good tidings, and that your hearts thereby might be at rest. Victory cometh only by the help of Allah. Lo! Allah is Mighty, Wise.
011 iḏ yuġaššeekumu alnnuʿāsa amanatan minhu wayunazzilu ʿalaykum mina alssamā-i māan liyuṭahhirakum bihi wayuḏhiba ʿankum rijza alššaytani waliyarbiṭa ʿalā quloobikum wayuṯabbita bihi al-aqdāma When He made the slumber fall upon you as a reassurance from him and sent down water from the sky upon you, that thereby He might purify you, and remove from you the fear of Satan, and make strong your hearts and firm (your) feet thereby.
012 iḏ yooḥee rabbuka ilā almalā-ikati annee maʿakum faṯabbitoo allāḏeena āmanoo saolqee fee quloobi allāḏeena kafaroo alrruʿba faiḍriboo fawqa al-aʿnāqi waiḍriboo minhum kulla banānin When thy Lord inspired the angels, (saying): I am with you. So make those who believe stand firm. I will throw fear into the hearts of those who disbelieve. Then smite the necks and smite of them each finger.
013 [romanization missing] That is because they opposed Allah and His messenger. Whoso opposeth Allah and His messenger, (for him) lo! Allah is severe in punishment.
014 [romanization missing] That (is the award), so taste it, and (know) that for disbelievers is the torment of the Fire.
015 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo iṯa laqeetumu allāḏeena kafaroo zaḥfan falā tuwalloohumu al-adbāra O ye who believe! When ye meet those who disbelieve in battle, turn not your backs to them.
016 waman yuwallihim yawma-iḏin duburahu illā mutaḥarrifan liqitālin aw mutaḥayyizan ilā fi-atin faqad bāa biġaḍabin mina allāhi wama/wāhu jahannamu wabi/sa almaṣeeru Whoso on that day turneth his back to them, unless manoeuvring for battle or intent to join a company, he truly hath incurred wrath from Allah, and his habitation will be hell, a hapless journey's end.
017 falam taqtuloohum walākinna allāha qatalahum wamā ramayta iḏ ramayta walākinna allāha ramā waliyubliya almu/mineena minhu balāan ḥasanan inna allāha sameeʿun ʿaleemun Ye (Muslims) slew them not, but Allah slew them. And thou (Muhammad) threwest not when thou didst throw, but Allah threw, that He might test the believers by a fair test from Him. Lo! Allah is Hearer, Knower.
018 [romanization missing] That (is the case); and (know) that Allah (it is) Who maketh weak the plan of disbelievers.
019 in tastaftiḥoo faqad jāakumu alfatḥu wa-in tantahoo fahuwa ḫayrun lakum wa-in taʿoodoo naʿud walan tuġniya ʿankum fi-atukum šay-an walaw kaṯurat waanna allāha maʿa almu/mineena (O Qureysh!) If ye sought a judgment, now hath the judgment come unto you. And if ye cease (from persecuting the believers) it will be better for you, but if ye return (to the attack) We also shall return. And your host will avail you naught, however numerous it be, and (know) that Allah is with the believers (in His Guidance).
020 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo aṭeeʿoo allāha warasoolahu walā tawallaw ʿanhu waantum tasmaʿoona O ye who believe! Obey Allah and His messenger, and turn not away from him when ye hear (him speak).
021 walā takoonoo kaallaḏeena qāloo samiʿnā wahum lā yasmaʿoona Be not as those who say, we hear, and they hear not.
022 inna šarra alddawābbi ʿinda allāhi alṣummu albukmu allāḏeena lā yaʿqiloona Lo! the worst of beasts in Allah's sight are the deaf, the dumb, who have no sense.
023 walaw ʿalima allāhu feehim ḫayran laasmaʿahum walaw asmaʿahum latawallaw wahum muʿriḍoona Had Allah known of any good in them He would have made them hear, but had He made them hear they would have turned away, averse.
024 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo istajeeboo lillāhi walilrrasooli iṯa daʿākum limā yuḥyeekum waiʿlamoo anna allāha yaḥoolu bayna almar-i waqalbihi waannahu ilayhi tuḥšaroona O ye who believe! Obey Allah, and the messenger when He calleth you to that which quickeneth you, and know that Allah cometh in between the man and his own heart, and that He it is unto Whom ye will be gathered.
025 waittaqoo fitnatan lā tuṣeebanna allāḏeena ḏalamoo minkum ḫassatan waiʿlamoo anna allāha šadeedu alʿiqābi And guard yourselves against a chastisement which cannot fall exclusively on those of you who are wrong-doers, and know that Allah is severe in punishment.
026 waoḏkuroo iḏ antum qaleelun mustaḍʿafoona fee al-arḍi taḫāfoona an yataḫattafakumu alnnāsu faāwākum waayyadakum binaṣrihi warazaqakum mina alttayyibāti laʿallakum taškuroona And remember, when ye were few and reckoned feeble in the land, and were in fear lest men should extirpate you, how He gave you refuge, and strengthened you with His help, and made provision of good things for you, that haply ye might be thankful.
027 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo lā taḫoonoo allāha waalrrasoola wataḫoonoo amānātikum waantum taʿlamoona O ye who believe! Betray not Allah and His messenger, nor knowingly betray your trusts.
028 waiʿlamoo annamā amwālukum waawlādukum fitnatun waanna allāha ʿindahu ajrun ʿaḏeemun And know that your possessions and your children are a test, and that with Allah is immense reward.
029 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmānoo in tattaqoo allāha yajʿal lakum furqānan wayukaffir ʿankum sayyi-ātikum wayaġfir lakum waallāhu ḏoo alfaḍli alʿaḏeemi O ye who believe! If ye keep your duty to Allah, He will give you discrimination (between right and wrong) and will rid you of your evil thoughts and deeds, and will forgive you. Allah is of Infinite Bounty.
030 wa-iḏ yamkuru bika allāḏeena kafaroo liyuṯbitooka aw yaqtulooka aw yuḫrijooka wayamkuroona wayamkuru allāhu waallāhu ḫayru almākireena And when those who disbelieve plot against thee (O Muhammad) to wound thee fatally, or to kill thee or to drive thee forth; they plot, but Allah (also) plotteth; and Allah is the best of plotters.
031 wa-iṯa tutlā ʿalayhim āyātunā qāloo qad samiʿnā law našāo laqulnā miṯla haṯa in haṯa illā asateeru al-awwaleena And when Our revelations are recited unto them they say: We have heard. If we wish we can speak the like of this. Lo! this is naught but fables of the men of old.
032 wa-iḏ qāloo allāhumma in kāna haṯa huwa alḥaqqa min ʿindika faamṭir ʿalaynā ḥijāratan mina alssamā-i awi i/tinā biʿaṯabin aleemin And when they said: O Allah! If this be indeed the truth from Thee, then rain down stones on us or bring on us some painful doom!
033 wamā kāna allāhu liyuʿaṯṯibahum waanta feehim wamā kāna allāhu muʿaṯṯibahum wahum yastaġfiroona But Allah would not punish them while thou wast with them, nor will He punish them while they seek forgiveness.
034 wamā lahum allā yuʿaṯṯibahumu allāhu wahum yaṣuddoona ʿani almasjidi alḥarāmi wamā kānoo awliyāahu in awliyāohu illā almuttaqoona walākinna akṯarahum lā yaʿlamoona What (plea) have they that Allah should not punish them, when they debar (His servants) from the Inviolable Place of Worship, though they are not its fitting guardians. Its fitting guardians are those only who keep their duty to Allah. But most of them know not.
035 wamā kāna ṣalātuhum ʿinda albayti illā mukāan wataṣdiyatan faḏooqoo alʿaṯaba bimā kuntum takfuroona And their worship at the (holy) House is naught but whistling and hand- clapping. Therefore (it is said unto them): Taste of the doom because ye disbelieve.
036 inna allāḏeena kafaroo yunfiqoona amwālahum liyaṣuddoo ʿan sabeeli allāhi fasayunfiqoonahā ṯumma takoonu ʿalayhim ḥasratan ṯumma yuġlaboona waallaḏeena kafaroo ilā jahannama yuḥšaroona Lo! those who disbelieve spend their wealth in order that they may debar (men) from the way of Allah. They will spend it, then it will become an anguish for them, then they will be conquered. And those who disbelieve will be gathered unto hell,
037 liyameeza allāhu alḫabeeṯa mina alttayyibi wayajʿala alḫabeeṯa baʿḍahu ʿalā baʿḍin fayarkumahu jameeʿan fayajʿalahu fee jahannama olā-ika humu alḫāsiroona That Allah may separate the wicked from the good, The wicked will He place piece upon piece, and heap them all together, and consign them unto hell. Such verily are the losers.
038 qul lillaḏeena kafaroo in yantahoo yuġfar lahum mā qad salafa wa-in yaʿoodoo faqad maḍat sunnatu al-awwaleena Tell those who disbelieve that if they cease (from persecution of believers) that which is past will be forgiven them; but if they return (thereto) then the example of the men of old hath already gone (before them, for a warning).
039 waqātiloohum ḥattā lā takoona fitnatun wayakoona alddeenu kulluhu lillāhi fa-ini intahaw fa-inna allāha bimā yaʿmaloona baṣeerun And fight them until persecution is no more, and religion is all for Allah. But if they cease, then lo! Allah is Seer of what they do.
040 wa-in tawallaw faiʿlamoo anna allāha mawlākum niʿma almawlā waniʿma alnnaṣeeru And if they turn away, then know that Allah is your Befriender - a Transcendent Patron, a Transcendent Helper!
041 waiʿlamoo annamā ġanimtum min šay-in faanna lillāhi ḫumusahu walilrrasooli waliḏee alqurbā waalyatāmā waalmasākeeni waibni alssabeeli in kuntum āmantum biallāhi wamā anzalnā ʿalā ʿabdinā yawma alfurqāni yawma iltaqā aljamʿāni waallāhu ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerun And know that whatever ye take as spoils of war, lo! a fifth thereof is for Allah, and for the messenger and for the kinsman (who hath need) and orphans and the needy and the wayfarer, if ye believe in Allah and that which We revealed unto Our slave on the Day of Discrimination, the day when the two armies met. And Allah is Able to do all things.
042 iḏ antum bialʿudwati alddunyā wahum bialʿudwati alquṣwā waalrrakbu asfala minkum walaw tawāʿadtum laiḫtalaftum fee almeeʿādi walākin liyaqḍiya allāhu amran kāna mafʿoolan liyahlika man halaka ʿan bayyinatin wayaḥyā man ḥayya ʿan bayyinatin wa-inna allāha lasameeʿun ʿaleemun When ye were on the near bank (of the valley) and they were on the yonder bank, and the caravan was below you (on the coast plain). And had ye trysted to meet one another ye surely would have failed to keep the tryst, but (it happened, as it did, without the forethought of either of you) that Allah might conclude a thing that must be done; that he who perished (on that day) might perish by a clear proof (of His Sovereignty) and he who survived might survive by a clear proof (of His Sovereignty). Lo! Allah in truth is Hearer, Knower.
043 iḏ yureekahumu allāhu fee manāmika qaleelan walaw arākahum kaṯeeran lafašiltum walatanāzaʿtum fee al-amri walākinna allāha sallama innahu ʿaleemun biṯati alṣudoori When Allah showed them unto thee (O Muhammad) in thy dream as few in number, and if He had shown them to thee as many, ye (Muslims) would have faltered and would have quarrelled over the affair. But Allah saved (you). Lo! He knoweth what is in the breasts (of men).
044 wa-iḏ yureekumoohum iḏi iltaqaytum fee aʿyunikum qaleelan wayuqallilukum fee aʿyunihim liyaqḍiya allāhu amran kāna mafʿoolan wa-ilā allāhi turjaʿu al-omooru And when He made you (Muslims), when ye met (them), see them with your eyes as few, and lessened you in their eyes, (it was) that Allah might conclude a thing that must be done. Unto Allah all things are brought back.
045 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo iṯa laqeetum fi-atan faoṯbutoo waoḏkuroo allāha kaṯeeran laʿallakum tufliḥoona O ye who believe! When ye meet an army, hold firm and think of Allah much, that ye may be successful.
046 waaṭeeʿoo allāha warasoolahu walā tanāzaʿoo fatafšaloo wataḏhaba reeḥukum waiṣbiroo inna allāha maʿa alssabireena And obey Allah and His messenger, and dispute not one with another lest ye falter and your strength depart from you; but be steadfast! Lo! Allah is with the steadfast.
047 walā takoonoo kaallaḏeena ḫarajoo min diyārihim baṭaran wari-āa alnnāsi wayaṣuddoona ʿan sabeeli allāhi waallāhu bimā yaʿmaloona muḥeeṭun Be not as those who came forth from their dwellings boastfully and to be seen of men, and debar (men) from the way of Allah, while Allah is surrounding all they do.
048 wa-iḏ zayyana lahumu alššaytanu aʿmālahum waqāla lā ġāliba lakumu alyawma mina alnnāsi wa-innee jārun lakum falammā tarāati alfi-atāni nakaṣa ʿalā ʿaqibayhi waqāla innee baree-on minkum innee arā mā lā tarawna innee aḫāfu allāha waallāhu šadeedu alʿiqābi And when Satan made their deeds seem fair to them and said: No-one of mankind can conquer you this day, for I am your protector. But when the armies came in sight of one another, he took flight, saying: Lo! I am guiltless of you. Lo! I see that which ye see not. Lo! I fear Allah. And Allah is severe in punishment.
049 iḏ yaqoolu almunāfiqoona waallaḏeena fee quloobihim maraḍun ġarra hāolā-i deenuhum waman yatawakkal ʿalā allāhi fa-inna allāha ʿazeezun ḥakeemun When the hypocrites and those in whose hearts is a disease said: Their religion hath deluded these. Whoso putteth his trust in Allah (will find that) lo! Allah is Mighty, Wise.
050 walaw tarā iḏ yatawaffā allāḏeena kafaroo almalā-ikatu yaḍriboona wujoohahum waadbārahum waḏooqoo ʿaṯaba alḥareeqi If thou couldst see how the angels receive those who disbelieve, smiting faces and their backs and (saying): Taste the punishment of burning!
051 [romanization missing] This is for that which your own hands have sent before (to the Judgment), and (know) that Allah is not a tyrant to His slaves.
052 kada/bi āli firʿawna waallaḏeena min qablihim kafaroo bi-āyāti allāhi faaḫaḏahumu allāhu biḏunoobihim inna allāha qawiyyun šadeedu alʿiqābi (Their way is) as the way of Pharaoh's folk and those before them; they disbelieved the revelations of Allah, and Allah took them in their sins. Lo! Allah is Strong, severe in punishment.
053 [romanization missing] That is because Allah never changeth the grace He hath bestowed on any people until they first change that which is in their hearts, and (that is) because Allah is Hearer, Knower.
054 kada/bi āli firʿawna waallaḏeena min qablihim kaṯṯaboo bi-āyāti rabbihim faahlaknāhum biḏunoobihim waaġraqnā āla firʿawna wakullun kānoo ḏālimeena (Their way is) as the way of Pharaoh's folk and those before them; they denied the revelations of their Lord, so We destroyed them in their sins. And We drowned the folk of Pharaoh. All were evil-doers.
055 inna šarra alddawābbi ʿinda allāhi allāḏeena kafaroo fahum lā yu/minoona Lo! the worst of beasts in Allah's sight are the ungrateful who will not believe.
056 allāḏeena ʿāhadta minhum ṯumma yanquḍoona ʿahdahum fee kulli marratin wahum lā yattaqoona Those of them with whom thou madest a treaty, and then at every opportunity they break their treaty, and they keep not duty (to Allah).
057 fa-immā taṯqafannahum fee alḥarbi fašarrid bihim man ḫalfahum laʿallahum yaṯṯakkaroona If thou comest on them in the war, deal with them so as to strike fear in those who are behind them, that haply they may remember.
058 wa-immā taḫāfanna min qawmin ḫiyānatan fainbiḏ ilayhim ʿalā sawa-in inna allāha lā yuḥibbu alḫā-ineena And if thou fearest treachery from any folk, then throw back to them (their treaty) fairly. Lo! Allah loveth not the treacherous.
059 walā yaḥsabanna allāḏeena kafaroo sabaqoo innahum lā yuʿjizoona And let not those who disbelieve suppose that they can outstrip (Allah's Purpose). Lo! they cannot escape.
060 waaʿiddoo lahum mā istaṭaʿtum min quwwatin wamin ribati alḫayli turhiboona bihi ʿaduwwa allāhi waʿaduwwakum waāḫareena min doonihim lā taʿlamoonahumu allāhu yaʿlamuhum wamā tunfiqoo min šay-in fee sabeeli allāhi yuwaffa ilaykum waantum lā tuḏlamoona Make ready for them all thou canst of (armed) force and of horses tethered, that thereby ye may dismay the enemy of Allah and your enemy, and others beside them whom ye know not. Allah knoweth them. Whatsoever ye spend in the way of Allah it will be repaid to you in full, and ye will not be wronged.
061 wa-in janaḥoo lilssalmi faijnaḥ lahā watawakkal ʿalā allāhi innahu huwa alssameeʿu alʿaleemu And if they incline to peace, incline thou also to it, and trust in Allah. Lo! He, even He, is the Hearer, the Knower.
062 wa-in yureedoo an yaḫdaʿooka fa-inna ḥasbaka allāhu huwa allāḏee ayyadaka binaṣrihi wabialmu/mineena And if they would deceive thee, then lo! Allah is Sufficient for thee. He it is Who supporteth thee with His help and with the believers,
063 waallafa bayna quloobihim law anfaqta mā fee al-arḍi jameeʿan mā allāfta bayna quloobihim walākinna allāha allāfa baynahum innahu ʿazeezun ḥakeemun And (as for the believers) hath attuned their hearts. If thou hadst spent all that is in the earth thou couldst not have attuned their hearts, but Allah hath attuned them. Lo! He is Mighty, Wise.
064 yā ayyuhā alnnabiyyu ḥasbuka allāhu wamani ittabaʿaka mina almu/mineena O Prophet! Allah is Sufficient for thee and those who follow thee of the believers.
065 yā ayyuhā alnnabiyyu ḥarriḍi almu/mineena ʿalā alqitāli in yakun minkum ʿišroona sabiroona yaġliboo mi-atayni wa-in yakun minkum mi-atun yaġliboo alfan mina allāḏeena kafaroo bi-annahum qawmun lā yafqahoona O Prophet! Exhort the believers to fight. If there be of you twenty steadfast they shall overcome two hundred, and if there be of you a hundred (steadfast) they shall overcome a thousand of those who disbelieve, because they (the disbelievers) are a folk without intelligence.
066 al-āna ḫaffafa allāhu ʿankum waʿalima anna feekum ḍaʿfan fa-in yakun minkum mi-atun sabiratun yaġliboo mi-atayni wa-in yakun minkum alfun yaġliboo alfayni bi-iḏni allāhi waallāhu maʿa alssabireena Now hath Allah lightened your burden, for He knoweth that there is weakness in you. So if there be of you a steadfast hundred they shall overcome two hundred, and if there be of you a thousand (steadfast) they shall overcome two thousand by permission of Allah. Allah is with the steadfast.
067 mā kāna linabiyyin an yakoona lahu asrā ḥattā yuṯḫina fee al-arḍi tureedoona ʿaraḍa alddunyā waallāhu yureedu al-āḫirata waallāhu ʿazeezun ḥakeemun It is not for any prophet to have captives until he hath made slaughter in the land. Ye desire the lure of this world and Allah desireth (for you) the Hereafter, and Allah is Mighty, Wise.
068 lawlā kitābun mina allāhi sabaqa lamassakum feemā aḫaḏtum ʿaṯabun ʿaḏeemun Had it not been for an ordinance of Allah which had gone before, an awful doom had come upon you on account of what ye took.
069 fakuloo mimmā ġanimtum ḥalālan ṭayyiban waittaqoo allāha inna allāha ġafoorun raḥeemun Now enjoy what ye have won, as lawful and good, and keep your duty to Allah. Lo! Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.
070 yā ayyuhā alnnabiyyu qul liman fee aydeekum mina al-asrā in yaʿlami allāhu fee quloobikum ḫayran yu/tikum ḫayran mimmā oḫiḏa minkum wayaġfir lakum waallāhu ġafoorun raḥeemun O Prophet! Say unto those captives who are in your hands: If Allah knoweth any good in your hearts He will give you better than that which hath been taken from you, and will forgive you. Lo! Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.
071 wa-in yureedoo ḫiyānataka faqad ḫānoo allāha min qablu faamkana minhum waallāhu ʿaleemun ḥakeemun And if they would betray thee, they betrayed Allah before, and He gave (thee) power over them. Allah is Knower, Wise.
072 inna allāḏeena āmanoo wahājaroo wajāhadoo bi-amwālihim waanfusihim fee sabeeli allāhi waallaḏeena āwaw wanaṣaroo olā-ika baʿḍuhum awliyāo baʿḍin waallaḏeena āmanoo walam yuhājiroo mā lakum min walāyatihim min šay-in ḥattā yuhājiroo wa-ini istanṣarookum fee alddeeni faʿalaykumu alnnaṣru illā ʿalā qawmin baynakum wabaynahum meeṯāqun waallāhu bimā taʿmaloona baṣeerun Lo! those who believed and left their homes and strove with their wealth and their lives for the cause of Allah, and those who took them in and helped them: these are protecting friends one of another. And those who believed but did not leave their homes, ye have no duty to protect them till they leave their homes; but if they seek help from you in the matter of religion then it is your duty to help (them) except against a folk between whom and you there is a treaty. Allah is Seer of what ye do.
073 waallaḏeena kafaroo baʿḍuhum awliyāo baʿḍin illā tafʿaloohu takun fitnatun fee al-arḍi wafasādun kabeerun And those who disbelieve are protectors one of another - If ye do not so, there will be confusion in the land, and great corruption.
074 waallaḏeena āmanoo wahājaroo wajāhadoo fee sabeeli allāhi waallaḏeena āwaw wanaṣaroo olā-ika humu almu/minoona ḥaqqan lahum maġfiratun warizqun kareemun Those who believed and left their homes and strove for the cause of Allah, and those who took them in and helped them - these are the believers in truth. For them is pardon, and bountiful provision.
