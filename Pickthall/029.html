29. al-`Ankabut: The Spider

001 alif-lām-meem Alif. Lam. Mim.
002 aḥasiba alnnāsu an yutrakoo an yaqooloo āmannā wahum lā yuftanoona Do men imagine that they will be left (at ease) because they say, We believe, and will not be tested with affliction ?
003 walaqad fatannā allāḏeena min qablihim falayaʿlamanna allāhu allāḏeena ṣadaqoo walayaʿlamanna alkaṯibeena Lo! We tested those who were before you. Thus Allah knoweth those who are sincere, and knoweth those who feign.
004 am ḥasiba allāḏeena yaʿmaloona alssayyi-āti an yasbiqoonā sāa mā yaḥkumoona Or do those who do ill-deeds imagine that they can outstrip Us ? Evil (for them) is that which they decide.
005 man kāna yarjoo liqāa allāhi fa-inna ajala allāhi laātin wahuwa alssameeʿu alʿaleemu Whoso looketh forward to the meeting with Allah (let him know that) Allah's reckoning is surely nigh, and He is the Hearer, the Knower.
006 waman jāhada fa-innamā yujāhidu linafsihi inna allāha laġaniyyun ʿani alʿālameena And whosoever striveth, striveth only for himself, for lo! Allah is altogether Independent of (His) creatures.
007 waallaḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati lanukaffiranna ʿanhum sayyi-ātihim walanajziyannahum aḥsana allāḏee kānoo yaʿmaloona And as for those who believe and do good works, We shall remit from them their evil deeds and shall repay them the best that they did.
008 wawaṣaynā al-insāna biwālidayhi ḥusnan wa-in jāhadāka litušrika bee mā laysa laka bihi ʿilmun falā tuṭiʿhumā ilayya marjiʿukum faonabbi-okum bimā kuntum taʿmaloona We have enjoined on man kindness to parents; but if they strive to make thee join with Me that of which thou hast no knowledge, then obey them not. Unto Me is your return and I shall tell you what ye used to do.
009 waallaḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati lanudḫilannahum fee alssaliḥeena And as for those who believe and do good works, We verily shall make them enter in among the righteous.
010 wamina alnnāsi man yaqoolu āmannā biallāhi fa-iṯa ooḏiya fee allāhi jaʿala fitnata alnnāsi kaʿaṯabi allāhi wala-in jāa naṣrun min rabbika layaqoolunna innā kunnā maʿakum awa laysa allāhu bi-aʿlama bimā fee ṣudoori alʿālameena Of mankind is he who saith: We believe in Allah, but, if he be made to suffer for the sake of Allah, he mistaketh the persecution of mankind for Allah's punishment; and then, if victory cometh from thy Lord, will say: Lo! we were with you (all the while). Is not Allah Best Aware of what is in the bosoms of (His) creatures ?
011 walayaʿlamanna allāhu allāḏeena āmanoo walayaʿlamanna almunāfiqeena Verily Allah knoweth those who believe, and verily He knoweth the hypocrites.
012 waqāla allāḏeena kafaroo lillaḏeena āmanoo ittabiʿoo sabeelanā walnaḥmil ḫatayākum wamā hum bihamileena min ḫatayāhum min šay-in innahum lakaṯiboona Those who disbelieve say unto those who believe: Follow our way (of religion) and we verily will bear your sins (for you). They cannot bear aught of their sins. Lo! they verily are liars.
013 walayaḥmilunna aṯqālahum waaṯqālan maʿa aṯqālihim walayus-alunna yawma alqiyāmati ʿammā kānoo yaftaroona But they verily will bear their own loads and other loads beside their own, and they verily will be questioned on the Day of Resurrection concerning that which they invented.
014 walaqad arsalnā nooḥan ilā qawmihi falabiṯa feehim alfa sanatin illā ḫamseena ʿāman faaḫaḏahumu alttoofānu wahum ḏālimoona And verily we sent Noah (as Our messenger) unto his folk, and he continued with them for a thousand years save fifty years; and the flood engulfed them, for they were wrong-doers.
015 faanjaynāhu waaṣ-haba alssafeenati wajaʿalnāhā āyatan lilʿālameena And We rescued him and those with him in the ship, and made of it a portent for the peoples.
016 wa-ibrāheema iḏ qāla liqawmihi oʿbudoo allāha waittaqoohu ṯalikum ḫayrun lakum in kuntum taʿlamoona And Abraham! (Remember) when he said unto his folk: Serve Allah, and keep your duty unto Him; that is better for you if ye did but know.
017 innamā taʿbudoona min dooni allāhi awṯānan wataḫluqoona ifkan inna allāḏeena taʿbudoona min dooni allāhi lā yamlikoona lakum rizqan faibtaġoo ʿinda allāhi alrrizqa waoʿbudoohu waoškuroo lahu ilayhi turjaʿoona Ye serve instead of Allah only idols, and ye only invent a lie. Lo! those whom ye serve instead of Allah own no provision for you. So seek your provision from Allah, and serve Him, and give thanks unto Him, (for) unto Him ye will be brought back.
018 wa-in tukaṯṯiboo faqad kaṯṯaba omamun min qablikum wamā ʿalā alrrasooli illā albalāġu almubeenu But if ye deny, then nations have denied before you. The messenger is only to convey (the message) plainly.
019 awa lam yaraw kayfa yubdi-o allāhu alḫalqa ṯumma yuʿeeduhu inna ṯalika ʿalā allāhi yaseerun See they not how Allah produceth creation, then reproduceth it ? Lo! for Allah that is easy.
020 qul seeroo fee al-arḍi faonḏuroo kayfa badaa alḫalqa ṯumma allāhu yunši-o alnnaš-ata al-āḫirata inna allāha ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerun Say (O Muhammad): Travel in the land and see how He originated creation, then Allah bringeth forth the later growth. Lo! Allah is Able to do all things.
021 yuʿaṯṯibu man yašāo wayarḥamu man yašāo wa-ilayhi tuqlaboona He punisheth whom He will and showeth mercy unto whom He will, and unto Him ye will be turned.
022 wamā antum bimuʿjizeena fee al-arḍi walā fee alssamā-i wamā lakum min dooni allāhi min waliyyin walā naṣeerin Ye cannot escape (from Him) in the earth or in the sky, and beside Allah there is for you no friend or helper.
023 waallaḏeena kafaroo bi-āyāti allāhi waliqā-ihi olā-ika ya-isoo min raḥmatee waolā-ika lahum ʿaṯabun aleemun Those who disbelieve in the revelations of Allah and in (their) Meeting with Him, such have no hope of My mercy. For such there is a painful doom.
024 famā kāna jawāba qawmihi illā an qāloo oqtuloohu aw ḥarriqoohu faanjāhu allāhu mina alnnāri inna fee ṯalika laāyātin liqawmin yu/minoona But the answer of his folk was only that they said: "Kill him" or "Burn him." Then Allah saved him from the Fire. Lo! herein verily are portents for folk who believe.
025 waqāla innamā ittaḫaḏtum min dooni allāhi awṯānan mawaddata baynikum fee alḥayāti alddunyā ṯumma yawma alqiyāmati yakfuru baʿḍukum bibaʿḍin wayalʿanu baʿḍukum baʿḍan wama-wākumu alnnāru wamā lakum min nasireena He said: Ye have chosen only idols instead of Allah. The love between you is only in the life of the world. Then on the Day of Resurrection ye will deny each other and curse each other, and your abode will be the Fire, and ye will have no helpers.
026 faāmana lahu looṭun waqāla innee muhājirun ilā rabbee innahu huwa alʿazeezu alḥakeemu And Lot believed him, and said: Lo! I am a fugitive unto my Lord. Lo! He, only He, is the Mighty, the Wise.
027 wawahabnā lahu ishaqa wayaʿqooba wajaʿalnā fee ḏurriyyatihi alnnubuwwata waalkitāba waātaynāhu ajrahu fee alddunyā wa-innahu fee al-āḫirati lamina alssaliḥeena And We bestowed on him Isaac and Jacob, and We established the prophethood and the Scripture among his seed, and We gave him his reward in the world, and lo! in the Hereafter he verily is among the righteous.
028 walooṭan iḏ qāla liqawmihi innakum lata/toona alfahišata mā sabaqakum bihā min aḥadin mina alʿālameena And Lot! (Remember) when he said unto his folk: Lo! ye commit lewdness such as no creature did before you.
029 a-innakum lata/toona alrrijāla wataqṭaʿoona alssabeela wata/toona fee nādeekumu almunkara famā kāna jawāba qawmihi illā an qāloo i/tinā biʿaṯabi allāhi in kunta mina alssadiqeena For come ye not in unto males, and cut ye not the road (for travellers), and commit ye not abomination in your meetings ? But the answer of his folk was only that they said: Bring Allah's doom upon us if thou art a truthteller!
030 qāla rabbi onṣurnee ʿalā alqawmi almufsideena He said: My Lord! Give me victory over folk who work corruption.
031 walammā jāat rusulunā ibrāheema bialbušrā qāloo innā muhlikoo ahli haṯihi alqaryati inna ahlahā kānoo ḏālimeena And when Our messengers brought Abraham the good news, they said: Lo! we are about to destroy the people of that township, for its people are wrong-doers.
032 qāla inna feehā looṭan qāloo naḥnu aʿlamu biman feehā lanunajjiyannahu waahlahu illā imraatahu kānat mina alġābireena He said: Lo! Lot is there. They said: We are best aware of who is there. We are to deliver him and his household, all save his wife, who is of those who stay behind.
033 walammā an jāat rusulunā looṭan see-a bihim wadaqa bihim ḏarʿan waqāloo lā taḫaf walā taḥzan innā munajjooka waahlaka illā imraataka kānat mina alġābireena And when Our messengers came unto Lot, he was troubled upon their account, for he could not protect them; but they said: Fear not, nor grieve! Lo! we are to deliver thee and thy household, (all) save thy wife, who is of those who stay behind.
034 innā munziloona ʿalā ahli haṯihi alqaryati rijzan mina alssamā-i bimā kānoo yafsuqoona Lo! We are about to bring down upon the folk of this township a fury from the sky because they are evil-livers.
035 walaqad taraknā minhā āyatan bayyinatan liqawmin yaʿqiloona And verily of that We have left a clear sign for people who have sense.
036 wa-ilā madyana aḫāhum šuʿayban faqāla yā qawmi oʿbudoo allāha waorjoo alyawma al-āḫira walā taʿṯaw fee al-arḍi mufsideena And unto Midian We sent Shu'eyb, their brother. He said: O my people! Serve Allah, and look forward to the Last Day, and do not evil, making mischief, in the earth.
037 fakaṯṯaboohu faaḫaḏat-humu alrrajfatu faaṣbaḥoo fee dārihim jāṯimeena But they denied him, and the dreadful earthquake took them, and morning found them prostrate in their dwelling place.
038 waʿādan waṯamooda waqad tabayyana lakum min masākinihim wazayyana lahumu alššaytanu aʿmālahum faṣaddahum ʿani alssabeeli wakānoo mustabṣireena And (the tribes of) A'ad and Thamud! (Their fate) is manifest unto you from their (ruined and deserted) dwellings. Satan made their deeds seem fair unto them and so debarred them from the Way, though they were keen observers.
039 waqāroona wafirʿawna wahāmāna walaqad jāahum moosā bialbayyināti faistakbaroo fee al-arḍi wamā kānoo sābiqeena And Korah, Pharaoh and Haman! Moses came unto them with clear proofs (of Allah's Sovereignty), but they were boastful in the land. And they were not winners (in the race).
040 fakullan aḫaḏnā biḏanbihi faminhum man arsalnā ʿalayhi hasiban waminhum man aḫaḏat-hu alṣayḥatu waminhum man ḫasafnā bihi al-arḍa waminhum man aġraqnā wamā kāna allāhu liyaḏlimahum walākin kānoo anfusahum yaḏlimoona So We took each one in his sin; of them was he on whom We sent a hurricane, and of them was he who was overtaken by the (Awful) Cry, and of them was he whom We caused the earth to swallow, and of them was he whom We drowned. It was not for Allah to wrong them, but they wronged themselves.
041 maṯalu allāḏeena ittaḫaḏoo min dooni allāhi awliyāa kamaṯali alʿankabooti ittaḫaḏat baytan wa-inna awhana albuyooti labaytu alʿankabooti law kānoo yaʿlamoona The likeness of those who choose other patrons than Allah is as the likeness of the spider when she taketh unto herself a house, and lo! the frailest of all houses is the spider's house, if they but knew.
042 inna allāha yaʿlamu mā yadʿoona min doonihi min šay-in wahuwa alʿazeezu alḥakeemu Lo! Allah knoweth what thing they invoke instead of Him. He is the Mighty, the Wise.
043 watilka al-amṯālu naḍribuhā lilnnāsi wamā yaʿqiluhā illā alʿālimoona As for these similitudes, We coin them for mankind, but none will grasp their meaning save the wise.
044 khalaqa allāhu alssamāwāti waal-arḍa bialḥaqqi inna fee ṯalika laāyatan lilmu-mineena Allah created the heavens and the earth with truth. Lo! therein is indeed a portent for believers.
045 otlu mā ooḥiya ilayka mina alkitābi waaqimi alṣalāta inna alṣalāta tanhā ʿani alfaḥšā-i waalmunkari walaḏikru allāhi akbaru waallāhu yaʿlamu mā taṣnaʿoona Recite that which hath been inspired in thee of the Scripture, and establish worship. Lo! worship preserveth from lewdness and iniquity, but verily remembrance of Allah is more important. And Allah knoweth what ye do.
046 walā tujādiloo ahla alkitābi illā biallatee hiya aḥsanu illā allāḏeena ḏalamoo minhum waqooloo āmannā biallaḏee onzila ilaynā waonzila ilaykum wa-ilāhunā wa-ilāhukum wahidun wanaḥnu lahu muslimoona And argue not with the People of the Scripture unless it be in (a way) that is better, save with such of them as do wrong; and say: We believe in that which hath been revealed unto us and revealed unto you; our God and your God is One, and unto Him we surrender.
047 wakaṯalika anzalnā ilayka alkitāba faallaḏeena ātaynāhumu alkitāba yu/minoona bihi wamin hāolā-i man yu/minu bihi wamā yajḥadu bi-āyātinā illā alkāfiroona In like manner We have revealed unto thee the Scripture, and those unto whom We gave the Scripture aforetime will believe therein; and of these (also) there are some who believe therein. And none deny Our revelations save the disbelievers.
048 wamā kunta tatloo min qablihi min kitābin walā taḫuttuhu biyameenika iḏan lairtāba almubṭiloona And thou (O Muhammad) wast not a reader of any scripture before it, nor didst thou write it with thy right hand, for then might those have doubted, who follow falsehood.
049 bal huwa āyātun bayyinātun fee ṣudoori allāḏeena ootoo alʿilma wamā yajḥadu bi-āyātinā illā alṯṯālimoona But it is clear revelations in the hearts of those who have been given knowledge, and none deny Our revelations save wrong-doers.
050 waqāloo lawlā onzila ʿalayhi āyātun min rabbihi qul innamā al-āyātu ʿinda allāhi wa-innamā anā naḏeerun mubeenun And they say: Why are not portents sent down upon him from his Lord ? Say: Portents are with Allah only, and I am but a plain warner.
051 awa lam yakfihim annā anzalnā ʿalayka alkitāba yutlā ʿalayhim inna fee ṯalika laraḥmatan waḏikrā liqawmin yu/minoona Is it not enough for them that We have sent down unto thee the Scripture which is read unto them ? Lo! herein verily is mercy, and a reminder for folk who believe.
052 qul kafā biallāhi baynee wabaynakum šaheedan yaʿlamu mā fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi waallaḏeena āmanoo bialbatili wakafaroo biallāhi olā-ika humu alḫāsiroona Say (unto them, O Muhammad): Allah sufficeth for witness between me and you. He knoweth whatsoever is in the heavens and the earth. And those who believe in vanity and disbelieve in Allah, they it is who are the losers.
053 wayastaʿjiloonaka bialʿaṯabi walawlā ajalun musamman lajāahumu alʿaṯabu walaya/tiyannahum baġtatan wahum lā yašʿuroona They bid thee hasten on the doom (of Allah). And if a term had not been appointed, the doom would assuredly have come unto them (ere now). And verily it will come upon them suddenly when they perceive not.
054 yastaʿjiloonaka bialʿaṯabi wa-inna jahannama lamuḥeeṭatun bialkāfireena They bid thee hasten on the doom, when lo! hell verily will encompass the disbelievers
055 yawma yaġšāhumu alʿaṯabu min fawqihim wamin taḥti arjulihim wayaqoolu ḏooqoo mā kuntum taʿmaloona On the day when the doom will overwhelm them from above them and from underneath their feet, and He will say: Taste what ye used to do!
056 yā ʿibādiya allāḏeena āmanoo inna arḍee wāsiʿatun fa-iyyāya faoʿbudooni O my bondmen who believe! Lo! My earth is spacious. Therefor serve Me only.
057 kullu nafsin ṯa-iqatu almawti ṯumma ilaynā turjaʿoona Every soul will taste of death. Then unto Us ye will be returned.
058 waallaḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati lanubawwi-annahum mina aljannati ġurafan tajree min taḥtihā al-anhāru ḫālideena feehā niʿma ajru alʿāmileena Those who believe and do good works, them verily We shall house in lofty dwellings of the Garden underneath which rivers flow. There they will dwell secure. How sweet the guerdon of the toilers,
059 allāḏeena ṣabaroo waʿalā rabbihim yatawakkaloona Who persevere, and put their trust in their Lord!
060 wakaayyin min dābbatin lā taḥmilu rizqahā allāhu yarzuquhā wa-iyyākum wahuwa alssameeʿu alʿaleemu And how many an animal there is that beareth not its own provision! Allah provideth for it and for you. He is the Hearer, the Knower.
061 wala-in saaltahum man ḫalaqa alssamāwāti waal-arḍa wasaḫḫara alššamsa waalqamara layaqoolunna allāhu faannā yu/fakoona And if thou wert to ask them: Who created the heavens and the earth, and constrained the sun and the moon (to their appointed work) ? they would say: Allah. How then are they turned away ?
062 allāhu yabsuṭu alrrizqa liman yašāo min ʿibādihi wayaqdiru lahu inna allāha bikulli šay-in ʿaleemun Allah maketh the provision wide for whom He will of His bondmen, and straiteneth it for whom (He will). Lo! Allah is Aware of all things.
063 wala-in saaltahum man nazzala mina alssamā-i māan faaḥyā bihi al-arḍa min baʿdi mawtihā layaqoolunna allāhu quli alḥamdu lillāhi bal akṯaruhum lā yaʿqiloona And if thou wert to ask them: Who causeth water to come down from the sky, and therewith reviveth the earth after its death ? they verily would say: Allah. Say: Praise be to Allah! But most of them have no sense.
064 wamā haṯihi alḥayātu alddunyā illā lahwun walaʿibun wa-inna alddāra al-āḫirata lahiya alḥayawānu law kānoo yaʿlamoona This life of the world is but a pastime and a game. Lo! the home of the Hereafter - that is Life, if they but knew.
065 fa-iṯa rakiboo fee alfulki daʿawoo allāha muḫliṣeena lahu alddeena falammā najjāhum ilā albarri iṯa hum yušrikoona And when they mount upon the ships they pray to Allah, making their faith pure for Him only, but when He bringeth them safe to land, behold! they ascribe partners (unto Him),
066 liyakfuroo bimā ātaynāhum waliyatamattaʿoo fasawfa yaʿlamoona That they may disbelieve in that which We have given them, and that they may take their ease. But they will come to know.
067 awa lam yaraw annā jaʿalnā ḥaraman āminan wayutaḫattafu alnnāsu min ḥawlihim afabialbatili yu/minoona wabiniʿmati allāhi yakfuroona Have they not seen that We have appointed a sanctuary immune (from violence), while mankind are ravaged all around them ? Do they then believe in falsehood and disbelieve in the bounty of Allah ?
068 waman aḏlamu mimmani iftarā ʿalā allāhi kaḏiban aw kaṯṯaba bialḥaqqi lammā jāahu alaysa fee jahannama maṯwan lilkāfireena Who doeth greater wrong than he who inventeth a lie concerning Allah, or denieth the truth when it cometh unto him ? Is not there a home in hell for disbelievers ?
69 waallaḏeena jāhadoo feenā lanahdiyannahum subulanā wa-inna allāha lamaʿa almuḥsineena [Could not extract English for verse 69]
