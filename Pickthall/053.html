53. an-Najm: The Star

001 waalnnajmi iṯa hawā By the Star when it setteth,
002 mā ḍalla sahibukum wamā ġawā Your comrade erreth not, nor is deceived;
003 wamā yanṭiqu ʿani alhawā Nor doth he speak of (his own) desire.
004 in huwa illā waḥyun yooha It is naught save an inspiration that is inspired,
005 ʿallamahu šadeedu alquwā Which one of mighty powers hath taught him,
006 [romanization missing] One vigorous; and he grew clear to view
007 wahuwa bial-ofuqi al-aʿlā When he was on the uppermost horizon.
008 thumma danā fatadallā Then he drew nigh and came down
009 fakāna qāba qawsayni aw adnā Till he was (distant) two bows' length or even nearer,
010 faawha ilā ʿabdihi mā awha And He revealed unto His slave that which He revealed.
011 mā kaḏaba alfu-ādu mā raā The heart lied not (in seeing) what it saw.
012 afatumāroonahu ʿalā mā yarā Will ye then dispute with him concerning what he seeth ?
013 walaqad raāhu nazlatan oḫrā And verily he saw him yet another time
014 ʿinda sidrati almuntahā By the lote-tree of the utmost boundary,
015 ʿindahā jannatu alma/wā Nigh unto which is the Garden of Abode.
016 iḏ yaġšā alssidrata mā yaġšā When that which shroudeth did enshroud the lote-tree,
017 mā zāġa albaṣaru wamā ṭaġā The eye turned not aside nor yet was overbold.
018 laqad raā min āyāti rabbihi alkubrā Verily he saw one of the greater revelations of his Lord.
019 afaraaytumu allāta waalʿuzzā Have ye thought upon Al-Lat and Al-'Uzza
020 wamanāta alṯṯāliṯata al-oḫrā And Manat, the third, the other ?
021 alakumu alṯṯakaru walahu al-onṯā Are yours the males and His the females ?
022 tilka iḏan qismatun ḍeezā That indeed were an unfair division!
023 in hiya illā asmāon sammaytumoohā antum waābāokum mā anzala allāhu bihā min sultanin in yattabiʿoona illā alṯṯanna wamā tahwā al-anfusu walaqad jāahum min rabbihimu alhudā They are but names which ye have named, ye and your fathers, for which Allah hath revealed no warrant. They follow but a guess and that which (they) themselves desire. And now the guidance from their Lord hath come unto them.
024 am lil-insāni mā tamannā Or shall man have what he coveteth ?
025 falillāhi al-āḫiratu waal-oolā But unto Allah belongeth the after (life), and the former.
026 wakam min malakin fee alssamāwāti lā tuġnee šafāʿatuhum šay-an illā min baʿdi an ya/ḏana allāhu liman yašāo wayarda And how many angels are in the heavens whose intercession availeth naught save after Allah giveth leave to whom He chooseth and accepteth.
027 inna allāḏeena lā yu/minoona bial-āḫirati layusammoona almalā-ikata tasmiyata al-onṯā Lo! it is those who disbelieve in the Hereafter who name the angels with the names of females.
028 wamā lahum bihi min ʿilmin in yattabiʿoona illā alṯṯanna wa-inna alṯṯanna lā yuġnee mina alḥaqqi šay-ān And they have no knowledge thereof. They follow but a guess, and lo! a guess can never take the place of the truth.
029 faaʿriḍ ʿan man tawallā ʿan ḏikrinā walam yurid illā alḥayāta alddunyā Then withdraw (O Muhammad) from him who fleeth from Our remembrance and desireth but the life of the world.
030 [romanization missing] Such is their sum of knowledge. Lo! thy Lord is Best Aware of him who strayeth, and He is Best Aware of him whom goeth right.
031 walillāhi mā fee alssamāwāti wamā fee al-arḍi liyajziya allāḏeena asāoo bimā ʿamiloo wayajziya allāḏeena aḥsanoo bialḥusnā And unto Allah belongeth whatsoever is in the heavens and whatsoever is in the earth, that He may reward those who do evil with that which they have done, and reward those who do good with goodness.
032 allāḏeena yajtaniboona kabā-ira al-iṯmi waalfawahiša illā allāmama inna rabbaka wāsiʿu almaġfirati huwa aʿlamu bikum iḏ anšaakum mina al-arḍi wa-iḏ antum ajinnatun fee buṭooni ommahātikum falā tuzakkoo anfusakum huwa aʿlamu bimani ittaqā Those who avoid enormities of sin and abominations, save the unwilled offences - (for them) lo! thy Lord is of vast mercy. He is Best Aware of you (from the time) when He created you from the earth, and when ye were hidden in the bellies of your mothers. Therefor ascribe not purity unto yourselves. He is Best Aware of him who wardeth off (evil).
033 afaraayta allāḏee tawallā Didst thou (O Muhammad) observe him who turned away,
034 waaʿta qaleelan waakdā And gave a little, then was grudging ?
035 aʿindahu ʿilmu alġaybi fahuwa yarā Hath he knowledge of the Unseen so that he seeth ?
036 am lam yunabba/ bimā fee ṣuḥufi moosā Or hath he not had news of what is in the books of Moses
037 wa-ibrāheema allāḏee waffā And Abraham who paid his debt:
038 allā taziru wāziratun wizra oḫrā That no laden one shall bear another's load,
039 waan laysa lil-insāni illā mā saʿā And that man hath only that for which he maketh effort,
040 waanna saʿyahu sawfa yurā And that his effort will be seen.
041 thumma yujzāhu aljazāa al-awfā And afterward he will be repaid for it with fullest payment;
042 waanna ilā rabbika almuntahā And that thy Lord, He is the goal;
043 waannahu huwa adhaka waabkā And that He it is who maketh laugh, and maketh weep,
044 waannahu huwa amāta waaḥyā And that He it is Who giveth death and giveth life;
045 waannahu ḫalaqa alzzawjayni alṯṯakara waal-onṯā And that He createth the two spouses, the male and the female,
046 min nuṭfatin iṯa tumnā From a drop (of seed) when it is poured forth;
047 waanna ʿalayhi alnnaš-ata al-oḫrā And that He hath ordained the second bringing forth;
048 waannahu huwa aġnā waaqnā And that He it is Who enricheth and contenteth;
049 waannahu huwa rabbu alššiʿrā And that He it is Who is the Lord of Sirius;
050 waannahu ahlaka ʿādan al-oolā And that He destroyed the former (tribe of) A'ad,
051 waṯamooda famā abqā And (the tribe of) Thamud He spared not;
052 waqawma nooḥin min qablu innahum kānoo hum aḏlama waaṭġā And the folk of Noah aforetime, Lo! they were more unjust and more rebellious;
053 waalmu/tafikata ahwā And Al-Mu'tafikah He destroyed
054 faġaššāhā mā ġaššā So that there covered them that which did cover.
055 fabi-ayyi ālā-i rabbika tatamārā Concerning which then, of the bounties of thy Lord, canst thou dispute ?
056 haṯa naḏeerun mina alnnuḏuri al-oolā This is a warner of the warners of old.
057 azifati al-āzifatu The threatened Hour is nigh.
058 laysa lahā min dooni allāhi kāšifatun None beside Allah can disclose it.
059 afamin haṯa alḥadeeṯi taʿjaboona Marvel ye then at this statement,
060 watadhakoona walā tabkoona And laugh and not weep,
061 waantum sāmidoona While ye amuse yourselves ?
62 faosjudoo lillāhi waoʿbudoo [Could not extract English for verse 62]
