30. ar-Rum: The Romans

001 alif-lām-meem Alif. Lam. Mim.
002 ghulibati alrroomu The Romans have been defeated
003 fee adnā al-arḍi wahum min baʿdi ġalabihim sayaġliboona In the nearer land, and they, after their defeat will be victorious
004 fee biḍʿi sineena lillāhi al-amru min qablu wamin baʿdu wayawma-iḏin yafraḥu almu/minoona Within ten years - Allah's is the command in the former case and in the latter - and in that day believers will rejoice
005 binaṣri allāhi yanṣuru man yašāo wahuwa alʿazeezu alrraḥeemu In Allah's help to victory. He helpeth to victory whom He will. He is the Mighty, the Merciful.
006 waʿda allāhi lā yuḫlifu allāhu waʿdahu walākinna akṯara alnnāsi lā yaʿlamoona It is a promise of Allah. Allah faileth not His promise, but most of mankind know not.
007 yaʿlamoona ḏāhiran mina alḥayāti alddunyā wahum ʿani al-āḫirati hum ġāfiloona They know only some appearance of the life of the world, and are heedless of the Hereafter.
008 awa lam yatafakkaroo fee anfusihim mā ḫalaqa allāhu alssamāwāti waal-arḍa wamā baynahumā illā bialḥaqqi waajalin musamman wa-inna kaṯeeran mina alnnāsi biliqā-i rabbihim lakāfiroona Have they not pondered upon themselves ? Allah created not the heavens and the earth, and that which is between them, save with truth and for a destined end. But truly many of mankind are disbelievers in the meeting with their Lord.
009 awa lam yaseeroo fee al-arḍi fayanḏuroo kayfa kāna ʿāqibatu allāḏeena min qablihim kānoo ašadda minhum quwwatan waaṯāroo al-arḍa waʿamaroohā akṯara mimmā ʿamaroohā wajāat-hum rusuluhum bialbayyināti famā kāna allāhu liyaḏlimahum walākin kānoo anfusahum yaḏlimoona Have they not travelled in the land and seen the nature of the consequence for those who were before them ? They were stronger than these in power, and they dug the earth and built upon it more than these have built. Messengers of their own came unto them with clear proofs (of Allah's Sovereignty). Surely Allah wronged them not, but they did wrong themselves.
010 thumma kāna ʿāqibata allāḏeena asāoo alssoo-ā an kaṯṯaboo bi-āyāti allāhi wakānoo bihā yastahzi-oona Then evil was the consequence to those who dealt in evil, because they denied the revelations of Allah and made a mock of them.
011 allāhu yabdao alḫalqa ṯumma yuʿeeduhu ṯumma ilayhi turjaʿoona Allah produceth creation, then He reproduceth it, then unto Him ye will be returned.
012 wayawma taqoomu alssāʿatu yublisu almujrimoona And in the day when the Hour riseth the unrighteous will despair.
013 walam yakun lahum min šurakā-ihim šufaʿāo wakānoo bišurakā-ihim kāfireena There will be none to intercede for them of those whom they made equal with Allah. And they will reject their partners (whom they ascribed unto Him).
014 wayawma taqoomu alssāʿatu yawma-iḏin yatafarraqoona In the day when the Hour cometh, in that day they will be sundered.
015 faammā allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati fahum fee rawḍatin yuḥbaroona As for those who believed and did good works, they will be made happy in a Garden.
016 waammā allāḏeena kafaroo wakaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā waliqā-i al-āḫirati faolā-ika fee alʿaṯabi muhdaroona But as for those who disbelieved and denied Our revelations, and denied the meeting of the Hereafter, such will be brought to doom.
017 fasubhana allāhi ḥeena tumsoona waḥeena tuṣbiḥoona So glory be to Allah when ye enter the night and when ye enter the morning -
018 walahu alḥamdu fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi waʿašiyyan waḥeena tuḏhiroona Unto Him be praise in the heavens and the earth! - and at the sun's decline and in the noonday.
019 yuḫriju alḥayya mina almayyiti wayuḫriju almayyita mina alḥayyi wayuḥyee al-arḍa baʿda mawtihā wakaṯalika tuḫrajoona He bringeth forth the living from the dead, and He bringeth forth the dead from the living, and He reviveth the earth after her death. And even so will ye be brought forth.
020 wamin āyātihi an ḫalaqakum min turābin ṯumma iṯa antum bašarun tantaširoona And of His signs is this: He created you of dust, and behold you human beings, ranging widely!
021 wamin āyātihi an ḫalaqa lakum min anfusikum azwājan litaskunoo ilayhā wajaʿala baynakum mawaddatan waraḥmatan inna fee ṯalika laāyātin liqawmin yatafakkaroona And of His signs is this: He created for you helpmeets from yourselves that ye might find rest in them, and He ordained between you love and mercy. Lo! herein indeed are portents for folk who reflect.
022 wamin āyātihi ḫalqu alssamāwāti waal-arḍi waiḫtilāfu alsinatikum waalwānikum inna fee ṯalika laāyātin lilʿālimeena And of His signs is the creation of the heavens and the earth, and the difference of your languages and colours. Lo! herein indeed are portents for men of knowledge.
023 wamin āyātihi manāmukum biallayli waalnnahāri waibtiġāokum min faḍlihi inna fee ṯalika laāyātin liqawmin yasmaʿoona And of His signs is your slumber by night and by day, and your seeking of His bounty. Lo! herein indeed are portents for folk who heed.
024 wamin āyātihi yureekumu albarqa ḫawfan waṭamaʿan wayunazzilu mina alssamā-i māan fayuḥyee bihi al-arḍa baʿda mawtihā inna fee ṯalika laāyātin liqawmin yaʿqiloona And of His signs is this: He showeth you the lightning for a fear and for a hope, and sendeth down water from the sky, and thereby quickeneth the earth after her death. Lo! herein indeed are portents for folk who understand.
025 wamin āyātihi an taqooma alssamāo waal-arḍu bi-amrihi ṯumma iṯa daʿākum daʿwatan mina al-arḍi iṯa antum taḫrujoona And of His signs is this: The heavens and the earth stand fast by His command, and afterward, when He calleth you, lo! from the earth ye will emerge.
026 walahu man fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi kullun lahu qānitoona Unto Him belongeth whosoever is in the heavens and the earth. All are obedient unto Him.
027 wahuwa allāḏee yabdao alḫalqa ṯumma yuʿeeduhu wahuwa ahwanu ʿalayhi walahu almaṯalu al-aʿlā fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wahuwa alʿazeezu alḥakeemu He it is Who produceth creation, then reproduceth it, and it is easier for Him. His is the Sublime Similitude in the heavens and the earth. He is the Mighty, the Wise.
028 [romanization missing] He coineth for you a similitude of yourselves. Have ye, from among those whom your right hands possess, partners in the wealth We have bestowed upon you, equal with you in respect thereof, so that ye fear them as ye fear each other (that ye ascribe unto Us partners out of that which We created) ? Thus We display the revelations for people who have sense.
029 bali ittabaʿa allāḏeena ḏalamoo ahwāahum biġayri ʿilmin faman yahdee man aḍalla allāhu wamā lahum min nasireena Nay, but those who do wrong follow their own lusts without knowledge. Who is able to guide him whom Allah hath sent astray ? For such there are no helpers.
030 faaqim wajhaka lilddeeni ḥaneefan fiṭrata allāhi allātee faṭara alnnāsa ʿalayhā lā tabdeela liḫalqi allāhi ṯalika alddeenu alqayyimu walākinna akṯara alnnāsi lā yaʿlamoona So set thy purpose (O Muhammad) for religion as a man by nature upright - the nature (framed) of Allah, in which He hath created man. There is no altering (the laws of) Allah's creation. That is the right religion, but most men know not -
031 muneebeena ilayhi waittaqoohu waaqeemoo alṣalāta walā takoonoo mina almušrikeena Turning unto Him (only); and be careful of your duty unto Him and establish worship, and be not of those who ascribe partners (unto Him);
032 mina allāḏeena farraqoo deenahum wakānoo šiyaʿan kullu ḥizbin bimā ladayhim fariḥoona Of those who split up their religion and became schismatics, each sect exulting in its tenets.
033 wa-iṯa massa alnnāsa ḍurrun daʿaw rabbahum muneebeena ilayhi ṯumma iṯa aṯaqahum minhu raḥmatan iṯa fareequn minhum birabbihim yušrikoona And when harm toucheth men they cry unto their Lord, turning to Him in repentance; then, when they have tasted of His mercy, behold! some of them attribute partners to their Lord
034 liyakfuroo bimā ātaynāhum fatamattaʿoo fasawfa taʿlamoona So as to disbelieve in that which We have given them. (Unto such it is said): Enjoy yourselves awhile, but ye will come to know.
035 am anzalnā ʿalayhim sultanan fahuwa yatakallamu bimā kānoo bihi yušrikoona Or have We revealed unto them any warrant which speaketh of that which they associate with Him ?
036 waiṯa aḏaqnā alnnāsa raḥmatan fariḥoo bihā wa-in tuṣibhum sayyi-atun bimā qaddamat aydeehim iṯa hum yaqnaṭoona And when We cause mankind to taste of mercy they rejoice therein; but if an evil thing befall them as the consequence of their own deeds, lo! they are in despair!
037 awa lam yaraw anna allāha yabsuṭu alrrizqa liman yašāo wayaqdiru inna fee ṯalika laāyātin liqawmin yu/minoona See they not that Allah enlargeth the provision for whom He will, and straiteneth (it for whom He will). Lo! herein indeed are portents for folk who believe.
038 faāti ṯa alqurbā ḥaqqahu waalmiskeena waibna alssabeeli ṯalika ḫayrun lillaḏeena yureedoona wajha allāhi waolā-ika humu almufliḥoona So give to the kinsman his due, and to the needy, and to the wayfarer. That is best for those who seek Allah's Countenance. And such are they who are successful.
039 wamā ātaytum min riban liyarbuwa fee amwāli alnnāsi falā yarboo ʿinda allāhi wamā ātaytum min zakātin tureedoona wajha allāhi faolā-ika humu almuḍʿifoona That which ye give in usury in order that it may increase on (other) people's property hath no increase with Allah; but that which ye give in charity, seeking Allah's Countenance, hath increase manifold.
040 allāhu allāḏee ḫalaqakum ṯumma razaqakum ṯumma yumeetukum ṯumma yuḥyeekum hal min šurakā-ikum man yafʿalu min ṯalikum min šay-in subhanahu wataʿālā ʿammā yušrikoona Allah is He Who created you and then sustained you, then causeth you to die, then giveth life to you again. Is there any of your (so-called) partners (of Allah) that doeth aught of that ? Praised and Exalted be He above what they associate (with Him)!
041 [romanization missing] Corruption doth appear on land and sea because of (the evil) which men's hands have done, that He may make them taste a part of that which they have done, in order that they may return.
042 qul seeroo fee al-arḍi faonḏuroo kayfa kāna ʿāqibatu allāḏeena min qablu kāna akṯaruhum mušrikeena Say (O Muhammad, to the disbelievers): Travel in the land, and see the nature of the consequence for those who were before you! Most of them were idolaters.
043 faaqim wajhaka lilddeeni alqayyimi min qabli an ya/tiya yawmun lā maradda lahu mina allāhi yawma-iḏin yaṣaddaʿoona So set thy purpose resolutely for the right religion, before the inevitable day cometh from Allah. On that day mankind will be sundered-
044 man kafara faʿalayhi kufruhu waman ʿamila saliḥan fali-anfusihim yamhadoona Whoso disbelieveth must (then) bear the consequences of his disbelief, while those who do right make provision for themselves -
045 liyajziya allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati min faḍlihi innahu lā yuḥibbu alkāfireena That He may reward out of His bounty those who believe and do good works. Lo! He loveth not the disbelievers (in His guidance).
046 wamin āyātihi an yursila alrriyaha mubašširātin waliyuḏeeqakum min raḥmatihi walitajriya alfulku bi-amrihi walitabtaġoo min faḍlihi walaʿallakum taškuroona And of His signs is this: He sendeth herald winds to make you taste His mercy, and that the ships may sail at His command, and that ye may seek his favour, and that haply ye may be thankful.
047 walaqad arsalnā min qablika rusulan ilā qawmihim fajāoohum bialbayyināti faintaqamnā mina allāḏeena ajramoo wakāna ḥaqqan ʿalaynā naṣru almu/mineena Verily We sent before thee (Muhammad) messengers to their own folk. Then we took vengeance upon those who were guilty (in regard to them). To help believers is ever incumbent upon Us.
048 allāhu allāḏee yursilu alrriyaha fatuṯeeru sahaban fayabsuṭuhu fee alssamā-i kayfa yašāo wayajʿaluhu kisafan fatarā alwadqa yaḫruju min ḫilālihi fa-iṯa asaba bihi man yašāo min ʿibādihi iṯa hum yastabširoona Allah is He Who sendeth the winds so that they raise clouds, and spreadeth them along the sky as pleaseth Him, and causeth them to break and thou seest the rain downpouring from within them. And when He maketh it to fall on whom He will of His bondmen, lo! they rejoice;
049 wa-in kānoo min qabli an yunazzala ʿalayhim min qablihi lamubliseena Though before that, even before it was sent down upon them, they were in despair.
050 faonḏur ilā āṯāri raḥmati allāhi kayfa yuḥyee al-arḍa baʿda mawtihā inna ṯalika lamuḥyee almawtā wahuwa ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerun Look, therefore, at the prints of Allah's mercy (in creation): how He quickeneth the earth after her death. Lo! He verily is the Quickener of the Dead, and He is Able to do all things.
051 wala-in arsalnā reeḥan faraawhu muṣfarran laḏalloo min baʿdihi yakfuroona And if We sent a wind and they beheld it yellow, they verily would still continue in their disbelief.
052 fa-innaka lā tusmiʿu almawtā walā tusmiʿu alṣumma aldduʿāa iṯa wallaw mudbireena For verily thou (Muhammad) canst not make the dead to hear, nor canst thou make the deaf to hear the call when they have turned to flee.
053 wamā anta bihādi alʿumyi ʿan ḍalālatihim in tusmiʿu illā man yu/minu bi-āyātinā fahum muslimoona Nor canst thou guide the blind out of their error. Thou canst make none to hear save those who believe in Our revelations so that they surrender (unto Him).
054 allāhu allāḏee ḫalaqakum min ḍaʿfin ṯumma jaʿala min baʿdi ḍaʿfin quwwatan ṯumma jaʿala min baʿdi quwwatin ḍaʿfan wašaybatan yaḫluqu mā yašāo wahuwa alʿaleemu alqadeeru Allah is He Who shaped you out of weakness, then appointed after weakness strength, then, after strength, appointed weakness and grey hair. He createth what He will. He is the Knower, the Mighty.
055 wayawma taqoomu alssāʿatu yuqsimu almujrimoona mā labiṯoo ġayra sāʿatin kaṯalika kānoo yu/fakoona And on the day when the Hour riseth the guilty will vow that they did tarry but an hour - thus were they ever deceived.
056 waqāla allāḏeena ootoo alʿilma waal-eemāna laqad labiṯtum fee kitābi allāhi ilā yawmi albaʿṯi fahaṯa yawmu albaʿṯi walākinnakum kuntum lā taʿlamoona But those to whom knowledge and faith are given will say: The truth is, ye have tarried, by Allah's decree, until the Day of Resurrection. This is the Day of Resurrection, but ye used not to know.
057 fayawma-iḏin lā yanfaʿu allāḏeena ḏalamoo maʿḏiratuhum walā hum yustaʿtaboona In that day their excuses will not profit those who did injustice, nor will they be allowed to make amends.
058 walaqad ḍarabnā lilnnāsi fee haṯa alqur-āni min kulli maṯalin wala-in ji/tahum bi-āyatin layaqoolanna allāḏeena kafaroo in antum illā mubṭiloona Verily We have coined for mankind in this Qur'an all kinds of similitudes; and indeed if thou camest unto them with a miracle, those who disbelieve would verily exclaim: Ye are but tricksters!
059 kaṯalika yaṭbaʿu allāhu ʿalā quloobi allāḏeena lā yaʿlamoona Thus doth Allah seal the hearts of those who know not.
60 faiṣbir inna waʿda allāhi ḥaqqun walā yastaḫiffannaka allāḏeena lā yooqinoona [Could not extract English for verse 60]
