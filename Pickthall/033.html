33. al-Ahzab: The Allies

001 yā ayyuhā alnnabiyyu ittaqi allāha walā tuṭiʿi alkāfireena waalmunāfiqeena inna allāha kāna ʿaleeman ḥakeemān O Prophet! Keep thy duty to Allah and obey not the disbelievers and the hypocrites. Lo! Allah is Knower, Wise.
002 waittabiʿ mā yooha ilayka min rabbika inna allāha kāna bimā taʿmaloona ḫabeerān And follow that which is inspired in thee from thy Lord. Lo! Allah is Aware of what ye do.
003 watawakkal ʿalā allāhi wakafā biallāhi wakeelān And put thy trust in Allah, for Allah is sufficient as Trustee.
004 mā jaʿala allāhu lirajulin min qalbayni fee jawfihi wamā jaʿala azwājakumu allā-ee tuḏāhiroona minhunna ommahātikum wamā jaʿala adʿiyāakum abnāakum ṯalikum qawlukum bi-afwāhikum waallāhu yaqoolu alḥaqqa wahuwa yahdee alssabeela Allah hath not assigned unto any man two hearts within his body, nor hath He made your wives whom ye declare (to be your mothers) your mothers, nor hath He made those whom ye claim (to be your sons) your sons. This is but a saying of your mouths. But Allah saith the truth and He showeth the way.
005 odʿoohum li-ābā-ihim huwa aqsaṭu ʿinda allāhi fa-in lam taʿlamoo ābāahum fa-iḫwānukum fee alddeeni wamawāleekum walaysa ʿalaykum junahun feemā aḫṭa/tum bihi walākin mā taʿammadat quloobukum wakāna allāhu ġafooran raḥeemān Proclaim their real parentage. That will be more equitable in the sight of Allah. And if ye know not their fathers, then (they are) your brethren in the faith, and your clients. And there is no sin for you in the mistakes that ye make unintentionally, but what your hearts purpose (that will be a sin for you). Allah is ever Forgiving, Merciful.
006 alnnabiyyu awlā bialmu/mineena min anfusihim waazwājuhu ommahātuhum waoloo al-arhami baʿḍuhum awlā bibaʿḍin fee kitābi allāhi mina almu/mineena waalmuhājireena illā an tafʿaloo ilā awliyā-ikum maʿroofan kāna ṯalika fee alkitābi masṭoorān The Prophet is closer to the believers than their selves, and his wives are (as) their mothers. And the owners of kinship are closer one to another in the ordinance of Allah than (other) believers and the fugitives (who fled from Mecca), except that ye should do kindness to your friends. This is written in the Book (of nature).
007 wa-iḏ aḫaḏnā mina alnnabiyyeena meeṯāqahum waminka wamin nooḥin wa-ibrāheema wamoosā waʿeesā ibni maryama waaḫaḏnā minhum meeṯāqan ġaleeḏān And when We exacted a covenant from the prophets, and from thee (O Muhammad) and from Noah and Abraham and Moses and Jesus son of Mary. We took from them a solemn covenant;
008 liyas-ala alssadiqeena ʿan ṣidqihim waaʿadda lilkāfireena ʿaṯaban aleemān That He may ask the loyal of their loyalty. And He hath prepared a painful doom for the unfaithful.
009 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo oḏkuroo niʿmata allāhi ʿalaykum iḏ jāatkum junoodun faarsalnā ʿalayhim reeḥan wajunoodan lam tarawhā wakāna allāhu bimā taʿmaloona baṣeerān O ye who believe! Remember Allah's favour unto you when there came against you hosts, and We sent against them a great wind and hosts ye could not see. And Allah is ever Seer of what ye do.
010 iḏ jāookum min fawqikum wamin asfala minkum wa-iḏ zāġati al-absaru wabalaġati alquloobu alḥanājira wataḏunnoona biallāhi alṯṯunoonā When they came upon you from above you and from below you, and when eyes grew wild and hearts reached to the throats, and ye were imagining vain thoughts concerning Allah.
011 hunālika ibtuliya almu-minoona wazulziloo zilzālan šadeedān There were the believers sorely tried, and shaken with a mighty shock.
012 wa-iḏ yaqoolu almunāfiqoona waallaḏeena fee quloobihim maraḍun mā waʿadanā allāhu warasooluhu illā ġuroorān And when the hypocrites, and those in whose hearts is a disease, were saying: Allah and His messenger promised us naught but delusion.
013 wa-iḏ qālat ta-ifatun minhum yā ahla yaṯriba lā muqāma lakum fairjiʿoo wayasta/ḏinu fareequn minhumu alnnabiyya yaqooloona inna buyootanā ʿawratun wamā hiya biʿawratin in yureedoona illā firārān And when a party of them said: O folk of Yathrib! There is no stand (possible) for you, therefor turn back. And certain of them (even) sought permission of the Prophet, saying: Our homes lie open (to the enemy). And they lay not open. They but wished to flee.
014 walaw duḫilat ʿalayhim min aqtarihā ṯumma su-iloo alfitnata laātawhā wamā talabbaṯoo bihā illā yaseerān If the enemy had entered from all sides and they had been exhorted to treachery, they would have committed it, and would have hesitated thereupon but little.
015 walaqad kānoo ʿāhadoo allāha min qablu lā yuwalloona al-adbāra wakāna ʿahdu allāhi mas-oolān And verily they had already sworn unto Allah that they would not turn their backs (to the foe). An oath to Allah must be answered for.
016 qul lan yanfaʿakumu alfirāru in farartum mina almawti awi alqatli wa-iḏan lā tumattaʿoona illā qaleelān Say: Flight will not avail you if ye flee from death or killing, and then ye dwell in comfort but a little while.
017 qul man ṯa allāḏee yaʿṣimukum mina allāhi in arāda bikum soo-an aw arāda bikum raḥmatan walā yajidoona lahum min dooni allāhi waliyyan walā naṣeerān Say: Who is he who can preserve you from Allah if He intendeth harm for you, or intendeth mercy for you. They will not find that they have any friend or helper other than Allah.
018 qad yaʿlamu allāhu almuʿawwiqeena minkum waalqā-ileena li-iḫwānihim halumma ilaynā walā ya/toona alba/sa illā qaleelān Allah already knoweth those of you who hinder, and those who say unto their brethren: "Come ye hither unto us!" and they come not to the stress of battle save a little,
019 ašihhatan ʿalaykum fa-iṯa jāa alḫawfu raaytahum yanḏuroona ilayka tadooru aʿyunuhum kaallaḏee yuġšā ʿalayhi mina almawti fa-iṯa ḏahaba alḫawfu salaqookum bi-alsinatin ḥidādin ašihhatan ʿalā alḫayri olā-ika lam yu/minoo faaḥbaṭa allāhu aʿmālahum wakāna ṯalika ʿalā allāhi yaseerān Being sparing of their help to you (believers). But when the fear cometh, then thou (Muhammad) seest them regarding thee with rolling eyes like one who fainteth unto death. Then, when the fear departeth, they scald you with sharp tongues in their greed for wealth (from the spoil). Such have not believed. Therefor Allah maketh their deeds fruitless. And that is easy for Allah.
020 yaḥsaboona al-aḥzāba lam yaḏhaboo wa-in ya/ti al-aḥzābu yawaddoo law annahum bādoona fee al-aʿrābi yas-aloona ʿan anbā-ikum walaw kānoo feekum mā qātaloo illā qaleelān They hold that the clans have not retired (for good); and if the clans should advance (again), they would fain be in the desert with the wandering Arabs, asking for the news of you; and if they were among you, they would not give battle, save a little.
021 laqad kāna lakum fee rasooli allāhi oswatun ḥasanatun liman kāna yarjoo allāha waalyawma al-āḫira waḏakara allāha kaṯeerān Verily in the messenger of Allah ye have a good example for him who looketh unto Allah and the Last Day, and remembereth Allah much.
022 walammā raā almu/minoona al-aḥzāba qāloo haṯa mā waʿadanā allāhu warasooluhu waṣadaqa allāhu warasooluhu wamā zādahum illā eemānan watasleemān And when the true believers saw the clans, they said: This is that which Allah and His messenger promised us. Allah and His messenger are true. It did but confirm them in their faith and resignation.
023 mina almu/mineena rijālun ṣadaqoo mā ʿāhadoo allāha ʿalayhi faminhum man qada naḥbahu waminhum man yantaḏiru wamā baddaloo tabdeelān Of the believers are men who are true to that which they covenanted with Allah. Some of them have paid their vow by death (in battle), and some of them still are waiting; and they have not altered in the least;
024 liyajziya allāhu alssadiqeena biṣidqihim wayuʿaṯṯiba almunāfiqeena in šāa aw yatooba ʿalayhim inna allāha kāna ġafooran raḥeemān That Allah may reward the true men for their truth, and punish the hypocrites if He will, or relent toward them (if He will). Lo! Allah is Forgiving, Merciful.
025 waradda allāhu allāḏeena kafaroo biġayḏihim lam yanāloo ḫayran wakafā allāhu almu/mineena alqitāla wakāna allāhu qawiyyan ʿazeezān And Allah repulsed the disbelievers in their wrath; they gained no good. Allah averted their attack from the believers. Allah is ever Strong, Mighty.
026 waanzala allāḏeena ḏāharoohum min ahli alkitābi min ṣayaseehim waqaḏafa fee quloobihimu alrruʿba fareeqan taqtuloona wata/siroona fareeqān And He brought those of the People of the Scripture who supported them down from their strongholds, and cast panic into their hearts. Some ye slew, and ye made captive some.
027 waawraṯakum arḍahum wadiyārahum waamwālahum waarḍan lam taṭaoohā wakāna allāhu ʿalā kulli šay-in qadeerān And He caused you to inherit their land and their houses and their wealth, and land ye have not trodden. Allah is ever Able to do all things.
028 yā ayyuhā alnnabiyyu qul li-azwājika in kuntunna turidna alḥayāta alddunyā wazeenatahā fataʿālayna omattiʿkunna waosarriḥkunna sarahan jameelān O Prophet! Say unto thy wives: If ye desire the world's life and its adornment, come! I will content you and will release you with a fair release.
029 wa-in kuntunna turidna allāha warasoolahu waalddāra al-āḫirata fa-inna allāha aʿadda lilmuḥsināti minkunna ajran ʿaḏeemān But if ye desire Allah and His messenger and the abode of the Hereafter, then lo! Allah hath prepared for the good among you an immense reward.
030 yā nisāa alnnabiyyi man ya/ti minkunna bifahišatin mubayyinatin yudaʿaf lahā alʿaṯabu ḍiʿfayni wakāna ṯalika ʿalā allāhi yaseerān O ye wives of the Prophet! Whosoever of you committeth manifest lewdness, the punishment for her will be doubled, and that is easy for Allah.
031 waman yaqnut minkunna lillāhi warasoolihi wataʿmal saliḥan nu/tihā ajrahā marratayni waaʿtadnā lahā rizqan kareemān And whosoever of you is submissive unto Allah and His messenger and doeth right, We shall give her her reward twice over, and We have prepared for her a rich provision.
032 yā nisāa alnnabiyyi lastunna kaaḥadin mina alnnisā-i ini ittaqaytunna falā taḫḍaʿna bialqawli fayaṭmaʿa allāḏee fee qalbihi maraḍun waqulna qawlan maʿroofān O ye wives of the Prophet! Ye are not like any other women. If ye keep your duty (to Allah), then be not soft of speech, lest he in whose heart is a disease aspire (to you), but utter customary speech.
033 waqarna fee buyootikunna walā tabarrajna tabarruja aljāhiliyyati al-oolā waaqimna alṣalāta waāteena alzzakāta waaṭiʿna allāha warasoolahu innamā yureedu allāhu liyuḏhiba ʿankumu alrrijsa ahla albayti wayuṭahhirakum taṭheerān And stay in your houses. Bedizen not yourselves with the bedizenment of the Time of Ignorance. Be regular in prayer, and pay the poor-due, and obey Allah and His messenger. Allah's wish is but to remove uncleanness far from you, O Folk of the Household, and cleanse you with a thorough cleansing.
034 waoḏkurna mā yutlā fee buyootikunna min āyāti allāhi waalḥikmati inna allāha kāna laṭeefan ḫabeerān And bear in mind that which is recited in your houses of the revelations of Allah and wisdom. Lo! Allah is Subtile, Aware.
035 inna almuslimeena waalmuslimāti waalmu/mineena waalmu/mināti waalqāniteena waalqānitāti waalssadiqeena waalssadiqāti waalssabireena waalssabirāti waalḫāšiʿeena waalḫāšiʿāti waalmutaṣaddiqeena waalmutaṣaddiqāti waalssa-imeena waalssa-imāti waalhafiḏeena furoojahum waalhafiḏāti waalṯṯakireena allāha kaṯeeran waalṯṯakirāti aʿadda allāhu lahum maġfiratan waajran ʿaḏeemān Lo! men who surrender unto Allah, and women who surrender, and men who believe and women who believe, and men who obey and women who obey, and men who speak the truth and women who speak the truth, and men who persevere (in righteousness) and women who persevere, and men who are humble and women who are humble, and men who give alms and women who give alms, and men who fast and women who fast, and men who guard their modesty and women who guard (their modesty), and men who remember Allah much and women who remember - Allah hath prepared for them forgiveness and a vast reward.
036 wamā kāna limu/minin walā mu/minatin iṯa qada allāhu warasooluhu amran an yakoona lahumu alḫiyaratu min amrihim waman yaʿṣi allāha warasoolahu faqad ḍalla ḍalālan mubeenān And it becometh not a believing man or a believing woman, when Allah and His messenger have decided an affair (for them), that they should (after that) claim any say in their affair; and whoso is rebellious to Allah and His messenger, he verily goeth astray in error manifest.
037 wa-iḏ taqoolu lillaḏee anʿama allāhu ʿalayhi waanʿamta ʿalayhi amsik ʿalayka zawjaka waittaqi allāha watuḫfee fee nafsika mā allāhu mubdeehi wataḫšā alnnāsa waallāhu aḥaqqu an taḫšāhu falammā qada zaydun minhā waṭaran zawwajnākahā likay lā yakoona ʿalā almu/mineena ḥarajun fee azwāji adʿiyā-ihim iṯa qaḍaw minhunna waṭaran wakāna amru allāhi mafʿoolān And when thou saidst unto him on whom Allah hath conferred favour and thou hast conferred favour: Keep thy wife to thyself, and fear Allah. And thou didst hide in thy mind that which Allah was to bring to light, and thou didst fear mankind whereas Allah hath a better right that thou shouldst fear Him. So when Zeyd had performed that necessary formality (of divorce) from her, We gave her unto thee in marriage, so that (henceforth) there may be no sin for believers in respect of wives of their adopted sons, when the latter have performed the necessary formality (of release) from them. The commandment of Allah must be fulfilled.
038 mā kāna ʿalā alnnabiyyi min ḥarajin feemā faraḍa allāhu lahu sunnata allāhi fee allāḏeena ḫalaw min qablu wakāna amru allāhi qadaran maqdoorān There is no reproach for the Prophet in that which Allah maketh his due. That was Allah's way with those who passed away of old - and the commandment of Allah is certain destiny -
039 allāḏeena yuballiġoona risālāti allāhi wayaḫšawnahu walā yaḫšawna aḥadan illā allāha wakafā biallāhi ḥaseebān Who delivered the messages of Allah and feared Him, and feared none save Allah. Allah keepeth good account.
040 mā kāna muḥammadun abā aḥadin min rijālikum walākin rasoola allāhi waḫātama alnnabiyyeena wakāna allāhu bikulli šay-in ʿaleemān Muhammad is not the father of any man among you, but he is the messenger of Allah and the Seal of the Prophets; and Allah is ever Aware of all things.
041 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo oḏkuroo allāha ḏikran kaṯeerān O ye who believe! Remember Allah with much remembrance.
042 wasabbiḥoohu bukratan waaṣeelān And glorify Him early and late.
043 huwa allāḏee yuṣallee ʿalaykum wamalā-ikatuhu liyuḫrijakum mina alṯṯulumāti ilā alnnoori wakāna bialmu/mineena raḥeemān He it is Who blesseth you, and His angels (bless you), that He may bring you forth from darkness unto light; and He is ever Merciful to the believers.
044 taḥiyyatuhum yawma yalqawnahu salāmun waaʿadda lahum ajran kareemān Their salutation on the day when they shall meet Him will be: Peace. And He hath prepared for them a goodly recompense.
045 yā ayyuhā alnnabiyyu innā arsalnāka šāhidan wamubašširan wanaḏeerān O Prophet! Lo! We have sent thee as a witness and a bringer of good tidings and a warner.
046 wadāʿiyan ilā allāhi bi-iḏnihi wasirājan muneerān And as a summoner unto Allah by His permission, and as a lamp that giveth light.
047 wabašširi almu/mineena bi-anna lahum mina allāhi faḍlan kabeerān And announce unto the believers the good tidings that they will have great bounty from Allah.
048 walā tuṭiʿi alkāfireena waalmunāfiqeena wadaʿ aṯahum watawakkal ʿalā allāhi wakafā biallāhi wakeelān And incline not to the disbelievers and the hypocrites. Disregard their noxious talk, and put thy trust in Allah. Allah is sufficient as Trustee.
049 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo iṯa nakaḥtumu almu/mināti ṯumma ṭallaqtumoohunna min qabli an tamassoohunna famā lakum ʿalayhinna min ʿiddatin taʿtaddoonahā famattiʿoohunna wasarriḥoohunna sarahan jameelān O ye who believe! If ye wed believing women and divorce them before ye have touched them, then there is no period that ye should reckon. But content them and release them handsomely.
050 yā ayyuhā alnnabiyyu innā aḥlalnā laka azwājaka allātee ātayta ojoorahunna wamā malakat yameenuka mimmā afāa allāhu ʿalayka wabanāti ʿammika wabanāti ʿammātika wabanāti ḫālika wabanāti ḫālātika allātee hājarna maʿaka waimraatan mu/minatan in wahabat nafsahā lilnnabiyyi in arāda alnnabiyyu an yastankiḥahā ḫāliṣatan laka min dooni almu/mineena qad ʿalimnā mā faraḍnā ʿalayhim fee azwājihim wamā malakat aymānuhum likaylā yakoona ʿalayka ḥarajun wakāna allāhu ġafooran raḥeemān O Prophet! Lo! We have made lawful unto thee thy wives unto whom thou hast paid their dowries, and those whom thy right hand possesseth of those whom Allah hath given thee as spoils of war, and the daughters of thine uncle on the father's side and the daughters of thine aunts on the father's side, and the daughters of thine uncle on the mother's side and the daughters of thine aunts on the mother's side who emigrated with thee, and a believing woman if she give herself unto the Prophet and the Prophet desire to ask her in marriage - a privilege for thee only, not for the (rest of) believers - We are Aware of that which We enjoined upon them concerning their wives and those whom their right hands possess - that thou mayst be free from blame, for Allah is ever Forgiving, Merciful.
051 turjee man tašāo minhunna watu/wee ilayka man tašāo wamani ibtaġayta mimman ʿazalta falā junaha ʿalayka ṯalika adnā an taqarra aʿyunuhunna walā yaḥzanna wayarḍayna bimā ātaytahunna kulluhunna waallāhu yaʿlamu mā fee quloobikum wakāna allāhu ʿaleeman ḥaleemān Thou canst defer whom thou wilt of them and receive unto thee whom thou wilt, and whomsoever thou desirest of those whom thou hast set aside (temporarily), it is no sin for thee (to receive her again); that is better; that they may be comforted and not grieve, and may all be pleased with what thou givest them. Allah knoweth what is in your hearts (O men), and Allah is ever Forgiving, Clement.
052 lā yaḥillu laka alnnisāo min baʿdu walā an tabaddala bihinna min azwājin walaw aʿjabaka ḥusnuhunna illā mā malakat yameenuka wakāna allāhu ʿalā kulli šay-in raqeebān It is not allowed thee to take (other) women henceforth, nor that thou shouldst change them for other wives even though their beauty pleased thee, save those whom thy right hand possesseth. And Allah is ever Watcher over all things.
053 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo lā tadḫuloo buyoota alnnabiyyi illā an yu/ḏana lakum ilā ṭaʿāmin ġayra nāḏireena ināhu walākin iṯa duʿeetum faodḫuloo fa-iṯa ṭaʿimtum faintaširoo walā musta/niseena liḥadeeṯin inna ṯalikum kāna yu/ḏee alnnabiyya fayastaḥyee minkum waallāhu lā yastaḥyee mina alḥaqqi wa-iṯa saaltumoohunna matāʿan fais-aloohunna min warā-i ḥijābin ṯalikum aṭharu liquloobikum waquloobihinna wamā kāna lakum an tu/ḏoo rasoola allāhi walā an tankiḥoo azwājahu min baʿdihi abadan inna ṯalikum kāna ʿinda allāhi ʿaḏeemān O Ye who believe! Enter not the dwellings of the Prophet for a meal without waiting for the proper time, unless permission be granted you. But if ye are invited, enter, and, when your meal is ended, then disperse. Linger not for conversation. Lo! that would cause annoyance to the Prophet, and he would be shy of (asking) you (to go); but Allah is not shy of the truth. And when ye ask of them (the wives of the Prophet) anything, ask it of them from behind a curtain. That is purer for your hearts and for their hearts. And it is not for you to cause annoyance to the messenger of Allah, nor that ye should ever marry his wives after him. Lo! that in Allah's sight would be an enormity.
054 in tubdoo šay-an aw tuḫfoohu fa-inna allāha kāna bikulli šay-in ʿaleemān Whether ye divulge a thing or keep it hidden, lo! Allah is ever Knower of all things.
055 lā junaha ʿalayhinna fee ābā-ihinna walā abnā-ihinna walā iḫwānihinna walā abnā-i iḫwānihinna walā abnā-i aḫawātihinna walā nisā-ihinna walā mā malakat aymānuhunna waittaqeena allāha inna allāha kāna ʿalā kulli šay-in šaheedān It is no sin for them (thy wives) to converse freely) with their fathers, or their sons, or their brothers, or their brothers' sons, or the sons of their sisters or of their own women, or their slaves. O women! Keep your duty to Allah. Lo! Allah is ever Witness over all things.
056 inna allāha wamalā-ikatahu yuṣalloona ʿalā alnnabiyyi yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo ṣalloo ʿalayhi wasallimoo tasleemān Lo! Allah and His angels shower blessings on the Prophet. O ye who believe! Ask blessings on him and salute him with a worthy salutation.
057 inna allāḏeena yu/ḏoona allāha warasoolahu laʿanahumu allāhu fee alddunyā waal-āḫirati waaʿadda lahum ʿaṯaban muheenān Lo! those who malign Allah and His messenger, Allah hath cursed them in the world and the Hereafter, and hath prepared for them the doom of the disdained.
058 waallaḏeena yu/ḏoona almu/mineena waalmu/mināti biġayri mā iktasaboo faqadi iḥtamaloo buhtānan wa-iṯman mubeenān And those who malign believing men and believing women undeservedly, they bear the guilt of slander and manifest sin.
059 yā ayyuhā alnnabiyyu qul li-azwājika wabanātika wanisā-i almu/mineena yudneena ʿalayhinna min jalābeebihinna ṯalika adnā an yuʿrafna falā yu/ḏayna wakāna allāhu ġafooran raḥeemān O Prophet! Tell thy wives and thy daughters and the women of the believers to draw their cloaks close round them (when they go abroad). That will be better, so that they may be recognised and not annoyed. Allah is ever Forgiving, Merciful.
060 la-in lam yantahi almunāfiqoona waallaḏeena fee quloobihim maraḍun waalmurjifoona fee almadeenati lanuġriyannaka bihim ṯumma lā yujāwiroonaka feehā illā qaleelān If the hypocrites, and those in whose hearts is a disease, and the alarmists in the city do not cease, We verily shall urge thee on against them, then they will be your neighbours in it but a little while.
061 malʿooneena ayna mā ṯuqifoo oḫiḏoo waquttiloo taqteelān Accursed, they will be seized wherever found and slain with a (fierce) slaughter.
062 sunnata allāhi fee allāḏeena ḫalaw min qablu walan tajida lisunnati allāhi tabdeelān That was the way of Allah in the case of those who passed away of old; thou wilt not find for the way of Allah aught of power to change.
063 yas-aluka alnnāsu ʿani alssāʿati qul innamā ʿilmuhā ʿinda allāhi wamā yudreeka laʿalla alssāʿata takoonu qareebān Men ask thee of the Hour. Say: The knowledge of it is with Allah only. What can convey (the knowledge) unto thee ? It may be that the Hour is nigh.
064 inna allāha laʿana alkāfireena waaʿadda lahum saʿeerān Lo! Allah hath cursed the disbelievers, and hath prepared for them a flaming fire,
065 khālideena feehā abadan lā yajidoona waliyyan walā naṣeerān Wherein they will abide for ever. They will find (then) no protecting friend nor helper.
066 yawma tuqallabu wujoohuhum fee alnnāri yaqooloona yā laytanā aṭaʿnā allāha waaṭaʿnā alrrasoolā On the day when their faces are turned over in the Fire, they say: Oh, would that we had obeyed Allah and had obeyed His messenger!
067 waqāloo rabbanā innā aṭaʿnā sādatanā wakubarāanā faaḍalloonā alssabeelā And they say: Our Lord! Lo! we obeyed our princes and great men, and they misled us from the Way.
068 rabbanā ātihim ḍiʿfayni mina alʿaṯabi wailʿanhum laʿnan kabeerān Our Lord! Oh, give them double torment and curse them with a mighty curse.
069 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo lā takoonoo kaallaḏeena aṯaw moosā fabarraahu allāhu mimmā qāloo wakāna ʿinda allāhi wajeehān O ye who believe! Be not as those who slandered Moses, but Allah proved his innocence of that which they alleged, and he was well esteemed in Allah's sight.
070 yā ayyuhā allāḏeena āmanoo ittaqoo allāha waqooloo qawlan sadeedān O ye who believe! Guard your duty to Allah, and speak words straight to the point;
071 yuṣliḥ lakum aʿmālakum wayaġfir lakum ḏunoobakum waman yuṭiʿi allāha warasoolahu faqad fāza fawzan ʿaḏeemān He will adjust your works for you and will forgive you your sins. Whosoever obeyeth Allah and His messenger, he verily hath gained a signal victory.
072 innā ʿaraḍnā al-amānata ʿalā alssamāwāti waal-arḍi waaljibāli faabayna an yaḥmilnahā waašfaqna minhā waḥamalahā al-insānu innahu kāna ḏalooman jahoolān Lo! We offered the trust unto the heavens and the earth and the hills, but they shrank from bearing it and were afraid of it. And man assumed it. Lo! he hath proved a tyrant and a fool.
