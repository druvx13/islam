37. as-Saffat: The Rangers

001 waalssaffāti ṣaffān By those who set the ranks in battle order
002 faalzzājirāti zajrān And those who drive away (the wicked) with reproof
003 faalttāliyāti ḏikrān And those who read (the Word) for a reminder,
004 inna ilāhakum lawahidun Lo! thy Lord is surely One;
005 rabbu alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wamā baynahumā warabbu almašāriqi Lord of the heavens and of the earth and all that is between them, and Lord of the sun's risings.
006 innā zayyannā alssamāa alddunyā bizeenatin alkawākibi Lo! We have adorned the lowest heaven with an ornament, the planets;
007 waḥifḏan min kulli šaytanin māridin With security from every froward devil.
008 lā yassammaʿoona ilā almala-i al-aʿlā wayuqḏafoona min kulli jānibin They cannot listen to the Highest Chiefs for they are pelted from every side,
009 duḥooran walahum ʿaṯabun wasibun Outcast, and theirs is a perpetual torment;
010 illā man ḫaṭifa alḫaṭfata faatbaʿahu šihābun ṯāqibun Save him who snatcheth a fragment, and there pursueth him a piercing flame.
011 faistaftihim ahum ašaddu ḫalqan am man ḫalaqnā inna ḫalaqnāhum min ṭeenin lāzibin Then ask them (O Muhammad): Are they stronger as a creation, or those (others) whom we have created ? Lo! We created them of plastic clay.
012 bal ʿajibta wayasḫaroona Nay, but thou dost marvel when they mock
013 wa-iṯa ḏukkiroo lā yaḏkuroona And heed not when they are reminded,
014 wa-iṯa raaw āyatan yastasḫiroona And seek to scoff when they behold a portent.
015 waqāloo in haṯa illā siḥrun mubeenun And they say: Lo! this is mere magic;
016 a-iṯa mitnā wakunnā turāban waʿiḏāman a-innā lamabʿooṯoona When we are dead and have become dust and bones, shall we then, forsooth, be raised (again) ?
017 awa ābāonā al-awwaloona And our forefathers ?
018 qul naʿam waantum dāḫiroona Say (O Muhammad): Ye, in truth; and ye will be brought low.
019 fa-innamā hiya zajratun wahidatun fa-iṯa hum yanḏuroona There is but one Shout, and lo! they behold,
020 waqāloo yā waylanā haṯa yawmu alddeeni And say: Ah, woe for us! This is the Day of Judgment.
021 haṯa yawmu alfaṣli allāḏee kuntum bihi tukaṯṯiboona This is the Day of Separation, which ye used to deny.
022 oḥšuroo allāḏeena ḏalamoo waazwājahum wamā kānoo yaʿbudoona (And it is said unto the angels): Assemble those who did wrong, together with their wives and what they used to worship
023 min dooni allāhi faihdoohum ilā ṣirati aljaḥeemi Instead of Allah, and lead them to the path to hell;
024 waqifoohum innahum masooloona And stop them, for they must be questioned.
025 mā lakum lā tanasaroona What aileth you that ye help not one another ?
026 bal humu alyawma mustaslimoona Nay, but this day they make full submission.
027 waaqbala baʿḍuhum ʿalā baʿḍin yatasāaloona And some of them draw near unto others, mutually questioning.
028 qāloo innakum kuntum ta/toonanā ʿani alyameeni They say: Lo! ye used to come unto us, imposing, (swearing that ye spoke the truth).
029 qāloo bal lam takoonoo mu/mineena They answer: Nay, but ye (yourselves) were not believers.
030 wamā kāna lanā ʿalaykum min ṣultanin bal kuntum qawman taġeena We had no power over you, but ye were wayward folk.
031 faḥaqqa ʿalaynā qawlu rabbinā innā laṯa-iqoona Now the Word of our Lord hath been fulfilled concerning us. Lo! we are about to taste (the doom).
032 faaġwaynākum innā kunnā ġāweena Thus we misled you. Lo! we were (ourselves) astray.
033 fa-innahum yawma-iḏin fee alʿaṯabi muštarikoona Then lo! this day they (both) are sharers in the doom.
034 innā kaṯalika nafʿalu bialmujrimeena Lo! thus deal We with the guilty.
035 innahum kānoo iṯa qeela lahum lā ilāha illā allāhu yastakbiroona For when it was said unto them, There is no God save Allah, they were scornful
036 wayaqooloona a-innā latārikoo ālihatinā lišāʿirin majnoonin And said: Shall we forsake our gods for a mad poet ?
037 bal jāa bialḥaqqi waṣaddaqa almursaleena Nay, but he brought the Truth, and he confirmed those sent (before him).
038 innakum laṯa-iqoo alʿaṯabi al-aleemi Lo! (now) verily ye taste the painful doom -
039 wamā tujzawna illā mā kuntum taʿmaloona Ye are requited naught save what ye did -
040 illā ʿibāda allāhi almuḫlaṣeena Save single-minded slaves of Allah;
041 olā-ika lahum rizqun maʿloomun For them there is a known provision,
042 fawākihu wahum mukramoona Fruits. And they will be honoured
043 fee jannāti alnnaʿeemi In the Gardens of delight,
044 ʿalā sururin mutaqābileena On couches facing one another;
045 yutafu ʿalayhim bika/sin min maʿeenin A cup from a gushing spring is brought round for them,
046 baydaa laṯṯatin lilššāribeena White, delicious to the drinkers,
047 lā feehā ġawlun walā hum ʿanhā yunzafoona Wherein there is no headache nor are they made mad thereby.
048 waʿindahum qasirātu alttarfi ʿeenun And with them are those of modest gaze, with lovely eyes,
049 kaannahunna bayḍun maknoonun (Pure) as they were hidden eggs (of the ostrich).
050 faaqbala baʿḍuhum ʿalā baʿḍin yatasāaloona And some of them draw near unto others, mutually questioning.
051 qāla qā-ilun minhum innee kāna lee qareenun A speaker of them saith: Lo! I had a comrade
052 yaqoolu a-innaka lamina almuṣaddiqeena Who used to say: Art thou in truth of those who put faith (in his words) ?
053 a-iṯa mitnā wakunnā turāban waʿiḏāman a-innā lamadeenoona Can we, when we are dead and have become mere dust and bones - can we (then) verily be brought to book ?

055 faittalaʿa faraāhu fee sawā-i aljaḥeemi Then looketh he and seeth him in the depth of hell.

057 walawlā niʿmatu rabbee lakuntu mina almuhdareena And had it not been for the favour of my Lord, I too had been of those haled forth (to doom).
058 afamā naḥnu bimayyiteena Are we then not to die

060 inna haṯa lahuwa alfawzu alʿaḏeemu Lo! this is the supreme triumph.
061 limiṯli haṯa falyaʿmali alʿāmiloona For the like of this, then, let the workers work.

063 innā jaʿalnāhā fitnatan lilṯṯālimeena Lo! We have appointed it a torment for wrong-doers.
064 innahā šajaratun taḫruju fee aṣli aljaḥeemi Lo! it is a tree that springeth in the heart of hell.
