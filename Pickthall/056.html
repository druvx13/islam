56. al-Waqi`ah: That Which is Coming

001 iṯa waqaʿati alwāqiʿatu When the event befalleth -
002 laysa liwaqʿatihā kaṯibatun There is no denying that it will befall -
003 khāfiḍatun rāfiʿatun Abasing (some), exalting (others);
004 iṯa rujjati al-arḍu rajjān When the earth is shaken with a shock
005 wabussati aljibālu bassān And the hills are ground to powder
006 fakānat habāan munbaṯṯān So that they become a scattered dust,
007 wakuntum azwājan ṯalāṯatan And ye will be three kinds:
008 faaṣ-habu almaymanati mā aṣ-habu almaymanati (First) those on the right hand; what of those on the right hand ?
009 waaṣ-habu almaš-amati mā aṣ-habu almaš-amati And (then) those on the left hand; what of those on the left hand ?
010 waalssābiqoona alssābiqoona And the foremost in the race, the foremost in the race:
011 olā-ika almuqarraboona Those are they who will be brought nigh
012 fee jannāti alnnaʿeemi In gardens of delight;
013 thullatun mina al-awwaleena A multitude of those of old
014 waqaleelun mina al-āḫireena And a few of those of later time.
015 ʿalā sururin mawḍoonatin On lined couches,
016 muttaki-eena ʿalayhā mutaqābileena Reclining therein face to face.
017 yaṭoofu ʿalayhim wildānun muḫalladoona There wait on them immortal youths
018 bi-akwābin waabāreeqa waka/sin min maʿeenin With bowls and ewers and a cup from a pure spring
019 lā yuṣaddaʿoona ʿanhā walā yunzifoona Wherefrom they get no aching of the head nor any madness,
020 wafākihatin mimmā yataḫayyaroona And fruit that they prefer
021 walaḥmi ṭayrin mimmā yaštahoona And flesh of fowls that they desire.
022 waḥoorun ʿeenun And (there are) fair ones with wide, lovely eyes,
023 kaamṯāli allu/lui almaknooni Like unto hidden pearls,
024 jazāan bimā kānoo yaʿmaloona Reward for what they used to do.
025 lā yasmaʿoona feehā laġwan walā ta/ṯeemān There hear they no vain speaking nor recrimination
026 illā qeelan salāman salāmān (Naught) but the saying: Peace, (and again) Peace.
027 waaṣ-habu alyameeni mā aṣ-habu alyameeni And those on the right hand; what of those on the right hand ?
028 fee sidrin maḫḍoodin Among thornless lote-trees
029 waṭalḥin manḍoodin And clustered plantains,
030 waḏillin mamdoodin And spreading shade,
031 wamā-in maskoobin And water gushing,
032 wafākihatin kaṯeeratin And fruit in plenty
033 lā maqṭooʿatin walā mamnooʿatin Neither out of reach nor yet forbidden,
034 wafurušin marfooʿatin And raised couches;
035 innā anša/nāhunna inšaan Lo! We have created them a (new) creation
036 fajaʿalnāhunna abkārān And made them virgins,
037 ʿuruban atrābān Lovers, friends,
038 li-aṣ-habi alyameeni For those on the right hand;
039 thullatun mina al-awwaleena A multitude of those of old
040 waṯullatun mina al-āḫireena And a multitude of those of later time.
041 waaṣ-habu alššimāli mā aṣ-habu alššimāli And those on the left hand: What of those on the left hand ?
042 fee samoomin waḥameemin In scorching wind and scalding water
043 waḏillin min yaḥmoomin And shadow of black smoke,
044 lā bāridin walā kareemin Neither cool nor refreshing.
045 innahum kānoo qabla ṯalika mutrafeena Lo! heretofore they were effete with luxury
046 wakānoo yuṣirroona ʿalā alḥinṯi alʿaḏeemi And used to persist in the awful sin.
047 wakānoo yaqooloona a-iṯa mitnā wakunnā turāban waʿiḏāman a-innā lamabʿooṯoona And they used to say: When we are dead and have become dust and bones, shall we then, forsooth, be raised again,
048 awa ābāonā al-awwaloona And also our forefathers ?
049 qul inna al-awwaleena waal-āḫireena Say (unto them, O Muhammad): Lo! those of old and those of later time
050 lamajmooʿoona ilā meeqāti yawmin maʿloomin Will all be brought together to the tryst of an appointed day.
051 thumma innakum ayyuhā alddalloona almukaṯṯiboona Then lo! ye, the erring, the deniers,
052 laākiloona min šajarin min zaqqoomin Ye verily will eat of a tree called Zaqqum
053 famāli-oona minhā albuṭoona And will fill your bellies therewith;
054 fašāriboona ʿalayhi mina alḥameemi And thereon ye will drink of boiling water,
055 fašāriboona šurba alheemi Drinking even as the camel drinketh.
056 haṯa nuzuluhum yawma alddeeni This will be their welcome on the Day of Judgment.
057 naḥnu ḫalaqnākum falawlā tuṣaddiqoona We created you. Will ye then admit the truth ?
058 afaraaytum mā tumnoona Have ye seen that which ye emit ?
059 aantum taḫluqoonahu am naḥnu alḫāliqoona Do ye create it or are We the Creator ?
060 naḥnu qaddarnā baynakumu almawta wamā naḥnu bimasbooqeena We mete out death among you, and We are not to be outrun,
061 ʿalā an nubaddila amṯālakum wanunši-akum fee mā lā taʿlamoona That We may transfigure you and make you what ye know not.
062 walaqad ʿalimtumu alnnaš-ata al-oolā falawlā taḏakkaroona And verily ye know the first creation. Why, then, do ye not reflect ?
063 afaraaytum mā taḥruṯoona Have ye seen that which ye cultivate ?
064 aantum tazraʿoonahu am naḥnu alzzāriʿoona Is it ye who foster it, or are We the Fosterer ?
065 law našāo lajaʿalnāhu ḥutaman faḏaltum tafakkahoona If We willed, We verily could make it chaff, then would ye cease not to exclaim:
066 innā lamuġramoona Lo! we are laden with debt!
067 bal naḥnu maḥroomoona Nay, but we are deprived!
068 afaraaytumu almāa allāḏee tašraboona Have ye observed the water which ye drink ?
069 aantum anzaltumoohu mina almuzni am naḥnu almunziloona Is it ye who shed it from the raincloud, or are We the Shedder ?
070 law našāo jaʿalnāhu ojājan falawlā taškuroona If We willed We verily could make it bitter. Why then, give ye not thanks ?
071 afaraaytumu alnnāra allātee tooroona Have ye observed the fire which ye strike out;
072 aantum anša/tum šajaratahā am naḥnu almunši-oona Was it ye who made the tree thereof to grow, or were We the grower ?
073 naḥnu jaʿalnāhā taḏkiratan wamatāʿan lilmuqweena We, even We, appointed it a memorial and a comfort for the dwellers in the wilderness.
074 fasabbiḥ biismi rabbika alʿaḏeemi Therefor (O Muhammad), praise the name of thy Lord, the Tremendous.
075 falā oqsimu bimawāqiʿi alnnujoomi Nay, I swear by the places of the stars -
076 wa-innahu laqasamun law taʿlamoona ʿaḏeemun And lo! that verily is a tremendous oath, if ye but knew -
077 innahu laqur-ānun kareemun That (this) is indeed a noble Qur'an
078 fee kitābin maknoonin In a Book kept hidden
079 lā yamassuhu illā almuṭahharoona Which none toucheth save the purified,
080 tanzeelun min rabbi alʿālameena A revelation from the Lord of the Worlds.
081 afabihaṯa alḥadeeṯi antum mudhinoona Is it this Statement that ye scorn,
082 watajʿaloona rizqakum annakum tukaṯṯiboona And make denial thereof your livelihood ?
083 falawlā iṯa balaġati alḥulqooma Why, then, when (the soul) cometh up to the throat (of the dying)
084 waantum ḥeena-iḏin tanḏuroona And ye are at that moment looking
085 wanaḥnu aqrabu ilayhi minkum walākin lā tubṣiroona - And We are nearer unto him than ye are, but ye see not -
086 falawlā in kuntum ġayra madeeneena Why then, if ye are not in bondage (unto Us),
087 tarjiʿoonahā in kuntum sadiqeena Do ye not force it back, if ye are truthful ?
088 faammā in kāna mina almuqarrabeena Thus if he is of those brought nigh,
089 farawḥun warayhanun wajannatu naʿeemin Then breath of life, and plenty, and a Garden of delight.
090 waammā in kāna min aṣ-habi alyameeni And if he is of those on the right hand,
091 fasalāmun laka min aṣ-habi alyameeni Then (the greeting) "Peace be unto thee" from those on the right hand.
092 waammā in kāna mina almukaṯṯibeena alddalleena But if he is of the rejecters, the erring,
093 fanuzulun min ḥameemin Then the welcome will be boiling water
094 wataṣliyatu jaḥeemin And roasting at hell-fire.
095 inna haṯa lahuwa ḥaqqu alyaqeeni Lo! this is certain truth.
96 fasabbiḥ biismi rabbika alʿaḏeemi [Could not extract English for verse 96]
