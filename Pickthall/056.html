56. al-Waqi`ah: That Which is Coming

1 
iṯa waqaʿati alwāqiʿatu
When the event befalleth -

2 
laysa liwaqʿatihā kaṯibatun
There is no denying that it will befall -

3 
khāfiḍatun rāfiʿatun
Abasing (some), exalting (others);

4 
iṯa rujjati al-arḍu rajjān
When the earth is shaken with a shock

5 
wabussati aljibālu bassān
And the hills are ground to powder

6 
fakānat habāan munbaṯṯān
So that they become a scattered dust,

7 
wakuntum azwājan ṯalāṯatan
And ye will be three kinds:

8 
faaṣ-habu almaymanati mā aṣ-habu almaymanati
(First) those on the right hand; what of those on the right hand ?

9 
waaṣ-habu almaš-amati mā aṣ-habu almaš-amati
And (then) those on the left hand; what of those on the left hand ?

10 
waalssābiqoona alssābiqoona
And the foremost in the race, the foremost in the race:

11 
olā-ika almuqarraboona
Those are they who will be brought nigh

12 
fee jannāti alnnaʿeemi
In gardens of delight;

13 
thullatun mina al-awwaleena
A multitude of those of old

14 
waqaleelun mina al-āḫireena
And a few of those of later time.

15 
ʿalā sururin mawḍoonatin
On lined couches,

16 
muttaki-eena ʿalayhā mutaqābileena
Reclining therein face to face.

17 
yaṭoofu ʿalayhim wildānun muḫalladoona
There wait on them immortal youths

18 
bi-akwābin waabāreeqa waka/sin min maʿeenin
With bowls and ewers and a cup from a pure spring

19 
lā yuṣaddaʿoona ʿanhā walā yunzifoona
Wherefrom they get no aching of the head nor any madness,

20 
wafākihatin mimmā yataḫayyaroona
And fruit that they prefer

21 
walaḥmi ṭayrin mimmā yaštahoona
And flesh of fowls that they desire.

22 
waḥoorun ʿeenun
And (there are) fair ones with wide, lovely eyes,

23 
kaamṯāli allu/lui almaknooni
Like unto hidden pearls,

24 
jazāan bimā kānoo yaʿmaloona
Reward for what they used to do.

25 
lā yasmaʿoona feehā laġwan walā ta/ṯeemān
There hear they no vain speaking nor recrimination

26 
illā qeelan salāman salāmān
(Naught) but the saying: Peace, (and again) Peace.

27 
waaṣ-habu alyameeni mā aṣ-habu alyameeni
And those on the right hand; what of those on the right hand ?

28 
fee sidrin maḫḍoodin
Among thornless lote-trees

29 
waṭalḥin manḍoodin
And clustered plantains,

30 
waḏillin mamdoodin
And spreading shade,

31 
wamā-in maskoobin
And water gushing,

32 
wafākihatin kaṯeeratin
And fruit in plenty

33 
lā maqṭooʿatin walā mamnooʿatin
Neither out of reach nor yet forbidden,

34 
wafurušin marfooʿatin
And raised couches;

35 
innā anša/nāhunna inšaan
Lo! We have created them a (new) creation

36 
fajaʿalnāhunna abkārān
And made them virgins,

37 
ʿuruban atrābān
Lovers, friends,

38 
li-aṣ-habi alyameeni
For those on the right hand;

39 
thullatun mina al-awwaleena
A multitude of those of old

40 
waṯullatun mina al-āḫireena
And a multitude of those of later time.

41 
waaṣ-habu alššimāli mā aṣ-habu alššimāli
And those on the left hand: What of those on the left hand ?

42 
fee samoomin waḥameemin
In scorching wind and scalding water

43 
waḏillin min yaḥmoomin
And shadow of black smoke,

44 
lā bāridin walā kareemin
Neither cool nor refreshing.

45 
innahum kānoo qabla ṯalika mutrafeena
Lo! heretofore they were effete with luxury

46 
wakānoo yuṣirroona ʿalā alḥinṯi alʿaḏeemi
And used to persist in the awful sin.

47 
wakānoo yaqooloona a-iṯa mitnā wakunnā turāban waʿiḏāman a-innā lamabʿooṯoona
And they used to say: When we are dead and have become dust and bones, shall we then, forsooth, be raised again,

48 
awa ābāonā al-awwaloona
And also our forefathers ?

49 
qul inna al-awwaleena waal-āḫireena
Say (unto them, O Muhammad): Lo! those of old and those of later time

50 
lamajmooʿoona ilā meeqāti yawmin maʿloomin
Will all be brought together to the tryst of an appointed day.

51 
thumma innakum ayyuhā alddalloona almukaṯṯiboona
Then lo! ye, the erring, the deniers,

52 
laākiloona min šajarin min zaqqoomin
Ye verily will eat of a tree called Zaqqum

53 
famāli-oona minhā albuṭoona
And will fill your bellies therewith;

54 
fašāriboona ʿalayhi mina alḥameemi
And thereon ye will drink of boiling water,

55 
fašāriboona šurba alheemi
Drinking even as the camel drinketh.

56 
haṯa nuzuluhum yawma alddeeni
This will be their welcome on the Day of Judgment.

57 
naḥnu ḫalaqnākum falawlā tuṣaddiqoona
We created you. Will ye then admit the truth ?

58 
afaraaytum mā tumnoona
Have ye seen that which ye emit ?

59 
aantum taḫluqoonahu am naḥnu alḫāliqoona
Do ye create it or are We the Creator ?

60 
naḥnu qaddarnā baynakumu almawta wamā naḥnu bimasbooqeena
We mete out death among you, and We are not to be outrun,

61 
ʿalā an nubaddila amṯālakum wanunši-akum fee mā lā taʿlamoona
That We may transfigure you and make you what ye know not.

62 
walaqad ʿalimtumu alnnaš-ata al-oolā falawlā taḏakkaroona
And verily ye know the first creation. Why, then, do ye not reflect ?

63 
afaraaytum mā taḥruṯoona
Have ye seen that which ye cultivate ?

64 
aantum tazraʿoonahu am naḥnu alzzāriʿoona
Is it ye who foster it, or are We the Fosterer ?

65 
law našāo lajaʿalnāhu ḥutaman faḏaltum tafakkahoona
If We willed, We verily could make it chaff, then would ye cease not to exclaim:

66 
innā lamuġramoona
Lo! we are laden with debt!

67 
bal naḥnu maḥroomoona
Nay, but we are deprived!

68 
afaraaytumu almāa allāḏee tašraboona
Have ye observed the water which ye drink ?

69 
aantum anzaltumoohu mina almuzni am naḥnu almunziloona
Is it ye who shed it from the raincloud, or are We the Shedder ?

70 
law našāo jaʿalnāhu ojājan falawlā taškuroona
If We willed We verily could make it bitter. Why then, give ye not thanks ?

71 
afaraaytumu alnnāra allātee tooroona
Have ye observed the fire which ye strike out;

72 
aantum anša/tum šajaratahā am naḥnu almunši-oona
Was it ye who made the tree thereof to grow, or were We the grower ?

73 
naḥnu jaʿalnāhā taḏkiratan wamatāʿan lilmuqweena
We, even We, appointed it a memorial and a comfort for the dwellers in the wilderness.

74 
fasabbiḥ biismi rabbika alʿaḏeemi
Therefor (O Muhammad), praise the name of thy Lord, the Tremendous.

75 
falā oqsimu bimawāqiʿi alnnujoomi
Nay, I swear by the places of the stars -

76 
wa-innahu laqasamun law taʿlamoona ʿaḏeemun
And lo! that verily is a tremendous oath, if ye but knew -

77 
innahu laqur-ānun kareemun
That (this) is indeed a noble Qur'an

78 
fee kitābin maknoonin
In a Book kept hidden

79 
lā yamassuhu illā almuṭahharoona
Which none toucheth save the purified,

80 
tanzeelun min rabbi alʿālameena
A revelation from the Lord of the Worlds.

81 
afabihaṯa alḥadeeṯi antum mudhinoona
Is it this Statement that ye scorn,

82 
watajʿaloona rizqakum annakum tukaṯṯiboona
And make denial thereof your livelihood ?

83 
falawlā iṯa balaġati alḥulqooma
Why, then, when (the soul) cometh up to the throat (of the dying)

84 
waantum ḥeena-iḏin tanḏuroona
And ye are at that moment looking

85 
wanaḥnu aqrabu ilayhi minkum walākin lā tubṣiroona
- And We are nearer unto him than ye are, but ye see not -

86 
falawlā in kuntum ġayra madeeneena
Why then, if ye are not in bondage (unto Us),

87 
tarjiʿoonahā in kuntum sadiqeena
Do ye not force it back, if ye are truthful ?

88 
faammā in kāna mina almuqarrabeena
Thus if he is of those brought nigh,

89 
farawḥun warayhanun wajannatu naʿeemin
Then breath of life, and plenty, and a Garden of delight.

90 
waammā in kāna min aṣ-habi alyameeni
And if he is of those on the right hand,

91 
fasalāmun laka min aṣ-habi alyameeni
Then (the greeting) "Peace be unto thee" from those on the right hand.

92 
waammā in kāna mina almukaṯṯibeena alddalleena
But if he is of the rejecters, the erring,

93 
fanuzulun min ḥameemin
Then the welcome will be boiling water

94 
wataṣliyatu jaḥeemin
And roasting at hell-fire.

95 
inna haṯa lahuwa ḥaqqu alyaqeeni
Lo! this is certain truth.

96 
fasabbiḥ biismi rabbika alʿaḏeemi
Therefor (O Muhammad) praise the name of thy Lord, the Tremendous.
