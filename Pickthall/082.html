82. al-Infitar: The Cleaving Asunder

1 
iṯa alssamāo infaṭarat
When the heaven is cleft asunder,

2 
wa-iṯa alkawākibu intaṯarat
When the planets are dispersed,

3 
wa-iṯa albiharu fujjirat
When the seas are poured forth,

4 
wa-iṯa alqubooru buʿṯirat
And the sepulchres are overturned,

5 
ʿalimat nafsun mā qaddamat waaḫḫarat
A soul will know what it hath sent before (it) and what left behind.

6 
yā ayyuhā al-insānu mā ġarraka birabbika alkareemi
O man! What hath made thee careless concerning thy Lord, the Bountiful,

7 
allāḏee ḫalaqaka fasawwāka faʿadalaka
Who created thee, then fashioned, then proportioned thee ?

8 
fee ayyi ṣooratin mā šāa rakkabaka
Into whatsoever form He will, He casteth thee.

9 
kallā bal tukaṯṯiboona bialddeeni
Nay, but ye deny the Judgment.

10 
wa-inna ʿalaykum lahafiḏeena
Lo! there are above you guardians,

11 
kirāman kātibeena
Generous and recording,

12 
yaʿlamoona mā tafʿaloona
Who know (all) that ye do.

13 
inna al-abrāra lafee naʿeemin
Lo! the righteous verily will be in delight.

14 
wa-inna alfujjāra lafee jaḥeemin
And lo! the wicked verily will be in hell;

15 
yaṣlawnahā yawma alddeeni
They will burn therein on the Day of Judgment,

16 
wamā hum ʿanhā biġā-ibeena
And will not be absent thence.

17 
wamā adrāka mā yawmu alddeeni
Ah, what will convey unto thee what the Day of Judgment is!

18 
thumma mā adrāka mā yawmu alddeeni
Again, what will convey unto thee what the Day of Judgment is!

19 
yawma lā tamliku nafsun linafsin šay-an waal-amru yawma-iḏin lillāhi
A day on which no soul hath power at all for any (other) soul. The (absolute) command on that day is Allah's.
