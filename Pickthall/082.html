82. al-Infitar: The Cleaving Asunder

001 iṯa alssamāo infaṭarat When the heaven is cleft asunder,
002 wa-iṯa alkawākibu intaṯarat When the planets are dispersed,
003 wa-iṯa albiharu fujjirat When the seas are poured forth,
004 wa-iṯa alqubooru buʿṯirat And the sepulchres are overturned,
005 ʿalimat nafsun mā qaddamat waaḫḫarat A soul will know what it hath sent before (it) and what left behind.
006 yā ayyuhā al-insānu mā ġarraka birabbika alkareemi O man! What hath made thee careless concerning thy Lord, the Bountiful,
007 allāḏee ḫalaqaka fasawwāka faʿadalaka Who created thee, then fashioned, then proportioned thee ?
008 fee ayyi ṣooratin mā šāa rakkabaka Into whatsoever form He will, He casteth thee.
009 kallā bal tukaṯṯiboona bialddeeni Nay, but ye deny the Judgment.
010 wa-inna ʿalaykum lahafiḏeena Lo! there are above you guardians,
011 kirāman kātibeena Generous and recording,
012 yaʿlamoona mā tafʿaloona Who know (all) that ye do.
013 inna al-abrāra lafee naʿeemin Lo! the righteous verily will be in delight.
014 wa-inna alfujjāra lafee jaḥeemin And lo! the wicked verily will be in hell;
015 yaṣlawnahā yawma alddeeni They will burn therein on the Day of Judgment,
016 wamā hum ʿanhā biġā-ibeena And will not be absent thence.
017 wamā adrāka mā yawmu alddeeni Ah, what will convey unto thee what the Day of Judgment is!
018 thumma mā adrāka mā yawmu alddeeni Again, what will convey unto thee what the Day of Judgment is!
19 
yawma lā tamliku nafsun linafsin šay-an waal-amru yawma-iḏin lillāhi
A day on which no soul hath power at all for any (other) soul. The (absolute) command on that day is Allah's.
