36. Ya Sin: Ya Sin

001 yā-seen Ya Sin.
002 waalqur-āni alḥakeemi By the wise Qur'an,
003 innaka lamina almursaleena Lo! thou art of those sent
004 ʿalā ṣiratin mustaqeemin On a straight path,
005 tanzeela alʿazeezi alrraḥeemi A revelation of the Mighty, the Merciful,
006 litunḏira qawman mā onḏira ābāohum fahum ġāfiloona That thou mayst warn a folk whose fathers were not warned, so they are heedless.
007 laqad ḥaqqa alqawlu ʿalā akṯarihim fahum lā yu/minoona Already hath the judgment, (for their infidelity) proved true of most of them, for they believe not.
008 innā jaʿalnā fee aʿnāqihim aġlālan fahiya ilā al-aḏqāni fahum muqmaḥoona Lo! We have put on their necks carcans reaching unto the chins, so that they are made stiff-necked.
009 wajaʿalnā min bayni aydeehim saddan wamin ḫalfihim saddan faaġšaynāhum fahum lā yubṣiroona And We have set a bar before them and a bar behind them, and (thus) have covered them so that they see not.
010 wasawāon ʿalayhim aanḏartahum am lam tunḏirhum lā yu/minoona Whether thou warn them or thou warn them not, it is alike for them, for they believe not.
011 innamā tunḏiru mani ittabaʿa alṯṯikra waḫašiya alrraḥmāna bialġaybi fabašširhu bimaġfiratin waajrin kareemin Thou warnest only him who followeth the Reminder and feareth the Beneficent in secret. To him bear tidings of forgiveness and a rich reward.
012 innā naḥnu nuḥyee almawtā wanaktubu mā qaddamoo waāṯārahum wakulla šay-in ahsaynāhu fee imāmin mubeenin Lo! We it is Who bring the dead to life. We record that which they send before (them, and their footprints. And all things We have kept in a clear Register.
013 waiḍrib lahum maṯalan aṣ-haba alqaryati iḏ jāahā almursaloona Coin for them a similitude: The people of the city when those sent (from Allah) came unto them;
014 iḏ arsalnā ilayhimu iṯnayni fakaṯṯaboohumā faʿazzaznā biṯāliṯin faqāloo innā ilaykum mursaloona When We sent unto them twain, and they denied them both, so We reinforced them with a third, and they said: Lo! we have been sent unto you.
015 qāloo mā antum illā bašarun miṯlunā wamā anzala alrraḥmānu min šay-in in antum illā takḏiboona They said: Ye are but mortals like unto us. The Beneficent hath naught revealed. Ye do but lie!
016 qāloo rabbunā yaʿlamu innā ilaykum lamursaloona They answered: Our Lord knoweth that we are indeed sent unto you,
017 wamā ʿalaynā illā albalāġu almubeenu And our duty is but plain conveyance (of the message).
018 qāloo innā taṭayyarnā bikum la-in lam tantahoo lanarjumannakum walayamassannakum minnā ʿaṯabun aleemun (The people of the city) said: We augur ill of you. If ye desist not, we shall surely stone you, and grievous torture will befall you at our hands.
019 qāloo ta-irukum maʿakum a-in ḏukkirtum bal antum qawmun musrifoona They said: Your evil augury be with you! Is it because ye are reminded (of the truth) ? Nay, but ye are froward folk!
020 wajāa min aqsa almadeenati rajulun yasʿā qāla yā qawmi ittabiʿoo almursaleena And there came from the uttermost part of the city a man running. He cried: O my people! Follow those who have been sent!
021 ittabiʿoo man lā yas-alukum ajran wahum muhtadoona Follow those who ask of you no fee, and who are rightly guided.
022 wamā liya lā aʿbudu allāḏee faṭaranee wa-ilayhi turjaʿoona For what cause should I not serve Him Who hath created me, and unto Whom ye will be brought back ?
023 aattaḫiḏu min doonihi ālihatan in yuridni alrraḥmānu biḍurrin la tuġni ʿannee šafāʿatuhum šay-an walā yunqiḏooni Shall I take (other) gods in place of Him when, if the Beneficent should wish me any harm, their intercession will avail me naught, nor can they save ?
024 innee iḏan lafee ḍalālin mubeenin Then truly I should be in error manifest.
025 innee āmantu birabbikum faismaʿooni Lo! I have believed in your Lord, so hear me!
026 qeela odḫuli aljannata qāla yā layta qawmee yaʿlamoona It was said (unto him): Enter paradise. He said: Would that my people knew
027 bimā ġafara lee rabbee wajaʿalanee mina almukrameena With what (munificence) my Lord hath pardoned me and made me of the honoured ones!
028 wamā anzalnā ʿalā qawmihi min baʿdihi min jundin mina alssamā-i wamā kunnā munzileena We sent not down against his people after him a host from heaven, nor do We ever send.
029 in kānat illā ṣayḥatan wahidatan fa-iṯa hum ḫāmidoona It was but one Shout, and lo! they were extinct.
030 yā ḥasratan ʿalā alʿibādi mā ya/teehim min rasoolin illā kānoo bihi yastahzi-oona Ah, the anguish for the bondmen! Never came there unto them a messenger but they did mock him!
031 alam yaraw kam ahlaknā qablahum mina alqurooni annahum ilayhim lā yarjiʿoona Have they not seen how many generations We destroyed before them, which indeed returned not unto them;
032 wa-in kullun lammā jameeʿun ladaynā muhdaroona But all, without exception, will be brought before Us.
033 waāyatun lahumu al-arḍu almaytatu aḥyaynāhā waaḫrajnā minhā ḥabban faminhu ya/kuloona A token unto them is the dead earth. We revive it, and We bring forth from it grain so that they eat thereof;
034 wajaʿalnā feehā jannātin min naḫeelin waaʿnābin wafajjarnā feehā mina alʿuyooni And We have placed therein gardens of the date-palm and grapes, and We have caused springs of water to gush forth therein,
035 liya/kuloo min ṯamarihi wamā ʿamilat-hu aydeehim afalā yaškuroona That they may eat of the fruit thereof, and their hands made it not. Will they not, then, give thanks ?
036 subhana allāḏee ḫalaqa al-azwāja kullahā mimmā tunbitu al-arḍu wamin anfusihim wamimmā lā yaʿlamoona Glory be to Him Who created all the sexual pairs, of that which the earth groweth, and of themselves, and of that which they know not!
037 waāyatun lahumu allāylu naslaḫu minhu alnnahāra fa-iṯa hum muḏlimoona A token unto them is night. We strip it of the day, and lo! they are in darkness.
038 waalššamsu tajree limustaqarrin lahā ṯalika taqdeeru alʿazeezi alʿaleemi And the sun runneth on unto a resting-place for him. That is the measuring of the Mighty, the Wise.
039 waalqamara qaddarnāhu manāzila ḥattā ʿāda kaalʿurjooni alqadeemi And for the moon We have appointed mansions till she return like an old shrivelled palm-leaf.
040 lā alššamsu yanbaġee lahā an tudrika alqamara walā allāylu sābiqu alnnahāri wakullun fee falakin yasbaḥoona It is not for the sun to overtake the moon, nor doth the night outstrip the day. They float each in an orbit.
041 waāyatun lahum annā ḥamalnā ḏurriyyatahum fee alfulki almašḥooni And a token unto them is that We bear their offspring in the laden ship,
042 waḫalaqnā lahum min miṯlihi mā yarkaboona And have created for them of the like thereof whereon they ride.
043 wa-in naša/ nuġriqhum falā ṣareeḫa lahum walā hum yunqaḏoona And if We will, We drown them, and there is no help for them, neither can they be saved;
044 illā raḥmatan minnā wamatāʿan ilā ḥeenin Unless by mercy from Us and as comfort for a while.
045 wa-iṯa qeela lahumu ittaqoo mā bayna aydeekum wamā ḫalfakum laʿallakum turḥamoona When it is said unto them: Beware of that which is before you and that which is behind you, that haply ye may find mercy (they are heedless).
046 wamā ta/teehim min āyatin min āyāti rabbihim illā kānoo ʿanhā muʿriḍeena Never came a token of the tokens of their Lord to them, but they did turn away from it!
047 wa-iṯa qeela lahum anfiqoo mimmā razaqakumu allāhu qāla allāḏeena kafaroo lillaḏeena āmanoo anuṭʿimu man law yašāo allāhu aṭʿamahu in antum illā fee ḍalālin mubeenin And when it is said unto them: Spend of that wherewith Allah hath provided you, those who disbelieve say unto those who believe: Shall we feed those whom Allah, if He willed, would feed ? Ye are in naught else than error manifest.
048 wayaqooloona matā haṯa alwaʿdu in kuntum sadiqeena And they say: When will this promise be fulfilled, if ye are truthful ?
049 mā yanḏuroona illā ṣayḥatan wahidatan ta/ḫuḏuhum wahum yaḫiṣimoona They await but one Shout, which will surprise them while they are disputing.
050 falā yastaṭeeʿoona tawṣiyatan walā ilā ahlihim yarjiʿoona Then they cannot make bequest, nor can they return to their own folk.
051 wanufiḫa fee alṣoori fa-iṯa hum mina al-ajdāṯi ilā rabbihim yansiloona And the trumpet is blown and lo! from the graves they hie unto their Lord,
052 qāloo yā waylanā man baʿaṯanā min marqadinā haṯa mā waʿada alrraḥmānu waṣadaqa almursaloona Crying: Woe upon us! Who hath raised us from our place of sleep ? This is that which the Beneficent did promise, and the messengers spoke truth.
053 in kānat illā ṣayḥatan wahidatan fa-iṯa hum jameeʿun ladaynā muhdaroona It is but one Shout, and behold them brought together before Us!
054 faalyawma lā tuḏlamu nafsun šay-an walā tujzawna illā mā kuntum taʿmaloona This day no soul is wronged in aught; nor are ye requited aught save what ye used to do.
055 inna aṣ-haba aljannati alyawma fee šuġulin fākihoona Lo! those who merit paradise this day are happily employed,
056 hum waazwājuhum fee ḏilālin ʿalā al-arā-iki muttaki-oona They and their wives, in pleasant shade, on thrones reclining;
057 lahum feehā fākihatun walahum mā yaddaʿoona Theirs the fruit (of their good deeds) and theirs (all) that they ask;
058 salāmun qawlan min rabbin raḥeemin The word from a Merciful Lord (for them) is: Peace!
059 waimtāzoo alyawma ayyuhā almujrimoona But avaunt ye, O ye guilty, this day!
060 alam aʿhad ilaykum yā banee ādama an lā taʿbudoo alššaytana innahu lakum ʿaduwwun mubeenun Did I not charge you, O ye sons of Adam, that ye worship not the devil - Lo! he is your open foe! -
061 waani oʿbudoonee haṯa ṣiratun mustaqeemun But that ye worship Me ? That was the right path.
062 walaqad aḍalla minkum jibillan kaṯeeran afalam takoonoo taʿqiloona Yet he hath led astray of you a great multitude. Had ye then no sense ?
063 haṯihi jahannamu allātee kuntum tooʿadoona This is hell which ye were promised (if ye followed him).
064 iṣlawhā alyawma bimā kuntum takfuroona Burn therein this day for that ye disbelieved.
065 alyawma naḫtimu ʿalā afwāhihim watukallimunā aydeehim watašhadu arjuluhum bimā kānoo yaksiboona This day We seal up their mouths, and their hands speak out to Us and their feet bear witness as to what they used to earn.
066 walaw našāo laṭamasnā ʿalā aʿyunihim faistabaqoo alṣirata faannā yubṣiroona And had We willed, We verily could have quenched their eyesight so that they should struggle for the way. Then how could they have seen ?
067 walaw našāo lamasaḫnāhum ʿalā makānatihim famā istataʿoo muḍiyyan walā yarjiʿoona And had We willed, We verily could have fixed them in their place, making them powerless to go forward or turn back.
068 waman nuʿammirhu nunakkis-hu fee alḫalqi afalā yaʿqiloona He whom we bring unto old age, We reverse him in creation (making him go back to weakness after strength). Have ye then no sense ?
069 wamā ʿallamnāhu alššiʿra wamā yanbaġee lahu in huwa illā ḏikrun waqur-ānun mubeenun And We have not taught him (Muhammad) poetry, nor is it meet for him. This is naught else than a Reminder and a Lecture making plain,
070 liyunḏira man kāna ḥayyan wayaḥiqqa alqawlu ʿalā alkāfireena To warn whosoever liveth, and that the word may be fulfilled against the disbelievers.
071 awa lam yaraw annā ḫalaqnā lahum mimmā ʿamilat aydeenā anʿāman fahum lahā mālikoona Have they not seen how We have created for them of Our handiwork the cattle, so that they are their owners,
072 waḏallalnāhā lahum faminhā rakoobuhum waminhā ya/kuloona And have subdued them unto them, so that some of them they have for riding, some for food ?
073 walahum feehā manāfiʿu wamašāribu afalā yaškuroona Benefits and (divers) drinks have they from them. Will they not then give thanks ?
074 waittaḫaḏoo min dooni allāhi ālihatan laʿallahum yunṣaroona And they have taken (other) gods beside Allah, in order that they may be helped.
075 lā yastaṭeeʿoona naṣrahum wahum lahum jundun muhdaroona It is not in their power to help them; but they (the worshippers) are unto them a host in arms.
076 falā yaḥzunka qawluhum innā naʿlamu mā yusirroona wamā yuʿlinoona So let not their speech grieve thee (O Muhammad). Lo! We know what they conceal and what proclaim.
077 awa lam yarā al-insānu annā ḫalaqnāhu min nuṭfatin fa-iṯa huwa ḫaṣeemun mubeenun Hath not man seen that We have created him from a drop of seed ? Yet lo! he is an open opponent.
078 waḍaraba lanā maṯalan wanasiya ḫalqahu qāla man yuḥyee alʿiḏāma wahiya rameemun And he hath coined for Us a similitude, and hath forgotten the fact of his creation, saying: Who will revive these bones when they have rotted away ?
079 qul yuḥyeehā allāḏee anšaahā awwala marratin wahuwa bikulli ḫalqin ʿaleemun Say: He will revive them Who produced them at the first, for He is Knower of every creation,
080 allāḏee jaʿala lakum mina alššajari al-aḫḍari nāran fa-iṯa antum minhu tooqidoona Who hath appointed for you fire from the green tree, and behold! ye kindle from it.
081 awa laysa allāḏee ḫalaqa alssamāwāti waal-arḍa biqādirin ʿalā an yaḫluqa miṯlahum balā wahuwa alḫallāqu alʿaleemu Is not He Who created the heavens and the earth Able to create the like of them ? Aye, that He is! for He is the All-Wise Creator,
082 innamā amruhu iṯa arāda šay-an an yaqoola lahu kun fayakoonu But His command, when He intendeth a thing, is only that He saith unto it: Be! and it is.
83 
fasubhana allāḏee biyadihi malakootu kulli šay-in wa-ilayhi turjaʿoona
Therefor Glory be to Him in Whose hand is the dominion over all things! Unto Him ye will be brought back.
