71. Nuh: Noah

001 innā arsalnā nooḥan ilā qawmihi an anḏir qawmaka min qabli an ya/tiyahum ʿaṯabun aleemun Lo! We sent Noah unto his people (saying): Warn thy people ere the painful doom come unto them.
002 qāla yā qawmi innee lakum naḏeerun mubeenun He said: O my people! Lo! I am a plain warner unto you
003 ani oʿbudoo allāha waittaqoohu waaṭeeʿooni (Bidding you): Serve Allah and keep your duty unto Him and obey me,
004 yaġfir lakum min ḏunoobikum wayu-aḫḫirkum ilā ajalin musamman inna ajala allāhi iṯa jāa lā yu-aḫḫaru law kuntum taʿlamoona That He may forgive you somewhat of your sins and respite you to an appointed term. Lo! the term of Allah, when it cometh, cannot be delayed, if ye but knew.
005 qāla rabbi innee daʿawtu qawmee laylan wanahārān He said: My Lord! Lo! I have called unto my people night and day
006 falam yazidhum duʿā-ee illā firārān But all my calling doth but add to their repugnance;
007 wa-innee kullamā daʿawtuhum litaġfira lahum jaʿaloo asabiʿahum fee aṯanihim waistaġšaw ṯiyābahum waaṣarroo waistakbaroo istikbārān And lo! whenever I call unto them that Thou mayst pardon them they thrust their fingers in their ears and cover themselves with their garments and persist (in their refusal) and magnify themselves in pride.
008 thumma innee daʿawtuhum jihārān And lo! I have called unto them aloud,
009 thumma innee aʿlantu lahum waasrartu lahum isrārān And lo! I have made public proclamation unto them, and I have appealed to them in private.
010 faqultu istaġfiroo rabbakum innahu kāna ġaffārān And I have said: Seek pardon of your Lord. Lo! He was ever Forgiving.
011 yursili alssamāa ʿalaykum midrārān He will let loose the sky for you in plenteous rain,
012 wayumdidkum bi-amwālin wabaneena wayajʿal lakum jannātin wayajʿal lakum anhārān And will help you with wealth and sons, and will assign unto you Gardens and will assign unto you rivers.
013 mā lakum lā tarjoona lillāhi waqārān What aileth you that ye hope not toward Allah for dignity
014 waqad ḫalaqakum aṭwārān When He created you by (divers) stages ?
015 alam taraw kayfa ḫalaqa allāhu sabʿa samāwātin ṭibāqān See ye not how Allah hath created seven heavens in harmony,
016 wajaʿala alqamara feehinna nooran wajaʿala alššamsa sirājān And hath made the moon a light therein, and made the sun a lamp ?
017 waallāhu anbatakum mina al-arḍi nabātān And Allah hath caused you to grow as a growth from the earth,
018 thumma yuʿeedukum feehā wayuḫrijukum iḫrājān And afterward He maketh you return thereto, and He will bring you forth again, a (new) forthbringing.
019 waallāhu jaʿala lakumu al-arḍa bisatan And Allah hath made the earth a wide expanse for you
020 litaslukoo minhā subulan fijājān That ye may thread the valley-ways thereof.
021 qāla nooḥun rabbi innahum ʿaṣawnee waittabaʿoo man lam yazidhu māluhu wawaladuhu illā ḫasārān Noah said: My Lord! Lo! they have disobeyed me and followed one whose wealth and children increase him in naught save ruin;
022 wamakaroo makran kubbārān And they have plotted a mighty plot,
023 waqāloo lā taḏarunna ālihatakum walā taḏarunna waddan walā suwāʿan walā yaġooṯa wayaʿooqa wanasrān And they have said: Forsake not your gods. Forsake not Wadd, nor Suwa', nor Yaghuth and Ya'uq and Nasr.
024 waqad aḍalloo kaṯeeran walā tazidi alṯṯālimeena illā ḍalālān And they have led many astray, and Thou increasest the wrong-doers in naught save error.
025 mimmā ḫaṭee-ātihim oġriqoo faodḫiloo nāran falam yajidoo lahum min dooni allāhi ansarān Because of their sins they were drowned, then made to enter a Fire. And they found they had no helpers in place of Allah.
026 waqāla nooḥun rabbi lā taḏar ʿalā al-arḍi mina alkāfireena dayyārān And Noah said: My Lord! Leave not one of the disbelievers in the land.
027 innaka in taḏarhum yuḍilloo ʿibādaka walā yalidoo illā fājiran kaffārān If Thou shouldst leave them, they will mislead Thy slaves and will beget none save lewd ingrates.
28 
rabbi iġfir lee waliwālidayya waliman daḫala baytiya mu/minan walilmu/mineena waalmu/mināti walā tazidi alṯṯālimeena illā tabāran
My Lord! Forgive me and my parents and him who entereth my house believing, and believing men and believing women, and increase not the wrong-doers in aught save ruin.
