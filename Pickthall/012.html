12. Yusuf: Joseph

001 alif-lām-rā tilka āyātu alkitābi almubeenu Alif. Lam. Ra. These are verse of the Scripture that maketh plain.
002 innā anzalnāhu qur-ānan ʿarabiyyan laʿallakum taʿqiloona Lo! We have revealed it, a Lecture in Arabic, that ye may understand.
003 naḥnu naquṣu ʿalayka aḥsana alqaṣaṣi bimā awḥaynā ilayka haṯa alqur-āna wa-in kunta min qablihi lamina alġāfileena We narrate unto thee (Muhammad) the best of narratives in that We have inspired in thee this Qur'an, though aforetime thou wast of the heedless.
004 iḏ qāla yoosufu li-abeehi yā abati innee raaytu aḥada ʿašara kawkaban waalššamsa waalqamara raaytuhum lee sājideena When Joseph said unto his father: O my father! Lo! I saw in a dream eleven planets and the sun and the moon, I saw them prostrating themselves unto me.
005 qāla yā bunayya lā taqṣuṣ ru/yāka ʿalā iḫwatika fayakeedoo laka kaydan inna alššaytana lil-insāni ʿaduwwun mubeenun He said: O my dear son! Tell not thy brethren of thy vision, lest they plot a plot against thee. Lo! Satan is for man an open foe.
006 wakaṯalika yajtabeeka rabbuka wayuʿallimuka min ta/weeli al-ahadeeṯi wayutimmu niʿmatahu ʿalayka waʿalā āli yaʿqooba kamā atammahā ʿalā abawayka min qablu ibrāheema wa-ishaqa inna rabbaka ʿaleemun ḥakeemun Thus thy Lord will prefer thee and will teach thee the interpretation of events, and will perfect His grace upon thee and upon the family of Jacob as He perfected it upon thy forefathers, Abraham and Isaac. Lo! thy Lord is Knower, Wise.
007 laqad kāna fee yoosufa wa-iḫwatihi āyātun lilssā-ileena Verily in Joseph and his brethren are signs (of Allah's Sovereignty) for the inquiring.
008 iḏ qāloo layoosufu waaḫoohu aḥabbu ilā abeenā minnā wanaḥnu ʿuṣbatun inna abānā lafee ḍalālin mubeenin When they said: Verily Joseph and his brother are dearer to our father than we are, many though we be. Lo! our father is in plain aberration.
009 oqtuloo yoosufa awi iṭraḥoohu arḍan yaḫlu lakum wajhu abeekum watakoonoo min baʿdihi qawman saliḥeena (One said): Kill Joseph or cast him to some (other) land, so that your father's favour may be all for you, and (that) ye may afterward be righteous folk.
010 qāla qā-ilun minhum lā taqtuloo yoosufa waalqoohu fee ġayābati aljubbi yaltaqiṭhu baʿḍu alssayyārati in kuntum fāʿileena One among them said: Kill not Joseph but, if ye must be doing, fling him into the depth of the pit; some caravan will find him.
011 qāloo yā abānā mā laka lā ta/mannā ʿalā yoosufa wa-innā lahu lanasiḥoona They said: O our father! Why wilt thou not trust us with Joseph, when lo! we are good friends to him ?
012 arsilhu maʿanā ġadan yartaʿ wayalʿab wa-innā lahu lahafiḏoona Send him with us to-morrow that he may enjoy himself and play. And lo! we shall take good care of him.
013 qāla innee layaḥzununee an ṯaḏhaboo bihi waaḫāfu an ya/kulahu alṯṯi/bu waantum ʿanhu ġāfiloona He said: Lo! in truth it saddens me that ye should take him with you, and I fear less the wolf devour him while ye are heedless of him.
014 qāloo la-in akalahu alṯṯi/bu wanaḥnu ʿuṣbatun innā iḏan laḫāsiroona They said: If the wolf should devour him when we are (so strong) a band, then surely we should have already perished.
015 falammā ḏahaboo bihi waajmaʿoo an yajʿaloohu fee ġayābati aljubbi waawḥaynā ilayhi latunabi-annahum bi-amrihim haṯa wahum lā yašʿuroona Then, when they led him off, and were of one mind that they should place him in the depth of the pit, We inspired in him: Thou wilt tell them of this deed of theirs when they know (thee) not.
016 wajāoo abāhum ʿišāan yabkoona And they came weeping to their father in the evening.
017 qāloo yā abānā innā ḏahabnā nastabiqu wataraknā yoosufa ʿinda matāʿinā faakalahu alṯṯi/bu wamā anta bimu/minin lanā walaw kunnā sadiqeena Saying: O our father! We went racing one with another, and left Joseph by our things, and the wolf devoured him, and thou believest not our saying even when we speak the truth.
018 wajāoo ʿalā qameeṣihi bidamin kaḏibin qāla bal sawwalat lakum anfusukum amran faṣabrun jameelun waallāhu almustaʿānu ʿalā mā taṣifoona And they came with false blood on his shirt. He said: Nay, but your minds have beguiled you into something. (My course is) comely patience. And Allah it is Whose help is to be sought in that (predicament) which ye describe.
019 wajāat sayyāratun faarsaloo wāridahum faadlā dalwahu qāla yā bušrā haṯa ġulāmun waasarroohu bidaʿatan waallāhu ʿaleemun bimā yaʿmaloona And there came a caravan, and they sent their waterdrawer. He let down his pail (into the pit). He said: Good luck! Here is a youth. And they hid him as a treasure, and Allah was Aware of what they did.
020 wašarawhu biṯamanin baḫsin darāhima maʿdoodatin wakānoo feehi mina alzzāhideena And they sold him for a low price, a number of silver coins; and they attached no value to him.
021 waqāla allāḏee ištarāhu min miṣra liimraatihi akrimee maṯwāhu ʿasā an yanfaʿanā aw nattaḫiḏahu waladan wakaṯalika makkannā liyoosufa fee al-arḍi walinuʿallimahu min ta/weeli al-ahadeeṯi waallāhu ġālibun ʿalā amrihi walākinna akṯara alnnāsi lā yaʿlamoona And he of Egypt who purchased him said unto his wife: Receive him honourably. Perchance he may prove useful to us or we may adopt him as a son. Thus we established Joseph in the land that We might teach him the interpretation of events. And Allah was predominant in His career, but most of mankind know not.
022 walammā balaġa ašuddahu ātaynāhu ḥukman waʿilman wakaṯalika najzee almuḥsineena And And when he reached his prime We gave him wisdom and knowledge. Thus We reward the good.
023 warāwadat-hu allātee huwa fee baytihā ʿan nafsihi waġallaqati al-abwāba waqālat hayta laka qāla maʿaṯa allāhi innahu rabbee aḥsana maṯwāya innahu lā yufliḥu alṯṯālimoona And she, in whose house he was, asked of him an evil act. She bolted the doors and said: Come! He said: I seek refuge in Allah! Lo! he is my lord, who hath treated me honourably. Lo! wrong-doers never prosper.
024 walaqad hammat bihi wahamma bihā lawlā an raā burhāna rabbihi kaṯalika linaṣrifa ʿanhu alssoo-a waalfaḥšāa innahu min ʿibādinā almuḫlaṣeena She verily desired him, and he would have desired her if it had not been that he saw the argument of his Lord. Thus it was, that We might ward off from him evil and lewdness. Lo! he was of Our chosen slaves.
025 waistabaqā albāba waqaddat qameeṣahu min duburin waalfayā sayyidahā ladā albābi qālat mā jazāo man arāda bi-ahlika soo-an illā an yusjana aw ʿaṯabun aleemun And they raced with one another to the door, and she tore his shirt from behind, and they met her lord and master at the door. She said: What shall be his reward, who wisheth evil to thy folk, save prison or a painful doom ?
026 qāla hiya rāwadatnee ʿan nafsee wašahida šāhidun min ahlihā in kāna qameeṣuhu qudda min qubulin faṣadaqat wahuwa mina alkaṯibeena (Joseph) said: She it was who asked of me an evil act. And a witness of her own folk testified: If his shirt is torn from before, then she speaketh truth and he is of the liars.
027 wa-in kāna qameeṣuhu qudda min duburin fakaḏabat wahuwa mina alssadiqeena And if his shirt is torn from behind, then she hath lied and he is of the truthful.
028 falammā raā qameeṣahu qudda min duburin qāla innahu min kaydikunna inna kaydakunna ʿaḏeemun So when he saw his shirt torn from behind, he said: Lo! this is of the guile of you women. Lo! the guile of you is very great.
029 yoosufu aʿriḍ ʿan haṯa waistaġfiree liḏanbiki innaki kunti mina alḫati-eena O Joseph! Turn away from this, and thou, (O woman), ask forgiveness for thy sin. Lo! thou art of the faulty.
030 waqāla niswatun fee almadeenati imraatu alʿazeezi turāwidu fatāhā ʿan nafsihi qad šaġafahā ḥubban innā lanarāhā fee ḍalālin mubeenin And women in the city said: The ruler's wife is asking of her slave-boy an ill- deed. Indeed he has smitten her to the heart with love. We behold her in plain aberration.
031 falammā samiʿat bimakrihinna arsalat ilayhinna waaʿtadat lahunna muttakaan waātat kulla wahidatin minhunna sikkeenan waqālati oḫruj ʿalayhinna falammā raaynahu akbarnahu waqattaʿna aydiyahunna waqulna haša lillāhi mā haṯa bašaran in haṯa illā malakun kareemun And when she heard of their sly talk, she sent to them and prepared for them a cushioned couch (to lie on at the feast) and gave to every one of them a knife and said (to Joseph): Come out unto them! And when they saw him they exalted him and cut their hands, exclaiming: Allah Blameless! This is no a human being. This is not other than some gracious angel.
032 qālat faṯalikunna allāḏee lumtunnanee feehi walaqad rāwadtuhu ʿan nafsihi faistʿṣama wala-in lam yafʿal mā āmuruhu layusjananna walayakoonan mina alssaġireena She said: This is he on whose account ye blamed me. I asked of him an evil act, but he proved continent, but if he do not my behest he verily shall be imprisoned, and verily shall be of those brought low.
033 qāla rabbi alssijnu aḥabbu ilayya mimmā yadʿoonanee ilayhi wa-illā taṣrif ʿannee kaydahunna aṣbu ilayhinna waakun mina aljāhileena He said: O my Lord! Prison is more dear than that unto which they urge me, and if Thou fend not off their wiles from me I shall incline unto them and become of the foolish.
034 faistajāba lahu rabbuhu faṣarafa ʿanhu kaydahunna innahu huwa alssameeʿu alʿaleemu So his Lord heard his prayer and fended off their wiles from him. Lo! He is Hearer, Knower.
035 thumma badā lahum min baʿdi mā raawoo al-āyāti layasjununnahu ḥattā ḥeenin And it seemed good to them (the men-folk) after they had seen the signs (of his innocence) to imprison him for a time.
036 wadaḫala maʿahu alssijna fatayāni qāla aḥaduhumā innee arānee aʿṣiru ḫamran waqāla al-āḫaru innee arānee aḥmilu fawqa ra/see ḫubzan ta/kulu alttayru minhu nabbi/nā bita/weelihi innā narāka mina almuḥsineena And two young men went to prison with him. One of them said: I dreamed that I was pressing wine. The other said: I dreamed that I was carrying upon my head bread whereof the birds were eating. Announce unto us the interpretation, for we see thee of those good (at interpretation).
037 qāla lā ya/teekumā ṭaʿāmun turzaqānihi illā nabba/tukumā bita/weelihi qabla an ya/tiyakumā ṯalikumā mimmā ʿallamanee rabbee innee taraktu millata qawmin lā yu/minoona biallāhi wahum bial-āḫirati hum kāfiroona He said: The food which ye are given (daily) shall not come unto you but I shall tell you the interpretation ere it cometh unto you. This is of that which my Lord hath taught me. Lo! I have forsaken the religion of folk who believe not in Allah and are disbelievers in the Hereafter.
038 waittabaʿtu millata ābā-ee ibrāheema wa-ishaqa wayaʿqooba mā kāna lanā an nušrika biallāhi min šay-in ṯalika min faḍli allāhi ʿalaynā waʿalā alnnāsi walākinna akṯara alnnāsi lā yaškuroona And I have followed the religion of my fathers, Abraham and Isaac and Jacob. It never was for us to attribute aught as partner to Allah. This is of the bounty of Allah unto us (the seed of Abraham) and unto mankind; but most men give not thanks.
039 yā sahibayi alssijni aarbābun mutafarriqoona ḫayrun ami allāhu alwahidu alqahhāru O my fellow-prisoners! Are divers lords better, or Allah the One, Almighty ?
040 mā taʿbudoona min doonihi illā asmāan sammaytumoohā antum waābāokum mā anzala allāhu bihā min sultanin ini alḥukmu illā lillāhi amara allā taʿbudoo illā iyyāhu ṯalika alddeenu alqayyimu walākinna akṯara alnnāsi lā yaʿlamoona Those whom ye worship beside Him are but names which ye have named, ye and your fathers. Allah hath revealed no sanction for them. The decision rests with Allah only, Who hath commanded you that ye worship none save Him. This is the right religion, but most men know not.
041 yā sahibayi alssijni ammā aḥadukumā fayasqee rabbahu ḫamran waammā al-āḫaru fayuṣlabu fata/kulu alttayru min ra/sihi quḍiya al-amru allāḏee feehi tastaftiyāni O my two fellow-prisoners! As for one of you, he will pour out wine for his lord to drink; and as for the other, he will be crucified so that the birds will eat from his head. Thus is the case judged concerning which ye did inquire.
042 waqāla lillaḏee ḏanna annahu nājin minhumā oḏkurnee ʿinda rabbika faansāhu alššaytanu ḏikra rabbihi falabiṯa fee alssijni biḍʿa sineena And he said unto him of the twain who he knew would be released: Mention me in the presence of thy lord. But Satan caused him to forget to mention it to his lord, so he (Joseph) stayed in prison for some years.
043 waqāla almaliku innee arā sabʿa baqarātin simānin ya/kuluhunna sabʿun ʿijāfun wasabʿa sunbulātin ḫuḍrin waoḫara yābisātin yā ayyuhā almalao aftoonee fee ru/yāya in kuntum lilrru/yā taʿburoona And the king said: Lo! I saw in a dream seven fat kine which seven lean were eating, and seven green ears of corn and other (seven) dry. O notables! Expound for me my vision, if ye can interpret dreams.
044 qāloo aḍġāṯu aḥlāmin wamā naḥnu bita/weeli al-aḥlāmi biʿālimeena They answered: Jumbled dreams! And we are not knowing in the interpretation of dreams.
045 waqāla allāḏee najā minhumā waiddakara baʿda ommatin anā onabbi-okum bita/weelihi faarsilooni And he of the two who was released, and (now) at length remembered, said: I am going to announce unto you the interpretation, therefore send me forth.
046 yoosufu ayyuhā alṣiddeequ aftinā fee sabʿi baqarātin simānin ya/kuluhunna sabʿun ʿijāfun wasabʿi sunbulātin ḫuḍrin waoḫara yābisātin laʿallee arjiʿu ilā alnnāsi laʿallahum yaʿlamoona (And when he came to Joseph in the prison, he exclaimed): Joseph! O thou truthful one! Expound for us the seven fat kine which seven lean were eating and the seven green ears of corn and other (seven) dry, that I may return unto the people, so that they may know.
047 qāla tazraʿoona sabʿa sineena daaban famā ḥaṣadtum faḏaroohu fee sunbulihi illā qaleelan mimmā ta/kuloona He said: Ye shall sow seven years as usual, but that which ye reap, leave it in the ear, all save a little which ye eat.
048 thumma ya/tee min baʿdi ṯalika sabʿun šidādun ya/kulna mā qaddamtum lahunna illā qaleelan mimmā tuhsinoona Then after that will come seven hard years which will devour all that ye have prepared for them, save a little of that which ye have stored.
049 thumma ya/tee min baʿdi ṯalika ʿāmun feehi yuġāṯu alnnāsu wafeehi yaʿṣiroona Then, after that, will come a year when the people will have plenteous crops and when they will press (wine and oil).
050 waqāla almaliku i/toonee bihi falammā jāahu alrrasoolu qāla irjiʿ ilā rabbika fais-alhu mā bālu alnniswati allātee qattaʿna aydiyahunna inna rabbee bikaydihinna ʿaleemun And the king said: Bring him unto me. And when the messenger came unto him, he (Joseph) said: Return unto thy lord and ask him what was the case of the women who cut their hands. Lo! my Lord knoweth their guile.
051 qāla mā ḫaṭbukunna iḏ rāwadtunna yoosufa ʿan nafsihi qulna haša lillāhi mā ʿalimnā ʿalayhi min soo-in qālati imraatu alʿazeezi al-āna ḥašaṣa alḥaqqu anā rāwadtuhu ʿan nafsihi wa-innahu lamina alssadiqeena He (the king) (then sent for those women and) said: What happened when ye asked an evil act of Joseph ? They answered: Allah Blameless! We know no evil of him. Said the wife of the ruler: Now the truth is out. I asked of him an evil act, and he is surely of the truthful.
052 [romanization missing] (Then Joseph said: I asked for) this, that he (my lord) may know that I betrayed him not in secret, and that surely Allah guideth not the snare of the betrayers.
053 wamā obarri-o nafsee inna alnnafsa laammāratun bialssoo-i illā mā raḥima rabbee inna rabbee ġafoorun raḥeemun I do not exculpate myself. Lo! the (human) soul enjoineth unto evil, save that whereon my Lord hath mercy. Lo! my Lord is Forgiving, Merciful.
054 waqāla almaliku i/toonee bihi astaḫliṣhu linafsee falammā kallamahu qāla innaka alyawma ladaynā makeenun ameenun And the king said: Bring him unto me that I may attach him to my person. And when he had talked with him he said: Lo! thou art to-day in our presence established and trusted.
055 qāla ijʿalnee ʿalā ḫazā-ini al-arḍi innee ḥafeeḏun ʿaleemun He said: Set me over the storehouses of the land. Lo! I am a skilled custodian.
056 wakaṯalika makannā liyoosufa fee al-arḍi yatabawwao minhā ḥayṯu yašāo nuṣeebu biraḥmatinā man našāo walā nuḍeeʿu ajra almuḥsineena Thus gave We power to Joseph in the land. He was the owner of it where he pleased. We reach with Our mercy whom We will. We lose not the reward of the good.
057 walaajru al-āḫirati ḫayrun lillaḏeena āmanoo wakānoo yattaqoona And the reward of the Hereafter is better, for those who believe and ward off (evil).
058 wajāa iḫwatu yoosufa fadaḫaloo ʿalayhi faʿarafahum wahum lahu munkiroona And Joseph's brethren came and presented themselves before him, and he knew them but they knew him not.
059 walammā jahhazahum bijahāzihim qāla i/toonee bi-aḫin lakum min abeekum alā tarawna annee oofee alkayla waanā ḫayru almunzileena And when he provided them with their provision he said: Bring unto me a brother of yours from your father. See ye not that I fill up the measure and I am the best of hosts ?
060 fa-in lam ta/toonee bihi falā kayla lakum ʿindee walā taqrabooni And if ye bring him not unto me, then there shall be no measure for you with me, nor shall ye draw near.
061 qāloo sanurāwidu ʿanhu abāhu wa-innā lafāʿiloona They said: We will try to win him from his father: that we will surely do.
062 waqāla lifityānihi ijʿaloo bidaʿatahum fee rihalihim laʿallahum yaʿrifoonahā iṯa inqalaboo ilā ahlihim laʿallahum yarjiʿoona He said unto his young men: Place their merchandise in their saddlebags, so that they may know it when they go back to their folk, and so will come again.
063 falammā rajaʿoo ilā abeehim qāloo yā abānā muniʿa minnā alkaylu faarsil maʿanā aḫānā naktal wa-innā lahu lahafiḏoona So when they went back to their father they said: O our father! The measure is denied us, so send with us our brother that we may obtain the measure, surely we will guard him well.
064 qāla hal āmanukum ʿalayhi illā kamā amintukum ʿalā aḫeehi min qablu faallāhu ḫayrun hafiḏan wahuwa arḥamu alrrahimeena He said: Can I entrust him to you save as I entrusted his brother to you aforetime ? Allah is better at guarding, and He is the Most Merciful of those who show mercy.
065 walammā fataḥoo matāʿahum wajadoo bidaʿatahum ruddat ilayhim qāloo yā abānā mā nabġee haṯihi bidaʿatunā ruddat ilaynā wanameeru ahlanā wanaḥfaḏu aḫānā wanazdādu kayla baʿeerin ṯalika kaylun yaseerun And when they opened their belongings they discovered that their merchandise had been returned to them. They said: O our father! What (more) can we ask ? Here is our merchandise returned to us. We shall get provision for our folk and guard our brother, and we shall have the extra measure of a camel (load). This (that we bring now) is a light measure.
066 qāla lan orsilahu maʿakum ḥattā tu/tooni mawṯiqan mina allāhi lata/tunnanee bihi illā an yuhata bikum falammā ātawhu mawṯiqahum qāla allāhu ʿalā mā naqoolu wakeelun He said: I will not send him with you till ye give me an undertaking in the name of Allah that ye will bring him back to me, unless ye are surrounded. And when they gave him their undertaking he said: Allah is the Warden over what we say.
067 waqāla yā baniyya lā tadḫuloo min bābin wahidin waodḫuloo min abwābin mutafarriqatin wamā oġnee ʿankum mina allāhi min šay-in ini alḥukmu illā lillāhi ʿalayhi tawakkaltu waʿalayhi falyatawakkali almutawakkiloona And he said: O my sons! Go not in by one gate; go in by different gates. I can naught avail you as against Allah. Lo! the decision rests with Allah only. In Him do I put my trust, and in Him let all the trusting put their trust.
068 walammā daḫaloo min ḥayṯu amarahum aboohum mā kāna yuġnee ʿanhum mina allāhi min šay-in illā hajatan fee nafsi yaʿqooba qadahā wa-innahu laḏoo ʿilmin limā ʿallamnāhu walākinna akṯara alnnāsi lā yaʿlamoona And when they entered in the manner which their father had enjoined, it would have naught availed them as against Allah; it was but a need of Jacob's soul which he thus satisfied; and lo! he was a lord of knowledge because We had taught him; but most of mankind know not.
069 walammā daḫaloo ʿalā yoosufa āwā ilayhi aḫāhu qāla innee anā aḫooka falā tabta-is bimā kānoo yaʿmaloona And when they went in before Joseph, he took his brother unto him, saying: Lo! I, even I, am thy brother, therefore sorrow not for what they did.
070 falammā jahhazahum bijahāzihim jaʿala alssiqāyata fee raḥli aḫeehi ṯumma aṯṯana mu-aṯṯinun ayyatuhā alʿeeru innakum lasāriqoona And when he provided them with their provision, he put the drinking-cup in his brother's saddlebag, and then a crier cried: O camel-riders! Lo! ye are surely thieves!
071 qāloo waaqbaloo ʿalayhim maṯa tafqidoona They cried, coming toward them: What is it ye have lost ?
072 qāloo nafqidu ṣuwāʿa almaliki waliman jāa bihi ḥimlu baʿeerin waanā bihi zaʿeemun They said: We have lost the king's cup, and he who bringeth it shall have a camel-load, and I (said Joseph) am answerable for it.
073 qāloo taallāhi laqad ʿalimtum mā ji/nā linufsida fee al-arḍi wamā kunnā sāriqeena They said: By Allah, well ye know we came not to do evil in the land, and are no thieves.
074 qāloo famā jazāohu in kuntum kaṯibeena They said: And what shall be the penalty for it, if ye prove liars ?
075 qāloo jazāohu man wujida fee raḥlihi fahuwa jazāohu kaṯalika najzee alṯṯālimeena They said: The penalty for it! He in whose bag (the cup) is found, he is the penalty for it. Thus we requite wrong-doers.
076 fabadaa bi-awʿiyatihim qabla wiʿā-i aḫeehi ṯumma istaḫrajahā min wiʿā-i aḫeehi kaṯalika kidnā liyoosufa mā kāna liya/ḫuḏa aḫāhu fee deeni almaliki illā an yašāa allāhu narfaʿu darajātin man našāo wafawqa kulli ḏee ʿilmin ʿaleemun Then he (Joseph) began the search with their bags before his brother's bag, then he produced it from his brother's bag. Thus did We contrive for Joseph. He could not have taken his brother according to the king's law unless Allah willed. We raise by grades (of mercy) whom We will, and over every lord of knowledge there is one more knowing.
077 qāloo in yasriq faqad saraqa aḫun lahu min qablu faasarrahā yoosufu fee nafsihi walam yubdihā lahum qāla antum šarrun makānan waallāhu aʿlamu bimā taṣifoona They said: If he stealeth, a brother of his stole before. But Joseph kept it secret in his soul and revealed it not unto them. He said (within himself): Ye are in worse case, and Allah knoweth best (the truth of) that which ye allege.
078 qāloo yā ayyuhā alʿazeezu inna lahu aban šayḫan kabeeran faḫuḏ aḥadanā makānahu innā narāka mina almuḥsineena They said: O ruler of the land! Lo! he hath a very aged father, so take one of us instead of him. Lo! we behold thee of those who do kindness.
079 qāla maʿaṯa allāhi an na/ḫuḏa illā man wajadnā matāʿanā ʿindahu innā iḏan laḏālimoona He said: Allah forbid that we should seize save him with whom we found our property; then truly we should be wrong-doers.
080 falammā istay-asoo minhu ḫalaṣoo najiyyan qāla kabeeruhum alam taʿlamoo anna abākum qad aḫaḏa ʿalaykum mawṯiqan mina allāhi wamin qablu mā farraṭtum fee yoosufa falan abraḥa al-arḍa ḥattā ya/ḏana lee abee aw yaḥkuma allāhu lee wahuwa ḫayru alhakimeena So, When they despaired of (moving) him, they conferred together apart. The eldest of them said: Know ye not how your father took an undertaking from you in Allah's name and how ye failed in the case of Joseph aforetime ? Therefore I shall not go forth from the land until my father giveth leave or Allah judgeth for me. He is the Best of Judges.
081 irjiʿoo ilā abeekum faqooloo yā abānā inna ibnaka saraqa wamā šahidnā illā bimā ʿalimnā wamā kunnā lilġaybi hafiḏeena Return unto your father and say: O our father! Lo! thy son hath stolen. We testify only to that which we know; we are not guardians of the Unseen.
082 wais-ali alqaryata allātee kunnā feehā waalʿeera allātee aqbalnā feehā wa-innā lasadiqoona Ask the township where we were, and the caravan with which we travelled hither. Lo! we speak the truth.
083 qāla bal sawwalat lakum anfusukum amran faṣabrun jameelun ʿasā allāhu an ya/tiyanee bihim jameeʿan innahu huwa alʿaleemu alḥakeemu (And when they came unto their father and had spoken thus to him) he said: Nay, but your minds have beguiled you into something. (My course is) comely patience! It may be that Allah will bring them all unto me. Lo! He, only He, is the Knower, the Wise.
084 watawallā ʿanhum waqāla yā asafā ʿalā yoosufa waibyaḍat ʿaynāhu mina alḥuzni fahuwa kaḏeemun And he turned away from them and said: Alas, my grief for Joseph! And his eyes were whitened with the sorrow that he was suppressing.
085 qāloo taallāhi taftao taḏkuru yoosufa ḥattā takoona ḥaraḍan aw takoona mina alhālikeena They said: By Allah, thou wilt never cease remembering Joseph till thy health is ruined or thou art of those who perish!
086 qāla innamā aškoo baṯṯee waḥuznee ilā allāhi waaʿlamu mina allāhi mā lā taʿlamoona He said: I expose my distress and anguish only unto Allah, and I know from Allah that which ye know not.
087 yā baniyya iḏhaboo fataḥassasoo min yoosufa waaḫeehi walā tay-asoo min rawḥi allāhi innahu lā yay-asu min rawḥi allāhi illā alqawmu alkāfiroona Go, O my sons, and ascertain concerning Joseph and his brother, and despair not of the Spirit of Allah. Lo! none despaireth of the Spirit of Allah save disbelieving folk.
088 falammā daḫaloo ʿalayhi qāloo yā ayyuhā alʿazeezu massanā waahlanā alḍurru waji/nā bibidaʿatin muzjātin faawfi lanā alkayla wataṣaddaq ʿalaynā inna allāha yajzee almutaṣaddiqeena And when they came (again) before him (Joseph) they said: O ruler! Misfortune hath touched us and our folk, and we bring but poor merchandise, so fill for us the measure and be charitable unto us. Lo! Allah will requite the charitable,
089 qāla hal ʿalimtum mā faʿaltum biyoosufa waaḫeehi iḏ antum jāhiloona He said: Know ye what ye did unto Joseph and his brother in your ignorance ?
090 qāloo a-innaka laanta yoosufa qāla anā yoosufu wahaṯa aḫee qad manna allāhu ʿalaynā innahu man yattaqi wayaṣbir fa-inna allāha lā yuḍeeʿu ajra almuḥsineena They said: Is it indeed thou who art Joseph ? He said: I am Joseph and this is my brother. Allah hath shown us favour. Lo! he who wardeth off (evil) and endureth (findeth favour); for lo! Allah loseth not the wages of the kindly.
091 qāloo taallāhi laqad āṯaraka allāhu ʿalaynā wa-in kunnā laḫati-eena They said: By Allah, verily Allah hath preferred thee above us, and we were indeed sinful.
092 qāla lā taṯreeba ʿalaykumu alyawma yaġfiru allāhu lakum wahuwa arḥamu alrrahimeena He said: Have no fear this day! May Allah forgive you, and He is the Most Merciful of those who show mercy.
093 iḏhaboo biqameeṣee haṯa faalqoohu ʿalā wajhi abee ya/ti baṣeeran wa/toonee bi-ahlikum ajmaʿeena Go with this shirt of mine and lay it on my father's face, he will become (again) a seer; and come to me with all your folk.
094 walammā faṣalati alʿeeru qāla aboohum innee laajidu reeḥa yoosufa lawlā an tufannidooni When the caravan departed their father had said: Truly I am conscious of the breath of Joseph, though ye call me dotard.
095 qāloo taallāhi innaka lafee ḍalālika alqadeemi (Those around him) said: By Allah, lo! thou art in thine old aberration.
096 falammā an jāa albašeeru alqāhu ʿalā wajhihi fairtadda baṣeeran qāla alam aqul lakum innee aʿlamu mina allāhi mā lā taʿlamoona Then, when the bearer of glad tidings came, he laid it on his face and he became a seer once more. He said: Said I not unto you that I know from Allah that which ye know not ?
097 qāloo yā abānā istaġfir lanā ḏunoobanā innā kunnā ḫati-eena They said: O our father! Ask forgiveness of our sins for us, for lo! we were sinful.
098 qāla sawfa astaġfiru lakum rabbee innahu huwa alġafooru alrraḥeemu He said: I shall ask forgiveness for you of my Lord. Lo! He is the Forgiving, the Merciful.
099 falammā daḫaloo ʿalā yoosufa āwā ilayhi abawayhi waqāla odḫuloo miṣra in šāa allāhu āmineena And when they came in before Joseph, he took his parents unto him, and said: Come into Egypt safe, if Allah will!
100 warafaʿa abawayhi ʿalā alʿarši waḫarroo lahu sujjadan waqāla yā abati haṯa ta/weelu ru/yāya min qablu qad jaʿalahā rabbee ḥaqqan waqad aḥsana bee iḏ aḫrajanee mina alssijni wajāa bikum mina albadwi min baʿdi an nazaġa alššaytanu baynee wabayna iḫwatee inna rabbee laṭeefun limā yašāo innahu huwa alʿaleemu alḥakeemu And he placed his parents on the dais and they fell down before him prostrate, and he said: O my father! This is the interpretation of my dream of old. My Lord hath made it true, and He hath shown me kindness, since He took me out of the prison and hath brought you from the desert after Satan had made strife between me and my brethren. Lo! my Lord is tender unto whom He will. He is the Knower, the Wise.
101 rabbi qad ātaytanee mina almulki waʿallamtanee min ta/weeli al-ahadeeṯi fatira alssamāwāti waal-arḍi anta waliyyee fee alddunyā waal-āḫirati tawaffanee musliman waalḥiqnee bialssaliḥeena O my Lord! Thou hast given me (something) of sovereignty and hast taught me (something) of the interpretation of events - Creator of the heavens and the earth! Thou art my Protecting Guardian in the world and the Hereafter. Make me to die muslim (unto Thee), and join me to the righteous.
102 [romanization missing] This is of the tidings of the Unseen which We inspire in thee (Muhammad). Thou wast not present with them when they fixed their plan and they were scheming.
103 wamā akṯaru alnnāsi walaw ḥaraṣta bimu/mineena And though thou try much, most men will not believe.
104 wamā tas-aluhum ʿalayhi min ajrin in huwa illā ḏikrun lilʿālameena Thou askest them no fee for it. It is naught else than a reminder unto the peoples.
105 wakaayyin min āyatin fee alssamāwāti waal-arḍi yamurroona ʿalayhā wahum ʿanhā muʿriḍoona How many a portent is there in the heavens and the earth which they pass by with face averted!
106 wamā yu/minu akṯaruhum biallāhi illā wahum mušrikoona And most of them believe not in Allah except that they attribute partners (unto Him).
107 afaaminoo an ta/tiyahum ġāšiyatun min ʿaṯabi allāhi aw ta/tiyahumu alssāʿatu baġtatan wahum lā yašʿuroona Deem they themselves secure from the coming on them of a pall of Allah's punishment, or the coming of the Hour suddenly while they are unaware ?
108 qul haṯihi sabeelee adʿoo ilā allāhi ʿalā baṣeeratin anā wamani ittabaʿanee wasubhana allāhi wamā anā mina almušrikeena Say: This is my Way: I call on Allah with sure knowledge. I and whosoever followeth me - Glory be to Allah! - and I am not of the idolaters.
109 wamā arsalnā min qablika illā rijālan nooḥee ilayhim min ahli alqurā afalam yaseeroo fee al-arḍi fayanḏuroo kayfa kāna ʿāqibatu allāḏeena min qablihim waladāru al-āḫirati ḫayrun lillaḏeena ittaqaw afalā taʿqiloona We sent not before thee (any messengers) save men whom We inspired from among the folk of the townships - Have they not travelled in the land and seen the nature of the consequence for those who were before them ? And verily the abode of the Hereafter, for those who ward off (evil), is best. Have ye then no sense ? -
110 [romanization missing] Till, when the messengers despaired and thought that they were denied, then came unto them Our help, and whom We would was saved. And Our wrath cannot be warded from the guilty.
