26. ash-Shu`ara': The Poets

1 
ta-seen-meem
ta-seen-meem
ta-seen-meem
Ta. Sin. Mim.

2 
tilka āyātu alkitābi almubeeni
These are revelations of the Scripture that maketh plain.

3 
laʿallaka bāḫiʿun nafsaka allā yakoonoo mu/mineena
It may be that thou tormentest thyself (O Muhammad) because they believe not.

4 
in naša/ nunazzil ʿalayhim mina alssamā-i āyatan faḏallat aʿnāquhum lahā ḫadiʿeena
If We will, We can send down on them from the sky a portent so that their necks would remain bowed before it.

5 
wamā ya/teehim min ḏikrin mina alrraḥmāni muḥdaṯin illā kānoo ʿanhu muʿriḍeena
Never cometh there unto them a fresh reminder from the Beneficent One, but they turn away from it.

6 
faqad kaṯṯaboo fasaya/teehim anbāo mā kānoo bihi yastahzi-oona
Now they have denied (the Truth); but there will come unto them tidings of that whereat they used to scoff.

7 
awa lam yaraw ilā al-arḍi kam anbatnā feehā min kulli zawjin kareemin
Have they not seen the earth, how much of every fruitful kind We make to grow therein ?

8 
inna fee ṯalika laāyatan wamā kāna akṯaruhum mu/mineena
Lo! herein is indeed a portent; yet most of them are not believers.

9 
wa-inna rabbaka lahuwa alʿazeezu alrraḥeemu
And lo! thy Lord! He is indeed the Mighty, the Merciful.

10 
wa-iḏ nādā rabbuka moosā ani i/ti alqawma alṯṯālimeena
And when thy Lord called Moses, saying: Go unto the wrongdoing folk,

11 
qawma firʿawna alā yattaqoona
The folk of Pharaoh. Will they not ward off (evil) ?

12 
qāla rabbi innee aḫāfu an yukaṯṯibooni
He said: My Lord! Lo! I fear that they will deny me,

13 
wayaḍeequ ṣadree walā yanṭaliqu lisānee faarsil ilā hāroona
And I shall be embarrassed, and my tongue will not speak plainly, therefor send for Aaron (to help me).

14 
walahum ʿalayya ḏanbun faaḫāfu an yaqtulooni
And they have a crime against me, so I fear that they will kill me.

15 
qāla kallā faiḏhabā bi-āyātinā innā maʿakum mustamiʿoona
He said: Nay, verily. So go ye twain with Our tokens. Lo! We shall be with you, Hearing.

16 
fa/tiyā firʿawna faqoolā innā rasoolu rabbi alʿālameena
And come together unto Pharaoh and say: Lo! we bear a message of the Lord of the Worlds,

17 
an arsil maʿanā banee isrā-eela
(Saying): Let the Children of Israel go with us.

18 
qāla alam nurabbika feenā waleedan walabiṯta feenā min ʿumurika sineena
(Pharaoh) said (unto Moses): Did we not rear thee among us as a child ? And thou didst dwell many years of thy life among us,

19 
wafaʿalta faʿlataka allātee faʿalta waanta mina alkāfireena
And thou didst that thy deed which thou didst, and thou wast one of the ingrates.

20 
qāla faʿaltuhā iḏan waanā mina alddalleena
He said: I did it then, when I was of those who are astray.

21 
fafarartu minkum lammā ḫiftukum fawahaba lee rabbee ḥukman wajaʿalanee mina almursaleena
Then I fled from you when I feared you, and my Lord vouchsafed me a command and appointed me (of the number) of those sent (by Him).

22 
watilka niʿmatun tamunnuhā ʿalayya an ʿabbadta banee isrā-eela
And this is the past favour wherewith thou reproachest me: that thou hast enslaved the Children of Israel.

23 
qāla firʿawnu wamā rabbu alʿālameena
Pharaoh said: And what is the Lord of the Worlds ?

24 
qāla rabbu alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wamā baynahumā in kuntum mooqineena
(Moses) said: Lord of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them, if ye had but sure belief.

25 
qāla liman ḥawlahu alā tastamiʿoona
(Pharaoh) said unto those around him: Hear ye not ?

26 
qāla rabbukum warabbu ābā-ikumu al-awwaleena
He said: Your Lord and the Lord of your fathers.

27 
qāla inna rasoolakumu allāḏee orsila ilaykum lamajnoonun
(Pharaoh) said: Lo! your messenger who hath been sent unto you is indeed a madman!

28 
qāla rabbu almašriqi waalmaġribi wamā baynahumā in kuntum taʿqiloona
He said: Lord of the East and the West and all that is between them, if ye did but understand.

29 
qāla la-ini ittaḫaḏta ilāhan ġayree laajʿalannaka mina almasjooneena
(Pharaoh) said: If thou choosest a god other than me, I assuredly shall place thee among the prisoners.

30

He said: Even though I show thee something plain ?

31 
qāla fa/ti bihi in kunta mina alssadiqeena
(Pharaoh) said: Produce it then, if thou art of the truthful!

32

Then he flung down his staff and it became a serpent manifest,

33

And he drew forth his hand and lo! it was white to the beholders.

34 
qāla lilmala-i ḥawlahu inna haṯa lasahirun ʿaleemun
(Pharaoh) said unto the chiefs about him: Lo! this is verily a knowing wizard,

35

Who would drive you out of your land by his magic. Now what counsel ye ?

36 
qāloo arjih waaḫāhu waibʿaṯ fee almadā-ini hašireena
They said: Put him off, (him) and his brother, and send into the cities summoners

37 
ya/tooka bikulli sahharin ʿaleemin
Who shall bring unto thee every knowing wizard.

38

So the wizards were gathered together at a set time on a day appointed.

39

And it was said unto the people: Are ye (also) gathering ?

40

(They said): Aye, so that we may follow the wizards if they are the winners.

41

And when the wizards came they said unto Pharaoh: Will there surely be a reward for us if we are the winners ?

42

He said: Aye, and ye will then surely be of those brought near (to me).

43

Moses said unto them: Throw what ye are going to throw!

44

Then they threw down their cords and their staves and said: By Pharaoh's might, lo! we verily are the winners.

45 
faalqā moosā ʿasahu fa-iṯa hiya talqafu mā ya/fikoona
Then Moses threw his staff and lo! it swallowed that which they did falsely show.

46 
faolqiya alssaḥaratu sājideena
And the wizards were flung prostrate,

47 
qāloo āmannā birabbi alʿālameena
Crying: We believe in the Lord of the Worlds,

48 
rabbi moosā wahāroona
The Lord of Moses and Aaron.

49

(Pharaoh) said: Ye put your faith in him before I give you leave. Lo! he doubtless is your chief who taught you magic! But verily ye shall come to know. Verily I will cut off your hands and your feet alternately, and verily I will crucify you every one.

50 
qāloo lā ḍayra innā ilā rabbinā munqaliboona
They said: It is no hurt, for lo! unto our Lord we shall return.

51

Lo! we ardently hope that our Lord will forgive us our sins because we are the first of the believers.

52

And We inspired Moses, saying: Take away My slaves by night, for ye will be pursued.

53

Then Pharaoh sent into the cities summoners,

54

(Who said): Lo! these indeed are but a little troop,

55

And lo! they are offenders against us.

56

And We rained on them a rain. And dreadful is the rain of those who have been warned.

174 
inna fee ṯalika laāyatan wamā kāna akṯaruhum mu/mineena
Lo! herein is indeed a portent, yet most of them are not believers.

175 
wa-inna rabbaka lahuwa alʿazeezu alrraḥeemu
And lo! thy Lord, He is indeed the Mighty, the Merciful.

176 
kaṯṯaba aṣ-habu al-aykati almursaleena
The dwellers in the wood (of Midian) denied the messengers (of Allah),

177 
iḏ qāla lahum šuʿaybun alā tattaqoona
When Shu'eyb said unto them: Will ye not ward off (evil) ?

178 
innee lakum rasoolun ameenun
Lo! I am a faithful messenger unto you,

179 
faittaqoo allāha waaṭeeʿooni
So keep your duty to Allah and obey me.

180 
wamā as-alukum ʿalayhi min ajrin in ajriya illā ʿalā rabbi alʿālameena
And I ask of you no wage for it; my wage is the concern only of the Lord of the Worlds.

181 
awfoo alkayla walā takoonoo mina almuḫsireena
Give full measure, and be not of those who give less (than the due).

182 
wazinoo bialqistasi almustaqeemi
And weigh with the true balance.

183 
walā tabḫasoo alnnāsa ašyāahum walā taʿṯaw fee al-arḍi mufsideena
Wrong not mankind in their goods, and do not evil, making mischief, in the earth.

184 
waittaqoo allāḏee ḫalaqakum waaljibillata al-awwaleena
And keep your duty unto Him Who created you and the generations of the men of old.

185 
qāloo innamā anta mina almusahhareena
They said: Thou art but one of the bewitched;

186 
wamā anta illā bašarun miṯlunā wa-in naḏunnuka lamina alkaṯibeena
Thou art but a mortal like us, and lo! we deem thee of the liars.

187 
faasqiṭ ʿalaynā kisafan mina alssamā-i in kunta mina alssadiqeena
Then make fragments of the heaven fall upon us, if thou art of the truthful.

188 
qāla rabbee aʿlamu bimā taʿmaloona
He said: My Lord is Best Aware of what ye do.

189 
fakaṯṯaboohu faaḫaḏahum ʿaṯabu yawmi alṯṯullati innahu kāna ʿaṯaba yawmin ʿaḏeemin
But they denied him, so there came on them the retribution of the day of gloom. Lo! it was the retribution of an awful day.

190 
inna fee ṯalika laāyatan wamā kāna akṯaruhum mu/mineena
Lo! herein is indeed a portent; yet most of them are not believers.

191 
wa-inna rabbaka lahuwa alʿazeezu alrraḥeemu
And lo! thy Lord! He is indeed the Mighty, the Merciful.

192 
wa-innahu latanzeelu rabbi alʿālameena
And lo! it is a revelation of the Lord of the Worlds,

193 
nazala bihi alrrooḥu al-ameenu
Which the True Spirit hath brought down

194 
ʿalā qalbika litakoona mina almunḏireena
Upon thy heart, that thou mayst be (one) of the warners,

195 
bilisānin ʿarabiyyin mubeenin
In plain Arabic speech.

196 
wa-innahu lafee zuburi al-awwaleena
And lo! it is in the Scriptures of the men of old.

197 
awa lam yakun lahum āyatan an yaʿlamahu ʿulamāo banee isrā-eela
Is it not a token for them that the doctors of the Children of Israel know it ?

198 
walaw nazzalnāhu ʿalā baʿḍi al-aʿjameena
And if We had revealed it unto one of any other nation than the Arabs,

199 
faqaraahu ʿalayhim mā kānoo bihi mu/mineena
And he had read it unto them, they would not have believed in it.

200 
kaṯalika salaknāhu fee quloobi almujrimeena
Thus do We make it traverse the hearts of the guilty.

201 
lā yu/minoona bihi ḥattā yarawoo alʿaṯaba al-aleema
They will not believe in it till they behold the painful doom,

202 
faya/tiyahum baġtatan wahum lā yašʿuroona
So that it will come upon them suddenly, when they perceive not.

203 
fayaqooloo hal naḥnu munḏaroona
Then they will say: Are we to be reprieved ?

204 
afabiʿaṯabinā yastaʿjiloona
Would they (now) hasten on Our doom ?

205 
afaraayta in mattaʿnāhum sineena
Hast thou then seen, if We content them for (long) years,

206 
thumma jāahum mā kānoo yooʿadoona
And then cometh that which they were promised,

207 
mā aġnā ʿanhum mā kānoo yumattaʿoona
(How) that wherewith they were contented naught availeth them ?

208 
wamā ahlaknā min qaryatin illā lahā munḏiroona
And We destroyed no township but it had its warners

209 
thikra wama kunna thalimeena
For reminder, for We never were oppressors.

210 
wamā tanazzalat bihi alššayateenu
The devils did not bring it down.

211 
wamā yanbaġee lahum wamā yastaṭeeʿoona
It is not meet for them, nor is it in their power,

212 
innahum ʿani alssamʿi lamaʿzooloona
Lo! verily they are banished from the hearing.

213 
falā tadʿu maʿa allāhi ilāhan āḫara fatakoona mina almuʿaṯṯabeena
Therefor invoke not with Allah another god, lest thou be one of the doomed.

214 
waanḏir ʿašeerataka al-aqrabeena
And warn thy tribe of near kindred,

215 
waiḫfiḍ janahaka limani ittabaʿaka mina almu/mineena
And lower thy wing (in kindness) unto those believers who follow thee.

216 
fa-in ʿaṣawka faqul innee baree-on mimmā taʿmaloona
And if they (thy kinsfolk) disobey thee, say: Lo! I am innocent of what they do.

217 
watawakkal ʿalā alʿazeezi alrraḥeemi
And put thy trust in the Mighty, the Merciful.

218 
allāḏee yarāka ḥeena taqoomu
Who seeth thee when thou standest up (to pray)

219 
wataqallubaka fee alssājideena
And (seeth) thine abasement among those who fall prostrate (in worship).

220 
innahu huwa alssameeʿu alʿaleemu
Lo! He, only He, is the Hearer, the Knower.

221 
hal onabbi-okum ʿalā man tanazzalu alššayateenu
Shall I inform you upon whom the devils descend ?

222

They descend on every sinful, false one.

223 
yulqoona alssamʿa waakṯaruhum kaṯiboona
They listen eagerly, but most of them are liars.

224 
waalššuʿarāo yattabiʿuhumu alġāwoona
As for poets, the erring follow them.

225 
alam tara annahum fee kulli wādin yaheemoona
Hast thou not seen how they stray in every valley,

226 
waannahum yaqooloona mā lā yafʿaloona
And how they say that which they do not ?

227 
illā allāḏeena āmanoo waʿamiloo alssalihati waḏakaroo allāha kaṯeeran waintaṣaroo min baʿdi mā ḏulimoo wasayaʿlamu allāḏeena ḏalamoo ayya munqalabin yanqaliboona
Save those who believe and do good works, and remember Allah much, and vindicate themselves after they have been wronged. Those who do wrong will come to know by what a (great) reverse they will be overturned!
