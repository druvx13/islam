001 [romanization missing] Ta. Sin. Mim.
002 tilka āyātu alkitābi almubeeni These are revelations of the Scripture that maketh plain.
003 laʿallaka bāḫiʿun nafsaka allā yakoonoo mu/mineena It may be that thou tormentest thyself (O Muhammad) because they believe not.

005 wamā ya/teehim min ḏikrin mina alrraḥmāni muḥdaṯin illā kānoo ʿanhu muʿriḍeena Never cometh there unto them a fresh reminder from the Beneficent One, but they turn away from it.

008 inna fee ṯalika laāyatan wamā kāna akṯaruhum mu/mineena Lo! herein is indeed a portent; yet most of them are not believers.
009 wa-inna rabbaka lahuwa alʿazeezu alrraḥeemu And lo! thy Lord! He is indeed the Mighty, the Merciful.
010 wa-iḏ nādā rabbuka moosā ani i/ti alqawma alṯṯālimeena And when thy Lord called Moses, saying: Go unto the wrongdoing folk,

018 qāla alam nurabbika feenā waleedan walabiṯta feenā min ʿumurika sineena (Pharaoh) said (unto Moses): Did we not rear thee among us as a child ? And thou didst dwell many years of thy life among us,
019 wafaʿalta faʿlataka allātee faʿalta waanta mina alkāfireena And thou didst that thy deed which thou didst, and thou wast one of the ingrates.
020 qāla faʿaltuhā iḏan waanā mina alddalleena He said: I did it then, when I was of those who are astray.
021 fafarartu minkum lammā ḫiftukum fawahaba lee rabbee ḥukman wajaʿalanee mina almursaleena Then I fled from you when I feared you, and my Lord vouchsafed me a command and appointed me (of the number) of those sent (by Him).
022 watilka niʿmatun tamunnuhā ʿalayya an ʿabbadta banee isrā-eela And this is the past favour wherewith thou reproachest me: that thou hast enslaved the Children of Israel.
023 qāla firʿawnu wamā rabbu alʿālameena Pharaoh said: And what is the Lord of the Worlds ?
024 qāla rabbu alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wamā baynahumā in kuntum mooqineena (Moses) said: Lord of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them, if ye had but sure belief.
025 qāla liman ḥawlahu alā tastamiʿoona (Pharaoh) said unto those around him: Hear ye not ?
026 qāla rabbukum warabbu ābā-ikumu al-awwaleena He said: Your Lord and the Lord of your fathers.
027 qāla inna rasoolakumu allāḏee orsila ilaykum lamajnoonun (Pharaoh) said: Lo! your messenger who hath been sent unto you is indeed a madman!
028 qāla rabbu almašriqi waalmaġribi wamā baynahumā in kuntum taʿqiloona He said: Lord of the East and the West and all that is between them, if ye did but understand.

Lo! he doubtless is your chief who taught you magic! But verily ye shall come to know. Verily I will cut off your hands and your feet alternately, and verily I will crucify you every one.
