20. Ta Ha: Ta Ha

001 [romanization missing] Ta. Ha.
002 mā anzalnā ʿalayka alqur-āna litašqā We have not revealed unto thee (Muhammad) this Qur'an that thou shouldst be distressed,
003 illā taḏkiratan liman yaḫšā But as a reminder unto him who feareth,
004 tanzeelan mimman ḫalaqa al-arḍa waalssamāwāti alʿulā A revelation from Him Who created the earth and the high heavens,
005 alrraḥmānu ʿalā alʿarši istawā The Beneficent One, Who is established on the Throne.
006 lahu mā fee alssamāwāti wamā fee al-arḍi wamā baynahumā wamā taḥta alṯṯarā Unto Him belongeth whatsoever is in the heavens and whatsoever is in the earth, and whatsoever is between them, and whatsoever is beneath the sod.
007 wa-in tajhar bialqawli fa-innahu yaʿlamu alssirra waaḫfā And if thou speakest aloud, then lo! He knoweth the secret (thought) and (that which is yet) more hidden.
008 allāhu lā ilāha illā huwa lahu al-asmāo alḥusnā Allah! There is no God save Him. His are the most beautiful names.
009 wahal atāka ḥadeeṯu moosā Hath there come unto thee the story of Moses ?
010 iḏ raā nāran faqāla li-ahlihi omkuṯoo innee ānastu nāran laʿallee āteekum minhā biqabasin aw ajidu ʿalā alnnāri hudān When he saw a fire and said unto his folk: Lo! Wait! I see a fire afar off. Peradventure I may bring you a brand therefrom or may find guidance at the fire.
011 falammā atāhā noodiya yā moosā And when he reached it, he was called by name: O Moses!
012 innee anā rabbuka faiḫlaʿ naʿlayka innaka bialwādi almuqaddasi ṭuwān Lo! I, even I, am thy Lord, So take off thy shoes, for lo! thou art in the holy valley of Tuwa.
013 waanā iḫtartuka faistamiʿ limā yooha And I have chosen thee, so hearken unto that which is inspired.
014 innanee anā allāhu lā ilāha illā anā faoʿbudnee waaqimi alṣalāta liḏikree Lo! I, even I, am Allah, There is no God save Me. So serve Me and establish worship for My remembrance.
015 inna alssāʿata ātiyatun akādu oḫfeehā litujzā kullu nafsin bimā tasʿā Lo! the Hour is surely coming. But I will to keep it hidden, that every soul may be rewarded for that which it striveth (to achieve).
016 falā yaṣuddannaka ʿanhā man lā yu/minu bihā waittabaʿa hawāhu fatardā Therefor, let not him turn thee aside from (the thought of) it who believeth not therein but followeth his own desire, lest thou perish.
017 wamā tilka biyameenika yā moosā And what is that in thy right hand, O Moses ?
018 qāla hiya ʿasaya atawakkao ʿalayhā waahuššu bihā ʿalā ġanamee waliya feehā maāribu oḫrā He said: This is my staff whereon I lean, and wherewith I bear down branches for my sheep, and wherein I find other uses.
019 qāla alqihā yā moosā He said: Cast it down, O Moses!
020 faalqāhā fa-iṯa hiya ḥayyatun tasʿā So he cast it down, and lo! it was a serpent, gliding.
021 qāla ḫuḏhā walā taḫaf sanuʿeeduhā seeratahā al-oolā He said: Grasp it and fear not. We shall return it to its former state.
022 waoḍmum yadaka ilā janahika taḫruj baydaa min ġayri soo-in āyatan oḫrā And thrust thy hand within thine armpit, it will come forth white without hurt. (That will be) another token.
023 linuriyaka min āyātinā alkubrā That We may show thee (some) of Our greater portents,
024 iḏhab ilā firʿawna innahu ṭaġā Go thou unto Pharaoh! Lo! he hath transgressed (the bounds).
025 qāla rabbi išraḥ lee ṣadree (Moses) said: My Lord! relieve my mind
026 wayassir lee amree And ease my task for me;
027 waoḥlul ʿuqdatan min lisānee And loose a knot from my tongue,
028 yafqahoo qawlee That they may understand my saying.
029 waijʿal lee wazeeran min ahlee Appoint for me a henchman from my folk,
030 hāroona aḫee Aaron, my brother.
031 ošdud bihi azree Confirm my strength with him
032 waašrik-hu fee amree And let him share my task,
033 kay nusabbiḥaka kaṯeerān That we may glorify Thee much
034 wanaḏkuraka kaṯeerān And much remember Thee.
035 innaka kunta binā baṣeerān Lo! Thou art ever Seeing us.
036 qāla qad ooteeta su/laka yā moosā He said: Thou art granted thy request, O Moses.
037 walaqad manannā ʿalayka marratan oḫrā And indeed, another time, already We have shown thee favour,
038 iḏ awḥaynā ilā ommika mā yooha When we inspired in thy mother that which is inspired,
039 ani iqḏifeehi fee alttābooti faiqḏifeehi fee alyammi falyulqihi alyammu bialssahili ya/ḫuḏhu ʿaduwwun lee waʿaduwwun lahu waalqaytu ʿalayka maḥabbatan minnee walituṣnaʿa ʿalā ʿaynee Saying: Throw him into the ark, and throw it into the river, then the river shall throw it on to the bank, and there an enemy to Me and an enemy to him shall take him. And I endued thee with love from Me that thou mightest be trained according to My will,
040 iḏ tamšee oḫtuka fataqoolu hal adullukum ʿalā man yakfuluhu farajaʿnāka ilā ommika kay taqarra ʿaynuhā walā taḥzana waqatalta nafsan fanajjaynāka mina alġammi wafatannāka futoonan falabiṯta sineena fee ahli madyana ṯumma ji/ta ʿalā qadarin yā moosā When thy sister went and said: Shall I show you one who will nurse him ? and we restored thee to thy mother that her eyes might be refreshed and might not sorrow. And thou didst kill a man and We delivered thee from great distress, and tried thee with a heavy trial. And thou didst tarry years among the folk of Midian. Then camest thou (hither) by (My) providence, O Moses,
041 waistanaʿtuka linafsee And I have attached thee to Myself.
042 iḏhab anta waaḫooka bi-āyātee walā taniyā fee ḏikree Go, thou and thy brother, with My tokens, and be not faint in remembrance of Me.
043 iḏhabā ilā firʿawna innahu ṭaġā Go, both of you, unto Pharaoh. Lo! he hath transgressed (the bounds).
044 faqoolā lahu qawlan layyinan laʿallahu yataḏakkaru aw yaḫšā And speak unto him a gentle word, that peradventure he may heed or fear.
045 qālā rabbanā innanā naḫāfu an yafruṭa ʿalaynā aw an yaṭġā They said: Our Lord! Lo! we fear that he may be beforehand with us or that he may play the tyrant.
046 qāla lā taḫāfā innanee maʿakumā asmaʿu waarā He said: Fear not. Lo! I am with you twain, Hearing and Seeing.
047 fa/tiyāhu faqoolā innā rasoolā rabbika faarsil maʿanā banee isrā-eela walā tuʿaṯṯibhum qad ji/nāka bi-āyatin min rabbika waalssalāmu ʿalā mani ittabaʿa alhudā So go ye unto him and say: Lo! we are two messengers of thy Lord. So let the children of Israel go with us, and torment them not. We bring thee a token from thy Lord. And peace will be for him who followeth right guidance.
048 innā qad ooḥiya ilaynā anna alʿaṯaba ʿalā man kaṯṯaba watawallā Lo! it hath been revealed unto us that the doom will be for him who denieth and turneth away.
049 qāla faman rabbukumā yā moosā (Pharaoh) said: Who then is the Lord of you twain, O Moses ?
050 qāla rabbunā allāḏee aʿta kulla šay-in ḫalqahu ṯumma hadā He said: Our Lord is He Who gave unto everything its nature, then guided it aright.
051 qāla famā bālu alqurooni al-oolā He said: What then is the state of the generations of old ?
052 qāla ʿilmuhā ʿinda rabbee fee kitābin lā yaḍillu rabbee walā yansā He said: The knowledge thereof is with my Lord in a Record. My Lord neither erreth nor forgetteth,
053 allāḏee jaʿala lakumu al-arḍa mahdan wasalaka lakum feehā subulan waanzala mina alssamā-i māan faaḫrajnā bihi azwājan min nabātin šattā Who hath appointed the earth as a bed and hath threaded roads for you therein and hath sent down water from the sky and thereby We have brought forth divers kinds of vegetation,
054 kuloo wairʿaw anʿāmakum inna fee ṯalika laāyātin li-olee alnnuhā (Saying): Eat ye and feed your cattle. Lo! herein verily are portents for men of thought.
055 minhā ḫalaqnākum wafeehā nuʿeedukum waminhā nuḫrijukum tāratan oḫrā Thereof We created you, and thereunto We return you, and thence We bring you forth a second time.
056 walaqad araynāhu āyātinā kullahā fakaṯṯaba waabā And We verily did show him all Our tokens, but he denied them and refused.
057 qāla aji/tanā lituḫrijanā min arḍinā bisiḥrika yā moosā He said: Hast come to drive us out from our land by thy magic, O Moses ?
058 falana/tiyannaka bisiḥrin miṯlihi faijʿal baynanā wabaynaka mawʿidan lā nuḫlifuhu naḥnu walā anta makānan suwān But we surely can produce for thee magic the like thereof; so appoint a tryst between us and you, which neither we nor thou shall fail to keep, at a place convenient (to us both).
059 qāla mawʿidukum yawmu alzzeenati waan yuḥšara alnnāsu ḍuhan (Moses) said: Your tryst shall be the day of the feast, and let the people assemble when the sun hath risen high.
060 fatawallā firʿawnu fajamaʿa kaydahu ṯumma atā Then Pharaoh went and gathered his strength, then came (to the appointed tryst).
061 qāla lahum moosā waylakum lā taftaroo ʿalā allāhi kaḏiban fayusḥitakum biʿaṯabin waqad ḫāba mani iftarā Moses said unto them: Woe unto you! Invent not a lie against Allah, lest He extirpate you by some punishment. He who lieth faileth miserably.
062 fatanāzaʿoo amrahum baynahum waasarroo alnnajwā Then they debated one with another what they must do, and they kept their counsel secret.
063 qāloo in haṯani lasahirāni yureedāni an yuḫrijākum min arḍikum bisiḥrihimā wayaḏhabā biṭareeqatikumu almuṯlā They said: Lo! these are two wizards who would drive you out from your country by their magic, and destroy your best traditions;
064 faajmiʿoo kaydakum ṯumma i/too ṣaffan waqad aflaḥa alyawma mani istaʿlā So arrange your plan, and come in battle line. Whoso is uppermost this day will be indeed successful.
065 qāloo yā moosā immā an tulqiya wa-immā an nakoona awwala man alqā They said: O Moses! Either throw first, or let us be the first to throw ?
066 qāla bal alqoo fa-iṯa ḥibāluhum waʿiṣiyyuhum yuḫayyalu ilayhi min siḥrihim annahā tasʿā He said: Nay, do ye throw! Then lo! their cords and their staves, by their magic, appeared to him as though they ran.
067 faawjasa fee nafsihi ḫeefatan moosā And Moses conceived a fear in his mind.
068 qulnā lā taḫaf innaka anta al-aʿlā We said: Fear not! Lo! thou art the higher.
069 waalqi mā fee yameenika talqaf mā ṣanaʿoo innamā ṣanaʿoo kaydu sahirin walā yufliḥu alssahiru ḥayṯu atā Throw that which is in thy right hand! It will eat up that which they have made. Lo! that which they have made is but a wizard's artifice, and a wizard shall not be successful to whatever point (of skill) he may attain.
070 faolqiya alssaḥaratu sujjadan qāloo āmannā birabbi hāroona wamoosā Then the wizards were (all) flung down prostrate, crying: We believe in the Lord of Aaron and Moses.
071 qāla āmantum lahu qabla an aṯana lakum innahu lakabeerukumu allāḏee ʿallamakumu alssiḥra falaoqattiʿanna aydiyakum waarjulakum min ḫilāfin walaoṣallibannakum fee juḏooʿi alnnaḫli walataʿlamunna ayyunā ašaddu ʿaṯaban waabqā (Pharaoh) said: Ye put faith in him before I give you leave. Lo! he is your chief who taught you magic. Now surely I shall cut off your hands and your feet alternately, and I shall crucify you on the trunks of palm trees, and ye shall know for certain which of us hath sterner and more lasting punishment.
072 qāloo lan nu/ṯiraka ʿalā mā jāanā mina albayyināti waallaḏee faṭaranā faiqḍi mā anta qadin innamā taqḍee haṯihi alḥayāta alddunyā They said: We choose thee not above the clear proofs that have come unto us, and above Him Who created us. So decree what thou wilt decree. Thou wilt end for us only this life of the world.
073 innā āmannā birabbinā liyaġfira lanā ḫatayānā wamā akrahtanā ʿalayhi mina alssiḥri waallāhu ḫayrun waabqā Lo! we believe in our Lord, that He may forgive us our sins and the magic unto which thou didst force us. Allah is better and more lasting.
074 innahu man ya/ti rabbahu mujriman fa-inna lahu jahannama lā yamootu feehā walā yaḥyā Lo! whoso cometh guilty unto his Lord, verily for him is hell. There he will neither die nor live.
075 waman ya/tihi mu/minan qad ʿamila alssalihati faolā-ika lahumu alddarajātu alʿulā But whoso cometh unto Him a believer, having done good works, for such are the high stations;
076 jannātu ʿādnin tajree min taḥtihā al-anhāru ḫālideena feehā waṯalika jazāo man tazakkā Gardens of Eden underneath which rivers flow, wherein they will abide for ever. That is the reward of him who groweth.
077 walaqad awḥaynā ilā moosā an asri biʿibādee faiḍrib lahum ṭareeqan fee albaḥri yabasan lā taḫāfu darakan walā taḫšā And verily We inspired Moses, saying: Take away My slaves by night and strike for them a dry path in the sea, fearing not to be overtaken, neither being afraid (of the sea).
078 faatbaʿahum firʿawnu bijunoodihi faġašiyahum mina alyammi mā ġašiyahum Then Pharaoh followed them with his hosts and there covered them that which did cover them of the sea.
079 waaḍalla firʿawnu qawmahu wamā hadā And Pharaoh led his folk astray, he did not guide them.
080 yā banee isrā-eela qad anjaynākum min ʿaduwwikum wawāʿadnākum jāniba alttoori al-aymana wanazzalnā ʿalaykumu almanna waalssalwā O Children of Israel! We delivered you from your enemy, and we made a covenant with you on the holy mountain's side, and sent down on you the manna and the quails,
081 kuloo min ṭayyibāti mā razaqnākum walā taṭġaw feehi fayaḥilla ʿalaykum ġaḍabee waman yaḥlil ʿalayhi ġaḍabee faqad hawā (Saying): Eat of the good things wherewith We have provided you, and transgress not in respect thereof lest My wrath come upon you: and he on whom My wrath cometh, he is lost indeed.
082 wa-innee laġaffārun liman tāba waāmana waʿamila saliḥan ṯumma ihtadā And lo! verily I am Forgiving toward him who repenteth and believeth and doeth good, and afterward walketh aright.
083 wamā aʿjalaka ʿan qawmika yā moosā And (it was said): What hath made thee hasten from thy folk, O Moses ?
084 qāla hum olā-i ʿalā aṯaree waʿajiltu ilayka rabbi litarda He said: They are close upon my track. I hastened unto Thee, my Lord, that Thou mightest be well pleased.
085 qāla fa-innā qad fatannā qawmaka min baʿdika waaḍallahumu alssāmiriyyu He said: Lo! We have tried thy folk in thine absence, and As-Samiri hath misled them.
086 farajaʿa moosā ilā qawmihi ġaḍbāna asifan qāla yā qawmi alam yaʿidkum rabbukum waʿdan ḥasanan afatala ʿalaykumu alʿahdu am aradtum an yaḥilla ʿalaykum ġaḍabun min rabbikum faaḫlaftum mawʿidee Then Moses went back unto his folk, angry and sad. He said: O my people! Hath not your Lord promised you a fair promise ? Did the time appointed then appear too long for you, or did ye wish that wrath from your Lord should come upon you, that ye broke tryst with me ?
087 qāloo mā aḫlafnā mawʿidaka bimalkinā walākinnā ḥummilnā awzāran min zeenati alqawmi faqaḏafnāhā fakaṯalika alqā alssāmiriyyu They said: We broke not tryst with thee of our own will, but we were laden with burdens of ornaments of the folk, then cast them (in the fire), for thus As-Samiri proposed.
088 faaḫraja lahum ʿijlan jasadan lahu ḫuwārun faqāloo haṯa ilāhukum wa-ilāhu moosā fanasiya Then he produced for them a calf, of saffron hue, which gave forth a lowing sound. And they cried: This is your god and the god of Moses, but he hath forgotten.
089 afalā yarawna allā yarjiʿu ilayhim qawlan walā yamliku lahum ḍarran walā nafʿān See they not, then, that it returneth no saying unto them and possesseth for them neither hurt nor use ?
090 walaqad qāla lahum hāroonu min qablu yā qawmi innamā futintum bihi wa-inna rabbakumu alrraḥmānu faittabiʿoonee waaṭeeʿoo amree And Aaron indeed had told them beforehand: O my people! Ye are but being seduced therewith, for lo! your Lord is the Beneficent, so follow me and obey my order.
091 qāloo lan nabraḥa ʿalayhi ʿākifeena ḥattā yarjiʿa ilaynā moosā They said: We shall by no means cease to be its votaries till Moses return unto us.
092 qāla yā hāroonu mā manaʿaka iḏ raaytahum ḍalloo He (Moses) said: O Aaron! What held thee back when thou didst see them gone astray,
093 allā tattabiʿani afaʿaṣayta amree That thou followedst me not ? Hast thou then disobeyed my order ?
094 qāla yabnaomma lā ta/ḫuḏ biliḥyatee walā bira/see innee ḫašeetu an taqoola farraqta bayna banee isrā-eela walam tarqub qawlee He said: O son of my mother! Clutch not my beard nor my head! I feared lest thou shouldst say: Thou hast caused division among the Children of Israel, and hast not waited for my word.
095 qāla famā ḫaṭbuka yā sāmiriyyu (Moses) said: And what hast thou to say, O Samiri ?
096 qāla baṣurtu bimā lam yabṣuroo bihi faqabaḍtu qabḍatan min aṯari alrrasooli fanabaḏtuhā wakaṯalika sawwalat lee nafsee He said: I perceived what they perceive not, so I seized a handful from the footsteps of the messenger, and then threw it in. Thus my soul commended to me.
097 qāla faiḏhab fa-inna laka fee alḥayāti an taqoola lā misāsa wa-inna laka mawʿidan lan tuḫlafahu waonḏur ilā ilāhika allāḏee ḏalta ʿalayhi ʿākifan lanuḥarriqannahu ṯumma lanansifannahu fee alyammi nasfān (Moses) said: Then go! and lo! in this life it is for thee to say: Touch me not! and lo! there is for thee a tryst thou canst not break. Now look upon thy god of which thou hast remained a votary. Verily we will burn it and will scatter its dust over the sea.
098 innamā ilāhukumu allāhu allāḏee lā ilāha illā huwa wasiʿa kulla šay-in ʿilmān Your God is only Allah, than Whom there is no other God. He embraceth all things in His knowledge.
099 kaṯalika naquṣu ʿalayka min anbā-i mā qad sabaqa waqad ātaynāka min ladunnā ṯikran Thus relate We unto thee (Muhammad) some tidings of that which happened of old, and We have given thee from Our presence a reminder.
100 man aʿraḍa ʿanhu fa-innahu yaḥmilu yawma alqiyāmati wizrān Whoso turneth away from it, he verily will bear a burden on the Day of Resurrection,
101 khālideena feehi wasāa lahum yawma alqiyāmati ḥimlān Abiding under it - an evil burden for them on the Day of Resurrection,
102 yawma yunfaḫu fee alṣoori wanaḥšuru almujrimeena yawma-iḏin zurqān The day when the Trumpet is blown. On that day we assemble the guilty white-eyed (with terror),
103 yataḫāfatoona baynahum in labiṯtum illā ʿašrān Murmuring among themselves: Ye have tarried but ten (days).
104 naḥnu aʿlamu bimā yaqooloona iḏ yaqoolu amṯaluhum ṭareeqatan in labiṯtum illā yawmān We are Best Aware of what they utter when their best in conduct say: Ye have tarried but a day.
105 wayas-aloonaka ʿani aljibāli faqul yansifuhā rabbee nasfān They will ask thee of the mountains (on that day). Say: My Lord will break them into scattered dust.
106 fayaḏaruhā qāʿan ṣafṣafān And leave it as an empty plain,
107 lā tarā feehā ʿiwajan walā amtān Wherein thou seest neither curve nor ruggedness.
108 yawma-iḏin yattabiʿoona alddāʿiya lā ʿiwaja lahu waḫašaʿati al-aṣwātu lilrraḥmāni falā tasmaʿu illā hamsān On that day they follow the summoner who deceiveth not, and voices are hushed for the Beneficent, and thou hearest but a faint murmur.
109 yawma-iḏin lā tanfaʿu alššafāʿatu illā man aḏina lahu alrraḥmānu waraḍiya lahu qawlān On that day no intercession availeth save (that of) him unto whom the Beneficent hath given leave and whose word He accepteth.
110 yaʿlamu mā bayna aydeehim wamā ḫalfahum walā yuḥeeṭoona bihi ʿilmān He knoweth (all) that is before them and (all) that is behind them, while they cannot compass it in knowledge.
111 waʿanati alwujoohu lilḥayyi alqayyoomi waqad ḫāba man ḥamala ḏulmān And faces humble themselves before the Living, the Eternal. And he who beareth (a burden of) wrongdoing is indeed a failure (on that day).
112 waman yaʿmal mina alssalihati wahuwa mu/minun falā yaḫāfu ḏulman walā haḍmān And he who hath done some good works, being a believer, he feareth not injustice nor begrudging (of his wage).
113 wakaṯalika anzalnāhu qur-ānan ʿarabiyyan waṣarrafnā feehi mina alwaʿeedi laʿallahum yattaqoona aw yuḥdiṯu lahum ḏikrān Thus we have revealed it as a Lecture in Arabic, and have displayed therein certain threats, that peradventure they may keep from evil or that it may cause them to take heed.
114 fataʿālā allāhu almaliku alḥaqqu walā taʿjal bialqur-āni min qabli an yuqda ilayka waḥyuhu waqul rabbi zidnee ʿilmān Then exalted be Allah, the True King! And hasten not (O Muhammad) with the Qur'an ere its revelation hath been perfected unto thee, and say: My Lord! Increase me in knowledge.
115 walaqad ʿahidnā ilā ādama min qablu fanasiya walam najid lahu ʿazmān And verily We made a covenant of old with Adam, but he forgot, and We found no constancy in him.
116 wa-iḏ qulnā lilmalā-ikati osjudoo li-ādama fasajadoo illā ibleesa abā And when We said unto the angels: Fall prostrate before Adam, they fell prostrate (all) save Iblis; he refused.
117 faqulnā yā ādamu inna haṯa ʿaduwwun laka walizawjika falā yuḫrijannakumā mina aljannati fatašqā Therefor we said: O Adam! This is an enemy unto thee and unto thy wife, so let him not drive you both out of the Garden so that thou come to toil.
118 inna laka allā tajooʿa feehā walā taʿrā It is (vouchsafed) unto thee that thou hungerest not therein nor art naked,
119 waannaka lā taḏmao feehā walā tadha And that thou thirstest not therein nor art exposed to the sun's heat.
120 fawaswasa ilayhi alššaytanu qāla yā ādamu hal adulluka ʿalā šajarati alḫuldi wamulkin lā yablā But the devil whispered to him, saying: O Adam! Shall I show thee the tree of immortality and power that wasteth not away ?
121 faakalā minhā fabadat lahumā saw-ātuhumā waṭafiqā yaḫṣifāni ʿalayhimā min waraqi aljannati waʿasa ādamu rabbahu faġawā Then they twain ate thereof, so that their shame became apparent unto them, and they began to hide by heaping on themselves some of the leaves of the Garden. And Adam disobeyed his Lord, so went astray.
122 thumma ijtabāhu rabbuhu fatāba ʿalayhi wahadā Then his Lord chose him, and relented toward him, and guided him.
123 qāla ihbita minhā jameeʿan baʿḍukum libaʿḍin ʿaduwwun fa-immā ya/tiyannakum minnee hudan famani ittabaʿa hudāya falā yaḍillu walā yašqā He said: Go down hence, both of you, one of you a foe unto the other. But when there come unto you from Me a guidance, then whoso followeth My guidance, he will not go astray nor come to grief.
124 waman aʿraḍa ʿan ḏikree fa-inna lahu maʿeešatan ḍankān wanaḥšuruhu yawma alqiyāmati aʿmān But he who turneth away from remembrance of Me, his will be a narrow life, and I shall bring him blind to the assembly on the Day of Resurrection.
125 qāla rabbi lima ḥašartanee aʿmā waqad kuntu baṣeerān He will say: My Lord! Wherefor hast Thou gathered me (hither) blind, when I was wont to see ?
126 qāla kaṯalika atatka āyātunā fanaseetahā wakaṯalika alyawma tunsā He will say: So (it must be). Our revelations came unto thee but thou didst forget them. In like manner thou art forgotten this Day.
127 wakaṯalika najzee man asrafa walam yu/min bi-āyāti rabbihi walaʿaṯabu al-āḫirati ašaddu waabqā Thus do We reward him who is prodigal and believeth not the revelations of his Lord; and verily the doom of the Hereafter will be sterner and more lasting.
128 afalam yahdi lahum kam ahlaknā qablahum mina alqurooni yamšoona fee masākinihim inna fee ṯalika laāyātin li-olee alnnuhā Is it not a guidance for them (to know) how many a generation We destroyed before them, amid whose dwellings they walk ? Lo! therein verily are signs for men of thought.
129 walawlā kalimatun sabaqat min rabbika lakāna lizāman waajalun musammān And but for a decree that had already gone forth from thy Lord, and a term already fixed, the judgment would have been inevitable (in this world).
130 faiṣbir ʿalā mā yaqooloona wasabbiḥ biḥamdi rabbika qabla ṭulooʿi alššamsi waqabla ġuroobihā wamin ānā-i allāyli fasabbiḥ waaṭrāfa alnnahāri laʿallaka tarda Therefor (O Muhammad), bear with what they say, and celebrate the praise of thy Lord ere the rising of the sun and ere the going down thereof. And glorify Him some hours of the night and at the two ends of the day, that thou mayst find acceptance.
131 walā tamuddanna ʿaynayka ilā mā mattaʿnā bihi azwājan minhum zahrata alḥayāti alddunyā linaftinahum feehi warizqu rabbika ḫayrun waabqā And strain not thine eyes toward that which We cause some wedded pairs among them to enjoy, the flower of the life of the world, that We may try them thereby. The provision of thy Lord is better and more lasting.
132 wa/mur ahlaka bialṣalāti waistabir ʿalayhā lā nas-aluka rizqan naḥnu narzuquka waalʿāqibatu lilttaqwā And enjoin upon thy people worship, and be constant therein. We ask not of thee a provision: We provided for thee. And the sequel is for righteousness.
133 waqāloo lawlā ya/teenā bi-āyatin min rabbihi awa lam ta/tihim bayyinatu mā fee alṣuḥufi al-oolā And they say: If only he would bring us a miracle from his Lord! Hath there not come unto them the proof of what is in the former scriptures ?
134 walaw annā ahlaknāhum biʿaṯabin min qablihi laqāloo rabbanā lawlā arsalta ilaynā rasoolan fanattabiʿa āyātika min qabli an naḏilla wanaḫzā And if we had destroyed them with some punishment before it, they would assuredly have said: Our Lord! If only Thou hadst sent unto us a messenger, so that we might have followed Thy revelations before we were (thus) humbled and disgraced!
135 
qul kullun mutarabbiṣun fatarabbaṣoo fasataʿlamoona man aṣ-habu alṣirati alssawiyyi wamani ihtadā
Say: Each is awaiting; so await ye! Ye will come to know who are the owners of the path of equity, and who is right.
