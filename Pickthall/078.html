78. an-Naba': The Tidings

1 
ʿamma yatasāaloona
Whereof do they question one another ?

2 
ʿani alnnaba-i alʿaḏeemi
(It is) of the awful tidings,

3 
allāḏee hum feehi muḫtalifoona
Concerning which they are in disagreement.

4 
kallā sayaʿlamoona
Nay, but they will come to know!

5 
thumma kallā sayaʿlamoona
Nay, again, but they will come to know!

6 
alam najʿali al-arḍa mihādān
Have We not made the earth an expanse,

7 
waaljibāla awtādān
And the high hills bulwarks ?

8 
waḫalaqnākum azwājān
And We have created you in pairs,

9 
wajaʿalnā nawmakum subātān
And have appointed your sleep for repose,

10 
wajaʿalnā allāyla libāsān
And have appointed the night as a cloak,

11 
wajaʿalnā alnnahāra maʿāšān
And have appointed the day for livelihood.

12 
wabanaynā fawqakum sabʿan šidādān
And We have built above you seven strong (heavens),

13 
wajaʿalnā sirājan wahhājān
And have appointed a dazzling lamp,

14 
waanzalnā mina almuʿṣirāti māan ṯajjājān
And have sent down from the rainy clouds abundant water,

15 
linuḫrija bihi ḥabban wanabātān
Thereby to produce grain and plant,

16 
wajannātin alfāfān
And gardens of thick foliage.

17 
inna yawma alfaṣli kāna meeqātān
Lo! the Day of Decision is a fixed time,

18 
yawma yunfaḫu fee alṣoori fata/toona afwājān
A day when the trumpet is blown and ye come in multitudes,

19 
wafutiḥati alssamāo fakānat abwābān
And the heaven is opened and becometh as gates,

20 
wasuyyirati aljibālu fakānat sarābān
And the hills are set in motion and become as a mirage.

21 
inna jahannama kānat mirsadān
Lo! hell lurketh in ambush,

22 
lilttaġeena maābān
A home for the rebellious.

23 
lābiṯeena feehā aḥqābān
They will abide therein for ages.

24 
lā yaḏooqoona feehā bardan walā šarābān
Therein taste they neither coolness nor (any) drink

25 
illā ḥameeman waġassāqān
Save boiling water and a paralysing cold:

26 
jazāan wifāqān
Reward proportioned (to their evil deeds).

27 
innahum kānoo lā yarjoona ḥisābān
For lo! they looked not for a reckoning;

28 
wakaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā kiṯṯabān
They called Our revelations false with strong denial.

29 
wakulla šay-in ahsaynāhu kitābān
Everything have We recorded in a Book.

30 
faḏooqoo falan nazeedakum illā ʿaṯabān
So taste (of that which ye have earned). No increase do We give you save of torment.

31 
inna lilmuttaqeena mafāzān
Lo! for the duteous is achievement -

32 Gardens enclosed and vineyards,

33 
wakawāʿiba atrābān
And maidens for companions,

34 
waka/san dihāqān
And a full cup.

35 
lā yasmaʿoona feehā laġwan walā kiṯṯabān
There hear they never vain discourse, nor lying -

36 
jazāan min rabbika ʿataan ḥisābān
Requital from thy Lord - a gift in payment -

37 
rabbi alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wamā baynahumā alrraḥmāni lā yamlikoona minhu ḫitabān
Lord of the heavens and the earth, and (all) that is between them, the Beneficent; with Whom none can converse.

38 
yawma yaqoomu alrrooḥu waalmalā-ikatu ṣaffan lā yatakallamoona illā man aḏina lahu alrraḥmānu waqāla ṣawābān
On the day when the angels and the Spirit stand arrayed, they speak not, saving him whom the Beneficent alloweth and who speaketh right.

39 That is the True Day. So whoso will should seek recourse unto his Lord.

40 
innā anḏarnākum ʿaṯaban qareeban yawma yanḏuru almaro mā qaddamat yadāhu wayaqoolu alkāfiru yā laytanee kuntu turābān
Lo! We warn you of a doom at hand, a day whereon a man will look on that which his own hands have sent before, and the disbeliever will cry: "Would that I were dust!"
