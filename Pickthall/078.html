78. an-Naba': The Tidings

001 ʿamma yatasāaloona Whereof do they question one another ?
002 ʿani alnnaba-i alʿaḏeemi (It is) of the awful tidings,
003 allāḏee hum feehi muḫtalifoona Concerning which they are in disagreement.
004 kallā sayaʿlamoona Nay, but they will come to know!
005 thumma kallā sayaʿlamoona Nay, again, but they will come to know!
006 alam najʿali al-arḍa mihādān Have We not made the earth an expanse,
007 waaljibāla awtādān And the high hills bulwarks ?
008 waḫalaqnākum azwājān And We have created you in pairs,
009 wajaʿalnā nawmakum subātān And have appointed your sleep for repose,
010 wajaʿalnā allāyla libāsān And have appointed the night as a cloak,
011 wajaʿalnā alnnahāra maʿāšān And have appointed the day for livelihood.
012 wabanaynā fawqakum sabʿan šidādān And We have built above you seven strong (heavens),
013 wajaʿalnā sirājan wahhājān And have appointed a dazzling lamp,
014 waanzalnā mina almuʿṣirāti māan ṯajjājān And have sent down from the rainy clouds abundant water,
015 linuḫrija bihi ḥabban wanabātān Thereby to produce grain and plant,
016 wajannātin alfāfān And gardens of thick foliage.
017 inna yawma alfaṣli kāna meeqātān Lo! the Day of Decision is a fixed time,
018 yawma yunfaḫu fee alṣoori fata/toona afwājān A day when the trumpet is blown and ye come in multitudes,
019 wafutiḥati alssamāo fakānat abwābān And the heaven is opened and becometh as gates,
020 wasuyyirati aljibālu fakānat sarābān And the hills are set in motion and become as a mirage.
021 inna jahannama kānat mirsadān Lo! hell lurketh in ambush,
022 lilttaġeena maābān A home for the rebellious.
023 lābiṯeena feehā aḥqābān They will abide therein for ages.
024 lā yaḏooqoona feehā bardan walā šarābān Therein taste they neither coolness nor (any) drink
025 illā ḥameeman waġassāqān Save boiling water and a paralysing cold:
026 jazāan wifāqān Reward proportioned (to their evil deeds).
027 innahum kānoo lā yarjoona ḥisābān For lo! they looked not for a reckoning;
028 wakaṯṯaboo bi-āyātinā kiṯṯabān They called Our revelations false with strong denial.
029 wakulla šay-in ahsaynāhu kitābān Everything have We recorded in a Book.
030 faḏooqoo falan nazeedakum illā ʿaṯabān So taste (of that which ye have earned). No increase do We give you save of torment.
031 inna lilmuttaqeena mafāzān Lo! for the duteous is achievement -
032 [romanization missing] Gardens enclosed and vineyards,
033 wakawāʿiba atrābān And maidens for companions,
034 waka/san dihāqān And a full cup.
035 lā yasmaʿoona feehā laġwan walā kiṯṯabān There hear they never vain discourse, nor lying -
036 jazāan min rabbika ʿataan ḥisābān Requital from thy Lord - a gift in payment -
037 rabbi alssamāwāti waal-arḍi wamā baynahumā alrraḥmāni lā yamlikoona minhu ḫitabān Lord of the heavens and the earth, and (all) that is between them, the Beneficent; with Whom none can converse.
038 yawma yaqoomu alrrooḥu waalmalā-ikatu ṣaffan lā yatakallamoona illā man aḏina lahu alrraḥmānu waqāla ṣawābān On the day when the angels and the Spirit stand arrayed, they speak not, saving him whom the Beneficent alloweth and who speaketh right.
039 [romanization missing] That is the True Day. So whoso will should seek recourse unto his Lord.
40 
innā anḏarnākum ʿaṯaban qareeban yawma yanḏuru almaro mā qaddamat yadāhu wayaqoolu alkāfiru yā laytanee kuntu turābān
Lo! We warn you of a doom at hand, a day whereon a man will look on that which his own hands have sent before, and the disbeliever will cry: "Would that I were dust!"
