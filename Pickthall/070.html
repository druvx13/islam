70. al-Ma`arij: The Ladders

001 saala sā-ilun biʿaṯabin wāqiʿin A questioner questioned concerning the doom about to fall
002 lilkāfireena laysa lahu dāfiʿun Upon the disbelievers, which none can repel,
003 mina allāhi ḏee almaʿāriji From Allah, Lord of the Ascending Stairways
004 taʿruju almalā-ikatu waalrrooḥu ilayhi fee yawmin kāna miqdāruhu ḫamseena alfa sanatin (Whereby) the angels and the Spirit ascend unto Him in a Day whereof the span is fifty thousand years.
005 faiṣbir ṣabran jameelān But be patient (O Muhammad) with a patience fair to see.
006 innahum yarawnahu baʿeedān Lo! they behold it afar off
007 wanarāhu qareebān While we behold it nigh:
008 yawma takoonu alssamāo kaalmuhli The day when the sky will become as molten copper,
009 watakoonu aljibālu kaalʿihni And the hills become as flakes of wool,
010 walā yas-alu ḥameemun ḥameemān And no familiar friend will ask a question of his friend
011 yubaṣaroonahum yawaddu almujrimu law yaftadee min ʿaṯabi yawmi-iḏin bibaneehi Though they will be given sight of them. The guilty man will long to be able to ransom himself from the punishment of that day at the price of his children
012 wasahibatihi waaḫeehi And his spouse and his brother
013 wafaṣeelatihi allātee tu/weehi And his kin that harboured him
014 waman fee al-arḍi jameeʿan ṯumma yunjeehi And all that are in the earth, if then it might deliver him.
015 kallā innahā laḏā But nay! for lo! it is the fire of hell
016 nazzāʿatan lilššawā Eager to roast;
017 tadʿoo man adbara watawallā It calleth him who turned and fled (from truth),
018 wajamaʿa faawʿā And hoarded (wealth) and withheld it.
019 inna al-insāna ḫuliqa halooʿān Lo! man was created anxious,
020 iṯa massahu alššarru jazooʿān Fretful when evil befalleth him
021 wa-iṯa massahu alḫayru manooʿān And, when good befalleth him, grudging;
022 illā almuṣalleena Save worshippers.
023 allāḏeena hum ʿalā ṣalātihim dā-imoona Who are constant at their worship
024 waallaḏeena fee amwālihim ḥaqqun maʿloomun And in whose wealth there is a right acknowledged
025 lilssā-ili waalmaḥroomi For the beggar and the destitute;
026 waallaḏeena yuṣaddiqoona biyawmi alddeeni And those who believe in the Day of Judgment,
027 waallaḏeena hum min ʿaṯabi rabbihim mušfiqoona And those who are fearful of their Lord's doom -
028 inna ʿaṯaba rabbihim ġayru ma/moonin Lo! the doom of their Lord is that before which none can feel secure -
029 waallaḏeena hum lifuroojihim hafiḏoona And those who preserve their chastity
030 illā ʿalā azwājihim aw mā malakat aymānuhum fa-innahum ġayru maloomeena Save with their wives and those whom their right hands possess, for thus they are not blameworthy;
031 famani ibtaġā warāa ṯalika faolā-ika humu alʿādoona But whoso seeketh more than that, those are they who are transgressors;
032 waallaḏeena hum li-amānātihim waʿahdihim rāʿoona And those who keep their pledges and their covenant,
033 waallaḏeena hum bišahādātihim qā-imoona And those who stand by their testimony
034 waallaḏeena hum ʿalā ṣalātihim yuhafiḏoona And those who are attentive at their worship.
035 olā-ika fee jannātin mukramoona These will dwell in Gardens, honoured.
036 famāli allāḏeena kafaroo qibalaka muhṭiʿeena What aileth those who disbelieve, that they keep staring toward thee (O Muhammad), open-eyed,
037 ʿani alyameeni waʿani alššimāli ʿizeena On the right and on the left, in groups ?
038 ayaṭmaʿu kullu imri-in minhum an yudḫala jannata naʿeemin Doth every man among them hope to enter the Garden of Delight ?
039 kallā innā ḫalaqnāhum mimmā yaʿlamoona Nay, verily. Lo! We created them from what they know.
040 falā oqsimu birabbi almašāriqi waalmaġāribi innā laqādiroona But nay! I swear by the Lord of the rising-places and the setting-places of the planets that We verily are Able
041 ʿalā an nubaddila ḫayran minhum wamā naḥnu bimasbooqeena To replace them by (others) better than them. And we are not to be outrun.
042 faḏarhum yaḫooḍoo wayalʿaboo ḥattā yulāqoo yawmahumu allāḏee yooʿadoona So let them chat and play until they meet their Day which they are promised,
043 yawma yaḫrujoona mina al-ajdāṯi sirāʿan kaannahum ilā nuṣubin yoofiḍoona The day when they come forth from the graves in haste, as racing to a goal,
44 
khāšiʿatan absaruhum tarhaquhum ḏillatun ṯalika alyawmu allāḏee kānoo yooʿadoona
With eyes aghast, abasement stupefying them: Such is the Day which they are promised.
