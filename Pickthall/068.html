68. al-Qalam: The Pen

001 noon waalqalami wamā yasṭuroona Nun. By the pen and that which they write (therewith),
002 mā anta biniʿmati rabbika bimajnoonin Thou art not, for thy Lord's favour unto thee, a madman.
003 wa-inna laka laajran ġayra mamnoonin And lo! thine verily will be a reward unfailing.
004 wa-innaka laʿalā ḫuluqin ʿaḏeemin And lo! thou art of a tremendous nature.
005 fasatubṣiru wayubṣiroona And thou wilt see and they will see
006 bi-ayyikumu almaftoonu Which of you is the demented.
007 inna rabbaka huwa aʿlamu biman ḍalla ʿan sabeelihi wahuwa aʿlamu bialmuhtadeena Lo! thy Lord is Best Aware of him who strayeth from His way, and He is Best Aware of those who walk aright.
008 falā tuṭiʿi almukaṯṯibeena Therefor obey not thou the rejecters
009 waddoo law tudhinu fayudhinoona Who would have had thee compromise, that they may compromise.
010 walā tuṭiʿ kulla ḥallāfin maheenin Neither obey thou each feeble oath-monger,
011 hammāzin maššā-in binameemin Detracter, spreader abroad of slanders,
012 mannāʿin lilḫayri muʿtadin aṯeemin Hinderer of the good, transgressor, malefactor
013 ʿutullin baʿda ṯalika zaneemin Greedy therewithal, intrusive.
014 an kāna ṯa mālin wabaneena It is because he is possessed of wealth and children
015 iṯa tutlā ʿalayhi āyātunā qāla asateeru al-awwaleena That, when Our revelations are recited unto him, he saith: Mere fables of the men of old.
016 sanasimuhu ʿalā alḫurṭoomi We shall brand him on the nose.
017 innā balawnāhum kamā balawnā aṣ-haba aljannati iḏ aqsamoo layaṣrimunnahā muṣbiḥeena Lo! We have tried them as We tried the owners of the garden when they vowed that they would pluck its fruit next morning,
018 walā yastaṯnoona And made no exception (for the Will of Allah);
019 fatafa ʿalayhā ta-ifun min rabbika wahum nā-imoona Then a visitation from thy Lord came upon it while they slept
020 faaṣbaḥat kaalṣareemi And in the morning it was as if plucked.
021 fatanādaw muṣbiḥeena And they cried out one unto another in the morning,
022 ani iġdoo ʿalā ḥarṯikum in kuntum sarimeena Saying: Run unto your field if ye would pluck (the fruit).
023 fainṭalaqoo wahum yataḫāfatoona So they went off, saying one unto another in low tones:
024 an lā yadḫulannahā alyawma ʿalaykum miskeenun No needy man shall enter it to-day against you.
025 waġadaw ʿalā ḥardin qādireena They went betimes, strong in (this) purpose.
026 falammā raawhā qāloo innā ladalloona But when they saw it, they said: Lo! we are in error!
027 bal naḥnu maḥroomoona Nay, but we are desolate!
028 qāla awsaṭuhum alam aqul lakum lawlā tusabbiḥoona The best among them said: Said I not unto you: Why glorify ye not (Allah) ?
029 qāloo subhana rabbinā innā kunnā ḏālimeena They said: Glorified be our Lord! Lo! we have been wrong-doers.
030 faaqbala baʿḍuhum ʿalā baʿḍin yatalāwamoona Then some of them drew near unto others, self-reproaching.
031 qāloo yā waylanā innā kunnā taġeena They said: Alas for us! In truth we were outrageous.
032 ʿasā rabbunā an yubdilanā ḫayran minhā innā ilā rabbinā rāġiboona It may be that our Lord will give us better than this in place thereof. Lo! we beseech our Lord.
033 kaṯalika alʿaṯabu walaʿaṯabu al-āḫirati akbaru law kānoo yaʿlamoona Such was the punishment. And verily the punishment of the Hereafter is greater if they did but know.
034 inna lilmuttaqeena ʿinda rabbihim jannāti alnnaʿeemi Lo! for those who keep from evil are gardens of bliss with their Lord.
035 afanajʿalu almuslimeena kaalmujrimeena Shall We then treat those who have surrendered as We treat the guilty ?
036 mā lakum kayfa taḥkumoona What aileth you ? How foolishly ye judge!
037 am lakum kitābun feehi tadrusoona Or have ye a scripture wherein ye learn
038 inna lakum feehi lamā taḫayyaroona That ye shall indeed have all that ye choose ?
039 am lakum aymānun ʿalaynā bāliġatun ilā yawmi alqiyāmati inna lakum lamā taḥkumoona Or have ye a covenant on oath from Us that reacheth to the Day of Judgment, that yours shall be all that ye ordain ?
040 salhum ayyuhum biṯalika zaʿeemun Ask them (O Muhammad) which of them will vouch for that!
041 am lahum šurakāo falya/too bišurakā-ihim in kānoo sadiqeena Or have they other gods ? Then let them bring their other gods if they are truthful
042 yawma yukšafu ʿan sāqin wayudʿawna ilā alssujoodi falā yastaṭeeʿoona On the day when it befalleth in earnest, and they are ordered to prostrate themselves but are not able,
043 khāšiʿatan absaruhum tarhaquhum ḏillatun waqad kānoo yudʿawna ilā alssujoodi wahum sālimoona With eyes downcast, abasement stupefying them. And they had been summoned to prostrate themselves while they were yet unhurt.
044 faḏarnee waman yukaṯṯibu bihaṯa alḥadeeṯi sanastadrijuhum min ḥayṯu lā yaʿlamoona Leave Me (to deal) with those who give the lie to this pronouncement. We shall lead them on by steps from whence they know not.
045 waomlee lahum inna kaydee mateenun Yet I bear with them, for lo! My scheme is firm.
046 am tas-aluhum ajran fahum min maġramin muṯqaloona Or dost thou (Muhammad) ask a fee from them so that they are heavily taxed ?
047 am ʿindahumu alġaybu fahum yaktuboona Or is the Unseen theirs that they can write (thereof) ?
048 faiṣbir liḥukmi rabbika walā takun kasahibi alḥooti iḏ nādā wahuwa makḏoomun But wait thou for thy Lord's decree, and be not like him of the fish, who cried out in despair.
049 lawlā an tadārakahu niʿmatun min rabbihi lanubiḏa bialʿarā-i wahuwa maḏmoomun Had it not been that favour from his Lord had reached him he surely had been cast into the wilderness while he was reprobate.
050 faijtabāhu rabbuhu fajaʿalahu mina alssaliḥeena But his Lord chose him and placed him among the righteous.
051 wa-in yakādu allāḏeena kafaroo layuzliqoonaka bi-absarihim lammā samiʿoo alṯṯikra wayaqooloona innahu lamajnoonun And lo! those who disbelieve would fain disconcert thee with their eyes when they hear the Reminder, and they say: Lo! he is indeed mad;
52 wamā huwa illā ḏikrun lilʿālameena [Could not extract English for verse 52]
